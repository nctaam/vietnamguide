Practical Advice When Offline in Vietnam

  • Offline Navigation: GPS works without cellular data. If you pre-downloaded offline maps in Google Maps or Organic Maps, track your position along national highways (QL1A, QL4D) instead of unpaved trails.
  • Lodging Confirmation: When communicating with taxi or xe om drivers, show your accommodation confirmation containing the full Vietnamese street name and diacritical marks.
  • Free Wi-Fi: Wi-Fi is ubiquitous in Vietnam. Roadside cafes and family homestays throughout rural provinces offer free connection. Look for signs reading "Wi-Fi Miễn Phí".
