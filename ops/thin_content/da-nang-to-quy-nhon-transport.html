Da Nang to Quy Nhon Transport (2026): Train vs Bus

Updated September 26, 2026

Planning your Da Nang to Quy Nhon transport connects central Vietnam's major air hub with one of the coast's most pristine, uncrowded beach destinations. Covering 300 kilometers south along the East Sea via Quang Nam and Quang Ngai provinces, the route allows travelers to leave the resort bustle of Da Nang and Hoi An behind for the golden sands, fresh seafood fishing villages, and historic Cham towers of Binh Dinh province.

The Concierge Verdict

Take the daytime Reunification Express railway train (SE1 or SE3) departing Da Nang Railway Station for Dieu Tri Station (the rail gateway to Quy Nhon). Trains take approximately 5.5 hours, provide comfortable air-conditioned soft seats or sleeper berths for 240,000 to 450,000 VND ($9.60 – $18 USD), and showcase peaceful coastal rice paddy scenery without highway traffic risks. For travelers seeking ultra-luxury, book The Vietage by Anantara, a dedicated high-end railway carriage offering gourmet multi-course dining and free-flow wine along the exact same rail tracks.

Transport Option Breakdown: Train vs Limousine vs Luxury Rail

1. Reunification Express Trains: Scenic & Reliable

Vietnam Railways operates multiple daily trains connecting Da Nang Railway Station (in downtown Hai Chau district) with Dieu Tri Station, located 10 kilometers inland from Quy Nhon center. The scenic daytime trains SE1 (departing around 08:30 AM) and SE3 (departing around 11:30 AM) offer the most pleasant travel experiences.

Passengers can choose between first-class 4-berth soft sleeper compartments (approx 450,000 VND / $18 USD) or second-class air-conditioned soft seats (approx 240,000 VND / $9.60 USD). Large panoramic train windows look out onto coastal lagoons, emerald rice paddies, and buffalo pastures. Upon arrival at Dieu Tri station, metered Mai Linh or Sun Taxi cabs wait to carry travelers into central Quy Nhon or out to Bai Xep for roughly 150,000 to 180,000 VND ($6 – $7.20 USD).

2. The Vietage: Five-Star Railway Luxury

For visitors celebrating special occasions or seeking Southeast Asia's premier railway experience, The Vietage is an exclusive custom-designed carriage attached to the daily national train. Limited to just 12 passenger seats, the carriage features private booth seating, marble bar counters, dedicated onboard head and shoulder massages, and a three-course fine dining menu paired with free-flowing French wines and artisanal cheeses.

3. Shared Highway Limousines & Sleeper Buses

Operators like Quy Thao, Son Tung, and Ha Linh run 9-seater DCar limousines and 34-berth sleeper buses departing Da Nang Central Bus Station or designated city offices along Nguyen Tat Thanh street. Limousines take roughly 5.5 to 6.0 hours along National Route 1A (QL1A).

While limousines offer the convenience of dropping passengers directly at their city hotel in Quy Nhon without needing a taxi from Dieu Tri station, highway traffic and ongoing road construction make the journey slightly more fatiguing than the train.

Dieu Tri Station Transfer Notice

Be aware that the main north-south national rail line does not pass directly through downtown Quy Nhon. All express passenger trains stop at Dieu Tri Station in Tuy Phuoc district. There is a small spur train that runs between Dieu Tri and Quy Nhon city station twice daily, but taking a metered taxi or booking a GrabCar from Dieu Tri is much faster and hassle-free.

Terminal Boarding Protocol & Electronic Ticketing

Modern transit networks across Vietnam operate predominantly on paperless verification. Knowing the boarding routine prevents confusion at crowded transport hubs:

  • Digital QR Code Check-in: Vietnam Railways (dsvn.vn) and major intercity coach operators (FUTA Bus Lines, Interbus, Hai Van) issue digital boarding vouchers directly to your smartphone. You do not need to print physical boarding passes. Present the PDF confirmation or SMS reservation code alongside your original passport at platform ticket barriers 20 to 30 minutes before departure.
  • Luggage Allowances & Cargo Handling: Standard express limousine vans allocate space for one 20kg checked suitcase plus a small daypack per passenger inside rear cargo compartments. Vietnam Railways permits up to 20kg of personal luggage per ticket without surcharge. High-speed ferries to Con Dao and Phu Quoc enforce a 15kg carry-on limit, with oversized diving duffels requiring a nominal excess baggage fee of 30,000–50,000 VND at pier scales.
  • Highway Rest Stop Realities (Trạm Dừng Chân): Overland coach journeys exceeding 3 hours schedule mandatory 20-minute rest breaks at commercial highway complexes (such as those along Expressways CT01 and CT02). These facilities feature clean Western-style restrooms, hot food noodle stalls (phở, bánh mì), and convenience marts. Note your bus license plate number carefully, as dozens of identical green or red coaches park simultaneously in busy transit lots.
  • Arrival Station Scam Prevention: When disembarking at provincial railway terminals or bus plazas, bypass aggressive freelance drivers who crowd exit doors whispering flat rates. Proceed directly to marked taxi pickup bays to hire metered Mai Linh cabs, or book via ride-hailing applications (Grab, Be) where fares and routes remain digitally locked.

Transit Booking Windows & Holiday Peak Fares

Fare pricing and seat availability fluctuate significantly around national public holidays. Following these timing rules guarantees confirmed reservations:

  • Standard Advance Reservation Windows: Regular weekend departures should be reserved 3 to 7 days ahead through reputable digital platforms (such as 12Go Asia or Vexere). For standard mid-week journeys outside holiday cycles, reserving 24 to 48 hours in advance provides ample seat selection.
  • Tet Holiday & Peak National Festivities: During the Lunar New Year (Tet Nguyen Dan, occurring in late January or February) and Reunification Day week (April 30 – May 1), rail tickets open 60 days in advance and sell out within hours. Intercity coach companies apply legal holiday surcharges of 30% to 50% to cover empty return legs. Book your transportation before locking in non-refundable hotel stays during these peak dates.
  • Child Fares & Family Policies: Vietnam Railways offers free travel for children under 6 years old sharing a berth with an adult; children aged 6 to 10 receive a 25% discount on passenger tickets. On luxury limousine vans, children occupying their own leather seat pay full adult fare regardless of age. High-speed boat services provide discounted youth tickets for children measuring under 1.2 meters in height.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book Da Nang to Quy Nhon train tickets?

For normal weekday travel, booking 2 to 3 days ahead on dsvn.vn or 12Go is sufficient. For weekend departures or holiday periods, reserve at least one week in advance for lower sleeper berths.

Is there a direct flight from Da Nang to Quy Nhon?

No, there are no scheduled commercial flights between Da Nang (DAD) and Quy Nhon (UIH) due to the relatively short overland distance. Train or private car is the fastest route.

Can I stop at Hoi An before continuing to Quy Nhon?

Yes, many travelers take a private car from Hoi An directly down National Route 1A to Quy Nhon, taking roughly 5 hours and visiting My Lai Memorial along the way.
