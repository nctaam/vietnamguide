Saigon to Vung Tau Transport (2026): Ferry vs Limousine

Updated September 26, 2026

Organizing your Saigon to Vung Tau transport connects Ho Chi Minh City with its closest coastal seaside retreat. Located 95 kilometers southeast across the Long Tau and Soai Rap river mouths, Vung Tau has long served as a primary weekend beach escape for city residents, expats, and foreign travelers. Visitors today choose between two excellent transit modes: the scenic Greenlines DP fast hydrofoil ferry gliding down the river from downtown Bach Dang Waterbus Pier, or shared luxury DCar limousines traveling via the Long Thanh – Dau Giay expressway network.

The Concierge Verdict

Take the Greenlines DP high-speed hydrofoil ferry departing from Bach Dang Pier in District 1 if you are staying in central Saigon. The boat takes roughly two hours, completely avoids highway traffic congestion, and provides panoramic views of mangrove forests and container shipping channels for 340,000 to 380,000 VND ($13.60 – $15.20 USD). Choose a luxury 9-seater DCar limousine van (such as Hoa Mai or Huy Hoang) if you are departing directly from Tan Son Nhat Airport or prefer direct door-to-door hotel drop-off in Vung Tau for 180,000 to 220,000 VND ($7.20 – $8.80 USD).

Transport Option Breakdown: Fast Ferry vs Luxury Limousine

1. Greenlines DP High-Speed Ferry: River to Sea

The Greenlines DP hydrofoil catamaran offers the most scenic and relaxing transit experience between Saigon and Vung Tau. Boats depart from Bach Dang Waterbus Station, situated right on Ton Duc Thang Street in District 1.

The two-hour voyage glides down the Saigon River, weaving past the mangrove wetlands of the Can Gio Biosphere Reserve before opening up into Ganh Rai Bay and docking at the Ho May Pier on Vung Tau's Front Beach (Bai Truoc). The passenger cabin features comfortable airline-style seating, cold air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, overhead television screens, clean restrooms, and a rear open-air deck for river photography. Standard adult weekday tickets cost 340,000 VND ($13.60 USD), rising to 380,000 VND ($15.20 USD) on weekends.

2. Shared DCar Limousines: Door-to-Door Ease

For passengers traveling with multiple pieces of heavy luggage or those departing directly from Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN), shared DCar limousines represent the standard choice. Reputable operators including Hoa Mai, Huy Hoang, and Vie Limousine run departures every 30 to 45 minutes throughout the day.

These 9-passenger customized Ford Transit vans feature leather captain chairs with electronic recline, massage lumbar pads, USB charging docks, and individual reading lamps. Vans utilize the CT01 expressway across Dong Nai before connecting onto National Route 51. The journey takes 2.0 to 2.5 hours depending on suburban traffic. Crucially, limousine tickets include free drop-off at any hotel or private residence throughout Vung Tau's Back Beach (Bai Sau) and Front Beach districts.

3. Private Vehicle Transfers & Weekend Warnings

If you travel as a family or group of three or more, booking a private driver in a Toyota Fortuner or Mitsubishi Xpander costs approximately 1,400,000 to 1,800,000 VND ($56 – $72 USD) one way, inclusive of highway toll charges. The driver picks you up from your hotel lobby at your chosen departure hour.

Pay close attention to weekend travel patterns: tens of thousands of Saigon residents travel to Vung Tau on Friday evenings and Saturday mornings, returning Sunday afternoon. During Sunday return windows (15:00 to 20:00), National Route 51 and the Long Thanh expressway experience severe traffic bottlenecks that can extend road journeys to 3.5 or 4 hours. Booking the Greenlines DP ferry on Sunday avoids these road delays entirely.

Terminal Boarding Protocol & Electronic Ticketing

Modern transit networks across Vietnam operate predominantly on paperless verification. Knowing the boarding routine prevents confusion at crowded transport hubs:

  • Digital QR Code Check-in: Vietnam Railways (dsvn.vn) and major intercity coach operators (FUTA Bus Lines, Interbus, Hai Van) issue digital boarding vouchers directly to your smartphone. You do not need to print physical boarding passes. Present the PDF confirmation or SMS reservation code alongside your original passport at platform ticket barriers 20 to 30 minutes before departure.
  • Luggage Allowances & Cargo Handling: Standard express limousine vans allocate space for one 20kg checked suitcase plus a small daypack per passenger inside rear cargo compartments. Vietnam Railways permits up to 20kg of personal luggage per ticket without surcharge. High-speed ferries to Con Dao and Phu Quoc enforce a 15kg carry-on limit, with oversized diving duffels requiring a nominal excess baggage fee of 30,000–50,000 VND at pier scales.
  • Highway Rest Stop Realities (Trạm Dừng Chân): Overland coach journeys exceeding 3 hours schedule mandatory 20-minute rest breaks at commercial highway complexes (such as those along Expressways CT01 and CT02). These facilities feature clean Western-style restrooms, hot food noodle stalls (phở, bánh mì), and convenience marts. Note your bus license plate number carefully, as dozens of identical green or red coaches park simultaneously in busy transit lots.
  • Arrival Station Scam Prevention: When disembarking at provincial railway terminals or bus plazas, bypass aggressive freelance drivers who crowd exit doors whispering flat rates. Proceed directly to marked taxi pickup bays to hire metered Mai Linh cabs, or book via ride-hailing applications (Grab, Be) where fares and routes remain digitally locked.

Transit Booking Windows & Holiday Peak Fares

Fare pricing and seat availability fluctuate significantly around national public holidays. Following these timing rules guarantees confirmed reservations:

  • Standard Advance Reservation Windows: Regular weekend departures should be reserved 3 to 7 days ahead through reputable digital platforms (such as 12Go Asia or Vexere). For standard mid-week journeys outside holiday cycles, reserving 24 to 48 hours in advance provides ample seat selection.
  • Tet Holiday & Peak National Festivities: During the Lunar New Year (Tet Nguyen Dan, occurring in late January or February) and Reunification Day week (April 30 – May 1), rail tickets open 60 days in advance and sell out within hours. Intercity coach companies apply legal holiday surcharges of 30% to 50% to cover empty return legs. Book your transportation before locking in non-refundable hotel stays during these peak dates.
  • Child Fares & Family Policies: Vietnam Railways offers free travel for children under 6 years old sharing a berth with an adult; children aged 6 to 10 receive a 25% discount on passenger tickets. On luxury limousine vans, children occupying their own leather seat pay full adult fare regardless of age. High-speed boat services provide discounted youth tickets for children measuring under 1.2 meters in height.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the ferry operate during heavy rain or monsoon weather?

Greenlines DP catamarans operate in moderate rain without disruption. However, during tropical depressions or typhoon warnings with rough sea swells in Ganh Rai Bay, port authorities suspend sailings for safety.

Can I bring luggage on the Greenlines DP ferry?

Yes, each ticket includes up to 20kg of personal luggage. Large suitcases are stored in designated front and rear baggage racks upon boarding.

Where does the ferry dock in Vung Tau?

Boats dock at the Greenlines DP terminal beside Ho May Park on Tran Phu Street (Front Beach), a 5-minute taxi ride from central Vung Tau hotels.
