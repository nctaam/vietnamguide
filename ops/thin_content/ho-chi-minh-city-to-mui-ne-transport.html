Ho Chi Minh City to Mui Ne: Express Bus, Train & Car

Updated September 26, 2026

Traveling from Ho Chi Minh City to Mui Ne has undergone a radical transformation. Covering approximately 200 km eastward into Binh Thuan province, this passage once required a grinding five-hour trek along heavily congested National Highway 1A. Following the completion of the Dau Giay-Phan Thiet Expressway (CT01), modern transit times have plummeted to just 2.5 hours, establishing Mui Ne as southern Vietnam's most accessible coastal beach getaway.

The Concierge Verdict

For most travelers, modern 9-passenger VIP limousine vans represent the best combination of speed, comfort, and value. Operators like G5Car and 3S Limo pick up passengers in central District 1 or directly outside Tan Son Nhat Airport, transporting you via the high-speed CT01 expressway directly to your resort doorstep along Nguyen Dinh Chieu in under 2.5 hours for roughly 280,000 VND to 320,000 VND. Take the scenic morning train (SPT2) only if you travel with young children needing restroom mobility or wish to avoid highway traffic altogether.

Transport Options At A Glance

Option 1: Express Limousine Van (Recommended)

The opening of the Dau Giay-Phan Thiet Expressway transformed luxury minibus travel along this corridor. Several reputable operators run nine-seater Ford Transit and Hyundai Solati vans customized with plush leather massage captain chairs, USB charging sockets, personal reading lamps, and onboard Wi-Fi.

Leading Operators & Pickup Points

Companies such as G5Car, 3S Limo, and ADT Limousine operate hourly departures from 6:00 AM through 8:00 PM. In Saigon, departures leave from office hubs along Pham Ngu Lao and De Tham in District 1, with select runs offering direct pickups from Tan Son Nhat International Airport domestic terminal. Tickets cost between 260,000 VND and 320,000 VND per seat.

Door-to-Door Resort Delivery

Unlike standard big buses that terminate at distant highway stations, limousine vans drop passengers directly at hotels and resorts spanning the entire fifteen-kilometer coastal strip along Nguyen Dinh Chieu and Huynh Thuc Khang in Mui Ne. The entire journey takes just 2 hours 15 minutes to 2.5 hours under normal traffic, saving both taxi fares and intermodal transfer hassle.

Option 2: Daily SPT2 Scenic Train to Phan Thiet

Vietnam Railways operates a dedicated daily tourist service, train SPT2, connecting Saigon Railway Station (Ga Sai Gon in District 3) with Phan Thiet Railway Station (Ga Phan Thiet).

Train Schedules & Ticket Fares

Daily departures pull out of Saigon at 6:45 AM, arriving in Phan Thiet around 10:30 AM (journey time roughly 3 hours 45 minutes). Air-conditioned soft-seat tickets range from 180,000 VND to 240,000 VND per adult. Scenic views open across Binh Duong and Dong Nai provinces before entering the rolling dragon fruit orchards of Binh Thuan, where thousands of sprawling cactus-like trellises stretch toward coastal hillsides. For the return journey, train SPT1 departs Phan Thiet at 1:10 PM, reaching Saigon at 5:00 PM.

Connecting from Phan Thiet Station to Mui Ne

Ga Phan Thiet sits on the western outskirts of Phan Thiet city, approximately twenty kilometers west of Mui Ne's resort strip. Upon exiting the terminal concourse, you have two transport options:

  • Metered Taxi: Mai Linh and Vinasun taxis wait outside the station. A direct taxi ride to Nguyen Dinh Chieu resorts costs 250,000 VND to 300,000 VND and takes 25 to 30 minutes.
  • Local Red Bus (Route 1 or Route 9): Stops along the main road 500 meters from the station, charging 35,000 VND per passenger with drop-offs along the Mui Ne beach road.

Option 3: Private Car Transfer

For traveling families, groups of four or more, or passengers landing late at Tan Son Nhat Airport, booking a private transfer offers maximum speed and zero friction. A driver meets you at your hotel lobby or airport arrivals hall with a name board, carrying your luggage straight onto the CT01 expressway.

Private transfers in a 4-seater sedan (Toyota Vios) cost 1,800,000 VND, while a 7-seater SUV (Toyota Fortuner or Mitsubishi Xpander) costs 2,100,000 VND to 2,400,000 VND, inclusive of expressway tolls and fuel. Transit time is just 2 hours 15 minutes door-to-door.

Terminal Boarding Protocol & Electronic Ticketing

Modern transit networks across Vietnam operate predominantly on paperless verification. Knowing the boarding routine prevents confusion at crowded transport hubs:

  • Digital QR Code Check-in: Vietnam Railways (dsvn.vn) and major intercity coach operators (FUTA Bus Lines, Interbus, Hai Van) issue digital boarding vouchers directly to your smartphone. You do not need to print physical boarding passes. Present the PDF confirmation or SMS reservation code alongside your original passport at platform ticket barriers 20 to 30 minutes before departure.
  • Luggage Allowances & Cargo Handling: Standard express limousine vans allocate space for one 20kg checked suitcase plus a small daypack per passenger inside rear cargo compartments. Vietnam Railways permits up to 20kg of personal luggage per ticket without surcharge. High-speed ferries to Con Dao and Phu Quoc enforce a 15kg carry-on limit, with oversized diving duffels requiring a nominal excess baggage fee of 30,000–50,000 VND at pier scales.
  • Highway Rest Stop Realities (Trạm Dừng Chân): Overland coach journeys exceeding 3 hours schedule mandatory 20-minute rest breaks at commercial highway complexes (such as those along Expressways CT01 and CT02). These facilities feature clean Western-style restrooms, hot food noodle stalls (phở, bánh mì), and convenience marts. Note your bus license plate number carefully, as dozens of identical green or red coaches park simultaneously in busy transit lots.
  • Arrival Station Scam Prevention: When disembarking at provincial railway terminals or bus plazas, bypass aggressive freelance drivers who crowd exit doors whispering flat rates. Proceed directly to marked taxi pickup bays to hire metered Mai Linh cabs, or book via ride-hailing applications (Grab, Be) where fares and routes remain digitally locked.

Transit Booking Windows & Holiday Peak Fares

Fare pricing and seat availability fluctuate significantly around national public holidays. Following these timing rules guarantees confirmed reservations:

  • Standard Advance Reservation Windows: Regular weekend departures should be reserved 3 to 7 days ahead through reputable digital platforms (such as 12Go Asia or Vexere). For standard mid-week journeys outside holiday cycles, reserving 24 to 48 hours in advance provides ample seat selection.
  • Tet Holiday & Peak National Festivities: During the Lunar New Year (Tet Nguyen Dan, occurring in late January or February) and Reunification Day week (April 30 – May 1), rail tickets open 60 days in advance and sell out within hours. Intercity coach companies apply legal holiday surcharges of 30% to 50% to cover empty return legs. Book your transportation before locking in non-refundable hotel stays during these peak dates.
  • Child Fares & Family Policies: Vietnam Railways offers free travel for children under 6 years old sharing a berth with an adult; children aged 6 to 10 receive a 25% discount on passenger tickets. On luxury limousine vans, children occupying their own leather seat pay full adult fare regardless of age. High-speed boat services provide discounted youth tickets for children measuring under 1.2 meters in height.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much did the new expressway reduce travel time?

Before the Dau Giay-Phan Thiet Expressway opened, the journey took four to five hours on congested National Highway 1A. The expressway has cut highway driving time to just over two hours, making Mui Ne a feasible weekend escape from Saigon.

Can I ride a scooter from Ho Chi Minh City to Mui Ne?

While adventurous motorcyclists occasionally ride the coastal backroads via Ba Ria and Lagi (220 km, taking 5-6 hours), motorbikes are strictly prohibited from driving on the CT01 expressway. Long-distance scooter riding along National Highway 1A involves heavy truck traffic and is not recommended.

Do limousine vans have luggage restrictions?

Each passenger on a 9-seater limousine is typically permitted one standard 24-inch suitcase (up to 20 kg) and one small backpack. If traveling with oversized surfboards or kitesurfing bags, notify the operator during booking or reserve an extra seat.
