Where to Stay in Vung Tau (2026): Best Beaches & Hotels

Updated September 26, 2026

Choosing where to stay in Vung Tau depends on whether your weekend trip prioritizes active ocean swimming, relaxed sunset dining, or peaceful hillside seclusion. Extending into the East Sea just two hours southeast of Ho Chi Minh City, Vung Tau wraps around two distinct coastal fronts: eastern-facing Back Beach (Bãi Sau) with wide surf sands, and western-facing Front Beach (Bãi Trước) with shaded seaside parks, hydrofoil ferry terminals, and evening seafood promenades.

The Concierge Verdict

Book a modern high-rise hotel along Thuy Van Street at Back Beach (Bai Sau) if your priority is morning ocean swimming, bodysurfing, and wide sandy beaches. Back Beach offers broad shores and fresh coastal breezes. Choose a boutique hotel at Front Beach (Bai Truoc) near Quang Trung Street if you prefer walking to the Greenlines DP fast ferry pier, enjoying panoramic sunset cocktail bars, and dining at local seafood markets without hiring motorbikes.

Neighborhood Base Comparison in Vung Tau

1. Back Beach (Bai Sau): The Premier Swimming Shore

Back Beach stretches for over three kilometers along the eastern edge of the peninsula. Lined with casuarina trees and broad paved sidewalks, Thuy Van Street forms the primary accommodation row for beachgoers.

High-end properties including The Imperial Hotel Vung Tau and Vias Hotel Vung Tau offer private beach clubs, infinity rooftop pools, and direct pedestrian bridges across the avenue to the sand, with rates from 1,600,000 to 3,200,000 VND ($64 – $128 USD). Mid-range and budget travelers will find dozens of clean, comfortable mini-hotels on La Van Cau and Phan Van Tri streets offering double rooms with air conditioning and sea glimpses for 350,000 to 600,000 VND ($14 – $24 USD).

2. Front Beach (Bai Truoc): Sunset Strolls & Dining

Front Beach faces southwest into Ganh Rai Bay, providing Front Beach Park with manicured green lawns, outdoor exercise areas, and shaded stone benches. It is also the docking location for Greenlines DP catamarans from Saigon.

Petro House Hotel and Rex Hotel provide heritage hospitality within walking distance of the municipal museum, Bach Dinh (White Palace), and coastal cafes along Ha Long Street. Rates average 550,000 to 1,200,000 VND ($22 – $48 USD). Front Beach is ideal for business travelers, couples seeking romantic sunset views over the water, and ferry travelers avoiding long transfers.

3. Mulberry Beach (Bai Dau): Mountain-Facing Sanctuaries

Heading north along Tran Phu Street brings you to Bai Dau, where steep forested cliffs drop directly into the sea. This rocky coast is home to upscale boutique resorts and private rental villas.

Marina Bay Vung Tau Resort & Spa features ocean-view infinity pools that seem to blend into the horizon, starting at 1,900,000 VND ($76 USD). It is completely insulated from weekend tourist crowds, although you will need a 10-minute taxi ride to reach city seafood markets.

Weekend Surges & Booking Guidelines

Vung Tau operates on two distinct pricing tiers: Monday through Thursday rates are extremely reasonable, while Saturday nights frequently see room prices double due to weekend demand from Ho Chi Minh City. If visiting on a weekend, book your hotel at least two weeks in advance and reserve your return Greenlines DP ferry ticket simultaneously.

Arrival Logistics & Transport Navigation

Arriving in Vung Tau smoothly requires choosing the right transit mode from regional arrival hubs to your accommodation door:

  • Railway & Bus Hub Connection: Arriving travelers land at Ho Chi Minh City transport hub (connecting via express highway or hydrofoil), located approximately 100 km southeast of central Saigon. Standard meter taxi fares run Express catamaran ticket costs 320,000–380,000 VND. Always choose metered operators (Mai Linh, Vinasun) or book via ride-hailing apps (Grab, Be) to eliminate fare negotiations.
  • Airport Transit Options: The closest air facility is Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN) in Ho Chi Minh City, located 95 km via Long Thanh - Dau Giay Expressway. Reliable door-to-door transit runs 180,000–240,000 VND per passenger via luxury limousine van (Hoa Mai or Huy Hoang). Many boutique properties arrange pre-booked airport pickups that meet you outside the arrivals hall with personalized name boards.
  • Night Bus Terminal Protocols: Long-distance sleeper routes typically terminate at Vung Tau Bus Station on Nam Ky Khoi Nghia Street. Early morning arrivals (between 04:30 and 06:00) can leave luggage safely at hotel reception lobbies before daytime check-in opens.
  • Local Scam Prevention: At Bai Sau (Back Beach), verify seafood restaurant menu weights carefully before ordering to avoid tourist markup traps.

Check-in Regulations & Guest Protocols (2026 Verification)

Vietnamese lodging operations follow specific administrative and security guidelines. Keeping these practical considerations in mind ensures effortless hotel check-ins:

  • Mandatory Police Guest Registration: Vietnamese law requires all licensed accommodation providers (from 5-star properties down to rural homestays) to register foreign guests on the local public security database within 24 hours of arrival. You must present an original valid passport and Vietnam entry visa (or eVisa confirmation document). Reception staff will photocopy or scan your documents and promptly return the physical passport.
  • Standard Check-in & Check-out Windows: Standard hotel check-in across Vietnam commences at 14:00, with check-out scheduled between 11:30 and 12:00. If your sleeper train or bus arrives at dawn, most hotels store backpacks in locked luggage rooms free of charge while you explore nearby morning noodle stalls. Guaranteed early check-in before 08:00 typically incurs a 50% day-rate surcharge.
  • Deposit Practices & Payment Options: Mid-range and luxury properties routinely request a temporary room key deposit of 500,000–1,000,000 VND ($20–$40 USD) in cash, or place an equivalent temporary hold on an international credit card (Visa, Mastercard). Cash deposits are returned in full during room inspection at check-out. Credit card pre-authorizations release back into your account within 7 to 14 banking days.
  • Keycard Electricity Controls & Room Etiquette: Guest rooms utilize electronic keycards for master circuit activation. When leaving your room, air conditioning compressors automatically shut down to comply with national green energy conservation standards. Tap water across Vietnam is non-potable; reputable hotels supply two complimentary 350ml glass bottles of purified water daily or feature filtered water refill dispensers in common corridors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Back Beach safe for ocean swimming?

Yes, Back Beach has professional municipal lifeguards patrolling daily from 06:00 to 18:00. Always swim between the red-and-yellow safety flags and heed warnings during rough afternoon swells.

How far is Back Beach from the ferry terminal?

Back Beach sits roughly 2.5 kilometers east of the Greenlines DP ferry terminal at Front Beach, taking 5 to 7 minutes by Grab taxi (approx 40,000 to 50,000 VND / $1.60 - $2.00 USD).

Can I rent a scooter easily in Vung Tau?

Yes, virtually all hotels and homestays provide semi-automatic and automatic scooter rentals for 120,000 to 150,000 VND ($4.80 – $6.00 USD) per day.
