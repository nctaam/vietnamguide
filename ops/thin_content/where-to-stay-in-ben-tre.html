Where to Stay in Ben Tre (2026): Best River & Eco-Stays

Updated September 26, 2026

Deciding where to stay in Ben Tre determines whether you experience the tourist-heavy day-trip circuit or immerse yourself in the quiet river rhythms of Vietnam's coconut capital. Located just 85 kilometers southwest of Ho Chi Minh City across the Rach Mieu Bridge, Ben Tre province is woven together by alluvial tributaries of the Tien and Ham Luong rivers. Selecting the right lodging base lets you cycle along tranquil canal paths shaded by water palms and wake to dawn birdsong.

The Concierge Verdict

Choose a riverside eco-lodge along the Ham Luong River or Nhon Thanh canals (such as Mango Home Riverside or Ben Tre Riverside Resort) for 850,000 to 1,650,000 VND ($34 – $66 USD) per night. These properties provide complimentary village bicycles, wooden sampan excursions, and lush garden terraces away from highway traffic. Travelers on a tight backpacker budget should book an orchard homestay in An Khanh or Chau Thanh for 350,000 to 550,000 VND ($14 – $22 USD) including home-cooked family dinners.

Ben Tre Accommodation Neighborhood Comparison

1. Ham Luong Riverfront & Nhon Thanh: Tropical Quietude

Nhon Thanh and the Ham Luong riverbank provide the ultimate Mekong escape. Tucked behind thick stands of nipa palm, these boutique retreats feel worlds away from urban chaos. However, they remain just 15 minutes by bicycle from local boat jetties.

Lodges here feature thatched bungalows with open-air garden showers, private river verandas, and on-site cooking classes. Rates range from 850,000 to 1,850,000 VND ($34 – $74 USD) per night with generous buffet breakfasts. Staff organize self-guided bike loops across flat canal bridges where you can watch artisan workshops weave coconut fiber floor mats and boil fresh coconut candy.

2. Chau Thanh & An Khanh: Authentic Farm Homestays

If you crave authentic cultural connection, head north of town into Chau Thanh district. Settlements like An Khanh and Phu Tuc are famous for rambutan, durian, and longan fruit orchards intersected by narrow irrigation canals.

Local families operate welcoming wooden stilt homestays where rooms cost 350,000 to 650,000 VND ($14 – $26 USD). Stays generally include communal evening banquets featuring elephant ear fish (cá tai tượng chiên xù) rolled in rice paper with fresh herbs, crisp banh xeo pancakes, and claypot snakehead fish. Sleeping under mosquito netting to the sound of croaking river frogs offers an unforgettable delta experience.

3. Ben Tre City Center: Night Market & Transit Stays

Downtown Ben Tre borders the northern bank of Truc Giang Lake and the Ben Tre River. The city center is practical for travelers relying on public bus links or arriving late in the evening from Ho Chi Minh City.

The city's flagship high-rise, Diamond Stars Hotel Ben Tre, offers contemporary 5-star rooms and an infinity pool overlooking the city rooftops for 1,200,000 to 1,800,000 VND ($48 – $72 USD). Modest budget hotels like Viet Uc charge 450,000 to 750,000 VND ($18 – $30 USD). Staying downtown puts you within a 5-minute stroll of the lively Ben Tre Night Market on Hung Vuong Street, famous for sugarcane juice and fresh river prawn noodle bowls.

Arrival Logistics & Transport Navigation

Arriving in Ben Tre (Mekong Delta) smoothly requires choosing the right transit mode from regional arrival hubs to your accommodation door:

  • Railway & Bus Hub Connection: Arriving travelers land at Ben Tre Provincial Coach Station on Highway 60, located approximately 3.5 km from downtown riverside promenade. Standard meter taxi fares run 50,000–70,000 VND. Always choose metered operators (Mai Linh, Vinasun) or book via ride-hailing apps (Grab, Be) to eliminate fare negotiations.
  • Airport Transit Options: The closest air facility is Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN) in Ho Chi Minh City, located 85 km southwest across Rach Mieu Bridge. Reliable door-to-door transit runs 90,000–120,000 VND via FUTA (Phuong Trang) bus from Western Coach Station (Ben Xe Mien Tay). Many boutique properties arrange pre-booked airport pickups that meet you outside the arrivals hall with personalized name boards.
  • Night Bus Terminal Protocols: Long-distance sleeper routes typically terminate at Phuong Trang Ben Tre office terminal. Early morning arrivals (between 04:30 and 06:00) can leave luggage safely at hotel reception lobbies before daytime check-in opens.
  • Local Scam Prevention: Agree on a fixed return fare before booking motorized sampan excursions along narrow coconut palm canals.

Check-in Regulations & Guest Protocols (2026 Verification)

Vietnamese lodging operations follow specific administrative and security guidelines. Keeping these practical considerations in mind ensures effortless hotel check-ins:

  • Mandatory Police Guest Registration: Vietnamese law requires all licensed accommodation providers (from 5-star properties down to rural homestays) to register foreign guests on the local public security database within 24 hours of arrival. You must present an original valid passport and Vietnam entry visa (or eVisa confirmation document). Reception staff will photocopy or scan your documents and promptly return the physical passport.
  • Standard Check-in & Check-out Windows: Standard hotel check-in across Vietnam commences at 14:00, with check-out scheduled between 11:30 and 12:00. If your sleeper train or bus arrives at dawn, most hotels store backpacks in locked luggage rooms free of charge while you explore nearby morning noodle stalls. Guaranteed early check-in before 08:00 typically incurs a 50% day-rate surcharge.
  • Deposit Practices & Payment Options: Mid-range and luxury properties routinely request a temporary room key deposit of 500,000–1,000,000 VND ($20–$40 USD) in cash, or place an equivalent temporary hold on an international credit card (Visa, Mastercard). Cash deposits are returned in full during room inspection at check-out. Credit card pre-authorizations release back into your account within 7 to 14 banking days.
  • Keycard Electricity Controls & Room Etiquette: Guest rooms utilize electronic keycards for master circuit activation. When leaving your room, air conditioning compressors automatically shut down to comply with national green energy conservation standards. Tap water across Vietnam is non-potable; reputable hotels supply two complimentary 350ml glass bottles of purified water daily or feature filtered water refill dispensers in common corridors.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days should I stay in Ben Tre?

Two days and one night is ideal. This timing gives you an afternoon to cycle through coconut palm trails, an evening to savor delta cuisine, and a peaceful morning rowboat tour before transferring deeper south to Can Tho or returning to Saigon.

Is Ben Tre better to stay in than My Tho?

Yes. My Tho is heavily commercialized and dominated by large tour bus crowds heading to the four islands. Ben Tre sits on the quieter southern bank of the Tien River, preserving far more rural charm and authentic village hospitality.

How do I travel from Ho Chi Minh City to my Ben Tre lodge?

Take an air-conditioned Futa bus or Thinh Phat limousine van from western Saigon to Ben Tre for 110,000 to 160,000 VND ($4.40 – $6.40 USD). Travel time across the Trung Luong expressway is roughly 2 hours. Local taxis from the terminal to rural river lodges cost 80,000 to 120,000 VND ($3.20 – $4.80 USD).
