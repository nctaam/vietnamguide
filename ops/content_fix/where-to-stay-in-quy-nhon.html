Where to Stay in Quy Nhon (2026): Beach Areas & Resorts

Updated September 26, 2026

Figuring out where to stay in Quy Nhon is straightforward once you understand how this coastal capital in Binh Dinh province is laid out. Quy Nhon avoids the high-rise congestion of Nha Trang and Da Nang, offering three distinct lodging bases: the walkable city beach promenade along Xuan Dieu, the relaxed fishing cove of Bai Xep 12 kilometers south, and secluded private-bay luxury resorts tucked between rocky headlands.

The Concierge Verdict

For first-time travelers wanting evening food walks and central convenience, Quy Nhon City Center (specifically along Xuan Dieu and Nguyen Tat Thanh avenues) is the top choice: you have direct access to the urban beach, night markets, and legendary seafood alleys. If you crave bohemian coastal tranquility, secluded swimming coves, and sociable beachfront cafes, stay in Bai Xep village. For romantic honeymoons or total resort seclusion, choose the private coves of Ghenh Rang or Nhon Ly.

Neighborhood Base Comparison

1. City Beach (Xuan Dieu & An Duong Vuong): Best for First-Timers

Xuan Dieu Street curves around Quy Nhon's crescent-shaped bay, lined with landscaped coconut parks, public fitness gear, and breezy open-air cafes. Staying here puts you in the heartbeat of the city: stepping onto the sand at sunrise, sipping robust iced milk coffee (ca phe sua da) for 20,000 VND, and strolling to the Ngoc Han Cong Chua seafood alley after dark.

Properties here include reputable mid-range options like Anya Hotel Quy Nhon, Fleur De Lys Hotel, and Saigon Quy Nhon Hotel, with nightly rates ranging from 800,000 to 2,200,000 VND. Budget hotels and guest rooms along adjacent streets like Nguyen Hue cost 400,000 to 700,000 VND per night.

2. Bai Xep Village: Best for Bohemian Village Charm

Tucked 12 kilometers south of the city along Highway 1D, Bai Xep is a working coastal fishing community built around a sheltered granite cove framed by steep green hills and round bamboo basket boats (thuyen thung).

The cove gained international renown through pioneer beachfront homestays like Life's A Beach and Haven Vietnam. Here you can sleep in thatched-roof bungalows, oceanfront dorms, or cozy guesthouses (Mira Bãi Xếp) priced from 350,000 to 1,200,000 VND. Days pass slowly. You swim in calm waters, eat fresh banana pancakes, and chat with fellow travelers without a hint of highway traffic noise.

3. Ghenh Rang Private Coves: Best for Secluded Luxury

Between Quy Nhon City and Bai Xep lie steep coastal cliffs hiding private sandy coves. This is where Vietnam's top luxury coastal sanctuaries are situated, including Anantara Quy Nhon Villas and Avani Quy Nhon Resort.

Villas here feature private plunge pools, panoramic South China Sea views, cliffside spa pavilions, and private beach dining. Rates range from 3,500,000 VND at Avani to upwards of 12,000,000 VND for private pool villas at Anantara. It is an extraordinary sanctuary for honeymoons and milestone anniversaries.

4. Nhon Ly & Phuong Mai Peninsula: Best for Ky Co Staging & Golf

Located 25 kilometers northeast of the city across the impressive Thi Nai sea-crossing bridge, Nhon Ly sits on the rugged Phuong Mai peninsula near Eo Gio coastal path and the boat pier for Ky Co Beach.

The anchor property here is FLC Quy Nhon Beach & Golf Resort, a sprawling complex featuring international golf courses, immense swimming lagoon pools, and a dedicated safari park. Room rates range from 1,600,000 to 3,800,000 VND. This base is ideal for families and golf enthusiasts who prioritize resort recreation over city street life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it better to stay in Quy Nhon city or Bai Xep?

Choose the city center if you want diverse local street food, lively night walks, and easy transport access. Choose Bai Xep if you want a relaxed, laid-back beach vacation where you can walk straight from your bed into the ocean.

How do I get from Dieu Tri train station to my hotel?

Dieu Tri railway station is 10 km west of the city. A metered Mai Linh or Sun Taxi into Quy Nhon center costs approximately 180,000 to 220,000 VND ($8 to $10 USD) and takes 20 minutes.

What is the best month to stay in Quy Nhon?

March through August offers sunny skies, calm emerald waters, and gentle coastal breezes. Avoid October and November due to heavy seasonal rains and rough surf.
