Where to Stay in Can Tho (2026): Best Areas & Hotels

Updated September 26, 2026

Knowing where to stay in Can Tho makes all the difference when planning an early morning trip to the Mekong Delta's floating markets. As the economic and cultural capital of the delta, Can Tho offers two primary lodging experiences: urban convenience around the vibrant Ninh Kieu Wharf promenade, and peaceful riverside eco-resorts tucked along the palm-fringed canals of Cai Rang and Phong Dien.

The Concierge Verdict

For most first-time visitors and travelers staying 1 to 2 nights, Ninh Kieu Wharf (Ben Ninh Kieu) is the absolute best base. It offers direct walking access to wooden sampan boat docks for 5:30 AM Cai Rang departures, street food night markets, pedestrian footbridges, and riverboat dinner cruises. If you have 2 or more nights and want immersive tropical tranquility among fruit orchards and canal waterways, book an eco-lodge or boutique resort south in Cai Rang or Phong Dien.

Can Tho Base Comparison

1. Ninh Kieu Wharf (Ben Ninh Kieu): Best for First-Time Visitors

Ben Ninh Kieu sits at the junction of the Can Tho and Hau (Bassac) rivers. This highly central location is unbeatable. The landscaped riverside park serves as the focal point of city life, featuring the Ho Chi Minh statue, colorful flower gardens, and the pedestrian bridge leading to Cai Khe.

Staying here means you can roll out of bed at 5:15 AM and walk straight onto your pre-booked wooden boat for Cai Rang floating market without dealing with morning taxis. In the evening, the area comes alive with the Ninh Kieu Night Market, street barbecue stalls, and glowing riverboats.

Recommended properties include TTC Hotel Premium Can Tho, Kim Tho Hotel, and Hau Giang Hotel, with prices between 700,000 and 1,800,000 VND per night.

2. Cai Rang & Phong Dien Canals: Best for Eco-Lodges & Orchards

Heading 6 to 15 kilometers southwest into the countryside brings you to the lush agricultural waterways of Cai Rang and Phong Dien. Here, small canals (rach) are bordered by water coconut palms, banana groves, and mangosteen orchards.

Accommodations in this area consist of charming rustic homestays and riverside eco-resorts such as Can Tho Ecolodge, Mekong Rustic Can Tho, and Nguyen Shack. Rooms cost between 600,000 and 2,000,000 VND. Guests enjoy complimentary bicycles for exploring narrow canal paths, cooking classes, and waking to the calls of rooster and morning birdlife.

3. Cai Khe Islet: Best for Upscale Riverfront Comfort

Connected to central Ninh Kieu by bridges, Cai Khe Islet is a greener, more modern urban sector home to several prominent international and domestic hotel chains.

The standout colonial-style property here is Victoria Can Tho Resort, located right on the banks of the Hau River with manicured French colonial gardens and a historic river cruiser. Other modern options include Muong Thanh Luxury Can Tho. Nightly rates range from 1,100,000 to 3,500,000 VND, offering quiet luxury while remaining just a 5-minute taxi ride from the main wharf.

4. Con Au (Au Islet): Best for Ultra-Luxury Island Wellness

For travelers seeking five-star wellness seclusion, Con Au is a private river island accessible only by boat. Here lies Azerai Can Tho, a tranquil minimalist resort designed with refined Vietnamese architecture, secluded banyan trees, an exceptional holistic spa, and private pool villas.

Rates average 6,500,000 to 14,000,000 VND per night. It offers private sunrise boat charters to Cai Rang, guided bicycle tours through fruit orchards, and world-class riverbank dining.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time should I leave my hotel for Cai Rang floating market?

Aim to board your boat between 5:30 AM and 6:00 AM. This ensures you arrive at the floating market right as wholesale trading peaks under the cool morning sunrise, well before tourist tour groups arrive at 7:30 AM.

Is 1 night in Can Tho enough?

One night is sufficient to see the night market and experience the morning floating market. Two nights allow you to explore rural canal cycling routes, cocoa farms, and ancient houses like Binh Thuy without rushing.

How much is a private boat to the floating market?

A private wooden sampan boat for 2 to 4 people hired directly at Ninh Kieu Wharf costs between 350,000 and 500,000 VND ($15 to $22 USD) for a 3-hour morning tour.
