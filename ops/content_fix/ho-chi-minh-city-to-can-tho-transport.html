Ho Chi Minh City to Can Tho: Limousine, Bus & Car

Updated September 26, 2026

Traveling from Ho Chi Minh City to Can Tho connects Vietnam's bustling southern metropolis with the economic and cultural heart of the Mekong Delta. Covering approximately 170 kilometers southwest along the Trung Luong - My Thuan expressway, this overland journey has become faster and smoother than ever, allowing travelers to reach Cai Rang floating market, lush fruit orchards, and riverfront canals in just 3 to 3.5 hours.

The Concierge Verdict

For comfort, speed, and point-to-point convenience, 9-seat DCar limousine vans (such as Vu Linh or Tri Nhan) represent the best overall choice. Costing 200,000 to 250,000 VND per seat, they include hotel pickup in District 1 and drop you directly at your Can Tho hotel. If departing from western Saigon, scheduled sleeper or seated buses from Mien Tay Bus Station operated by Futa (Phuong Trang) depart every 30 minutes for 165,000 VND.

Transport Options Comparison: HCMC to Can Tho

1. Luxury Limousine Vans: The Smartest Choice

Modern 9-seat and 11-seat DCar limousine vans offer plush leather captain chairs, USB charging ports, onboard Wi-Fi, and spacious legroom. Reputable delta operators like Vu Linh Limousine, Tri Nhan, and Loc Phat run departures hourly throughout the day.

Ticket prices range from 200,000 to 260,000 VND ($8 to $11 USD). The greatest benefit of limousine services is door-to-door transit: operators typically collect passengers from central District 1 and District 3 hotels or office hubs, transit the Trung Luong - My Thuan expressway, and deliver you directly to your accommodation in downtown Ninh Kieu district or riverside resorts in Can Tho.

2. Scheduled Express Buses (Phuong Trang / Futa Bus Lines)

Phuong Trang (Futa Bus Lines) is southern Vietnam's premier intercity coach company, operating clean, air-conditioned sleeper buses with bright orange liveries. Buses depart from Ben Xe Mien Tay (Western Bus Station in Binh Tan District) every 30 to 45 minutes around the clock.

Tickets cost 165,000 VND per passenger and include a bottle of drinking water and a wet towel. You can book tickets online via the Futa mobile app or at the station ticket windows. Once your bus arrives at Can Tho Central Bus Station (located 5 km south of the city center), Futa provides free shared shuttle vans (xe trung chuyen) that take you to any hotel in central Can Tho.

3. Private Car Transfers for Families & Groups

Hiring a private car with professional driver is the most stress-free option for small travel parties, families with luggage, or travelers departing directly from Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN). A comfortable 4-seat sedan costs around 1,800,000 VND, while a 7-seat SUV (Toyota Fortuner/Innova) runs 2,100,000 to 2,400,000 VND each way.

The journey takes just 3 hours via the expressway. A private car also affords the flexibility to stop along the way for coffee at rest stops in Tien Giang or make a short detour to the historic Vinh Trang Pagoda in My Tho.

4. Navigating the Trung Luong - My Thuan Expressway Route

The completion of the My Thuan 2 cable-stayed bridge and the Trung Luong - My Thuan expressway network has eliminated the notorious traffic bottlenecks that once choked National Highway 1A. Motor vehicles bypass provincial towns entirely, cruising at 80 to 90 km/h across the flat rice floodplains of Long An, Tien Giang, and Vinh Long provinces before crossing the Hau River into Can Tho.

Note that two-wheeled motorbikes and scooters are strictly prohibited from using the expressway. Motorcyclists must follow the older National Route QL1A, which features heavy truck traffic, frequent traffic lights, and requires 4.5 to 5 hours of riding time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book tickets to Can Tho?

During standard weekdays, booking 1 to 2 days in advance is sufficient. However, for travel during weekends, Lunar New Year (Tet), or national holidays (April 30 / May 1), reserve limousine seats at least one to two weeks ahead.

Can I visit Can Tho as a day trip from Ho Chi Minh City?

While physically possible with a 3-hour each-way drive, day trips miss Can Tho's primary attraction: the Cai Rang floating market, which peaks in trading activity between 5:30 AM and 7:30 AM. Staying overnight is strongly recommended.

Is there a train from Ho Chi Minh City to Can Tho?

No railway currently connects Ho Chi Minh City to Can Tho or the Mekong Delta. A high-speed railway line is in planning stages, but all present travel relies on expressways and road transport.
