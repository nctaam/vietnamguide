Where to Stay in Rach Gia (2026): Ferry Port & City Stays

Updated September 26, 2026

Deciding where to stay in Rach Gia is a crucial decision for travelers navigating southern Vietnam's maritime highways. As the coastal capital of Kien Giang province on the Gulf of Thailand, Rach Gia functions as the primary mainland departure harbor for high-speed hydrofoils and fast catamarans heading to Phu Quoc and Nam Du archipelago. Because most passenger ferries depart early in the morning between 07:00 and 08:30, selecting an accommodation base close to the ferry piers guarantees a stress-free boarding experience without pre-dawn taxi scrambles.

The Concierge Verdict

If your primary objective is boarding an early morning fast ferry to Phu Quoc or Nam Du, book a comfortable transit hotel within 300 to 600 meters of Ben Tau Rach Gia ferry terminal (such as Kiet Hong Hotel or Kim Co Hotel) for 350,000 to 650,000 VND ($14 – $26 USD) per night. For travelers seeking full-service amenities, swimming pools, and waterfront dining during a longer provincial stopover, choose the 4-star Sai Gon Rach Gia Hotel in the central district for 1,100,000 to 1,600,000 VND ($44 – $64 USD).

Rach Gia Accommodation Neighborhood Comparison

District 1: Ferry Pier District (Ben Tau Rach Gia)

The ferry pier cluster surrounds Nguyen Cong Tru, Tu Duc, and Bach Dang streets in Vinh Thanh ward. This neighborhood is designed around maritime departures. Staying here means you can roll out of bed at 06:30, walk 5 minutes straight to the Superdong or Phu Quoc Express ticket gates, and comfortably clear ticket inspection and security before the 07:15 or 08:00 morning sailings.

Notable lodging options include:

  • Kiet Hong Hotel: 400,000 to 600,000 VND ($16 – $24 USD) per night. Located just 250 meters from the pier entrance, featuring elevator access, strong air conditioning, and helpful front desk staff who verify live ferry departure schedules.
  • Kim Co Hotel: 350,000 to 500,000 VND ($14 – $20 USD) per night. Offers clean private en-suite rooms, hot rain showers, and luggage storage for returning travelers.
  • Thien Hai Hotel: 450,000 to 650,000 VND ($18 – $26 USD) per night. Positioned along Nguyen Cong Tru, providing spacious family rooms and scooter parking.

District 2: Central City & Lac Hong Riverfront

Located 2 to 3 kilometers east of the ferry terminal along Tran Phu and Lac Hong boulevards, central Rach Gia borders the Cai Lon river mouth and Kien Giang provincial administrative offices. This area features the atmospheric Rach Gia Central Market (Cho Rach Gia), where vendors sell tropical fruits and seafood caught overnight in the Gulf.

The standout establishment is Sai Gon Rach Gia Hotel (1,100,000 to 1,600,000 VND / $44 – $64 USD per night), a polished 4-star property featuring landscaped tropical gardens, an outdoor swimming pool, fitness center, international buffet breakfast, and complimentary shuttle transfers to the ferry terminal or Rach Gia Airport (VKG).

District 3: Hoa Bien Land Reclamation Zone (Khu Lan Bien)

Stretching southwest along Ton Duc Thang Street into the Gulf of Thailand, the Hoa Bien district is one of Vietnam's largest urban marine reclamation projects. It boasts wide coastal promenades, modern coffee houses (such as Trung Nguyen Legend and Highlands Coffee), and seafood beer gardens overlooking fishing flotillas moored in the bay.

Modern boutique hotels here, such as Phu Cuong Hotel (750,000 to 1,100,000 VND / $30 – $44 USD), offer ocean-view balconies, quiet residential surroundings, and easy access to sunset viewpoints. A taxi ride from the Hoa Bien district to the passenger ferry pier takes roughly 8 to 10 minutes (45,000 to 65,000 VND / $1.80 – $2.60 USD).

Local Culinary Highlight: Bun Ca Rach Gia

Staying overnight in Rach Gia provides the perfect opportunity to savor the city's signature culinary creation: Bun Ca Rach Gia (Kien Giang fish noodle soup). Unlike central Vietnamese versions that use fried fish cakes, Rach Gia bun ca features delicate fillets of steamed snakehead fish, plump river prawns simmered in pork fat, and a fragrant, golden broth flavored with wild ginger (ngai bun) and turmeric. A generous steaming bowl at street stalls along Mac Cuu or Ham Nghi costs between 35,000 and 45,000 VND ($1.40 – $1.80 USD).

Onward Ferry Logistics & Travel Advice

Before checking out of your hotel, keep these maritime travel rules in mind:

  • Port Check-In Requirements: Arrive at Ben Tau Rach Gia at least 30 to 45 minutes prior to scheduled departure. Foreign passengers must present their original physical passport; photocopies or smartphone photos are not accepted at the boarding gangway.
  • Rough Sea Conditions: During the southwest monsoon season (June to October), waves in the Gulf of Thailand can create rolling swells. If you are prone to seasickness, purchase motion sickness pills (10,000 VND / $0.40 USD) from local pharmacies near the pier.
  • Motorbike Transport: If you wish to bring a scooter to Phu Quoc, book a ticket on the Thanh Thoi roll-on roll-off car ferry departing from Thanh Thoi pier (8 kilometers north of the city) rather than the passenger high-speed boat.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I walk from ferry pier hotels to the boat departure gates?

Yes. Hotels along Nguyen Cong Tru, Tu Duc, and Bach Dang sit within 200 to 500 meters of Ben Tau Rach Gia, allowing you to walk to the boarding gate in under 5 minutes with rolling luggage.

Do hotels in Rach Gia offer luggage storage while I visit Phu Quoc or Nam Du?

Most reputable transit hotels in Rach Gia (including Kiet Hong and Kim Co) offer secure luggage storage for 30,000 to 50,000 VND ($1.20 – $2.00 USD) per day if you return to stay overnight on your return trip.

How far is Rach Gia Airport (VKG) from the ferry pier?

Rach Gia Airport is located 9 kilometers southeast of the passenger ferry pier. A metered taxi transfer takes approximately 15 to 20 minutes and costs 120,000 to 160,000 VND ($4.80 – $6.40 USD).
