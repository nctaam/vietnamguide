Da Nang vs Hoi An: Where should you actually sleep?

DA NANG

Best for 500Mbps fiber workspaces, sprawling My Khe beach mornings, and modern craft breweries. Clean grid roads, near zero tourist hustling.

HOI AN

Best for lantern-lit quiet dawns, heritage wooden shophouses, and bespoke linen tailoring. Crowded after 17:00; stay in An Bang beach or Cam Pho perimeter.
