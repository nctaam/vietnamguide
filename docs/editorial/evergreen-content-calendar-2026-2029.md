# VietnamGuide 36-Month Evergreen Publication Calendar

**Purpose:** Turn VietnamGuide into an English-first, evidence-led Vietnam travel planning authority for international visitors.

**Timeframe:** July 2026 through June 2029.

**Operating model:** Evidence-led Concierge. Every public guide must help a traveler make one real decision, show original judgment, include a source trail, record update discipline, and point to the next best route page.

**Important scheduling rule:** Do not create empty future-scheduled WordPress pages. A page may only be scheduled in WordPress after the full guide, images, metadata, source trail, update log, internal links, and verification checks are ready.

## Publication Cadence

### Monthly Rhythm

- **Guide A:** publish during week 2, Tuesday 09:00 Asia/Saigon.
- **Guide B:** publish during week 4, Tuesday 09:00 Asia/Saigon.
- **Refresh slot:** publish or verify during the final Friday of the month, 15:00 Asia/Saigon.
- **Internal-link pass:** update hubs, homepage shelves, related routes, and sitemap after every guide.
- **Quarterly audit:** first Friday of January, April, July, and October.

### Weekly Workflow

- **Monday:** source checks, image/license checks, outline lock.
- **Tuesday:** draft/build the guide in the WordPress block pattern system.
- **Wednesday:** editorial pass, EEAT pass, internal-link pass.
- **Thursday:** public QA, metadata QA, image liveness, sitemap/cache readiness.
- **Friday:** schedule/publish, verify live page, log evidence.

### Page Statuses

Use these statuses in the editorial board:

1. `Brief`
2. `Source checked`
3. `Drafted`
4. `WP built`
5. `EEAT verified`
6. `Scheduled`
7. `Published`
8. `Refresh due`
9. `Prune / merge candidate`

## Cluster Rules

### Planning and Logistics

Every logistics page must include:

- short answer first
- official/source trail
- live-check list
- what can change
- what to verify before booking
- related links to itinerary, cost, transport, visa, and best-time pages

### Destination Pages

Every destination page must include:

- best for / not ideal for
- how many days
- best time
- where to stay at area level
- getting there
- what to skip first
- proof images
- internal links to the relevant itinerary and comparison pages

### Comparison Pages

Every comparison page must include:

- direct verdict above the fold
- decision matrix
- traveler-fit rows
- skip logic
- route consequences
- cost/time impact where useful
- links to both destination pages and the planning spine

### Monetization-Safe Pages

Do not publish commercial pages until the supporting informational cluster is live.

Commercial pages must include:

- methodology
- exclusion criteria
- who should not buy
- update log
- source trail
- affiliate disclosure where relevant
- no fake review language

---

## Image Policy

- Use real destination photography for place proof.
- Use Wikimedia, official tourism sources, original photography, or properly licensed assets.
- AI images may be used only for diagrams, abstract route visuals, packing illustrations, or clearly labeled supporting graphics.
- AI images must never pretend to be proof of a real destination, hotel, food stall, cruise, or route condition.
- Every image used for proof must have a visible credit trail or owned-source record.

---

## Internal Link Operating Rules

Every new guide must link to:

- one hub page
- one planning spine page
- one itinerary page when route length matters
- one regional/destination comparison when location choice matters
- one practical logistics page when cost, transport, visa, money, SIM, safety, or insurance changes the decision

After publishing each guide:

1. Add it to the relevant hub.
2. Add it to the homepage reviewed-guide shelf only if it is a strategic route page.
3. Add it to at least two related-route panels.
4. Refresh sitemap/cache.
5. Record live QA in `ops/verification-log.md`.

## Pre-Publish Gate

No guide can be scheduled or published unless all checks are true:

- Page has a single primary decision.
- Hero is photo-led or otherwise visually useful.
- Verdict appears near the top.
- Source trail is visible.
- Update log is visible.
- Related routes are visible.
- At least three strategic internal links work.
- Images return HTTP `200`.
- Metadata title and description are written for English search intent.
- No unresolved placeholders remain.
- Sitemap/cache can be refreshed after publish.
- Publication is recorded in `ops/verification-log.md`.
