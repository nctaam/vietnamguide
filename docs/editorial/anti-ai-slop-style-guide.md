# VietnamGuide Anti-AI Slop Style Guide & Quality Constitution

**Document Code:** VG-EDITORIAL-AI-SLOP-01  
**Version:** 1.0.0 (2026-09-12)  
**Authority:** VietnamGuide Editorial Board  
**Target:** All writers, editors, agentic assistants, and verification tools producing or reviewing content for `vietnamguide.net`.

---

## 1. Executive Manifesto

Travelers do not read VietnamGuide to be entertained by synthetic cheerleading. They read VietnamGuide because they are about to commit thousands of dollars, weeks of personal leave, and their physical safety to a foreign country with distinct languages, legal systems, and transit logistics.

**AI Slop** is defined as any text generated by generic language models that prioritizes grammatical fluency and enthusiastic tone over verifiable ground truth, practical constraints, and genuine human judgment.

On VietnamGuide:
- **No empty adjectives:** If a temple is worth visiting, describe its architectural era, preservation state, entry fee, crowd timing, and walking terrain—never call it "breathtaking", "mesmerizing", or "picturesque".
- **Constraints before scenery:** A traveler cannot enjoy Ha Long Bay if their boat operator is unlicensed or if sea swells cancel the sailing. State the weather window, transfer time, and cancellation policy first.
- **Evidence over assertion:** Never say "take a taxi or bus". Specify the terminal name, official bus line number, ticket counter location, realistic VND fare, and whether Grab/Be works at that specific stop.

## 2. The Banned Travel Clichés Blacklist (150+ Forbidden Patterns)

Any article containing an instance of a **Tier 1 (Critical)** cliché will fail the publication gate and cannot be published or updated.

### Tier 1: Critical Violations (Instant Rejection)

#### A. Formulaic LLM Intros & Openers
- `Nestled in the heart of...`
- `Whether you're a foodie, history buff, or thrill-seeker...`
- `Look no further than...`
- `In this comprehensive guide, we will delve into...`
- `Without further ado...`
- `A testament to [anything]...`
- `A rich tapestry of [history/culture/tradition]...`
- `Bustling metropolis meets ancient charm...`
- `Steeped in history...`
- `A land of contrasts...`

#### B. Fluff Superlatives & Tourist Buzzwords
- `Hidden gem` (Unless literally hidden underground, e.g. Tu Lan Cave)
- `Must-visit destination` / `Must-see attraction`
- `Breathtaking views` / `Breathtaking scenery`
- `Mesmerizing beauty` / `Enchanting atmosphere`
- `Picturesque landscape` / `Postcard-perfect`
- `Unforgettable journey` / `Memory of a lifetime`
- `Off the beaten path` (Used generically for places with 500,000 annual tourists)
- `Melting pot of...`
- `Kaleidoscope of colors/experiences...`
- `An oasis of tranquility...`

#### C. Fake Sensory Cheerleading
- `A symphony of flavors...`
- `Tantalize your taste buds...`
- `Feast for the senses / Feast for the eyes...`
- `Sensory overload in the best way possible...`
- `Culinary adventure...`

#### D. Robotic Summaries & Sign-offs
- `In conclusion,...`
- `All in all,...`
- `To wrap things up,...`
- `So pack your bags and get ready for...`
- `Vietnam has something for everyone...`
- `Hope this guide helps you plan your dream trip...`
- `Happy travels!` / `Safe travels!`

## 3. The "Constraint-First" Content Formula

Every guide on VietnamGuide must follow the **Concierge Decision Architecture**:

```
[1. The Core Traveler Decision]
              │
              ▼
[2. Physical & Legal Constraints] (Visa, Season, Hours, Elevation, Cost)
              │
              ▼
[3. Trade-off Friction] (Time vs Money vs Comfort vs Weather Risk)
              │
              ▼
[4. Ground-Truth Data Matrix] (VND Prices, Terminal Names, Timetables)
              │
              ▼
[5. Unambiguous Concierge Verdict] ("Who should do this" vs "Who should skip")
```

### The First-Screen Rule
The first 150 words of every article must deliver an actionable verdict. Never open with a historical summary of when the city was founded. Open with what must be true for the traveler's visit to succeed.

*Example (Approved):*
> "Hanoi rarely fails because a traveler misses one famous stop. It fails when the first base, first night, day-trip order, weather plan, and northern route pressure are decided in the wrong sequence. If you have under 48 hours, base in the Old Quarter within 600 meters of Hoan Kiem Lake, book a single staged day trip to Ninh Binh, and skip Ha Long Bay entirely to avoid spending eight hours in highway transit."

## 4. Quantitative Human-Likeness Metrics (HLS Engine)

Automated linting tools (`ops/anti-ai-slop-linter.py`) evaluate content against 4 mathematical thresholds:

### Metric 1: Cliché Density ($CD$)
$$CD = \frac{\text{Tier 1 Clichés} \times 10 + \text{Tier 2 Warnings} \times 2}{\text{Word Count}} \times 1000$$
- **Target:** $CD = 0.00$ (Zero Tier 1 clichés permitted).

### Metric 2: Sentence Length Coefficient of Variation ($CV$)
AI text has remarkably uniform sentence lengths (typically 16–20 words per sentence, low standard deviation). Human writing displays high cadence variation:
$$CV = \frac{\sigma}{\mu} = \frac{\text{Standard Deviation of Sentence Length}}{\text{Mean Sentence Length}}$$
- **Required Threshold:** $CV \ge 0.45$.
- **Rhythm Rule:** Alternate between punchy assertions (4–8 words) and analytical context clauses (22–32 words).

### Metric 3: Evidence Anchor Density ($EAD$)
Every guide must contain verifiable anchor tokens:
- At least 5 explicit currency figures (`VND`, `₫`, or `$ USD`).
- At least 3 transit/time metrics (`hours`, `minutes`, `km`, `daily departures`).
- At least 2 specific local infrastructure entities (named terminals, gate codes, official decrees).

### Metric 4: Human-Likeness Score ($HLS$)
Combined score out of 100 calculated by the linter:
- **Pass Threshold:** $HLS \ge 80 / 100$.

## 5. Ground-Truth Reference Data for Vietnam Travel

Writers and automated checkers must anchor statements to these verified real-world operational facts:

### 1. Immigration & Visas (2026/2027 Standards)
- **Resolution 128/NQ-CP:** Unilateral visa exemption granted for **45 days** (UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Japan, South Korea, Russia, Belarus, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland). The former requirement to wait 30 days between visits has been completely abolished.
- **Resolution 127/NQ-CP:** 90-day E-visa (single or multiple entry) open to **all countries and territories**. Official fee: **$25 USD (Single)** / **$50 USD (Multiple)** via `evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn`.
- **Phu Quoc SEZ:** 30-day visa exemption applies to **all foreign passport holders** arriving directly by international flight or sealed domestic transit.
- **Chinese E-Passports:** Issued loose-leaf visa sticker (visa rời) at border control; requires $0 extra fee but an additional passport photo.
- **Passport Rule:** Must have $\ge 6$ months (183 days) validity from the date of arrival and at least 2 blank visa pages.

### 2. Urban Transit & Airport Connectivity
- **Hanoi (Noi Bai - HAN):**
  - GrabCar to Old Quarter: 250,000–320,000 VND (~$10–13 USD) including toll.
  - Express Bus 86: 45,000 VND (~$1.80 USD), departs every 45 mins from Terminal 2 Pillar 2.
  - Scam alert: Uniformed solicitors offering "metered rides" inside arrival hall often charge 600,000–800,000 VND. Ignore and walk to Grab pickup bay or official taxi stand.
- **Ho Chi Minh City (Tan Son Nhat - SGN):**
  - GrabCar pickup is strictly at the multi-story TCP Parking Garage (Floors 3–5), NOT outside the terminal curb.
  - Yellow Cab / Vinasun (red & white, phone `38.27.27.27`) and Mai Linh (green, phone `38.38.38.38`) are the only authorized metered street fleets. Counterfeits use `Mei Linh` or `Vinasum`.
- **Da Nang (DAD):**
  - Airport is only 4 km from city center and 7 km from My Khe Beach. GrabCar fare: 80,000–120,000 VND.

### 3. Railway Infrastructure
- **Hanoi Central Station:**
  - **Ga A (120 Le Duan):** Departure point for Reunification Express trains heading south (Ninh Binh, Hue, Da Nang, Saigon).
  - **Ga B (1 Tran Quy Cap):** Departure point for northern lines to Lao Cai (Sapa) and Hai Phong. Located on the opposite side of the tracks; a taxi driver must know which gate you need.
- **Ticketing:** Official state railway portal is `dsvn.vn`. E-tickets on smartphones with QR codes are accepted directly at gates.

### 4. Climate Microclimates
- **North (Hanoi, Ninh Binh, Ha Long):**
  - Winter/Spring (Jan–Mar): High humidity (85–95%), persistent grey overcast, drizzle, and "nồm" (sweating walls and tile floors). Temperatures 14–19°C.
  - Autumn (Sep–Nov): Clearest skies, pleasant 22–28°C, lowest rainfall.
- **Central (Hue, Da Nang, Hoi An):**
  - Rainy & Typhoon Season (Sep–Dec): Peak rainfall in Oct–Nov. Hoi An Ancient Town regularly floods when the Thu Bon River overflows.
  - Dry Season (Feb–Aug): Warm, sunny, peaking at 35–38°C in June–July.
- **South (HCMC, Mekong, Phu Quoc):**
  - Dry Season (Nov–Apr): Consistent sunshine, 28–34°C.
  - Wet Season (May–Oct): Heavy 45-minute afternoon downpours, rarely raining all day. Streets in low-lying HCMC districts experience tidal flooding (*triều cường*).

## 6. Verification Checklist Before Marking Content Complete

Every article editor must run this checklist:

- [ ] Zero instances of Tier 1 Banned Clichés.
- [ ] No generic opening sentences about "land of contrasts" or "rich history".
- [ ] First screen provides a concrete verdict or route trade-off.
- [ ] At least 5 specific currency figures (VND) with realistic 2026/2027 rates.
- [ ] Specific airport, bus terminal, or train station names identified for transit steps.
- [ ] Clear warning for known local frictions or scam traps.
- [ ] At least one comparison table, decision matrix, or packing checklist included.
- [ ] Sentence cadence has punchy rhythm ($CV \ge 0.45$).
- [ ] Linter score $\ge 80 / 100$.
