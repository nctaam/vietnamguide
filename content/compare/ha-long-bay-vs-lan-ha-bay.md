# Ha Long Bay vs Lan Ha Bay: Cruise Routes, Costs, and Port Selection

Choosing an overnight cruise in northern Vietnam comes down to an administrative and geographic boundary: whether to sail through the core waters of [Hạ Long Bay](/destinations/ha-long-bay-travel-guide) under Quảng Ninh Province jurisdiction, or navigate the southern limestone archipelago of **Lan Hạ Bay** off [Cát Bà Island](/destinations/cat-ba-travel-guide) under Hải Phòng municipality management. In September 2023, UNESCO formally united both bodies of water under the transboundary *Ha Long Bay – Cat Ba Archipelago* World Heritage designation, recognizing that both bays share the exact same 500-million-year-old drowned tower karst geology rising 80 meters to 200 meters out of the Gulf of Tonkin.

Yet on the water, provincial maritime regulations create two distinct travel experiences. Quảng Ninh Port Authority and Hải Phòng Port Authority enforce separate vessel licensing, sightseeing fee structures, anchorage zones, and open-water swimming rules. Ships licensed exclusively for Hạ Long Bay Route 1 or Route 2 cannot cross south into Lan Hạ lagoons, while Cát Bà vessels departing Bến Bèo Pier stay inside Hải Phòng waters. Understanding port transfers along the `CT04` and `CT06` expressways, fleet density, cave access, and 2026 cruise tariffs ensures you book the bay that fits your itinerary.

## Quick Verdict: Classic Quang Ninh Landmarks vs Secluded Cat Ba Coves

- **Choose Hạ Long Bay (Quảng Ninh Core Zone)** if this is your first visit to Vietnam and you want to walk inside colossal dry grottoes like Sửng Sốt Cave (10,000 m²), climb the 400 stone steps of Ti Tốp Island for a 360-degree panorama, or minimize road transit from Hanoi (135 km to 150 km in 2 hours 15 minutes via `CT04` and `CT06` to Bến Tuần Châu or Bãi Cháy). It is also the stronger pick for travelers with limited mobility who prefer concrete pier walkways over kayak-only lagoons.
- **Choose Lan Hạ Bay (Cát Bà Archipelago)** if you want 70% lower boat density at overnight anchorages, prioritize active outdoor excursions—paddling kayaks through the 70-meter tidal tunnel of Dark & Bright Cave (*Hang Sáng Tối*), swimming off the stern platform in quiet coves like Ba Trái Đào, and cycling 5 km through rainforested Việt Hải Village—or plan to base yourself onshore on [Cát Bà Island](/destinations/cat-ba-travel-guide) for 1,100,000 VND ($44.00 USD) full-day boat trips.
- **What About Bái Tử Long Bay?** Travelers seeking a third option northeast of Hòn Gai can review our [Bai Tu Long Bay Guide](/destinations/bai-tu-long-bay-guide), where fewer than 25 licensed overnight vessels sail toward Thiên Cảnh Sơn Cave and Vung Viêng Floating Village.

### 1. Hạ Long Bay Core Route 2: Monumental Dry Caves and High Traffic
Roughly 75% of overnight ships departing Bến Tuần Châu and Bến cảng tàu khách quốc tế Hạ Long (Sun Group Port on Đường Hạ Long, Bãi Cháy) sail **Route 2**. Between 14:00 and 17:00, 35 to 60 tenders queue along the concrete jetty of Bo Hòn Island so passengers can walk the 500-meter stone path through **Sửng Sốt Cave**, whose three chambers rise 30 meters high. Nearby, **Ti Tốp Island** packs hundreds of swimmers onto its 250-meter artificial crescent beach while hikers climb 400 steps to the 110-meter summit pavilion. At **Hang Luồn** (Luon Cave), passengers board 20-minute bamboo rowboats (50,000 VND / $2.00 USD) or kayaks through a 60-meter arch into a circular cliff lagoon where golden macaques forage at low tide.

### 2. Lan Hạ Bay: Tidal Lagoons, White-Sand Coves, and Rainforest Trails
Because Lan Hạ Bay packs 400 vegetated karst pinnacles into just 70 km² along the eastern flank of Cát Bà Island, the waterways are narrower, calmer, and ringed by over 130 small white-sand tidal beaches. Instead of dry concrete caves, Lan Hạ itineraries focus on water-level and forest exploration:
- **Dark & Bright Cave (*Hang Sáng Tối*)**: A 70-meter-long water tunnel where ceiling stalactites hang 1.5 meters above slack tide, opening into a silent, cliff-walled marine lake accessible only by 2-person kayak or bamboo sampan.
- **Ba Trái Đào (Three Peaches Islets) & Trà Báu**: Sheltered emerald coves where ships drop anchor in 4 meters to 6 meters of clear water, allowing guests to jump off hydraulic stern platforms, swim to natural sandbars, or paddle stand-up paddleboards (SUP).
- **Việt Hải Ancient Village & Cái Bèo Floating Village**: Cruisers disembark at Việt Hải pier and pedal bicycles 5 km (or ride a 15-minute electric buggy) through a limestone cave tunnel into a rainforest valley inside Cát Bà National Park. Day boats departing Bến Bèo also glide through **Cái Bèo**, a 7,000-year-old floating fishing settlement of 400 moored wooden households.

## Step-by-Step Logistics: CT04/CT06 Expressways, Ports, and Ferry Crossings

Coordinating your [Hanoi to Ha Long Bay transport](/plan/hanoi-to-ha-long-bay-transport) or [Hanoi to Cat Ba Island transport](/plan/hanoi-to-cat-ba-island-transport) requires matching your booking to the exact departure pier before 11:30 embarkation:

1. **Step 1: Verify Your Exact Embarkation Pier (72 Hours Before Sailing)**:
   - Check your voucher to confirm whether your ship boards at **Bến cảng quốc tế Tuần Châu** (Tuần Châu Ward, 12 km west of Bãi Cháy), **Bến cảng tàu khách quốc tế Hạ Long** (Sun Group Port at 09 Đường Hạ Long, Bãi Cháy), or **Bến Bèo Pier** (Đường 1/4, Cát Bà Town). Arriving at the wrong Quảng Ninh port costs 20 minutes and 220,000 VND ($8.80 USD) in a last-minute taxi across the Tuần Châu causeway.
2. **Step 2: Book the Morning Hanoi Highway Transfer (`CT04` & `CT06`)**:
   - **To Tuần Châu or Bãi Cháy Ports (for Hạ Long Bay & Luxury Lan Hạ Cruises)**: Shared 9-to-11-seat limousine vans pick up in Hanoi's Old Quarter between 08:00 and 08:45, travel 105 km east on the Hanoi–Hải Phòng Expressway (`CT04`) at 120 km/h, cross the 5.4-kilometer Bạch Đằng Bridge onto the 25 km Hải Phòng–Hạ Long Expressway (`CT06`), and reach Tuần Châu Marina between 11:00 and 11:30 (2 hours 15 minutes to 2 hours 30 minutes). One-way limousine seats cost 220,000 VND to 280,000 VND ($8.80 to $11.20 USD); private 4-seat sedans cost 1,350,000 VND to 1,600,000 VND ($54.00 to $64.00 USD). Note that 5-star Lan Hạ steel ships (such as Stellar of the Seas, Mon Cheri, Orchid, and Capella) check in at **Tuần Châu Lot 16 to Lot 32** and transfer guests south into Lan Hạ Bay via a 25-minute high-speed tender boat.
3. **Step 3: Cross to Cát Bà Island via Đồng Bài Ferry or Sun World Cable Car (for Bến Bèo Departures)**:
   - If you are taking a Cát Bà-based day boat or boutique junk from **Bến Bèo Pier**, take a combo bus (Cat Ba Express, Good Morning Cat Ba, or Daiichi Travel, 320,000 VND to 380,000 VND / $12.80 to $15.20 USD one-way, 3 hours 30 minutes total) from Hanoi down `CT04` and across the 5.44 km Tân Vũ – Lạch Huyện sea bridge to **Bến phà Đồng Bài** (which replaced Bến Gót in March 2024). From Đồng Bài, cross the 2.2 km channel by ferry (25 minutes, 20,000 VND / $0.80 USD per foot passenger) or speedometer speedboat (8 minutes) to Cái Viềng Pier, then ride 22 km (35 minutes) down Đường Xuyên Đảo to Cát Bà Town. To skip summer weekend ferry queues completely, ride the **3,955-meter Sun World Cát Bà Cable Car** from Cát Hải Station to Phù Long Station in 10 minutes for 100,000 VND to 150,000 VND ($4.00 to $6.00 USD) each way.
4. **Step 4: Connect Directly to or from Ninh Bình (`QL10` Corridor)**:
   - Do not backtrack through Hanoi if pairing a bay cruise with Tràng An. Book a direct 190 km [Ninh Binh to Ha Long Bay transfer](/plan/ninh-binh-to-ha-long-bay-transfer) along `QL10` via Nam Định, Thái Bình, and Hải Phòng (3 hours 30 minutes, departing Tam Cốc at 07:15 to reach Tuần Châu by 10:45 for 280,000 VND to 350,000 VND / $11.20 to $14.00 USD).

## On-the-Ground Pro-Tips: Swimming Rules, Typhoon Bans, and Cabin Checks

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Plastic Ban at Quảng Ninh Ports and Typhoon Sailing Halt Rules (*Lệnh Cấm Biển*)**: Bến Tuần Châu and Hạ Long International Cruise Port enforce a strict ban on single-use plastic water bottles (under 1.5 liters) and plastic bags; security scanners confiscate disposable bottles at the terminal gate, so pack a reusable metal flask to refill at ship water stations. During summer typhoon months (late June to early September), the Maritime Administration monitors Gulf of Tonkin pressure systems: if winds reach Beaufort Force 6 (39 km/h), port authorities halt all overnight departures by 09:00. Always verify refund terms using our [Ha Long Bay Cruise Questions Before Booking](/plan/ha-long-bay-cruise-questions-before-booking) checklist and keep 1 buffer night in Hanoi before your international flight home.

- **Understand the "24-Hour" Overnight Cruise Clock**: A "2-Day-1-Night" (2D1N) cruise actually provides **21 hours to 22 hours** on board: tenders depart Tuần Châu at 12:15 on Day 1, and ships serve early brunch at 09:30 before disembarking back at the dock between 10:45 and 11:15 on Day 2. If you want an unhurried full day of kayaking and cycling to Việt Hải without midday boat-change rush, book a **3D2N Lan Hạ itinerary** or spend 2 nights onshore in [Cát Bà Town](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-cat-ba) paired with a 7-hour Bến Bèo day cruise (950,000 VND to 1,250,000 VND / $38.00 to $50.00 USD).
- **Avoid Aft Lower-Deck Cabins Near Engine Rooms**: When booking mid-range ships ($120.00 to $160.00 USD), request a cabin on the **2nd Deck (Upper Deck)** or mid-ship forward; aft cabins on Deck 1 experience 55 dB to 65 dB diesel generator hum until 23:00 and when anchors raise at 05:45.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are Ha Long Bay and Lan Ha Bay part of the same UNESCO World Heritage Site?
Yes. In September 2023, the UNESCO World Heritage Committee expanded the original 1994 Hạ Long Bay boundary to incorporate the Cát Bà Archipelago and Lan Hạ Bay, forming a unified 65,650-hectare marine core zone with identical limestone tower formations.

### Can I visit both Ha Long Bay and Lan Ha Bay on a single cruise?
Most 2D1N cruises stay strictly inside either Quảng Ninh (Hạ Long) or Hải Phòng (Lan Hạ) waters to avoid paying dual provincial sightseeing tariffs (which would add 400,000 VND to 650,000 VND / $16.00 to $26.00 USD per passenger). However, select 3D2N itineraries and vessels departing Tuần Châu Lot 16 to Lot 32 cruise past the southern edge of Hạ Long Bay during the 25-minute tender transfer into Lan Hạ Bay.

### What are the best months of the year to cruise both bays?
The prime weather windows are **October to November** (22°C to 27°C, crisp visibility, low humidity, calm seas) and **March to May** (23°C to 29°C, warming water for swimming at Ba Trái Đào and Ti Tốp). January and February bring 14°C to 18°C mist and cool water, while July and August carry the highest risk of 48-hour tropical storm port closures.
