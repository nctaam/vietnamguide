# Where to Stay in Can Tho (2026): Best Areas & Hotels

As the commercial and waterway capital of the Mekong Delta, Can Tho sits 170 km southwest of Ho Chi Minh City along the broad western branch of the Mekong—known locally as the Hau River (`Sông Hậu`). Since the completion of the My Thuan 2 Bridge and the continuous Trung Luong–My Thuan–Can Tho Expressway (`CT01`), overland transit from Saigon takes just 3.0 to 3.5 hours instead of the old 5-hour highway crawl. `FUTA Bus Lines` (`Phuong Trang`) runs luxury 34-berth sleeper buses every 30 minutes from Mien Tay Bus Station to Can Tho Central Bus Station for 165,000 to 180,000 VND ($6.60 to $7.20 USD), and the ticket price includes a free `FUTA` shuttle van drop-off straight to your hotel door.

Picking the right neighborhood in Can Tho comes down to a simple trade-off between **05:15 AM floating market boat math** and **evening walkability**. Wholesale pineapple, watermelon, and pumpkin barges at **Cai Rang Floating Market (`Chợ Nổi Cái Răng`)** begin trading at dawn and thin out by 07:30 AM. Stay on the downtown riverfront at **Ninh Kieu Wharf (`Bến Ninh Kiều`)** and you can stroll to night markets and neon bridges after dinner, followed by a 35-to-40-minute upriver sampan ride at 05:15 AM. Stay 7 km upstream in a **Cai Rang or Ba Lang canal eco-lodge** and your morning boat ride to the floating market drops to just 5 to 10 minutes, though you will need a 15-minute Grab car (`100,000 to 140,000 VND`) whenever you want city nightlife.

## 1. Ninh Kieu Wharf (`Bến Ninh Kiều`) & Tan An Riverfront Core

If you are staying in Can Tho for one or two nights and want effortless evening walks without hailing taxis, book along `Hai Ba Trung` Street, `Nguyen Thai Hoc`, `Phan Dinh Phung`, or the quiet park peninsula of `Cái Khế` at the confluence of the Hau and Can Tho rivers. By 18:00, heat rises off the pavement and locals gather along the palm-lined **Ninh Kieu Wharf (`Bến Ninh Kiều`)** promenade. From here, you can walk across the S-shaped LED-lit **Ninh Kieu Pedestrian Love Bridge (`Cầu Đi Bộ Ninh Kiều`)**, burn incense coils inside the 1894 Cantonese **Ong Pagoda (`Chùa Ông`)**, and sample grilled river prawns and coconut ice cream at the **Can Tho Night Market (`Chợ Đêm Tây Đô`)**.

> **05:15 AM Cai Rang Boat Math from Ninh Kieu Wharf:** Walk down to the official Ninh Kieu Tourist Boat Pier at **05:15 to 05:30 AM**. Chartering a private wooden sampan (`Tàu gỗ riêng`) for 1 to 4 passengers costs **350,000 to 500,000 VND ($14.00 to $20.00 USD)** total for a 2.5-hour loop covering **Cai Rang Floating Market**, a floating pineapple boat stop, and the traditional **Chín Của rice noodle workshop (`Lò hủ tiếu Chín Của`)**. Solo budget travelers can join a shared boat at the pier booth for **100,000 VND ($4.00 USD) per person**.

### Standout Hotels Around Ninh Kieu Wharf
* **Colonial Riverfront Icon:** **Victoria Can Tho Resort** (2,600,000 to 4,500,000 VND / $104.00 to $180.00 USD) occupies a tranquil 2-hectare riverside garden estate in Cai Khe Ward just 600 meters across the footbridge from Ninh Kieu Wharf, complete with Indochine wooden shutters, two outdoor pools, and a private wooden jetty for sunrise boat pickups.
* **Upscale Rooftop & Promenade Stays:** **Charmant Suites A Boutique Hotel Can Tho**, **TTC Hotel Premium Can Tho**, and **Nesta Can Tho Hotel** (1,100,000 to 2,400,000 VND / $44.00 to $96.00 USD) deliver modern river-view rooms and rooftop infinity pools overlooking the Hau River confluence.
* **Mid-Range Walkable Boutiques:** Clean, elevator-equipped boutique hotels directly on `Hai Ba Trung` and `Phan Dinh Phung` streets (`Iris Hotel`, `KP Hotel`, `Lion 1 Hotel`) run **650,000 to 1,400,000 VND ($26.00 to $56.00 USD)** per night.

## 2. Cai Rang & Ba Lang Canal Eco-Lodges (`6–9 km` Upstream)

Travelers who dislike urban concrete and want to wake up inside the real delta countryside should skip downtown and book along the shaded fruit-orchard canals of **Cai Rang** and **Ba Lang**, located 6 to 9 km upstream of Ninh Kieu Wharf. Because these eco-lodges sit right along the Can Tho and Ba Lang river tributaries immediately south of **Cai Rang Floating Market**, you can sleep until 05:15 AM, step onto a wooden sampan at your resort's private dock at **05:30 AM**, and glide among the wholesale produce barges within **5 to 10 minutes**.

After breakfast—or a bowl of floating-boat `hủ tiếu` noodles (40,000 VND / $1.60 USD)—most properties provide complimentary bicycles so you can pedal along car-free 2-meter-wide concrete paths under jackfruit, pomelo, and star-apple trees.
* **Luxury Canal Retreat:** **Can Tho Ecolodge** (`Ba Lang Ward`, 2,400,000 to 4,200,000 VND / $96.00 to $168.00 USD) features 48 palm-thatched cottages built with recycled local cajeput wood, a large central swimming pool, and direct canal boat departures.
* **Boutique Waterway Lodges:** **Bamboo Eco Village** (1,800,000 to 2,800,000 VND / $72.00 to $112.00 USD), **Mekong Silt Ecolodge** (1,400,000 to 2,500,000 VND / $56.00 to $100.00 USD), **Nguyen Shack**, and **Green Village Mekong** (900,000 to 1,600,000 VND / $36.00 to $64.00 USD) offer open-air bamboo bungalows, outdoor garden showers, and cooking classes. Note that riding into Ninh Kieu Wharf for dinner takes 15 to 20 minutes and costs **100,000 to 140,000 VND ($4.00 to $5.60 USD)** each way by GrabCar.

## 3. Con Au Islet (`Cồn Ấu`) & Hau River Luxury Towers

For five-star seclusion or high-rise executive comfort, Can Tho offers two distinct luxury formats:
* **Car-Free Private River Island (`Cồn Ấu`):** Reached solely by a 5-minute private speedboat launch from Ninh Kieu Wharf, **Azerai Can Tho** (6,800,000 to 12,500,000 VND / $270.00 to $500.00 USD) occupies a lush 8-hectare alluvial islet shaded by century-old banyan trees (`Cây đa`). Its 60 contemporary pavilions and pool villas offer complete silence in the middle of the Hau River, paired with private wooden cruiser charters to Cai Rang.
* **High-Rise City Landmark (`30 Thang 4` & `Cồn Khương`):** Rising 31 stories above central Can Tho at `209 Duong 30 Thang 4`, **Sheraton Can Tho** (2,500,000 to 4,200,000 VND / $100.00 to $168.00 USD) is the tallest building in the Mekong Delta, offering sweeping panoramic views across the river bends and easy taxi access to both Ninh Kieu (5 minutes) and Cai Rang (10 minutes). Meanwhile, **Con Khuong Resort** on nearby Khuong Islet (`Cồn Khương`) provides riverside villa tranquility for 1,600,000 to 2,900,000 VND ($64.00 to $116.00 USD).

## 4. Phong Dien Orchard Belt (`My Khanh` / `Nhon Ai`, `12–15 km` Southwest)

Continue 6 km past Cai Rang into **Phong Dien District** (`Mỹ Khánh` and `Nhơn Ái` communes) and the main shipping river splits into a labyrinth of narrow, nipa-palm-fringed arroyos (`Rạch nhỏ`) where motorboats must cut their engines and switch to hand-paddled sampans. This is Can Tho's richest heritage orchard belt.

Staying at **Vam Xang Rustic** (1,500,000 to 2,600,000 VND / $60.00 to $104.00 USD) or family-run orchard homestays (500,000 to 1,100,000 VND / $20.00 to $44.00 USD) puts you steps away from **Mười Cương Cocoa Farm** (`Vườn Ca Cao Mười Cương`, entrance and tasting 50,000 VND / $2.00 USD), where a local family ferments, roasts, and stone-grinds organic Mekong cacao beans by hand.

## 5. Binh Thuy Ancient House, Son Islet (`Cồn Sơn`) & Airport Corridor

Located 6 km north of Ninh Kieu Wharf along `Bui Huu Nghia` and `Le Hong Phong` streets toward **Can Tho International Airport (`VCA`)**, **Binh Thuy District** is the best base for travelers catching morning flights or exploring community river life. Here you can tour the 1870 French-baroque and southern timber **Binh Thuy Ancient House (`Nhà Cổ Bình Thủy`)** (30,000 VND / $1.20 USD entrance, famous as the filming location for *The Lover*) and walk 500 meters to **Co Bac Ferry Pier (`Bến đò Cô Bắc`)**.

For just **20,000 VND ($0.80 USD) round-trip**, the 5-minute ferry crosses to **Son Islet (`Cồn Sơn`)**, an agricultural community island with zero cars or motorbikes where orchard families demonstrate "flying" snakehead fish (`Cá lóc bay`), pomelo gardens, and hand-pressed southern rice cakes (`Bánh dân gian Nam Bộ`). Binh Thuy also places you just 10 minutes (4 km, 60,000 to 90,000 VND Grab) from **Can Tho Airport (`VCA`)** for direct flights to Hanoi, Da Nang, Phu Quoc, and **Con Dao (`VCS`)**, or you can take a 60 km (1 hour 15 minutes) morning transfer south to **Tran De Port (`Sóc Trăng`)** to board the **Con Dao Express** high-speed catamaran (310,000 to 390,000 VND / $12.40 to $15.60 USD).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I stay at Ninh Kieu Wharf or an eco-lodge near Cai Rang Floating Market?
Choose **Ninh Kieu Wharf** if you are staying 1 night, arrive by evening bus, and want to walk to restaurants, the pedestrian Love Bridge, and the night market without taking taxis. Choose a **Cai Rang or Ba Lang canal eco-lodge** if you are staying 2 nights, travel with family or a partner, and want a peaceful palm-canal swimming pool with a 5-to-10-minute morning boat ride to the floating market.

### What time should I leave my hotel for Cai Rang Floating Market?
Depart between **05:15 and 05:30 AM**. From Ninh Kieu Wharf, the 35-to-40-minute boat ride gets you to Cai Rang right around 05:55 AM when sunrise light hits the river and wholesale fruit boats are most active. If you stay at a Cai Rang canal lodge, you can depart at **05:30 AM** and arrive inside the market by **05:40 AM**.

### How much does a private boat to Cai Rang Floating Market cost in 2026?
At the official Ninh Kieu Tourist Pier ticket counter, a private wooden sampan for 1 to 4 passengers costs **350,000 to 500,000 VND ($14.00 to $20.00 USD)** for a 2.5-hour route (Cai Rang Floating Market plus the Chín Của rice noodle workshop), or **600,000 to 750,000 VND ($24.00 to $30.00 USD)** for a 4-hour loop that adds the narrow Phong Dien small canals (`Rạch nhỏ`) and an orchard stop.

### How do I get from Ho Chi Minh City to my hotel in Can Tho?
Take a `FUTA Bus Lines` (`Phuong Trang`) sleeper bus from Saigon's Mien Tay Bus Station (departures every 30 minutes, **165,000 to 180,000 VND / $6.60 to $7.20 USD**, 3.0 to 3.5 hours via the `CT01` expressway and My Thuan 2 Bridge). Upon arrival at Can Tho Central Bus Station, show your ticket at the `FUTA` dispatch desk for a **free shuttle van transfer** directly to any hotel in Ninh Kieu or Cai Rang.
