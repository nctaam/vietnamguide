# Where to Stay in Ben Tre (2026): Best River & Eco-Stays

Skip the noon tour-bus crush in My Tho. Most day-trippers from Ho Chi Minh City crowd onto motorized barges at **11:00 AM**, queue at the same honey-tea stalls around Unicorn Islet (`Cồn Thới Sơn`), and leave before the heat breaks.

Ben Tre (`Xứ Dừa` — Vietnam's Coconut Kingdom) rewards overnight travelers who cross the Tien River and sleep inside the quiet canal network. The province sits just **85 km** (**1 hour 45 minutes to 2 hours 15 minutes**) south of Ho Chi Minh City via the **CT01 Trung Luong Expressway** and **Rach Mieu Bridge (`Cầu Rạch Miễu`)**. Fares stay remarkably affordable. Direct limousine vans operated by **Thinh Phat**, **Thao Chau**, and **Phuong Trang (FUTA Bus Lines)** depart every **30 to 45 minutes** from District 1 and District 5 for **120,000–180,000 VND ($4.80–$7.20 USD)** per seat. Once the afternoon day-tour boats pull away by **15:30**, the narrow water-palm creeks (`Rạch dừa nước`) belong entirely to local coconut barges, kingfishers, and cyclists pedaling shaded concrete village lanes.

> **Car-Free Canal Access Warning:** Many authentic garden homestays in **Nhon Thanh (`Xã Nhơn Thạnh`)** and **Phu Nhuan (`Xã Phú Nhuận`)** sit along **1.5-meter-wide concrete bicycle paths (`Đường đan`)** where 4-seat and 7-seat taxis cannot enter. Call ahead before crossing the river. Always message your lodge on WhatsApp or Zalo **30 minutes before arrival** so the host can meet your vehicle at the nearest bridge with a three-wheeled motorbike cart (`Xe lôi`) or a private river sampan to transfer your luggage.

## 1. Nhon Thanh & Phu Nhuan Coconut Canal Belt (`Xã Nhơn Thạnh & Phú Nhuận`)

Cross the Ben Tre River bridge south of the provincial capital and the asphalt roads quickly shrink into **1.5-meter concrete ribbons (`Đường đan`)** shaded by towering Siamese coconut palms (`Dừa xiêm`) and green-skin pomelo trees (`Bưởi da xanh`). Cars cannot drive into these orchard hamlets. You hear only bicycle bells, rooster calls, and the steady chug of wooden cargo sampans hauling husked coconuts down mud-banked creeks.

This **4–6 km** belt across **Nhon Thanh** and **Phu Nhuan** communes is the finest place to stay in Ben Tre if you want rural immersion without sacrificing hot-water showers, clean mosquito nets, and air-conditioned garden bungalows.

### Top Homestays & Farmstays in Nhon Thanh & Phu Nhuan
* **Jardin Du Mekong Homestay (`Phú Nhuận`)**: Set beside a quiet tributary creek, this French-Vietnamese family property features timber-and-thatch bungalows wrapped in hibiscus and bougainvillea gardens for **950,000–1,400,000 VND ($38–$56 USD)** including breakfast and free mountain bikes.
* **Ben Tre Farm Stay (`Phú Nhuận`)**: A long-running eco-lodge surrounded by nipa palms (`Dừa nước`), offering simple fan or AC rooms (**550,000–950,000 VND / $22–$38 USD**), outdoor hammocks, and guided morning walks to neighbor farms.
* **Gite Nam Hien (`Nhơn Thạnh`)**: Upgraded courtyard rooms with a small swimming pool, shaded cycling loops right outside the gate, and attentive family hosts (**1,050,000–1,400,000 VND / $42–$56 USD**).
* **Coco Farmstay & Ba Danh Homestay (`Nhơn Thạnh`)**: Welcoming family-run compounds priced at **600,000–1,100,000 VND ($24–$44 USD)** where guests join evening cooking sessions on open wood-fired or gas stoves.

### Dining & Early-Morning Canal Logistics
Stay here for the evening family dinner. Hosts prepare crispy fried Elephant Ear Fish (`Cá tai tượng chiên xù`, **180,000–250,000 VND** whole fish) rolled at the table in rice paper with starfruit, green banana, and herbs, alongside crunchy coconut heart salad (`Gỏi củ hủ dừa tôm thịt`) and braised pork in fresh coconut water (`Thịt kho nước dừa`).

Wake up early. Book a **07:30 AM** paddle sampan and motorboat loop (**350,000–500,000 VND** per boat for 2–4 people) directly through your homestay. At that hour, the river breeze is cool, the light is golden, and you can visit traditional clay brick kilns (`Lò gạch thủ công`), **Nhon Thanh sedge mat weavers (`Làng dệt chiếu Nhơn Thạnh`)**, and family coconut-husking sheds (`Lột vỏ dừa`) two full hours before day-tripper groups arrive from Saigon.

## 2. Ham Luong Riverfront & Ben Tre City Center (`Hung Vuong Street & Ward 7`)

Travelers who prefer direct taxi drop-off at the hotel lobby, elevator access, swimming pools, and walkable evening dining should base themselves along the northern bank of the **Ben Tre River (`Hung Vuong Street`, Ward 5)** or on the breezy **Ham Luong River (`Sông Hàm Luông`, Ward 7)**. The provincial capital is compact, safe, and refreshingly uncommercialized compared to Can Tho or My Tho.

### Best Resorts & City Hotels
* **Ben Tre Riverside Resort (Ward 7, `708 Nguyễn Văn Tư`)**: Built directly on the site of the historic Ham Luong ferry landing (`Bến phà Hàm Luông cũ`), this **4-star** property looks out across a **1.5-kilometer-wide** stretch of the Ham Luong River. Riverfront rooms and garden bungalows run **1,400,000–2,500,000 VND ($56–$100 USD)**, complete with an outdoor pool facing passing sand barges and an on-site pier for private boat pickups.
* **Diamond Stars Ben Tre Hotel (`140 Hùng Vương`, Ward 3)**: The city's premier **5-star** high-rise hotel sits right on the riverside promenade, offering panoramic river-view rooms, a rooftop pool, and modern soundproofing for **1,500,000–2,800,000 VND ($60–$112 USD)**.
* **Mango Home Riverside (`Mỹ Thạnh, Giồng Trôm` — 15 minutes east of town)**: A boutique eco-resort with colorful teak bungalows, an infinity pool beside the river, complimentary bicycle tours, and a **30-minute** sampan excursion included in rates of **1,200,000–2,200,000 VND ($48–$88 USD)**.

### Boat Charters & Evening Food Walks
From **Hung Vuong Pier (`Bến tàu Hùng Vương`)** or **Cai Coi Bridge (`Cầu Cái Cối`)**, you can charter a private wooden riverboat for a **3-hour** morning circuit across the Ben Tre River into the Nhon Thanh canals for **400,000–600,000 VND ($16–$24 USD)** total per boat (up to 4 passengers). In the evening, stroll along `Hung Vuong Street` to the **Ben Tre Night Market**, sample grilled coconut worms or hot `Bánh xèo ốc gạo` (rice-snail sizzling pancakes, **45,000–70,000 VND**), and withdraw cash at Vietcombank or BIDV ATMs along **Dong Khoi Boulevard (`Đại lộ Đồng Khởi`)**.

## 3. Chau Thanh & An Khanh / Phu Tuc Riverside (North Bank near Rach Mieu Bridge)

Situated along the southern bank of the **Tien River (`Sông Tiền`)** in **Chau Thanh District**, the communes of **An Khanh (`Xã An Khánh`)** and **Phu Tuc (`Xã Phú Túc`)** lie just **15 minutes** off the CT01 highway exit after crossing **Rach Mieu Bridge**. This zone overlooks **Unicorn Islet (`Cồn Thới Sơn`)** and **Phoenix Islet (`Cồn Phụng` — home to the historic Coconut Monk `Đạo Dừa` relics)**.

* **Mekong Home (`Ấp 9, Xã Phú Túc`)**: A standout riverside hideaway set deep inside a quiet coconut grove on the Tien River bank. Spacious private bungalows with outdoor stone bathtubs, a saltwater pool, free bikes, and traditional foot baths cost **1,600,000–2,600,000 VND ($64–$104 USD)**.
* **Rooster Mekong & Charming Countryside Homestay (`An Khánh / Phú Túc`)**: Comfortable mid-range garden stays priced at **700,000–1,500,000 VND ($28–$60 USD)** with easy access to family coconut candy workshops (`Lò kẹo dừa truyền thống`).
* **Forever Green Resort (`Phú Túc`)**: A sprawling riverside resort complex with large swimming pools and karaoke/event facilities (**1,500,000–2,400,000 VND**), popular with domestic weekend groups.

> **Rach Mieu Bridge Traffic Alert:** Until the new **Rach Mieu 2 Bridge (`Cầu Rạch Miễu 2`)** fully opens to traffic, the existing single-span **Rach Mieu 1 Bridge** experiences severe congestion on **Friday afternoons (16:00–19:30)** and **Sunday afternoons (15:00–19:00)**. Time your highway crossing before **14:00** on Sundays or travel on weekdays to avoid a **45-to-60-minute** bridge bottleneck.

## 4. Cho Lach Orchard & Bonsai Island (`Chợ Lách / Cù Lao Minh`)

Roughly **35 km** west of Ben Tre city along **National Highway 57 (`QL57`)** toward Vinh Long and Cai Be lies **Cho Lach District**, Vietnam's premier tropical fruit nursery and ornamental bonsai hub. Visit between **May and August** when orchard branches sag under ripe **Cai Mon durian (`Sầu riêng Cái Mơn`)**, mangosteen (`Măng cụt`), and rambutan (`Chôm chôm`). In the month preceding Lunar New Year (`Tết Nguyên Đán`), **Vinh Thanh Flower Village (`Làng hoa kiểng Vĩnh Thành`)** transforms into a sea of yellow marigolds, kumquat trees, and grafted bougainvillea. Homestays like **Coco Riverside Lodge** and **Mai Anh Homestay** charge **500,000–1,300,000 VND ($20–$52 USD)** and arrange orchard tasting rides by bicycle.

## 5. Ba Tri (`Sân Chim Vàm Hồ`) & Mo Cay Coconut Craft Core

Travelers with a private car or motorbike who want zero foreign tourists can push **22–38 km** deeper into the province:
* **Ba Tri District & Vam Ho Bird Sanctuary (`Sân chim Vàm Hồ`)**: Located along the lower Ba Lai River, this mangrove and wild date-palm forest shelters thousands of white storks, night herons, and cormorants between **April and October** (best viewed at **16:30–17:45** as flocks return to roost; eco-farm cabins run **450,000–900,000 VND**).
* **Mo Cay Nam (`Mỏ Cày Nam` — Thom Market `Chợ Thơm` along the Co Chien River)**: The industrial heartland of Vietnam's coconut trade, where barges line up for kilometers outside processing workshops turning husks into coir mats, shells into activated charcoal, and white meat into virgin coconut oil.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it better to stay overnight in Ben Tre or My Tho?
Stay overnight in **Ben Tre**. My Tho is an urban highway transit point where day-trip buses unload passengers between **10:30 AM and 14:30 PM**. Crossing the Rach Mieu Bridge into Ben Tre's **Nhon Thanh** or **Phu Tuc** orchard canals gives you car-free cycling lanes, home-cooked Mekong dinners, and quiet **07:30 AM** sampan rides before any tour groups arrive.

### How many nights should I spend in Ben Tre?
Spend **1 to 2 nights**. One night is ideal for a weekend break from Ho Chi Minh City (depart Saturday at **07:30 AM**, cycle the Nhon Thanh coconut paths in the afternoon, take a sunrise sampan ride Sunday morning, and return to HCMC before **14:00**). Two nights allow you to add **Cho Lach Fruit Island** or transfer onward to **Can Tho** (**115 km / 2.5 hours**) or **Tra Vinh** (**50 km / 1 hour 15 minutes** via the Co Chien Bridge).

### How do I get from Ben Tre to Can Tho without backtracking to Saigon?
Ask your hotel to book a seat on a direct provincial bus or private car (`1,100,000–1,400,000 VND` per 4-seat car) heading west via **QL60 and QL57** or across the **Ham Luong** and **Co Chien Bridges** through Tra Vinh and Vinh Long into Can Tho (**2.5 to 3 hours**). Cyclists and motorbike riders can also take the scenic island ferry route across **Cho Lach** and **An Binh Islet** into Vinh Long before continuing south to Ninh Kieu Wharf.

### Do Ben Tre homestays have air conditioning and hot water?
Yes. Almost all established homestays and eco-lodges in **Nhon Thanh**, **Phu Nhuan**, and **Phu Tuc** now provide private en-suite bathrooms with solar or electric hot-water showers, individual split-unit air conditioners, and high-speed Wi-Fi. Budget rooms around **550,000 VND ($22 USD)** sometimes offer fan-only options, so confirm air conditioning when booking between **March and May**, when afternoon temperatures reach **34°C to 36°C**.
