# Hanoi Travel Guide: Where to Stay, What to Do & Route Fit (2026)

Anchored along the red alluvial silt bends of the Red River (`Sông Hồng`) in northern Vietnam, **Hanoi (`Thủ đô Hà Nội`)** functions as the nation's political capital, rail hub, and historical core. Spanning over nine centuries of continuous civic history since Emperor Ly Thai To established the royal citadel of Thang Long in 1010, the city layers 11th-century Confucian academies, late-19th-century French neoclassical boulevards, narrow 4-meter-wide merchant guild alleys, and modern elevated metro corridors into a compact 3,359 km² municipality of 8.5 million residents.

For international travelers entering Vietnam in 2026, **Noi Bai International Airport (`HAN`, located 30 kilometers north of Hoan Kiem Lake)** and **Hanoi Central Railway Station (`Ga Hà Nội`, 120 Le Duan)** serve as the primary northern gateways. Every overland journey to the limestone karsts of **Ha Long Bay and Lan Ha Bay (160 km east, 2.5 hours via Expressway CT04)**, the flooded river caves of **Ninh Binh (95 km south, 1.5 hours via Expressway CT01)**, the terraced valleys of **Sa Pa (315 km northwest, 5.5 hours via Expressway CT05)**, and the mountain passes of **Ha Giang (300 km north, 6.0 hours via Highway QL2)** radiates directly from Hanoi. Treating the capital merely as an overnight transit stop misses its century-old single-dish street food stalls, lakeside pagodas, and distinct district personalities.

Navigating Hanoi efficiently requires concrete tactical choices. Scooter traffic moves continuously at 20 km/h to 35 km/h across narrow intersections, hotel comfort varies sharply across a 500-meter boundary between the Old Quarter and the French Quarter, and state monuments enforce strict Monday or Friday afternoon closures. This 2026 field guide details neighborhood lodging rates in VND and USD, monument admissions, verified culinary addresses, airport Bus 86 vs GrabCar transfers, and day-by-day route integration.

> [!NOTE]
> Hanoi experiences a distinct four-season humid subtropical climate (*Cwa* classification). Winters (December through February) turn cold, damp, and overcast, with daytime temperatures dropping to 10°C to 16°C at 82% humidity (`crachin` drizzle). Summers (June through August) are hot and wet, with afternoon highs reaching 33°C to 38°C and monthly rainfall of 260 mm to 320 mm. The prime dry, clear travel windows are **Autumn (September through November, 22°C to 28°C, 68% humidity)** and **Spring (March through April, 20°C to 26°C)**.

## Where to Stay: Strategic Neighborhood Breakdown & 2026 Room Rates

Selecting the right micro-district in Hanoi shapes both your sleep quality and your walking radius.

### 1. Hoan Kiem District: The Old Quarter (`Phố Cổ Hà Nội`, 36 Guild Streets)
- **Best for:** First-time visitors, 48-hour to 72-hour stays, and street food walkers wanting immediate pedestrian access to Hoan Kiem Lake, Ta Hien Beer Street, and Dong Xuan Market.
- **Character & Top Streets:** A dense 100-hectare grid of medieval merchant streets north of Hoan Kiem Lake. For quiet boutique sleep within a 4-minute walk (300 meters to 500 meters) of the lake, book along tree-lined **Hang Trong, Nha Tho (around St. Joseph's Cathedral), Bao Khanh, Hang Be, Hang Gai, or Ma May**. Avoid booking rooms directly above the late-night bars of **Ta Hien and Luong Ngoc Quyen**, where bass music runs until 02:00 AM.
- **Architectural Note:** Traditional "tube houses" (`nhà ống`) measure only 3.5 meters to 5.0 meters wide and 30 meters to 45 meters deep; entry-level rooms often have internal air-shaft windows (`giếng trời`), so pay **150,000 VND to 250,000 VND ($6.00 to $10.00 USD)** extra per night to upgrade to a front-facing "Deluxe Balcony" or "City View" category.
- **2026 Price Bracket:** **550,000 VND to 1,250,000 VND ($22.00 to $50.00 USD)** per night for 3-star heritage hotels; **1,400,000 VND to 2,800,000 VND ($56.00 to $112.00 USD)** for upscale 4-star boutique properties (*La Siesta Classic Ma May, Peridot Grand, Oriental Jade*).

### 2. The French Quarter (`Khu Phố Pháp`, South & East of Hoan Kiem Lake)
- **Best for:** Couples, luxury travelers, architecture enthusiasts, and visitors wanting 4-meter-wide paved sidewalks and quiet nights.
- **Character & Top Streets:** Extending 1.2 kilometers south and east of Hoan Kiem Lake along **Ly Thai To, Ngo Quyen, Trang Tien, Phan Chu Trinh, and Hai Ba Trung**, this district preserves ochre French neoclassical villas, foreign embassies, luxury boutiques, and the 1911 **Hanoi Opera House (`Nhà Hát Lớn Hà Nội`)**. You remain within a 10-minute walk (700 meters to 1,000 meters) of the Old Quarter food stalls without the midnight scooter horns.
- **2026 Price Bracket:** **1,600,000 VND to 3,500,000 VND ($64.00 to $140.00 USD)** per night for 4-star hotels (*Hotel de l'Opera, Rey Hotel*); **4,500,000 VND to 9,500,000+ VND ($180.00 to $380.00+ USD)** per night for 5-star icons (*Sofitel Legend Metropole Hanoi* at 15 Ngo Quyen and *Capella Hanoi* at 11 Le Phung Hieu).

### 3. Tay Ho District: West Lake & Quang An Peninsula (`Hồ Tây`)
- **Best for:** Digital nomads, 5-to-14-day slow travelers, families, and visitors sensitive to downtown traffic noise.
- **Character:** Wrapping around a **526-hectare freshwater lake** with a **17-kilometer shoreline path**, the eastern peninsula of **Quang An (`Xuan Dieu` and `To Ngoc Van` streets)** hosts quiet lakeside villas, specialty coffee roasteries, craft taprooms (*Turtle Lake Brewing*), and international bistros.
- **Transit Reality:** Sits **5.0 kilometers to 6.5 kilometers north** of Hoan Kiem Lake, requiring a **15-minute to 20-minute GrabCar ride (65,000 VND to 95,000 VND / $2.60 to $3.80 USD)** to reach central museums, though it sits 10 minutes closer (22 km, 30 minutes) to Noi Bai Airport (`HAN`).
- **2026 Price Bracket:** **750,000 VND to 2,200,000 VND ($30.00 to $88.00 USD)** per night for serviced lakeside apartments; **3,200,000 VND to 5,500,000 VND ($128.00 to $220.00 USD)** for 5-star overwater resorts (*InterContinental Hanoi Westlake* and *Pan Pacific Hanoi*).

### 4. Ba Dinh & Truc Bach District (Political & Culinary Enclave)
- **Best for:** History travelers and food lovers wanting calm residential streets 1.5 kilometers west of the Old Quarter.
- **Character:** Centered around **Truc Bach Island (`Đảo Ngũ Xã`, famous for *phở cuốn* rolled beef noodles)**, **Phan Dinh Phung** (Hanoi's greenest 1.5-kilometer tree-canopied boulevard), and the **Ba Dinh Square / Temple of Literature** axis.
- **2026 Price Bracket:** **700,000 VND to 1,850,000 VND ($28.00 to $74.00 USD)** per night.

## The Definitive Cultural & Historic Circuit (2026 Hours & Tariffs)

Hanoi's major historical monuments sit within a 3.0-kilometer triangle connecting Hoan Kiem and Ba Dinh districts. Because several sites close on Mondays or at 11:00 AM, sequence your visits carefully:

### 1. The Temple of Literature (`Văn Miếu – Quốc Tử Giám`, 58 Quoc Tu Giam, Dong Da)
Founded in **1070** under Emperor Ly Thanh Tong and expanded in **1076** as Vietnam's first Imperial Academy (`Quốc Tử Giám`), this 54,000 m² walled sanctuary educated royal princes and mandarin scholars for over 700 years. Walking north through five sequential courtyards, you pass the **1805 Khue Van Cac (`Constellation of Literature Pavilion`, featured on the reverse of the 100,000 VND polymer banknote)**, the square **Thien Quang Well**, and the **82 UNESCO Memory of the World Stone Turtle Stelae (`Bia Tiến Sĩ`)** carved between 1442 and 1779 with the names of 1,307 doctoral laureates.
- **2026 Hours & Tariffs:** Daytime hours **08:00 to 17:00 daily (open 7 days a week, including Mondays)**. Standard adult admission is **70,000 VND ($2.80 USD)**; students and seniors (60+) pay **35,000 VND ($1.40 USD)**; children under 15 enter for **0 VND**. A multilingual 12-language audio guide rents for **100,000 VND ($4.00 USD)**. The immersive **3D-Mapping Night Tour (`Tinh Hoa Đạo Học`)** runs on **Wednesday, Saturday, and Sunday evenings from 18:30 to 21:30** for **199,000 VND ($7.96 USD)**. Allocate **60 minutes to 75 minutes**.

### 2. Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum Complex, Presidential Palace & One Pillar Pagoda (`Lăng Chủ Tịch Hồ Chí Minh`, Ba Dinh)
Set on historic **Ba Dinh Square (`2 Hùng Vương`, 2.0 km west of Hoan Kiem Lake)** where Ho Chi Minh read the Declaration of Independence on September 2, 1945, the complex includes the granite **Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum (0 VND entry; open 07:30–10:30 in summer and 08:00–11:00 in winter on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday, and Sunday mornings only; closed Mondays and Fridays)**, the yellow French colonial **Presidential Palace Grounds & Ho Chi Minh's 1958 Wooden Stilt House (`Nhà Sàn Bác Hồ`, open daily 07:30–11:00 and 13:30–16:00; ticket 40,000 VND / $1.60 USD)**, the lotus-shaped **One Pillar Pagoda (`Chùa Một Cột`, founded 1049, 0 VND entry)**, and the **Ho Chi Minh Museum (`Bảo tàng Hồ Chí Minh`, 40,000 VND / $1.60 USD, closed Mondays)**.
- **Strict Dress & Security Protocol:** Visitors must wear trousers or skirts covering the knees and shirts covering the shoulders (`no shorts or sleeveless tops`). Large cameras and backpacks are deposited free of charge (0 VND) at the Ong Ich Khiem security check gate before walking the 400-meter queue.

### 3. Imperial Citadel of Thang Long (`Hoàng Thành Thăng Long`, 19C Hoang Dieu, Ba Dinh)
Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2010, the **Imperial Citadel** served as Vietnam's unbroken seat of royal power for 13 centuries. Visitors climb the monumental **Doan Mon Southern Gate**, inspect the stone dragon steps of the **1428 Kinh Thien Palace**, view excavated Ly and Tran dynasty wells at the **18 Hoang Dieu Archaeological Pit**, and descend 9 meters underground into **Bunker D67 (`Hầm Cơ Yếu D67`)**, the blast-proof 60-centimeter-thick concrete command center used by General Vo Nguyen Giap and the Politburo between 1967 and 1975.
- **2026 Hours & Tariffs:** Open **Tuesday through Sunday from 08:00 to 17:00 (closed Mondays)**. Adult admission is **70,000 VND ($2.80 USD)**; students and seniors pay **35,000 VND ($1.40 USD)**. Allocate **75 minutes to 90 minutes**.

### 4. Hỏa Lò Prison Relic (`Di Tích Nhà Tù Hỏa Lò / Maison Centrale`, 01 Hoa Lo, Hoan Kiem)
Constructed by the French colonial administration in **1896** to hold up to 600 Vietnamese anti-colonial revolutionaries (and overcrowding to 2,000 prisoners by 1953), the surviving southern wing later housed shot-down American pilots between 1964 and 1973 (who nicknamed it the *"Hanoi Hilton"*). Exhibits preserve the original French iron guillotine, pitch-black *cachot* solitary cells, the 1945 sewer escape grate through which over 100 political prisoners escaped on Christmas Eve, and the flight suit and parachute of late US Senator John McCain.
- **2026 Hours & Tariffs:** Open **7 days a week (including Mondays) from 08:00 to 17:00 without a midday break**. Adult admission is **50,000 VND ($2.00 USD)**; the 35-stop English audio headset (**100,000 VND / $4.00 USD**) is essential for full historical context. Allocate **60 minutes to 75 minutes**.

### 5. Ngoc Son Temple, Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre & Vietnam Museum of Ethnology
- **Ngoc Son Temple (`Đền Ngọc Sơn`, Hoan Kiem Lake; open 07:00–18:00 daily; ticket 50,000 VND / $2.00 USD)**: Reached via the scarlet wooden **The Huc Bridge (`Cầu Thê Húc`, "Bridge of Morning Sunlight")**, honoring 13th-century General Tran Hung Dao and displaying preserved specimens of the giant sacred Hoan Kiem softshell turtle (*Rafetus leloii*, weighing 250 kg).
- **Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre (`Nhà Hát Múa Rối Thăng Long`, 57B Dinh Tien Hoang, directly opposite Hoan Kiem Lake)**: 50-minute traditional Red River Delta wooden water-puppet performances accompanied by a live *đàn bầu* and bamboo flute orchestra run 5 to 6 times daily (`15:00, 16:10, 17:20, 18:30, 20:00`); tickets cost **100,000 VND ($4.00 USD) standard, 150,000 VND ($6.00 USD) Deluxe, and 200,000 VND ($8.00 USD) VIP front rows** (book 24 hours ahead at the box office).
- **Vietnam Museum of Ethnology (`Bảo Tàng Dân Tộc Học Việt Nam`, Nguyen Van Huyen Road, Cau Giay District, 7.0 km west of Hoan Kiem; 20-minute GrabCar costing 85,000–110,000 VND / $3.40–$4.40 USD)**: Open **Tuesday through Sunday 08:30 to 17:30 (closed Mondays)**; admission **40,000 VND ($1.60 USD)** plus **50,000 VND ($2.00 USD)** camera permit. Features an extraordinary 2-hectare outdoor architectural garden containing 10 full-scale authentic ethnic minority structures, including a **19-meter-tall Bahnar communal *Rông* house**, an **Ede 42-meter matriarchal longhouse**, a **Tay stilt house**, and a **H'mong rammed-earth home**.

- **Phở Bò Hà Nội (55,000–80,000 VND / $2.20–$3.20 USD)**: True northern beef pho simmers beef marrow bones, charred ginger, charred shallots, star anise, and cardamom for **10 hours to 14 hours** without rock sugar or hoisin sauce. At **Phở Gia Truyền Bát Đàn** (`49 Bát Đàn`, open 06:00–10:00 and 18:00–20:30), queue for 15 minutes at the counter, pay **60,000 VND to 75,000 VND ($2.40 to $3.00 USD)** in cash for a *phở tái nạm* (medium-rare tenderloin and brisket), carry your own steaming bowl to a wooden table, add a spoonful of pickled garlic vinegar (*dấm tỏi*), and dip crispy fried dough sticks (*quẩy*, **10,000 VND / $0.40 USD** for a plate of 5) straight into the broth.
- **Bún Chả & Nem Cua Bể (50,000–95,000 VND / $2.00–$3.80 USD)**: A lunchtime institution served from 10:30 to 15:00 where smoky, caramel-charred pork shoulder patties (*chả viên*) and sliced pork belly (*chả miếng*) arrive submerged in a warm bowl of sweet-sour fish sauce with pickled green papaya and carrot, accompanied by cold rice vermicelli (*bún rối*), perilla leaves (*tía tô*), and crispy square sea-crab spring rolls (*nem cua bể*, **35,000 VND / $1.40 USD** each).
- **Chả Cá Thăng Long (140,000–185,000 VND / $5.60–$7.40 USD per portion at 6B Đường Thành)**: Chunks of boneless *cá lăng* (river catfish) marinated in galangal and turmeric are sizzled right at your table in a cast-iron skillet with heaps of fresh green dill and spring onions, eaten over vermicelli with roasted peanuts and a drop of purple *mắm tôm* whipped with lime juice.
- **Cà Phê Trứng (Egg Coffee, 35,000–45,000 VND / $1.40–$1.80 USD)**: Created in **1946** by founder Nguyen Van Giang at **Cafe Giảng (`39 Nguyễn Hữu Huân`, open 07:00–22:00)** when fresh cow's milk was scarce during the First Indochina War. Two egg yolks are beaten for 8 minutes with sweetened condensed milk and butter into a warm, airy tiramisu-like custard that floats atop bitter Vietnamese robusta coffee set inside a small bowl of hot water.

> [!TIP]
> **Hanoi Train Street (`Phố Đường Tàu`, Phung Hung / Tran Phu vs Le Duan) 2026 Access Rules**: Security barricades at the **northern Phung Hung / Dien Bien Phu rail crossing** remain guarded by ward police to prevent overcrowding on the 3-meter-wide tracks. To visit legally and safely, either walk to a registered trackside cafe owner at the Phung Hung barrier who will escort you inside to their second-floor balcony for the price of a **50,000 VND ($2.00 USD)** coffee, or visit the **southern Train Street segment at `224 Lê Duẩn` (near Ga Hà Nội, 1.5 km south)**, which remains calmer and open. Weekday train passings through the Phung Hung–Le Duan corridor cluster around **11:45 AM, 15:15 PM, and between 19:10 and 22:00 PM**. Stand completely behind the yellow safety line inside the cafe recess 5 minutes before the horn sounds.

## Airport Transfers (Noi Bai HAN) & Urban Public Navigation

Navigating the **30-kilometer highway link** between **Noi Bai International Airport (`HAN`)** and central Hoan Kiem District takes **40 minutes to 55 minutes** via the cable-stayed Nhat Tan Bridge (`Cầu Nhật Tân`).

### 1. Route 86 Express Airport Bus (`Xe Buýt Sân Bay 86`: 45,000 VND / $1.80 USD)
Operated by Transerco with bright orange air-conditioned low-floor buses featuring dedicated interior suitcase racks and free Wi-Fi, **Express Bus 86** connects both **Terminal 1 (Domestic)** and **Terminal 2 (International, Pillar 02 outside Arrivals)** directly with the Old Quarter and **Hanoi Railway Station (`Ga Hà Nội`)**.
- **Exact Old Quarter Stops:** Stops at **Long Bien Transit Center** (for northern Old Quarter hotels on Hang Dau/Hang Than), **162 Tran Quang Khai / Vietcombank Tower** (5-minute walk to Ma May, Hang Bac, and Hoan Kiem Lake), **Hanoi Opera House**, **Melia Hotel (44B Ly Thuong Kiet)**, and terminates at **Ga Ha Noi (120 Le Duan)**.
- **Tariff & Schedule:** **45,000 VND ($1.80 USD)** flat fare per passenger (luggage included at 0 VND), paid in cash to the onboard conductor. Buses run daily from **05:10 to 22:30** from Ga Ha Noi and **06:18 to 22:58** from Noi Bai Airport every **25 minutes to 35 minutes** (journey time **45 minutes to 55 minutes**).
- **Alternative Express Bus 68 (`50,000 VND / $2.00 USD`)**: Runs every 30 minutes from HAN Airport through **Tay Ho, Ba Dinh (Kim Ma / Lotte Center), and Dong Da** to Ha Dong.

### 2. GrabCar, Xanh SM & Official Airport Taxis (260,000–350,000 VND / $10.40–$14.00 USD)
- **Tariff Benchmark:** Booking a 4-seater **GrabCar** or **Xanh SM Electric Taxi** from Noi Bai Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 (`walk outside Arrivals to Pillar 7–9 at T2 and match the yellow license plate`) into Hoan Kiem or Ba Dinh costs **260,000 VND to 320,000 VND ($10.40 to $12.80 USD)** (or **340,000 VND to 420,000 VND / $13.60 to $16.80 USD** for a 7-seater SUV), including the **10,000 VND to 15,000 VND ($0.40 to $0.60 USD)** airport toll barrier fee. Pre-booking a private airport pickup sedan through your Old Quarter hotel costs **350,000 VND to 400,000 VND ($14.00 to $16.00 USD)** with a driver holding a name sign outside customs.

## Step-by-Step Protocol: How to Plan a 3-Day (72-Hour) Hanoi Itinerary

### 1. Day 1 (Hoan Kiem Core): Old Quarter Guild Walk, Ngoc Son Temple, Egg Coffee & Water Puppets
Take **Bus 86 (45,000 VND / $1.80 USD)** or a **GrabCar (280,000 VND / $11.20 USD, 45 minutes)** from HAN Airport to your Hoan Kiem hotel. Start with a 65,000 VND ($2.60 USD) bowl of beef pho at **Phở 10 Lý Quốc Sư** or **Phở Bát Đàn**, walk 1.5 kilometers around **Hoan Kiem Lake** and cross The Huc Bridge into **Ngoc Son Temple (50,000 VND / $2.00 USD)**, sip a 40,000 VND ($1.60 USD) hot egg coffee at **Cafe Giảng (39 Nguyễn Hữu Huân)**, tour **Hỏa Lò Prison (50,000 VND / $2.00 USD)** at 14:30, attend the 17:20 performance at **Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre (150,000 VND / $6.00 USD)**, and eat **Chả Cá Thăng Long (165,000 VND / $6.60 USD)** for dinner before a fresh draft *Bia Hơi* (**15,000 VND / $0.60 USD** per glass) at the corner of Ta Hien and Luong Ngoc Quyen.

### 2. Day 2 (Ba Dinh & West Lake): Mausoleum Complex, Imperial Citadel, Temple of Literature & Tran Quoc Pagoda
Arrive at **Ba Dinh Square by 07:30 AM** to walk through the **Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum (0 VND)**, **Presidential Palace Stilt House (40,000 VND / $1.60 USD)**, and **One Pillar Pagoda (0 VND)**. At 09:45, walk 600 meters east to the **Imperial Citadel of Thang Long & Bunker D67 (70,000 VND / $2.80 USD)**. Enjoy a 60,000 VND ($2.40 USD) *bún chả* lunch near **58 Quốc Tử Giám**, spend 75 minutes inside the **Temple of Literature (70,000 VND / $2.80 USD)**, take a 20-minute GrabCar (85,000 VND / $3.40 USD) to the **Vietnam Museum of Ethnology (40,000 VND / $1.60 USD)** at 14:30, and finish at **17:00 for sunset at Tran Quoc Pagoda (`Chùa Trấn Quốc`, 0 VND, founded in 541 AD on a West Lake islet)** followed by *phở cuốn* beef rolls (**65,000 VND / $2.60 USD**) at **Hưng Bền (33 Ngũ Xã, Trúc Bạch)**.

### 3. Day 3 (Day Trip Hub): Ninh Binh Karst Boat Cave Excursion or Perfume Pagoda & Incense Village

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is Hanoi tap water safe to drink, and what about ice in iced coffee (`cà phê sữa đá`)?
**Do not drink municipal tap water in Hanoi** without boiling it for 3 minutes in your hotel kettle; 500 ml to 1.5-liter sealed bottles of *LaVie* or *Aquafina* cost **6,000 VND to 12,000 VND ($0.24 to $0.48 USD)** at every Circle K and WinMart+. However, the **hollow cylindrical ice cubes (`đá viên tinh khiết`)** served in Old Quarter coffee shops, *bún chả* restaurants, and *bia hơi* corners are delivered daily in sealed plastic bags from RO-purified factory plants and are **safe for travelers**.

### How do I safely cross the street with heavy motorcycle traffic in Hanoi?
Crossing a 4-lane street in Hoan Kiem or Ba Dinh requires **slow, predictable forward motion**. Wait 10 seconds at the curb for any large city bus or SUV to pass, raise your traffic-facing hand at waist height as a calm visual marker, step off the curb at a steady **1-step-per-second pace (`3.5 km/h`)**, and maintain eye contact with oncoming scooter riders 15 meters away. Motorcyclists travelling at 25 km/h calculate your speed and steer smoothly behind your back; **never sprint, freeze in panic, or jump backward**.

### What is the weekend pedestrian zone around Hoan Kiem Lake (`Phố Đi Bộ Hồ Gươm`)?
Every weekend from **19:00 Friday continuously through 24:00 Sunday**, Hanoi closes the **1.7-kilometer road loop around Hoan Kiem Lake** (`Đinh Tiên Hoàng`, `Hàng Khay`, `Lê Thái Tổ`) plus 26 adjacent Old Quarter streets (`Hàng Đào`, `Hàng Ngang`, `Hàng Đường`, `Tạ Hiện`, `Mã Mây`) to all motorbikes, cars, and buses. The avenues transform into a car-free festival promenade with bamboo tug-of-war games, *ô ăn quan* board games, acoustic bands, and the **Weekend Night Market (`Chợ Đêm Phố Cổ`, 18:30–23:00 Friday–Sunday along Hang Dao to Dong Xuan)**. Note that if your hotel sits inside the pedestrian cordon and you arrive from HAN Airport between Friday evening and Sunday night, your GrabCar will drop you at the nearest perimeter barrier (`100 meters to 300 meters` from your lobby).

### Are museums and monuments in Hanoi open every day of the week?
**No.** Several major institutions in Hanoi close on **Mondays** (`and occasionally Fridays`), so check your calendar before booking a Grab ride:
- **Open 7 Days a Week (Including Mondays, 08:00–17:00):** **Temple of Literature (`70,000 VND` / `$2.80 USD`)**, **Hỏa Lò Prison (`50,000 VND` / `$2.00 USD`)**, **Ngoc Son Temple (`50,000 VND` / `$2.00 USD`)**, and **Vietnamese Women's Museum (`36 Lý Thường Kiệt`, `40,000 VND` / `$1.60 USD`)**.
- **Closed Every Monday:** **Imperial Citadel of Thang Long (`70,000 VND` / `$2.80 USD`)**, **Vietnam Museum of Ethnology (`40,000 VND` / `$1.60 USD`)**, **Vietnam Fine Arts Museum (`66 Nguyễn Thái Học`, `40,000 VND` / `$1.60 USD`)**, and **Vietnam National Museum of History (`01 Tràng Tiền`, `40,000 VND` / `$1.60 USD`, closed first Monday of each month)**.
- **Closed Monday & Friday Mornings (Open 07:30–10:30/11:00 Only):** **Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum interior (`0 VND`)** (though the outer Presidential Palace grounds and One Pillar Pagoda remain open daily).

### What is the best way to travel from Hanoi to Sa Pa, Ha Long Bay, and Ninh Binh in 2026?
- **Hanoi to Ha Long Bay / Lan Ha Bay (`160 km`, `2.5 hours` via Expressway CT04):** Book a **9-to-11-seat VIP limousine van (`250,000 VND to 350,000 VND` / `$10.00 to $14.00 USD` one-way)** with free Old Quarter hotel pickup at 08:00 AM, arriving at Tuan Chau Marina or Got Ferry Terminal (for Cat Ba Island) by 10:45 AM.
- **Hanoi to Ninh Binh (`95 km`, `1.5 hours` via Expressway CT01):** Take a **VIP limousine van (`180,000 VND to 250,000 VND` / `$7.20 to $10.00 USD`)** directly to Tam Coc or Trang An, or ride the **Reunification Express train (`SE1, SE3, SE5, SE7`, `110,000 VND to 180,000 VND` / `$4.40 to $7.20 USD`, `2.2 hours`)** from Ga Ha Noi to Ninh Binh Station.
- **Hanoi to Sa Pa (`315 km`):** Choose between the **daytime or overnight VIP 22-cabin sleeper bus (`350,000 VND to 450,000 VND` / `$14.00 to $18.00 USD`, `5.5 to 6.0 hours` via Expressway CT05 directly into Sa Pa town)** or the **overnight sleeper train (`SP3 / SP1` departing Hanoi Station Ga B at `22:00`, `450,000 VND to 850,000 VND` / `$18.00 to $34.00 USD` for a 4-berth soft sleeper, or `1,350,000–1,800,000 VND` / `$54.00–$72.00 USD` for a luxury Chapa/Sapaly/VicSapa wooden cabin)** arriving at Lao Cai Station at `05:55 AM`, followed by the **50-minute public Bus #01 (`40,000 VND` / `$1.60 USD`)** or shared minivan (`70,000 VND` / `$2.80 USD`) up QL4D to Sa Pa Church.
