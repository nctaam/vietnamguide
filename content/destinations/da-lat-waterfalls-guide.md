# Da Lat Waterfalls Guide: Top 5 Falls, Routes & Map (2026)

Perched on the Lang Biang Plateau at an elevation of 1,500 meters above sea level in Lam Dong Province, the highland city of **Da Lat** sits atop a stepped volcanic fault zone where the Cam Ly and Da Nhim rivers drop 400 meters to 700 meters toward the southern plains. Cool pine-scented air (averaging 15°C to 24°C year-round with 80% relative humidity) and 1,800 millimeters of annual rainfall feed Southeast Asia's most concentrated network of granite and basalt waterfalls within a 50-kilometer radius of Xuan Huong Lake.

Each cascade along the plateau rim serves a distinct travel style. Five kilometers south of Da Lat Market along the newly widened 4-lane **Prenn Pass** (National Route QL20), **Datanla Waterfall** operates as an engineered outdoor adventure center featuring a 2,400-meter Wiegand alpine coaster (250,000 VND / $10.00 USD) and UIAA-certified canyoning courses (1,950,000 VND / $78.00 USD). Thirty kilometers to 50 kilometers further out along Provincial Road DT725 and Highway QL20, **Elephant Falls** (*Thác Voi*) and the seven-tiered volcanic amphitheater of **Pongour Waterfall** (*Thác Pongour*) plunge across raw, unmanicured gorges surrounded by robusta coffee plantations and silk-weaving towns.

## 1. Datanla Waterfall: 2,400-Meter Alpine Coaster & Technical Canyoning

Positioned 5 kilometers south of central Da Lat (a 12-minute, **85,000 VND to 110,000 VND / $3.40 to $4.40 USD** GrabCar ride down the pine-lined Prenn Pass on Highway QL20), **Datanla Waterfall** drops through a seven-tier granite ravine at 1,380 meters altitude. Open daily from **07:00 to 17:00**, Datanla divides into a public upper sightseeing zone and a restricted lower canyon reserved for licensed abseiling expeditions.

### Datanla New Alpine Coaster (Track 3) vs Old Track (Track 1)
Instead of walking the 450 stone steps down the 120-meter ravine, 95% of visitors ride the **New Alpine Coaster (Track 3)** from the upper highway plaza. Spanning **2,400 meters** of stainless-steel tubular rails, it is the longest forest mountain coaster in Vietnam:
- **2026 Coaster Tariffs**: **250,000 VND ($10.00 USD)** for an adult round-trip ticket (or **170,000 VND / $6.80 USD** one-way); **150,000 VND ($6.00 USD)** for children between 1.0m and 1.4m.
- **Ride Mechanics**: Two-person sleds reach a maximum governed speed of **40 km/h**, fitted with electromagnetic proximity sensors that automatically brake if you come within 25 meters of the sled ahead.
- **High Rope Course & Zipline**: Adjacent to the coaster station, a 6-level treetop obstacle course (up to 20 meters above the pine floor) runs from 07:30 to 15:30 for **350,000 VND ($14.00 USD)** (or **280,000 VND / $11.20 USD** during the 07:30 to 08:30 early-bird hour), while the 1,500-meter forest zipline costs **800,000 VND to 1,000,000 VND ($32.00 to $40.00 USD)**.

### UIAA-Certified Canyoning Expeditions in Lower Datanla Gorge
Datanla's lower 3-kilometer canyon is the only government-licensed technical canyoning venue in Vietnam. Operated by certified outfitters (**Viet Action Tours**, **Highland Sport Travel**, and **Dalat Canyoning**), full 6-hour tours run from **08:00 to 14:30** for **1,950,000 VND to 2,250,000 VND ($78.00 to $90.00 USD)** per person (inclusive of hotel pickup, 3mm neoprene wetsuit, Petzl helmet and harness, GoPro photos, and picnic lunch). Your 5-kilometer river descent covers six stages:
1. **18-Meter Dry Practice Abseil**: 30 minutes of ground school learning figure-8 descender braking and hand-signal protocols.
2. **Tyrolean Traverse Zipline**: Sliding down a 50-meter tensioned rope straight into the river channel.
3. **Natural Granite Water Slides**: Sliding backward on your life vest down two water-polished 10-meter basalt chutes.
4. **25-Meter Main Waterfall Rappelling**: Abseiling straight through the roaring white curtain of the 25-meter main cascade before dropping the final 3 meters into the plunge pool.
5. **7-Meter & 11-Meter Free-Fall Cliff Jump**: Jumping from an overhanging granite ledge into a 6-meter-deep emerald basin.
6. **14-Meter "Washing Machine" Abseil**: The technical finale, where hydraulic spray spins rappellers inside a narrow rock cleft before flushing them out into the calm lower stream.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Arrival Timing at Datanla**: Tour buses from Nha Trang and Ho Chi Minh City pack the Alpine Coaster ticket booth between 09:15 and 11:30, pushing waiting lines past 45 minutes. Arrive at the Datanla gate between **07:15 and 08:15** to ride the 2,400-meter coaster twice with zero wait and capture mist-free photos of the main granite cascade.

## 2. Pongour Waterfall: The Seven-Tiered Basalt Amphitheater (50 km South)

Located 50 kilometers south of central Da Lat (65 minutes by motorbike or 50 minutes by car via Highway QL20 to the Km 260 marker in Tan Thanh Commune, Duc Trong District), **Pongour Waterfall** (*Thác Pongour*, or *Thác Bảy Tầng*) is the widest terraced cascade in the Central Highlands. Fed by the Da Nhim River below the Dai Ninh reservoir, the waterfall plunges **40 meters** down seven horizontal steps of hexagonal volcanic basalt stretching **100 meters** across a semicircular gorge.

### Walking Trails, Electric Shuttles & Seasonal Water Flow
- **Tickets & Operating Hours**: Open daily from **07:00 to 17:00**. Entry costs **40,000 VND ($1.60 USD)** for adults and **20,000 VND ($0.80 USD)** for children. Motorbike parking at the upper pavilion costs **5,000 VND ($0.20 USD)**.
- **Getting Down to the Riverbed**: From the ticket gate, a shaded 800-meter stone staircase winds downhill for **12 to 15 minutes** through old-growth dipterocarp trees and wild orchids. Alternatively, 14-seat electric buggies shuttle visitors down the 1.2-kilometer paved service road in 4 minutes for **20,000 VND ($0.80 USD)** one-way or **30,000 VND ($1.20 USD)** round-trip.
- **Seasonal Discharge Dynamics**: Between **July and November**, monsoon rains release a booming 100-meter-wide wall of white water that fills the basin with cool spray 60 meters away. During the dry season from **January through April**, upstream hydroelectric gates divert part of the Da Nhim flow, turning the seven basalt ledges into delicate silver streams where you can walk across the flat volcanic stone shelves to the center of the amphitheater.

## 3. Elephant Falls (*Thác Voi*) & The 71-Meter Lady Buddha at Linh An Pagoda

Situated 30 kilometers southwest of Da Lat in Nam Ban Town, Lam Ha District (a 50-minute ride over the scenic **Ta Nung Pass** on Provincial Road DT725), **Elephant Falls** (*Thác Voi*, or *Liêng Rơwoa* in the indigenous K'Ho language) drops **30 meters** over a sheer 40-meter-wide basalt cliff. Massive moss-covered boulders at the bottom of the gorge resemble a herd of kneeling wild elephants emerging from the mist.

### Current Access Status, Wind Cave (*Hang Gió*) & Linh An Pagoda Vantage Point
Historically, visitors paid **30,000 VND ($1.20 USD)** at the lower ticket booth to clamber down 145 steep, spray-slickened rock steps into the dark cavern of **Wind Cave** (*Hang Gió*) right behind the waterfall curtain.
- **2026 Safety & Gate Advisory**: Following rockfall erosion along the lower iron handrail steps, Lam Ha District authorities periodically close the bottom staircase gate for structural reinforcement. Even when the lower cave path is gated, you get a commanding, mist-soaked view of the entire 30-meter cascade for **0 VND ($0.00 USD)** by entering the courtyard of **Linh Ẩn Pagoda** (*Chùa Linh Ẩn*) right next door (open **06:30 to 17:30**).
- **The 71-Meter Guan Yin (Lady Buddha) Statue**: Completed inside the Linh An temple grounds overlooking Elephant Falls, this **71-meter-tall** white statue of Bodhisattva Avalokiteshvara is the tallest in Lam Dong Province. Walk down the pagoda's lower bonsai garden terrace (50 meters from the cliff edge) to feel the waterfall spray and hear the roar echoing across the valley.
- **Cuong Hoan Traditional Silk Factory**: Situated 400 meters from Linh An Pagoda on DT725 (open 07:30 to 17:00, entry **20,000 VND / $0.80 USD**), this working 30-Year silk mill shows silkworm cocoons boiling in 80°C vats, mechanical reeling looms spinning raw thread, and hand-dyed silk scarves (250,000 VND to 600,000 VND / $10.00 to $24.00 USD).

## 4. Prenn Waterfall & Dambri Waterfall (Southern Highway QL20)

### Prenn Waterfall (10 km South at Foot of Prenn Pass)
Sitting directly at the bottom of the 10-kilometer Prenn Pass (open **07:30 to 17:00**, entry **50,000 VND / $2.00 USD** adult, **25,000 VND / $1.00 USD** child), **Prenn Waterfall** drops 10 meters over a curved sandstone lip into a botanical valley at 1,180 meters elevation. A wide concrete promenade passes directly *behind* the falling water sheet, letting families and seniors photograph through the cascade without climbing slick boulders. Optional park rides include a short 300-meter cross-canyon cable car (**50,000 VND / $2.00 USD**) and rowboat rentals (**60,000 VND / $2.40 USD**).

### Dambri Waterfall (130 km Southwest in Bao Loc)
If you are traveling overland between Da Lat and Ho Chi Minh City along Highway QL20, detour 18 kilometers northwest from Bao Loc City (130 kilometers from Da Lat) to **Dambri Waterfall** (*Thác Dambri*, open **07:00 to 17:00**). Plunging an unbroken **60 meters** down a sheer volcanic cliff inside 300 hectares of primary forest, Dambri is the tallest waterfall in Lam Dong. The **200,000 VND ($8.00 USD)** adult all-in ticket (**100,000 VND / $4.00 USD** child) includes rides on the **50-meter glass cliffside elevator**, a **1,650-meter alpine coaster** winding down to the lower Dasara falls, and a 36-meter Ferris wheel.

### Transport Costs: Self-Drive Scooter vs Private 8-Hour Car Charter
- **125cc Motorbike Rental**: **130,000 VND to 180,000 VND ($5.20 to $7.20 USD)** per 24 hours (Honda AirBlade, Vision, or semi-automatic Wave/Blade) from hotels near Da Lat Market. Adding 3.5 liters of RON 95 petrol (**80,000 VND / $3.20 USD**) covers the entire 115-kilometer loop. Note that motorcycles are allowed on the rebuilt 4-lane **Prenn Pass** (speed limit 50–60 km/h) and **Ta Nung Pass**, but are banned from the fenced Lien Khuong – Prenn toll expressway (where cars pay a **36,000 VND to 40,000 VND / $1.44 to $1.60 USD** toll); follow blue motorbike lane signs onto parallel National Highway QL20 near Lien Khuong Airport.
- **Private 4-Seat or 7-Seat Car Charter**: Booking a private car with driver through **Lado Taxi** (`0263 3666 777`), **GrabCar Charter**, or your hotel desk for an 8-hour, 120-kilometer 3-waterfall loop (Datanla, Elephant/Linh An, and Pongour) costs **950,000 VND to 1,300,000 VND ($38.00 to $52.00 USD)** total, including fuel, waiting time, and expressway tolls. Divided among 3 or 4 travelers, that works out to just **250,000 VND to 325,000 VND ($10.00 to $13.00 USD)** per person.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which waterfall is best if I only have half a day in Da Lat?
Choose **Datanla Waterfall** (located just 5 kilometers or 12 minutes south of Da Lat Market on Prenn Pass). You can ride the 2,400-meter Alpine Coaster 3 for **250,000 VND ($10.00 USD)**, walk the pine-shaded granite tiers, and return to town within 2.5 hours. If you have 4 hours and want a massive natural cascade rather than theme-park rides, take a private taxi 50 kilometers south to **Pongour Waterfall** (entry **40,000 VND / $1.60 USD**).

### Is the lower staircase at Elephant Falls open in 2026?
The steep rock staircase descending to the base of **Elephant Falls** and Wind Cave (*Hang Gió*) is frequently closed by Lam Ha safety inspectors due to slippery moss and rockfall hazards. However, you should still include Nam Ban on your loop: walking through the adjacent **Linh An Pagoda** (entry **0 VND / $0.00 USD**) gives you direct cliffside views of the roaring 30-meter cascade alongside the 71-meter white Lady Buddha statue.

### Is canyoning at Datanla safe for first-time beginners?
Yes, when booked with one of the licensed Da Lat adventure operators (**1,950,000 VND to 2,250,000 VND / $78.00 to $90.00 USD**). Every group completes 30 minutes of dry-cliff abseil instruction with two certified guides (one controlling a top safety belay rope and one stationed in the plunge pool below) using CE/UIAA-tested harnesses and helmets. You must be comfortable swimming in deep water with a buoyancy vest.

### What is the best month to photograph Pongour and Datanla Waterfalls?
Visit between **September and December** (late rainy season into early dry season). During this 4-month window, upstream highland reservoirs remain full so Pongour's entire 100-meter basalt amphitheater roars with white water, while morning skies stay crisp and sunny (18°C to 22°C) before afternoon clouds build.
