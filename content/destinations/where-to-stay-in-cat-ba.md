# Where to Stay on Cat Ba Island (2026): Best Areas

Spanning 260 km² of rugged limestone peaks, subtropical valleys, and sheltered coves, Cat Ba Island is the largest island in the Gulf of Tonkin and a designated UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. Most travelers come here for one reason: boarding an early kayak and wooden boat excursion from Ben Beo Pier into **Lan Ha Bay**. This 7,000-hectare seascape of over 300 vegetated karst towers and white-sand beaches receives a fraction of central Ha Long Bay's cruise ship crowds.

Picking a hotel on Cat Ba in 2026 demands current street-level awareness. Over the past two years, a massive central bay land-reclamation project has transformed the inner harbor directly opposite Cat Ba Town's main promenade (1 Thang 4 Street). Book an outdated "sea-view" listing without checking its exact location and your balcony may face construction infill rather than open water. Below is the realistic 2026 guide to five distinct accommodation zones across the island and bay, complete with VND and USD room rates, Hanoi combo bus transfer logistics, and noise warnings.

## 1. Cat Ba Town Southeast Waterfront: 1 Thang 4 & Nui Ngoc Streets

Roughly three-quarters of independent visitors base themselves in **Cat Ba Town** at the southeastern corner of the island. Transit logistics run like clockwork here. Integrated bus-plus-speedboat or ferry operators from Hanoi's Old Quarter—led by **Cat Ba Express, Good Morning Cat Ba, and Daiichi Travel**—complete the journey in 3.5 hours via the CT04 Expressway and the Dong Bai–Cai Vieng crossing (or the 3,955m Phu Long cable car, 100,000 to 150,000 VND standalone ticket). Total combo tickets cost 320,000 to 380,000 VND ($12.80 to $15.20 USD) per person and drop you right at your hotel lobby on **1 Thang 4 Street** or **Nui Ngoc Street**.

Nui Ngoc Street runs one block behind the promenade and acts as the island's dining and service hub. Everything is walkable. Within 300 meters, you can eat at popular traveler spots (**Yummy Restaurant**, **Quiri Pub & Cocktail Bar**, and **Casa Bonita**), withdraw cash at Agribank or VietinBank ATMs, and rent automatic scooters for 120,000 to 150,000 VND ($4.80 to $6 USD) per day. Mid-range hotels along Nui Ngoc and southern 1 Thang 4 charge 600,000 to 1,800,000 VND ($24 to $72 USD) per night.

Morning boat trips start just around the headland. **Ben Beo Pier (Bến Bèo)** sits 1.5 km east of town—a 5-minute, 30,000 VND ($1.20 USD) ride by open-air electric buggy—and serves as the departure point for 08:00 Lan Ha Bay full-day kayak and Dark & Bright Cave boat tours (650,000 to 950,000 VND / $26 to $38 USD including seafood lunch and hotel pickup). Before booking along the harbor, ask the property for a 2026 balcony photo or pick a hillside floor on southern Nui Ngoc so your room looks above the central promenade rooftops.

## 2. Cat Co 1, 2 & 3 Cliffside Beach Coves: Luxury Resorts & Karst Panoramas

Just 1.5 km south of Cat Ba Town via a scenic cliffside coastal walkway, three sandy coves—**Cat Co 1, Cat Co 2, and Cat Co 3**—curve into steep limestone headlands facing open Lan Ha Bay. Because these beaches look south toward offshore karst islands rather than the inner harbor, they enjoy unobstructed turquoise water views free from central bay reclamation work.

Cat Ba's premier swimming beaches and five-star cliff resorts occupy these coves. Spanning the headlands above Cat Co 1 and Cat Co 2, **Flamingo Cat Ba Resorts** features green planted facades, heated indoor pools, and Japanese onsen mineral baths for 2,800,000 to 5,200,000 VND ($112 to $208 USD) per night. Over on Cat Co 3 Beach, **Hôtel Perle d'Orient Cat Ba (MGallery)** delivers French-Indochine luxury, tiered infinity pools, and private beach loungers for 3,500,000 to 6,200,000 VND ($140 to $248 USD) per night. Electric buggies and taxis wait outside both resorts, reaching Nui Ngoc restaurants in 4 minutes (50,000 VND) and Ben Beo Pier in 8 minutes (80,000 VND). Sea temperatures stay warm at 27°C to 30°C from May through October, making Cat Co ideal for early morning swims before afternoon day-trippers arrive from town.

## 3. Tung Dinh Street & Cannon Fort Hillside: Quiet Lake & Hill Views

Inland behind Nui Ngoc Street and curving along the shore of **Tung Dinh Lake** at the base of Cannon Fort Hill (`Pháo Đài Thần Công`), Tung Dinh Street provides a peaceful refuge from waterfront noise. During summer weekends between May and August, seafood halls and karaoke bars on central 1 Thang 4 Street blast music until 23:30. Walk 400 meters over to Tung Dinh Street and night noise vanishes.

Boutique hotels, family guesthouses, and social hostels overlooking Tung Dinh Lake charge 450,000 to 1,200,000 VND ($18 to $48 USD) per night. You stay within an 8-minute walk of Nui Ngoc dining and tour offices while sleeping beside quiet karst cliffs. Note that the summit of Cannon Fort itself remains closed to visitors in 2026; ask your receptionist for current viewpoint access before climbing the hill road.

## 4. Viet Hai Ancient Village & Xuan Dam: Car-Free National Park Eco-Lodges

Want total immersion inside the UNESCO Biosphere Reserve? Leave town behind and sleep inside **Cat Ba National Park**, primary forest habitat of the critically endangered Golden-headed Cat Ba Langur (fewer than 80 individuals remain in the wild).

Deep inside a limestone valley surrounded by primary jungle sits **Viet Hai Ancient Village**. No public car road reaches this enclave. You arrive either via a 45-minute boat ride from Ben Beo Pier across Lan Ha Bay to Viet Hai Dock (followed by a 5 km electric buggy or bicycle ride into the valley) or by trekking the rugged **12 km jungle trail** (4 to 5 hours) from National Park HQ (120,000 VND entrance ticket) over Ngu Lam Peak. Inside the car-free village, bamboo bungalows at **Whisper Nature** and **Viet Hai Homestay** cost 500,000 to 1,300,000 VND ($20 to $52 USD) per night. If you prefer road access, book **Cat Ba Eco Lodge** in quiet **Xuan Dam** (12 km northwest of town, 180,000 VND taxi), where timber chalets in an orchard valley run 900,000 to 1,900,000 VND ($36 to $76 USD) per night near Hospital Cave (80,000 VND).

> [!TIP]
> **1 Night Land + 1 Night Boat Strategy:** With 2 nights available, book **Night 1** at a Cat Co cliff resort or Tung Dinh hotel to hike Cat Ba National Park (`Ngu Lam Peak`) in the morning, then board an **overnight Lan Ha Bay boutique cruise** from Ben Beo Pier at `11:30` on Day 2 (`$135–$240 USD` per person) to wake up among the silent karsts of **Three Peaches Beach (`Ba Trái Đào`)**.

## 5. Overnight Lan Ha Bay Boutique Cruises: Sleeping on the Bay

Travelers debating between two nights on land in Cat Ba Town versus one night aboard a boat should weigh the after-hours calm of **Lan Ha Bay**. Embarking from **Ben Beo Pier** (or Got / Tuan Chau marinas), smaller **10-to-25-cabin wooden and steel junks** sail past day-boat crowds to drop anchor in secluded southern lagoons near **Three Peaches Beach (Ba Trái Đào)** and **Viet Hai**.

Overnight cruise packages cost 3,400,000 to 6,000,000 VND ($135 to $240 USD) per person all-inclusive. That price bundles an en-suite ocean-view cabin, four seafood meals, sunset and dawn kayaking through hidden lagoons, and squid fishing. While a town hotel gives you cheaper evening street food and bar options, sleeping on the water lets you kayak mirror-still karsts at 06:30 before a single day boat leaves Ben Beo harbor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the fastest way from Hanoi Old Quarter to Cat Ba Island hotels in 2026?
Book an integrated **bus + speedboat (or cable car) combo ticket** (320,000 to 380,000 VND / $12.80 to $15.20 USD, 3.5 hours total) with **Cat Ba Express, Good Morning Cat Ba, or Daiichi Travel**. Their coach picks you up in Hanoi's Old Quarter, drives along the CT04 Expressway to Dong Bai Terminal (where you cross by 7-minute speedboat or the 3,955m Phu Long cable car, 100,000 to 150,000 VND), and transfers you to an island bus that drops you directly at your Cat Ba Town or Cat Co hotel.

### How do I avoid the waterfront construction in Cat Ba Town in 2026?
Skip mid-strip hotels directly on central **1 Thang 4 Street**, which face the central bay land-reclamation project. Instead, book a cliffside resort on **Cat Co 1, 2, or 3 Beaches** (1.5 km south), a quiet lakeside room on **Tung Dinh Street**, or an elevated hotel on the southern **Nui Ngoc hillside** looking south toward Lan Ha Bay.

### How do I reach Ben Beo Pier from my hotel for morning Lan Ha Bay boat tours?
**Ben Beo Pier (Bến Bèo)** sits 1.5 km east of Cat Ba Town and 2.5 km from Cat Co Beaches. If you book a full-day Lan Ha Bay kayak and boat tour (650,000 to 950,000 VND, departing at 08:00), operators include free hotel pickup between 07:30 and 07:50. Otherwise, hail a green electric buggy (30,000 VND per person, 5 minutes) from Nui Ngoc Street.

### Should I bring cash if I stay in Viet Hai Ancient Village?
Yes. While hotels and restaurants in Cat Ba Town and Cat Co Resorts accept Visa/Mastercard and have ATMs along Nui Ngoc and 1 Thang 4 streets, **Viet Hai Village** has zero ATM machines and intermittent card terminal connectivity. Withdraw 1,500,000 to 2,500,000 VND ($60 to $100 USD) in town before boarding the 45-minute boat from Ben Beo or starting the 12 km National Park trek.
