# Where to Stay in Mui Ne: Beach Resorts & Coast Areas

Stretching across an 18 km arc of palm-fringed shoreline between Phan Thiet City and the red sand headlands of Hon Rom, Mui Ne no longer requires a five-hour highway crawl behind slow freight trucks. Since the opening of the 99 km Dau Giay–Phan Thiet Expressway (CT01), door-to-door limousine vans from Ho Chi Minh City reach the beach strip in 2.5 to 3 hours for 200,000 to 350,000 VND ($8 to $14 USD) per seat. Rail passengers can instead board the morning SPT2 train departing Saigon Station at 06:40 and arriving at Phan Thiet Station at 10:35 for 185,000 to 265,000 VND ($7.40 to $10.60 USD), followed by a 20-minute taxi ride (180,000 VND) east to the coast.

Geography governs every hotel booking along this bay. Most visitors assume "Mui Ne" refers to a single compact beach town, yet the coastal corridor actually spans four distinct wards linked by a single two-lane road that changes names from Nguyen Thong to Nguyen Dinh Chieu and finally Huynh Thuc Khang. Pick the wrong street number and you may find concrete seawalls instead of dry sand outside your bungalow.

> [!WARNING]
> **The Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street Number & Seawall Erosion Rule:** Odd-numbered addresses sit on the inland (mountain) side of Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street and require crossing two-way traffic to reach the water, while even-numbered addresses sit directly on the beach side. More importantly, coastal currents have eroded the sand along Central Ham Tien (addresses #110 to #170 Nguyen Dinh Chieu), where waves slap directly against a concrete seawall during high tide. Book between addresses #1 and #100 in West Ham Tien if all-day dry sand for swimming matters to your trip.

## West Ham Tien (#1 to #100 Nguyen Dinh Chieu): Best Wide-Sand Beach & Low-Rise Resorts

Protected by the rocky Phu Hai headland jutting into the South China Sea immediately to the west, longshore drift deposits a wide ribbon of fine sand from address #1 to #100 Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street. Even during peak winter high tides, guests walk 50 to 80 meters across dry sand before reaching the waterline. Strict municipal height limits kept tall concrete towers out of this zone.

Mature coconut groves instead shade low-rise Cham-roof bungalows and Indochine villas right on the sand. Flagship properties here include **The Anam Mui Ne** (#18 Nguyen Dinh Chieu, 3,800,000 to 6,500,000 VND / $152 to $260 USD), **Anantara Mui Ne Resort** (#12A Nguyen Dinh Chieu, 4,100,000 to 6,200,000 VND / $164 to $248 USD), **Sailing Club Resort Mui Ne** (#24 Nguyen Dinh Chieu, 3,200,000 to 5,100,000 VND / $128 to $204 USD), **Bamboo Village Beach Resort & Spa** (#38 Nguyen Dinh Chieu, 2,200,000 to 3,600,000 VND / $88 to $144 USD), and **Coco Beach Resort** (#58 Nguyen Dinh Chieu, 2,400,000 to 3,900,000 VND / $96 to $156 USD).

Seasons split this shoreline into two distinct moods. From November through March, dry northeast thermal winds accelerate after 11:00 AM to a steady 18 to 25 knots. IKO-certified kitesurfing schools line the beach, charging 1,600,000 to 2,100,000 VND ($64 to $84 USD) per hour for private instruction. Early risers still get calm swimming water from 06:00 AM until 10:30 AM before whitecaps build.

## Central & East Ham Tien (#101 to #190+ Nguyen Dinh Chieu): Budget Guesthouses, Bo Ke Seafood & Fairy Stream

Proceeding east past address #100 Nguyen Dinh Chieu, manicured resort walls give way to a walkable strip of surf shops, bakeries, scooter rental garages, and open-air seafood grills. Backpackers and digital nomads gravitate here for lower room rates and active evening street life. Clean air-conditioned rooms on the inland side or courtyard hotels on the sea side cost 550,000 to 1,500,000 VND ($22 to $60 USD) per night at dependable bases such as **iHome Backpacker Resort**, **Mui Ne Hills Budget Hotel** (set on the sand hill 300 meters inland, 650,000 to 1,200,000 VND), and **Poshanu Resort** (#80–#100 border zone).

Dinner revolves around the **Bo Ke (Bờ Kè) seawall strip** between addresses #118 and #140 Nguyen Dinh Chieu. Plastic tables sit right above the crashing surf while vendors grill live scallops with scallion oil for 120,000 VND ($4.80 USD) per dozen, steamed flower crab for 380,000 VND ($15.20 USD) per kilogram, and garlic-butter lobster for 950,000 VND ($38.00 USD) per kilogram.

At the eastern edge of Ham Tien (around #180 to #190 Nguyen Dinh Chieu), a concrete bridge crosses the **Fairy Stream (Suối Tiên)**. Lodging within a 10-minute walk of this bridge lets you enter the ankle-deep red-and-white sandstone canyon at 06:30 AM for the 15,000 VND ($0.60 USD) ticket, beating the 08:30 AM tour buses. Because high tide hits the concrete seawall along #110 to #170, book a hotel with a raised beachfront garden and pool, or walk 10 minutes west to address #90 whenever you want wide dry sand for swimming.

## Phu Hai Headland (Km 8–9 Nguyen Thong): Elevated Cliffs & Cultural Landmarks

Before Nguyen Thong Street descends into West Ham Tien, it climbs over the basalt headland of Phu Hai. Hotels here trade continuous flat beach access for elevated sea views, rocky coves, and quick access to Phan Thiet's historic sights. Within 5 minutes by taxi, you can visit the 8th-century **Po Sah Inu Cham Towers** (15,000 VND / $0.60 USD ticket) or tour the **Fish Sauce Museum (Làng Chài Xưa)** on Nguyen Thong Street (100,000 VND / $4.00 USD admission).

Top resorts here include **Victoria Phan Thiet Beach Resort & Spa** (coral-stone bungalows for 2,600,000 to 4,200,000 VND / $104 to $168 USD) and **Phu Hai Beach Resort & Spa** (1,800,000 to 3,100,000 VND / $72 to $124 USD). Across the road on the hillside overlooking the bay, **Centara Mirage Resort Mui Ne** (2,100,000 to 3,800,000 VND / $84 to $152 USD) caters to families with a large waterpark complex and lazy river, while the 18-hole **Sea Links Golf & Country Club** sits immediately uphill (green fees from 2,400,000 VND / $96 USD).

## Mui Ne Fishing Village, Hon Rom & Suoi Nuoc (Huynh Thuc Khang Street, 8–15 km East)

Driving 3 km east past the Fairy Stream along Huynh Thuc Khang Street brings you around the sheltered western hook of the peninsula into **Mui Ne Fishing Village**. Hundreds of round basket boats (`thuyền thúng`) and wooden trawlers bob in the calm anchorage. Every morning at 05:45 AM, fishermen haul nets of silver anchovies, squid, and crabs onto the wet sand below the harbor viewpoint.

Continuing 2 km past the harbor leads to the **Red Sand Dunes (Đồi Cát Đỏ)** and the outer bay of **Hon Rom and Suoi Nuoc** (8 to 15 km east of Ham Tien). Photographers favor this eastern sector for 04:30 AM private 4x4 Jeep departures (550,000 to 650,000 VND / $22 to $26 USD per four-seat jeep) to **Bau Trang (White Sand Dunes)**, located 35 km northeast up the coastal road (15,000 to 20,000 VND gate ticket plus 800,000 VND / $32 USD for a 20-minute dune Land Cruiser ride). Sleeping in Hon Rom cuts 25 minutes off that pre-dawn drive.

Accommodation here splits between spacious resorts and quiet homestays:
* **Pandanus Resort** (#3 Nguyen Huu Tho, beside the Red Sand Dunes): A 10-hectare garden resort with a huge freeform pool, daily market walking tours, and rates of 1,800,000 to 3,200,000 VND ($72 to $128 USD).
* **Muine Bay Resort** and **Sailing Bay Beach Resort**: Quiet bayfront complexes along the Hon Rom curve costing 1,400,000 to 2,700,000 VND ($56 to $108 USD) per night.
* **Suoi Nuoc Homestays & Surf Camps**: Simple family-run bungalows costing 450,000 to 950,000 VND ($18 to $38 USD) per night.

## Tien Thanh & Ke Ga South Coast (20–30 km South of Phan Thiet)

Drivers exiting the Phan Thiet intersection of the CT01 Expressway often turn south onto coastal Route DT719 toward **Tien Thanh** and **Ke Ga Bay** (20 to 30 km southwest of Phan Thiet). This peaceful casuarina-lined coast ends at the granite **Ke Ga Lighthouse**, built in 1899 on a rocky islet reachable via a 10-minute boat ride (100,000 VND / $4.00 USD round-trip). Top properties include **Radisson Resort Phan Thiet** (2,400,000 to 4,100,000 VND / $96 to $164 USD) and **Azerai Ke Ga Bay** (6,200,000 to 9,500,000 VND / $248 to $380 USD).

## How to Choose & Book Your Mui Ne Base: Step-by-Step Checklist

1. **Check the exact street number on Nguyen Dinh Chieu:** Confirm whether the address is an even number (beachside) or odd number (inland side across the road), and target #1 to #100 for wide dry sand at high tide.
2. **Match your dates to the wind calendar:** From November to March, strong afternoon winds make West Ham Tien ideal for kitesurfers but choppy for casual swimmers after 11:00 AM; April to October brings calmer seas.
3. **Avoid weekend expressway surges:** Because Ho Chi Minh City residents drive here in 2.5 hours via CT01, Friday and Saturday night resort rates jump 25% to 40%.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which side of Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street is directly on the beach?
Even-numbered addresses (#2 through #190 Nguyen Dinh Chieu) sit directly on the ocean side of the road. Odd-numbered addresses (#1 through #189) sit on the inland side; while they cost 30% to 45% less, you must cross the road and walk through public beach lanes.

### Is it better to stay in Ham Tien or Mui Ne Fishing Village?
Book **West or Central Ham Tien** (#1 to #140 Nguyen Dinh Chieu) if you want walkable access to restaurants, specialty coffee shops, spas, and wide sandy swimming beaches. Choose **Mui Ne Fishing Village or Hon Rom** only if you prefer an all-inclusive pool resort or a quiet base right beside the morning harbor market and Red Sand Dunes.

### How much does it cost to get around Mui Ne between hotels and the sand dunes?
Metered Mai Linh and Sun Taxis charge 14,000 to 16,000 VND ($0.55 to $0.65 USD) per kilometer, making a 6 km hop along Nguyen Dinh Chieu cost 85,000 to 100,000 VND ($3.40 to $4.00 USD). Local Bus #1 also runs the entire strip every 30 minutes from 05:30 AM to 06:30 PM for 13,000 to 35,000 VND ($0.50 to $1.40 USD), while 125cc scooters rent for 150,000 to 200,000 VND ($6.00 to $8.00 USD) per day.
