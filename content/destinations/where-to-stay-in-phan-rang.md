# Where to Stay in Phan Rang (2026): Hotels & Resorts

Ringed by a horseshoe of granite mountains that block inland rain clouds, [Phan Rang](/destinations/phan-rang/) (`Phan Rang-Tháp Chàm`, capital of Ninh Thuan Province) sits inside Vietnam's driest and sunniest coastal basin. While the rest of central Vietnam deals with heavy autumn deluges, Phan Rang records just `700 to 800 mm` of annual rainfall across 300+ days of bright sunshine, feeding sprawling table-grape vineyards, salt pans, and cactus-studded sand dunes.

Geography and wind dictate where you should book your hotel. **Cam Ranh International Airport (`CXR`)** lies 60 km north (1 hour 15 minutes via National Highway `QL1A` or the scenic coastal road `DT702`, 550,000 to 750,000 VND / $22 to $30 USD by private taxi), while **Thap Cham Railway Station** (`Ga Tháp Chàm`) sits 7 km west of the city center, welcoming daily North–South **Reunification Express** (`SE1/SE2`, `SE3/SE4`, `SE21/SE22`) trains from Ho Chi Minh City (5.5 to 6 hours, 320,000 to 650,000 VND / $13 to $26 USD) and [Nha Trang](/destinations/nha-trang/) (2 hours, 110,000 to 220,000 VND). Meanwhile, the dramatic granite cove of [Vinh Hy](/destinations/vinh-hy/) sits 38 km northeast along coastal road `DT702` (45 minutes by car or rented motorbike), and highland [Da Lat](/destinations/da-lat/) rises 110 km northwest over the 1,500-meter Ngoan Muc Pass (`Đèo Ngoạn Mục`, `QL27`).

Within Phan Rang itself, accommodations divide into four distinct zones ranging from `350,000 VND` ($14 USD) downtown city hotels to `4,500,000 VND` ($180 USD) beachfront pool villas along **Ninh Chu Beach**.

> **Field Note:** Respect Phan Rang's seasonal wind calendar before choosing your neighborhood. From **November through March**, the Northeast Monsoon (`Gió mùa Đông Bắc`) accelerates through the Ninh Thuan coastal funnel, generating relentless **25 to 35 knot** (`45 to 65 km/h`) cross-onshore winds every afternoon. Those gales make **My Hoa Lagoon** (12 km northeast on `DT702`) Asia's premier flat-water kiteboarding arena, but they also whip stinging sand across exposed beach loungers on **Ninh Chu Beach**. For calm, mirror-flat ocean swimming at Ninh Chu and Binh Son, visit between **April and September**, or book a resort with a wind-sheltered courtyard pool during winter.

## 1. Ninh Chu Beach Crescent (`Yen Ninh` Street)

Curving north from the Binh Son beachfront toward Tri Thuy Bridge and Dam Nai Lagoon (`Đầm Nại`), the 10 km arc of **Ninh Chu Beach** (`Bãi biển Ninh Chữ`) along tree-lined **Yen Ninh Street** (`Đường Yên Ninh`) is Phan Rang's premier resort strip. Casuarina pines (`Dương liễu`) and coconut palms buffer the beachfront properties from the road, and the seabed slopes so gradually that bathers can wade 80 meters offshore in waist-deep water.

### Best Resorts & Beach Villas on Ninh Chu Beach

* **Hoan My Resort Ninh Chu** (**1,800,000–4,500,000 VND** / **$72–$180 USD** per night; Yen Ninh Street): The benchmark four-star-plus resort on the crescent, featuring 120 modern hotel rooms and 32 walled beachfront pool villas (**3,200,000–4,500,000 VND** / **$128–$180 USD**), a huge saltwater horizon pool facing the East Sea, a full spa, and a reliable buffet breakfast. Book a **Beachfront Villa** if traveling between November and March so your private courtyard wall blocks the afternoon monsoon gusts.
* **Saigon Ninh Chu Hotel & Resort** (**1,300,000–2,600,000 VND** / **$52–$104 USD** per night; 19 An Duong Vuong, North Ninh Chu): Set at the northern tip of the crescent where the beach meets the fishing boat channel of Dam Nai Lagoon, this spacious four-star property offers panoramic sea-view balconies from every floor, two tennis courts, and quiet palm lawns away from city traffic.
* **TTC Resort Ninh Thuan** (**1,100,000–2,300,000 VND** / **$44–$92 USD** per night; Yen Ninh Street): Spread across 7 hectares of manicured gardens, TTC blends terracotta Champa-inspired brick bungalows (`Brang` architecture) with an oceanfront swimming pool and family water park access.
* **Long Thuan Hotel & Resort** (**950,000–1,900,000 VND** / **$38–$76 USD** per night; 01 Yen Ninh): A large, budget-friendly beachfront complex bridging Ninh Chu and Binh Son with 24-hour electric buggies and spacious family rooms.
* **Con Ga Vang Resort (Golden Rooster Resort)** (**1,050,000–2,100,000 VND** / **$42–$84 USD** per night; Yen Ninh Street): Set across 4 hectares of mature casuarina trees right beside TTC Resort, offering single-story garden cottages with private verandas 60 meters from the tide line.

## 2. Binh Son Marine Park (`16 Thang 4` Avenue East)

Where the city's central arterial boulevard—**16 Thang 4 Avenue** (`Đường 16 Tháng 4`)—terminates at the ocean, **Binh Son Marine Park** (`Công viên Biển Bình Sơn`) forms Phan Rang's lively urban seafront. Over the past three years, three-star and four-star mini-hotels, serviced apartments, and seafood beer gardens have popped up along the grid of streets (`Tran Quang Dieu`, `Muoi Sau Thang Tu`, and southern `Yen Ninh`) within 300 meters of the sand.

### Best Mid-Range Hotels & Weekend Acoustic Trade-Offs

* **Ninh Chu 2 Guesthouse & Boutique Mini-Hotels** (**550,000–1,400,000 VND** / **$22–$56 USD** per night): Modern six-story hotels such as **Sunbay Park Annex**, **Devesun Hotel**, **Nu Hoang Hotel**, and **Chau Thanh Hotel** offer elevator access, rooftop plunge pools, and crisp air-conditioned doubles for half the price of the big Yen Ninh resorts. Many rent 125cc Honda Air Blade scooters at the front desk for **130,000 to 150,000 VND** (**$5.20 to $6.00 USD**) per day so you can ride coastal route `DT702` up to Vinh Hy Bay.
* **Seafood Dining at Your Doorstep:** Staying around Binh Son lets you walk 150 meters to open-air coastal restaurants (**Hải Sản Phố Biển**, **Quán Ốc Nhớ**, and the night stalls along `Yen Ninh`) where grilled sea urchin (**35,000–45,000 VND** each), steamed moon crab (**450,000 VND/kg**), garlic-butter razor clams (**140,000 VND**), and crispy clay-pot rice (**120,000 VND**) are served until midnight.

**The Weekend Noise Trade-Off:** Because Binh Son Marine Park is the main evening gathering spot for Phan Rang locals, Friday through Sunday nights bring loud portable karaoke speakers (`Kẹo kéo`), youth dance rehearsals, and municipal stage concerts that echo across the beachfront until 22:30. Light sleepers booking near Binh Son Park should request an upper-floor room (floors 4 to 7) facing away from the main park plaza.

## 3. Central Phan Rang-Thap Cham (`Thong Nhat` Street & Cham Heritage Hub)

Three to five kilometers inland from the coast, downtown **Phan Rang-Thap Cham** centers around the commercial crosshairs of **Thong Nhat Street** (`Đường Thống Nhất`), **16 Thang 4 Square** (`Quảng trường 16 Tháng 4`), and **Ngo Gia Tu Street**.

### Why Base in Downtown Phan Rang?

If your primary focus is Cham civilization, train logistics, and authentic Ninh Thuan street food rather than all-day beach lounging, central town hotels (**350,000–900,000 VND** / **$14–$36 USD** per night, led by **Hoan My City Hotel**, **Anh Xuan Hotel**, **Phong Lan Hotel**, and **Minh Quan Hotel**) offer three decisive advantages:

1. **Fast Access to Po Klong Garai Cham Towers & Thap Cham Station:** The red-brick 13th-century temple complex of **Po Klong Garai** (`Tháp Po Klong Garai`, entrance fee `20,000 VND` / `$0.80 USD` plus `15,000 VND` electric shuttle, open 07:00–17:30) crowns Trau Hill (`Đồi Trầu`) 7 km northwest of downtown and 1.5 km from **Thap Cham Railway Station** (`Ga Tháp Chàm`). Basing on `Thong Nhat` cuts your early-morning taxi ride to the station or towers to 10 minutes (`80,000–110,000 VND` / `$3.20–$4.40 USD`).
2. **Traditional Craft Villages & Sand Dunes:** From the `Thong Nhat` intersection, you sit 9 km south of **Bau Truc Cham Pottery Village** (`Làng gốm Bàu Trúc`) and **My Nghiep Brocade Weaving Village** (`Làng dệt Mỹ Nghiệp`), and 13 km southeast of **Nam Cuong Sand Dunes** (`Đồi cát Nam Cương`, best visited at 05:30 AM sunrise, `0 VND` entry).
3. **Phan Rang Culinary Classics:** Within two blocks of `16 Thang 4 Square` and `Ngo Gia Tu`, family stalls serve clay-mold rice pancakes dipped in braised mackerel broth (`Bánh căn / Bánh xèo Phan Rang` at **Quán Hải** or **Quán Quê Hương**, `35,000–50,000 VND` / `$1.40–$2.00 USD`), fish-cake rice noodles (`Bánh canh chả cá Nhường`, `30,000–45,000 VND`), and roasted steppe lamb with wild herbs (`Đùi cừu nướng Ninh Thuận`, `180,000–280,000 VND` per plate).

## 4. My Hoa Lagoon Kiteboarding Corridor (12 km Northeast on `DT702`)

Twelve kilometers northeast of Ninh Chu Bridge along the coastal cliff road `DT702` toward [Where to Stay in Vinh Hy](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-vinh-hy/), **My Hoa Lagoon** (`Biển Mỹ Hòa`, Vinh Hai Commune) features a 4 km barrier reef that traps a waist-deep, butter-flat turquoise lagoon inside the outer ocean surf.

Between **November and March** (and during the secondary southwest wind window from **June to August**), international kitesurfers and wing-foilers fill My Hoa's beachfront eco-lodges, surf hostels, and glamping pods (**Phi Kite School & Resort**, **My Hoa Lagoon Kitesurfing Center**, and **Sirocco My Hoa**, **450,000–1,800,000 VND** / **$18–$72 USD** per night; 3-hour IKO kite lessons **2,200,000–2,800,000 VND** / **$88–$112 USD**, equipment storage and rescue pack **250,000 VND/day**). Watch the lunar tide chart carefully: at mid-to-high tide (`1.1 to 1.8 meters`), the lagoon provides 800 meters of glass-smooth riding water, whereas dead low tide exposes sharp dead coral plates requiring neoprene reef booties. My Hoa also makes an ideal midway basecamp: you sit 10 minutes north of Ninh Chu Beach, 15 minutes south of **Hang Rai Ancient Coral Reef** (**40,000 VND** entry) and **Thai An Grape Vineyards** (**0 VND** entry, fresh grapes **50,000–80,000 VND/kg**), and 25 minutes (26 km) south of **Vinh Hy Bay** (**80,000–120,000 VND** glass-bottom coral boats) and the ultra-luxury cliffside pavilions of **Amanoi** (**28,000,000–95,000,000+ VND** / **$1,100–$3,800+ USD** per night).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I stay in Phan Rang (Ninh Chu Beach) or Vinh Hy Bay?

Base at **Ninh Chu Beach** in [Phan Rang](/destinations/phan-rang/) (`1,200,000–4,500,000 VND` / `$48–$180 USD`) if you want a wide, walk-in sandy swimming beach, large resort pools, proximity to **Thap Cham Railway Station** (20 minutes) and **Po Klong Garai Cham Towers**, and easy city dining. Base 38 km northeast in [Vinh Hy](/destinations/vinh-hy/) (`350,000–1,800,000 VND` in the fishing village or `$1,100+ USD` at **Amanoi**) if you prefer dramatic vertical granite cliffs inside **Nui Chua National Park**, glass-bottom coral boat trips, and floating lobster rafts—keeping in mind that Vinh Hy village itself has a concrete harbor wall rather than a wide walk-in sand beach.

### How do I get from Cam Ranh Airport (`CXR`) or Thap Cham Train Station to Ninh Chu Beach?

From **Cam Ranh International Airport (`CXR`)**, Ninh Chu Beach is **60 km south (1 hour 15 minutes)** via `QL1A` or coastal route `DT702`; a pre-booked 4-seat private car costs `550,000 to 750,000 VND` ($22 to $30 USD). From **Thap Cham Railway Station** (`Ga Tháp Chàm`), metered Mai Linh, Sun Taxi, or Grab cars cover the **7 km to downtown Phan Rang in 12 minutes** (`90,000–120,000 VND` / `$3.60–$4.80 USD`) or the **12 km to Ninh Chu Beach in 20 minutes** (`150,000–190,000 VND` / `$6.00–$7.60 USD`).

### What is the best time of year to book a beach resort in Phan Rang?

Because Phan Rang receives only `700 to 800 mm` of rain annually, sunshine is reliable almost year-round. **April to September** offers the calmest, warmest seas (`27°C–29°C`) for swimming and snorkeling at Ninh Chu and Vinh Hy. **November to March** brings cooler air (`24°C–28°C`) and powerful **25 to 35 knot** Northeast Monsoon winds—ideal for kitesurfing at **My Hoa Lagoon**, though breezy on open beaches. Avoid only the brief tropical storm window between **mid-October and mid-November**.
