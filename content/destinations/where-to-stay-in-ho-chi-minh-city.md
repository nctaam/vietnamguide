# Where to Stay in Ho Chi Minh City: Best Areas & Hotels

Choosing your hotel base in Ho Chi Minh City determines how much time you spend walking shaded boulevards versus sitting in humid motorbike traffic. Saigon sprawls across nineteen urban districts and the eastern municipal city of Thu Duc, yet ninety percent of short-stay visitors sleep within a compact 6 km radius stretching from Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN) southeast to the Saigon River bend. Since the commercial launch of Metro Line 1 between Ben Thanh Underground Station and Suoi Tien, crossing from eastern riverside neighborhoods into the colonial core takes 12 minutes instead of 40 minutes by car.

Pick your neighborhood by its exact logistical role. First-time sightseers with 48 hours to 72 hours do best in eastern District 1 around Dong Khoi or central Ben Thanh Ward. Light sleepers who prize leafy French villas and specialty coffee shops should cross one street north into District 3. Families, remote workers, and riverside resort guests gain far more space in Thao Dien. Early flyers with 06:00 departures need a soundproof transit room in Tan Binh District, just 1.2 km from the airport gates.

Always verify that your room category explicitly specifies a street-facing window or balcony before paying.

## 1. Dong Khoi, Nguyen Hue & Saigon Riverfront (Ben Nghe Ward, District 1)

Ben Nghe Ward forms the historic and architectural centerpiece of Ho Chi Minh City. Anchored by the 1897 Saigon Opera House, Notre-Dame Cathedral, the Central Post Office, and Nguyen Hue Walking Street, this eastern sector of District 1 offers the widest sidewalks in southern Vietnam. Walk 500 meters from your lobby to Bach Dang Wharf for a 15,000 VND Saigon Waterbus ticket, or ride Metro Line 1 from the underground Opera House and Ben Thanh stations for 7,000 VND to 20,000 VND ($0.28 USD to $0.80 USD).

Saigon's flagship five-star properties cluster within 400 meters of Lam Son Square. Historic icons including Caravelle Saigon, Hotel Continental, Park Hyatt Saigon, and the river-facing Hotel Majestic Saigon at 1 Dong Khoi price standard rooms between 3,500,000 VND and 9,500,000 VND ($140 USD to $380 USD) per night. For mid-upscale design without heritage surcharges, check the side streets of Ly Tu Trong and Dong Du. Four-star boutique hotels on these blocks charge 1,500,000 VND to 2,800,000 VND ($60 USD to $112 USD) per night and sit 4 minutes on foot from specialty espresso bars and riverfront lounges.

Skip low-floor rooms facing Nguyen Hue Boulevard on weekends. Municipal concerts fill the plaza until 23:00.

## 2. Ben Thanh & Tao Dan Park Edge (Ben Thanh Ward, District 1)

Stretching west from Ben Thanh Market toward the 10-hectare canopy of Tao Dan Park, Ben Thanh Ward serves as the practical workhorse of District 1. If your itinerary includes early group pickups for the Cu Chi Tunnels (70 km northwest, 1.5 hours) or Mekong Delta boat trips to Ben Tre (85 km south, 2 hours), ninety percent of tour vans collect passengers directly along Le Thanh Ton, Ly Tu Trong, and Thu Khoa Huan streets between 07:00 and 07:45.

This grid divides into two micro-zones. The eastern alleys around 15A and 15B Le Thanh Ton house Little Tokyo, where ramen counters, izakayas, and Japanese spas operate until 01:00 AM alongside business hotels charging 1,200,000 VND to 2,100,000 VND ($48 USD to $85 USD). Western blocks bordering Tao Dan Park along Truong Dinh deliver quieter mornings where locals practice tai chi at 06:00. Clean three-star hotels here cost 950,000 VND to 1,600,000 VND ($38 USD to $64 USD) per night, keeping you 350 meters from Ben Thanh Metro Line 1 Station.

## 3. Pham Ngu Lao & Bui Vien Backpacker Strip (District 1 West)

South of September 23 Park lies Pham Ngu Lao Ward, the primary budget transit hub of southern Vietnam. Every evening at 19:00, police barriers close Bui Vien Street to vehicles, turning an 800 meters strip into an open-air club. Rival bars point subwoofers into the street, pumping 110dB electronic bass until 04:00 AM.

Never book a room directly on Bui Vien Street if you value sleep. Earplugs cannot block low-frequency vibrations rattling single-pane windows.

Capture the transit advantages of this ward without losing sleep by booking 300 meters south on Co Giang, Co Bac, or lower De Tham streets. Curtained hostel pods cost 180,000 VND to 400,000 VND ($7 USD to $16 USD) per night, while private en-suite guesthouse rooms in quiet alleys run 550,000 VND to 950,000 VND ($22 USD to $38 USD). Morning carts on Co Giang serve broken rice (*com tam*) for 40,000 VND ($1.60 USD). Better yet, you can walk 200 meters in 4 minutes to the FUTA Bus Lines (Phuong Trang) and The Sinh Tourist offices on Pham Ngu Lao and De Tham to board sleeper buses to Mui Ne (190,000 VND, 4.5 hours), Da Lat (290,000 VND, 7 hours), or Phnom Penh (600,000 VND, 6 hours).

## 4. District 3 (Vo Van Tan, Tu Xuong, Nguyen Thi Minh Khai & Turtle Lake)

Cross Nguyen Thi Minh Khai Street north from Independence Palace and the urban texture shifts immediately. District 3 preserves Saigon's densest canopy of giant dipterocarp trees, yellow French colonial villas, and narrow culinary alleys. The War Remnants Museum at 28 Vo Van Tan (40,000 VND admission, open 07:30 to 17:30) and the fountain plaza of Turtle Lake (*Ho Con Rua*) anchor the southern blocks, while pink Tan Dinh Church sits 1.2 km northeast.

District 3 excels for couples and slow travelers staying 3 days to 7 days. Boutique aparthotels, villa conversions, and serviced studios on Tu Xuong, Vo Van Tan, and Nguyen Thi Minh Khai cost 850,000 VND to 1,900,000 VND ($34 USD to $76 USD) per night. Within 300 meters of Turtle Lake, specialty roasteries pour Da Lat Arabica coffee for 55,000 VND ($2.20 USD), and family kitchens serve crab noodle soup (*banh canh cua*) for 60,000 VND ($2.40 USD). Reaching Dong Khoi takes 15 minutes on foot or 6 minutes in a 35,000 VND ($1.40 USD) GrabCar.

## 5. Thao Dien (Thu Duc City / Former District 2)

Wrapped inside a wide bend of the Saigon River 5 km northeast of downtown, Thao Dien functions as a leafy riverside village. Today, the elevated Thao Dien and An Phu stations on Metro Line 1 carry passengers straight into Ben Thanh Underground Station in 12 minutes for a flat 12,000 VND ($0.48 USD) fare, bypassing Saigon Bridge traffic.

Inside the peninsula, Xuan Thuy and Quoc Huong streets host international bistros, sourdough bakeries, and craft breweries. Boutique riverfront resorts—including Villa Song Saigon and Mia Saigon—price luxury rooms and suites from 2,900,000 VND to 5,800,000 VND ($115 USD to $232 USD) and run 15 minutes speedboat shuttles to Bach Dang Wharf, while modern serviced apartments rent for 1,200,000 VND to 2,400,000 VND ($48 USD to $96 USD) per night.

## 6. Tan Son Nhat Airport Area (SGN — Truong Son & Bach Dang, Tan Binh)

Located 7 km northwest of District 1, Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN) sits right inside the urban grid. Midday GrabCar rides from downtown take 22 minutes (110,000 VND to 150,000 VND / $4.40 USD to $6.00 USD), but rush-hour gridlock between 07:00 and 09:00 or 16:30 and 19:00 stretches that drive to 55 minutes.

Book an airport room in Tan Binh District only for arrivals after 23:30 or flights before 07:30 AM. Soundproof transit hotels along Truong Son and Bach Dang streets stand 800 meters to 2.0 km (5 minutes to 10 minutes) from Terminals 1, 2, and 3, charging 600,000 VND to 1,300,000 VND ($24 USD to $52 USD) per night. Daytime flyers can catch Airport Bus #109 (15,000 VND) or Bus #152 (5,000 VND) straight to Ben Thanh Market in 35 minutes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which district in Ho Chi Minh City is best for first-time visitors?
Stay in **District 1**, specifically **Ben Nghe Ward** (Dong Khoi and Nguyen Hue) or **Ben Thanh Ward** (near Tao Dan Park). You can walk to Independence Palace, Notre-Dame Cathedral, and Ben Thanh Market within 10 minutes to 15 minutes, and ride Metro Line 1 for 7,000 VND to 20,000 VND.

### Is it better to stay in District 1 or District 3?
Pick **District 1** for stays of 1 night to 2 nights when you want immediate doorway access to colonial landmarks and tour pickups. Choose **District 3** (Vo Van Tan and Turtle Lake) for stays of 3 nights or more, quieter tree-lined sidewalks, and boutique studios at 850,000 VND to 1,900,000 VND ($34 USD to $76 USD).

### How much does a good hotel cost in Ho Chi Minh City in 2026?
Private budget rooms in quiet Co Giang or Co Bac alleys cost **550,000 VND to 950,000 VND** ($22 USD to $38 USD). Mid-range four-star hotels in District 1 and District 3 run **1,300,000 VND to 2,600,000 VND** ($52 USD to $105 USD), while five-star heritage icons range from **3,500,000 VND to 9,500,000 VND** ($140 USD to $380 USD) per night.
