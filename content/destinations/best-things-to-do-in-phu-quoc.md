# Best Things to Do in Phu Quoc (2026): Hon Thom Cable Car, An Thoi Islands, Beaches & 30-Day Visa Free

Floating in the warm turquoise waters of the **Gulf of Thailand (`Vịnh Thái Lan`)** `45 kilometers` west of Ha Tien, **Phu Quoc (`Đảo Ngọc Phú Quốc`, `574 km²`)** is Vietnam's largest island and the **only destination in the country with a universal `30-Day Visa-Free Direct Entry Policy` for all foreign nationalities**. Whether you hold a US, Canadian, Australian, Indian, or European passport, flying directly into **Phu Quoc International Airport (`PQC`)** from an international hub (such as Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Seoul, or Taipei) and staying strictly on the island grants you an automatic **30-day visa-free stamp (`0 USD`)**!

Because Phu Quoc is nearly the size of Singapore (stretching `50 kilometers` from North Island to South Island), planning your stay around its **three distinct geographic zones** is essential: **Central Island (`Dương Đông` & `Ông Lang`)** for walkable beaches, night markets, and boutique resorts; **South Island (`An Thới`, `Bãi Sao`, `Bãi Khem` & `Sunset Town`)** for the world-record **Hon Thom Over-Sea Cable Car (`7,899.9m`)** and **4-Island Coral Snorkeling Speedboats**; and **North Island (`Gành Dầu`, `Rạch Vẹm` & `Vườn Quốc Gia`)** for starfish coves, primary forest trails, and **Vinpearl Safari / VinWonders**. Here is the complete 2026 empirical guide to Phu Quoc.

## 9 Best Things to Do in Phu Quoc (Ranked by Island Zone)

### 1. Ride the `7,899.9-Meter` Hon Thom Over-Sea Cable Car (`Cáp Treo Hòn Thơm`)
Departing from the Roman Colosseum-inspired **An Thoi Station** at the southern tip of Phu Quoc, the **Sun World Hon Thom Cable Car (`850,000 VND` round-trip including Aquatopia Water Park & Exotica Village)** holds the Guinness World Record for the **longest non-stop 3-wire sea-crossing cable car on earth (`7,899.9 meters` / `20 minutes` each way)**. Suspended up to `164 meters` above the emerald channels of the An Thoi Archipelago, your glass cabin glides over hundreds of turquoise coral reefs and wooden fishing boats before landing on **Hon Thom ("Pineapple Island")**.
- **Critical Timing Tip (Mind the Midday Shutdown!)**: The cable car often pauses operation for a scheduled midday technical inspection between **`11:30 AM and 13:30 PM`** (check the daily board at An Thoi Station upon arrival). Board between **`09:00 and 10:30 AM`** so you reach Hon Thom in the morning, or do the **4-Island Speedboat Tour** in the morning and ride the cable car **one-way back to the mainland between `15:30 and 16:45 PM`** in golden afternoon light!

### 2. Snorkel the Southern Coral Islets (`Tour Cano 4 Đảo`: `Móng Tay`, `Gầm Ghì`, `Mây Rút`)
While the beaches on the main island are great for swimming, Phu Quoc's clearest water and healthiest shallow coral gardens sit `15–25 minutes` south of An Thoi Port across the uninhabited islets of the **An Thoi Archipelago (`Quần Đảo An Thới`)**:
- **Hon Gam Ghi (`Hòn Gầm Ghì` / `Dăm Ngang`) & Hon Buom**: Shallow `1.5-to-4-meter` brain and table coral reefs teeming with sergeant-major damselfish (where your canoe anchors for 60 minutes of snorkeling).
- **Hon Mong Tay (Fingernail Island) & Hon May Rut Trong (`Hòn Mây Rút Trong`)**: Crescent white-sand lagoons shaded by leaning coconut palms for swimming, beach swings, and a fresh seafood lunch (book the combined 4-Island Speedboat + Hon Thom Cable Car package for `1,350,000–1,550,000 VND` / `$54–$62 USD`).

### 3. Swim at Phu Quoc's Best Natural Beaches (Matched to the Wind Direction!)
Because Phu Quoc is an island exposed to seasonal monsoons, **one coast is always calm and crystal clear while the opposite coast has waves**:
1. **Dry Season (November through April — Northeast Wind): West Coast (`Bãi Ông Lang`, `Bãi Trường`, `Bãi Vũng Bầu`) AND Southeast Coast (`Bãi Sao`, `Bãi Khem`) Are Both Calm**:
   - **Ong Lang Beach (`Bãi Ông Lang`, `7 km` north of Duong Dong)**: Golden-sand coves framed by black volcanic rocks, boutique eco-resorts (Mango Bay, ChenSea), zero high-rises, and the best sunset views on the island.
   - **Sao Beach (`Bãi Sao`, `25 km` southeast) & Khem Beach (`Bãi Khem`)**: Famous for **powdery, flour-white silica sand** and shallow, turquoise water (at Bai Sao, walk to the quieter northern end near Paradiso Beach Club where the sand and water are spotless).
2. **Green / Summer Season (May through October — Southwest Wind)**: When summer winds bring waves to the West Coast (Long Beach / Ong Lang), head straight to the **sheltered East/Southeast Coast (`Bãi Sao` and `Bãi Khem`)**, which sits in the lee of the island's hills and remains calm for swimming!

### 4. Visit `Làng Chài Rạch Vẹm` (`Starfish Beach`) & `Mũi Hàm Rồng` (And Protect the Starfish!)
On the rustic northern coast (`22 km` north of Duong Dong via a newly paved road), **Rach Vem Fishing Village (`Làng Chài Rạch Vẹm`, free entry)** features wooden floating seafood rafts (`nhà bè trên biển`) built on stilts above shallow, glass-clear water. From **November to April**, hundreds of bright-red **horned sea stars (`sao biển đỏ`, *Protoreaster nodosus*)** gather on the white sandbars here and along neighboring **Ham Rong Cape (`Mũi Hàm Rồng` — accessible by a `50,000–80,000 VND` wooden boat transfer from Rach Vem)**.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Never Lift Starfish Out of the Water for Photos (It Kills Them in Seconds!)**: Marine sea stars breathe through delicate water-vascular tube feet and dermal gills on their underside. Lifting a starfish into the air (or moving it onto dry sand for a selfie) causes immediate air embolism and suffocation, killing the animal within minutes! Photograph the red starfish **through the crystal-clear ankle-deep water** without touching or picking them up.

### 5. Tour an Authentic Wooden Fish-Sauce Barrel House (`Nhà Thùng Nước Mắm`) & Organic Pepper Farm
Phu Quoc holds **European Union Protected Designation of Origin (`PDO`) status** for two prized agricultural products:
- **Phu Quoc Anchovy Fish Sauce (`Nước Mắm Phú Quốc`, `40°–43°N` natural protein rating)**: Step inside **`Nhà Thùng Nước Mắm Phụng Hưng`** (opposite Phu Quoc Prison in An Thoi, free), **`Khải Hoàn`** (`11 Cầu Hùng Vương, Dương Đông`), or **`Thịnh Phát`**, where up to 80 colossal **braided-rattan *bời lời* wood vats (`thùng gỗ bời lời`)**—each holding `12 to 15 tons` of wild `cá cơm than` (black anchovies) and sea salt—ferment slowly for **12 to 15 months** to produce amber first-press `nước mắm nhĩ`. (Note: airline cabin rules forbid carrying fish sauce in hand luggage or checked bags on planes, so buy a gift box at the barrel house and have them ship it to your hotel in Saigon or Hanoi!).
- **Phu Quoc Red & Black Pepper (`Hồ Tiêu Khu Tượng`)**: Stop at a family pepper garden along the **Khu Tuong / Bung Goi** road (`5 km` northeast of Duong Dong, free entry) to walk through 3-meter-tall green pepper vines and buy sun-dried **sweet-spicy Red Peppercorns (`Tiêu Hồng / Tiêu Đỏ Phú Quốc`, `120,000–180,000 VND` per `250g` jar)**.

### 6. Explore `Nhà Tù Phú Quốc` (`Coconut Tree Prison`) & Cliffside `Chùa Hộ Quốc`
- **Phu Quoc Prison Historical Site (`Di Tích Nhà Tù Phú Quốc`, `350 Nguyễn Văn Cừ, An Thới`, free entry)**: Once the largest prisoner-of-war camp in South Vietnam (holding up to 40,000 prisoners between 1967 and 1973), preserving original barbed-wire "Tiger Cages" (`Chuồng Cọp`) and a 120-meter underground escape tunnel dug using only metal soup spoons.
- **Ho Quoc Zen Monastery (`Thiền Viện Trúc Lâm Hộ Quốc`, `10 km` north of An Thoi, free entry)**: Phu Quoc's grandest Buddhist temple, carved from ironwood and stone on a mountainside looking straight out over the East Sea (arrive at `06:00 AM` for sunrise or `08:00 AM` before the midday heat).

### 7. Ride the Free Electric `VinBus` to `Vinpearl Safari`, `VinWonders` or `Thị Trấn Hoàng Hôn` (`Sunset Town`)
One of the best travel hacks on Phu Quoc in 2026 is the island's **100% Free `VinBus` Electric Public Bus Network (`Xe Buýt Điện VinBus Miễn Phí 0 VND` — download the `VinBus` app for live GPS tracking!)**:
- **Routes `17` & `19`**: Run every `15–30 minutes` from **Phu Quoc Airport (`PQC`)** through **Long Beach (`Trần Hưng Đạo`)** and **Duong Dong Town** all the way north to **Grand World**, **VinWonders (`950,000 VND`)**, and **Vinpearl Safari (`750,000 VND` — Vietnam's best open-enclosure wildlife conservation park where you ride an electric safari bus among giraffes, rhinos, Bengal tigers, and ring-tailed lemurs)**!

## What to Eat in Phu Quoc: `Bún Quậy Kiến Xây`, Herring Salad (`Gỏi Cá Trích`) & Ham Ninh Crab

1. **`Bún Quậy Kiến Xây` (Phu Quoc's Famous "Stir-It-Yourself" Fresh Shrimp-and-Squid Rice Noodle Soup, `65,000–90,000 VND`)**: Watching the open kitchen at **`Bún Quậy Kiến Xây`** (`28 Bạch Đằng, Dương Đông` — right beside the harbor, or `71A Trần Hưng Đạo`) is pure theater: fresh rice batter is pressed directly into boiling water to make noodles on the spot, while raw minced shrimp paste and squid paste are smeared inside your bowl and flash-cooked with boiling broth. Walk to the condiment bar to whisk (`quậy`) your own fiery dipping sauce of **kumquat juice (`tắc`), salt, sugar, MSG, and crushed green chilies**!
2. **`Gỏi Cá Trích Phú Quốc` (Fresh Silver Herring Salad Rolled with Grated Coconut, Forest Herbs & Roasted Peanut Sauce, `130,000–180,000 VND`)**: Eat at **`Nhà Hàng Ra Khơi`** (`131 Đường 30 Tháng 4, Dương Đông`) or **`Xin Chào Seafood Restaurant`** (`66 Trần Hưng Đạo`, sunset oceanfront terrace).
3. **`Ghẹ Hàm Ninh Hấp` (Steamed Ham Ninh Blue Flower Crabs, `450,000–650,000 VND/kg`)**: Medium-sized, thin-shelled crabs caught in the seagrass beds off **Ham Ninh Fishing Village (`Làng Chài Hàm Ninh`)** on the east coast, steamed whole and dipped in black-pepper-lime salt (`muối tiêu chanh Phú Quốc`).
4. **`Bún Kèn Cô Thu` (Creamy Coconut-Milk & Lemongrass Barracuda Fish Noodle Soup with Green Papaya, `40,000–50,000 VND` at `98 Đường 30 Tháng 4`, morning only!)**.

## Step-by-Step HowTo: The Ideal 4-Day / 3-Night Phu Quoc Island Itinerary

Take a `15-minute` GrabCar (`120,000–180,000 VND`) or the **free `VinBus #17`** from **Phu Quoc Airport (`PQC`)** to your resort in **Long Beach (`Bãi Trường`)** or **Ong Lang Beach (`Bãi Ông Lang`)**. Spend the afternoon swimming, watch a fiery west-coast sunset at **OCSEN Beach Bar** or **Mango Bay**, and head into **Duong Dong Town** for a bowl of **`Bún Quậy Kiến Xây` (`28 Bạch Đằng`)** and a stroll through **Phu Quoc Night Market (`Chợ Đêm Phú Quốc`)**.

### 2. Second Step — Day 2 (`South Island`): 4-Island Coral Snorkeling Speedboat + `Cáp Treo Hòn Thơm` & `Sunset Town`
Spend `08:30 AM to 16:30 PM` on the **An Thoi 4-Island Speedboat & Hon Thom Cable Car Combo (`1,450,000 VND`)**: snorkel the coral gardens of **`Hòn Gầm Ghì`** and **`Hòn Mây Rút`**, have lunch on the island, enjoy the water slides at **Aquatopia (`Hòn Thơm`)**, and ride the **`7,899.9-meter` 3-wire cable car** back to **Sunset Town (`Thị Trấn Hoàng Hôn`)** at `16:15 PM` in time for sunset over **Kiss Bridge (`Cầu Hôn`)**.

### 3. Final Step — Day 3 & Day 4: `Bãi Sao` / `Bãi Khem`, Fish Sauce Barrel House & `Rạch Vẹm` Starfish Beach
On Day 3, visit **`Chùa Hộ Quốc` (`0 VND`)**, **Phu Quoc Prison (`0 VND`)**, **`Nhà Thùng Nước Mắm Phụng Hưng`**, and swim all afternoon on the powdery white sand of **`Bãi Sao` (`Sao Beach`)** or **`Bãi Khem`**. On Day 4, head north to **Rach Vem Starfish Beach (`Làng Chài Rạch Vẹm`)**, **Khu Tuong Pepper Farm**, or **Vinpearl Safari (`via the free VinBus`)** before your evening flight out of `PQC`.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Who qualifies for the 30-Day Visa-Free entry to Phu Quoc in 2026?
**Every foreign passport holder in the world (`regardless of nationality`) qualifies for a `30-day` visa exemption in Phu Quoc**, provided you meet three simple rules:
1. Your passport is valid for at least **6 months** beyond arrival.
2. You fly (`or arrive by sea`) **directly into Phu Quoc (`PQC`) from an international country** (or transit through the international airside transfer zone of Hanoi `HAN` / Ho Chi Minh City `SGN` on a single itinerary without entering mainland Vietnam), and your onward departure ticket within 30 days is **also to an international destination**.
3. You stay strictly on **Phu Quoc Island** (`if you later decide to visit mainland Vietnam—such as Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi—you must apply for a $25 USD Vietnam e-Visa at evisa.gov.vn before flying to the mainland!`).

### Where is the best area to stay in Phu Quoc: Central (`Dương Đông` / `Ông Lang`), South (`An Thới` / `Bãi Khem`), or North (`Gành Dầu`)?
For **80% of independent travelers**, staying in **Central Phu Quoc (`Upper Long Beach / Trần Hưng Đạo` or `Ông Lang Beach`)** is the best choice: you are only `15–20 minutes` from the airport, walking distance to dozens of authentic local restaurants (`Đường 30 Tháng 4`), bars, and the night market, and exactly halfway between the North and South sights. Choose **South Island (`Bãi Khem` — `JW Marriott`, `New World`, `Premier Residences`)** for 5-star white-sand resort luxury and immediate access to the Hon Thom Cable Car, or **North Island (`Gành Dầu` / `Bãi Dài`)** if traveling with young kids specifically for **VinWonders and Vinpearl Safari**.

### When is the best time of year to visit Phu Quoc?
The prime dry, sunny season in Phu Quoc runs from **November through April** (`temperatures 26°C–31°C, calm turquoise water on the West Coast and Starfish Beach, and low humidity`). **May to October** is the green monsoon season (`hotel rates drop by 30%–50%, and while West Coast beaches have waves, East Coast **Bãi Sao** and **Bãi Khem** remain sheltered and swimmable!`).
