# Best Things to Do in Phu Quoc (2026): Hon Thom Cable Car, An Thoi Islands, Beaches & 30-Day Visa Free

Floating in the warm turquoise waters of the **Gulf of Thailand (Vịnh Thái Lan)** 45 kilometers west of Ha Tien and 120 kilometers west of Rach Gia Port, **Phu Quoc (Đảo Ngọc Phú Quốc, 574 km²)** is Vietnam's largest island and the **only destination in the country with a universal 30-Day Visa-Free Direct Entry Policy for all foreign nationalities**. Passport holders from the United States, Canada, Australia, India, or any European state who fly directly into **Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC)** from an international hub (such as Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Seoul, or Taipei) and stay strictly on the island receive an automatic **30-day visa-free entry stamp (0 VND / $0.00 USD)** upon arrival.

Because Phu Quoc spans 50 kilometers from North Island to South Island and 25 kilometers across its widest forested ridge, organizing your itinerary around its **three distinct geographic zones** saves hours of road transit and hundreds of thousands of VND in taxi fares. **Central Island (Dương Đông and Ông Lang)** anchors walkable sunset beaches, night markets, and boutique resorts within 10 km to 18 km of the airport. **South Island (An Thới, Bãi Sao, Bãi Khem, and Sunset Town)** hosts the world-record **Hon Thom Over-Sea Cable Car (7899.9 meters)** and **4-Island Coral Snorkeling Speedboats**. **North Island (Gành Dầu, Rạch Vẹm, and Vườn Quốc Gia)** covers primary dipterocarp forest trails, starfish sandbars, and the **Vinpearl Safari and VinWonders** entertainment complex located 32 kilometers north of Duong Dong. Here is the complete 2026 empirical guide to Phu Quoc.

## 9 Best Things to Do in Phu Quoc (Ranked by Island Zone)

### 1. Ride the 7899.9-Meter Hon Thom Over-Sea Cable Car (Cáp Treo Hòn Thơm)
Departing from the Roman Colosseum-inspired **An Thoi Station** at the southern tip of Phu Quoc (28 kilometers south of Duong Dong Town, a 35-minute taxi ride costing 320,000 VND to 380,000 VND / $12.80 to $15.20 USD), the **Sun World Hon Thom Cable Car (850,000 VND / $34.00 USD round-trip including Aquatopia Water Park and Exotica Village)** holds the Guinness World Record for the **longest non-stop 3-wire sea-crossing cable car on earth (7899.9 meters or 7.9 km, taking 20 minutes each way)**. Suspended up to 164 meters above the emerald channels of the An Thoi Archipelago, your 30-passenger glass cabin glides at 8.5 meters per second across six massive concrete sea towers over shallow coral reefs and wooden squid boats before landing on **Hon Thom (Pineapple Island)**.
- **Critical Timing Protocol (Mind the Midday Shutdown)**: Operators pause the cable line for a scheduled midday technical inspection between **11:30 and 13:30** on standard weekdays. Board at An Thoi Station between **09:00 and 10:30** so you reach Hon Thom in the morning and enjoy 3 hours at the 20-slide **Aquatopia Water Park** and **Roaring Timbers** wooden roller coaster before the tour crowds arrive. Alternatively, join the **4-Island Speedboat Tour** at 08:30 in the morning and ride the cable car **one-way back to the mainland between 15:30 and 16:45** in warm afternoon light.
- **Sunset Town & Kiss Bridge Connection**: Immediately adjacent to An Thoi Station sits **Sunset Town (Thị Trấn Hoàng Hôn)** and the twin-span **Kiss Bridge (Cầu Hôn, 800 meters long, separated by a 30 cm gap at the center for sunset photography)**. Entry to Kiss Bridge costs 50,000 VND ($2.00 USD) before 16:00 and 100,000 VND ($4.00 USD) during the 16:00–18:30 sunset window, though standard Hon Thom cable car tickets purchased at the booth include complimentary bridge access on the same calendar day.

### 2. Snorkel the Southern Coral Islets (Tour Cano 4 Đảo: Móng Tay, Gầm Ghì, Mây Rút)
While the beaches on the main island offer calm swimming, Phu Quoc's clearest water (visibility reaching 8 meters to 12 meters between November and April) and healthiest shallow coral gardens sit 6 kilometers to 14 kilometers south of An Thoi Port across the uninhabited islets of the **An Thoi Archipelago (Quần Đảo An Thới, 15 minutes to 25 minutes by twin-engine speedboat)**:
- **Hon Gam Ghi (Hòn Gầm Ghì / Dăm Ngang) & Hon Buom**: Shallow 1.5-meter to 4.5-meter brain coral, pore coral, and table coral reefs populated by sergeant-major damselfish, parrotfish, and blue sea stars. Speedboats anchor here for 60 minutes of surface snorkeling using provided masks and fins.
- **Hon Mong Tay (Fingernail Island) & Hon May Rut Trong (Hòn Mây Rút Trong)**: Crescent white-sand lagoons shaded by leaning coconut palms for 90 minutes of swimming, freshwater showers (20,000 VND / $0.80 USD), and an 8-dish fresh seafood lunch. Booking the combined **4-Island Speedboat + Hon Thom Cable Car package costs 1,350,000 VND to 1,550,000 VND ($54.00 to $62.00 USD)** per adult including hotel pickup in Duong Dong or Long Beach, saving 400,000 VND ($16.00 USD) compared to chartering a private boat and buying cable car passes separately. Private speedboat charters for groups of 4 to 8 travelers run 2,800,000 VND to 3,500,000 VND ($112.00 to $140.00 USD) for a 5-hour customized loop.

### 3. Swim at Phu Quoc's Best Natural Beaches (Matched to the Wind Direction)
Because Phu Quoc sits exposed to shifting seasonal monsoons in the Gulf of Thailand, **one coastline always remains calm and glass-clear while the opposite shore experiences onshore swells and floating debris**:
1. **Dry Season (November through April, Northeast Wind, 25°C–31°C, 72% humidity)**: Both the **West Coast (Bãi Ông Lang, Bãi Trường, Bãi Vũng Bầu, Bãi Dài)** and the **Southeast Coast (Bãi Sao, Bãi Khem)** stay calm and swimmable.
   - **Ong Lang Beach (Bãi Ông Lang, 7.5 km north of Duong Dong, 15 minutes by motorbike or a 110,000 VND / $4.40 USD GrabCar)**: Golden-sand coves framed by black volcanic boulders, low-density eco-resorts (Mango Bay, ChenSea, Camia), zero concrete high-rises, and unobstructed western sunset horizons.
   - **Long Beach (Bãi Trường, stretching 20 km south from Duong Dong past PQC Airport)**: The most convenient swimming and sunset cocktail strip on the island; rent a padded wooden sun lounger for 100,000 VND ($4.00 USD) or buy a 90,000 VND ($3.60 USD) fresh coconut or craft beer at **OCSEN Beach Bar & Club** (arrive by 16:30 to secure a front-row beanbag before the 17:45 sunset and 22:00 fire show).
   - **Sao Beach (Bãi Sao, 25 km southeast of Duong Dong) & Khem Beach (Bãi Khem, 27 km southeast)**: Famous for **powdery, flour-white silica sand** and shallow turquoise water extending 80 meters offshore at waist depth. At Bai Sao, walk 400 meters north toward **Paradiso Beach Club** (sunbed rental 150,000 VND / $6.00 USD) where the shoreline is raked clean every morning at 07:00.
2. **Green Monsoon Season (May through October, Southwest Wind, 26°C–32°C, 280 mm monthly rainfall)**: When summer southwest winds generate 1.0-meter to 1.5-meter waves along the West Coast (Long Beach and Ong Lang), head 25 kilometers across the island to the **sheltered East and Southeast Coast (Bãi Sao and Bãi Khem)**, which sits in the lee of the southern hills and stays calm for morning swimming.

### 4. Visit Làng Chài Rạch Vẹm (Starfish Beach) & Mũi Hàm Rồng (And Protect the Starfish)
On the quiet northern coast (22 kilometers north of Duong Dong via DT973 and a newly paved concrete access road, taking 40 minutes by scooter or 300,000 VND / $12.00 USD each way by GrabCar), **Rach Vem Fishing Village (Làng Chài Rạch Vẹm, 0 VND entry)** features rustic wooden floating seafood rafts (*nhà bè trên biển*) built on timber stilts 150 meters to 300 meters offshore above shallow, ankle-deep water. From **November through April**, hundreds of bright-red **horned sea stars (*sao biển đỏ*, *Protoreaster nodosus*)** gather on the white sandbars here and along pristine **Ham Rong Cape (Mũi Hàm Rồng)**, accessible by a 10-minute wooden motorboat shuttle from the Rach Vem pier costing **50,000 VND to 80,000 VND ($2.00 to $3.20 USD) round-trip per passenger**.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Never Lift Starfish Out of the Water for Photos (It Kills Them Within Minutes)**: Marine sea stars breathe through delicate water-vascular tube feet and dermal papulae on their underside. Lifting a starfish into the air or placing it onto dry sand for a smartphone photo causes immediate air embolism and suffocation, killing the animal within 2 to 5 minutes. Photograph the red starfish **through the clear 30 cm water** without touching, rearranging, or lifting them above the surface.

### 5. Tour an Authentic Wooden Fish-Sauce Barrel House (Nhà Thùng Nước Mắm) & Organic Pepper Farm
Phu Quoc holds **European Union Protected Designation of Origin (PDO) status** for two prized agricultural exports that you can taste directly at family estates:
- **Phu Quoc Anchovy Fish Sauce (Nước Mắm Phú Quốc, 40°N–43°N natural nitrogen protein rating)**: Step inside **Nhà Thùng Nước Mắm Phụng Hưng** (located on Nguyen Van Cu road directly opposite Phu Quoc Prison in An Thoi, 08:00–17:00, 0 VND entry), **Khải Hoàn** (11 Cầu Hùng Vương, Dương Đông, 0 VND entry), or **Thịnh Phát** (Đường 30 Tháng 4). Inside the high-ceilinged warehouses, 60 to 80 colossal **braided-rattan *bời lời* wood vats (*thùng gỗ bời lời*)** standing 4.0 meters tall and holding **12 tons to 15 tons** of wild *cá cơm than* (black anchovies) mixed with Ba Ria sea salt ferment slowly for **12 months to 15 months** to yield amber first-press *nước mắm nhĩ* (priced at 180,000 VND to 320,000 VND / $7.20 to $12.80 USD per liter). Because domestic and international airlines strictly prohibit carrying fermented fish sauce in cabin bags or checked luggage on flights departing PQC, purchase your glass bottles at the barrel house counter and pay 60,000 VND to 90,000 VND ($2.40 to $3.60 USD) for courier shipping straight to your hotel in Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi within 48 hours.
- **Phu Quoc Red & Black Pepper (Hồ Tiêu Khu Tượng)**: Stop at a family-run organic pepper garden such as **Hồng Anh** or **Đức Thạnh** along the **Khu Tuong and Bung Goi road (5.5 kilometers northeast of Duong Dong, 12 minutes by motorbike, 0 VND admission)** to walk beneath 3.5-meter-tall living pepper vines and purchase sun-dried **sweet-spicy Red Peppercorns (Tiêu Hồng / Tiêu Đỏ Phú Quốc, 120,000 VND to 180,000 VND / $4.80 to $7.20 USD per 250g jar)** and roasted pepper salt (*muối tiêu dưỡng sinh*, 35,000 VND / $1.40 USD per tub).

### 6. Explore Nhà Tù Phú Quốc (Coconut Tree Prison) & Cliffside Chùa Hộ Quốc
- **Phu Quoc Prison Historical Site (Di Tích Nhà Tù Phú Quốc, 350 Nguyễn Văn Cừ, An Thới, 25 km south of Duong Dong; open 08:00–11:30 and 13:30–17:00; 0 VND admission, 30,000 VND / $1.20 USD optional audio headset)**: Formerly the largest prisoner-of-war detention complex in South Vietnam (confining up to 40,000 political and military prisoners across 400 hectares between 1967 and 1973), the outdoor sector preserves reconstructed barbed-wire **Catso Tiger Cages (Chuồng Cọp Kẽm Gai)** and a 120-meter underground escape tunnel excavated by prisoners in 1969 using only metal soup spoons and mess tins. Allocate 45 minutes to 60 minutes here before the midday break at 11:30.
- **Ho Quoc Zen Monastery (Thiền Viện Trúc Lâm Hộ Quốc, 10 kilometers north of An Thoi and 18 kilometers southeast of Duong Dong; open 06:00–18:00; 0 VND entry)**: Phu Quoc's largest Buddhist sanctuary spans 110 hectares on a forested mountain slope facing straight out over the East Sea. Built from ironwood (*gỗ lim*) and carved green stone, the courtyard features a 30-meter jade-colored Avalokiteshvara statue and a 70-step dragon staircase. Arrive between **06:00 and 08:00** to watch sunrise over the water at a comfortable 25°C before tour buses arrive at 09:30.

### 7. Ride the Free Electric VinBus to Vinpearl Safari, VinWonders & Grand World
One of the highest-value transport upgrades on Phu Quoc in 2026 is the island's **100% Free VinBus Electric Public Bus Network (Xe Buýt Điện VinBus Miễn Phí, 0 VND / $0.00 USD fare; download the free VinBus smartphone app for live GPS arrival tracking)**:
- **Routes 17, 19, and 20**: Clean, air-conditioned green electric buses depart every **15 minutes to 30 minutes** from **06:00 until 23:30** connecting **Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC)**, **InterContinental / Regent Long Beach**, **Duong Dong Town (along Trần Hưng Đạo and Nguyễn Trung Trực)**, and **Ong Lang turnaround** all the way 32 kilometers north to **Grand World (0 VND entry, featuring the 11,200-bamboo-pole Legend of Bamboo pavilion by architect Vo Trong Nghia)**, **VinWonders Theme Park (950,000 VND / $38.00 USD)**, and **Vinpearl Safari (750,000 VND / $30.00 USD; or 1,350,000 VND / $54.00 USD for the 1-Day 2-Park Combo)**. Taking the free VinBus saves 420,000 VND to 500,000 VND ($16.80 to $20.00 USD) each way compared to a metered taxi between Central Long Beach and Ganh Dau.

### 8. Trek Suối Tranh Waterfall & Phu Quoc National Park (Vườn Quốc Gia Phú Quốc)
Covering 314 km² across the mountainous northern spine of the island (reaching 603 meters at Mount Chúa), **Phu Quoc National Park (UNESCO Kien Giang Biosphere Reserve)** protects evergreen dipterocarp rainforest, wild orchids, and silvered langurs.
- **Suoi Tranh Waterfall (Khu Du Lịch Suối Tranh, 9 kilometers southeast of Duong Dong on DT47; open 07:00–17:00; admission 30,000 VND / $1.20 USD)**: Best visited during the **May through November rainy season** when monsoon rains feed a 15-kilometer forest stream cascading over mossy granite boulders into natural swimming pools. A shaded 1.2-kilometer stone footpath takes 25 minutes each way beneath ancient forest canopy. In the peak dry months of February through April, Suoi Tranh slows to a trickle; opt instead for the **Ganh Dau Forest Trail (6 kilometers east of Ganh Dau Cape, 0 VND admission, 90-minute flat walk beneath 30-meter *dầu rái* resin trees)**.

### 9. Kayak the Cửa Cạn River or Catch Sunset at Dinh Cậu Rock Temple
- **Dinh Cau Temple & Lighthouse (Miếu Dinh Cậu, Bach Dang Street, Duong Dong Harbor; open 07:00–20:30; 0 VND admission)**: Built in 1937 atop a wave-battered granite headland at the mouth of the Duong Dong River, this sea-goddess shrine climbs 29 stone steps beneath a century-old banyan tree and offers a front-row 17:45 sunset vantage point just 350 meters west of the **Phu Quoc Night Market (Chợ Đêm Phú Quốc, open 17:30–23:00)**.
- **Cua Can River Kayaking (Sông Cửa Cạn, 14 kilometers north of Duong Dong)**: Rent a double sea kayak for **150,000 VND to 200,000 VND ($6.00 to $8.00 USD) for 2 hours** at the Cua Can wood bridge or Vung Bau lagoon to paddle 4 kilometers upstream through calm nipa-palm estuaries without motorboat wake.

## Getting Around Phu Quoc in 2026: Scooter Rental, Grab & Ferry Tariffs

Because Phu Quoc has no metered municipal subway and sights sit 15 kilometers to 35 kilometers apart, match your daily transport mode to your group size:
- **Motorbike & Scooter Rental (120,000 VND to 180,000 VND / $4.80 to $7.20 USD per 24 hours)**: Renting a 125cc Honda AirBlade or Honda Vision from your resort desk in Duong Dong or Ong Lang costs 150,000 VND ($6.00 USD) per day plus 60,000 VND ($2.40 USD) of 95-octane gasoline for 100 kilometers of riding. Main north-south arteries (DT45, DT46, DT973, and the 4-lane Tran Hung Dao highway) are 100% paved asphalt with modest 50 km/h to 60 km/h traffic speeds. Always wear a strapped helmet and carry a physical 1968 International Driving Permit (IDP) alongside your home motorcycle license to comply with traffic police checkpoints outside Duong Dong and An Thoi.
- **GrabCar, Xanh SM Electric Taxi & Private Full-Day Car Charter**: Both **Grab** and **Xanh SM (all-electric VinFast sedans)** operate reliably across PQC Airport, Duong Dong, Long Beach, Ong Lang, and Sunset Town. A 10-kilometer ride from PQC Airport to central Duong Dong takes 15 minutes and costs **130,000 VND to 180,000 VND ($5.20 to $7.20 USD)**; a 28-kilometer ride from Duong Dong south to An Thoi Cable Car Station takes 35 minutes and costs **320,000 VND to 390,000 VND ($12.80 to $15.60 USD)**. For families or groups of 3 to 6 travelers exploring the South Island or North Island beaches, chartering a **private 7-seater SUV with driver for an 8-hour day (80 km to 100 km allowance) costs 850,000 VND to 1,100,000 VND ($34.00 to $44.00 USD)** total, which is cheaper than booking four separate long-distance Grab trips.
- **Mainland High-Speed Ferries to Bai Vong Port (Cảng Bãi Vòng, 14 km east of Duong Dong)**: Travelers continuing overland into the Mekong Delta without flying can board **Superdong** or **Phu Quoc Express** high-speed catamarans from Bai Vong Port to **Ha Tien (45 km, 1.25 hours, 230,000 VND to 250,000 VND / $9.20 to $10.00 USD)** or **Rach Gia (120 km, 2.5 hours, 330,000 VND to 380,000 VND / $13.20 to $15.20 USD)**. Remember that boarding a ferry or domestic flight to mainland Vietnam requires a valid Vietnam e-Visa ($25.00 USD single-entry) or a 45-day bilateral visa exemption stamp, as the 30-day Phu Quoc-only visa waiver applies strictly to the island.

## What to Eat in Phu Quoc: Bún Quậy Kiến Xây, Herring Salad (Gỏi Cá Trích) & Ham Ninh Crab

1. **Bún Quậy Kiến Xây (Phu Quoc's Famous Stir-It-Yourself Fresh Shrimp-and-Squid Rice Noodle Soup, 65,000 VND to 90,000 VND / $2.60 to $3.60 USD)**: Watching the open kitchen at **Bún Quậy Kiến Xây** (28 Bạch Đằng, Dương Đông, 200 meters from Dinh Cau Harbor, open 07:00–23:45; or 71A Trần Hưng Đạo) is culinary theater. Cooks press fresh fermented rice batter through a steel cylinder directly into boiling water to extrude noodles on the spot, while raw minced shrimp paste, fish paste, and whole baby squid are smeared inside your ceramic bowl and flash-poached with ladlefuls of boiling broth. Walk to the self-service condiment counter to whisk (*quậy*) your own bright orange dipping sauce of **fresh kumquat juice (*tắc*), sea salt, white sugar, and crushed bird's-eye chilies**.
2. **Gỏi Cá Trích Phú Quốc (Fresh Silver Herring Salad Rolled with Grated Coconut, Forest Herbs & Roasted Peanut Sauce, 130,000 VND to 180,000 VND / $5.20 to $7.20 USD per platter for two)**: Translucent fillets of morning-caught silver herring are cured in lime juice, tossed with sliced onions, mint, and freshly grated white coconut flesh, then wrapped at the table in rice paper with wild *lá cóc* and *bứa* forest leaves and dipped into a warm *nước mắm Phú Quốc* sauce topped with crushed roasted peanuts. Order it at **Nhà Hàng Ra Khơi** (131 Đường 30 Tháng 4, Dương Đông, open 09:00–22:30) or **Xin Chào Seafood Restaurant** (66 Trần Hưng Đạo, open 11:00–22:00 with a sunset oceanfront terrace).
3. **Ghẹ Hàm Ninh Hấp (Steamed Ham Ninh Blue Flower Crabs, 450,000 VND to 650,000 VND / $18.00 to $26.00 USD per 1.0 kg)**: Medium-sized, firm-fleshed swimming crabs (`4 to 6 crabs per kilogram`) harvested from the shallow seagrass flats off **Ham Ninh Fishing Village (Làng Chài Hàm Ninh, 14 km east of Duong Dong)**, steamed whole with lemongrass stalks and dipped in black-pepper-lime salt (*muối tiêu chanh Phú Quốc*). Always select live swimming crabs from the aerated tank and verify the scale weight before steaming.
4. **Bún Kèn Cô Thu (Creamy Coconut-Milk & Lemongrass Barracuda Fish Noodle Soup with Green Papaya, 40,000 VND to 50,000 VND / $1.60 to $2.00 USD at 98 Đường 30 Tháng 4, Dương Đông, served 06:00–11:00 morning only)**: A Khmer-influenced island specialty where flaky *cá nhồng* (barracuda) is simmered in turmeric, lemongrass, and rich coconut milk, ladled over thin vermicelli with shredded green papaya, Thai basil, and red chili oil.

## Step-by-Step HowTo: The Ideal 4-Day / 3-Night Phu Quoc Island Itinerary

### 1. First Step: Day 1 Arrival at PQC, Sunset at Ông Lang or Dương Đông & Bún Quậy Kiến Xây
Take a 15-minute GrabCar (130,000 VND to 180,000 VND / $5.20 to $7.20 USD) or the **free VinBus Route 17 (0 VND)** from **Phu Quoc Airport (PQC)** to your resort in **Long Beach (Bãi Trường, 8 km)** or **Ong Lang Beach (Bãi Ông Lang, 18 km)**. Spend 2 hours swimming in the afternoon, watch the 17:45 west-coast sunset with a 95,000 VND ($3.80 USD) cocktail at **OCSEN Beach Bar** or **Mango Bay**, and ride 10 minutes into **Duong Dong Town** for a steaming bowl of **Bún Quậy Kiến Xây (28 Bạch Đằng, 75,000 VND / $3.00 USD)** followed by a 45-minute stroll through **Phu Quoc Night Market (Chợ Đêm Phú Quốc)** for grilled sea urchin with scallion oil (*nhum nướng mỡ hành*, 35,000 VND / $1.40 USD per piece) and rolled Thai-style coconut ice cream (40,000 VND / $1.60 USD).

### 2. Second Step: Day 2 (South Island) 4-Island Coral Snorkeling Speedboat + Cáp Treo Hòn Thơm & Sunset Town
Dedicate **08:30 to 17:30** to the **An Thoi 4-Island Speedboat & Hon Thom Cable Car Combo (1,450,000 VND / $58.00 USD)**: snorkel the shallow coral reefs of **Hòn Gầm Ghì** and **Hòn Bườm** for 90 minutes, swim and eat a fresh seafood lunch on **Hòn Mây Rút Trong**, ride the water slides for 2 hours at **Aquatopia Water Park (Hòn Thơm)**, and board the **7899.9-meter 3-wire cable car** at 16:00 back to **Sunset Town (Thị Trấn Hoàng Hôn)** in time for 17:15 sunset photos on **Kiss Bridge (Cầu Hôn)**.

### 3. Final Step: Day 3 & Day 4 at Bãi Sao / Bãi Khem, Fish Sauce Barrel House & Rạch Vẹm Starfish Beach
On Day 3, start at 08:00 at cliffside **Chùa Hộ Quốc (0 VND)**, tour **Phu Quoc Prison (0 VND)** at 09:30, taste 43°N first-press fish sauce at **Nhà Thùng Nước Mắm Phụng Hưng (0 VND)** at 10:45, and spend 3.5 hours swimming on the white silica sand of **Bãi Sao (Sao Beach)** or **Bãi Khem** before a herring salad dinner (*gỏi cá trích*, 150,000 VND / $6.00 USD) at **Ra Khơi Restaurant**. ## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Who qualifies for the 30-Day Visa-Free entry to Phu Quoc in 2026?
**Every foreign passport holder in the world (regardless of nationality) qualifies for a 30-day visa exemption (0 VND / $0.00 USD) in Phu Quoc**, provided you satisfy three statutory border rules:
1. Your passport remains valid for at least **6 months** beyond your arrival date and has 2 blank visa pages.
2. You arrive **directly into Phu Quoc (PQC Airport or Bai Vong / An Thoi Seaport) from an international country** (or transfer airside through the international transit zone of Hanoi HAN or Ho Chi Minh City SGN without clearing mainland immigration), and your onward departure ticket within 30 days is **also to an international destination** outside Vietnam.
3. You remain strictly on **Phu Quoc Island** for the entire stay. If you later decide to fly or take a 2.5-hour ferry to mainland Vietnam (such as Ho Chi Minh City, Rach Gia, Ha Tien, or Hanoi), you must apply online for a **$25.00 USD single-entry (or $50.00 USD multiple-entry) 90-day Vietnam e-Visa at evisa.gov.vn** at least 4 to 5 working days before departing the island.

### Where is the best area to stay in Phu Quoc: Central (Dương Đông / Ông Lang), South (An Thới / Bãi Khem), or North (Gành Dầu)?
For **80% of first-time independent travelers**, staying in **Central Phu Quoc (Upper Long Beach along Trần Hưng Đạo, 450,000 VND to 1,800,000 VND / $18.00 to $72.00 USD per night; or Ông Lang Beach, 850,000 VND to 3,200,000 VND / $34.00 to $128.00 USD per night)** provides the best logistical balance. You sit only 15 minutes to 20 minutes (8 km to 18 km) from PQC Airport, within walking distance or a 40,000 VND ($1.60 USD) Grab ride of 50+ local seafood restaurants along Đường 30 Tháng 4, and halfway between the North Island and South Island attractions. Choose **South Island (Bãi Khem and Sunset Town, 1,200,000 VND to 8,500,000 VND / $48.00 to $340.00 USD per night at JW Marriott, New World, or Premier Residences)** for powdery white-sand resort beaches and 5-minute access to the Hon Thom Cable Car, or **North Island (Gành Dầu / Bãi Dài, 1,400,000 VND to 4,500,000 VND / $56.00 to $180.00 USD)** if traveling with children specifically for **VinWonders and Vinpearl Safari**.

### When is the best time of year to visit Phu Quoc?
The prime dry, sunny season in Phu Quoc runs for 6 months from **November through April** (daytime temperatures 26°C–31°C, sea surface temperature 28°C, calm turquoise water with 10-meter visibility on the West Coast and Starfish Beach, and low monthly rainfall of 30 mm to 60 mm). **May through October** brings the southwest monsoon season (hotel rates drop by 30% to 50%, saving $30.00 to $90.00 USD per night; while West Coast beaches experience afternoon rain showers and 1.2-meter waves, East Coast **Bãi Sao** and **Bãi Khem** remain sheltered and swimmable).
