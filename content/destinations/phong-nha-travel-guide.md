# Phong Nha Travel Guide: Caves, Seasons and Route Fit

Set along the limestone spine of the Annamite Range in Quảng Bình province, **Phong Nha - Ke Bang National Park** protects 85,754 hectares of core karst plateau and 195,400 hectares of buffer rainforest dating back 400 million years—making it the oldest major karst massif in Asia. Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2003 for its geological evolution and expanded in 2015 for terrestrial biodiversity, the park contains more than 400 surveyed caverns spanning 220 km of underground passages, including **Hang Son Doong** (the planet's largest cave chamber by volume) and **Hang En** (the third largest).

Because Phong Nha town (Sơn Trạch) sits 45 km inland west of the coastal QL1A highway and railway junction at **Dong Hoi**, adding it to a Vietnam itinerary requires a deliberate 2-day to 3-day detour. This guide evaluates whether Phong Nha fits your route, compares accessible boardwalk caves against multi-day jungle expeditions, and details the October–November flood window that regularly submerges river cave entrances.

## Quick Verdict: Is the Phong Nha Detour Worth Your Days?

- **Strong Yes — If You Have 12+ Days in Vietnam and Visit Between February and August:** Allocate **2 nights to 3 nights** in Sơn Trạch or Bồng Lai Valley. Spend Day 1 combining **Paradise Cave** (**270,000 VND**) and **Phong Nha Cave** (**150,000 VND** ticket plus shared **700,000 VND** boat) or the **Dark Cave** zip-line and mud bath (**450,000 VND**). Spend Day 2 on a 1-day or 2-day jungle cave trek such as **Tu Lan** (**2,000,000 VND**) or **Hang En** (**8,500,000 VND** to **9,200,000 VND**).
- **Conditional — If You Only Want Easy Boardwalk Sightseeing:** You do not need technical rope skills to appreciate Phong Nha. The 1.0 km lit wooden boardwalk inside Paradise Cave and the 20-minute Son River dragon boat glide into Phong Nha Cave require only moderate stair climbing.
- **Clean Skip — If Your Trip Is Under 10 Days or Falls in Peak Flood Season (October 1 to November 15):** Diverting 45 km inland from Ga Đồng Hới consumes two 10-hour overnight train legs or domestic flight buffers. Furthermore, autumn tropical depressions dump 400 mm to 700 mm of rain across Quảng Bình, raising the Son River by 4 meters to 6 meters and shutting wet caves completely.

> [!WARNING]
> **Critical Monsoon Flood Window (Late September to Mid-November):** During peak Central Vietnam storms, water levels inside Phong Nha Cave and Dark Cave rise rapidly, submerging cave mouths up to the limestone roof. Even dry higher-elevation caverns like Paradise Cave can become unreachable when floodwaters submerge low-lying bridges along Provincial Road ĐT20. Always verify live park operation status 48 hours before boarding a train to Dong Hoi in autumn.

## 1. The Accessible Show Caves: Paradise Cave, Phong Nha & Dark Cave

### Paradise Cave (Động Thiên Đường)
Discovered in 2005 by local forestman Hồ Khanh and surveyed by the British Cave Research Association (BCRA) at 31.4 km in total length, Paradise Cave is the longest dry cavern in Asia:
- **2026 Tariff & Buggy Rules:** Standard adult admission is **270,000 VND** ($10.80), which now bundles the mandatory 1.6 km round-trip electric golf buggy transfer from the main parking pavilion through primary forest to the mountain foot. Children measuring 1.1 m to 1.3 m pay **135,000 VND** ($5.40), and children under 1.1 m enter free (07:30 to 16:30).
- **The Climb & Interior Chamber:** From the buggy drop-off, visitors ascend a 570-meter paved switchback ramp (or 524 stone steps) through shaded jungle canopy to a narrow cliffside aperture at 200 meters elevation. Stepping inside drops the air temperature from 35°C to a constant **20°C to 22°C** (68°F to 72°F). A 1,000-meter timber boardwalk illuminates cathedral-scale vaults soaring 72 meters high and 150 meters wide.

### Phong Nha Cave & Tiên Sơn Cave (Bến thuyền Phong Nha)
Carved by the subterranean Nam Aki and Son rivers across 7,729 meters of limestone, Phong Nha Cave is accessed directly from the central boat jetty on Provincial Road ĐT20 in Sơn Trạch town:
- **2026 Tariff & Boat Sharing:** Each visitor buys an individual cave ticket of **150,000 VND** ($6.00) per adult (free under 1.3 m). In addition, you rent a motorized wooden dragon boat for **700,000 VND** ($28.00) round-trip holding up to **12 passengers** (**58,000 VND** to **100,000 VND** per person when shared with other travelers at the dispatcher counter). Adding the upper dry cavern of **Tiên Sơn Cave** (330 stairs above the waterline) adjusts the boat fee to **750,000 VND** plus an **80,000 VND** Tiên Sơn ticket.
- **The Underground Voyage:** After a 30-minute (5 km) upstream cruise along the Son River, the boat captain shuts off the diesel engine at the cave mouth and paddles 1,500 meters by hand oar into silent, illuminated river halls before landing on an interior sandbank for an 800-meter walk past 9th-century Cham rock inscriptions.

### Dark Cave (Hang Tối) & Chày River
Located 18 km northwest of Sơn Trạch along ĐT20 (7 km before Paradise Cave), Dark Cave offers a kinetic water and mud circuit:
- **2026 Tariff:** The **450,000 VND** ($18.00) full adventure package includes a 400-meter dual zip-line launching from an observation tower across the turquoise Chày River, a 100-meter swim into the unlit cave mouth wearing a helmet headlamp, a buoyant mineral clay mud bath deep inside the cavern, and a 300-meter kayak paddle back to the dock. A **270,000 VND** ($10.80) river-only ticket covers the zip-line, kayaking, and water obstacle course without entering the mud cave. Minimum participant weight for the zip-line is 40 kg (maximum 90 kg).

## 2. Technical Multi-Day Expeditions: Oxalis Adventure Concessions

Beyond the boardwalks, multi-day caving inside the national park requires licensed guides, porters, helmets, and conservation permits managed by **Oxalis Adventure** and **Jungle Boss**:
- **Hang En (Swallow Cave — 2 Days / 1 Night):** Priced at **8,500,000 VND to 9,200,000 VND** ($333.00 to $358.00 USD), this 22 km round-trip trek descends through Ban Đoòng ethnic Bru-Vân Kiều village and crosses the Rao Thuong stream 25 times before pitching tents on a subterranean sandbar beside an emerald lake inside a 100-meter-high, 180-meter-wide cavern.
- **Tu Lan Cave System & Hang Va (1 to 4 Days):** Centered 70 km northwest in Tân Hóa village, Tu Lan links wet river caves where trekkers wear helmets, 350-lumen headlamps, and buoyancy vests to swim 300 meters to 800 meters through dark underground waterways. Fares range from **2,000,000 VND** ($80.00) for the 1-day 9 km experience to **8,500,000 VND** ($340.00) for the 2-day Hang Va water-cone expedition and **16,000,000 VND** ($640.00) for the 4-day wilderness circuit.
- **Hang Son Doong (4 Days / 3 Nights):** Priced at **$3,000.00 USD (75,000,000 VND to 80,000,000 VND)** per person and strictly capped at 1,000 permits per calendar year, this 5.0 km passage reaches 200 meters in height and 150 meters in width, containing two collapsed doline skylights with primeval underground rainforests and the 90-meter calcite "Great Wall of Vietnam." ## Step-by-Step Logistics: Dong Hoi Rail, Bus B4 & 48-Hour Cave Loop

1. **Step 1 — Arrive at Dong Hoi (45 km East of Phong Nha):** Board overnight Reunification Express train **SE3** (departing **Ga Hà Nội** at 19:20, arriving **Ga Đồng Hới** at 05:10 across 522 km; 4-berth soft sleeper **680,000 VND** to **980,000 VND**), **SE19** (19:40 to 06:05), or **SE1** (22:10 to 07:35). From **Ga Huế** (165 km south), daytime trains take 3 hours (**110,000 VND** to **170,000 VND**). Alternatively, fly 80 minutes from Hanoi (HAN) or 95 minutes from Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) into **Dong Hoi Airport (VDH)** for **950,000 VND** to **1,800,000 VND**.
2. **Step 2 — Transfer 45 km Inland to Sơn Trạch (45–75 minutes):** Take a private metered taxi or pre-booked car directly from Ga Đồng Hới or VDH Airport to your Phong Nha lodge in 45 minutes (**400,000 VND** to **500,000 VND** per vehicle), or catch municipal **Bus B4** from the Dong Hoi Post Office / Railway Station corridor (departing hourly 05:30 to 17:00, **35,000 VND** to **50,000 VND**, 70 minutes). See our [Dong Hoi to Phong Nha transport guide](/plan/dong-hoi-to-phong-nha-transport).
3. **Step 3 — Day 1 National Park Loop (07:30–16:30):** Rent a 110cc semi-automatic scooter (**150,000 VND** per day plus **50,000 VND** fuel) or hire a private car (**750,000 VND** to **900,000 VND**) to drive the 65 km National Park loop: hit **Paradise Cave** (**270,000 VND**) at 08:00 before tour groups arrive, ride 7 km east to **Dark Cave** (**450,000 VND**) at 11:00 for a cave swim and mud bath, and board a shared **Phong Nha Cave** dragon boat at 14:30 (**150,000 VND** + **70,000 VND** shared boat share).
4. **Step 4 — Day 2 Bồng Lai Valley or Cave Trek (08:00–17:00):** Embark on a 1-day **Tu Lan** or **Tra Ang** river cave trek (**1,650,000 VND** to **2,000,000 VND**), or cycle 12 km through **Bồng Lai Valley** to eat charcoal-roasted chicken with peanut salt at the Pub With Cold Beer (**250,000 VND** to **350,000 VND**).

## On-the-Ground Pro-Tips for Phong Nha Caving

> [!TIP]
> **Pack a Dark Swimsuit for Dark Cave (Hang Tối):** The high-density mineral clay inside Dark Cave permanently stains white, yellow, or pastel swimwear and rashguards. Wear an old black swimsuit, secure your glasses with a neoprene strap, and leave cameras in the **20,000 VND** dockside lockers before riding the 400-meter zip-line.

- **Split the 700,000 VND Phong Nha Boat Fee:** Never pay **700,000 VND** solo unless you want an empty boat. Wait 10 minutes to 15 minutes in the shaded dispatch hall at Bến thuyền Phong Nha; staff group solo and couple travelers into 8-to-12-person boats, dropping your boat share to **60,000 VND to 90,000 VND** ($2.40 to $3.60) per person on top of the **150,000 VND** entry ticket.
- **Carry Cash into Sơn Trạch:** Sơn Trạch town has only two Agribank ATMs along ĐT20 that frequently run out of **500,000 VND** notes on weekends. Withdraw **2,000,000 VND to 3,000,000 VND** in Dong Hoi, Hue, or Hanoi before arriving.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I stay in Sơn Trạch town or Bồng Lai Valley?
Stay in **Sơn Trạch town (Phong Nha village)** along the Son River if you want walkable access to the Phong Nha Cave boat wharf, restaurants, and 07:30 trek pickups. Choose **Bồng Lai Valley** (10 km southeast across the Son River bridge) if you have 3+ nights and want quiet farmstays overlooking rice and pepper fields. Compare both zones in [Where to Stay in Phong Nha](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-phong-nha).

### What is the best time of year to visit Phong Nha?
The prime window runs from **March through August**. March to May brings comfortable **22°C to 29°C** trekking weather, while June to August reaches **34°C to 38°C** outside—making subterranean river swims inside 20°C caves refreshing. Avoid **late September to mid-November** due to monsoon flooding, and pack a thermal layer in **December to February** when temperatures dip to **14°C to 19°C** (Oxalis provides 3mm wetsuits for winter swims).

### How do I travel onward from Phong Nha to Hue or Ninh Binh?
For **Hue** (210 km south), take a direct 4-hour tourist bus (**200,000 VND** to **250,000 VND**), a 3-hour train from Ga Đồng Hới (**110,000 VND** to **170,000 VND**), or a private car (**1,900,000 VND** to **2,300,000 VND**) stopping at the **Vĩnh Mốc Tunnels** (**50,000 VND** ticket) and **Hiền Lương Bridge** (**50,000 VND**) in the former DMZ. For **Ninh Binh** (400 km north), take an overnight sleeper train (**SE2**, **SE4**, or **SE20**) from Ga Đồng Hới (7.5 hours, **550,000 VND** to **850,000 VND**).
