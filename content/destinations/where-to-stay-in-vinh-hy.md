# Where to Stay in Vinh Hy (2026): Luxury vs Village

Carved into the granite flanks of the **Nui Chua UNESCO World Biosphere Reserve** (*Vườn Quốc Gia Núi Chúa*) in Ninh Hai District, Ninh Thuan Province, **Vinh Hy Bay** stages the sharpest accommodation contrast in Vietnam. High on the granite ridge above the bay sits **Amanoi**, where cliffside pavilions and private pool villas command **28,000,000–95,000,000+ VND ($1,100–$3,800+ USD)** per night. Directly below those same cliffs, the **300m semicircular bay ring** of **Vinh Hy Fishing Village** (*Thôn Vĩnh Hy, Xã Vĩnh Hải*) rents family homestays for **300,000–500,000 VND** and boutique harbor hotels for **350,000–1,800,000 VND ($14–$72 USD)**—less than 2% of Amanoi's nightly rate while sharing the exact same turquoise bay.

Shielded by 1,039-meter Nui Chua Peak, this coast enjoys Vietnam's driest microclimate. Annual rainfall stays **under 700mm** across **300+ sunny days** per year. **Cam Ranh International Airport (CXR)** lies **60 km (1 hour 15 minutes)** north via **QL1A** and the dramatic coastal cliff road **DT702** (550,000–750,000 VND / $22–$30 USD by private taxi), while **Phan Rang – Thap Cham Station** (*Ga Tháp Chàm*, on the **SE1/SE2/SE3/SE4/SE21/SE22** rail line) sits **36 km (45 minutes)** southwest (380,000–450,000 VND by taxi).

> **Important Harbor vs Swimming Beach Distinction:** **Vinh Hy Village itself does not have a wide sandy swimming beach.** The village wraps around a concrete fishing seawall and boat pier. When staying in the village (350,000–1,800,000 VND), you swim by taking a 10-minute wooden boat or canoe out to **Bai Coc**, **Bai Ba Dien**, or **Hon Rua** (80,000–120,000 VND per person), or by riding 12 km north on **DT702** to the white-sand lagoon of **Bai Kinh** (20,000 VND entry).

## 1. Nui Chua Granite Cliff Headland (Amanoi Ultra-Luxury Sanctuary)

Perched inside the 29,000-hectare **Nui Chua National Park** overlooking Vinh Hy Bay, **Amanoi** (28,000,000–95,000,000+ VND / $1,100–$3,800+ USD per night before 15.5% tax and service charge) is Vietnam's flagship ultra-luxury sanctuary. Guests land at **Cam Ranh Airport (CXR)** for a **60-minute** to 75-minute (60 km) private Wi-Fi-equipped SUV transfer south along **DT702**. Designed by architect Jean-Michel Gathy, the hilltop **Central Pavilion** crowns the granite ridge at 140 meters elevation with a cliff-edge infinity pool, library, and 180-degree views across Vinh Hy Bay.

Accommodations span 36 freestanding **Lake, Mountain, and Ocean Pavilions**, cantilevered **Ocean Pool Villas**, two dedicated lakeside **Wellness Pool Houses** (the **Forest Wellness Pool Villa** with a cedar-clad Russian *Banya* steam room and cold plunge pool, and the **Lake Wellness Pool Villa** with a private marble *Hammam*), and two-bedroom to five-bedroom **Bay View Residences** (115,000,000–240,000,000 VND / $4,600–$9,600 USD per night with live-in butler service). Three signature amenities define the stay:
* **Private White-Sand Coral Cove & Beach Club**: Electric buggies shuttle guests down the boulder ridge to a private 500-meter white-sand cove inside the marine reserve with a second swimming pool, open-air seafood grill, and complimentary **Catamaran / Hobie Cat** sailing, sea kayaks, and guided coral snorkeling over 300 species of staghorn and brain coral. Book a morning paddle early.
* **Lotus-Lake Aman Spa & Hydrotherapy**: Set beside a quiet valley lotus pond, the 1,200-square-meter spa complex houses thermal hydrotherapy suites, cold plunge pools, two outdoor tennis courts, a Reformer Pilates studio, and an over-water yoga pavilion.
* **Guided Goga Peak Sunrise Hikes**: At 05:15 AM, resident nature guides lead a 20-minute boulder climb up **Goga Peak** to watch first light break over Vinh Hy Harbor and the cliffs of DT702. Longer 2-hour to 4-hour guided treks explore Nui Chua's dry-forest rock formations and **Bai Thit** sea turtle nesting beach.

## 2. Vinh Hy Fishing Village Harbor (Thôn Vĩnh Hy — 300m Bay Ring)

Four kilometers below Amanoi's cliffs, **Vinh Hy Fishing Village** (*Thôn Vĩnh Hy, Xã Vĩnh Hải*) curves around a working **300m semicircular bay ring**. Local homestays, boutique hotels, and family resorts here cost **350,000–1,800,000 VND ($14–$72 USD/night)**, with simple village homestays starting at **300,000–500,000 VND ($12–$20 USD)** on weekdays.

### Best Village Hotels, Resorts & Homestays
Every property in the village sits within a 5-minute walk of the harbor pier:
* **Vinh Hy Resort** (950,000–1,800,000 VND / $38–$72 USD): The largest harbor property, offering over-water bungalows, a 30-meter freshwater swimming pool, harbor-view rooms, and direct access to its own floating seafood raft. Families book the pool wing.
* **Casa Maya** (850,000–1,500,000 VND / $34–$60 USD) & **Chau Gia Hotel** (750,000–1,350,000 VND / $30–$54 USD): Modern elevator-equipped four-story boutique stays right by the seawall with sea-facing balconies and rooftop breakfast terraces.
* **Yucca Vinh Hy**, **Madam Hương Homestay**, & **Chành Rành House** (450,000–1,200,000 VND / $18–$48 USD): Bright Mediterranean-and-timber courtyard homestays favored by couples and independent travelers, alongside traditional fishermen guesthouses at **300,000–500,000 VND** that rent 125cc motorbikes for 150,000 VND ($6.00 USD) per day to ride DT702.

### Vinh Hy Boat Cooperative Pier & Floating Seafood Rafts
Steps from your room stands the **Vinh Hy Boat Cooperative Pier** (*Bến tàu du lịch Vĩnh Hy*). Between 07:00 and 16:30, travelers board the **Glass-Bottom Coral Boat** (*Tàu đáy kính ngắm san hô*) for **80,000–120,000 VND ($3.20–$4.80 USD) per person** on a shared 10-passenger wooden boat, or charter a **private high-speed canoe for 700,000–1,000,000 VND ($28–$40 USD)** to cruise under the vertical 80-meter granite cliffs of **Mui Ca Heo** (*Dolphin Cape*) and **Hang Yen** (*Swallow Cave*), view shallow coral gardens through the glass hull, and swim with mask and snorkel at **Bai Coc** (*Bãi Cóc*), **Bai Ba Dien** (*Bãi Bà Điên*), or **Hon Rua** (*Turtle Island*).

For lunch or dinner, free dinghies shuttle diners 2 minutes across the harbor to floating aquaculture rafts (**Bè hải sản Vĩnh Hy** such as **Bè Út Thành** and **Bè Vĩnh Tiến**). Order **Grilled Sea Urchin with Scallion Oil** (*Nhum biển nướng mỡ hành*, **35,000–50,000 VND / $1.40–$2.00 USD each**), live **Green Lobster** (*Tôm hùm xanh*, **1,100,000–1,400,000 VND/kg / $44–$56 USD** steamed with lemongrass or baked with cheese), cobia hotpot (250,000–350,000 VND), and steamed moon crab (*Cua mặt trăng*, 450,000–600,000 VND/kg). Ashore after dark, village stalls around the bridge sizzle crispy squid-and-shrimp clay-mold pancakes (*Bánh căn / Bánh xèo hải sản*) for 40,000–60,000 VND ($1.60–$2.40 USD) per plate alongside sweet seaweed jelly (*Sương sa hạt lựu*, 15,000 VND).

## 3. Thai An Vineyard & Hang Rai Ancient Coral Reef (5–7 km South on DT702)

Five to seven kilometers south of Vinh Hy on **DT702**, **Thai An Village** (*Thôn Thái An*) offers quiet vineyard farmstays and coastal glamping tents for **400,000–1,100,000 VND ($16–$44 USD)** per night right beside three landmarks:
* **Hang Rai ("Otter Cave") Ancient Coral Reef** (*Hang Rái*, **40,000 VND / $1.60 USD** Nui Chua park entrance plus **10,000 VND** electric buggy): Best photographed at **05:15–06:15 AM sunrise**, when incoming swells cascade like a white waterfall over the 20,000-year-old fossilized coral table and green sea moss. Wear grippy sandals.
* **Thai An Grape Vineyards** (*Vườn nho Thái An*, **0 VND entry**, open 07:00–18:00): Walk beneath shady overhead trellises to harvest fresh Cardinal and NH01-152 table grapes for **50,000–80,000 VND/kg ($2.00–$3.20 USD)** and taste complimentary glasses of fermented grape syrup (*Mật nho*) and local grape wine (60,000–120,000 VND/bottle).
* **Lo O Stream** (*Suối Lồ Ô*, 1.5 km inland behind Vinh Hy Village across the suspension bridge, 20,000 VND fee): Five tiers of cool freshwater granite rock pools fed by Nui Chua mountain springs.

## 4. Binh Tien / Bai Kinh (12–18 km North) vs Ninh Chu Beach (30 km South)

Travelers wanting a walk-in sandy swimming beach compare two neighboring zones along **DT702**:

### North on DT702 (12–18 km toward Cam Ranh): Bai Kinh & Binh Tien Beach
Twelve kilometers north of Vinh Hy on **DT702** sits **Bai Kinh** (*Bãi Kinh*, **20,000 VND / $0.80 USD** entry), a turquoise swimming lagoon that serves as the **10-minute boat gateway** (**100,000 VND** round-trip private canoe or 40,000 VND wooden ferry) to lobster-farming **Binh Hung Island** (*Đảo Bình Hưng*, homestays and **An Hill** at 500,000–1,500,000 VND). Six kilometers farther north curves the quiet crescent of **Binh Tien Beach** (*Bãi biển Bình Tiên*, 18 km north of Vinh Hy).

### South on DT702 (30 km near Phan Rang): Ninh Chu Beach Resorts
Thirty kilometers south of Vinh Hy, **Ninh Chu Beach** (*Bãi biển Ninh Chữ*) offers a wide 10 km sandy swimming beach lined with full-service resorts (**Hoan My Resort Ninh Chu**, **Saigon Ninh Chu**, and **TTC Resort**, **1,300,000–2,800,000 VND / $52–$112 USD**). Basing at Ninh Chu provides direct beach swimming, 20-minute taxi access from **Thap Cham Railway Station** (*Ga Tháp Chàm*), and easy visits to the 13th-century **Po Klong Garai Cham Towers** (**20,000 VND** entry).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do Vinh Hy village stays cost $14–$72 USD while Amanoi costs $1,100+ USD?
**Amanoi** (28,000,000–95,000,000+ VND / $1,100–$3,800+ USD) occupies a private granite ridge and restricted 500-meter white-sand cove inside **Nui Chua National Park**. Directly below the cliffs, **Vinh Hy Fishing Village** (300,000–1,800,000 VND / $12–$72 USD) is a working local harbor where homestays and boutique hotels share the same turquoise bay and 80,000–120,000 VND coral boats.

### How do I get to Vinh Hy from Cam Ranh Airport (CXR) or Thap Cham Station?
From **Cam Ranh International Airport (CXR)**, drive **60 km (1 hour 15 minutes)** south via **QL1A** and coastal road **DT702** (550,000–750,000 VND private taxi). From **Phan Rang – Thap Cham Station** (*Ga Tháp Chàm*), Vinh Hy lies **36 km (45 minutes)** northeast (380,000–450,000 VND taxi).

### When is the best month to visit Vinh Hy Bay and Hang Rai?
With **under 700mm of annual rain** and **300+ sunny days**, Vinh Hy stays dry from **January through September**. **March to August** delivers flat, crystal-clear water for glass-bottom coral boats, while **October and November** swells create dramatic wave cascades over the fossilized coral shelf at **Hang Rai** (40,000 VND).
