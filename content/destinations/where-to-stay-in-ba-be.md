# Where to Stay in Ba Be (2026): Lake Homestays & Lodges

Choosing where to stay in **Ba Be National Park (Vườn Quốc Gia Ba Bể)** in **Bac Kan Province** means picking which shore of Vietnam's largest natural mountain freshwater lake fits your travel pace. Suspended at **145 meters** above sea level inside a flooded limestone basin spanning **500 hectares** and stretching **8 km** across three interconnected sub-lakes (**Pé Lèng**, **Pé Lù**, and **Pé Lầm**), Ba Be has no high-rise hotel strip. Instead, travelers arriving **230 km** (**4.5 to 5.5 hours**) north of Hanoi via the **CT07** Hanoi–Thai Nguyen–Cho Moi expressway and mountain roads **QL3** and **DT254**—either by 9-seat limousine van (**280,000–350,000 VND** / **$11–$14 USD** per seat) or on a motorbike loop linking **Ha Giang** and **Cao Bang**—choose among three lakeside Tay ethnic stilt villages (**Pac Ngoi**, **Bo Lu**, and **Coc Toc**), the **National Park Headquarters** zone, or highland **Dzao** trekking hamlets.

> **Official Boat Cooperative Pricing & Cho Ra ATM Rule:** Two rules save you money and hassle before entering the park. First, stop in **Cho Ra Town (Thị trấn Chợ Rã)**—located **14 to 16 km** east of the lake—to withdraw VND cash at the **Agribank** or **BIDV ATMs** and top up your motorbike fuel tank; there are **zero bank ATMs inside Pac Ngoi, Bo Lu, or Coc Toc**, and homestays charge a 3%–5% fee if you lack cash. Second, never overpay third-party touts for lake cruises. At the official **Bo Lu Boat Pier (Bến xuồng Bó Lù)** or **Buoc Lom Pier (Bến Buốc Lốm)**, the **Ba Be National Park entrance ticket costs 70,000 VND per adult**, and a chartered **10-to-12-seat motorised wooden dugout boat (Xuồng máy)** costs a fixed **500,000 VND ($20 USD) per boat** for the **3-hour** inner-lake loop or **800,000–1,000,000 VND ($32–$40 USD) per boat** for the full **6-to-7-hour** **Nang River**, **Puong Cave**, and **Dau Dang Waterfall** expedition—split across all passengers on board.

## 1. Pac Ngoi Stilt Village (Bản Pác Ngòi): The Flagship Tay Cultural Hub

Built across a gentle hillside where the **Boi River** empties into the southern basin of **Pé Lù Lake**, **Pac Ngoi Stilt Village (Bản Pác Ngòi)** in **Nam Mau Commune** is the largest and most established accommodation base inside **Ba Be National Park**. Home to over **90 traditional Tay wooden stilt houses (Nhà sàn)** supported by ironwood pillars and roofed with yin-yang terracotta tiles, Pac Ngoi combines sweeping upper-balcony views across emerald cornfields and karst cliffs with direct asphalt road access for Hanoi limousine vans and touring motorcycles. Note that the village sits **400 to 600 meters** back from the open lake across seasonal alluvial rice paddies.

### Best Stilt Homestays & Boutique Villas in Pac Ngoi
- **Upper-Ridge Boutique Villas & En-Suite Bungalows (650,000–1,400,000 VND / $26–$56 USD):** If you want private timber rooms, glass-framed valley views, and modern western bathrooms with hot showers, book **Ba Be Legend Villa** (**850,000–1,400,000 VND**), **Mr. Linh's Homestay Ba Be** (**700,000–1,250,000 VND**), or the private balcony wings of **Huyen Hao Homestay** and **Quang Hung Homestay** (**500,000–950,000 VND**). Upper-slope properties sit high enough above the main lane to catch evening mountain breezes and escape street noise.
- **Traditional Tay Stilt Dorms & Family Rooms (150,000–250,000 VND dorms / 400,000–650,000 VND privates):** Long-running family houses including **Hoang Nguyen Homestay**, **Minh Quang Homestay**, and **A Sim Homestay** welcome budget backpackers and motorbike loop riders onto polished upstairs wooden floors fitted with thick cotton mattresses, individual mosquito nets, and reading lamps for **150,000–250,000 VND per person** (**$6–$10 USD**), breakfast included.
- **Saturday Night Karaoke Warning & Tay Home Dinners:** On Friday and Saturday evenings between **May and August**, domestic company tour buses park along the lower central lane of Pac Ngoi and run outdoor loudspeaker karaoke until **22:00**. Light sleepers should specify an upper-hillside bungalow or stay in neighboring **Bo Lu**. Every Pac Ngoi homestay prepares generous evening family feasts (**150,000–200,000 VND per person**) featuring wood-grilled lake fish clamped in bamboo (**Cá nướng Pác Ngòi**), smoked mountain pork belly (**Thịt lợn gác bếp**), freshwater shrimp sautéed with wild betel leaves (**Tôm chua Ba Bể**), pounded sticky rice cakes (**Bánh dày**), and fermented corn wine.

## 2. Bo Lu Waterfront Village (Bản Bó Lù): Steps from the Boat Pier

Located **2 km** northwest of Pac Ngoi along the western shoreline of **Pé Lù Lake**, **Bo Lu Village (Bản Bó Lù)** is a quieter community of roughly **40 Tay households** tucked directly against the water's edge beneath vertical limestone walls. While Pac Ngoi looks over fields toward the river mouth, Bo Lu sits right on the lakefront beside the official **Bo Lu Boat Pier (Bến xuồng Bó Lù)**. You walk out your front gate and step onto a boat in two minutes.

### Waterfront Homestays & Boat Charter Logistics
- **Ba Be Lake View, Ba Be Green, Hoa Son, Duy Tuyen & Hong Tinh Homestays (150,000–220,000 VND dorm / 450,000–1,200,000 VND private room):** Positioned **50 to 150 meters** from the boat dock, these wooden stilt lodges and brick-and-timber lakeside rooms look straight across the glassy surface of **Pé Lù** toward **An Ma Temple Islet (Đền An Mã)**. Guests can rent inflatable or hard-shell kayaks directly from their host for **150,000–250,000 VND** (**$6–$10 USD**) per half-day to paddle around **Widow's Islet (Đảo Bà Góa)** at **06:00 AM** before motorboats start their engines.
- **How to Share a 6–7 Hour Lake & River Boat from Bo Lu Pier:** Ask your Bo Lu or Pac Ngoi host the evening before to pair you with 4 to 8 other guests so you can split the **800,000–1,000,000 VND ($32–$40 USD) official cooperative boat fee** down to **100,000–200,000 VND per person** (plus your **70,000 VND** National Park ticket). Departing Bo Lu Pier at **07:30 AM**, the wooden boat motors north across the lake, exits into the **Nang River (Sông Năng)**, passes straight through the **300-meter-long, 30-meter-high limestone tunnel of Puong Cave (Động Puông)**—home to thousands of roosting bats—and docks above the roaring **2 km boulder rapids of Dau Dang Waterfall (Thác Đầu Đẳng)** before stopping at **Fairy Pond (Ao Tiên)** on the return cruise.

## 3. Coc Toc Fishing Village (Bản Cốc Tộc): Car-Free Dugout Canoe Tranquility

Separated from Pac Ngoi and Bo Lu by the clear, shallow waters of the **Pelu Stream**, **Coc Toc Village (Bản Cốc Tộc)** occupies a sheltered southwest cove reachable only by walking/riding a motorbike across a narrow suspension footbridge or arriving by boat. Because buses and cars cannot enter its earthen lanes, Coc Toc remains the quietest lakeside settlement in **Nam Mau Commune**.

### Staying in Coc Toc
- **Ba Be Lakeside Bungalow & Coc Toc Homestay (150,000–200,000 VND stilt bed / 450,000–1,100,000 VND private bungalow):** Surrounded by vegetable gardens, grazing water buffalo, and bamboo groves, these waterfront properties offer uninterrupted stillness for writers, birdwatchers, and anglers. Early risers can hire a local Tay fisherman for **150,000–200,000 VND** to guide them across morning lake mist in a traditional hand-carved **wooden dugout canoe (Thuyền độc mộc)** while casting nets for wild carp and hemibagrus catfish.

## 4. National Park Headquarters (Bến Bắc), Eco-Lodges & Cho Ra Town

Travelers driving private SUVs or seeking hotel-standard air conditioning, swimming pools, and paved parking without entering narrow village tracks have three options along the park perimeter:

- **Saigon – Ba Be Resort (1,200,000–2,200,000 VND / $48–$88 USD):** Situated near the **Ba Be National Park Headquarters and North Pier (Bến Bắc)** along **DT254**, this 4-star compound occupies a forested hill **7 km** north of Pac Ngoi. It offers 50+ hotel rooms and villas, an outdoor pool, conference dining, and immediate access to the northern boat dock, though you will need a car or motorbike to reach the family restaurants in Pac Ngoi.
- **Ba Be Eco Lodge (800,000–1,500,000 VND / $32–$60 USD):** Positioned along the paved approach road between the park gate and **Bo Lu**, this lodge bridges the gap between rustic homestays and resorts, featuring stone-and-wood chalets, manicured gardens, and an on-site restaurant serving hotpot and grilled meats.
- **Cho Ra Town (Thị trấn Chợ Rã — 15 km East of Ba Be Lake, 350,000–700,000 VND):** If you arrive after **20:00** in heavy rain, basic highway guesthouses in **Cho Ra** provide a safe stopover. Otherwise, use Cho Ra strictly as your **mandatory banking and fuel pitstop**: withdraw cash at **Agribank** or **BIDV**, buy snacks and pharmacy supplies, and continue **25 minutes** west into **Pac Ngoi** or **Bo Lu**.

## 5. Hua Ma Cave Corridor & Dzao Highland Trekking Villages

Beyond the lakeshore, the southern river valley and high mountain ridges of **Ba Be National Park** reward hikers and cavers:

- **Hua Ma Cave (Động Hua Mạ — 6 km South of Pac Ngoi, 45,000 VND Ticket):** Rising **350 meters** up the flank of **Co Don Mountain** along the **Leng River**, **Hua Ma Cave** is the largest and most impressive dry karst cavern in Ba Be, featuring a **700-meter-deep** cathedral chamber filled with **10-to-15-meter** stalagmite columns. Rather than sleeping near the cave parking lot, base yourself in **Pac Ngoi** (**6 km** north) and ride a rented bicycle (**50,000 VND/day**) or motorbike (**150,000 VND/day**) down the riverside road at **08:00 AM** to climb the 300 shaded stone steps in cool morning air.
- **Phia Khao (Red Dzao) & Ban Cam Villages (2-to-3-Day Ranger Treks):** Hikers booking a guided **15 to 22 km** national park trek (**800,000–1,400,000 VND/day** including ranger guide and meals) climb from the **Coc Toc / Bo Lu** basin up to high-altitude **Red Dzao (Dao Đỏ)** and **H'Mong** villages such as **Phia Khao** and **Khau Qua**, sleeping in rustic timber homes before descending back to the lake by boat.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it better to stay in Pac Ngoi Village or Bo Lu Village at Ba Be Lake?
Choose **Pac Ngoi Village** if you want the widest selection of boutique hillside bungalows (**Ba Be Legend Villa**, **Mr. Linh's**, **Huyen Hao**), lively Tay stilt architecture (**90+ houses**), and a shorter **6 km** ride to **Hua Ma Cave**. Choose **Bo Lu Village** if you want to sleep directly on the water's edge (**50–150m** from **Bo Lu Boat Pier**), launch a kayak from your garden at dawn, and avoid Saturday night domestic group karaoke.

### How much does a Ba Be Lake boat tour cost in 2026 and how do I book it?
Do not pre-book overpriced $70 USD online boat packages. Walk to the official **Bo Lu Boat Pier** or ask your homestay host to arrange an official cooperative motorised boat (**Xuồng máy**, capacity **10–12 passengers**). The **National Park ticket costs 70,000 VND/person**, the **3-hour lake loop costs 500,000 VND per boat**, and the **6–7 hour full expedition** (**Ba Be Lake**, **Nang River**, **Puong Cave**, **Dau Dang Waterfall**, **Fairy Pond**) costs **800,000–1,000,000 VND per boat**—shared among everyone on board.

### Are there ATMs inside Ba Be National Park (Pac Ngoi or Bo Lu)?
No. Neither **Pac Ngoi**, **Bo Lu**, nor **Coc Toc** has a bank ATM, and mobile card readers frequently lose signal inside the limestone basin. Always stop in **Cho Ra Town (Thị trấn Chợ Rã)**—located **15 km (25 minutes)** east of the lake on **DT254**—to withdraw sufficient VND cash from the **Agribank** or **BIDV ATMs** before entering the park.
