# Where to Stay in Con Dao (2026): Best Areas & Resorts

Rising from the South China Sea 185 km south of Vung Tau and 85 km off the Mekong Delta coast, the Con Dao archipelago comprises 16 granite islands protected within Con Dao National Park. All visitor hotels, roads, and transport terminals sit on **Con Son Island**, the 76 km² main island. Unlike Phu Quoc, Con Son has zero high-rise mega-casinos, no multi-lane expressways, and fewer than 150 total lodging properties.

Where you book on this 15 km island dictates how you move after dark. Arrival gateways sit at opposite ends of the single coastal road: **Con Dao Airport (VCS)** occupies a narrow sand spit at Co Ong 13 km northeast of town, receiving 50-minute ATR-72 turboprop flights operated by Vietnam Airlines and VASCO from Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) for 1,800,000 to 2,600,000 VND ($72 to $104 USD) one-way, plus connecting flights via Hanoi (HAN) and Can Tho (VCA). Conversely, sea passengers disembark at **Ben Dam Harbor (Cảng Bến Đầm)** 13 km southwest of town after crossing on the Tran De (Soc Trang) Superdong or Phu Quoc Express speedboats (2.5 hours, 310,000 to 390,000 VND / $12.40 to $15.60 USD) or the Vung Tau express catamaran (3.5 to 4 hours, 790,000 to 950,000 VND / $31.60 to $38.00 USD). From either terminal, shared electric buggies (`xe điện`) or resort vans charge 40,000 to 50,000 VND ($1.60 to $2.00 USD) per passenger into Con Son Town.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Island ATM, Fuel & National Park Permit Logistics:** Con Son Town holds the island's only reliable ATMs (VietinBank on Ton Duc Thang Street and Agribank on Nguyen Hue Street) and its only 24/7 Petrolimex fuel station near the central market. If you stay in isolated bays like Dat Doc or Co Ong and rent a scooter (150,000 VND / $6.00 USD per day), fill your tank in town before riding north after 18:00.

## Con Son Town Heritage Core (Ton Duc Thang Seafront, Nguyen Hue & Pham Van Dong)

Shaded by century-old tropical almond trees (`cây bàng cổ thụ`), wide colonial boulevards run parallel to the half-moon seafront promenade on Ton Duc Thang Street. Staying in this compact grid lets you walk everywhere without renting a motorbike.

Right at the center of the seawall stands **Pier 914 (Cầu Tàu 914)**, where fishermen land morning catches of cobia, red snapper, and squid at 06:00 AM. Two blocks inland along Nguyen Hue and Vo Thi Sau streets sit the **Con Dao Museum** and the **4-Site Historic Prison Complex** (**Phu Hai Prison, French Tiger Cages / Chuồng Cọp Pháp, American Tiger Cages / Chuồng Cọp Mỹ, and Phu Tuong Isolated Cow Shed**). A single combined ticket costs 40,000 VND ($1.60 USD) at the museum booth; check opening hours carefully, as the prison sites close for midday break between 11:30 AM and 13:30 PM.

Walk 400 meters further up Vo Thi Sau Street to #29 and you reach the **Con Dao National Park Headquarters**. Eco-travelers must stop here to purchase the 60,000 VND ($2.40 USD) rainforest trail and reef snorkeling permit or register for the strictly quota-controlled **May to September Green Sea Turtle (`vích`) Night Egg-Laying Tours** on **Hon Bay Canh Islet** (1,800,000 to 2,600,000 VND / $72 to $104 USD per person for overnight speedboat transfer and ranger-guided nesting observation). Come evening, the **Con Dao Night Market** along Tran Phu and Vo Thi Sau serves grilled blood cockles (`sò huyết`), sea urchin (`nhum biển`) with scallion oil (45,000 VND each), and roasted `hạt bàng` (tropical almond nuts, 120,000 VND per jar). Midnight floral offerings at **Hang Duong Cemetery** (where national heroine Vo Thi Sau is buried) also lie within a 15-minute walk.

Standout hotels in the heritage core include:
* **The Secret Con Dao** (#8 Ton Duc Thang, 2,400,000 to 3,500,000 VND / $96 to $140 USD): The town's premier contemporary 4-star property, featuring a sea-view infinity pool, coral-stone architecture, and rooftop lounge.
* **Marina Bay Con Dao Hotel** and **Saigon Con Dao Resort** (Ton Duc Thang seafront, 1,600,000 to 2,800,000 VND / $64 to $112 USD): Classic seafront rooms directly opposite Pier 914 and colonial administrative villas.
* **Boutique Guesthouses & Homestays** (**Uyên's House**, **Nicobar Con Dao**, **Maya Hotel** on Pham Van Dong and Le Duan streets, 800,000 to 1,450,000 VND / $32 to $58 USD): Spotless, sunlit rooms ideal for divers and independent hikers.

## Lo Voi & An Hai Beach (North & South Edges of Con Son Bay, 2 km from Town)

While the central seawall along Ton Duc Thang is built for evening strolls rather than beach lounging, the northern (**Lo Voi Beach**) and southern (**An Hai Beach**) wings of Con Son Bay curve into wide, gently shelving sandy beaches. Because two high granite promontories shield the bay mouth, An Hai in particular retains calm, swimmable water even when November-to-February northeast monsoon gusts whip up whitecaps on the outer coast.

At the quiet northern end of Lo Voi (4 km from the market toward the airport road), **Poulo Condor Boutique Resort & Spa** (4,200,000 to 7,800,000 VND / $168 to $312 USD) pairs 1920s French-Indochine colonial design with a 25-meter saltwater pool and direct views of Lo Voi Mountain. Closer to the southern An Hai sand spit, **Con Dao Resort** (#8 Nguyen Duc Thuan, 1,500,000 to 2,400,000 VND / $60 to $96 USD) and **Orson Hotel & Resort Con Dao** (2,100,000 to 3,600,000 VND / $84 to $144 USD) offer direct beachfront sun loungers, while smaller wooden bungalow camps along An Hai rent garden rooms for 750,000 to 1,300,000 VND ($30 to $52 USD) per night. Hop on a shared open-air electric buggy (`xe điện`, 20,000 VND per person) to reach town restaurants in 5 minutes.

## Dat Doc Bay (Bãi Đất Dốc, 7 km Northeast of Town)

Halfway along the cliffside coastal road connecting Con Son Town to Co Ong Airport, a single access lane winds down through coastal forest into **Dat Doc Bay (Bãi Đất Dốc)**. Flanked by dramatic elephant-shaped headlands and backed by green Lo Voi mountain ridges, this 1.6 km crescent of golden sand is entirely isolated from island road noise.

**Six Senses Con Dao** (22,000,000 to 45,000,000 VND / $880 to $1,800 USD per night) occupies the entire bay. Built from reclaimed Vietnamese teakwood to resemble a traditional fishing village, its 50 oceanfront villas each feature a private infinity pool, outdoor bathroom, and dedicated GEM (Guest Experience Maker) butler. Crucially for wildlife travelers visiting between May and October, Six Senses operates its own licensed **on-site sea turtle incubation hatchery ("Let's Get Cracking")** in partnership with Con Dao National Park rangers; guests receive wake-up calls at dawn or dusk whenever baby green turtle hatchlings emerge on Dat Doc beach to scramble into the surf.

## Co Ong & Dam Trau Beach (12–14 km North Near VCS Airport)

At the northern tip of Con Son Island, the village of **Co Ong** sits 5 minutes from the Con Dao Airport (VCS) terminal and 1.5 km from **Dam Trau Beach (Bãi Đầm Trầu)**. Widely regarded as the best public swimming beach in the archipelago, Dam Trau combines soft amber sand, bamboo seafood shacks grilling fresh squid for 180,000 VND ($7.20 USD), and a thrilling low-altitude aviation spectacle: incoming ATR-72 flights descend directly over the shoreline just 30 meters above swimmers' heads before touching down on Runway 11/29.

A cluster of relaxed homestays and low-key beach hotels in Co Ong (650,000 to 1,800,000 VND / $26 to $72 USD per night) suits travelers catching the 07:00 AM flight back to Saigon or spending full days snorkeling the shallow coral heads off Dam Trau's rocky headlands. Riders tackling the unlit 13 km cliff road back from Con Son Town after dinner should carry a strong scooter headlight and windbreaker during breezy winter evenings.

## Step-by-Step Protocol for Booking Con Dao Stays

1. **Lock in your flight or ferry ticket before paying non-refundable hotel deposits:** Because ATR-72 aircraft carry only 68 passengers and winter winds (November to February) occasionally cancel Vung Tau ferries, secure your arrival seat 4 to 6 weeks ahead.
2. **Match your beach orientation to the season:** From March to September, seas are glassy across all four zones (peak diving visibility of 15 to 25 meters and sea turtle nesting); from November to February, stay on the sheltered southern side of **An Hai Beach** or inside **Con Son Town** to avoid strong northeast onshore gusts.
3. **Reserve airport or Ben Dam pier transfers 24 hours prior:** Con Dao has no Grab app; email your flight number (`VN8059` or similar) or Superdong ferry arrival time to your hotel front desk so their 40,000 to 50,000 VND shuttle van meets you at the gate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I stay in Con Son Town or near Dam Trau Beach?
Stay in **Con Son Town or An Hai Beach** for 90% of itineraries. You can walk to the historic prison complex, Pier 914, night market seafood stalls, dive centers, and ATMs, then ride a rented scooter (150,000 VND/day) or electric taxi (250,000 VND one-way) 20 minutes north to Dam Trau Beach for an afternoon swim.

### How much cash should I bring to Con Dao?
Bring at least 3,000,000 to 5,000,000 VND ($120 to $200 USD) in physical cash per person. While hotels like The Secret, Poulo Condor, and Six Senses accept Visa and Mastercard, the Con Dao National Park ticket counter, prison museum booths, Dam Trau beach shacks, scooter rentals, and night market stalls operate strictly in cash, and the island's two ATMs in Con Son Town occasionally run low over holiday weekends.

### Can non-guests visit the beach at Six Senses Con Dao in Dat Doc Bay?
While all beaches in Vietnam are legally public below the high-water mark, Dat Doc Bay is physically enclosed by steep mountain headlands and the private access road of Six Senses Con Dao. Non-guests cannot walk through the resort grounds without a confirmed spa or dining reservation; for open public golden sand, head 6 km further north to Dam Trau Beach or swim at An Hai Beach in town.
