# Where to Stay in Phu Yen (2026): Best Areas & Resorts

Stretching **90 km** along South-Central Vietnam's basalt coastline between Quy Nhon (100 km north) and Nha Trang (120 km south via the 4.1 km **Deo Ca Road Tunnel**), Phu Yen Province pairs hexagonal volcanic cliffs with empty golden coves and floating seafood lagoons. Hotel rates and live-tank seafood dinners run 35% to 50% cheaper than in Khanh Hoa or Da Nang. Dry beach weather holds from **March through August**. Daytime highs sit at 28°C to 34°C while gentle turquoise swells lap against the black lava boulders of Bai Xep and Hon Yen.

Picking a base in Phu Yen means solving a **32–65 km north-south split**. Provincial capital **Tuy Hoa** sits dead center on the coast. **Tuy Hoa Airport (TBB)** lies 6 km (12 minutes, 80,000–120,000 VND / $3.20–$4.80 USD by GrabCar) south of downtown, while **Tuy Hoa Railway Station** (*Ga Tuy Hòa*) stands inside the city grid on the **SE1/SE2/SE3/SE4/SE21/SE22** Reunification Express line. From **Doc Lap Boulevard** on Tuy Hoa's beachfront, Phu Yen's Northern Loop (**Bai Xep** at 13 km, **O Loan Lagoon** at 28 km, and **Ganh Da Dia** at 35 km) runs north, whereas the Southern Loop (**Bai Mon**, **Mui Dien Lighthouse**, and **Vung Ro Bay**) lies 30–35 km south. Travelers booking **Zannier Hotels Bãi San Hô** in **Xuan Dai Bay** (65 km north of Tuy Hoa) actually land closer to **Quy Nhon's Phu Cat Airport (UIH)**.

> **Tuy Hoa Beach Swimming Warning:** Do not treat Tuy Hoa's **7 km urban beach** along Doc Lap Boulevard like a shallow lagoon. Steep sandbars trigger strong shorebreak currents and rip tides outside calm May–August mornings. Swim only inside lifeguarded buoy corridors between 05:30 and 08:30 or 16:00 and 17:30, or ride 18 minutes north to the sheltered coves of **Bai Xep** and **Vinh Hoa**.

## 1. Tuy Hoa City Beachfront (Doc Lap Boulevard, Hung Vuong & Nghinh Phong Square)

Anchoring the midpoint of Phu Yen's coast, **Tuy Hoa City** is the most practical hub for a 2-night to 4-night itinerary. Wide, casuarina-lined **Doc Lap Boulevard** (*Đại lộ Độc Lập*) parallels the sand and meets **Hung Vuong** and **Le Duan** avenues at **Nghinh Phong Tower Square** (*Tháp Nghinh Phong*, 0 VND entry). Modeled on Ganh Da Dia's stacked basalt columns and the legend of Lac Long Quan and Au Co, the twin 35-meter and 30-meter towers frame a 2-meter wind gap that plays natural sea-breeze chords and hosts free 3D laser shows nightly from 19:30 to 22:00. Three kilometers inland, the 11th-century Cham brick sanctuary of **Nhan Tower** (*Tháp Nhạn*, 0 VND entry) crowns 64-meter Nhan Mountain above the Da Rang River.

### Flagship Tuy Hoa Beach Hotels & Resorts
Tuy Hoa's beachfront strip combines international lifestyle towers with Mediterranean villa compounds:
* **Stelia Beach Resort** (Lot C1 Doc Lap Boulevard, 2,800,000–5,200,000 VND / $112–$208 USD): Mediterranean terracotta villas shaded under casuarina pines right opposite Nghinh Phong Square, featuring two infinity pools and the bamboo-domed **Gozo Brew House**. Book early on summer weekends.
* **Rosa Alba Resort & Villas Tuy Hoa** (88 Le Duan, 2,200,000–4,200,000 VND / $88–$168 USD): Ocean-view rooms and private pool villas connected to the sand by a private underground pedestrian tunnel beneath Doc Lap Boulevard. Families love the kids' pool.
* **Mandala Hotel & Spa Phu Yen** (Hung Vuong Avenue, 2,000,000–3,500,000 VND / $80–$140 USD): Contemporary tower prized for its rooftop infinity pool and mineral mud spa.
* **Sala Tuy Hoa Beach Hotel** (51 Doc Lap Boulevard, 1,400,000–2,500,000 VND / $56–$100 USD): Low-rise four-star favorite whose beachfront pool and lawn sit directly on the sand with zero road crossing.
* **Wink Hotel Tuy Hoa Beach** (01 Dien Bien Phu, 1,100,000–1,900,000 VND / $44–$75 USD): Sleek 20-story stay offering guaranteed 24-hour check-in/check-out (**STAY24**), self-service laundry, and a rooftop pool bar.

### Local Dining & Ocean Tuna Specialties
Central Tuy Hoa puts Phu Yen's famous dishes within a 30,000 VND taxi hop. Grab a table at **Bà Tám Ocean Tuna** (*Mắt cá ngừ đại dương Bà Tám*, **289 Le Duan**, open 08:00–21:00) for **Steamed Ocean Tuna Eyes** (50,000–65,000 VND / $2.00–$2.60 USD per claypot braised with jujubes, goji berries, and herbs) and fresh tuna sashimi. Order **Chives Noodle Soup** (*Bánh canh hẹ*) along **Le Thanh Ton** or **Dien Bien Phu** for 25,000–35,000 VND ($1.00–$1.40 USD) per bowl topped with mackerel fishcake and a thick blanket of chopped green chives. In the evening, climb to the foot of Nhan Mountain for **Grilled Corn Cake & Hot Steamed Rice Cakes** (*Bánh bèo nóng chân Tháp Nhạn*, 20,000 VND / $0.80 USD per tray with crispy pork cracklings).

## 2. Bai Xep & Ganh Ong Headland (Bãi Xép, An Chan — 13 km North)

Ride 13 km (18 minutes, 160,000–220,000 VND GrabCar) north of Tuy Hoa to reach **Bai Xep** (*Bãi Xép* in An Chan Commune), a golden crescent cove framed by black volcanic boulders. Rising at the northern tip is cactus-covered **Ganh Ong Cliff** (*Gành Ông*, 20,000 VND / $0.80 USD ticket), filmed in *Yellow Flowers on the Green Grass*. Waves stay gentle here. Because the rocky headland blocks heavy ocean chop, Bai Xep offers safer, calmer swimming than downtown Tuy Hoa.

Two seaside resorts anchor the cove. **Sao Mai Beach Resort** (1,900,000–3,600,000 VND / $76–$144 USD) spreads palm-thatched bungalows and beachfront pool villas under casuarina trees beside a saltwater infinity pool. Next door, **Que Toi Village Resort** (1,600,000–3,100,000 VND / $64–$124 USD) opens directly onto the calm bay with kayak rentals and shaded hammocks. Mornings feel completely unhurried. Independent travelers also book quiet coastal homestays in neighboring **My Quang and Ganh Do fishing villages** for 600,000–1,300,000 VND ($24–$52 USD) per night.

## 3. An Hai Lagoon, Ganh Da Dia & Mang Lang Church (32–38 km North)

Thirty-two to 38 km (40 minutes) north of Tuy Hoa in **Tuy An District** lies Phu Yen's volcanic and culinary core. While most visitors tour this zone on a half-day car loop from Tuy Hoa, sunrise photographers book coastal homestays near **An Hai** or **Hon Yen** for 500,000–1,400,000 VND ($20–$56 USD).

Three stops define the northern loop:
* **Ganh Da Dia** (*Gành Đá Đĩa*, 40,000 VND / $1.60 USD entrance plus 15,000 VND electric buggy): Vietnam's premier hexagonal basalt column formation, where ancient lava flows fractured upon hitting seawater into 35,000 black prism pillars. Arrive before 07:30.
* **Mang Lang Church** (*Nhà thờ Mằng Lăng*, 0 VND entry): Built in 1892 in Gothic style, its subterranean grotto displays the first printed **1651** Romanized Vietnamese (*Quốc ngữ*) catechism book (*Phép giảng tám ngày*) by Jesuit missionary **Alexandre de Rhodes**.
* **O Loan Lagoon** (*Đầm Ô Loan* beside An Hai Bridge): Famous for **Grilled Blood Cockles** (*Sò huyết Ô Loan*, 180,000–240,000 VND/kg / $7.20–$9.60 USD) and emperor crab served on floating bamboo rafts (**Quán Tuấn** and **Quán Thúy Kiều**). Five kilometers south, **Hon Yen** exposes purple coral beds at low tide on the 1st and 15th lunar days.

## 4. Xuan Dai Bay & Bai San Ho (Song Cau — 60–68 km North of Tuy Hoa)

Sixty to 68 km north of Tuy Hoa (and just 35 km south of Quy Nhon), **Song Cau Town** wraps around island-dotted **Xuan Dai Bay** (*Vịnh Xuân Đài*). Occupying a **98-hectare private peninsula** here is **Zannier Hotels Bãi San Hô** (12,500,000–32,000,000+ VND / $500–$1,280+ USD per night). Its 73 villas span stilted **Paddy Field Villas**, Cham-style **Beach Pool Villas** along a private **1 km coral beach**, and Bana-inspired **Hilltop Pool Villas**, paired with grandmother-style Vietnamese feasts at **Bà Hai** restaurant.

> **Airport Note for Zannier Bãi San Hô:** **Quy Nhon Phù Cát Airport (UIH)** (65 km / 70 minutes north) and **Diêu Trì Railway Station** (45 km / 50 minutes north) sit closer to Bãi San Hô than **Tuy Hoa Airport (TBB)** (72 km / 80 minutes south). Mid-range travelers exploring Xuan Dai Bay stay near **Nhat Tu Son Island** (50,000 VND causeway ticket) at **Hoa Lan Resort** or **Timothe Beach Bungalow** (700,000–1,600,000 VND / $28–$64 USD).

## 5. Vung Ro Bay & Mui Dien Sunrise Cape (30–35 km South at Ca Pass)

Thirty to 35 km south of Tuy Hoa at the foot of **Deo Ca Pass**, granite cliffs frame **Mui Dien Lighthouse** (*Mũi Điện / Mũi Đại Lãnh*, 20,000 VND / $0.80 USD ticket) and pristine **Bai Mon Beach** (*Bãi Môn*). Hikers start climbing the 1.2 km stone stair trail at **05:00 AM** to catch mainland Vietnam's earliest sunrise at 05:15–05:30 AM before swimming in Bai Mon's freshwater-fed cove. Five kilometers farther south, **Vung Ro Bay** (*Vịnh Vũng Rô*) commemorates the historic **Ho Chi Minh Trail at Sea "No-Number Ships"** (0 VND) and hosts floating aquaculture rafts (**Bè nổi Vũng Rô**) serving live lobster (1,000,000–1,350,000 VND/kg) and cobia hotpot (250,000–350,000 VND). Because local lodging is limited to highway guesthouses (350,000–750,000 VND) or Bai Mon tent camping (80,000–150,000 VND), most visitors sleep in **Tuy Hoa** and take a 04:15 AM taxi (400,000–550,000 VND round-trip) south.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I stay in Tuy Hoa City or at a Bai Xep beach resort?
Pick **Tuy Hoa City** (1,100,000–5,200,000 VND / $44–$208 USD at **Wink**, **Sala**, or **Stelia Beach Resort**) for walkable evenings at Nghinh Phong Tower, Bà Tám ocean tuna eyes at 289 Le Duan, and equal 30–35 km drives to Ganh Da Dia and Mui Dien. Choose **Bai Xep** (13 km north, 1,600,000–3,600,000 VND at **Sao Mai** or **Que Toi**) for calm cove swimming right outside your bungalow.

### Is Zannier Hotels Bãi San Hô closer to Quy Nhon or Tuy Hoa?
It sits closer to Quy Nhon. Downtown Quy Nhon is 35 km north, **Dieu Tri Station** is 45 km north, and **Phu Cat Airport (UIH)** is 65 km north, whereas **Tuy Hoa Airport (TBB)** lies 72 km south.

### How many days do I need for Phu Yen's North and South loops?
Three days and two nights cover both halves cleanly. Spend Day 1 on the Northern Loop (**Bai Xep**, **O Loan Lagoon** blood cockles, **Mang Lang Church**, and **Ganh Da Dia**), and Day 2 on a 04:15 AM sunrise trip to **Mui Dien Lighthouse**, **Bai Mon**, and **Vung Ro Bay** floating rafts.
