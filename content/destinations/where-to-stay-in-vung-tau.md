# Where to Stay in Vung Tau (2026): Best Beaches & Hotels

Vung Tau is Southern Vietnam's closest ocean peninsula. Curving into the East Sea just **100–115 km** (**2.0 to 2.5 hours** by road) southeast of Ho Chi Minh City, the cape splits into two completely different coastlines separated by twin volcanic headlands — **Big Mountain (`Núi Lớn`)** and **Small Mountain (`Núi Nhỏ`)**. Pick the wrong side of the peninsula and you will either end up facing a fishing boat anchorage when you wanted surfable sand, or stuck in a high-rise condo tower **4 km** away from the city's best sunset cafes and seafood bistros.

Getting here from Ho Chi Minh City is straightforward. Luxury 9-seat VIP limousine vans operated by **Hoa Mai**, **Huy Hoang**, **Toan Thang**, and **Anh Quoc** depart every **15 to 30 minutes** from District 1 (`Nguyễn Thái Bình Street`) and Tan Son Nhat Airport via **National Highway 51 (`QL51`)** for **160,000–220,000 VND ($6.40–$8.80 USD)**, dropping passengers directly at their hotel door in Vung Tau. To skip highway truck traffic entirely, board the **GreenlinesDP High-Speed Hydrofoil Ferry** at **Bach Dang Wharf (District 1, HCMC)**, which skims down the Saigon River and across Ganh Rai Bay to **Cau Da / Ho May Pier** on Vung Tau's Front Beach in exactly **2 hours** for **320,000–350,000 VND ($12.80–$14.00 USD)** one way.

> **The Vung Tau Weekday vs. Saturday Pricing Rule:** Vung Tau is Ho Chi Minh City's primary weekend escape, creating the sharpest two-tier hotel pricing split in southern Vietnam. From **Sunday through Thursday**, ocean-view 4-star hotels and serviced condos drop to **40%–50% off** weekend tariffs. On **Saturday nights** and public holidays (`30/4–1/5`, `2/9`, and `Tết`), room rates surge by **40% to 100%** and beachfront properties sell out days in advance. Always book Sunday-to-Thursday stays if your schedule is flexible.

## 1. Back Beach (`Bãi Sau / Thùy Vân Street` — Main Swimming & Resort Strip)

Stretching nearly **10 kilometers** along the eastern side of the peninsula from the foot of **Small Mountain (`Núi Nhỏ`)** north toward Paradise Golf Resort, **Back Beach (`Bãi Sau`)** is Vung Tau's true ocean swimming beach. Following the major **2025/2026 Thuy Van Beach Park (`Công viên Bãi Sau Thùy Vân`)** civic overhaul, the old concrete walls and cluttered beachfront kiosks have been replaced by open pedestrian plazas, cycling lanes, public shower stations, and direct sand access across from `Thùy Vân Street`.

Stay on Back Beach if your top priority is walking straight from your hotel elevator onto wide, firm sand with rolling waves and lifeguards.

### Best Hotels, Resorts & Serviced Apartments on Back Beach
* **The IMPERIAL Hotel Vung Tau (`159 Thùy Vân`)**: Vung Tau's signature **5-star** Victorian-inspired heritage property (**2,800,000–3,600,000 VND** weekdays; **4,200,000–5,200,000 VND / $168–$208 USD** Saturdays). It is the only hotel on `Thùy Vân Street` with a private pedestrian bridge crossing the coastal road directly into its own walled **Imperial Beach Club** with beachfront pools and sun loungers.
* **Pullman Vung Tau (`15 Thi Sách`, 400m behind Back Beach)**: International **5-star** business and leisure standard with crisp soundproof rooms, an expansive breakfast buffet, and an outdoor pool (**2,400,000–4,500,000 VND / $96–$180 USD**).
* **Premier Pearl Hotel & ibis Styles Vung Tau (`Thùy Vân Street`)**: Contemporary high-rise hotels facing the surf. **Premier Pearl** features a dramatic 19th-floor rooftop infinity pool overlooking Hon Ba Island (**1,600,000–3,100,000 VND**), while **ibis Styles** delivers playful, colorful rooms ideal for young couples and families (**1,200,000–2,200,000 VND / $48–$88 USD**).
* **Serviced Apartment Towers (`The Sóng An Gia` on Thi Sách, `CSJ Tower`, `Vung Tau Melody`)**: Highly popular with families and friend groups. A 1-bedroom or 2-bedroom apartment with a washing machine, kitchenette, and huge rooftop pool costs just **750,000–1,500,000 VND ($30–$60 USD)** on weekdays, jumping to **1,400,000–2,600,000 VND ($56–$104 USD)** on Saturday nights (note: Friday/Saturday elevator waits at `The Sóng` can take **10–15 minutes** at peak check-in hours around **14:00**).

### Southern Back Beach Landmarks
At the southern tip of `Thùy Vân Street`, hike the **847 stone steps** up **Small Mountain (`Núi Nhỏ`)** to the **32-meter Christ the King Statue (`Tượng Chúa Kitô Vua`)**, open daily **07:00–17:00** with **0 VND (free) admission** (strict dress code: shorts and skirts must cover the knees, and sleeveless tops are barred from climbing inside the statue's shoulder lookout). Below the cape lies **Hon Ba Island (`Miếu Hòn Bà`)**, reachable on foot across a rocky causeway only during low tide.

## 2. Front Beach (`Bãi Trước / Quang Trung & Hạ Long Street` — Sunset Bay & Food Hub)

Facing west across a sheltered crescent bay framed by coconut palms and anchored fishing boats, **Front Beach (`Bãi Trước`)** is the historic, culinary, and social heart of Vung Tau. **GreenlinesDP hydrofoil ferries** dock right here at **Cau Da Pier**, immediately below the **Ho May Park Cable Car (`Cáp treo Hồ Mây`, 400,000 VND** round-trip ticket to the mountaintop amusement park) and the French colonial **White Palace (`Bạch Dinh`, 15,000 VND** entry, built in 1898 as a retreat for Governor-General Paul Doumer).

### Top Hotels in Front Beach
* **Fusion Suites Vung Tau (`2 Trương Công Định`)**: Located one block behind the Front Beach park, this wellness-focused lifestyle hotel features light-filled suites, multi-level swimming pools, a 19th-floor rooftop infinity pool gazing over the entire bay, and spa treatments available on-site (**2,200,000–3,800,000 VND / $88–$152 USD**).
* **Grand Hotel Vung Tau, Palace Hotel & Petro House (`Nguyễn Du & Quang Trung`)**: Classic heritage and 4-star hotels surrounded by leafy boulevards and colonial facades (**950,000–1,900,000 VND / $38–$76 USD**).

### Where to Eat & Swim Near Front Beach
Do not book Front Beach expecting wide sandy surf — the main bay is a maritime boat harbor with a narrow tidal strip. When you want a quick dip from Front Beach, walk or take a **30,000 VND** Grab ride **1.5 km** south along scenic `Hạ Long Street` to **Pineapple Beach (`Bãi Dứa / Lãng Du`)**, a clean, sheltered pocket cove tucked under the rocky cliffs.

For food and nightlife, Front Beach beats Back Beach effortlessly. Walk five minutes inland to **`Trần Hưng Đạo`**, **`Nguyễn Trường Tộ`**, and **`Đồ Chiểu`** streets for crispy prawn mini-pancakes at **Bánh Khọt Gốc Vú Sữa (`14 Nguyễn Trường Tộ`, 70,000–85,000 VND** per plate), stingray hotpot with sour bamboo shoots along **`Trương Công Định` (`Lẩu cá đuối Hoàng Minh`, 220,000–350,000 VND)**, and fresh sponge cake (`Bánh bông lan trứng muối Gốc Cột Điện`, **45,000–60,000 VND**).

## 3. Bai Dau & Nghinh Phong Cape (`Trần Phú` & `Hạ Long` Coastal Roads)

North of Front Beach, **`Trần Phú Coastal Road`** winds around the dramatic rocky flanks of **Big Mountain (`Núi Lớn`)** into **Bai Dau (`Bãi Dâu` — Mulberry Beach)**. Here the city high-rises disappear, replaced by crashing waves over dark granite boulders, hillside bougainvillea, and open-air sunset terraces.

* **Marina Bay Vung Tau Resort & Spa (`115 Trần Phú`)**: Vung Tau's finest low-rise luxury resort (**2,300,000–4,200,000 VND / $92–$168 USD**), featuring private ocean-facing bungalows and twin infinity pools built directly above the rocky surf looking straight toward the sunset.
* **Mercure Vung Tau (`03–06 Hạ Long` at Nghinh Phong Cape `Mũi Nghinh Phong`)**: Split between a hillside villa wing and an oceanfront wing perched on the rocky point where Front Beach and Back Beach meet (**2,000,000–3,600,000 VND / $80–$144 USD**).
* **Seafood Dining on Tran Phu**: Reserve a sea-wall table by **17:15** at **Gành Hào 1 (`03 Trần Phú`)** or **Ốc Tự Nhiên 2 (`34 Trần Phú`)** to feast on grilled blood cockles, steamed squid with ginger, and garlic butter mantis shrimp (**300,000–550,000 VND** per person) as the sun drops into the East Sea.

## 4. Long Hai & Phuoc Hai Fishing Coast (`25–30 km` Northeast)

If Vung Tau city feels too urban on weekends, drive **25–30 km** northeast across the Cua Lap Bridge to **Long Hai** and **Phuoc Hai** at the foot of historic **Minh Dam Mountain (`Núi Minh Đạm`)**. This quieter shoreline swaps condo towers for pine-shaded beaches and colorful basket-boat fishing villages (`Làng chài Phước Hải`). Top stays include **Fleur de Lys Resort & Spa Long Hai** (Mediterranean-style hillside cottages, **1,900,000–3,600,000 VND**), **Anoasis Resort Long Hai**, and **Oceanami Villas & Beach Club** (**1,800,000–3,800,000 VND / $72–$152 USD**).

## 5. Ho Tram & Binh Chau Hot Springs (`45–55 km` East)

Continuing **45–55 km** east of Vung Tau along the newly widened coastal highway (`Đường ven biển Vũng Tàu – Bình Châu`) brings you to **Ho Tram (`Hồ Tràm`)**, southern Vietnam's premier purpose-built **5-star** beachfront resort corridor. Unlike Vung Tau city, Ho Tram resorts sit directly on uninterrupted, Casuarina-lined private sand with zero coastal road separating your sun lounger from the ocean:
* **The Grand Ho Tram Strip (`InterContinental Grand Ho Tram`, `Holiday Inn Resort Ho Tram Beach` & `Ixora Ho Tram by Fusion`)**: A massive integrated beachfront complex (**3,200,000–6,800,000 VND / $128–$272 USD**) situated directly across from **The Bluffs Grand Ho Tram**, a Greg Norman-designed **18-hole championship links golf course**.
* **Melia Ho Tram Beach Resort & Angsana / Dhawa Ho Tram**: Low-density tropical pool villas and oceanfront suites (**3,500,000–7,500,000 VND / $140–$300 USD**), most offering **complimentary scheduled coach transfers directly from District 1, Ho Chi Minh City** (`2 hours 15 minutes` via the Long Thanh Expressway).
* **Minera Hot Springs Binh Chau (`Bình Châu`, 20 km north of Ho Tram)**: Natural **37°C–82°C** geothermal mineral pools, mud baths, and hot-spring egg boiling wells surrounded by melaleuca forest (**2,400,000–4,500,000 VND** including unlimited spa soaking).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Front Beach or Back Beach better in Vung Tau?
Choose **Back Beach (`Bãi Sau / Thùy Vân`)** if you want to swim in the ocean, walk along a **10 km** sandy shoreline, and stay in large beachfront resorts (`The IMPERIAL`, `Premier Pearl`) or family condo towers (`The Sóng`). Choose **Front Beach (`Bãi Trước`)** or **Bai Dau (`Trần Phú`)** if you care more about sunset bay views, arriving on foot from the **GreenlinesDP ferry**, and walking to Vung Tau's best local restaurants (`Bánh khọt Gốc Vú Sữa`, `Gành Hào`).

### Can you swim at Front Beach in Vung Tau?
Front Beach (`Bãi Trước`) is primarily a sheltered fishing boat harbor with a stone seawall promenade, making it poor for swimming except at the small corner beach near Coconut Park at high tide. For clean swimming on the western side of the peninsula, go **1.5 km** south along `Hạ Long Street` to **Pineapple Beach (`Bãi Dứa`)** or head over to **Back Beach (`Bãi Sau`)**.

### How much more expensive are Vung Tau hotels on Saturday nights?
Expect to pay **40% to 100% more** on Saturday nights compared to Sunday-through-Thursday nights. A beachfront 4-star room or 2-bedroom apartment at `The Sóng` that costs **900,000–1,200,000 VND** on a Tuesday night routinely prices at **1,800,000–2,400,000 VND** on Saturday.
