# Where to Stay in Nha Trang: Best Areas & Hotels

Protected by 19 offshore islands, Nha Trang Bay records 300+ sunny days per year and enjoys a dry swimming season from January through August. Water temperatures hold at 26°C to 32°C. While Central Vietnam neighbors further north battle winter surf in January and February, Khanh Hoa Province wakes up to calm mornings and clear visibility across the coral reefs of Hon Mun and Hon Tam.

Geography defines your trip here. First-time visitors often book a beachfront resort labeled "Nha Trang" only to discover at Cam Ranh International Airport (CXR) that their hotel sits 25 km south along the isolated Bai Dai strip—or on a boat-only island requiring pier transfers. Anchor yourself on Central Tran Phu Boulevard or the Nguyen Thien Thuat inner grid for walkable seafood dining, PADI dive shops, and rooftop infinity pools at 600,000–4,800,000 VND ($24–$192 USD) per night. Pick North Bay for quieter beaches near Po Nagar Cham Towers, Bai Dai Cam Ranh for gated five-star lawns 5–10 minutes from CXR Airport, or Ninh Van Bay for boat-access pool villas.

> [!TIP]
> **Cam Ranh Airport (CXR) Transit Math:** Cam Ranh International Airport sits 35 km (40 minutes) south of downtown Nha Trang via Cu Hin Pass. Skip 450,000 VND curb touts and board **Dat Moi Airport Bus #18** at Arrivals Pillar 4 for 65,000 VND ($2.60 USD); it runs every 30 minutes (05:30–21:30), drops directly along Tran Phu Boulevard, and terminates on Yersin Street. A private GrabCar to Central Tran Phu costs 280,000–350,000 VND ($11.20–$14.00 USD).

## 1. Central Tran Phu Beachfront (Loc Tho Ward — 2/4 Square & Agarwood Tower)

Curving 6 km along the bay, **Tran Phu Boulevard** forms Nha Trang's signature urban shoreline. Its prime central stretch in **Loc Tho Ward** clusters around **2/4 Square** (*Quảng trường 2/4*) and the pink lotus spire of **Agarwood Tower** (*Tháp Trầm Hương*). A 60 meters wide municipal park shaded by casuarina pines and coconut palms separates the four-lane coastal avenue from the golden sand. Dawn brings out the crowds. Locals swim at 05:30 in calm 27°C bay water while lifeguards staff watchtowers until 18:00 and attendants rent padded sunbeds under thatched umbrellas for 50,000–100,000 VND ($2–$4 USD).

High-rise towers lining Central Tran Phu offer direct beach access across the four-lane boulevard and rooftop infinity pools at 1,200,000–4,800,000 VND ($48–$192 USD) per night. Flagship five-star addresses include **InterContinental Nha Trang** (2,900,000–4,800,000 VND, featuring three outdoor swimming pools and immediate access to Sailing Club Beach Bar) and **Sheraton Nha Trang Hotel & Spa** (2,600,000–4,200,000 VND, famed for its 6th-floor infinity edge pool and 28th-floor Altitude rooftop lounge). Mid-range travelers book **Novotel Nha Trang** (1,800,000–2,900,000 VND, where every balcony overlooks Hon Tre Island), **Havana Nha Trang Hotel** (1,200,000–1,900,000 VND, connected straight to the beach via a private underground pedestrian tunnel beneath Tran Phu Boulevard), or **Panorama Nha Trang** (1,200,000–1,700,000 VND, known for its 40th-floor cantilevered glass-bottom sky pool).

Island logistics are effortless here. Ride 10 minutes south by GrabCar (4.5 km, 65,000–90,000 VND) to **Vinh Truong Tourist Pier** (*Bến tàu Du lịch Nha Trang*, 388 Vo Thi Sau) for 3-island wooden boat and speedboat tours (650,000–890,000 VND / $26–$36 USD with lunch and snorkeling gear) and morning PADI dive boats to Hon Mun Marine Park (1,400,000–1,800,000 VND for two dives). Right next door sits the **VinWonders Cable Car** terminal (950,000 VND / $38 USD round-trip with theme park admission).

## 2. Nguyen Thien Thuat, Hung Vuong & Biet Thu Inner Grid (1–3 Blocks Behind Tran Phu)

Walk 150m to 400m (1–3 blocks) inland from Tran Phu Boulevard and coastal traffic fades into a compact grid along **Nguyen Thien Thuat**, **Hung Vuong**, and **Biet Thu** streets. You still reach the beach in 4 minutes beside Nha Trang Night Market. Rates drop by 40 percent.

Quiet boutique hotels, Japanese business inns, and serviced studio apartments with balconies and washing machines rent for 600,000–1,500,000 VND ($24–$60 USD) per night. PADI trainees gather on Biet Thu Street at 07:15 for morning boat check-ins at **Sailing Club Divers** and **Rainbow Divers**. Ten minutes north across Le Thanh Ton Street, local institutions anchor the evening food route: queue at **Nem nướng Đặng Văn Quyên** (16A Lan Ong, 95,000–130,000 VND for DIY rice-paper platters of grilled pork sausage, crispy ram rolls, and green mango) or grill honey-marinated beef cubes over clay charcoal braziers at **Bò nướng Lạc Cảnh** (77 Nguyen Binh Khiem, operating since 1975, 180,000–260,000 VND).

Transit hubs sit close by. The northern **Dat Moi Bus #18** terminal on **Yersin Street** is a 600 meters walk north (65,000 VND to CXR Airport), while **Nha Trang Railway Station** (17 Thai Nguyen) stands 2 km west (6 minutes, 40,000 VND GrabCar) for Reunification Express SE1/SE3 and overnight SNT1/SNT2 sleeper trains to Ho Chi Minh City (480,000–850,000 VND).

## 3. North Bay (Pham Van Dong Boulevard, Vinh Hai & Bai Tien)

North of the Cai River (*Sông Cái*) across the 458 meters Tran Phu Bridge, the coastal highway becomes **Pham Van Dong Boulevard**, curving past **Vinh Hai** to **Bai Tien** headland. Tour buses thin out after sunset. Sea-view apartment-hotels along Hon Chong Beach rent for 800,000–1,600,000 VND ($32–$64 USD) per night, while five-star **Amiana Resort Nha Trang** out on Bai Tien charges 4,200,000–5,500,000 VND ($168–$220 USD; full North Bay range 800,000–5,500,000 VND) for garden rooms and ocean villas framing a 2,500-square-meter natural seawater lagoon pool and sheltered private sandy cove.

North Bay places three major cultural and wellness landmarks within a 5–10 minutes taxi ride:
* **Po Nagar Cham Towers** (*Tháp Bà Po Nagar*): Built between the 8th and 12th centuries atop Cu Lao Hill overlooking the Cai River fishing fleet, these four red-brick Hindu sanctuaries charge 30,000 VND ($1.20 USD) entry from 06:00 to 17:30.
* **Thap Ba Seafood Street** (*Bờ Kè Tháp Bà*): Riverbank live-tank restaurants serving steamed flower crab, sweet snails (*ốc hương*), grilled sea urchin (35,000 VND), and clay-mold *bánh căn* rice pancakes for 250,000–450,000 VND ($10–$18 USD) per person.
* **I-Resort Hot Mineral Mud Bath** (4 km upriver in Vinh Ngoc): Soak 20 minutes in private warm mineral mud tubs followed by hydrotherapy waterfalls and geothermal swimming pools for 400,000–450,000 VND ($16–$18 USD).

## 4. Bai Dai Cam Ranh Resort Strip (Nguyen Tat Thanh Boulevard)

Check the map before booking a "Cam Ranh" address. Stretching 15 km along six-lane **Nguyen Tat Thanh Boulevard** on the Cam Ranh Peninsula, **Bai Dai** (*Long Beach*) sits 25 km south of Nha Trang over Cu Hin Pass, but just 5–10 minutes (4–9 km, 90,000–140,000 VND GrabCar) north of **Cam Ranh International Airport (CXR)**.

No public road separates these resorts from the surf. Gated five-star complexes occupy 15-hectare to 35-hectare private white-sand beach lawns with nightly rates of 2,800,000–8,500,000 VND ($112–$340 USD). Standouts include **Alma Resort** (12 cascading pools and waterpark), **The Anam Cam Ranh** (Indochine colonial villas shaded by 3,000 coconut palms), **Radisson Blu**, **Mövenpick**, **Fusion Resort** (daily spa treatments included in room rates), and **JW Marriott Cam Ranh**. Nothing is walkable outside the guarded gates. Every taxi trip into Nha Trang city takes 35 minutes and costs 300,000–380,000 VND ($12–$15 USD) each way.

## 5. Hon Tre Island, Hon Tam & Ninh Van Bay (Boat-Only Sanctuaries)

Offshore islands and roadless peninsulas split into two boat-only tiers. Facing the city, **Hon Tre Island** (**Vinpearl Resort** and **Nha Trang Marriott Resort**) and **Hon Tam Resort** run complimentary 10-minute speedboats 24/7 from Vinh Truong Pier, charging 2,400,000–5,200,000 VND ($96–$208 USD) per night with easy access to the VinWonders theme park, championship golf course, and Hon Tam's terraced island mineral mud bath complex.

Eighteen kilometers north across Nha Phu Lagoon lies roadless **Ninh Van Bay**, reached via a 15-minute to 20-minute private speedboat from a mainland lounge jetty. Ultra-luxury eco-retreats here—**Six Senses Ninh Van Bay**, **L'Alya Ninh Van Bay**, and **An Lam Retreats**—command 8,500,000–28,000,000 VND ($340–$1,120 USD) per night for granite-boulder and overwater pool villas perched above private coral reefs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it better to stay on Tran Phu Beach in Nha Trang or at Bai Dai Cam Ranh?
Match your zone to your evening plans. Stay on **Central Tran Phu** or the **Nguyen Thien Thuat inner grid** (600,000–4,800,000 VND / $24–$192 USD) if you want walkable seafood dining, PADI dive boats from Vinh Truong Pier (10 minutes away), and I-Resort mud baths. Pick **Bai Dai Cam Ranh** (2,800,000–8,500,000 VND / $112–$340 USD) for gated five-star beachfront lawns just 5–10 minutes from CXR Airport when you do not plan to pay 300,000–380,000 VND each way by taxi to dine in Nha Trang city.

### What is the best month to visit Nha Trang for calm beach swimming and diving?
January through August is dry season. Backed by 300+ sunny days per year, Nha Trang reaches peak underwater reef visibility (15m–25m at Hon Mun Marine Park) between **March and August**, when daytime temperatures hold at 30°C–33°C and bay waters stay glassy. Skip boat-dependent island stays in **late October and November**, when northeast monsoon rains and 2 meters swells disrupt speedboats.

### How do I get from Cam Ranh Airport (CXR) to Central Nha Trang hotels?
Walk outside CXR Terminal T1 or T2 to **Pillar 4** and board orange **Dat Moi Airport Bus #18** (65,000 VND / $2.60 USD per ticket, departing every 30 minutes from 05:30 to 21:30), which drives 35 km north in 40 minutes and drops passengers right along Tran Phu Boulevard before ending on Yersin Street. Prefer a direct car? A private GrabCar costs 280,000–350,000 VND ($11.20–$14.00 USD) including airport tolls.
