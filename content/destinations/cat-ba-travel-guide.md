# Cat Ba Travel Guide: Island Base, Lan Ha Bay, and Park Time

Covering 260 square kilometers across an archipelago of 367 islands, **Cat Ba Island** is the ecological anchor of northern Vietnam's coastal karst landscape. While politically part of Hai Phong municipality (45 km east of downtown Hai Phong), Cat Ba borders the southern rim of Ha Long Bay and sits directly inside **Lan Ha Bay** and the **Cat Ba UNESCO Biosphere Reserve**.

Unlike the commercial, high-rise cruise hubs on the Quang Ninh mainland, Cat Ba functions as an active outdoor staging base. It allows travelers to sleep on solid ground, hire local motorboats out of **Ben Beo Pier** (1.5 km from town), hike 12 km through subtropical rainforest to mountain peaks rising 225 meters to 322 meters, and kayak through tidal archways into secluded marine lagoons.

Planning a trip to Cat Ba in 2026 requires understanding island transport shifts at **Dong Bai Ferry Terminal**, trail networks, seasonal marine conditions (18°C to 29°C air temperatures, 78% average humidity), and heritage preservation regulations. This empirical guide covers national park trekking, Lan Ha Bay boat logistics, cliffside beaches, rock climbing, local dining, accommodation tiers, and island transport.

## Lan Ha Bay and Ben Beo Pier Expedition Logistics

Most maritime exploration around Cat Ba departs from **Ben Beo Pier** (*Bến Bèo*), situated 1.5 kilometers southeast of the Cat Ba town promenade. Electric passenger golf carts and metered taxis run continuously between the waterfront strip and the harbor gate (06:00 to 21:00) for 20,000 VND to 40,000 VND ($0.80 to $1.60 USD) per ride, covering the 1.5 km hill road in 5 minutes.

```
[Cat Ba Town Promenade]
         |
         v (1.5 km via electric cart / taxi: 5 minutes | 20,000 VND – 40,000 VND / $0.80 – $1.60 USD)
[Ben Beo Harbor Terminal] (Municipal port levy: 80,000 VND / $3.20 USD included in day tour)
         |
         +---> [Cai Beo Ancient Floating Village] (1.2 km channel | 15 minutes | 7,000-year maritime lineage)
         |
         +---> [Ba Trai Dao Islets] (9.5 km southeast | 45 minutes | White sand tidal coves)
         |
         +---> [Bright & Dark Cave] (14 km northeast | 60 minutes | 100m tidal arch into karst lake)
```

### 1. Cai Beo Floating Fishing Village
Cruises passing out of Ben Beo immediately navigate 1.2 kilometers through **Cai Beo**, recognized by archaeologists as one of the oldest coastal fishing settlements in Southeast Asia, with marine stone tools dating back over 7,000 years. Approximately 300 floating wooden houses supported by buoyant plastic drums line the 150 meters wide waterway. Families raise cobia, grouper, and red snapper inside 4 meters deep submerged mesh cages, selling fresh fish directly to passing galleys for 250,000 VND to 450,000 VND ($10.00 to $18.00 USD) per kilogram.

### 2. Kayaking Bright & Dark Cave (*Hang Sáng - Hang Tối*)
Located 14 kilometers northeast of Ben Beo (60 minutes by wooden cruiser at 14 km/h) on the maritime boundary between Lan Ha Bay and Ha Long Bay, this flooded karst formation features two water-level tunnels:
- **Bright Cave (*Hang Sáng*):** Always above sea level with a 3.5 meters ceiling clearance, allowing tandem kayaks or bamboo rowboats (50,000 VND or $2.00 USD per passenger) to pass through a 100 meters arch into an enclosed interior lagoon (*áng*) surrounded by sheer 60 meters to 85 meters limestone walls.
- **Dark Cave (*Hang Tối*):** A 200 meters subterranean cavern that floods completely during spring high tides. Guided passage (25 minutes round-trip with headlamps) is only permitted when tidal levels drop below 1.2 meters.

### 3. Ba Trai Dao Beaches (Three Peaches) and Bioluminescence
Situated 9.5 kilometers southeast of Ben Beo Pier (45 minutes sailing), **Ba Trai Dao** consists of three rounded limestone islets (23 meters tall) sheltering crescent white-sand coves exposed for 4.0 hours to 5.0 hours during low and mid tides. Water visibility reaches 3.0 meters to 4.5 meters, making it the primary anchorage for swimming and stand-up paddleboarding. After sunset (18:30 to 21:00), specialized night tours (750,000 VND to 950,000 VND, or $30.00 to $38.00 USD) guide kayakers 2.5 km into sheltered Tai Keo coves where microscopic *Noctiluca scintillans* plankton glow electric blue with every paddle stroke.

> [!NOTE]
> Booking an independent shared day boat (08:00 to 15:30, 7.5 hours total) through licensed Cat Ba Town operators such as Cat Ba Ventures, Cat Ba Vision, or Langur Adventures costs 650,000 VND to 950,000 VND ($26.00 to $38.00 USD) per person. Chartered private wooden boats for 2 to 6 passengers cost 3,200,000 VND to 4,500,000 VND ($128.00 to $180.00 USD) for a 6.0 hours circuit.

## Cat Ba National Park: Rainforest Hiking, Caves, and Rock Climbing

Established in 1986, **Cat Ba National Park** encompasses 17,360 hectares (10,900 terrestrial hectares and 6,460 marine hectares) centered 13 kilometers north of Cat Ba Town (25 minutes by motorbike along Highway DT356). It protects the critically endangered **Cat Ba Langur** (*Trachypithecus poliocephalus*), one of the rarest primates on Earth, with roughly 75 to 85 individuals surviving on isolated 150 meters to 250 meters karst cliffs inside strict sanctuary zones.

```
[Park Headquarters Gate] (Elevation: 30m | Entry ticket: 120,000 VND / $4.80 USD)
         |
         v (Flat paved & gravel path | 800 meters | 15 minutes)
[Kim Giao Forest Trailhead] (Elevation: 75m)
         |
         v (Steep limestone step ascent | 1,200 meters | 35 minutes)
[Ngu Lam Intermediate Pavilion] (Elevation: 165m)
         |
         v (Jagged summit ridge scramble | 400 meters | 20 minutes)
[Ngu Lam Peak Summit] (Elevation: 225m | 360° Karst Canopy Panorama)
```

### 1. The Ngu Lam Peak Trail Profile
The standard out-and-back hike (4.8 km round-trip, 2.5 hours to 3.0 hours) to **Ngu Lam Peak** starts at the park headquarters ticket booth (07:00 to 17:00, 120,000 VND or $4.80 USD adult entry; 10,000 VND or $0.40 USD scooter parking). The trail begins with an 800 meters walk (15 minutes) through secondary forest before climbing 1,200 meters (35 minutes) through the **Kim Giao forest**, a dense stand of endemic evergreen trees (*Nageia fleuryi*) whose pale timber was historically used by Vietnamese royal courts to fashion poison-detecting chopsticks.

Past the intermediate timber pavilion at 165 meters altitude, the final 400 meters (20 minutes) requires scrambling up weathered limestone crags equipped with steel handrails to the 225 meters summit. Wear grippy trail shoes and carry 1.5 liters of water (15,000 VND or $0.60 USD at the gate kiosk). Early morning departures at 07:30 keep temperatures near 22°C before midday heat reaches 29°C at 82% humidity.

### 2. Trung Trang Cave (*Động Trung Trang*) and Butterfly Valley Climbing
Located 1.2 kilometers south of the park entrance along Highway DT356 (3 minutes by scooter), **Trung Trang Cave** cuts 300 meters horizontally through a limestone ridge. Lit walkways (45 minutes transit time, ceiling heights of 8 meters to 15 meters) guide visitors past active stalactites and Pleistocene fossil deposits; entry is included in the 120,000 VND ($4.80 USD) park pass. For vertical athletes, **Butterfly Valley** (*Thung Lũng Bướm*, located 9.0 kilometers northwest of town via Lien Minh village, 20 minutes drive) offers 55 bolted sport climbing routes up 20 meters to 35 meters limestone overhangs. Half-day guided climbing or deep-water soloing excursions (4.0 hours) cost 950,000 VND to 1,250,000 VND ($38.00 to $50.00 USD) per climber, including UIAA-certified harnesses, ropes, and chalk bags.

> [!WARNING]
> **Hospital Cave (*Hang Quân Y*) Closure Notice:** The 3-story reinforced concrete military hospital constructed inside a karst mountain (8.5 km north of Cat Ba Town on DT356) between 1963 and 1965 remains closed to tourists for structural safety audits. Skip roadside ticket touts near the 8.5 km marker and continue 4.5 km further north to Trung Trang Cave.

## Cannon Fort: Heritage Artillery and Sunset Views

Perched on a steep coastal promontory 177 meters above Cat Ba Town, **Cannon Fort** (*Pháo Đài Thần Công*) was built by French colonial engineers in 1942 and used by Vietnamese coastal artillery units until 1975. The site preserves two 130mm naval defense guns, 250 meters of stone blast trenches, underground command bunkers, and twin observation decks overlooking **Ben Beo Pier** (1.5 km east) and the islands of **Lan Ha Bay**.

Travelers can walk 2.5 kilometers (35 minutes uphill climb at a 12% road grade) from the 1/4 Street intersection or take a motorbike taxi (10 minutes) for 30,000 VND to 40,000 VND ($1.20 to $1.60 USD) one-way. Entry costs 50,000 VND ($2.00 USD), and the summit cafe sells Vietnamese iced coffee for 35,000 VND ($1.40 USD). Arrive between 16:15 and 17:30 to watch sunset light strike the 100 meters karst spires offshore.

## Coastal Beaches and the Cat Co Cliff Boardwalk

Cat Ba Town features three sandy coves tucked along its southern headland (800 meters to 1.5 km from the central pier): **Cat Co 1**, **Cat Co 2**, and **Cat Co 3**, plus **Tung Thu Beach** (2.0 km east). Electric shuttle buggies link town hotels to all beaches in 5 minutes for 10,000 VND to 20,000 VND ($0.40 to $0.80 USD) per person.

```
[Cat Co 1 Beach] (180m sand crescent | Lounger rental: 50,000 VND – 100,000 VND / $2.00 – $4.00 USD)
         |
         v (450m concrete cantilevered boardwalk | 15m above sea level | 20 minutes walk)
[Cat Co 3 Beach] (220m sand cove | Deeper swimming channel | Sunset orientation)
```

1. **Cat Co 1 and Cat Co 2:** Separated by a 120 meters rocky spur, these beaches offer gentle shelved sand (150 meters to 180 meters wide), freshwater showers (20,000 VND or $0.80 USD), and shaded deck chairs (50,000 VND to 100,000 VND, or $2.00 to $4.00 USD).
2. **Cat Co 3 and the Cliff Path:** Connected to Cat Co 1 by a 450 meters red-railed concrete boardwalk bolted 15 meters above the crashing surf (20 minutes walk, 0 VND entry), Cat Co 3 offers deeper water (2.0 meters to 3.5 meters depth near the safety buoys) and unobstructed western sunset angles.

## Viet Hai Village: The Isolated Karst Basin

Surrounded by 200 meters vertical limestone walls inside the eastern core of Cat Ba National Park, **Viet Hai** is an agricultural commune of 87 households situated 11 kilometers east of Cat Ba Town by air.

- **Access by Sea:** Board a wooden boat from Ben Beo Pier (45 minutes, 8.5 km water route) to Viet Hai Pier (30,000 VND or $1.20 USD dock fee; shared boat transfers cost 150,000 VND to 200,000 VND or $6.00 to $8.00 USD round-trip). From the dock, rent a mountain bike (50,000 VND or $2.00 USD for 3.0 hours) or ride an electric buggy (35,000 VND to 50,000 VND or $1.40 to $2.00 USD) along the 4.5 kilometers paved lane (15 minutes by bike, 8 minutes by cart) through a 120 meters blasted rock tunnel into the valley.

- **Access by Trans-Park Jungle Trek:** Fit hikers can tackle the 12 kilometers cross-island trek (4.5 hours to 6.0 hours one-way) from Park Headquarters past **Ao Ech** (Frog Pond, 3.2 hectares flooded forest at 95 meters elevation, 5.5 km from the gate) down to Viet Hai. Hiring a licensed park ranger or local trek guide (500,000 VND to 700,000 VND or $20.00 to $28.00 USD per group) is strongly advised across the unmarked 6.5 km karst ridge between Ao Ech and Viet Hai.

## Accommodation Zones & Island Dining Terroir

Where you sleep on Cat Ba shapes your daily transit expenses and noise levels:

- **1/4 Street Waterfront & Nui Ngoc Backstreets (0 km to 0.5 km from center):** The main hub for backpacker hostels (200,000 VND to 350,000 VND or $8.00 to $14.00 USD per dorm or budget room) and mid-range family hotels (750,000 VND to 1,500,000 VND or $30.00 to $60.00 USD per night), located 5 minutes (1.5 km) from Ben Beo Pier.

- **Cat Co Headland Luxury Resorts (1.2 km to 1.8 km south):** Beachfront towers including Hôtel Perle d'Orient Cat Ba (5-star) and Flamingo Cat Ba charge 2,800,000 VND to 5,500,000 VND ($112.00 to $220.00 USD) per night with direct elevator access to Cat Co 1, 2, and 3 beaches.

- **Xuan Dam & Viet Hai Eco-Lodges (12 km to 15 km from town):** Quiet garden bungalows along the West Coast road (25 minutes by scooter) or inside Viet Hai valley cost 650,000 VND to 1,300,000 VND ($26.00 to $52.00 USD) per night.

Dining along **1/4 Street** (*Đường 1 Tháng 4*) and floating rafts in Cat Ba Bay centers on live Gulf of Tonkin catch weighed by the kilogram:

- **Geoduck Clams (*Tu Hài*):** Grilled over charcoal with scallion oil (50,000 VND to 80,000 VND or $2.00 to $3.20 USD per clam).

- **Mantis Shrimp (*Bề Bề*) & Sea Snails (*Ốc Hương*):** Salt-roasted mantis shrimp runs 350,000 VND to 500,000 VND ($14.00 to $20.00 USD) per kg; tamarind sea snails cost 150,000 VND to 220,000 VND ($6.00 to $8.80 USD) per 400g plate, while a local bowl of *bún tôm* (shrimp noodle soup) costs 40,000 VND to 60,000 VND ($1.60 to $2.40 USD).

## Island Transport and 2026 Arrival Logistics

Reaching Cat Ba Island from Hanoi (150 kilometers total distance) or Ha Long Bay relies on upgraded expressway, ferry, and cable car links:

```
[Hanoi Old Quarter] (07:30 / 10:30 / 14:00 departures)
         |
         v (CT04 Hanoi–Hai Phong Expressway + Tan Vu–Lach Huyen Sea Bridge: 120 km | 2.0 hours)
[Dong Bai Ferry Terminal] (Cat Hai Island Mainland)
         |
         +---> [Bay View Public Ferry] (3.2 km channel | 30 minutes | 15,000 VND / $0.60 USD foot; 45,000 VND / $1.80 USD moto)
         |
         +---> [High-Speed Boat / Sun World Cable Car] (8 to 12 minutes | 60,000 VND – 100,000 VND / $2.40 – $4.00 USD)
         |
         v
[Cai Vieng / Phu Long Port] (Northwestern Cat Ba Island)
         |
         v (Highway DT356 coastal route: 24 km | 40 minutes at 45 km/h)
[Cat Ba Town Center] (Total Hanoi door-to-door time: 3.5 hours | 320,000 VND – 380,000 VND / $12.80 – $15.20 USD)
```

Following the permanent replacement of the legacy Got Terminal, mainland traffic crosses from **Dong Bai Ferry Terminal** (*Bến Phà Đồng Bài*, open 05:30 to 18:30 daily, with ferries departing every 30 minutes) across the 3.2 km Lach Huyen channel to **Cai Vieng Pier** (*Bến Cái Viềng*). Walk-on passengers pay 15,000 VND ($0.60 USD), riders with motorbikes pay 45,000 VND ($1.80 USD), and 4-to-7-seat passenger cars pay 190,000 VND ($7.60 USD) per crossing.

Alternatively, the **Sun World Cat Ba Cable Car** links Cat Hai Station to Phu Long Station across 3,955 meters of open water in 12 minutes (reaching 214.8 meters on its highest concrete tower) for 60,000 VND ($2.40 USD) one-way or 100,000 VND ($4.00 USD) round-trip. Booking a combined **bus-speedboat-bus ticket** with **Daiichi Travel, Cat Ba Express, or Good Morning Cat Ba** (320,000 VND to 380,000 VND, or $12.80 to $15.20 USD one-way) remains the fastest option (3.5 hours total door-to-door from Hanoi's Old Quarter across 150 km).

Travelers arriving from Ha Long City can also board the **Tuan Chau to Gia Luan Car Ferry** (45 minutes crossing, 80,000 VND or $3.20 USD per foot passenger, 100,000 VND or $4.00 USD with motorbike, departing at 07:30, 09:00, 11:30, 13:30, and 15:00). From Gia Luan Pier, a 22 km (35 minutes) public bus (40,000 VND or $1.60 USD) or private taxi (300,000 VND to 350,000 VND, or $12.00 to $14.00 USD) runs south to Cat Ba Town.

> [!TIP]
> Rent a 110cc to 125cc Honda Wave or AirBlade in Cat Ba Town for 120,000 VND to 150,000 VND ($4.80 to $6.00 USD) per 24 hours (50,000 VND or $2.00 USD fuel fills the tank). Ride the 51 kilometers full island loop (2.0 hours riding time) combining the forested interior Highway DT356 (24 km) with the dramatic coastal cliffs of the West Coast Road (27 km via Xuan Dam).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best season to visit Cat Ba Island?
The premier travel window runs from **October to April**, bringing dry skies, calm seas, and mild temperatures between 18°C and 25°C ideal for trekking the 225 meters Ngu Lam Peak and kayaking Lan Ha Bay. May to August offers warm 28°C seawater for swimming at Ba Trai Dao, though July and August see weekend waits of 60 minutes to 90 minutes for private cars at Dong Bai Ferry Terminal.

### Can I do a day trip to Lan Ha Bay without booking an overnight cruise?
Yes. Staying in a Cat Ba Town hotel (750,000 VND or $30.00 USD per night) and booking a 7.5 hours Lan Ha Bay day boat (650,000 VND to 950,000 VND or $26.00 to $38.00 USD departing Ben Beo Pier at 08:00 and returning at 15:30) delivers the exact same limestone scenery, Bright Cave kayaking (100 meters tunnel), and seafood lunch for a fraction of a $350.00 USD overnight cabin cruise.

### Is Cat Ba Island suitable for families with young children?
Yes. Families enjoy the 12 minutes Sun World cable car ride (3,955 meters over the sea), level cycling (4.5 km) in Viet Hai village, and calm mornings at Cat Co 1 Beach. Skip the steep 400 meters limestone scramble atop Ngu Lam Peak with children under 7 years old, choosing the paved 300 meters Trung Trang Cave walk (45 minutes) instead.
