# Da Nang Travel Guide: Beaches, City Base & Day Trips

Central Vietnam's commercial, aviation, and coastal resort hub sits along the sweeping crescent of Da Nang Bay, roughly equidistant between Hanoi (764 km north) and Ho Chi Minh City (960 km south). Home to 1.25 million residents, Da Nang offers an urban infrastructure profile rarely found elsewhere in Southeast Asia: a 30 km ribbon of white-sand ocean beaches, 6-lane palm-lined boulevards free of gridlock, the 200-meter-wide tidal Han River spanned by 6 illuminated bridges, and the 693-meter rainforest peaks of the Son Tra Peninsula rising directly above the bay.

Known during the French protectorate era as Tourane and later expanded into a major air and naval logistics port in 1965, contemporary Da Nang operates as the logistical anchor for our [Central Vietnam itinerary](/itineraries/central-vietnam-itinerary/). Because Da Nang International Airport (DAD) sits just 3.5 km from the downtown riverfront and 5.5 km from My Khe Beach, travelers frequently compare basing here against nearby heritage towns in our [Da Nang vs Hoi An comparison](/compare/da-nang-vs-hoi-an/). From a single sea-view hotel in Da Nang, you can reach the lantern-lit merchant streets of [Hoi An Ancient Town](/destinations/hoi-an-ancient-town-guide/) (28 km south, 40 minutes), the royal citadels and tombs of [Hue Imperial City](/destinations/hue-imperial-city-guide/) (98 km north across the Hai Van Pass, 2 hours by car or 3 hours by scenic coastal train), and the 4th-century Hindu sanctuary of My Son (45 km southwest, 70 minutes).

This 2026 field guide covers neighborhood base selection, 2026 admission fees in VND and USD, automatic-scooter checkpoints on the Son Tra Peninsula, Marble Mountains cave routes, Dragon Bridge pyrotechnic schedules, and month-by-month weather data.

### The Eastern Coastal Strip (My Khe Beach, An Thuong & Non Nuoc)
Stretching 12 km south from the base of the Son Tra Peninsula past the Marble Mountains toward Cua Dai, the eastern coastline detailed in our [Da Nang beaches guide](/destinations/da-nang-beaches-guide/) divides into three contiguous pockets:
* **My Khe Beach & Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard**: My Khe features a 90-meter-wide shelf of fine white quartz sand with gentle 1.5-meter surf breaks and lifeguarded swimming zones open from 05:00 to 18:30. Renting a padded sun lounger under a thatched palm umbrella costs **40,000 VND ($1.60 USD)** for the day, fresh green coconuts cost **25,000 VND to 30,000 VND ($1.00 to $1.20 USD)**, and 1-hour fiberglass surfboard or stand-up paddleboard (SUP) rentals run **150,000 VND to 250,000 VND ($6.00 to $10.00 USD)**. Across the 6-lane Vo Nguyen Giap coastal avenue, 25-story to 40-story 4-star and 5-star hotels (such as Sala Danang Beach, TMS Hotel, and Four Points by Sheraton) offer ocean-view balcony rooms with rooftop infinity pools for **1,200,000 VND to 3,200,000 VND ($48.00 to $128.00 USD)** per night. Open-air live-tank seafood restaurants along Vo Nguyen Giap serve steamed tiger prawns, garlic butter mantis shrimp, and grilled scallops for **350,000 VND to 650,000 VND ($14.00 to $26.00 USD)** per person.
* **The An Thuong Expat Quarter (An Thượng 1 to An Thượng 38)**: Located 200 meters inland from My Khe Beach behind Nguyen Van Thoai and Chau Thi Vinh Te streets, the newly granite-paved An Thuong pedestrian quarter packs specialty coffee roasters (flat whites 45,000 VND to 65,000 VND / $1.80 to $2.60 USD), wood-fired pizzerias, cow-working spaces (day passes 150,000 VND to 220,000 VND / $6.00 to $8.80 USD), craft beer taprooms (80,000 VND to 120,000 VND / $3.20 to $4.80 USD per pint), and 45-square-meter serviced apartments renting for **700,000 VND to 1,400,000 VND ($28.00 to $56.00 USD) per night** or **9,500,000 VND to 16,000,000 VND ($380.00 to $640.00 USD) per month**.
* **Non Nuoc Beach (Truong Sa Road)**: Starting 5 km south of My Khe and running past the Marble Mountains, Non Nuoc houses expansive low-rise 5-star international resorts (Hyatt Regency Danang, Pullman Danang Beach Resort, Furama Resort, and Sheraton Grand) set on 15-hectare to 25-hectare landscaped gardens with multiple lagoon pools, priced at **3,800,000 VND to 8,500,000 VND ($152.00 to $340.00 USD)** per night.

### The Western Riverbank Downtown (Hai Chau District)
Situated on the west bank of the Han River, **Hai Chau District** is Da Nang's traditional commercial and culinary center:
* **Bach Dang Riverfront Promenade**: Running 2.4 km along the western bank of the Han River from Dragon Bridge north to the Novotel tower, Bach Dang Street features a 6-meter-wide tiled pedestrian riverwalk illuminated every evening from 18:30 to 23:00.
* **Da Nang Museum of Cham Sculpture & Con Market**: At the southern foot of the Dragon Bridge (2 Hai Thang Chin Street), the **Da Nang Museum of Cham Sculpture** (founded in 1915; admission **60,000 VND / $2.40 USD**, open 07:00 to 17:00, allow 60 to 75 minutes) displays over 400 sandstone Hindu deities, Shiva lingams, and apsara reliefs dating from the 5th to 15th centuries. Just 1.2 km northwest, **Han Market** on Tran Phu Street and **Con Market (Chợ Cồn)** at 290 Hung Vuong Street anchor the stops in our [Da Nang street food guide](/destinations/da-nang-street-food-guide/), where bowls of turmeric *Mì Quảng* (35,000 VND to 50,000 VND / $1.40 to $2.00 USD), *Bún Chả Cá* fishcake noodle soup at 109 Nguyen Chi Thanh (35,000 VND to 50,000 VND / $1.40 to $2.00 USD), and crispy *Bánh Xèo Bà Dưỡng* in Alley K280/23 Hoang Dieu (80,000 VND to 120,000 VND / $3.20 to $4.80 USD for a full platter with pork skewers) draw local crowds daily.

### Linh Ung Pagoda and the 67-Meter Lady Buddha (Chùa Linh Ứng Bãi Bụt)
Perched on a 120-meter coastal shelf at Bai But overlooking the entire 15 km arc of Da Nang Bay, **Linh Ung Pagoda** is reachable by any car, Grab taxi, or scooter along the wide 4-lane Hoang Sa road:
* **The 67-Meter Guanyin Statue (Tượng Phật Bà Quan Âm)**: Standing **67 meters tall** (equivalent to a 30-story building) atop a 35-meter-wide carved stone lotus pedestal, this white statue of the Bodhisattva of Mercy is the tallest coastal Guanyin monument in Vietnam. Its interior houses 17 stories, each containing 21 miniature Buddha statues.
* **Admission, Hours & Dress Code**: Admission to Linh Ung Pagoda is **0 VND (free)**, with a voluntary **5,000 VND to 10,000 VND ($0.20 to $0.40 USD)** contribution at the motorbike parking shed. The grounds open daily from **06:00 to 21:00**. Arrive between **06:15 and 07:45** for soft morning light on the white statue and 24°C temperatures, or between **16:30 and 18:00** to watch the sunset light up the Da Nang skyline across the bay. Both men and women must wear clothing covering shoulders and knees; free wrap-around sarongs are lent at the main sanctuary gate.

### Mandatory Automatic-Scooter Ban & Ranger Checkpoints Above Bai Bac
If you continue driving north past Linh Ung Pagoda and the InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort toward the **800-Year Banyan Tree (`Cây Đa Ngàn Năm`)**, **Dong Dinh Museum (50,000 VND ticket)**, and **Ban Co Peak (`Đỉnh Bàn Cờ` at 693 meters)**, strict traffic laws apply:
* **Why Automatic Scooters Are Prohibited**: Upper Son Tra forest roads feature narrow 4-meter lanes with sustained **12% to 18% gradients** and blind hairpin switchbacks. On the 7 km descent from Ban Co Peak, riders on CVT belt-driven automatic scooters (`xe tay ga` such as Honda AirBlade, Honda Vision, Honda Lead, Honda PCX, or Yamaha NVX) lack engine-gear braking; riding the hand brakes for 10 minutes boils the brake fluid, resulting in total brake failure at 50 km/h above sheer 200-meter cliffs.
* **Manned Ranger & Police Checkpoints (`Trạm Gác Sơn Trà`)**: Municipal rangers staff physical barriers at the **Bai Bac intersection (next to the InterContinental entrance)** and the **Yet Kieu military road gate** from **07:30 to 18:30** (all upper mountain roads close completely at 19:00). Any rider on an automatic scooter is legally barred from passing the checkpoint.
* **How to Legally Reach Ban Co Peak (693 Meters)**: Rent a **110cc semi-automatic foot-gear motorbike (`xe số` such as a Honda Wave Alpha, Honda Blade, or Yamaha Sirius for 120,000 VND to 150,000 VND / $4.80 to $6.00 USD per 24 hours)** and shift down into **1st or 2nd gear** on all descents while carrying a valid 1968 International Driving Permit as explained in our [Vietnam motorbike license guide](/plan/vietnam-motorbike-license-laws/). Alternatively, book a 4-seat SUV or UAZ jeep tour up to Ban Co Peak for **600,000 VND to 900,000 VND ($24.00 to $36.00 USD)** for a 2.5-hour round-trip.
* **Spotting the Endangered Red-Shanked Douc Langur**: Son Tra shelters over 1,300 critically endangered **red-shanked douc langurs (`voọc chà vá chân nâu`)**, recognizable by their maroon lower legs, white forearms, and golden facial rings. Bring a 200 mm to 400 mm telephoto lens or 8x binoculars between **06:00 and 08:00** or **15:30 and 17:15** along the 4 km stretch between the InterContinental gate and the Banyan Tree, where family troops feed on young fig leaves in the canopy 15 meters overhead.

## 3. Marble Mountains (Ngũ Hành Sơn): Caves, Pagodas & 2026 Ticket Tariffs

Rising abruptly from the flat coastal plain 9 km south of My Khe Beach along Le Van Hien / Non Nuoc Road (15 minutes via a **100,000 VND to 140,000 VND / $4.00 to $5.60 USD** GrabCar, or reachable via public Bus R16 / Bus #1 toward Hoi An for **20,000 VND to 30,000 VND / $0.80 to $1.20 USD**), the **Marble Mountains (`Ngũ Hành Sơn`)** comprise five jagged limestone and marble crags named after the five elements: *Thủy Sơn* (Water), *Mộc Sơn* (Wood), *Hỏa Sơn* (Fire), *Kim Sơn* (Metal), and *Thổ Sơn* (Earth).

### 2026 Official Ticket & Elevator Price Breakdown
All visitor footpaths, Buddhist grottoes, and summit viewpoints are concentrated on **Thủy Sơn (Water Mountain)**, the tallest peak at 106 meters:
* **Thủy Sơn Main Mountain Admission**: **40,000 VND ($1.60 USD)** per adult; **10,000 VND ($0.40 USD)** for students; **0 VND** for children under 6 years old.
* **43-Meter Glass Cliffside Elevator (Optional)**: **15,000 VND ($0.60 USD) per person one-way** (or **30,000 VND / $1.20 USD round-trip**). The elevator lifts you 43 meters vertically in 40 seconds directly to the Xa Loi Tower terrace, saving a 156-step stone climb in 32°C humidity.
* **Âm Phủ Cave (`Động Âm Phủ` — Separate Base Entrance)**: **20,000 VND ($0.80 USD)** per adult. Located at the base of Thủy Sơn next to Gate 2, this 300-meter cavern depicts the Nine Levels of Buddhist Hell and the Heaven Stairway; allow 30 minutes before taking the elevator or stairs up the main peak.
* **Operating Hours & Duration**: Open daily from **07:00 to 17:00** (last elevator ticket sold at 16:45). Budget **90 minutes to 2 hours** to explore the entire circuit.

### Optimal 90-Minute Walking Circuit Inside Thủy Sơn
1. **Enter at Gate 1 (Elevator) at 07:15**: Pay 55,000 VND ($2.20 USD total for the 40,000 VND mountain ticket + 15,000 VND upward elevator) to reach the **Xá Lợi Seven-Story Pagoda** and **Linh Ứng Pagoda** while the stone paths are cool and empty.
2. **Tàng Chơn Cave & Vân Thông Chimney**: Behind Linh Ứng Pagoda, step into Tàng Chơn Cave (`Động Tàng Chơn`), which holds Champa sandstone altars, and climb through the narrow 1.2-meter granite chute of **Vân Thông Cave** out onto the upper sky viewpoint overlooking the East Sea.
3. **Huyền Không Cave (`Động Huyền Không` — The Sunbeam Cavern)**: Walk 200 meters north to the centerpiece of the mountain. Its open-dome cathedral cavern rises 35 meters high. Between **10:00 and 11:30** on sunny days, five natural holes in the collapsed limestone roof project dramatic shafts of sunlight through swirling incense smoke onto a 4-meter seated Buddha statue carved in 1825, inside the same chamber where local guerrillas operated a concealed surgical clinic just 3 km from the American Marble Mountain Air Facility during the war.
4. **Descend on Foot via Gate 1 Stairs**: Walk down the shaded 156 stone steps past the Vọng Giang Đài river viewpoint (looking west over the Co Co River) and visit **Âm Phủ Cave (20,000 VND)** at the bottom.

> [!TIP]
> **Footwear & Marble Shop Advice at Ngũ Hành Sơn**:
> * **Slick Polished Marble Steps**: Three centuries of sandal traffic and coastal mist have polished the limestone steps inside Vân Thông and Huyền Không caves to a mirror sheen. Wear rubber-soled sneakers or trail sandals—never smooth leather flip-flops.
> * **Skip the Elevator Queue by Using Gate 2**: If you arrive after 09:00 and see a 25-minute tour-bus line at the Gate 1 elevator, walk 80 meters south to **Gate 2 (the Stone Stairway Gate)**, pay the 40,000 VND ($1.60 USD) ticket, and climb the shaded 156 steps in 10 minutes. Also note that the marble carving shops lining Huyền Trân Công Chúa Street at the base charge 3x to 5x markup for small jade or soapstone figurines unless you bargain firmly.

## 4. Dragon Bridge Fire Show (`Cầu Rồng`) & Han River Nightlife

Completed in 2013 at a cost of $85 million USD to mark the 38th anniversary of the city's liberation, the **Dragon Bridge (`Cầu Rồng`)** spans **666 meters** across six traffic lanes in the undulating silhouette of a golden Ly Dynasty dragon illuminated by 2,500 color-changing LED lights.

### 2026 Weekend Fire & Water Show Schedule (Friday, Saturday & Sunday at 21:00)
* **Show Days & Exact Time**: Every **Friday, Saturday, and Sunday night at 21:00 sharp** (plus major public holidays), traffic police close the Dragon Bridge to all motorbikes and cars from **20:45 to 21:20**.
* **What Happens During the 15-Minute Show**: At 21:00, the dragon's head (facing east toward My Khe Beach) roars and shoots **two rounds of 9 real fireballs** (18 fire blasts in total, reaching 10 meters to 15 meters into the night sky and radiating heat 25 meters away), immediately followed by **three high-pressure blasts of atomized water mist** lasting 3 minutes.
* **Best Free & Paid Vantage Points**:
  1. **On the Bridge Deck (15 Meters Behind the Dragon's Neck — 0 VND)**: Walk onto the pedestrian sidewalk at the eastern end of the bridge by **20:35**. Stand 20 meters west of the dragon's head if the wind is blowing east—otherwise the final 3-minute water spray will soak your clothes and camera lens!
  2. **Trần Hưng Đạo Riverfront Promenade & Love Lock Bridge (`Cầu Tình Yêu` — 0 VND)**: Located directly below the dragon's head on the east bank next to the marble **Carp Turning into Dragon Statue (`Cá Chép Hóa Rồng`)**. Offers the classic side-profile photo of the fireballs reflecting in the Han River, followed by a walk through **Sơn Trà Night Market (`Chợ Đêm Sơn Trà`)** just 100 meters behind the dragon's tail (grilled lobster halves 180,000 VND to 250,000 VND / $7.20 to $10.00 USD, sugarcane juice 15,000 VND / $0.60 USD).
  3. **Han River Night Cruise Boat (`Du Thuyền Sông Hàn` — 150,000 VND)**: Board a 45-minute to 60-minute sightseeing boat at the Han River Marina (32 Bach Dang Street) for the **20:15 departure (150,000 VND / $6.00 USD per person**, includes 1 bottle of water and fruit plate). Boats anchor 60 meters off the bow of the Dragon Bridge at 20:55 for an unobstructed water-level view.

## 5. Top 4 Day Trips from Da Nang (With 2026 Distances, Times & Prices)

Da Nang sits at the exact center of the Central Heritage Triangle, allowing you to execute four world-class day trips detailed in our [best things to do in Da Nang guide](/destinations/best-things-to-do-in-da-nang/) without changing hotels:

### 1. Sun World Ba Na Hills & the Golden Bridge (`Cầu Vàng` — 28 km West)
Perched at **1,487 meters elevation** atop Mount Chua (where daytime temperatures stay **18°C to 23°C**, roughly 8°C to 10°C cooler than Da Nang beach), [Ba Na Hills and the Golden Bridge](/destinations/ba-na-hills-golden-bridge-guide/) was founded in 1919 as a French hill station:
* **2026 Ticket Tariffs**: Standard international adult admission is **900,000 VND to 950,000 VND ($36.00 to $38.00 USD)** for the round-trip 5,801-meter single-wire cable car (20 minutes ascent), Golden Bridge, French Village, Le Jardin d'Amour, and Fantasy Park, or **1,250,000 VND ($50.00 USD)** for the combo ticket including the **350,000 VND ($14.00 USD)** international lunch buffet at Four Seasons / Arapang Restaurant (children 1.0m to 1.4m tall: 750,000 VND / $30.00 USD ticket, or 950,000 VND with buffet). Note that the updated Alpine Coaster rides (`Máng Trượt`) now charge **70,000 VND ($2.80 USD)** per ride, and the Debay Wine Cellar charges **100,000 VND ($4.00 USD)** including one glass of wine.
* **Transport & Crowd Strategy**: A round-trip private 4-seat car from My Khe Beach to Ba Na Hills (28 km each way, 45 minutes drive) costs **600,000 VND to 700,000 VND ($24.00 to $28.00 USD)** including 6 hours of waiting time, or take the official Sun World shuttle bus for **150,000 VND ($6.00 USD) round-trip**. Depart your hotel at **06:45** to board the first cable car cabin at **07:30** on the **Hội An – Marseille Line**, which drops you directly at the 150-meter-long **Golden Bridge (`Cầu Vàng`)** supported by two giant fiberglass-and-mesh stone hands before the 09:30 tour groups and afternoon mountain clouds roll in.

### 2. The Hai Van Pass (`Đèo Hải Vân`) & Lang Co Lagoon (28 km North)
Climbing 21 km over a coastal spur of the Annamite Range to the Hai Van Gate brick fortress (`Hải Vân Quan`, 0 VND entry, restored in 2024) at **496 meters elevation**, the Hai Van Pass divides Da Nang from Hue. Since through-truck traffic uses the 6.28 km Hai Van Road Tunnel below the mountain, the winding 21 km pass road is quiet and scenic:
* **3 Ways to Cross**:
  1. **Heritage Coastal Train (`HĐ1/HĐ2/HĐ3/HĐ4` or `SE` Trains)**: As compared in our [Da Nang to Hue train vs car guide](/itineraries/da-nang-to-hue-train-vs-car/), the 102 km cliffside rail journey between Da Nang Railway Station (791 Hai Phong Street) and Hue takes **2 hours and 45 minutes to 3 hours and 15 minutes** and costs **110,000 VND to 180,000 VND ($4.40 to $7.20 USD)** for an air-conditioned soft seat (sit on the **right side northbound** or **left side southbound** for uninterrupted ocean cliff views).
  2. **Private Car with Stops**: A private 4-seat sedan from Da Nang to Hue over the pass (including stops at the 496-meter summit bunker, Lang Co Beach, and oyster farms on Lap An Lagoon) takes **3.5 to 4 hours** and costs **1,150,000 VND to 1,400,000 VND ($46.00 to $56.00 USD)** one-way.
  3. **Easy Rider or Self-Drive Motorbike**: One-way motorbike rental with luggage transfer between Da Nang/Hoi An and Hue costs **350,000 VND to 450,000 VND ($14.00 to $18.00 USD)** for self-drive or **1,100,000 VND to 1,350,000 VND ($44.00 to $54.00 USD)** on the back of a professional Easy Rider motorcycle.

### 3. Hoi An Ancient Town (`Phố Cổ Hội An` — 28 km South)
Located 28 km south down the flat coastal highway, [Hoi An Ancient Town](/destinations/hoi-an-ancient-town-guide/) is a 40-minute drive from My Khe Beach (**300,000 VND to 380,000 VND / $12.00 to $15.20 USD** by GrabCar, or see our [Da Nang Airport to Hoi An transport guide](/plan/da-nang-airport-to-hoi-an/)). Leave Da Nang at 14:30 to combine Marble Mountains (15:00 to 16:30) with late-afternoon tailoring, the **120,000 VND ($4.80 USD)** Hoi An Heritage Ticket (covering 5 historic merchant houses and the Japanese Covered Bridge), and a **150,000 VND ($6.00 USD)** 20-minute lantern sampan ride on the Thu Bon River before taking a 350,000 VND ($14.00 USD) GrabCar back to Da Nang at 21:00.

### 4. My Son Sanctuary (`Thánh Địa Mỹ Sơn` — 45 km Southwest)
Set inside a lush ring of cat's-tooth mountains 45 km southwest of Da Nang (70 minutes drive via a **850,000 VND to 1,100,000 VND / $34.00 to $44.00 USD** round-trip private car), the UNESCO-listed **My Son Sanctuary** served as the religious center of the Champa Kingdom from the 4th to the 13th centuries. The **150,000 VND ($6.00 USD)** admission ticket includes the Champa Museum, the 2 km electric buggy shuttle through the forest, a 25-minute live traditional Apsara Shiva dance performance (scheduled at 09:15, 10:45, 14:00, and 15:30), and 71 red-brick Hindu temple ruins across Groups B, C, D, and G. Arrive at **07:30** to walk the brick towers before temperatures exceed 34°C.

## 6. Monthly Weather, Rainfall & Sea State Matrix for Da Nang

Unlike Northern Vietnam (which has a cold January winter) or Southern Vietnam (where rains start in May), Central Vietnam follows a shifted coastal climate where **February through August is dry and sunny**, while **September 20 through December 15 brings heavy northeast monsoon rains**.

| Seasonal Window | Months | Temp & Monthly Rainfall | Ocean & Surf Conditions | Operational Routing Advice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prime Dry Spring** | **Feb 15 – May 15** | 23–31°C / 25–65 mm rain / 72% humidity | Calm 25–27°C turquoise water; 15 m snorkel visibility | **#1 Window for Da Nang**: ideal for My Khe swimming, Cham Islands snorkeling (`850,000 VND` tour), & Hai Van Pass |
| **Hot Dry Summer** | **May 16 – Aug 31** | 27–37°C / 60–110 mm rain / high UV index | Glass-calm 28–30°C sea; safe family swimming | Swim 05:30–09:00 and 16:00–18:30; spend 11:00–15:30 at Ba Na Hills (`20°C` at `1,487m`) or Cham Museum |
| **Autumn Monsoon & Surf** | **Sept 15 – Nov 30** | 23–29°C / **550–750 mm rain** / storm alerts | 1.5–2.5 m swells; red rip-current flags; **Cham Islands closed** | Peak surfing season for experienced boarders; keep indoor food/museum plans ready during 48-hour rain bands |
| **Cool Transition Winter** | **Dec 15 – Feb 14** | 19–25°C / 85–140 mm rain / breezy skies | 21–23°C cool water; moderate onshore chop | Comfortable 22°C weather for climbing Marble Mountains, cycling Son Tra, and walking Hoi An without heat |

## Step-by-Step Arrival Protocol at Da Nang International Airport (`DAD`)

Da Nang International Airport (`DAD`) is the most centrally located major airport in Vietnam, situated directly inside Hai Chau District just **3.5 km from Dragon Bridge (10 minutes)** and **5.5 km from My Khe Beach (15 minutes)**:

1. **Step 1 — Exit Domestic (T1) or International (T2) Arrivals**: After clearing immigration at Terminal 2 (which processes 90-day e-Visas in 15 to 25 minutes) and collecting baggage, buy a local Viettel 4G/5G SIM card at the official arrivals counter (**150,000 VND to 250,000 VND / $6.00 to $10.00 USD** for 15 days of 5 GB/day data) or withdraw cash from the fee-free **TPBank or VPBank ATMs** outside the terminal exit (up to 5,000,000 VND / $200.00 USD per withdrawal with 0 VND local bank fee).
2. **Step 2 — Walk 80 Meters to the Official Grab Pickup Zone**: Open the [Grab app](/plan/grab-in-vietnam-guide/) or Xanh SM electric taxi app before stepping outside. Do not accept rides from individuals holding laminated "Grab" badges inside the terminal sidewalk, as they charge 250,000 VND to 350,000 VND for a 5 km trip. Walk 80 meters to the designated ride-hailing lane in the central parking lot.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Visiting Da Nang

### How many days should I spend in Da Nang vs. Hoi An and Hue?
For a classic 5-to-7-day Central Vietnam itinerary, allocate **3 full days (3 nights) to Da Nang**, **2 to 3 nights to Hoi An**, and **1 to 2 nights to Hue** (or base for all 5 nights in a My Khe beachfront hotel in Da Nang and visit Hoi An and Hue on day trips). In 3 days in Da Nang, spend **Day 1** on My Khe Beach, Marble Mountains (40,000 VND + 15,000 VND elevator), and the 67-meter Lady Buddha on Son Tra Peninsula (0 VND); **Day 2** riding the Hai Van Pass (496 meters) or visiting Ba Na Hills and the Golden Bridge (900,000 VND to 950,000 VND); and **Day 3** visiting My Son Sanctuary (150,000 VND) or snorkeling at the [Cham Islands](/destinations/cham-islands-travel-guide/) before watching the Friday/Saturday/Sunday **21:00 Dragon Bridge fire show**.

### Can I base myself in Da Nang for my whole trip and visit Hoi An on afternoon trips?
Yes—and as analyzed in our [Da Nang vs Hoi An base guide](/compare/da-nang-vs-hoi-an/), over 40% of repeat visitors prefer this setup. Because Da Nang offers newer high-rise hotels at **30% lower nightly rates than Hoi An** (a 4-star ocean-view room with rooftop pool on My Khe Beach costs 1,100,000 VND / $44.00 USD, compared to 1,800,000 VND / $72.00 USD in Hoi An), plus a 15-minute airport transfer and wide beaches that have not suffered coastal erosion, you can spend your mornings swimming at My Khe and take a **40-minute, 320,000 VND ($12.80 USD) GrabCar** south to Hoi An at 15:30 for lantern walks and riverside dining.

### Is My Khe Beach safe for swimming and beginner surfing?
From **March through August**, My Khe Beach is one of the safest urban swimming beaches in Asia: the sandy seabed slopes gradually at 2% to 3%, water temperatures sit between 26°C and 30°C, and municipal lifeguards monitor roped-off swimming zones with yellow-and-red flags from **05:00 to 18:30**. From **September 15 through December**, strong northeast monsoon winds generate 1.5-meter to 2.5-meter shore breaks and rip currents—making autumn the prime season for surf lessons (**600,000 VND to 850,000 VND / $24.00 to $34.00 USD** for a 2-hour 1-on-1 coaching session with a 9-foot foam longboard), while casual swimmers must strictly stay inside the green/yellow buoy lines and obey red no-swim flags.
