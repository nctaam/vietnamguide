# Where to Stay in Chau Doc (2026): Sam Mountain & River

Chau Doc anchors the northwest frontier of the Mekong Delta in An Giang Province, where the wide **Hau River (`Sông Hậu` / Bassac River)** meets the Vinh Te Canal just `25 km` downstream from the Cambodian border. Overland sleeper coaches operated by **FUTA Bus Lines (Phuong Trang)** and **Hue Nghia** run `245 km` (`5.5–6.0 hours`) southwest from Ho Chi Minh City's Mien Tay Bus Station (`210,000–250,000 VND`), while direct air-conditioned vans from Can Tho cover `115 km` along Highway `QL91` in `2.5–3.0 hours` (`130,000–160,000 VND`). For cross-border travelers, Chau Doc serves as Vietnam's premier river port to Cambodia. Every morning at `07:00 AM` and `07:30 AM`, high-speed river cruisers run by **Hang Chau Tourist**, **Mandarin**, and **Blue Cruiser** cast off from the downtown waterfront (`880,000–1,250,000 VND` / `$35–$50 USD`), clear immigration at the **Vinh Xuong – Kaam Samnor river border gate**, and dock at Phnom Penh Autonomous Port in `5 hours`.

Choosing where to sleep in Chau Doc comes down to two contrasting settings: the breezy **Hau River Confluence (`Ngã Ba Sông Châu Đốc`)** in the town center or the `284m` granite slopes of **Sam Mountain (`Núi Sam`)** located `6 km` (`12 minutes` by taxi) to the southwest. Pick a riverfront hotel if you hold a ticket for the `06:30 AM` Phnom Penh speedboat check-in or want to stroll two blocks to **Chau Doc Market (`Chợ Châu Đốc`)** for dawn turmeric fish noodles. Head up the southwestern ridge of **Sam Mountain** if you have two nights and want an infinity pool overlooking flooded emerald rice paddies toward Cambodia alongside early-morning excursions to **Tra Su Melaleuca Forest (`Rừng Tràm Trà Sư`)** `23 km` south.

> **2026 Phnom Penh Boat & Sam Mountain Festival Rule:** If you are booked on the `07:00 AM` or `07:30 AM` high-speed boat to Phnom Penh, sleep within `500m` of the **Victoria Chau Doc Speedboat Pier** (`1 Le Loi`) so you can walk to the `06:30 AM` passport desk in `3 minutes` without gambling on dawn taxis. Conversely, during the **Lady Xu Festival (`Lễ hội Vía Bà Chúa Xứ`)** in the 4th Lunar Month (late May to early June) and weekend nights from January through March, over `100,000` domestic pilgrims jam the lower streets of Sam Mountain—skip budget guesthouses at the mountain foot during those peak periods, stay high on the slope at **Victoria Nui Sam Lodge** or along the river in town, and never accept "free" incense sticks, lotus buds, or caged birds from street touts outside `Miếu Bà`.

## 1. Hau River Confluence & Chau Doc Town Center (`Ngã Ba Sông Châu Đốc`)

Downtown Chau Doc wraps neatly around the riverfront promenade at **30/4 Park (`Công viên 30/4`)**—instantly recognizable by its giant metallic sculpture of two leaping `Cá Basa` catfish—along `Le Loi`, `Doc Phu Thu`, `Chi Lang`, and `Trung Nu Vuong`. Everything here runs on river time. Staying within three blocks of the waterfront places you right beside the **Victoria Speedboat Pier** as well as the public wooden sampan jetties, where private `2-hour` sunrise boat charters (`250,000–350,000 VND/boat` for up to `4` passengers) putter out across the confluence to visit the silver-painted floating fish-farm houses (`Làng bè Châu Đốc`), where families raise thousands of basa and red tilapia in submerged steel-mesh cages underneath their living room floorboards.

Two blocks inland, **Chau Doc Market (`Chợ Châu Đốc`)** comes alive by `05:30 AM`. Celebrated across the Mekong Delta as the **Kingdom of Fermented Fish (`Vương Quốc Mắm`)**, its aromatic central aisles display pyramid mounds of fermented snakehead (`Mắm cá lóc`), seasonal mud carp (`Mắm cá linh`), fermented papaya salad (`Mắm thái`), and sun-dried, palm-sugar glazed catfish (`Khô cá tra phồng`, `180,000–280,000 VND/kg`). Grab a plastic stool along `Chi Lang` or `Doc Phu Thu` for a fragrant morning bowl of **Chau Doc turmeric fish noodle soup (`Bún cá Châu Đốc`, `35,000–45,000 VND`)** simmered with aromatic fingerroot (`Ngải bún`), topped with golden `Bông điên điển` sesbania blossoms and a side plate of crackling roast pork belly (`Heo quay`), then wash it down with iced palmyra palm fruit in syrup (`Thốt nốt tươi`, `25,000 VND/cup`).

### Best Riverfront & Town Center Hotels
* **Victoria Chau Doc Hotel (`1 Le Loi`):** Built in classic low-rise French-colonial architecture directly at the three-way junction of the Hau River and Chau Doc River, this `4-star` heritage hotel remains the town's premier waterfront address. Its `92` timber-floored rooms and suites (`2,800,000–5,200,000 VND` / `$112–$208 USD`) feature louvered shutters and private wrought-iron balconies watching wooden rice barges and Cham ferries glide past. Guests enjoy a riverside swimming pool, open-air **Bassac Bar** cocktails, and a private dock where the `07:00 AM` Phnom Penh speedboats tie up steps from the breakfast terrace.
* **Hung Cuong Hotel (`96 Dong Da`) & Dong Xuyen Hotel (`10 Doc Phu Thu`):** Both `3-star` town-center properties (`750,000–1,350,000 VND` / `$30–$54 USD`) offer spacious air-conditioned twin and double rooms, elevator service, and included buffet breakfast within a `5-minute` flat walk of Chau Doc Market and the `06:30 AM` Cambodia boat check-in counter. Upper-floor river-facing rooms at **Hung Cuong Hotel** catch cool morning breezes off the Bassac channel.
* **Paris Hotel (`Le Loi`) & Regius Hotel (`Doc Phu Thu`):** Clean, family-run budget-to-midrange townhouses (`400,000–850,000 VND` / `$16–$34 USD`) equipped with strong Wi-Fi, motorbike parking, and front desks that can arrange early morning tuk-tuks (`Xe lôi đạp`) or taxis to Sam Mountain.

## 2. Sam Mountain (`Núi Sam`): Sunset Ridge Lodges vs Pilgrimage Base

Rising `284m` abruptly out of pancake-flat alluvial paddies `6 km` (`12 minutes` via `90,000–120,000 VND` Mai Linh, Xanh SM, or Grab taxi) southwest of downtown along `Tan Lo Kieu Luong`, **Sam Mountain (`Núi Sam`)** offers two sharply contrasting accommodation zones: the neon-lit pilgrimage strip at the base and the tranquil granite ridge high on the mountain flank.

Along the northern foot of the mountain stand four national heritage monuments within a `400m` walking radius. **Lady Xu Temple (`Miếu Bà Chúa Xứ`)** draws millions of southern worshippers seeking business prosperity before its green-tiled roof. Next door sit the ornate yellow-and-pink Hindu-Islamic arches of **Tay An Pagoda (`Chùa Tây An`, founded `1847`)**, the nine-stepped red-granite **Tomb of Thoai Ngoc Hau (`Lăng Thoại Ngọc Hầu`)** honoring the Mandarin general who excavated the `87 km` Vinh Te Canal between `1819` and `1824`, and the **Sam Mountain Cable Car (`Cáp treo Núi Sam`, `150,000 VND` round-trip)**. Halfway up the western cliff face clings **Hang Pagoda (`Chùa Hang / Chùa Phước Điền`)**, where peaceful bougainvillea-draped courtyards and rock grottoes look out over an endless green chessboard of Vietnamese and Cambodian rice fields bordered by sugar palms (`Cây thốt nốt`).

### Best Sam Mountain Retreats
* **Victoria Nui Sam Lodge (`Vinh Dong 1, Nui Sam Hillside`):** Terraced high on the quiet southwestern ridge of Sam Mountain well above the temple traffic and loudspeaker noise, this boutique eco-lodge arranges `36` private French-rustic stone bungalows down a wildflower-planted hillside (`2,400,000–4,500,000 VND` / `$96–$180 USD`). Every bungalow features red clay floor tiles, high vaulted ceilings, and a private stone deck facing an unbroken `180-degree` panorama of the An Giang plains. The lodge's dramatic cliff-edge infinity pool and open-air **La Taverne Restaurant** are unmatched between `17:00` and `18:15` when the sun sinks into the flooded paddies along the Cambodian frontier.
* **Sang Nhu Ngoc Resort (`QL91 Foot of Sam Mountain`) & Murray Guesthouse:** **Sang Nhu Ngoc Resort** occupies a quiet, landscaped garden plot on the southern side of the mountain base (`1,100,000–1,900,000 VND` / `$44–$76 USD`) with detached single-story villas, a large swimming pool, and quick highway access south toward Tra Su Forest. Budget travelers wanting to sleep near the temples outside festival dates can book clean rooms with rooftop views at **Murray Guesthouse** (`550,000–850,000 VND`).

## 3. Chau Phong Cham Muslim Village (`Làng Chăm Châu Phong`)

Cross the new **Chau Doc Bridge (`Cầu Châu Đốc`)**—opened in `2024` to replace the historic Con Tien car ferry—or board a `15-minute` wooden sampan from the downtown market pier to reach **Da Phuoc** and **Chau Phong Cham Village (`Làng Chăm Châu Phong`)** on the east bank. Roughly `15,000` Sunni Muslim Cham residents inhabit weathered ironwood stilt houses (`Nhà sàn người Chăm`) built `2–3m` above the ground to stay dry during the annual autumn Mekong floods. Towering above the stilt rooftops is the white-and-emerald **Mubarak Mosque (`Thánh đường Hồi giáo Mubarak`)**, designed by modernist Vietnamese architect Nguyen Ba Lang in `1965` with onion domes and crescent minarets, alongside the grand **Masjid Jamiul Azhar** (`1959`).

Afternoon walks through the shady lanes of Chau Phong lead to the **Chau Phong Brocade Weaving Cooperative (`Hợp tác xã Dệt thổ cẩm Châu Phong`)**, where Cham women work wooden foot looms to weave intricate geometric silk sarongs, headscarves (`Khăn Ma-tơ-ra`), and shoulder bags (`120,000–450,000 VND`). Stop at local Halal family kitchens to taste rich **Cham beef curry (`Cà ri bò Chăm`)** cooked with star anise, cinnamon, and coconut milk alongside grilled **Cham fermented beef sausage (`Tung lò mò`, `80,000–150,000 VND`)** stuffed into sun-dried beef casings with sour sticky rice. Travelers seeking deep cultural immersion can arrange overnight stays with accredited community stilt-house homestays (`350,000–750,000 VND` including home-cooked Halal dinner and breakfast).

## 4. Tra Su Melaleuca Forest (`Rừng Tràm Trà Sư`) & Seven Mountains (`Thất Sơn`)

Drive `23 km` (`35 minutes` by taxi `300,000–400,000 VND` round-trip or rented motorbike `150,000 VND/day` via `QL91` and `DT948`) south of Sam Mountain into Tinh Bien District to reach **Tra Su Melaleuca Forest (`Rừng Tràm Trà Sư`)**. Planted across `845 hectares` of flooded alum wetland, this dense cajeput (`Tràm`) sanctuary shelters more than `70` species of waterbirds—including painted storks, purple herons, Oriental darters (`Điêng điểng`), and black-crowned night herons—plus fruit bats and lotus canals crossed by Vietnam's longest bamboo footbridge (`Cầu tre vạn bước`).

* **The Tra Su Duckweed & Boat Timing Rule:** Avoid arriving between `10:30 AM` and `14:00 PM`, when harsh overhead sun drives birds deep into the foliage and tour buses crowd the jetty. Time your arrival for **`07:00–08:30 AM`** to catch cool morning mist filtering through the paperbark trunks, or **`15:30–17:00 PM`** to watch thousands of white egrets and storks spiral home to roost from the telescope on the `25m` observation tower. The full boat-and-entry ticket costs **`200,000 VND/person`** (`100,000 VND` forest & bamboo-bridge admission + `50,000 VND` long-tail motorboat transfer (`Tắc ráng`) along the main canal + `50,000 VND` silent `30-minute` hand-paddled wooden sampan (`Xuồng chèo tay`) gliding through a thick neon-green carpet of duckweed and water lettuce `Bèo tấm`). Peak emerald duckweed coverage and water levels occur during the **Mekong Flood Season (`Mùa nước nổi`, September through November)**.
* **Combining with Ta Pa Lake & Cam Mountain (`Thất Sơn`):** After leaving Tra Su, continue `18 km` south into Tri Ton District to photograph the mineral-blue quarry waters of **Ta Pa Lake (`Hồ Tà Pạ`)**, the elevated Khmer Theravada pillars of **Ta Pa Pagoda (`Chùa Tà Pạ`)**, palm-lined **Latra Road (`Hàng thốt nốt trái tim`)**, and `710m`-high **Cam Mountain (`Núi Cấm`)**, the tallest peak in the mystical Seven Mountains (`Thất Sơn`) range.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I stay at Victoria Chau Doc Hotel or Victoria Nui Sam Lodge?
Book **Victoria Chau Doc Hotel** (`1 Le Loi`) if you are spending **1 night** before boarding the `07:00 AM` high-speed boat to Phnom Penh or want to walk to Chau Doc Market and riverfront cafés. Reserve a stone bungalow at **Victoria Nui Sam Lodge** (`6 km` southwest on Sam Mountain) if you are staying **2 nights**, prioritize a cliff-edge infinity pool overlooking the rice fields at sunset, and plan early trips to Hang Pagoda and Tra Su Melaleuca Forest.

### How does the Chau Doc to Phnom Penh high-speed boat work in 2026?
**Hang Chau Tourist**, **Mandarin Cruiser**, and **Blue Cruiser** depart the Chau Doc riverfront daily at `07:00 AM` and `07:30 AM` (`880,000–1,250,000 VND` / `$35–$50 USD` one-way; check in at `06:30 AM`). During the `5-hour` cruise up the Mekong/Bassac channel, the boat stops at the **Vinh Xuong (Vietnam) and Kaam Samnor (Cambodia) river border posts**, where onboard crew process Cambodia Visa on Arrival (`$30–$35 USD` cash in clean, untorn US bills plus one passport photo) or pre-approved Cambodia e-Visas before docking in central Phnom Penh around `12:30 PM`.

### When is the best month to visit Chau Doc and Tra Su Forest?
**September through November** (`Mùa nước nổi` / Mekong floating-water season) is the best window to visit. Rising Mekong floodwaters coat **Tra Su Melaleuca Forest** in its thickest emerald carpet of `Bèo tấm` water lettuce, while Chau Doc Market overflows with seasonal `Cá linh` fish and bright yellow `Bông điên điển` flowers.
