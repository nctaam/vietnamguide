# Best Things to Do in Quy Nhon (2026): Ky Co Beach, Eo Gio Cliffs, Cham Towers & Bai Xep Cove

Tucked into a horseshoe bay of golden sand and granite headlands halfway between Hoi An (`300 km` north) and Nha Trang (`215 km` south), **Quy Nhon (`Quy Nhơn`, Binh Dinh Province)** is Vietnam's most rewarding unspoiled coastal city. While Da Nang and Nha Trang are lined with international tower blocks, Quy Nhon retains an unhurried, fiercely authentic Vietnamese seaside rhythm: a `5-kilometer` crescent boulevard (**`Đường Xuân Diệu`**) with zero beach touts, **11th-to-13th-century Hindu Champa brick temple towers** rising right inside the city and on surrounding hilltops (`for 20,000 VND entry`), dramatic volcanic sea cliffs at **Eo Gio (`Eo Gió`)** and **Ky Co (`Kỳ Co`)**, and sleepy fishing coves like **Bai Xep (`Bãi Xép`)** and **Nhon Ly (`Nhơn Lý`)**.

Even better, because 90% of Quy Nhon's visitors are domestic Vietnamese food lovers, beachfront live seafood along **Xuan Dieu Street** and **Jumping Shrimp Pancakes (`Bánh Xèo Tôm Nhảy`)** cost roughly **30% to 45% less** than equivalent meals in Nha Trang or Phu Quoc. Here is the complete 2026 empirical guide to sights, speedboat tariffs, Cham ruins, and dining in Quy Nhon.

## 8 Best Things to Do in Quy Nhon (2026 Insider Field Guide)

### 1. Walk the `Eo Gió` Sea Cliffs & Swim in the Emerald Lagoon of `Kỳ Co` (`Nhơn Lý` Peninsula)
Drive `22 kilometers` (`30 minutes` by motorbike or GrabCar) northeast out of downtown Quy Nhon across the **`2.47-kilometer` Thi Nai Over-Lagoon Bridge (`Cầu Thị Nại`)** onto the wind-sculpted **Phuong Mai Peninsula (`Bán Đảo Phương Mai`)** to **Nhon Ly Fishing Village (`Làng Chài Nhơn Lý`)**:
- **Eo Gio (`Eo Gió` — *"The Strait of Wind"*, ticket `25,000 VND`)**: A horseshoe amphitheater of jagged granite cliffs dropping straight into crashing turquoise surf. A stone footpath lined with red-and-white railings hugs the cliff contour above sea caves where swiftlets build prized edible bird's nests (`yến sào`). Immediately beside the Eo Gio entrance, climb the steps to **Ngoc Hoa Sanctuary (`Tịnh Xá Ngọc Hòa`, free)** to see Vietnam's tallest **Twin-Faced Statue of Quan Am Buddha (`30 meters` high)**—one gold face looking toward the mountains for forest peace, one silver face looking out to sea to protect fishermen.
- **Ky Co Beach (`Bãi Kỳ Co` — *"The Palawan of Vietnam"*)**: Framed on three sides by vertical rocky headlands, Ky Co is a `1-kilometer` crescent of flour-white sand and crystal-clear two-tone emerald water with natural tidal rock pools (`Hồ Suối Đẹp`).
  - **How to Visit (`Speedboat vs Road`)**: During the calm dry season (**March through August**), book a **Nhon Ly Speedboat Package (`650,000–850,000 VND` / `$26–$34 USD` per person)**, which includes a thrilling `12-minute` boat ride along the sea cliffs to Ky Co pier, Ky Co entrance ticket, `45 minutes` of coral snorkeling at **Hon Seo Islet (`Bãi Dứa / Hòn Sẹo`)**, freshwater showers, and a **7-to-8-dish fresh seafood lunch** back in Nhon Ly village! If visiting between **September and February** (or if you get seasick), drive up the paved mountain road and pay **`170,000 VND` (`120,000 VND` ticket + `50,000 VND` mandatory steep-slope electric buggy)** straight down to the sand.

### 2. Explore the Ancient Kingdom of Vijaya: `Tháp Bánh Ít` & `Tháp Đôi` Cham Towers
From the **`11th to 15th centuries` (`1000–1471 AD`)**, Binh Dinh Province was the royal capital of the **Champa Kingdom (Vijaya Era)**, leaving behind eight distinct complexes of red-brick Hindu temples:
- **Banh It Towers (`Tháp Bánh Ít` / "Silver Towers", `18 km` north of town, ticket `20,000 VND`)**: Perched atop a `100-meter` green hill overlooking the Kon River delta, four surviving `11th-century` sanctuary towers—led by the `22-meter` main *Kalan* with curved boat-prow roof arches and sandstone Garuda carvings—offer `360-degree` country views and almost zero crowds.
- **Twin Towers (`Tháp Đôi`, right on `Trần Hưng Đạo Street` in downtown Quy Nhon, ticket `20,000 VND`)**: Two richly carved late-`12th-century` towers (`20m` and `18m` tall) blending classic Champa brickwork with **Angkor-era Khmer stone friezes** of dancing figures, mythical *Makara* sea monsters, and lotus-petal cornices.

> [!TIP]
> **Pair `Tháp Bánh Ít` with `Chùa Thiên Hưng` (`Thien Hung Zen Monastery`) & `Tháp Cánh Tiên` on a Half-Day Northern Loop**: Only `10 kilometers` from Banh It Towers sits **Thien Hung Pagoda (`Chùa Thiên Hưng`, An Nhon District, free entry)**—famous for its 12-story **Bell Tower**, jade gardens, and curved wooden bridges reflecting in lotus ponds bordered by emerald rice paddies.

### 3. Spend an Afternoon (or 2 Nights) in Car-Free `Làng Chài Bãi Xép` (`Bai Xep Fishing Cove`)
Just `12 kilometers` (`15 minutes` by taxi for `150,000 VND`) south of downtown Quy Nhon along the scenic `QL1D` coastal cliff road, **Bai Xep (`Bãi Xép`)** is a tiny fishing village where narrow pedestrian stone alleys wind between brightly painted fisherman cottages down to a golden cove dotted with round bamboo basket boats (`thuyền thúng`) and giant granite boulders. Grab a cold craft beer on a woven hammock at **Life's a Beach** or **Big Tree Bistro**, swim at sunset, and order whole grilled red snapper and garlic scallops cooked on charcoal braziers right on the sand for `150,000–250,000 VND`.

### 4. Snorkel the Golden Seaweed Reefs at `Cù Lao Hòn Khô` & Walk `Mũi Vi Rồng`
- **Hon Kho Island (`Đảo Hòn Khô`, `16 km` east of Quy Nhon at **Nhon Hai Fishing Village**)**: A `5-minute` boat ride (`100,000 VND` round-trip, or `350,000–550,000 VND` with SUP & snorkeling) brings you to a granite island encircled by a rustic wooden cliff boardwalk and shallow coral beds. If you visit between **May and July**, shallow snorkeling reveals underwater forests of **golden *Sargassum* seaweed (`mùa rong mơ Hòn Khô`)** swaying in `1-to-2-meter` turquoise water—and at low tide, a `500-meter` natural sandbar (`con đường giữa biển`) emerges across the channel!
- **Ghenh Rang – Tien Sa (`Ghềnh Ráng Tiên Sa` & `Bãi tắm Hoàng Hậu` Egg-Stone Beach, `3 km` south of center, free)**: Walk the pine hill above the southern end of Quy Nhon Bay to see the smooth, egg-shaped cobblestones of **Queen Nam Phuong's Private Beach (`Bãi Đá Trứng`)** and the hilltop tomb of romantic poet **Han Mac Tu**.

## What to Eat in Quy Nhon: `Bánh Xèo Tôm Nhảy`, Fish-Cake Noodles & Seafood Street (`Đường Xuân Diệu`)

1. **`Bánh Xèo Tôm Nhảy Quy Nhơn` (Crispy Rice-Flour Crepes with Live Lagoon Shrimp & Thin Rice Paper, `35,000–55,000 VND/plate`)**: Unlike the thick yellow turmeric crepes of Saigon or Da Nang, Binh Dinh's signature `Bánh Xèo` is fried ultra-crisp in small cast-iron molds using uncolored rice batter, beef tenderloin, mung beans, and sweet red-shell **Thi Nai Lagoon shrimp (`tôm đất đầm Thị Nại`)** before being wrapped at your table in paper-thin `bánh tráng` with green mango and spicy mango-peanut-fish sauce. Eat at **`Bánh Xèo Tôm Nhảy Gia Vỹ 2`** (`14 Diên Hồng`), **`Bánh Xèo Ông Hùng`** (`24 Diên Hồng`), or **`Bánh Xèo Bà Năm Mỹ Cang`** (`Cầu Mỹ Cang`, Tuy Phuoc).
2. **`Bún Chả Cá & Bún Sứa Quy Nhơn` (Steamed Mackerel Fish-Cake & Crunchy Jellyfish Noodle Soup, `35,000–50,000 VND`)**: Order a steaming bowl for breakfast. Try the legendary local institutions **`Bún Cá Ngọc Liên`** (`379 Nguyễn Huệ`), **`Bún Cá Phượng Tèo`** (`209–211 Nguyễn Huệ`), or **`Bún Cá Thụy`** (`261 Tăng Bạt Hổ`).
3. **`Bánh Hỏi Cháo Lòng Hồng Thanh` (`22 Phan Bội Châu`, `40,000–55,000 VND`)**: Quy Nhon's traditional breakfast—fine woven rice-vermicelli sheets brushed with chive oil, served alongside grilled pork, five-spice offal, and a bowl of hot peppery rice porridge.
4. **Live Beachfront Seafood Along `Đường Xuân Diệu` & `Phố Ẩm Thực Ngô Văn Sở`**: Walk along **Xuan Dieu Boulevard** at `18:30 PM` to dine on **Huynh De Emperor Crab (`Cua Huỳnh Đế`, `750,000–1,100,000 VND/kg`)**, grilled jumping snails (`ốc nhảy`), and sea urchin at **`Hải Sản Hải Sỹ`** (`35B Nguyễn Huệ`), **`Cây Dừa`** (`33A Nguyễn Huệ`), or **`7 Thơm`** (`along Xuân Diệu`). For `30,000–80,000 VND` plates of steamed clams and snails, visit **Oc Thuy Kieu (`5D Tăng Bạt Hổ`)** or **Ngo Van So Food Street (`Phố Ẩm Thực Ngô Văn Sở`)**.

## Step-by-Step HowTo: The Ideal 3-Day / 2-Night Quy Nhon Coastal Itinerary

### 1. First Step — Day 1: Arrive via `UIH` Airport or `Diêu Trì` Train, Visit `Tháp Đôi`, `Ghềnh Ráng` & `Xuân Diệu` Seafood
Fly into **Phu Cat Airport (`UIH`, `30 km` north — take the `50,000 VND` airport bus to `01 Nguyễn Tất Thành` or a `250,000–300,000 VND` taxi)** or arrive by train at **Dieu Tri Station (`Ga Diêu Trì`, `12 km` west)**. Spend your first afternoon visiting **Thap Doi Twin Cham Towers (`20,000 VND`)** and **Queen's Egg-Stone Beach (`Ghềnh Ráng`)**, swim at **Quy Nhon City Beach**, and feast on **`Bánh Xèo Tôm Nhảy Gia Vỹ`** (`14 Diên Hồng`) followed by cocktails at **Surf Bar** right on the sand!

### 2. Second Step — Day 2: Morning Speedboat to `Bãi Kỳ Co` & `Hòn Sẹo` Coral Reef + Afternoon Sunset at `Eo Gió`
Cross **Thi Nai Bridge** at `07:45 AM` to **Nhon Ly Fishing Village**: board a morning speedboat (`650k–850k VND` all-inclusive) to swim in the turquoise cove of **Ky Co Beach**, snorkel coral gardens at **Hon Seo** (or **Hon Kho Island**), eat a fresh seafood lunch in the fishing village, and walk the cliffside trail of **Eo Gio (`25,000 VND`)** and **Ngoc Hoa Twin Buddha** in golden `16:00 PM` light.

### 3. Final Step — Day 3: Morning `Tháp Bánh Ít` (Silver Cham Towers) & `Chùa Thiên Hưng` + Afternoon in `Bãi Xép` Cove
Start your third morning with a bowl of **`Bún Cá Ngọc Liên` (`379 Nguyễn Huệ`)**, then ride `25 minutes` north to photograph the 11th-century hilltop **Banh It Cham Towers (`20,000 VND`)** and **Thien Hung Zen Monastery (`0 VND`)** before spending a lazy final afternoon swimming and eating grilled squid on the sand at **Bai Xep Fishing Village (`12 km` south)**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I get to Quy Nhon from Hoi An, Da Nang, or Nha Trang in 2026?
You have three easy ways to reach Quy Nhon:
1. **By Train (`Reunification Express` / `SE` Trains to `Ga Diêu Trì`)**: All mainline North–South trains stop at **Dieu Tri Railway Station (`Ga Diêu Trì`)**, located **`12 kilometers` (`20 minutes` / `150,000 VND` by Grab or taxi)** west of downtown Quy Nhon. The scenic coastal train takes **`4.5 to 5.5 hours` from Da Nang (`250,000–480,000 VND`)** or **`3.5 to 4.5 hours` from Nha Trang (`200,000–380,000 VND`)**.
2. **By Luxury Limousine Van (`4 to 5.5 hours`, `250,000–400,000 VND`)**: VIP 9-seat limousines (`Sơn Tùng`, `Limo24h`, `Quang Hạnh`) pick up directly from Hoi An, Da Nang, or Nha Trang and drop you at your hotel in Quy Nhon.
3. **By Flight to Phu Cat Airport (`UIH`, `30 km` north of Quy Nhon)**: `1-hour` direct flights operate daily from **Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`)** and **Hanoi (`HAN`)**; outside the terminal, the official **Airport Shuttle Bus (`50,000 VND` / `$2.00 USD`, 45 minutes)** runs straight to `01 Nguyễn Tất Thành` in central Quy Nhon after every landing!

### When is the best time of year to visit Quy Nhon and Ky Co Beach?
The prime dry, sunny beach season in Quy Nhon runs for seven months from **February through August** (with **March to August** delivering calm, glass-clear turquoise seas ideal for taking the speedboat out to **Ky Co Beach** and snorkeling **Hon Kho / Hon Seo**). Avoid **October through December**, when the central coast typhoon and northeast monsoon season brings heavy rain and high waves that close island speedboats.

### Should I stay in Downtown Quy Nhon (`Xuân Diệu`) or out in `Bãi Xép` / `Nhơn Lý`?
If you want walkable street food, lively evening seafood stalls, beach bars (`Surf Bar`), and easy access to both the northern peninsula (`Ky Co / Eo Gio`) and southern coves, stay in **Downtown Quy Nhon along `Xuân Diệu` or `An Dương Vương` Boulevards** (`Fleur De Lys Hotel`, `Anya Premier`, or boutique hotels for `600,000–2,200,000 VND/night`). If you want total peace, bohemian beach bars, or luxury cliffside villas (`Avani Quy Nhon`, `Anantara Quy Nhon`, or `Life's a Beach`), stay `12 km` south in **Bai Xep (`Ghềnh Ráng`)**!
