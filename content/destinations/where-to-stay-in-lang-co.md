# Where to Stay in Lang Co (2026): Beach & Lagoon Resorts

Stretched along a narrow 10 km white-sand peninsula in Phu Loc District, Thua Thien Hue Province, [Lang Co](/destinations/lang-co/) occupies the dramatic coastal hinge between [Da Nang](/destinations/da-nang/) and [Hue](/destinations/hue/). Along its eastern shore, the turquoise swells of the East Sea roll onto **Lang Co Spit** and **Canh Duong Beach**. Across its western edge, the brackish, mirror-flat waters of **Lap An Lagoon** (`Đầm Lập An`) cover 800 hectares against the forested granite walls of the Truong Son Range and **Bach Ma National Park** (28 km inland).

Location dictates your entire stay here. **Da Nang International Airport (`DAD`)** lies just 30 km south (40 to 45 minutes by private car, 450,000 to 600,000 VND / $18 to $24 USD) if your driver takes the 6.28 km **Hai Van Tunnel** (`Hầm Hải Vân`, toll fee 110,000 VND per car), or 55 to 70 minutes over the switchbacks of the 21 km **Hai Van Pass** (`Đèo Hải Vân`, `QL1A`) rising to 496 meters elevation. In the opposite direction, the Imperial Citadel of [Hue](/destinations/hue/) sits 65 km north (1 hour 15 minutes via `QL1A` through Phu Bai Tunnel and Phuoc Tuong Tunnel, 700,000 to 850,000 VND / $28 to $34 USD by taxi), while **Hue Phu Bai Airport (`HUI`)** is 50 km north (50 minutes).

Yet Lang Co is split into three distinct lodging zones that do not connect on foot. A traveler booking the luxury sanctuary of **Laguna Lang Co** (`Banyan Tree Lang Co` and `Angsana Lang Co`) stays 14 km north of Lang Co town near **Chan May Port** (`Cảng Chân Mây`), separated from the town's oyster shacks by a mountain spur and a 20-minute taxi ride (220,000 to 280,000 VND / $8.80 to $11.20 USD each way). Conversely, travelers booking along the 10 km sandbar of `Lac Long Quan` street must distinguish between quiet east-side beachfront bungalows and noisy west-side guesthouses backing onto National Highway 1A (`QL1A`).

> **Booking Warning:** Never book a west-facing budget hotel directly on the `QL1A` truck corridor without checking which side of the road the building occupies. Heavy 18-wheel container trucks and overnight sleeper buses bypass the Hai Van Pass by funneling through the Hai Van Tunnel exit at the southern tip of Lang Co, rumbling along the western strip of `Lac Long Quan` (`QL1A`) 24 hours a day. Always choose east-side beachfront rooms facing the sea or quiet west-side rooms set directly on `Nguyen Van` street along **Lap An Lagoon**, which sits 400 meters to 1.2 km west of the highway traffic.

## 1. Laguna Lang Co & Canh Duong Beach (Chan May Luxury Enclave)

Fourteen kilometers north of the Hai Van Tunnel portal, where travelers turn off `QL1A` onto Provincial Road `DT49B` toward **Chan May Deep-Water Port**, the 280-hectare **Laguna Lang Co** integrated resort occupies a private 3 km crescent at **Canh Duong Beach** (`Bãi biển Cảnh Dương`) framed by emerald jungle ridges. This enclave operates as a self-contained coastal estate with an 18-hole, par-71 championship golf course designed by Sir Nick Faldo (green fees 3,200,000 to 4,400,000 VND / $128 to $176 USD for 18 holes including caddy and shared cart).

### Banyan Tree Lang Co vs Angsana Lang Co

Two sister five-star properties share the Laguna shoreline and connect via a 1.5 km internal canal boat and electric buggy network:

* **Banyan Tree Lang Co** (`7,800,000–12,500,000 VND` / `$312–$500 USD` per night for Lagoon Pool Villas and Beach Pool Villas; two-bedroom Hillside Seaview Pool Villas reach `18,500,000–26,000,000 VND` / `$740–$1,040 USD`): Every single key here is an all-pool villa modeled after traditional imperial garden houses of [Hue](/destinations/hue/), featuring timber lattice screens, Champa sandstone carvings, and private infinity plunge pools. Couples book the cantilevered Hillside Pool Villas for 180-degree views over the East Sea, or the Beach Pool Villas for direct barefoot access to the sand.
* **Angsana Lang Co** (`3,800,000–6,500,000 VND` / `$152–$260 USD` per night for Deluxe Rooms and One-Bedroom Pool Lofts): Built in a sleek contemporary low-rise block right beside Banyan Tree, Angsana targets families and groups. Its centerpiece is a 300-meter serpentine freeform swimming pool that winds through the entire resort grounds to the beach.
* **Laguna Park Townhouses** (`2,900,000–4,600,000 VND` / `$116–$184 USD` per night): Two-bedroom and three-bedroom garden residences lining the 18th fairway of the Laguna Golf Course, ideal for families cooking their own breakfasts with full kitchenettes while accessing Angsana's pools and water sports center.

### The Captive Dining Trade-Off at Laguna Lang Co

Isolation is both Laguna's greatest luxury and its main budget trap. Because the resort gates sit 4.5 km off `QL1A` and 14 km from Lang Co town, you cannot stroll out the front lobby to a local street market. Inside the complex, dinner at Banyan Tree's Thai restaurant **Saffron** or beachfront **Azura**, or Angsana's **Moomba** and **Rice Bar**, costs `350,000 to 900,000 VND` ($14 to $36 USD) per main course plus 15.5% service charge and VAT. To eat authentic local seafood without paying resort prices, walk 600 meters south along the sand to the local family shacks at **Canh Duong Village** (`Quán Hải Sản Tuyết`, `Quán Thanh Tâm`), where a kilogram of steamed flower crab (`Ghẹ hấp`) or grilled tiger prawns costs `350,000 to 550,000 VND` ($14 to $22 USD) and side dishes run `120,000 to 180,000 VND` ($4.80 to $7.20 USD).

## 2. Lang Co Spit Beachfront (`Lac Long Quan` Street)

For travelers who want to wake up on the famous 10 km sandbar directly beneath the northern foot of the Hai Van Pass, mid-range beachfront resorts line the eastern side of `Lac Long Quan` street. Here the peninsula measures just 350 to 600 meters wide from sea to lagoon.

### Best Mid-Range Beach Resorts on the Sandbar

* **Lang Co Beach Resort** (`1,100,000–2,200,000 VND` / `$44–$88 USD` per night; 463 Lac Long Quan): Operating across 9 hectares of coconut palms with 100 meters of private beachfront, this traditionalHue-roofed villa resort offers two freshwater pools and wooden beachfront bungalows. Always pay the extra 350,000 VND ($14 USD) for an **Ocean Front Villa** near the sand rather than a **Budget Garden Room** near the highway entrance gate, where nocturnal container trucks on `QL1A` rattle the windowpanes.
* **Thanh Tam Seaside Resort** (`850,000–1,650,000 VND` / `$34–$66 USD` per night; 392 Lac Long Quan): A practical three-star coastal option right next door with direct sand access, a 25-meter pool, and family quad rooms popular with domestic road-trippers driving between [Where to Stay in Da Nang](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-da-nang/) and [Where to Stay in Hue](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-hue/).
* **Nirvana Spa & Resort** (`950,000–1,800,000 VND` / `$38–$72 USD` per night; 130 Lac Long Quan): Located 3 km farther north up the spit where beach crowds thin out completely, offering spacious sea-facing duplex suites and quiet morning swims from April through August.

Staying on `Lac Long Quan` puts you within 1.5 km of **Lang Co Railway Station** (`Ga Lăng Cô`), where regional heritage trains (`HĐ1/HĐ2` and `HĐ3/HĐ4` connecting Hue and Da Nang for `150,000 to 180,000 VND` / `$6.00 to $7.20 USD`) and select North–South **Reunification Express** (`SE21/SE22`) services pause right beside the lagoon before climbing the dramatic cliffside rail tracks of the Hai Van Pass.

## 3. Lap An Lagoon & `Nguyen Van` Oyster Loop

Just west of the `QL1A` bridge at the southern entrance to Lang Co town, **Nguyen Van Street** (`Đường Nguyễn Văn`) branches off to trace a serene 7 km asphalt loop around the perimeter of **Lap An Lagoon** (`Đầm Lập An`). Fed by both mountain streams from the Bach Ma range and tidal inflows from the sea, this shallow 800-hectare lagoon is Vietnam's premier blood-cockle and rock-oyster cultivation basin.

### Lagoon Homestays, Oyster Rafts & Sunset Sandbar Walks

Because commercial trucks stay on `QL1A`, `Nguyen Van` street enjoys near-total silence after 20:00. Boutique guesthouses, lakefront homestays, and small family hotels (**Lap An Lagoon Homestay**, **Tam Giang Villa Lang Co**, and **Hau Loc Guesthouse**) rent clean air-conditioned double rooms with lagoon balconies for `450,000 to 1,350,000 VND` ($18 to $54 USD) per night.

At low tide between 16:00 and 18:00, a curved white-sand path emerges in the center of the lagoon, allowing guests to wade 150 meters out into ankle-deep water while purple sunset light reflects off the peaks of Bach Ma. Dinner here is unbeatable for value. Along `Nguyen Van` street, over-water bamboo stilt restaurants—led by **Bé Thân**, **Bé Đen**, **Làng Chài Lăng Cô**, and **Vietpearl Seafood**—serve freshly harvested Lap An oysters (`Hàu đầm Lập An`) grilled with scallion oil and peanuts or baked with cheese for `120,000 to 160,000 VND` ($4.80 to $6.40 USD) per kilogram, steamed blood cockles (`Sò huyết đầm Lập An`) for `150,000 VND` ($6.00 USD) per plate, and sour-sweet cobia fish hotpot (`Lẩu cá bớp`) for `250,000 to 350,000 VND` ($10 to $14 USD).

> **Field Note:** Lang Co experiences a sharp seasonal weather split driven by the Hai Van mountain barrier. From **April to August**, the coast bakes in 30°C to 35°C sunshine with glass-calm turquoise surf and 28°C water ideal for swimming and kayaking. Between **October and February**, the Northeast Monsoon (`Gió mùa Đông Bắc`) banks against the northern face of the Hai Van Pass, dropping air temperatures to 18°C to 23°C, bringing 1,800 mm of autumn rain, and whipping up rough 1.5-to-2.5-meter shorebreak swells where ocean swimming is restricted by red flags. During winter months, book either a heated pool villa at **Banyan Tree Lang Co** (which offers indoor thermal spa circuits) or a lagoon homestay on `Nguyen Van` street for misty mountain photography and hot seafood dinners.

## 4. Bach Ma National Park Gate & Canh Duong Village Stays (28 km Inland / 12 km North)

Travelers pairing Lang Co's coast with high-altitude rainforest trekking have two rustic alternatives:

1. **Bach Ma National Park Foot & Summit Villas (28 km northwest via `QL1A` to Cau Hai town):** Turning inland at Phu Loc, trekking groups sleep either at garden lodges near the park headquarters (`450,000–950,000 VND` / `$18–$38 USD`) or inside restored French colonial stone villas (**Do Quyen Villa**, **Phong Lan Villa**, `900,000–1,600,000 VND` / `$36–$64 USD`) near the 1,450-meter summit (`Vọng Hải Đài`), where temperatures stay 7°C to 10°C cooler than Lang Co beach and trails lead to the five-tiered **Five Lakes** (`Ngũ Hồ`) and 300-meter **Do Quyen Rhododendron Waterfall** (park entry `60,000 VND` / `$2.40 USD` per adult; chartered 16-seat summit van from the gate `900,000 VND` / `$36 USD` round-trip).
2. **Canh Duong Beach Village Homestays (`450,000–1,100,000 VND` / `$18–$44 USD`):** Located 3 km east of `QL1A` at Loc Vinh Commune just south of Laguna Lang Co, local beachfront guesthouses and pine-forest camping grounds cater to students and motorbike riders looking for bonfires, 15,000 VND morning bowls of crab-paste rice noodles (`Bánh canh cua rời`), and uncrowded morning swims.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I travel from Da Nang Airport (`DAD`) to Lang Co resorts?

From **Da Nang International Airport (`DAD`)**, southern Lang Co town (`Lap An Lagoon` and `Lac Long Quan` street) sits **30 km north (40 to 45 minutes)** via the 6.28 km **Hai Van Tunnel** (private 4-seat taxi `450,000–600,000 VND` / `$18–$24 USD` including the `110,000 VND` tunnel toll). If you are staying 14 km farther north at **Laguna Lang Co** (`Banyan Tree` or `Angsana`), the 44 km drive takes **55 to 60 minutes** (`650,000–850,000 VND` / `$26–$34 USD` by private car), or you can reserve seats on the complimentary scheduled **Laguna Shuttle Bus** operating daily between Da Nang Airport, Laguna Lang Co, and Hue City.

### Is it better to stay at Laguna Lang Co or near Lap An Lagoon?

Book **Laguna Lang Co** (`3,800,000–12,500,000 VND` / `$152–$500 USD`) if you want a quiet, self-contained five-star beach resort with private pools, a 300-meter lazy river, golf, and zero highway noise. Choose **Lap An Lagoon** (`Nguyen Van` street, `450,000–1,350,000 VND` / `$18–$54 USD`) or **Lang Co Spit** (`850,000–2,200,000 VND`) if you are riding the **Hai Van Pass** by motorbike, taking the heritage train between Hue and Da Nang, and want to eat `150,000 VND` plates of fresh oysters right on the water.

### Can I visit Hue and Da Nang as day trips while staying in Lang Co?

Yes. Lang Co sits directly between both cities: downtown [Da Nang](/destinations/da-nang/) is **30 km (40 minutes)** south through the Hai Van Tunnel, **Hoi An Ancient Town** is **60 km (1 hour 20 minutes)** south, and the **Hue Imperial Citadel** and royal tombs are **55 to 65 km (1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes)** north along `QL1A`.
