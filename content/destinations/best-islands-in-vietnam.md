# Best Islands in Vietnam: Phu Quoc, Con Dao, Cat Ba, and More

Vietnam's offshore islands divide into three distinct maritime zones: the limestone karst archipelagos of the Gulf of Tonkin in the north, the volcanic and granitic biosphere islands off the central coast, and the tropical coral islands of the South China Sea and the Gulf of Thailand in the south. Because monsoon wind patterns invert between these latitudes, choosing the right island requires matching your travel month, mainland departure hub, and appetite for resort infrastructure versus raw marine conservation.

This comparative island guide ranks Vietnam's seven best islands using verified 2026 air and high-speed catamaran schedules, port-to-pier ticket tariffs, national park conservation fees, and foreign-passport security rules.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Foreign Passport Security Restriction (Binh Ba & Binh Hung Islands):** Although Vietnamese domestic travel blogs frequently praise Binh Ba (`Đảo Bình Ba`) and Binh Hung (`Đảo Bình Hưng`) in Cam Ranh Bay for lobster farming and clear coves, both islands overlook the strategic headquarters of Naval Region 4 (`Vùng 4 Hải quân`). Foreign passport holders and Viet Kieu without a Vietnamese national ID card (`CCCD`) are strictly barred from boarding ferries at Ba Ngoi Port (`Cảng Ba Ngồi`) or Bai Kinh Pier. Choose **Phu Quy**, **Con Dao**, or **Hon Mun / Hon Tam** in Nha Trang instead.

## 2. Phu Quoc vs. Con Dao: The Southern Heavyweights

### Phu Quoc Island: High-Convenience Resort Comfort (`574 km²`)
Sitting in the warm waters of the Gulf of Thailand `45 km` west of Ha Tien, [Phu Quoc Island](/destinations/phu-quoc-travel-guide/) is Vietnam's largest island and only island jurisdiction with a dedicated **30-day visa-free exemption** for foreign nationals flying directly into Phu Quoc International Airport (`PQC`) from abroad (or transiting airside through `SGN` or `HAN` on a single international itinerary). Domestic flights from Ho Chi Minh City take `55 minutes` (`900,000–1,800,000 VND` one-way), while Superdong and Phu Quoc Express catamarans cross from Rach Gia in `2 hours 15 minutes` (`330,000–340,000 VND`) and from Ha Tien in `1 hour 15 minutes` (`230,000–250,000 VND`).

Along the sheltered western shoreline, Long Beach (`Bãi Trường`) and Ong Lang Beach (`Bãi Ông Lang`) enjoy flat, glassy water from November through April (`27°C–31°C`). On the southern tip, the `7,899.9-meter` Hon Thom 3-wire sea-crossing cable car (`650,000 VND` round-trip) links An Thoi port to the An Thoi Archipelago (`Quần đảo An Thới`), where speedboats visit fingernail-shaped coral keys such as Hon May Rut and Hon Gam Ghi (`850,000–1,100,000 VND` four-island day tour). Read our [Phu Quoc versus Con Dao Comparison](/compare/phu-quoc-vs-con-dao/) for a direct breakdown of accommodation tiers.

### The Con Dao Archipelago: Protected Marine Wilderness (`16 Islands`)
Roughly `185 km` south of Vung Tau in the open South China Sea, [Con Dao](/destinations/con-dao-travel-guide/) is the antithesis of Phu Quoc's commercial scale. Eighty percent of the `76 km²` archipelago falls inside Con Dao National Park (`60,000 VND` conservation fee for jungle trails; `150,000 VND` for offshore marine zones). Because Co Ong Airport (`VCS`) has a `1,830-meter` runway without night approach lighting, daily capacity is capped by daytime ATR-72 turboprop and regional jet slots from Ho Chi Minh City (`50 minutes`, `1,650,000–2,400,000 VND` one-way) and Hanoi. Budget travelers board the Superdong or Phu Quoc Express catamarans from Tran De Port in Soc Trang (`2 hours 30 minutes`, `310,000–390,000 VND` one-way).

Morning hours in Con Son Town revolve around the sobering Con Dao Prison Complex (`40,000 VND` combined ticket covering Phu Hai Camp, the French Tiger Cages, and the American Tiger Cages), followed by afternoon swimming at Dam Trau Beach (`Bãi Đầm Trầu`, `14 km` north of town) right beneath the aircraft approach path. Between June and September, female green sea turtles (*Chelonia mydas*) haul ashore at night on Bay Canh Island (`Hòn Bảy Cạnh`); ranger-guided overnight turtle-watching permits cost `600,000–1,200,000 VND` per person and must be reserved through the Con Dao National Park headquarters (`29 Võ Thị Sáu`).

## 3. Northern Gulf Islands: Cat Ba & Co To

### Cat Ba Island: The Adventure Hub of Lan Ha Bay (`260 km²`)
Forming the southwestern wall of the Ha Long–Lan Ha karst complex, [Cat Ba Island](/destinations/cat-ba-island-guide/) is the most accessible island in northern Vietnam. Integrated tourist coaches operated by Cat Ba Express and Daiichi Travel depart Hanoi's Old Quarter at `07:30`, `10:30`, and `14:00`, crossing the Tan Vu–Lach Huyen sea bridge to Got Pier (`Bến Gót`), boarding a `20-minute` ferry or speedboat to Cai Vieng Pier, and continuing along the coastal cliff road to Cat Ba Town in `3.5 hours` total (`300,000–380,000 VND` one-way). Alternatively, the Cat Hai–Phu Long cable car (`3,955 meters`, `100,000–150,000 VND`) bypasses ferry queues in `9 minutes`.

Unlike the heavily regulated cruise lanes of core Ha Long Bay, Cat Ba's Ben Beo Pier (`Bến Bèo`, `1.5 km` from town) launches full-day wooden boats into [Lan Ha Bay](/destinations/lan-ha-bay-guide/) for `650,000–950,000 VND` per person, including kayaking through Dark and Bright Cave (`Hang Sáng Tối`), swimming at Ba Trai Dao coves, and cycling into Viet Hai Village. Hike inland as well. Cat Ba National Park (`120,000 VND` entrance fee) protects the steep limestone trail up Ngu Lam Peak (`Đỉnh Ngự Lâm`, `2.5 hours` round-trip) and the last wild population of the critically endangered Cat Ba langur (*Trachypithecus poliocephalus*).

### Co To Island: The Northeast Gulf Frontier
Located `80 km` east of Ha Long in Quang Ninh Province, Co To Island (`Đảo Cô Tô`) draws far fewer international tourists than Cat Ba. High-speed catamarans depart Ao Tien International Port (`Cảng Ao Tiên` in Van Don, `2.5 hours` by expressway limousine from Hanoi for `260,000–320,000 VND`) and reach Co To Pier in `75 minutes` (`250,000–300,000 VND` one-way). Foreign visitors no longer require a pre-arranged provincial police permit for main Co To Island, though you must present your original passport and valid Vietnam e-Visa at Ao Tien Port check-in and hotel registration. Highlights on the island include the `3-kilometer` white sandbar of Hong Van Beach (`Bãi Hồng Vàn`), Van Chay Beach, and the wave-eroded sedimentary rock folds of Cau My / Mong Rong Cliff (`Bãi đá Móng Rồng`) at sunrise.

> [!TIP]
> **Checking Northern Winter Sea Forecasts:** Between December and February, Northeast Monsoon cold fronts (`gió mùa Đông Bắc`) bring `14°C–18°C` air temperatures and rough swells to the Gulf of Tonkin. Keep Cat Ba or Co To visits focused on kayaking and trekking during winter, or visit in **April–June** and **September–November** for comfortable swimming.

## 4. Central Coast Volcanic & Biosphere Islands: Cham Islands, Ly Son & Phu Quy

### Cham Islands (`Cù Lao Chàm`, Hoi An)
Sitting `15 km` off Cua Dai Beach (`Bến Cửa Đại` in [Hoi An](/destinations/hoi-an-travel-guide/)), the eight granitic islands of the Cham Archipelago form a UNESCO World Biosphere Reserve famous for its zero-single-use-plastic policy. High-speed canoes make the crossing from Cua Dai Pier (`6 km` east of Hoi An Ancient Town) to Bai Lang Pier (`Bến Bãi Làng`) on Hon Lao in `15 to 20 minutes` (`400,000–450,000 VND` round-trip boat ticket, plus the `70,000 VND` island admission ticket and `20,000 VND` ecological fee). During the calm summer window (**March through August**), underwater visibility along Bai Xep and Hon Dai reefs reaches `10 to 18 meters`, making Cham Islands the easiest half-day or overnight snorkeling escape in central Vietnam. Between October and January, northeast monsoon swells frequently suspend Cua Dai speedboat departures.

### Ly Son Island (`Đảo Lý Sơn`, Quang Ngai)
Formed by five extinct volcanoes `30 km` off the Quang Ngai coast, Ly Son offers dramatic black-basalt geology unlike any other Vietnamese island. Travelers fly into Chu Lai Airport (`VCL`) or take the Reunification Express train to Quang Ngai Station, ride a `45-minute` taxi or shuttle to Sa Ky Port (`Cảng Sa Kỳ`), and board the `35-minute` Superdong high-speed ferry (`170,000–215,000 VND` one-way). On Big Island (`Đảo Lớn`), electric buggies and rented scooters (`150,000 VND` per day) climb Thoi Loi Peak (`Đỉnh Thới Lới`, `169 meters`) to view a freshwater reservoir inside a volcanic crater above terraced white-sand garlic fields, then descend to Hang Pagoda (`Chùa Hang`, `20,000 VND`) carved into a coastal sea cliff and the natural basalt arch at To Vo Gate (`Cổng Tò Vò`). A `10-minute` morning speedboat (`80,000–100,000 VND` round-trip) crosses to Be Island (`Đảo Bé`), where turquoise coves sit against black volcanic boulders.

### Phu Quy Island (`Đảo Phú Quý`, Binh Thuan) & Nha Trang Bay Islands (`Hòn Mun` / `Hòn Tằm`)
Lying `56 nautical miles` (`110 km`) southeast of Phan Thiet, Phu Quy (`18 km²`) has emerged as Vietnam's favorite independent scooter-loop island. High-speed catamarans (`Superdong Phú Quý` and `Phú Quý Express`, `350,000 VND` one-way seated, `400,000 VND` sleeper berth) complete the crossing from Phan Thiet Port (`Cảng Phan Thiết`, reachable in `2.5 hours` from Ho Chi Minh City via the Dau Giay–Phan Thiet Expressway) in `2 hours 30 minutes`. Phu Quy features the cliffside infinity lagoon of Khe Sung Suong at Ganh Hang (`Gành Hàng`), Cao Cat Mountain (`Núi Cao Cát`) overlooking Linh Son Pagoda, and shallow coral flats off Hon Tranh Islet (`250,000 VND` round-trip canoe and snorkeling tour).

Travelers who want calm island reefs without long offshore ferry crossings can instead base in [Nha Trang](/destinations/best-things-to-do-in-nha-trang/) and ride a `15-to-25-minute` speedboat from Vinh Truong Pier (`Cảng Du lịch Nha Trang`) out to **Hon Mun Marine Protected Area** (`Hòn Mun`, `1,500,000–1,900,000 VND` for a 2-tank PADI boat dive) or **Hon Tam Island** (`Hòn Tằm`, `750,000–990,000 VND` round-trip speedboat package including terraced mineral mud baths, infinity pools, and a white-sand beach). Similarly, in the Gulf of Thailand halfway between Rach Gia and Nam Du, **Hon Son Island** (`Đảo Hòn Sơn`, `1 hour 30 minutes` by Superdong or Phu Quoc Express catamaran from Rach Gia Port for `170,000–190,000 VND`) offers a `16-kilometer` coastal ring road lined with leaning coconut palms at Bang Beach (`Bãi Bàng`) and the `450-meter` Ma Thien Lanh summit trail, while remaining far easier for foreign travelers to access than restricted border outposts.

## 5. Step-by-Step Island Selection & Booking Protocol (HowTo)

1. **Match Your Island to the Active Monsoon Window:** Book **Phu Quoc** between November and April; book **Con Dao**, **Cham Islands**, **Ly Son**, and **Phu Quy** between March and August when the South China Sea is flat; and book **Cat Ba** or **Co To** in October–November or April–May.
2. **Verify Passport Eligibility and Ferry Manifest Rules:** Confirm your island allows foreign nationals (skip Binh Ba and Binh Hung). Carry your physical passport (`Hộ chiếu`) and printed e-Visa to mainland ferry terminals (`Rạch Giá`, `Hà Tiên`, `Trần Đề`, `Sa Kỳ`, `Phan Thiết`, `Cửa Đại`), as port authorities check passport numbers against the electronic passenger manifest before boarding.
3. **Reserve Morning Ferry Crossings in Advance:** Offshore swells increase after `13:00` as afternoon thermal winds build. Always book the `07:00–09:00` morning high-speed catamaran departure `3 to 7 days` ahead on weekday sailings and `14 days` ahead around public holidays (`30 April–1 May` and `2 September`).
4. **Leave a 24-Hour Buffer Before International Flights:** Never schedule a high-speed ferry from Con Dao, Ly Son, or Phu Quy on the same day as your long-haul flight home from `SGN` or `HAN`. Tropical depressions can halt maritime port clearance for `24 to 48 hours`.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### Which island in Vietnam is best for first-time visitors?
Winter travelers (**November to April**) seeking easy flights, warm swimming water, and international resorts should start with **Phu Quoc Island**. Travelers already visiting Hanoi who want dramatic limestone scenery and kayaking without flying will prefer **Cat Ba Island** (`Lan Ha Bay`) as their top northern island. Between **March and August**, **Con Dao** stands out for quiet marine reserves, sea turtle conservation, and historical depth.

### Do I need a visa to visit Phu Quoc or other Vietnamese islands?
Foreign tourists of all nationalities qualify for a **30-day visa exemption on Phu Quoc Island** only if they fly directly into Phu Quoc International Airport (`PQC`) from a country outside Vietnam (or transfer airside at `SGN`/`HAN` without entering domestic Vietnam). If you visit Con Dao, Cat Ba, Ly Son, Cham Islands, or travel from Phu Quoc to the Vietnamese mainland, you must hold a standard [Vietnam 90-day e-Visa](/plan/vietnam-evisa/) (`$25 USD` single-entry) or qualify under a bilateral national visa waiver.

### Can foreign tourists visit Nam Du Island (`Quần đảo Nam Du`)?
Nam Du Archipelago (`65 km` offshore from Rach Gia in Kien Giang Province, `2 hours 15 minutes` by Superdong ferry for `210,000–250,000 VND`) sits in a sensitive maritime border zone. While enforcement fluctuates, border police at Rach Gia Port frequently deny boarding to foreign passport holders who do not hold a pre-approved Kien Giang Provincial Immigration Office permit arranged through a licensed Vietnamese tour agency. Unless you have a confirmed border permit in hand, choose **Phu Quoc** or **Hon Son** after verifying with port dispatch.
