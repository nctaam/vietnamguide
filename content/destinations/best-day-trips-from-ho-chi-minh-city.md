# 8 Best Day Trips from Ho Chi Minh City (2026): Cu Chi Tunnels, Mekong Delta, Can Gio, Ba Den & Vung Tau

After two days of motorbikes, French-colonial boulevards, war history museums, and rooftop bars in **Ho Chi Minh City (`Sài Gòn`)**, stepping outside the metropolis reveals a completely different Southern Vietnam. Within `1.5 to 2.5 hours` of District 1, you can crawl through the hand-dug wartime underground labyrinth of the **Cu Chi Tunnels (`Địa Đạo Củ Chi`)**, glide in a wooden sampan beneath water-coconut palms in the **Mekong Delta (`Bến Tre` & `Mỹ Tho`)**, take a speedboat through the UNESCO mangrove biosphere of **Can Gio (`Rừng Ngập Mặn Cần Giờ`)**, ride a futuristic cable car up Southern Vietnam's highest sacred peak (**`Núi Bà Đen` — Black Virgin Mountain, `986m`**), or zip across the East Sea by hydrofoil to **Vung Tau (`Vũng Tàu`)** or **Mui Ne (`Mũi Né` via the new `CT01` Expressway)**.

To avoid tourist traps (`such as spending 3 hours at commission handicrafts factories on $12 USD budget bus tours`), choosing the right sector and transport mode is critical. Here is the complete 2026 empirical guide comparing all 8 day trips from Ho Chi Minh City, with verified ticket prices, speedboat schedules, and DIY vs small-group logistics.

## Deep Dive: The 6 Best Day Trips from Saigon (And How to Avoid Tourist Traps)

### 1. Cu Chi Tunnels (`Địa Đạo Củ Chi` — Why You Should Choose `Bến Dược` Over `Bến Đình`!)
Dug by hand out of rock-hard ironstone laterite clay between `1946 and 1968`, the **Cu Chi Tunnels** stretched for over **`250 kilometers`** on three subterranean levels (`3m`, `6m`, and `10m` deep), housing underground field hospitals, kitchens with smoke-dispersing chimneys (`Bếp Hoàng Cầm`), command bunkers, and booby-trapped entrances right beneath the US 25th Infantry Division's base camp.
- **Critical Decision — `Bến Đình` (`125,000 VND`) vs `Bến Dược` (`35,000–70,000 VND`)**:
  - **90% of standard tourist buses go to `Bến Đình` (`55 km` from D1)**: Its tunnels have been widened and concrete-lined for larger Western tourists, and it has a noisy AK-47 / M16 live-fire shooting range (`bullets cost 60,000–75,000 VND each, minimum 10 rounds = 600,000–750,000 VND`), but you often wait in long queues behind 15 tour groups.
  - **Smart Independent Choice — `Bến Dược` (`70 km` from D1, 15 minutes further up the road)**: Preserves the **original Viet Cong Saigon–Gia Dinh Military Command Headquarters** with far fewer foreign tour groups, tighter authentic tunnel sections, the monumental **Ben Duoc Memorial Temple**, and a cheaper entrance ticket (**`35,000–70,000 VND`**)!
- **Best Way to Get There**:
  1. **By Luxury Speedboat (`Les Rives` or `Saigon River Tour`, `$78–$95 USD` departing `Bến Bạch Đằng` in District 1 at `07:00 or 08:00 AM`)**: A `75-minute` breeze up the Saigon River with fruit and pastries on board, arriving at Cu Chi **an hour before the highway buses**!
  2. **By Public Bus for `30,000 VND` (`$1.20 USD`)**: Catch **Bus `#13`** from **23/9 Park (`Ben Thanh`)** to **Cu Chi Bus Station** (`10,000 VND`), then transfer right across the lot to **Bus `#79` (`Cu Chi – Đền Bến Dược`, `8,000 VND`)** straight to the Ben Duoc gate!

### 2. The Mekong Delta: Skip Commercial `Mỹ Tho` Touts and Head Deeper into `Bến Tre` or `Cái Bè`
The single biggest day-trip mistake in Ho Chi Minh City is booking a `$12–$15 USD` big-bus "My Tho 4-Island Tour" on Pham Ngu Lao Street: those tours spend only 10 minutes in a crowded rowboat channel at **Thoi Son (`Unicorn Island`)** while shuttling you through four high-pressure souvenir, honey-tea, and snake-wine sales pitches.
- **How to Experience the Real Mekong Delta on a Day Trip**:
  - **Choose a `Bến Tre` Rural Waterways Tour (`$32–$55 USD` in a 9-to-12-seat limousine, or DIY by FUTA bus to Ben Tre City)**: Crossing the Tien River into **Ben Tre Province (`Xứ Dừa` — the "Coconut Kingdom")** takes you into a maze of quiet, unpaved island lanes (`Châu Thành`, `Mỏ Cày`, `Nhơn Thạnh`). You ride a motorized `xe lôi` (`tuk-tuk`) or bicycle under pomelo and jackfruit trees, visit a real family brick kiln and coconut-husking workshop (`where nothing is forced on you`), row a sampan down silent nipa-palm creeks, and eat crisp **fried Elephant-Ear Fish (`Cá Tai Tượng Chiên Xù`)** rolled in rice paper at a riverside garden!
  - **Or Go 30 Minutes Further to `Cái Bè` & `Vĩnh Long` (`An Bình` Island)**: Thanks to the completed `CT01` Trung Luong – My Thuan Expressway, `Cái Bè` (`115 km`) is now only **`2.0 hours`** from District 1.

> [!TIP]
> **Can You Combine `Cu Chi Tunnels` + `Mekong Delta` in One Day?** Yes! If you only have **one free day in Southern Vietnam**, booking a **VIP Limousine Combo Tour (`Cu Chi Tunnels in the morning 07:30–11:30 AM` $\rightarrow$ `Bến Tre / Mỹ Tho Mekong Delta in the afternoon 13:30–17:00 PM`, `$48–$68 USD`)** or hiring a private car (`2,000,000 VND`) is a tried-and-tested 11-hour circuit (`returning to District 1 by 19:00 PM`). Just make sure your operator uses a fast 9-to-16-seat van rather than a slow 45-seat coach!

### 3. Black Virgin Mountain (`Núi Bà Đen`, `986m`) & The `12:00 PM` Cao Dai Great Temple Ceremony (`Tây Ninh`)
Located `95 kilometers` northwest of Saigon in Tay Ninh Province (`just 35 km beyond Cu Chi Tunnels`), this two-stop cultural and mountain day trip has become a runaway favorite in 2026:
1. **Morning (`11:30 AM – 12:45 PM`) — The Midday Prayer Ceremony at the `Tòa Thánh Tây Ninh` (`Cao Dai Holy See`, free entry)**: Founded in `1926`, **Caodaism (`Đạo Cao Đài`)** blends Buddhism, Taoism, Confucianism, and Christianity, venerating saints ranging from Victor Hugo to Sun Yat-sen under the watch of the **Divine All-Seeing Eye (`Thiên Nhãn`)**. Arrive at the technicolor dragon-pillar cathedral by **`11:40 AM`** (`remove shoes and wear long pants/covered shoulders`) to watch from the upper gallery as white-, yellow-, blue-, and red-robed priests chant to traditional string music at **`12:00 PM` noon sharp**!
2. **Afternoon (`13:30–17:00 PM`) — Ride the Cable Car Up `Núi Bà Đen` (`986m` — "The Roof of Southern Vietnam")**:Rising nearly a kilometer straight out of the flat rice plains, **Sun World Ba Den Mountain (`10,000 VND` gate fee)** operates three modern Doppelmayr cable-car lines:
   - **Van Son Peak Line (`Tuyến Đỉnh Vân Sơn`, `400,000–450,000 VND` round-trip)**: Glides `8 minutes` through the clouds to the cool `986-meter` summit plateau, crowned by Asia's tallest mountaintop bronze Buddha (**`Tượng Phật Bà Tây Bổ Đà Sơn`, `72 meters` tall**) and the giant sandstone statue of **Maitreya Bodhisattva (`Tượng Phật Di Lặc`, carved from `6,688` sandstone blocks)** above cascading water terraces!
   - **Chua Hang Pagoda Line (`Tuyến Chùa Bà`, `250,000 VND` RT, or `650,000 VND` All-Access Combo for both lines)**: Visits the historic 300-year-old cave temple of **Linh Son Tien Thach Tu (`Chùa Bà`)** halfway up the cliff.

### 4. Can Gio UNESCO Mangrove Biosphere (`Đảo Khỉ` & `Chiến Khu Rừng Sác` — `50 km` Southeast)
Recognized by UNESCO as Vietnam's first **Mangrove Biosphere Reserve (`75,740 hectares` of coastal *Rhizophora* mangrove forest — the "Green Lungs of Saigon")**, **Can Gio (`Cần Giờ`)** sits just `50 kilometers` southeast of District 1 across the Soai Rap River (take the `5,000 VND` **Binh Khanh Ferry** at Nha Be, then drive `35 minutes` down the tree-shaded Rung Sac Boulevard):
- **Explore `Lâm Viên Cần Giờ` (`Monkey Island` & `Rừng Sác` Guerilla Base, ticket `70,000 VND`)**: Walk the raised boardwalks through giant prop-rooted mangroves inhabited by over **2,000 wild long-tailed macaques** (hold firmly onto your eyeglasses, hats, phones, and water bottles—the monkeys are bold pickpockets!), feed giant saltwater crocodiles at the conservation lagoon, and take a **10-minute forest speedboat (`600,000 VND` per boat for up to 6 people, or walk `2 km`)** deep into the flooded mangrove creeks to see the reconstructed **Rung Sac Special Forces Amphibious Base (`Đoàn 10 Rừng Sác`)**.
- **Lunch at `Chợ Hải Sản Hàng Dương` (Hang Duong Seafood Market, right beside `Bãi Biển 30/4`)**: Buy live mud crabs (`cua gạch Cần Giờ`), mantis shrimp (`bề bề cháy tỏi`), and grilled oysters from the market stalls and have them steamed or tamarind-fried on the spot for `20,000–30,000 VND` cooking fee per kg!

### 5. Vung Tau Coastal Peninsula (`100 km` Southeast by Hydrofoil or VIP Limousine)
For a salty sea breeze and fresh seafood:
- **Getting There**: Board the **GreenlinesDP High-Speed Hydrofoil (`320,000–350,000 VND` one-way, `2.0 hours`)** or take a **Hoa Mai / Huy Hoang VIP 9-Seat Limousine (`180,000–220,000 VND`, `2.0 hours` via `CT01` Expressway)** straight to **Front Beach (`Bãi Trước`)** and **Back Beach (`Bãi Sau`)**.
- **Top Sights**: Climb the **`811` stone steps** up Small Mountain (`Núi Nhỏ`) and spiral up the `133` interior stairs inside the **`32-meter` Statue of Christ the King (`Tượng Chúa Kitô Vua`, free entry — wear knee-length shorts/skirt; closed Mondays)** to stand on the statue's shoulders overlooking the East Sea; visit the `1907` **Vung Tau Lighthouse (`Hải Đăng Vũng Tàu`)** and French colonial **White Palace (`Bạch Dinh`, `15,000 VND`)**; and eat crispy mini shrimp pancakes at **`Bánh Khọt Gốc Vú Sữa`** (`14 Nguyễn Trường Tộ`, `65,000 VND/plate`)!

## Step-by-Step HowTo: How to Plan Your Saigon Day Trips Over 2 or 3 Days

### 1. First Step — Half-Day 1: `07:30 AM` Early Morning Trip to `Địa Đạo Củ Chi` (`Bến Dược`)
Depart District 1 at **`07:30 AM`** by river speedboat or VIP limousine (or Bus #13 + #79) to explore the **Ben Duoc Tunnels** before the midday heat and tour crowds arrive. You will be back in District 1 by **`13:30 PM`**, leaving your entire afternoon free for the **War Remnants Museum (`40,000 VND`)** and **Independence Palace (`40,000–65,000 VND`)**!

### 2. Second Step — Full-Day 2: Waterway Excursion into `Bến Tre` (or `Cái Bè`) in the Mekong Delta
Dedicate your second day (`08:00 AM – 17:30 PM`) to a small-group or private trip across the Tien River into **Ben Tre's coconut canals** (or if you only have 1 day total, book the combined **Cu Chi + Ben Tre Mekong 1-Day VIP Limousine Tour**).

### 3. Final Step — Optional Day 3: Choose `Núi Bà Đen` (`986m`) + `Cao Đài` Temple OR `Cần Giờ` Mangroves
If you have a third day in the south, take the cultural-and-mountain circuit to **Tay Ninh (`12:00 PM` Cao Dai ceremony + `Núi Bà Đen` cable car)** or head to the coast at **Can Gio Mangrove Biosphere** / **Vung Tau**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### If I only have time for ONE day trip from Ho Chi Minh City, should I choose Cu Chi Tunnels or the Mekong Delta?
- **Choose the `Cu Chi Tunnels` (`Half-Day`, `5 to 6 hours` round-trip)** if you are fascinated by **Vietnam War history**, want to see underground engineering firsthand, and prefer to be back in Saigon by `13:30 PM` for city sightseeing and dining.
- **Choose the `Mekong Delta` (`Bến Tre` or `Cái Bè`, `Full-Day`, `8 to 9 hours`)** if you want **tropical river scenery, boat rides under coconut palms, rural cycling, and fruit orchards**.
- **Best Compromise**: Book the **Full-Day Cu Chi Tunnels + Mekong Delta Combo (`07:30 AM – 19:00 PM`, `$48–$68 USD` in a 9-to-16-seat VIP limousine)** so you don't have to skip either!

### Does the Vung Tau hydrofoil still run directly from Bach Dang Pier in District 1 in 2026?
Because of Saigon River bridge and port renovation schedules in Ho Chi Minh City, **GreenlinesDP high-speed ferries** periodically adjust whether they embark directly at **Bach Dang Pier (`10B Tôn Đức Thắng`, District 1)** or connect via shuttle/metro to the terminal in **Thu Duc / Nha Be**, while the new larger **Thang Long / Phu Quoc Express ferry route** operates seasonally. Always check the live daily sailing schedule on `greenlines-dp.com` 24 hours before departure—or simply take a **VIP 9-seat DCar Limousine (`Hoa Mai`, `Toàn Thắng`, or `Huy Hoàng`, departing every 20 minutes from District 1 for `180,000–220,000 VND`, taking just `2.0 hours` door-to-door)**.

### Is Mui Ne doable as a day trip from Ho Chi Minh City now that the new expressway is open?
**Yes!** Before the **`CT01` Dau Giay – Phan Thiet Expressway** opened in 2023, driving from Saigon to Mui Ne along `QL1A` took a grueling `4.5 to 5.5 hours` each way. Today, the expressway cuts the drive down to **`2.5 to 3.0 hours` each way (`200 km`)**. You can depart District 1 at `06:30 AM`, arrive in Mui Ne by `09:30 AM`, wade the **Fairy Stream (`Suối Tiên`)**, eat a beachfront seafood lunch, take a 4x4 Jeep over the **White Sand Dunes (`Bàu Trắng`)** and **Red Sand Dunes** at sunset, and be back in Ho Chi Minh City by `20:30 PM`!

### What is the best time of year for day trips from Ho Chi Minh City?
Southern Vietnam operates on a two-season tropical monsoon cycle (`27°C–34°C` year-round). The **Dry Season (December to April)** offers sunny mornings and zero afternoon rain, though March and April reach `35°C–37°C` at midday—making `07:30 AM` departures to Cu Chi Tunnels and Black Virgin Mountain essential. During the **Green / Rainy Season (May to November)**, mornings remain bright and clear while heavy tropical downpours typically arrive for `45 to 90 minutes` between `14:30 PM and 17:00 PM`, when you are already heading back toward Saigon in an air-conditioned limousine or speedboat.
