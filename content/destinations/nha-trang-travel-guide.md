# Nha Trang Travel Guide: Beach, Food, Cam Ranh and Islands

Khanh Hoa's provincial capital arcs around a deep, crescent-shaped bay along Vietnam's south-central coast, 400 km northeast of Ho Chi Minh City via the CT01 expressway. Shielded from open ocean swells by 19 offshore granite islands—led by the 32-square-kilometer barrier of Hon Tre Island—Nha Trang Bay receives 260 to 280 sunny days per year and records an annual rainfall of roughly 1,350 mm. A 6.2 km municipal sand beach runs directly alongside Tran Phu Boulevard, backed by dive shops, fresh shellfish stalls, Cham brick sanctuaries, and geothermal mud springs.

Planning a trip here requires understanding how sharply the coastline splits into distinct zones. Central Tran Phu Boulevard is a high-density corridor of 40-story hotel towers, neon seafood plazas, and nightclubs catering to domestic families, Korean golfers, and long-stay expats. Drive 25 km south over the Cu Hin coastal pass toward Cam Ranh International Airport (CXR), however, and the high-rises vanish completely along the 17 km powder-sand sweep of Bai Dai (Long Beach).

This 2026 guide details Cam Ranh airport transfers, the new 4.5-hour CT01 highway link to Saigon, Po Nagar and Hon Chong entrance fees, Hon Mun and Hon Tam speedboat tariffs, geothermal mud bath packages, and neighborhood food prices. For an even deeper breakdown of neighborhoods and sightseeing, pair this guide with [Where to Stay in Nha Trang](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-nha-trang/) and [Best Things to Do in Nha Trang](/destinations/best-things-to-do-in-nha-trang/).

### The Urban Beachfront (Tran Phu Boulevard)
Tran Phu Boulevard runs 6.2 km from the Cai River Bridge south to Cau Da Port, separated from the sand by a manicured promenade of coconut palms and public exercise parks.
- **Beach Access & Sunbeds**: The entire beachfront is public and patrolled by lifeguards from 05:30 to 18:00. Municipal cushioned loungers under thatched umbrellas rent for **70,000 to 100,000 VND ($2.80 to $4.00 USD)** per day, while daybeds at **Sailing Club Nha Trang** (72–74 Tran Phu) or **Louisiane Brewhouse** (Lot 29 Tran Phu, where house-brewed passionfruit wheat beer and pilsner glasses cost **65,000 to 95,000 VND / $2.60 to $3.80 USD**) cost **150,000 to 250,000 VND ($6.00 to $10.00 USD)**.
- **Ocean Drop-Off Note**: Central Nha Trang Beach shelves steeply within 8 meters of the waterline. Between October and December, northeast monsoon swells create a strong shore break and rip currents; swim strictly inside the yellow-and-red buoy lanes or shift north to Hon Chong Cove where offshore reefs flatten the waves.

### The Cam Ranh Peninsula (Bai Dai / Long Beach)
Located 8 to 15 minutes north of Cam Ranh Airport (CXR) along Nguyen Tat Thanh Boulevard, Bai Dai is a master-planned strip of 5-star beachfront compounds including The Anam Cam Ranh, Alma Resort, Radisson Blu, Movenpick, and Mia Resort. The sand here is finer and paler than downtown Nha Trang, with a gentle 50-meter wading shelf ideal for young children. No independent street-food stalls, pharmacies, or convenience shops operate immediately outside the resort gates on Nguyen Tat Thanh Highway; taking a GrabCar or resort shuttle 30 km north into downtown Nha Trang for dinner takes **35 to 40 minutes** and costs **280,000 to 350,000 VND ($11.20 to $14.00 USD)** each way.

### Northern Bay & Pham Van Dong Corridor (Vinh Hoa / Ba Lang)
Curving 3.5 to 6.5 km north of the Cai River Bridge past Hon Chong Promontory toward the Vega City and Libera coastal theatre complex, the Pham Van Dong corridor offers a calmer, lower-cost alternative to central Tran Phu. High-rise apartment hotels (such as Muong Thanh Luxury Vien Trieu and Scenia Bay) rent modern sea-view studios for **500,000 to 1,100,000 VND ($20.00 to $44.00 USD)** per night. Because a submerged coral shelf buffers Ba Lang Beach, wave height here stays 30% to 40% lower than on southern Tran Phu during autumn winds, and a 4-seat GrabCar back to the Dam Market or Night Market takes just **8 to 12 minutes** for **50,000 to 75,000 VND ($2.00 to $3.00 USD)**.

### Po Nagar Cham Towers (Thap Ba Ponagar)
Built between the 8th and 13th centuries by the Hindu Panduranga principality of the Champa Kingdom, Po Nagar stands on a granite knoll 50 meters above the Cai River estuary (a 10-minute, **45,000 to 65,000 VND** taxi ride north of central Tran Phu). Four kiln-fired red brick sanctuary towers survive without visible mortar joints. The 22.8-meter **North Tower (Thap Chinh)** dates to 817 CE and houses a 10th-century black granite statue of Yan Po Nagar (Bhagavati) seated on a carved stone yoni altar. Admission costs **30,000 VND ($1.20 USD)** per person. Remove footwear before stepping inside the brick sanctums and cover knees and shoulders; free woven temple robes are lent at the robing counter beside the main stairs.

### Hon Chong Promontory & Lithophone Hall
Located 1.3 km north of Po Nagar along Pham Van Dong Street, **Hon Chong** features giant weathered granite boulders balanced above clear tide pools looking straight across to Hon Do (Red Island). Entry costs **30,000 VND ($1.20 USD)** for adults and **15,000 VND ($0.60 USD)** for students. Your ticket includes a 15-minute live performance inside the traditional timber assembly hall, where musicians play the ancient Highland *đàn đá* (stone xylophone), *đàn bầu* monochord, and bamboo T'rưng every 30 minutes between 08:00 and 16:30.

### Long Son Pagoda (Chua Long Son)
Founded in 1886 and relocated to the base of Trai Thuy Hill after a 1900 cyclone, Long Son Pagoda is free to enter (**0 VND**). Climb the 193 stone steps to reach the 14-meter reclining stone Buddha at step 44 and the 24-meter white concrete seated Buddha (*Kim Thân Phật Tổ*, erected in 1964 atop a 7-meter lotus pedestal, total height 31 meters) crowning the ridge. Ignore unofficial vendors on the lower stairs who try to hand you postcards or incense sticks and then demand 50,000 to 100,000 VND.

## 4. Coastal Gastronomy: Signature Khanh Hoa Dishes & Fair Prices

Skip the laminated Russian/English tourist menus directly on Tran Phu Boulevard and walk 300 to 600 meters inland into the grid around Lan Ong, Yersin, Thap Ba, and Ngo Gia Tu streets.

1. **Nem Nuong Ninh Hoa (Charcoal-Grilled Pork Skewers)**: Minced pork shoulder pounded with garlic and fish sauce is grilled over wood embers and served with raw herb platters, green mango slices, pickled shallots, crispy fried *ram* rolls, and rice paper sheets to dip into a warm, savory shrimp-and-pork-liver peanut sauce. A full platter at **Nem Nuong Dang Van Quyen** (16A Lan Ong or 02 Phan Boi Chau) costs **60,000 to 80,000 VND ($2.40 to $3.20 USD)** per person.
2. **Bun Ca Sua (Sailfish Cake & Crunchy Jellyfish Noodle Soup)**: Simmered from local sailfish (*cá cờ*) and mackerel bones rather than pork fat, the crystal-clear broth is topped with steamed and fried fish cakes alongside translucent, crunchy white jellyfish pieces (*sứa*). Order a bowl at **Bun Ca Co Ba** (123 Yersin) or **Bun Ca Nguyen Loan** (123 Ngo Gia Tu) for **40,000 to 55,000 VND ($1.60 to $2.20 USD)**.
3. **Banh Can Hai San (Clay-Mold Seafood Rice Pancakes)**: Poured into unglazed terracotta molds over glowing charcoal, these thick rice-flour cakes are crowned with whole baby squid, tiger prawns, or quail eggs and drowned in a bowl of warm sweet-and-sour fish sauce with minced mango and *xíu mại* pork meatballs. A 10-cake tray at **Banh Can 51 To Hien Thanh** costs **50,000 to 90,000 VND ($2.00 to $3.60 USD)**.
4. **Thap Ba Street Shellfish Alley (`Phố Hải Sản Tháp Bà`)**: Located 400 meters north of Po Nagar Cham Towers, restaurants such as **Quan Oc Long Vu** (30 Thap Ba) display live buckets of sweet sea snails (*ốc hương*), blood cockles (*sò huyết*), sea urchins (*nhum biển* grilled with scallion oil for **35,000 VND** each), and mantis shrimp. Expect to pay **180,000 to 350,000 VND ($7.20 to $14.00 USD)** per diner including cold Saigon Special beers (**20,000 VND / $0.80 USD** per bottle).

## 5. Airport Transfers (CXR), CT01 Highway & Regional Connections

Cam Ranh International Airport (**CXR**) sits 35 km south of central Nha Trang. Once you clear baggage claim, connects to the city and neighboring hubs follow clear pricing tiers:

                                                  +---> [West via QL27C Pass: 135 km / 3.2 hrs] ---> [Da Lat] (200,000 - 250,000 VND)

- **Dat Moi Airport Bus Route 18**: Yellow shuttle buses wait outside both Terminal 1 (Domestic) and Terminal 2 (International) from 05:30 to 21:55, syncing with arriving flights every 30 minutes. The flat fare to any hotel stop along Tran Phu Boulevard (ending at Yersin Street) is **65,000 VND ($2.60 USD)** per person, taking **45 to 55 minutes**.
- **GrabCar, Xanh SM & Fixed Airport Taxis**: A 4-seat ride via the Grab or Xanh SM app from CXR to central Tran Phu costs **300,000 to 350,000 VND ($12.00 to $14.00 USD)** including the 10,000 VND airport toll booth fee, covering the 35 km scenic coastal road in **40 to 45 minutes**. Walking straight to the metered taxi rank without pre-booking costs **400,000 to 480,000 VND ($16.00 to $19.20 USD)**.
- **CT01 Expressway to Ho Chi Minh City (4.5 to 5.0 Hours)**: The fully connected CT01 expressway corridor (Nha Trang–Cam Lam–Vinh Hao–Phan Thiet–Dau Giay) has cut road transit to Ho Chi Minh City (400 km) down to **4.5 to 5.0 hours** by private car or **5.5 to 6.0 hours** by VIP 22-cabin sleeper bus (**320,000 to 550,000 VND / $12.80 to $22.00 USD**).

## 6. Seasonal Weather, Underwater Visibility & Rain Shadow Window

Because Khanh Hoa province sits at the northern edge of Vietnam's south-central rain-shadow belt, its dry season lasts 8 full months from January through August—making it an ideal summer beach alternative when Phu Quoc and Saigon face daily monsoon squalls.

| Season & Months | Daytime Temp (°C) | Monthly Rainfall (mm) | Hon Mun Diving Visibility | Sea State & Activity Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Early Dry (Jan – Mar)** | 24°C – 29°C | 25 – 45 mm | 10 – 15 meters | Mild breeze, comfortable 25°C water, great for sightseeing & mud baths |
| **Prime Marine Window (Apr – Aug)**| 29°C – 35°C | 40 – 90 mm | 18 – 28 meters | Glassy flat bay, 28°C water, peak scuba diving & island speedboats |
| **Shoulder Transition (Sep)** | 27°C – 32°C | 140 – 190 mm | 10 – 15 meters | Sunny mornings with short 40-minute late-afternoon showers |
| **NE Monsoon Peak (Oct – Nov)** | 24°C – 28°C | 320 – 410 mm | 3 – 6 meters | Heavy surf on Tran Phu beach; pivot to I-Resort mud baths & Po Nagar |
| **Late Winter (Dec)** | 23°C – 27°C | 150 – 210 mm | 6 – 10 meters | Rains taper after mid-December; onshore wind builds small beach surf |

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

### How many days should I allocate to Nha Trang?
Allocate **2 to 3 nights (3 full days)**. Spend Day 1 relaxing on Tran Phu Beach or at a Bai Dai resort in Cam Ranh followed by dinner along Thap Ba Seafood Street, dedicate Day 2 to a morning two-tank dive or speedboat trip to Hon Mun and Hon Tam islands, and spend Day 3 visiting Po Nagar Cham Towers, Long Son Pagoda, and soaking for 2 hours at I-Resort Geothermal Mud Baths before catching an afternoon limousine van to Da Lat or sleeper train to Da Nang or Saigon.

### Is it better to stay in downtown Nha Trang or Cam Ranh (Bai Dai)?
Base yourself in **central Nha Trang (Tran Phu Boulevard)** if you want to walk out of your hotel lobby to dozens of local *nem nướng* and seafood eateries, craft breweries, and island boat piers without paying for long taxi rides. Choose **Cam Ranh (Bai Dai)** if you want a quiet, self-contained 5-star beachfront resort 10 minutes from CXR airport with powdery sand and calm wading water for children.

### Are Hon Mun coral reefs open for scuba diving in 2026?
Yes, regulated outer reef sites around Hon Mun, Madonna Rock, Moray Beach, and neighboring Hon Rom remain open to licensed PADI and SSI dive operators under the Khanh Hoa Bay Management Board's conservation zoning. To protect shallow regenerating staghorn coral beds, unguided mass-tour helmet walking is restricted to designated sandy platforms off Hon Tam and Mini Beach.
