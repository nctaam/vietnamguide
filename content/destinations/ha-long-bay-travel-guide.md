# Ha Long Bay Travel Guide: Cruise, Timing, and Route Fit (2026)

Rising from the shallow continental shelf of the Gulf of Tonkin in northeastern Vietnam, **Ha Long Bay** spans `1,553 square kilometers` and contains `1,969` monolithic limestone karst islets, sea arches, and phreatic caves formed over `500 million years`. First inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1994 for natural aesthetics and expanded in 2000 for karst geomorphology, the boundary was formally enlarged in September 2023 to incorporate the neighboring [Cat Ba Archipelago](/destinations/cat-ba-travel-guide/) (`Lan Ha Bay`) in Hai Phong Province.

Attracting over `7.5 million` annual visitors, Ha Long Bay is also one of Southeast Asia's most heavily regulated maritime corridors. Over `500` licensed day boats and overnight steel cruise ships operate along `5` numbered government sightseeing routes, governed by strict port check-in windows, mandatory Quang Ninh Provincial People's Council environmental tariffs, and real-time weather sailing clearances.

For travelers planning a voyage in 2026, matching your schedule to the right bay sector, departure port (**Tuan Chau International Marina** vs. **Ha Long International Cruise Port** at Bai Chay, `12 km` apart), and cruise duration (`4-hour` Route 1, `6-hour` Route 2, or `2D1N` / `3D2N` overnight routes) prevents port terminal confusion and peak-hour cave bottlenecks.

Legitimate operators refund `100%` of unused cruise payments when an official port ban is issued prior to embarkation.

### Deep-Dive: Route 1 vs. Route 2 On-the-Water Mechanics

Route 1 (`3.5 to 4.0 hours`, `18 km` circuit) concentrates on the western inner karsts situated `4 km` off Tuan Chau Island. Passengers climb `90` stone steps to **Thien Cung Cave** (*Động Thiên Cung*, `130 meters` long at `25 meters` elevation) and visit adjacent **Dau Go Cave** (*Hang Đầu Gỗ*, `5,000 square meters`), where General Tran Hung Dao concealed ironwood stakes in `1288` to defeat the Yuan naval fleet. On the return leg, vessels navigate past **Hon Dinh Huong** (Incense Burner Islet, printed on the reverse of Vietnam's `200,000 VND` polymer banknote) and **Hon Trong Mai** (`12-meter` twin Fighting Cocks limestone stacks).

Route 2 (`5.5 to 6.0 hours` Day / `24 hours` Overnight, `35 km` circuit) sails `14 km` southeast to Bo Hon Island in the heart of the UNESCO core zone. Passengers ascend `100` stone stairs into **Sung Sot Cave** (*Hang Sửng Sốt*), a three-chamber cavern covering `10,000 square meters` with a `30-meter` vaulted ceiling illuminated by recessed path lighting. Next, ships anchor off **Ti Top Island**, where `400` stone steps climb `85 meters` to a `360-degree` observation pavilion overlooking a `300-meter` crescent swimming beach. The afternoon concludes at **Luon Cave** (*Hang Luồn*), a `100-meter` tidal rock tunnel (`2.5 to 4.0 meters` ceiling clearance depending on tide) entered by `2-person` kayak or `6-seat` bamboo sampan (`50,000–100,000 VND` / `$2.00–$3.95 USD`, `45 minutes`).

### Deep-Dive: Route 3 & Route 4 Floating Villages (Cua Van & Vung Vieng)

Travelers booking a `3D2N` itinerary or a specialized Bai Tu Long ship sail beyond the high-traffic Route 2 corridor into Routes 3 and 4. **Route 3** (`500,000 VND` / `$19.60 USD` overnight permit) centers on the **Cua Van Floating Cultural Center** (`18 km` south of Tuan Chau), where a sheltered lagoon ringed by vertical cliffs preserves traditional net-casting exhibits and rowed sampan excursions (`60 minutes`). Nearby **Tien Ong Cave** (`1,000 square meters` at `5 meters` elevation) contains freshwater snail-shell middens dating back `8,000 to 10,000 years` to the prehistoric Soi Nhu culture.

Further northeast, **Route 4** (`400,000 VND` / `$15.70 USD` overnight permit) enters the southern border of Bai Tu Long Bay. Ships anchor near **Thien Canh Son Cave** (*Động Thiên Cảnh Sơn*, `100` stone steps up Cong Do Island) and transfer passengers by tender to **Vung Vieng Floating Fishing Village** (`24 km` from Bai Chay). Here, local aquaculture families row visitors in bamboo boats (`45 minutes`, `50,000 VND` / `$2.00 USD` included in cruise fare) past floating grouper cages, pearl oyster lines, and a natural `15-meter` limestone water gate.

## 3. Departure Ports: Tuan Chau Marina vs. Ha Long International Port

Showing up at the wrong cruise port is the single most common logistical mistake in Quang Ninh. The two primary terminals sit `12 km` apart (`20 minutes` by taxi for `150,000–200,000 VND` / `$5.90–$7.85 USD`), and overnight vessels lock their gangways between `12:15` and `12:30`.

### 1. Tuan Chau International Marina (`Cảng Tàu Khách Quốc Tế Tuần Châu`)

Located on Tuan Chau Island `12 km` southwest of central Bai Chay (connected to National Highway `QL18` by a `2 km` causeway), Tuan Chau operates two artificial harbors (Terminal 1 and Terminal 2) spanning `8 km` of concrete quays with berths for `2,000` vessels. Over `65%` of standard Route 1 and Route 2 day boats, traditional wooden junks, and major overnight fleets depart here, including **Paradise Vietnam** (`Lot 16`), **Bhaya Cruises** (`Lot 15`), **Au Co**, and **Stellar of the Seas** (which boards speedboats at Lot `17` or Got Pier to enter Lan Ha Bay). Arrive by `08:15–08:45` for `6-hour` day cruises (`09:00` departure) or `11:30–12:00` for overnight cruises (`12:30` departure).

### 2. Sun World Ha Long International Cruise Port (`Cảng Tàu Khách Quốc Tế Hạ Long`)

Located at `9 Ha Long Road, Bai Chay Ward`, directly beside the Sun World theme park and `12 km` east of Tuan Chau, this terminal features a `3-story`, `13,500-square-meter` passenger hall designed by architect Bill Bensley. It serves modern `99-passenger` to `300-passenger` steel day catamarans (such as **Ambassador Day Cruise**, **Jade Sails**, **Sea Octopus**, and **Amethyst**), luxury overnight ships (such as **Ambassador Overnight**, **Catherine Cruise**, and **Grand Pioneers**), and **Route 4 Bai Tu Long Bay** vessels (such as **Indochina Junk**). Passengers departing from this private port pay a `60,000 VND` (`$2.35 USD`) terminal facility fee rather than the `40,000 VND` (`$1.60 USD`) Tuan Chau municipal port fee.

### 3. Alternative Terminals: Got Pier (Lan Ha Bay) & Ao Tien Port (Bai Tu Long Bay)

Travelers booked on Lan Ha Bay vessels depart from **Got Ferry Terminal / Gia Luan** (`25 km` southwest in Cat Hai, Hai Phong), located `120 km` (`1 hour 45 minutes`) from Hanoi via the `5.44 km` Tan Vu–Lach Huyen sea bridge. Vessels operating here (**Mon Cheri**, **Orchid**, **Peony**, **Heritage Binh Chuan**) take guests kayaking through **Dark & Bright Cave** (*Hang Sáng Tối*, `70,000 VND` / `$2.75 USD` fee bundled) and cycling `5 km` into **Viet Hai Village** on Cat Ba Island.

Meanwhile, **Ao Tien International Port** sits `55 km` northeast of Bai Chay in Van Don District (`50 minutes` drive along the `CT06` Ha Long–Van Don Expressway). Opened in `2023` with `5` modern finger piers, Ao Tien dispatches high-speed catamarans to Quan Lan and Co To islands (`200,000–300,000 VND` / `$7.85–$11.80 USD` one-way, `45–60 minutes`) alongside multi-day Bai Tu Long National Park expeditions.

### How to Audit Vessel Quality and Avoid Online "Phantom Ship" Scams

Because Quang Ninh mandated white hull paint across all Ha Long Bay vessels and retired older single-hull wooden boats, scam websites occasionally reuse identical photos of modern steel ships under fabricated brand names. Before paying a deposit, verify three empirical specifications: first, confirm the ship's **IMO or Quang Ninh registration number** (`QN-XXXX`) and exact year of steel hull launch (`2019–2026` ships feature double-bottom steel hulls and quiet generator insulation). Second, check cabin square footage (`28 to 45 square meters` on true 5-star ships versus `14 to 18 square meters` on converted 3-star boats). Third, request the exact lot number at Tuan Chau (`Lots 12–26`) or verify check-in desk assignment at Sun World International Port.

## 5. Overland & Aerial Transit: Hanoi to Ha Long Expressway (`CT04` / `CT06`)

The opening of the `4-lane` **Hanoi–Hai Phong Expressway (`CT04`)** and the **Ha Long–Hai Phong Expressway (`CT06`)** across the Bach Dang Bridge reduced the Hanoi-to-Ha Long road distance from `180 km` (`4.0 hours` on congested `QL18`) to `155 km` (`2.0 to 2.5 hours`).

### Step-by-Step Transfer Options & Verified 2026 Fares

Converted `9-seat` or `11-seat` Ford Transit and Hyundai Solati **DCar Luxury Limousine Vans** (`350,000–450,000 VND` / `$13.70–$17.65 USD` one-way) depart hourly between `06:00` and `18:00`. The ticket price includes door-to-door pickup across Hoan Kiem District (Hanoi Old Quarter), a `15-minute` highway rest stop at `Km 52` in Hai Duong, and drop-off at Tuan Chau Marina or Bai Chay hotels. Middle captain seats (`Seats 3–6`) cost `400,000–450,000 VND` (`$15.70–$17.65 USD`), while front passenger (`Seats 1–2`) and rear bench seats (`Seats 7–9`) cost `350,000–380,000 VND` (`$13.70–$14.90 USD`). Vetted operators include **Ha Long Travel Limousine**, **Quang Muoi**, and **Phuc Xuyen**.

For families or groups carrying hard-shell suitcases, a **Private Sedan or SUV Charter** (`1,600,000–2,400,000 VND` / `$62.75–$94.10 USD` one-way) eliminates shared pickup delays. A private 4-seater sedan costs `1,600,000–1,900,000 VND` (`$62.75–$74.50 USD`) one-way including `215,000 VND` (`$8.45 USD`) in `CT04/CT06` highway tolls, while a 7-seater SUV (Toyota Fortuner or Kia Carnival) costs `2,000,000–2,400,000 VND` (`$78.40–$94.10 USD`). Travelers landing at **Noi Bai International Airport (`HAN`)** (`175 km`, `2 hours 45 minutes` via the `QL18`–`CT04` bypass) can book a direct airport limousine seat (`450,000–500,000 VND` / `$17.65–$19.60 USD`) or private sedan (`1,850,000–2,100,000 VND` / `$72.55–$82.35 USD`). Alternatively, regional flights into **Cat Bi Airport (`HPH`, Hai Phong)** sit just `45 km` (`45 minutes` via `CT06`, taxi `550,000–650,000 VND` / `$21.60–$25.50 USD`) from Tuan Chau Marina.

For aerial photography, **Hai Au Aviation** operates `12-passenger` Cessna Grand Caravan 208B-EX amphibious seaplanes from Noi Bai Terminal 1 to Tuan Chau Marina Lot `7`. The `45-minute` flight combines a `30-minute` transit with a `15-minute` low-altitude orbit (`150 to 300 meters` above Bo Hon and Dau Be islands) for `4,500,000–9,800,000 VND` (`$176.00–$385.00 USD`) one-way. Travelers already in Ha Long can book a standalone `25-minute` scenic bay flight departing and landing at Tuan Chau for `2,400,000 VND` (`$94.10 USD`).

## 6. Mainland Attractions: Sun World Queen Cable Car & Quang Ninh Museum

If you spend a night ashore in Bai Chay or Hon Gai before or after your cruise, two architectural landmarks warrant a `3-hour` mainland excursion.

Stretching `2.2 km` (`2,222 meters`) from Bai Chay across the Cua Luc shipping channel to the summit of Ba Deo Hill, the **Sun World Ha Long Queen Cable Car** (`380,000 VND` / `$14.90 USD`) is a double-deck reversible aerial tramway holding two Guinness World Records: the highest cable car tower (`188.88 meters` on Tower `T1`) and largest cabin capacity (`230` passengers per cabin). Tickets cost `380,000 VND` (`$14.90 USD`) for adults (`>1.4m`), `280,000 VND` (`$11.00 USD`) for children (`1.0m–1.4m`), and `0 VND` under `1.0m`. The fare includes rides on the **Sun Wheel** (`215 meters` above sea level, `15-minute` rotation), the Japanese Zen Garden, and the Kojima Koi Bridge.

Situated `7.0 km` east of Bai Chay (`12 minutes` by taxi for `100,000 VND` / `$3.95 USD`) along the Tran Quoc Nghien coastal promenade in Hon Gai, the **Quang Ninh Museum** (`40,000 VND` / `$1.60 USD`) is a striking black-glass cube designed by Spanish architect Salvador Perez Arroyo to resemble a polished block of anthracite coal. Open daily from `08:00` to `12:00` and `13:00` to `17:00` (except the last Monday of each month), admission is `40,000 VND` (`$1.60 USD`) for adults (`20,000 VND` / `$0.80 USD` for students, `10,000 VND` / `$0.40 USD` for children). Inside, `3` floors display an `18-meter` fin whale skeleton, a `13th-century` Bach Dang battle exhibit, and a walk-through replica of an underground coal-mining shaft (`+30,000 VND` / `$1.20 USD` for the special Yen Tu Buddhist treasure gallery).

### Regional Quang Ninh Seafood Benchmarks (Hon Gai vs. Bai Chay)

Travelers staying overnight on land should cross the Bai Chay Bridge (`5.0 km`, `10 minutes` by taxi for `80,000 VND` / `$3.15 USD`) into **Hon Gai**, where local residents dine away from tourist markups. Ha Long's signature delicacy is **Chả mực giã tay** (hand-pounded cuttlefish cakes fried golden), which costs `450,000–550,000 VND` (`$17.65–$21.60 USD`) per kilogram at **Ha Long Market 1** (`Chợ Hạ Long 1` in Bach Dang Ward) or `55,000–75,000 VND` (`$2.15–$2.95 USD`) served over steamed rice rolls (*Bánh cuốn chả mực*) along Nguyen Van Cu Street. Morning bowls of **Bún bề bề** (mantis shrimp rice noodles) and **Sam biển** (horseshoe crab salad) run `50,000–90,000 VND` (`$2.00–$3.55 USD`) per portion.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions About Visiting Ha Long Bay

### Can I visit Ha Long Bay as a day trip from Hanoi without feeling rushed?
Yes. Because the `155 km` `CT04/CT06` expressway cuts Hanoi-to-marina travel time to `2.5 hours` each way, a `6-hour` Route 2 luxury day cruise (departing Hanoi at `07:00`, cruising from `10:00` to `16:00`, and returning to Hanoi by `19:00`) gives you `6 full hours` on the bay to visit Sung Sot Cave, climb Ti Top Island's `400` steps, and kayak `45 minutes` through Luon Cave.

### Are kayak or bamboo boat fees at Luon Cave included in the cruise price?
Mid-range and luxury day cruises (`1,350,000+ VND` / `$52.95+ USD`) and `90%` of overnight cruises bundle the `50,000–100,000 VND` (`$2.00–$3.95 USD`) Luon Cave or Dark & Bright Cave kayak/sampan fee into your booking voucher. Budget day tours (`750,000–950,000 VND` / `$29.40–$37.25 USD`) frequently leave it optional; carry `150,000 VND` (`$5.90 USD`) in small paper bills to pay the floating ticket raft directly and tip your bamboo sampan rower `30,000–50,000 VND` (`$1.20–$2.00 USD`).

### What is the corkage fee policy for bringing my own drinks onto an overnight cruise?
In accordance with Quang Ninh Province's **Single-Use Plastic Ban** (effective since September 2019), passengers are prohibited from carrying disposable plastic water bottles (`<1.5 liters`), plastic bags, or styrofoam onto any sightseeing vessel (fines reach `5,000,000 VND` / `$196 USD` for operators). Furthermore, overnight cruise lines charge corkage fees of `300,000–800,000 VND` (`$11.80–$31.40 USD`) per bottle of wine or spirits and `30,000–50,000 VND` (`$1.20–$2.00 USD`) per can of outside beer. Use the refillable glass water carafes provided in your cabin.

### Should I choose Ha Long Bay, Lan Ha Bay, or Bai Tu Long Bay?
Book **Ha Long Bay (Route 2)** if you are taking a `6-hour` day trip or want to enter the monumental `10,000-square-meter` Sung Sot Cave and climb the `400` stone stairs to Ti Top Island's viewpoint. Select **Lan Ha Bay** (departing via Got Pier / Cat Ba) for `2D1N` or `3D2N` overnight ships with newer `2020–2026` steel hulls, longer kayaking sessions (`90 minutes` at Dark & Bright Cave), and cycling `5 km` into Viet Hai Village. Prioritize **Bai Tu Long Bay (Route 4)** if you value quiet anchorages and visiting Vung Vieng Fishing Village (`24 km` northeast) with minimal boat traffic.
