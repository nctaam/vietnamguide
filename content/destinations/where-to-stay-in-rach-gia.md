# Where to Stay in Rach Gia (2026): Ferry Port vs City Stays

Rach Gia (`Thành phố Rạch Giá`), the provincial capital of Kien Giang on the southwestern coast of the Mekong Delta facing the Gulf of Thailand, operates primarily as Southern Vietnam's busiest passenger maritime springboard. The city sits 250 kilometers southwest of Ho Chi Minh City via Expressway CT01 and National Route 80 (QL80), 115 kilometers west of Can Tho via National Route 61 (QL61), and 92 kilometers southeast of Ha Tien along the coastal highway. Over 85% of independent travelers arrive here with one logistical objective: boarding morning high-speed catamarans to Phu Quoc (`Đảo Phú Quốc`, 120 km offshore, 2.5 hours), Nam Du (`Quần đảo Nam Du`, 65 km offshore, 2.0 hours), or Hon Son (`Đảo Hòn Sơn`, 45 km offshore, 1.5 hours).

Your hotel zone dictates whether a 07:00 morning ferry departure is a calm 4-minute walk or a stressful pre-dawn race against terminal manifest cutoffs. Three distinct districts host traveler accommodations in 2026: the immediate **Bến Tàu Ferry Port** perimeter along Nguyen Cong Tru, Tran Phu, and Tu Do streets (50 meters to 450 meters from the pier gates); **Central Rach Gia & Phu Cuong Reclaimed Seafront** around Nguyen Trung Truc avenue, Lac Hong park, and Ton Duc Thang boulevard (1.5 km to 3.5 km south); and the **An Hoa Urban Zone** near Rach Gia Airport (`VKG`, 5.5 km to 8.0 km southeast).

---

> [!TIP]
> **Ferry Buffer Golden Rule**: Morning catamarans operated by Superdong, Phu Quoc Express, and Lightning 68 enforce strict 45-minute manifest check-in deadlines and detach gangways 10 minutes before departure. If your boat departs between 06:30 and 08:00, book a hotel within 350 meters of the Nguyen Cong Tru pier gate (280,000 VND to 650,000 VND / $11.20 to $26.00 USD per night). This eliminates pre-dawn taxi dependency and gives you a 3-minute walk to the boarding turnstiles.

## Zone 1: Bến Tàu Ferry Port (Nguyen Cong Tru, Tu Do & Tran Phu)

The **Rach Gia Passenger Ferry Port (`Cảng hành khách Rạch Giá`)**, situated at the western terminus of Nguyen Cong Tru and Tu Do streets in Vinh Thanh ward at the mouth of the Cai Lon River, handles all high-speed passenger catamarans to Phu Quoc, Hon Son, and Nam Du. Official ticket counters and air-conditioned waiting lounges for **Superdong Speed Ferry**, **Phu Quoc Express**, and **Lightning 68-1** operate here daily from **05:30 to 16:30**.

### Operational Advantages
When your vessel departs between 06:30 and 07:30, Vietnam Maritime Administration (`Cục Hàng hải Việt Nam`) safety rules require passengers to clear ticket inspection 30 minutes to 45 minutes prior to cast-off. Sleeping inside the 400-meter port perimeter lets you wake up at 05:50, buy a hot roasted-pork baguette (`bánh mì heo quay`, 20,000 VND / $0.80 USD) and iced condensed-milk coffee (`cà phê sữa đá`, 18,000 VND / $0.72 USD) along Nguyen Cong Tru at 06:05, and walk 100 meters directly to your vessel boarding ramp by 06:15 without waiting for a taxi in pre-dawn humidity.

### District Characteristics
Streets immediately outside the port security arch (specifically Nguyen Cong Tru, Tu Do, Tran Phu, and Ham Nghi) are lined with 4-story to 6-story masonry mini-hotels (`khách sạn mini`) and family guesthouses (`nhà nghỉ`). Rooms focus on transit utility: firm spring mattresses, 12,000 BTU split-unit air conditioning calibrated for 32°C delta afternoons, private tiled bathrooms with hot water heaters, elevators in newer properties, and 50 Mbps Wi-Fi. Reception desks open at 05:00 to return guest IDs and arrange luggage storage (30,000 VND to 50,000 VND / $1.20 to $2.00 USD per suitcase per day).

### Recommended Properties Near the Ferry Gates
- **Kiet Hong Hotel** (`40 Nguyen Cong Tru`, 120 meters / 2-minute walk from ferry gates): Spotless ceramic-tile twin and double rooms priced at **320,000 VND to 480,000 VND ($12.80 to $19.20 USD)** per night. Ground-floor staff print ferry boarding passes, store excess bags for Nam Du weekenders, and accept late 23:30 bus check-ins.
- **Phuc Ngoc Hotel** (`18 Nguyen Cong Tru`, 80 meters / 1-minute walk from pier turnstiles): Compact budget double rooms at **280,000 VND to 420,000 VND ($11.20 to $16.80 USD)** and quad family rooms at **520,000 VND ($20.80 USD)**. Offers 24-hour front desk coverage and complimentary bottled water.
- **Dong Hai Hotel** (`26–28 Tu Do Street`, 150 meters from Superdong ticket booth): Newly refurbished mid-range rooms with elevator access and harbor views from upper floors for **380,000 VND to 550,000 VND ($15.20 to $22.00 USD)** per night.
- **Sea Stars Hotel Rach Gia** (`2E Ba Thang Hai`, 550 meters / 6-minute walk or 25,000 VND / $1.00 USD taxi from port): Upgraded three-star property charging **650,000 VND to 950,000 VND ($26.00 to $38.00 USD)** per night, featuring soundproof windows, elevator service, and a 06:00 hot breakfast buffet.

## Zone 2: Central Rach Gia, Waterfront & Phu Cuong Seafront

Separated from the northern passenger pier by the 180-meter span of the Ngo Quyen and Nguyen Trung Truc canal bridges over the Cai Lon estuary, **Central Rach Gia (`Vĩnh Thanh Vân` and `Vĩnh Lạc` wards)** unfolds along Nguyen Trung Truc boulevard, Quang Trung commercial street, Lac Hong coastal park, and the 400-hectare **Phu Cuong Reclaimed Seafront (`Khu Đô Thị Lấn Biển Phú Cường`)**.

### Operational Advantages & Sightseeing within 1.5 Kilometers
Choosing Central Rach Gia rewards travelers arriving before 15:00 who want cultural landmarks and evening dining rather than a bare transit room:
- **Nguyen Trung Truc Communal Temple (`Đền thờ Nguyễn Trung Trực`, 14 Le Loi Street, 900 meters south of the ferry port; open 06:30–18:00; 0 VND admission)**: Shaded by 100-year-old banyan trees overlooking the river mouth, this revered 19th-century shrine honors the fisherman-turned-partisan leader who burned the French warship *L'Espérance* on the Nhat Tao River in 1861. A bronze statue stands 400 meters away in the harbor plaza, and the temple's traditional herbal medicine clinic (`Phòng thuốc Nam từ thiện`) still dispenses free remedies to local residents.
- **Kien Giang Provincial Museum (`Bảo tàng Kiên Giang`, 27 Nguyen Van Troi Street, 1.6 km from the pier; open Tuesday–Sunday 07:30–11:00 and 13:30–17:00; admission 20,000 VND / $0.80 USD)**: Housed inside the 1920 mansion of landlord Tran Nhuan (`Nhà lớn Trần Nhuận`), the building combines French neoclassical brick arches with intricate carved ironwood (`gỗ căm xe`) interior courtyards and displays Oc Eo gold artifacts (dating back 1,500 to 2,000 years) alongside Khmer and Hoa maritime heritage.
- **Tam Bao Pagoda (`Chùa Tam Bảo`, 03 Su Thien Chieu, 1.9 km from the pier; open 07:00–17:30; 0 VND entry)** and **Phat Lon Pagoda (`Chùa Phật Lớn / Sắc Tứ Tam Bảo`, 151 Quang Trung, 2.2 km from the pier; 0 VND entry)**: Historic Khmer Theravada and Vietnamese Mahayana sanctuaries dating to the late 18th century with gilded Buddha statues and lotus ponds.
- **Lac Hong Coastal Park & Phu Cuong Sophia Sunset Promenade (`Công viên Lạc Hồng` & `Đường Tôn Đức Thắng`, 2.0 km to 3.8 km south of the pier)**: Every afternoon between 16:30 and 21:30, sea breezes cool the coastal walkway to 27°C while open-air seafood terraces and coffee lounges look west across the Gulf of Thailand.

### Transit Reality Between Central Hotels and the Ferry Pier
Central Rach Gia hotels sit **1.5 kilometers to 3.8 kilometers** south of the Nguyen Cong Tru passenger pier. A morning ride with **Mai Linh Taxi** (hotline `0297 38 38 38 38`), **Taxi Phu Quoc Kien Giang** (`0297 3 68 68 68`), **GrabCar**, or **Xanh SM Electric Taxi** takes **6 minutes to 11 minutes** and costs **35,000 VND to 75,000 VND ($1.40 to $3.00 USD)**. Always ask your hotel receptionist at 20:00 the night before to pre-book a 06:00 metered cab if you hold a 07:00 ferry ticket.

### Recommended Central & Seafront Properties
- **Sai Gon Rach Gia Hotel** (`844 Nguyen Trung Truc`, 3.2 km from the ferry pier; 8-minute taxi costing 55,000 VND / $2.20 USD): The premier four-star full-service hotel in Kien Giang Province. Spacious 36 m² deluxe rooms cost **950,000 VND to 1,600,000 VND ($38.00 to $64.00 USD)** per night, including a 40-dish hot buffet breakfast starting at 06:00, a 25-meter outdoor swimming pool, gym, and complimentary scheduled shuttle service to **Rach Gia Airport (`VKG`)**.
- **Hoa Binh - Rach Gia Resort** (`62–64 Co Bac, Vinh Bao Ward`, 1.4 km from the ferry pier; 5-minute taxi costing 35,000 VND / $1.40 USD): Quiet four-star garden compound set along the canal bank with an outdoor pool, fitness room, and 32 m² balcony rooms running **750,000 VND to 1,250,000 VND ($30.00 to $50.00 USD)** per night.
- **Phu Cuong Hotel / Sophia Center Stays** (`Ton Duc Thang & Ba Thang Hai, Phu Cuong Urban Zone`, 3.5 km from port): Modern coastal boutique rooms with sea-facing balconies priced at **700,000 VND to 1,350,000 VND ($28.00 to $54.00 USD)** per night.
- **Thanh Hai Hotel** (`32 Nguyen Trung Truc`, 1.6 km from port): Dependable mid-range downtown choice with clean 24 m² air-conditioned rooms at **380,000 VND to 520,000 VND ($15.20 to $20.80 USD)** per night.

## Zone 3: An Hoa Urban District, Rach Soi & Rach Gia Airport (VKG)

Occupying the southeastern corridor of the municipality 5.5 kilometers to 8.5 kilometers from the passenger pier, **An Hoa (`Phường An Hòa`)** and neighboring **Rach Soi (`Phường Rạch Sỏi`)** connect directly with National Route 80 (QL80 toward Can Tho and Long Xuyen) and National Route 63 (QL63 toward Ca Mau and U Minh Thuong National Park, 60 km south).

### Operational Profile & VKG Flight Connections
An Hoa caters to corporate visitors, highway road-trippers with private cars, and passengers flying through **Rach Gia Airport (`Sân bay Rạch Giá`, IATA code `VKG`)**, situated 7.5 kilometers southeast of the ferry port on Cach Mang Thang Tam street (QL80). **Vietnam Airlines (operated by VASCO)** flies 68-seat ATR-72 turboprop aircraft connecting Rach Gia (`VKG`) with **Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`, 50 minutes flight time, one-way fares 750,000 VND to 1,450,000 VND / $30.00 to $58.00 USD)** and seasonal 40-minute hops to **Phu Quoc (`PQC`, 650,000 VND to 1,150,000 VND / $26.00 to $46.00 USD)**.

### Strategic Trade-Off
Skip An Hoa and Rach Soi hotels if your primary goal is boarding an early 06:45 or 07:30 ferry to Phu Quoc or Nam Du. The passenger pier lies **6.5 kilometers to 8.5 kilometers northwest** across two canal bridges, and 06:30 school and market traffic along Nguyen Trung Truc can stretch taxi transit times to **20 minutes to 25 minutes** (meter fare **95,000 VND to 135,000 VND / $3.80 to $5.40 USD**).

### Essential Maritime Buffer & Weather Protocols
1. **Physical Original Passport Requirement**: Maritime Port Authority inspectors (`Cảng vụ Hàng hải Kiên Giang`) require your **original physical passport** (or Vietnamese `CCCD` chip ID card) both at the ticket window and at the Pier 1–4 security gate. Smartphone screenshots are rejected at the gangway.
2. **Strict 45-Minute Check-In & 15-Minute Gate Closure**: Arrive at the pier gate **45 minutes before departure**. Boarding turnstiles lock 15 minutes prior to cast-off and vessel ramps retract 10 minutes before departure. Latecomers forfeit their fare with zero refund.
3. **Southwest Monsoon Swells (May through October, 26°C–32°C, 280 mm rain, 82% humidity)**: During the summer southwest monsoon (`gió mùa Tây Nam`), open-water swells in the Gulf of Thailand reach **1.8 meters to 2.5 meters**. Book a **lower-deck rear or middle-cabin seat** (where pitch and roll are 50% gentler than the upper bow), and purchase **Dimenhydrinate 50 mg motion-sickness tablets (`thuốc chống say sóng`, 10,000 VND / $0.40 USD per blister pack)** at any pharmacy on Nguyen Cong Tru street 30 minutes before boarding. When tropical depressions push winds to **Beaufort Force 6 or Force 7 (over 39 km/h)**, the Kien Giang Port Authority suspends all high-speed ferry sailings by **05:45 AM**; tickets are refunded 100% in cash at the pier booth.

## Intercity Bus Connections: Rach Soi Terminal (Mai Thi Hong Hanh)

Long-distance sleeper coaches from Ho Chi Minh City, Can Tho, Chau Doc, and Ca Mau terminate at the **Kien Giang Provincial Bus Station (`Bến xe Kiên Giang / Bến xe Rạch Sỏi`)**, located at 268 Mai Thi Hong Hanh street (National Route 61) in Rach Soi ward, **8.5 kilometers south of the Rach Gia Ferry Port**.

- **Ho Chi Minh City (`Bến xe Miền Tây`) to Rach Gia (250 km via CT01 Trung Luong–My Thuan–Can Tho Expressway)**: **Phuong Trang (FUTA Bus Lines, hotline `1900 6067`)**, **Kumho Samco**, and **Thien Thanh Limousine** run 34-berth and 22-cabin VIP sleeper buses every **30 minutes to 45 minutes** (24 hours a day). Transit takes **5.5 hours to 6.5 hours** and costs **190,000 VND to 280,000 VND ($7.60 to $11.20 USD)** one-way.
- **Can Tho (`Bến xe Trung tâm Cần Thơ`) to Rach Gia (115 km via QL61 / Lo Te–Rach Soi Highway)**: Departures every 45 minutes from 05:30 to 19:00; transit takes **2.25 hours to 2.75 hours** and costs **110,000 VND to 150,000 VND ($4.40 to $6.00 USD)**.
- **Ha Tien (`Bến xe Hà Tiên`) to Rach Gia (92 km via QL80)**: Minivans and local buses run every 60 minutes from 06:00 to 17:30; transit takes **2.0 hours** and costs **90,000 VND to 130,000 VND ($3.60 to $5.20 USD)**.
- **Crucial Free FUTA Feeder Van Tip (`Xe Trung Chuyển Miễn Phí 0 VND`)**: Every **Phuong Trang (FUTA)** bus ticket into Rach Soi Bus Station includes a **100% free 16-seat orange shuttle van transfer (`0 VND`)** straight from Rach Soi to the **Rach Gia Ferry Port on Nguyen Cong Tru** or any hotel in Central Rach Gia (a 20-minute transfer that saves a **120,000 VND to 155,000 VND / $4.80 to $6.20 USD** private taxi fare). Tell the bus conductor your hotel name or say *"Bến tàu Phú Quốc"* before the bus pulls into Rach Soi so they hand you a shuttle tag.

## What to Eat in Rach Gia Before Your Ferry: Bún Cá Kiên Giang & Seafood

Do not leave Rach Gia without tasting Kien Giang's signature morning and evening specialties within 5 minutes of your hotel:
1. **`Bún Cá Kiên Giang` (Rach Gia Snakehead Fish & Shrimp Rice Noodle Soup, 40,000 VND to 55,000 VND / $1.60 to $2.20 USD)**: Unlike turmeric-yellow Chau Doc fish noodles, authentic Rach Gia *bún cá* features a crystal-clear, sweet pork-and-fish-bone broth topped with boneless flakes of steamed snakehead fish (*cá lóc đồng*), roasted fish roe (*trứng cá*), and peeled sea shrimp (*tôm biển*), served with banana blossom and dipped in 40°N Phu Quoc fish sauce with crushed red chilies. Eat at **`Bún Cá Út Ơi`** (`106 Nguyễn Trung Trực`, open 06:00–21:00, 1.5 km from port) or the morning stalls on **Nguyen Cong Tru Street** (open 05:30–10:00, 100 meters from the pier).
2. **`Bánh Tầm Bì Thốt Nốt` (Thick Tapioca Noodles with Coconut Cream, Shredded Pork Skin & Grilled Meatballs, 30,000 VND to 40,000 VND / $1.20 to $1.60 USD)**: Served warm from 06:00 to 10:30 along **Tran Phu** and **Nguyen Trung Truc** streets.
3. **`Lẩu Cá Nhồng Chua Cay` & Grilled Gulf Squid (`Mực Nướng Muối Ớt`, 120,000 VND to 220,000 VND / $4.80 to $8.80 USD)**: Dine along the **Lac Hong Park** and **Ton Duc Thang** seafront terraces between 17:30 and 22:00.

## Practical Checklist & Transit Warnings

Follow these four operational steps when organizing your Rach Gia stopover:

- [ ] **Book High-Speed Ferry Tickets 3 to 7 Days Ahead**: Weekend sailings and public holiday departures (`April 30–May 1`, `September 2`, and `Tết`) sell out days in advance. Book e-tickets with QR codes directly via `superdong.com.vn` or `phuquocexpress.com` (`330,000–380,000 VND` / `$13.20–$15.20 USD`).
- [ ] **Verify Hand-Luggage Weight Limits (15 kg Free)**: Standard catamaran tickets include **15 kg** of free personal baggage. Oversized 28-inch suitcases, surfboards, or carton boxes over 15 kg incur a **30,000 VND to 100,000 VND ($1.20 to $4.00 USD)** cargo handling fee at the pier freight desk.
- [ ] **Withdraw VND Cash Before Boarding to Nam Du or Hon Son**: While Phu Quoc has dozens of ATMs, smaller islands like **Nam Du** and **Hon Son** have almost no reliable international Visa/Mastercard ATMs. Withdraw **2,000,000 VND to 4,000,000 VND ($80.00 to $160.00 USD)** in cash from the **Vietcombank** or **Agribank ATMs** on Tran Phu Street (250 meters east of the Rach Gia pier gate) before boarding your morning ferry.
- [ ] **Confirm Bai Vong Port Shuttle on Phu Quoc**: When your Rach Gia ferry docks at **Bai Vong Port (`Cảng Bãi Vòng`)** on Phu Quoc's east coast 2.5 hours later, step outside the pier gate to catch the **Superdong / Phu Quoc Express shuttle bus (30,000 VND to 50,000 VND / $1.20 to $2.00 USD)** or a **GrabCar (180,000 VND to 220,000 VND / $7.20 to $8.80 USD, 20 minutes across 14 km)** into Duong Dong Town.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is it necessary to stay overnight in Rach Gia before taking the morning ferry to Phu Quoc or Nam Du?
**Yes, if your catamaran departs between 06:45 and 08:30.** While overnight sleeper buses depart Ho Chi Minh City (`Bến xe Miền Tây`) at 22:00 and 23:00 (arriving at Rach Soi Bus Station between 04:30 and 05:30), a 45-minute highway delay or slow shuttle transfer can cause you to miss the 06:15 pier check-in cutoff for the 06:45 Nam Du boat. Arriving in Rach Gia by late afternoon the day before, eating a fresh seafood dinner at Lac Hong Park, and sleeping 100 meters from the pier at **Kiet Hong** or **Phuc Ngoc Hotel (280,000 VND to 450,000 VND / $11.20 to $18.00 USD)** guarantees a stress-free morning boarding.

### Can I leave my heavy suitcase at a Rach Gia ferry port hotel while visiting Nam Du or Phu Quoc for 2 to 4 days?
**Yes.** Almost every hotel along **Nguyen Cong Tru** and **Tu Do Street** (`Kiet Hong Hotel`, `Phuc Ngoc Hotel`, `Dong Hai Hotel`) offers locked ground-floor luggage storage for **30,000 VND to 50,000 VND ($1.20 to $2.00 USD) per bag per day** (and often free of charge if you book a room with them both before and after your island trip). Keep your passport, cash, and electronics in your daypack.

### What should I do if my high-speed ferry from Rach Gia is canceled due to rough seas?
Walk immediately to your carrier's official ticket window inside **Rach Gia Port** to collect a **100% cash refund (or free rebooking onto the next day's sailing)**. Because 1,000+ stranded passengers will look for rooms simultaneously when a gale warning (`Gió cấp 6–7`) is posted at 06:00, walk straight across the street to book a room on **Nguyen Cong Tru** or take a 40,000 VND ($1.60 USD) taxi into **Central Rach Gia (`Nguyen Trung Truc`)** before 07:30 AM. Alternatively, check seat availability on the afternoon **ATR-72 flight from Rach Gia Airport (`VKG`) to Phu Quoc (`PQC`, 40 minutes, 850,000 VND to 1,250,000 VND / $34.00 to $50.00 USD)**.

### How much does a Grab or metered taxi cost between Rach Gia Airport (VKG), Rach Soi Bus Station, and the Ferry Port?
A metered **Mai Linh** or **GrabCar** ride between **Rach Gia Airport (`VKG`)** and the **Bến Tàu Ferry Port (7.5 km)** takes **18 minutes to 22 minutes** and costs **105,000 VND to 135,000 VND ($4.20 to $5.40 USD)**. A taxi from **Rach Soi Bus Station (`Bến xe Kiên Giang`, 8.5 km)** to the Ferry Port takes **20 minutes** and costs **120,000 VND to 155,000 VND ($4.80 to $6.20 USD)** (or **0 VND** if you ride the free **Phuong Trang FUTA shuttle van** included with your bus ticket).
