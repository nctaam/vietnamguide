# 12 Best Beaches in Vietnam (2026): Where to Go by Month, Monsoon Calendar & Island Comparison

Stretching for **`3,260 kilometers` (`2,025 miles`)** across 15 degrees of latitude from the Gulf of Tonkin down to the Gulf of Thailand, Vietnam is a **true 365-day-a-year beach destination**—but only if you match your beach to the right monsoon window! Because Vietnam's coastline curves like a giant letter **"S"** around the Truong Son Mountain Range, when winter northeast winds bring cool mist and waves to Da Nang or Ha Long Bay in **December through February**, the southern islands of **Phu Quoc (`Phú Quốc`)** and **Con Dao (`Côn Đảo`)** bask in `29°C` sunshine and glass-flat turquoise water. Conversely, when summer southwest monsoons bring afternoon rain to Phu Quoc in **May through August**, the central coast beaches of **Hoi An (`An Bàng`)**, **Da Nang (`Mỹ Khê`)**, **Quy Nhon (`Kỳ Co`)**, and **Nha Trang / Cam Ranh** enjoy months of uninterrupted blue skies and crystal-clear swimming.

Here is the complete 2026 empirical guide to Vietnam's **12 best mainland and island beaches**, organized with a month-by-month weather matrix, airport/ferry logistics, water clarity ratings, and realistic hotel budgets.

## The 12 Best Beaches in Vietnam (Ranked by Sand Quality, Water Clarity & Vibe)

### 1. `Bãi Sao` & `Bãi Khem` — South Phu Quoc Island (`Best: November to August`)
Located on the sheltered southeastern tail of **Phu Quoc Island (`25 km` south of Duong Dong Town)**, **Sao Beach (`Bãi Sao`)** and neighboring **Khem Beach (`Bãi Khem`)** boast the **whitest, finest powdery silica sand in Southern Vietnam**—looking more like Boracay or the Maldives than the mainland coast. Because both bays curve east/southeast inside a headland pocket, the water is ankle-to-waist-deep and turquoise during the **November–April dry season**, and **remains calm and swimmable even during the May–September summer southwest monsoon** when Phu Quoc's west coast (`Long Beach`) gets rough waves!
- **Insider Tip**: At **Bai Sao**, skip the crowded central tour-bus parking lot and walk `400 meters` north to the quiet palm-shaded strip in front of **Paradiso Beach Club** (`sunbed + fresh coconut 100,000 VND`). At **Bai Khem**, stay at (`or buy a day pass/lunch at`) **JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay**, **New World Resort**, or **Premier Residences**.

### 2. `Bãi Đầm Trầu` & `Bãi Nhát` — Con Dao Archipelago (`Best: March to September`)
Situated `185 kilometers` off the southern coast (`45 minutes` by flight from Ho Chi Minh City `SGN` to `VCS`, or `4 hours` by high-speed ferry from Vung Tau / Tran De), the 16-island **Con Dao National Park (`Quần Đảo Côn Đảo`)** is Vietnam's most pristine marine sanctuary:
- **Dam Trau Beach (`Bãi Đầm Trầu`, `14 km` north of Con Son Town right beside Co Ong Airport, free access)**: A `1.2-kilometer` arc of golden-amber sand backed by primary jungle hills and bamboo beach shacks serving `80,000 VND` grilled squid—where ATR-72 and Embraer regional jets glide `30 meters` directly over the water to land on the runway! Walk over the rocky headland at low tide to **Secret Beach (`Bãi Suối Nóng`)**, an untouched lagoon with zero buildings.
- **Green Sea Turtle Nesting & Hatchling Season (`May to September` on `Hòn Bảy Cạnh`)**: Con Dao protects 85% of Vietnam's nesting **Green Sea Turtles (`Vích`)**. During summer nights, Rangers lead eco-tours (`800,000–1,800,000 VND`) to watch mother turtles lay eggs on the sand and release baby hatchlings into the sea at dawn!

### 3. `Bãi Kỳ Co` & `Bãi Xép` — Quy Nhon, Binh Dinh (`Best: March to August`)
Quy Nhon remains refreshingly uncrowded. Halfway between Hoi An and Nha Trang, **Quy Nhon (`Quy Nhơn`)** offers Central Vietnam's most dramatic cliff-backed coves without international crowds:
- **Ky Co Beach (`Bãi Kỳ Co`, `25 km` northeast of Quy Nhon via Nhon Ly)**: Accessible by a `15-minute` speedboat ride (`650,000–850,000 VND` package including coral snorkeling at **Hon Seo** and a 7-dish seafood lunch) or by mountain road (`170,000 VND` gate + electric buggy), Ky Co's two-tone neon-turquoise water and natural granite rock pools (`Hồ Suối Đẹp`) rival Palawan.
- **Bai Xep (`Làng Chài Bãi Xép`, `12 km` south)**: A car-free fishing village cove where backpackers and luxury resort guests (`Avani` / `Anantara`) share golden sand with round bamboo coracle boats.

### 4. `Bãi Biển Mỹ Khê` & `Biển Mân Thái` — Da Nang (`Best: March to Early September`)
Named by *Forbes* as one of the world's most attractive urban beaches, **My Khe Beach (`Bãi Biển Mỹ Khê`)** sweeps for `10 kilometers` along **Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard** (just 15 minutes from Da Nang International Airport `DAD`) before merging south into **Non Nuoc Beach (`Bãi Non Nước`)** at the foot of the Marble Mountains.
- **Why Travelers Love It**: Spotless white sand cleaned every night, lifeguard towers operating `05:00–19:00`, free freshwater showers, `40,000 VND` sunbeds, and **`05:15 AM` sunrise Stand-Up Paddleboarding (`SUP`, `150,000–250,000 VND`)** at the northern end (`Mân Thái` Fishing Beach) looking up at the `67-meter` **Lady Buddha on Son Tra Peninsula**! Morning water is glass-calm. (Note: from **September to February**, northeast swells turn My Khe into Vietnam's best **beginner surfing beach**, with board rentals for `150,000 VND/2 hours`).

### 5. `Bãi Biển An Bàng` & `Cù Lao Chàm` (`Cham Islands`) — Hoi An (`Best: March to August`)
Located just **`4.5 kilometers` (`15 minutes` by free hotel bicycle or `60,000 VND` Grab ride)** northeast of Hoi An Ancient Town through green vegetable farms (`Làng Rau Trà Quế`), **An Bang Beach (`Bãi Biển An Bàng`)** and neighboring **Hidden Beach** offer a laid-back bohemian alternative to Da Nang's high-rises:
- Spend the day on a cushioned sun lounger (free when you buy a drink or lunch) at **The DeckHouse**, **Shore Club**, **Soul Kitchen** (live music every afternoon/weekend), or **Dolphin Kitchen**, then pedal back into the lantern-lit Ancient Town at dusk.
- **Want Crystal-Clear Island Snorkeling?** From **March to August**, board a `20-minute` high-speed canoe (`600,000–850,000 VND` day tour) from **Cua Dai Pier** out to the UNESCO Biosphere of the **Cham Islands (`Cù Lao Chàm`)** to swim on palm-fringed **Bai Ong (`Bãi Ông`)** and **Bai Chong (`Bãi Chồng`)**!

### 6. `Bãi Dài Cam Ranh` & `Bãi Biển Dốc Lết` — Nha Trang / Khanh Hoa (`Best: January to Early September`)
Because Khanh Hoa Province is shielded by coastal headlands and 19 offshore islands, it enjoys **`300+ sunny days per year`** and an unusually long **8-month dry beach season (`January through August`)**:
- **Doc Let Beach (`Bãi Biển Dốc Lết`, `45 km` north of Nha Trang in Ninh Hoa)**: A `10-kilometer` crescent of chalk-white sand and ultra-shallow, wave-free turquoise water (you can walk 80 meters out and the water is still only chest-deep—ideal for families with children!).
- **Bai Dai Cam Ranh (`Bãi Dài`, `20 km` south of Nha Trang right beside `CXR` Cam Ranh Airport)**: A `15-kilometer` strip of untouched golden sand home to Vietnam's newest 5-star beachfront pool-villa resorts (`Alma`, `The Anam`, `Radisson Blu`, `Mövenpick`, `JW Marriott Cam Ranh`).

### 7. `Đảo Bé` (`An Bình` Islet, Ly Son) & `Vịnh Vĩnh Hy` — Ninh Thuan (`Best: April to August`)
- **Be Island (`Đảo Bé` / `An Bình`, Ly Son Volcanic Archipelago, Quang Ngai)**: A tiny `0.69 km²` car-free island (`12 minutes` by `120,000 VND` round-trip speedboat from Big Ly Son Island) where **Bai Ngang (`Bãi Ngang`)** features electric-blue coral water framed by black `2-million-year-old` volcanic basalt arches and coconut palms!
- **Vinh Hy Bay (`Vịnh Vĩnh Hy`) & `Bãi Kinh` / `Ninh Chữ` (Ninh Thuan Province, `35 km` from Phan Rang along the `DT702` Nui Chua National Park coastal road)**: Situated in **Vietnam's driest, sunniest microclimate** (`less than 750mm of rain per year!`), Vinh Hy offers crystal-clear coral bays, glass-bottom boats (`100,000 VND`), and world-class luxury at **Amanoi**.

### 8. `Bãi Minh Châu` (`Quan Lạn` Island) & `Bãi Cát Cò` (`Cát Bà` Island) — Northern Vietnam (`Best: May to September`)
If you are visiting **Northern Vietnam during the hot summer months (`May through September`)**, skip the artificial sand of Bai Chay (`Ha Long City`) and head out to the outer islands:
- **Minh Chau & Quan Lan Beaches (`Đảo Quan Lạn`, Bai Tu Long Bay — `45 minutes` by `200,000 VND` speedboat from Ao Tien Port, Van Don)**: `3 kilometers` of squeaky-clean white silica sand shaded by an ancient coastal *trâm* forest with almost zero foreign tourists.
- **Cat Co 1, 2 & 3 Beaches (`Đảo Cát Bà`) & `Bãi Ba Trái Đào` (`Three Peaches Beach`, Lan Ha Bay)**: Small sandy coves tucked between towering limestone karsts (`best visited on a midweek morning or by kayak from a Lan Ha Bay cruise`).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Critical Seasonal Warning for Central Coast Beaches (`October to December`)**: Every year, travelers book non-refundable beachfront resorts in **Hoi An (`An Bàng` / `Cửa Đại`)**, **Da Nang**, or **Nha Trang** in **October or November**, unaware that this is Central Vietnam's peak **typhoon and northeast monsoon season** (`red "No Swimming" flags, strong rip currents, and coastal erosion`). If traveling in **October, November, or December**, fly straight south to **Phu Quoc Island (`PQC`)** or **Mui Ne**, where the dry season is in full swing!

## Step-by-Step HowTo: How to Pick the Right Vietnam Beach for Your Trip

### 1. First Step — Filter Immediately by Your Travel Month (`Nov–Apr` = South vs `Mar–Aug` = Central)
Look at the month matrix above first:
- Traveling **November to February**? Book **Phu Quoc (`Bãi Sao`, `Bãi Khem`, `Ông Lang`)**, **Con Dao (`Đầm Trầu`)**, or **Mui Ne**.
- Traveling **March or April**? You have the green light for **both Central Vietnam (`Đà Nẵng`, `Hội An`, `Quy Nhơn`, `Nha Trang`) AND Southern Islands (`Phú Quốc`, `Côn Đảo`)**!
- Traveling **May to August**? Head straight to **Central Vietnam (`Đà Nẵng`, `Hội An`, `Cù Lao Chàm`, `Lý Sơn`, `Quy Nhơn`, `Nha Trang`, `Vĩnh Hy`)** or **Con Dao**.

### 2. Second Step — Match the Beach to Your Travel Style (`Urban Convenience vs Wild Island`)
- **Want a `10-minute` airport transfer, fast Wi-Fi, great street food & walkable nightlife?** Choose **Da Nang (`Mỹ Khê`)** or **Nha Trang (`Trần Phú`)**.
- **Want historic lantern streets + afternoon beach clubs?** Choose **Hoi An (`An Bàng` Beach)**.
- **Want powdery white island sand & coral snorkeling?** Choose **South Phu Quoc (`Bãi Sao` / `Bãi Khem`)**, **Quy Nhon (`Bãi Kỳ Co`)**, or **Con Dao (`Bãi Đầm Trầu`)**.

### 3. Final Step — Book Direct Domestic Flights (`or Sleeper Trains`) 3 to 4 Weeks Ahead
Vietnam's top beach hubs have convenient airports right on the coast: **Da Nang (`DAD` — 10 mins to beach)**, **Phu Quoc (`PQC` — 15 mins to Long Beach / 25 mins to Bai Khem)**, **Cam Ranh / Nha Trang (`CXR` — 5 mins to Bai Dai / 40 mins to Nha Trang)**, **Quy Nhon (`UIH` — 40 mins to town)**, and **Con Dao (`VCS` — 5 mins to Dam Trau Beach)**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Which beach in Vietnam has the clearest turquoise water and whitest sand?
For **powdery flour-white sand and shallow turquoise water**, the top five beaches in Vietnam are:
1. **`Bãi Sao` & `Bãi Khem` (`South Phu Quoc Island`)** — best from November to August.
2. **`Bãi Kỳ Co` (`Quy Nhon`)** and **`Đảo Bé` (`Ly Son Island`)** — best from April to August (`15–20 meters` underwater visibility).
3. **`Bãi Đầm Trầu` & `Hòn Bảy Cạnh` (`Con Dao Islands`)** — best from March to September.
4. **`Bãi Biển Dốc Lết` & `Hòn Mun / Bãi Mini` (`Nha Trang Bay`)** — best from February to August.

### Which is better for a beach holiday: Da Nang (`My Khe`) or Phu Quoc Island?
It depends 100% on the **time of year** and what you want to do outside the beach:
- **Choose `Phu Quoc Island` (`November through April`)** if you want a pure tropical island resort holiday (`warm 29°C winter water when Da Nang is cool and wavy, west-coast ocean sunsets, the 7.9 km Hon Thom Cable Car, An Thoi coral island hopping, and 30-day visa-free international entry`).
- **Choose `Da Nang` + `Hoi An` (`March through August`)** if you are visiting in spring or summer and want to combine daily morning swims at **My Khe / An Bang Beach** with world-class cultural day trips (**Hoi An Ancient Town**, **Marble Mountains**, **Son Tra Peninsula**, **My Son Sanctuary**, **Ba Na Hills**, and **Hue Imperial City**).

### Can you swim at the beaches near Hanoi (`Ha Long Bay`) or Ho Chi Minh City (`Vung Tau`)?
- Near **Hanoi**, the artificial beaches in **Ha Long City (`Bãi Cháy`)** have calm but greenish-opaque bay water due to silt from the Red River delta; for clear open-sea swimming in the North (`May to September`), take the ferry out to **Quan Lan / Minh Chau Island (`Bai Tu Long Bay`)**, **Co To Island**, or **Cat Ba / Three Peaches Beach (`Lan Ha Bay`)**.
- Near **Ho Chi Minh City**, **Vung Tau (`100 km`)** and **Can Gio (`50 km`)** sit near the mouths of the Saigon and Dong Nai rivers, so the water has dark alluvial sand (`great for seafood and sea breezes, but not turquoise`). For clear water near Saigon, drive `2.0 hours` east to **Ho Tram / Ho Coc**, `2.5 hours` along the `CT01` Expressway to **Mui Ne**, or take a `45-minute` flight to **Con Dao (`VCS`)** or **Phu Quoc (`PQC`)**!
