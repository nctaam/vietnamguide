# 12 Best Beaches in Vietnam (2026): Where to Go by Month, Monsoon Calendar & Island Comparison

Stretching for 3,260 kilometers across 15 degrees of latitude from the Gulf of Tonkin down to the Gulf of Thailand, Vietnam operates as a true 365-day beach destination, provided you align your coastal hub with the right monsoon window. Because the national coastline curves in a steep S-shape around the 1,100 km Truong Son Mountain Range, weather systems flip dramatically between regions.

When winter northeast winds bring 18°C mist and 1.5 meters surf swells to Da Nang or Ha Long Bay from December through February, the southern islands of **Phu Quoc (Phú Quốc)** and **Con Dao (Côn Đảo)** bask in 29°C to 31°C sunshine and calm turquoise water. Conversely, when summer southwest monsoons bring afternoon rain to Phu Quoc's western shore from May through September, the central coast beaches of **Hoi An (An Bàng)**, **Da Nang (Mỹ Khê)**, **Quy Nhon (Kỳ Co)**, and **Nha Trang / Cam Ranh (Bãi Dài)** enjoy 4 months to 6 months of blue skies and 15 meters underwater visibility.

This 2026 empirical guide ranks Vietnam's 12 best mainland and island beaches, pairing a month-by-month monsoon matrix with verified domestic flight fares, high-speed ferry schedules, water clarity metrics, and beachfront accommodation budgets.

## The 12 Best Beaches in Vietnam (Ranked by Sand Quality, Water Clarity & Logistics)

### 1. Bai Sao & Bai Khem: South Phu Quoc Island (Best: November to August)
Located on the sheltered southeastern tail of **Phu Quoc Island** (25 km south of Duong Dong Town and 18 km south of Phu Quoc International Airport PQC, 25 minutes by GrabCar for 220,000 VND to 280,000 VND, or $8.80 to $11.20 USD), **Sao Beach (Bãi Sao)** and neighboring **Khem Beach (Bãi Khem)** feature the finest creamy-white silica sand in southern Vietnam.

Because both 1.5 km crescent bays face east-southeast inside protected headlands, the water remains waist-deep for 80 meters offshore and glassy during the November to April dry season (28°C to 31°C). When the May to September southwest monsoon churns up 1.5 meters waves along Phu Quoc's western Long Beach (Bãi Trường), the eastern leeward bays of Bai Sao and Bai Khem stay calm and swimmable.

- **Tactical Beach Access**: At Bai Sao, bypass the crowded central bus lot at My Lan Restaurant and walk 400 meters north to the palm-lined strip outside Paradiso Beach Club, where sunbed rental plus a fresh coconut costs 100,000 VND to 150,000 VND ($4.00 to $6.00 USD). At Bai Khem, book a room or purchase a day dining voucher (500,000 VND, or $20.00 USD) at JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay (7,500,000 VND, or $300.00 USD per night), New World Phu Quoc Resort (4,200,000 VND, or $168.00 USD), or Premier Residences Phu Quoc Emerald Bay (1,950,000 VND to 2,600,000 VND, or $78.00 to $104.00 USD).

- **An Thoi Island Hopping & Cable Car**: Three kilometers south at Sunset Town (An Thoi Port), board the 7.9 km Sun World Hon Thom 3-wire sea cable car (20 minutes flight 50 meters above coral reefs; round-trip ticket 850,000 VND, or $34.00 USD per adult; 700,000 VND, or $28.00 USD for children 1.0 m to 1.4 m; 1,150,000 VND, or $46.00 USD with mango buffet). Travelers can also charter a 4-island speedboat (750,000 VND, or $30.00 USD group tour; 2,200,000 VND, or $88.00 USD private canoe for 5 hours) to snorkel at Hon May Rut, Hon Gam Ghi, and Hon Mong Tay.

### 2. Bai Dam Trau & Hon Bay Canh: Con Dao Archipelago (Best: March to September)
Positioned 185 km south of Vung Tau and 230 km southeast of Ho Chi Minh City, the 16-island **Con Dao National Park (Quần đảo Côn Đảo)** protects 14,000 hectares of marine coral reefs and primary forest.

Travelers arrive via a 45 minutes flight from Ho Chi Minh City (SGN to VCS on Vietnam Airlines / VASCO, 1,650,000 VND to 2,400,000 VND, or $66.00 to $96.00 USD one-way) or by high-speed ferry from Tran De Port in Soc Trang (Superdong or Phu Quoc Express, 2.5 hours covering 90 km, 320,000 VND to 390,000 VND, or $12.80 to $15.60 USD) or Vung Tau Cau Da Port (3.5 hours to 4.0 hours, 790,000 VND to 950,000 VND, or $31.60 to $38.00 USD).

- **Dam Trau Beach (Bãi Đầm Trầu)**: Located 14 km north of Con Son Town right beside Co Ong Airport (20 minutes by rented scooter at 150,000 VND, or $6.00 USD per day, or 180,000 VND, or $7.20 USD taxi), this 1.2 km arc of golden-amber sand requires 0 VND ($0.00 USD) entrance fee. Bamboo shacks grill fresh squid and parrotfish for 120,000 VND to 180,000 VND ($4.80 to $7.20 USD) while ATR-72 aircraft descend 30 meters directly overhead.

- **Secret Lagoons & Low-Tide Coves**: At low tide on Dam Trau, scramble 300 meters over the northern granite boulders for 10 minutes to reach **Suoi Nong Secret Lagoon (Bãi Suối Nóng)**. Along the southern coast road, **Bai Nhat (Bãi Nhát, 6 km from town)** emerges for 4 hours daily at low tide with white sand and smooth pebbles.

- **Sea Turtle Nesting Season (May to September)**: Roughly 85% of Vietnam's green sea turtles (*Chelonia mydas*) nest across Con Dao's offshore islets. Book a National Park ranger speedboat to **Hon Bay Canh (Hòn Bảy Cạnh, 45 minutes boat ride)** for overnight turtle egg-laying observation (1,500,000 VND to 2,200,000 VND, or $60.00 to $88.00 USD) or a morning hatchling release trip (750,000 VND to 950,000 VND, or $30.00 to $38.00 USD). Daily visitor permits are strictly capped.

- **Con Dao Accommodation Tiers**: Luxury travelers stay at **Six Senses Con Dao** on privately sheltered Dat Doc Beach (18,500,000 VND, or $740.00 USD per villa night), while boutique travelers book The Secret Con Dao (2,400,000 VND, or $96.00 USD) or Poulo Condor Boutique Resort (3,200,000 VND, or $128.00 USD). Book rooms early.

### 3. Bai Ky Co & Bai Xep: Quy Nhon, Binh Dinh (Best: March to August)
Situated halfway between Hoi An (300 km north, 4.5 hours drive) and Nha Trang (215 km south, 3.5 hours drive), **Quy Nhon** delivers cliff-framed turquoise coves at 40% lower prices than major resort cities. Flights from Hanoi (HAN, 95 minutes) or Saigon (SGN, 60 minutes) land at Phu Cat Airport (UIH), located 32 km northwest of town (40 minutes by airport bus for 50,000 VND, or $2.00 USD, or 300,000 VND, or $12.00 USD private taxi).

- **Ky Co Cove (Bãi Kỳ Co, 25 km northeast of Quy Nhon)**: Reached via Nhon Ly fishing village (30 minutes drive across the 2.5 km Thi Nai Sea Bridge), Ky Co features a 1.0 km crescent of powdery sand and neon-blue water enclosed by 80 meters granite cliffs. Water clarity exceeds 15 meters. Board a 15 minutes local speedboat from Nhon Ly Pier (650,000 VND to 850,000 VND, or $26.00 to $34.00 USD package with coral snorkeling at **Hon Seo** and a 7-dish seafood lunch) or drive over the mountain pass (170,000 VND, or $6.80 USD), pairing your trip with the **Eo Gio** cliff walkway (2 km away, 25,000 VND, or $1.00 USD).

- **Bai Xep Fishing Cove (Bãi Xép, 13 km south of Quy Nhon on QL1D)**: A pedestrian-only fishing hamlet where round bamboo basket boats (*thuyền thúng*) bob in a 500 meters bay. Budget travelers stay at Life's a Beach Hostel (350,000 VND to 800,000 VND, or $14.00 to $32.00 USD), while luxury guests book **Avani Quy Nhon Resort** (3,400,000 VND, or $136.00 USD) or **Anantara Quy Nhon Villas** (11,500,000 VND, or $460.00 USD).

### 4. My Khe Beach, Man Thai & Non Nuoc: Da Nang (Best: March to Early September)
Sweeping for 10 kilometers along Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard from the foot of Son Tra Peninsula south to the Marble Mountains, **My Khe Beach (Bãi biển Mỹ Khê)** and **Non Nuoc Beach (Bãi Non Nước)** sit just 5.5 km (12 minutes by GrabCar for 75,000 VND to 100,000 VND, or $3.00 to $4.00 USD) east of Da Nang International Airport (DAD).

- **Urban Swim & Sunrise SUP**: Municipal crews rake the 60 meters wide white sand strip nightly, and lifeguard towers staff swimmable zones from 05:00 to 19:00. Public freshwater showers cost 5,000 VND ($0.20 USD), and wooden sun loungers rent for 40,000 VND ($1.60 USD) per day. Arrive at **Man Thai Fishing Beach** at 05:15 AM to rent a Stand-Up Paddleboard (SUP, 150,000 VND to 250,000 VND, or $6.00 to $10.00 USD for 90 minutes) and paddle 800 meters offshore while looking up at the 67 meters tall Lady Buddha statue on Son Tra Peninsula (10 km away).

- **Autumn & Winter Beginner Surfing**: From October to February, northeast swells transform My Khe into a reliable beginner surf break, with soft-top surfboard rentals costing 150,000 VND ($6.00 USD) for 2 hours and private 90 minutes surf coaching priced at 650,000 VND ($26.00 USD).

### 5. An Bang Beach, Hidden Beach & Cham Islands: Hoi An (Best: March to August)
Located 4.5 km northeast of Hoi An Ancient Town along Hai Ba Trung Street through the organic herb gardens of Tra Que Village (15 minutes by free hotel bicycle or 10 minutes by GrabCar for 60,000 VND to 80,000 VND, or $2.40 to $3.20 USD), **An Bang Beach (Bãi biển An Bàng)** and **Hidden Beach (Bãi biển Tĩnh Lặng, 1.2 km south)** escaped the severe coastal erosion that narrowed Cua Dai Beach.

- **Beach Clubs on An Bang**: Shaded bamboo daybeds at **The DeckHouse**, **Shore Club**, **Soul Kitchen**, and **Dolphin Kitchen** are complimentary when ordering 150,000 VND to 300,000 VND ($6.00 to $12.00 USD) in fresh passionfruit juices, wood-fired pizzas, or grilled Hoi An snapper.

- **UNESCO Cham Islands Snorkeling**: For 15 meters underwater visibility, ride 5 km south to **Cua Dai Pier** at 08:00 AM and board a 20 minutes high-speed canoe (650,000 VND to 850,000 VND, or $26.00 to $34.00 USD day tour including 70,000 VND biosphere fee, two 45 minutes reef snorkel stops, and seafood lunch) across 15 km of open sea to the **Cham Islands (Cù Lao Chàm)**, swimming on palm-lined **Bai Ong (Bãi Ông)** and **Bai Chong (Bãi Chồng)**.

### 6. Cam Ranh Long Beach & Doc Let: Nha Trang / Khanh Hoa (Best: January to Early September)
Shielded by Hon Tre Island and the Hon Gom Peninsula, Khanh Hoa Province logs 300 sunny days per year and enjoys an 8 months dry swimming window from late January through early September:

- **Bai Dai Cam Ranh (Bãi Dài, 25 km south of Nha Trang)**: Starting just 3 km to 8 km north of Cam Ranh International Airport (CXR, 10 minutes transfer for 100,000 VND to 150,000 VND, or $4.00 to $6.00 USD), this 15 km unbroken ribbon of fine golden sand hosts Vietnam's highest concentration of modern 5-star beachfront pool resorts, including **The Anam Cam Ranh** (4,200,000 VND, or $168.00 USD), **Alma Resort** (3,800,000 VND, or $152.00 USD), **Radisson Blu** (2,900,000 VND, or $116.00 USD), and **Ana Mandara Cam Ranh** (3,500,000 VND, or $140.00 USD).

- **Doc Let Beach (Bãi biển Dốc Lết, 48 km north of Nha Trang in Ninh Hoa)**: A 50 minutes drive north via National Highway QL1A (550,000 VND, or $22.00 USD private taxi, or 50,000 VND, or $2.00 USD Phuong Trang Bus #3) brings you to a 10 km chalk-white sandbar where turquoise water remains 1.0 meters to 1.3 meters deep even 90 meters from shore (Pax Ana Doc Let Resort 2,100,000 VND, or $84.00 USD per night).

- **Nha Trang Bay Offshore Islands**: In Nha Trang Bay itself, take a 20 minutes speedboat from Vinh Truong Pier to **Mini Beach (Bãi Mini on Hon Mieu, admission + sunbed 200,000 VND, or $8.00 USD)** and **Hon Tam** (mud bath + beach day pass 740,000 VND, or $29.60 USD).

### 7. Dao Be (An Binh Islet, Ly Son) & Vinh Hy Bay: Quang Ngai & Ninh Thuan (Best: April to August)
- **Be Island (Đảo Bé, Ly Son Volcanic Archipelago, Quang Ngai)**: From Sa Ky Port (40 km south of Chu Lai Airport VCL), board a 35 minutes Super Biển Đông hydrofoil (28 km offshore, 200,000 VND, or $8.00 USD) to Big Ly Son Island, then hop a 12 minutes speedboat (3 nautical miles, 120,000 VND, or $4.80 USD round-trip) to 0.69 km² car-free **Be Island**. Here, **Bai Ngang (Bãi Ngang)** pairs 20 meters clarity coral lagoons with 2-million-year-old black basalt lava cliffs (basket boat coral viewing 100,000 VND, or $4.00 USD).

- **Vinh Hy Bay (Vịnh Vĩnh Hy) & Bai Kinh (Ninh Thuan, 60 km south of Cam Ranh Airport CXR)**: Tucked beneath the arid granite peaks of Nui Chua National Park along coastal road DT702, Ninh Thuan sits inside Vietnam's driest rain-shadow microclimate (under 750 mm of rain annually, 310 sunny days). Swim at **Bai Kinh** (entrance 20,000 VND, or $0.80 USD; 5 minutes boat across to **Binh Hung Island** for 20,000 VND, or $0.80 USD), charter a glass-bottom coral boat in Vinh Hy Bay (100,000 VND to 150,000 VND, or $4.00 to $6.00 USD), or splurge at **Amanoi** (35,000,000 VND, or $1,400.00 USD).

### 8. Quan Lan Island & Cat Ba / Lan Ha Bay: Northern Vietnam (Best: May to September)
When northern summer temperatures climb to 31°C to 35°C between May and September, bypass the silty mainland water of Bai Chay in Ha Long City and ride out to the outer Gulf of Tonkin islands:

- **Minh Chau & Quan Lan Beaches (Đảo Quan Lạn, Bai Tu Long Bay)**: Drive 2.5 hours east of Hanoi via the CT04/CT06 Expressway (195 km) to **Ao Tien International Port** in Van Don (limousine van 260,000 VND, or $10.40 USD), then take a 45 minutes high-speed ferry (200,000 VND, or $8.00 USD one-way) to Quan Lan. **Minh Chau Beach** offers 3.0 km of squeaky white quartz sand backed by a 100-year-old coastal *trâm* forest (Angsana Quan Lan Resort 3,600,000 VND, or $144.00 USD; boutique hotels 800,000 VND, or $32.00 USD).

- **Cat Co 1, 2 & 3 (Cat Ba Island) & Ba Trai Dao (Lan Ha Bay)**: Reached in 3.5 hours from Hanoi via the Dong Bai–Cai Vieng ferry or 10 minutes Sun World Cat Ba cable car (100,000 VND, or $4.00 USD), Cat Ba features three cliff-ringed sandy coves (Cat Co 1, 2, and 3 connected by a 1.2 km cliffside walkway), while day cruises (750,000 VND to 950,000 VND, or $30.00 to $38.00 USD for 7 hours) kayak around **Ba Trai Dao (Three Peaches Beach)** and **Monkey Island**.

### 9. Mui Ne & Hon Rom: Phan Thiet, Binh Thuan (Best: November to April)
Thanks to the completed Dau Giay–Phan Thiet Expressway (CT01), driving the 200 km from Ho Chi Minh City to **Mui Ne** now takes just 2.5 hours (limousine bus 220,000 VND to 280,000 VND, or $8.80 to $11.20 USD; train SPT2 from Saigon Station 3.8 hours, 190,000 VND, or $7.60 USD). Receiving only 800 mm of rain per year, Mui Ne stays dry throughout winter when Central Vietnam is rainy.

While central Ham Tien has narrow sand at high tide, the 10 km stretch along **Phu Hai Beach** (The Anam Mui Ne 3,600,000 VND, or $144.00 USD; Sailing Club Resort 3,100,000 VND, or $124.00 USD) and **Hon Rom** offers wide golden sand and 20-knot afternoon thermals from November to March (kitesurfing lessons 1,500,000 VND, or $60.00 USD per hour; sunrise Red and White Sand Dunes jeep tour 550,000 VND, or $22.00 USD for 4 hours).

### 10. Ong Lang & Ganh Dau: Northwest Phu Quoc (Best: November to April)
For travelers who prefer boutique sunset coves over the high-rise resorts of Long Beach, **Ong Lang Beach (Bãi Ông Lang, 8 km north of Duong Dong, 15 minutes taxi for 120,000 VND, or $4.80 USD)** strings together 4.0 km of golden sand pockets divided by black volcanic rock outcrops and shaded by sea-almond trees.

Swim in calm 29°C dry-season water, snorkel 100 meters off the rocks, and dine barefoot at Mango Bay Resort (2,900,000 VND, or $116.00 USD), Chen Sea Resort (4,500,000 VND, or $180.00 USD), or Camia Resort (2,100,000 VND, or $84.00 USD). Further north at **Ganh Dau Cape (28 km north)**, Starfish Beach (*Rạch Vẹm*) sits just 4.0 km across the channel from Cambodia's Bokor Mountains.

### 11. Ho Tram & Ho Coc: Ba Ria-Vung Tau (Best: December to May)
When you want a clean weekend beach escape from Ho Chi Minh City without boarding an airplane, skip silty Vung Tau (100 km) and drive 120 km southeast (2.0 hours via the Long Thanh Expressway and coastal Highway DT994; limousine van 180,000 VND to 220,000 VND, or $7.20 to $8.80 USD) to **Ho Tram** and **Ho Coc**.

Backed by the 11,000-hectare Phuoc Buu coastal nature reserve, this 15 km quiet shoreline hosts Melia Ho Tram Beach Resort (3,500,000 VND, or $140.00 USD), InterContinental Grand Ho Tram (3,800,000 VND, or $152.00 USD), and the natural 82°C mineral pools of Binh Chau Hot Springs (18 km north, entrance + mud bath 350,000 VND, or $14.00 USD).

### 12. Xuan Dung (Dam Mon Peninsula) & Ninh Van Bay: North Khanh Hoa (Best: February to August)
North of Nha Trang, two secluded peninsulas deliver castaway calm: **Ninh Van Bay** (accessible only by 20 minutes resort speedboat from Nha Trang, home to **Six Senses Ninh Van Bay** at 22,000,000 VND, or $880.00 USD, and **L'Alya Ninh Van Bay** at 7,500,000 VND, or $300.00 USD) and **Xuan Dung Beach** on the Hon Gom Peninsula in Van Phong Bay (85 km north of Nha Trang, 90 minutes drive), where glass-calm cobalt water laps against 30 meters desert sand dunes and the easternmost point of mainland Vietnam (*Mũi Đôi*).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Critical Typhoon & Rip-Current Warning for Central Coast Beaches (October to December):** Every autumn, travelers book non-refundable beachfront stays in **Hoi An (An Bàng)**, **Da Nang (Mỹ Khê)**, or **Nha Trang** between October 1 and December 15, unaware that northeast monsoon winds drive 2.5 meters waves, red "No Swimming" flags, and 350 mm monthly downpours across Central Vietnam. If your vacation falls in October, November, or December, route your beach days exclusively to **Phu Quoc Island (PQC)**, **Con Dao (VCS)**, or **Mui Ne**.

## Step-by-Step HowTo: Picking the Right Vietnam Beach for Your Itinerary

1. **Lock In Your Monsoon Window First**: Consult the seasonal table above before checking hotel rates. Winter departures between **November 1 and February 28** belong in the south at Phu Quoc, Con Dao, Mui Ne, or Ho Tram. Spring schedules in **March and April** unlock every central and southern beach simultaneously. Summer vacations between **May 1 and August 31** shine brightest along the Central Coast (Da Nang, Hoi An, Cham Islands, Quy Nhon, Ly Son, Nha Trang, Cam Ranh, Vinh Hy) or Con Dao.

2. **Match Airport Transfer Times to Your Available Days**: If you only have 3 days to 4 days, pick a fly-in hub with a sub-25 minutes airport transfer: **Da Nang (DAD, 12 minutes / 5.5 km to My Khe)**, **Cam Ranh (CXR, 10 minutes / 5 km to Bai Dai)**, **Phu Quoc (PQC, 15 minutes / 10 km to Long Beach or 25 minutes / 18 km to Bai Khem)**, or **Con Dao (VCS, 5 minutes / 2 km to Dam Trau)**. Save ferry-connected islands (Ly Son, Quan Lan, Cham Islands) for trips of 5 days or longer.

3. **Book Domestic Flights or Ferry Seats 21 Days Ahead**: Domestic fares from Hanoi (HAN) or Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) to DAD, PQC, CXR, and UIH average 1,100,000 VND to 2,200,000 VND ($44.00 to $88.00 USD) one-way when booked 3 weeks in advance, while scarce Con Dao (VCS) turbo-prop seats and summer weekend ferries to Quan Lan or Phu Quoc sell out 14 days before departure.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Which beach in Vietnam has the clearest turquoise water and whitest sand?
For powdery flour-white sand and 12 meters to 20 meters underwater visibility, five beaches lead the country:

1. **Bai Sao & Bai Khem (South Phu Quoc Island)**: Best from November to August; fine white silica sand and waist-deep turquoise water.

2. **Bai Ky Co (Quy Nhon)** & **Dao Be (Ly Son Island)**: Best from April to August; 15 meters to 20 meters coral visibility framed by granite cliffs and volcanic rock.

3. **Bai Dam Trau & Hon Bay Canh (Con Dao Islands)**: Best from March to September; protected marine national park reefs and sea turtle nesting bays.

4. **Doc Let Beach & Bai Dai Cam Ranh (Khanh Hoa)**: Best from February to August; 10 km to 15 km crescents of gentle, wave-free swimming water.

5. **Minh Chau Beach (Quan Lan Island, Bai Tu Long Bay)**: Best from May to September; 3 km of pure white quartz sand in Northern Vietnam.

### Which is better for a beach holiday: Da Nang (My Khe) or Phu Quoc Island?
Your choice depends on the calendar month and whether you want cultural day trips or a pure island resort stay:

- **Choose Phu Quoc Island (November through April)** when you need guaranteed 29°C winter sun, west-coast ocean sunsets at Ong Lang, powdery white sand at Bai Khem, the 7.9 km Hon Thom sea cable car (850,000 VND, or $34.00 USD), An Thoi coral island hopping, and Phu Quoc's special **30-day visa-free entry** for international arrivals flying directly into PQC.

- **Choose Da Nang + Hoi An (March through August)** when traveling in spring or summer and wanting to pair morning swims at My Khe (5.5 km from DAD airport) or An Bang Beach (4.5 km from Hoi An) with UNESCO cultural excursions to [Hoi An](/destinations/hoi-an-ancient-town-guide/), the Marble Mountains (8 km, 40,000 VND, or $1.60 USD ticket), My Son Sanctuary (45 km, 150,000 VND, or $6.00 USD), and the Hai Van Pass (28 km).

### Can you swim in clear water near Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City?
- **Near Hanoi**: Mainland beaches in Ha Long City (Bai Chay, 155 km from Hanoi) have calm but emerald-opaque water due to Red River silt. For clear open-ocean swimming in the North between May and September, take the 45 minutes high-speed ferry from Ao Tien Port (200,000 VND, or $8.00 USD) to **Quan Lan / Minh Chau Island** or **Co To Island**, or kayak to **Three Peaches Beach (Ba Trái Đào)** in Lan Ha Bay.

- **Near Ho Chi Minh City**: Vung Tau (100 km) and Can Gio (50 km) sit beside the mouths of the Saigon and Dong Nai estuaries, resulting in brownish alluvial water. For clean swimming within driving range of Saigon, travel 2.0 hours (120 km) east to **Ho Tram / Ho Coc**, drive 2.5 hours (200 km) along the CT01 Expressway to **Mui Ne**, or board a 45 minutes flight from SGN to **Con Dao (VCS)** or **Phu Quoc (PQC)**.
