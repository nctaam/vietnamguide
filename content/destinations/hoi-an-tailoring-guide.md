# Hoi An Tailoring Guide (2026): Custom Suits, Dress Prices, Best Shops & 3-Fitting Timeline

For over four centuries, since 16th- and 17th-century Chinese, Japanese, Dutch, and Portuguese merchant vessels anchored along the Thu Bon River to trade raw Quang Nam mulberry silk (`lụa Mã Châu` and `lụa Duy Xuyên`), **Hoi An Ancient Town (`Phố Cổ Hội An`)** has been Vietnam's undisputed garment-making capital. Today, more than **400 custom tailor storefronts** operate within a two-square-kilometer radius around **`Trần Phú`**, **`Nguyễn Thái Học`**, **`Lê Lợi`**, and **`Trần Hưng Đạo`**.

Yet two travelers can walk into Hoi An on the exact same morning and leave with opposite outcomes. One commissions a breathable, **half-canvas `Super 130s` Australian Merino wool suit (`$180–$320 USD` / `4,500,000–8,000,000 VND`)** lined in cooling Bemberg cupro that fits better than an `$1,100 USD` off-the-rack suit at home. The other follows a hotel motorbike tout into a high-commission backstreet shop, pays `$110 USD` for a synthetic polyester suit sold as *"cashmere wool,"* rushes through a single fitting in 18 hours, and watches the lapels bubble after the first dry-cleaning. This 2026 empirical guide covers verified prices, fabric testing, the 5 most reputable ateliers, and the mandatory 3-fitting schedule.

## Fabric Science & Construction: How to Test Real Wool vs Polyester & Fused vs Half-Canvas

In Hoi An, the word **"Cashmere"** is used by budget shops as a generic marketing term for *any* suiting fabric—even `80% polyester / 20% viscose` blends that cost the shop `$4 USD` per meter. Because Vietnam does not produce raw sheep's wool domestically, all genuine suiting wool must be imported (carrying import duties). Therefore, **it is physically impossible for any shop in Hoi An to sell a `100% pure Merino wool` suit for `$80–$100 USD`**. Use these three empirical tests at the fabric wall:

### 1. The Lighter Burn Test (`Wool` & `Mulberry Silk` vs `Polyester` & `Rayon`)
Before paying your 50% deposit, ask the consultant to snip a `2-centimeter` corner from the exact bolt of cloth you selected and hold a cigarette lighter flame to it:
- **Genuine `100% Wool` or `Cashmere`**: Burns slowly, self-extinguishes when the flame is removed, smells distinctly like **singed human hair**, and leaves a **crumbly, matte-black ash** that turns to powder when rubbed between your thumb and forefinger.
- **Genuine `100% Mulberry Silk (`Lụa Tơ Tằm`)`**: Smells like charred hair/feathers, stops burning as soon as the flame pulls away, and leaves a soft, dark brittle ash.
- **Synthetic `Polyester` / `Nylon` / `Acrylic`**: Catches fire quickly with black chemical smoke, smells like **burning plastic**, and melts into a **hard, shiny plastic bead** at the edge of the fabric that cannot be crushed into powder.
- **Plant-Based `100% Linen`, `Cotton`, or `Bamboo` (`Rayon`)**: Burns brightly like paper, smells like **burning dry leaves or wood**, and leaves a feathery gray ash with zero melted plastic beads.

### 2. The Palm Squeeze & Wrinkle Recovery Test
Grab a handful of the suiting fabric on the bolt and squeeze it tightly in your fist for `5 seconds`, then release:
- **High-Grade Merino Wool (`Super 110s–140s`)**: Springs back immediately with almost no sharp crease lines and feels cool yet dry against your skin.
- **Cheap Polyester Blend**: Holds stubborn, sharp crease lines and feels slightly slick or warm when rubbed rapidly.

### 3. Jacket Inner Construction (`Half-Canvas` vs `Fused`) & Lining (`Bemberg Cupro` vs `Polyester`)
Even a `$300 USD` wool fabric will wear like a plastic raincoat if the tailor lines the inside of the jacket with cheap, non-breathable polyester satin!
- **Always Specify `Bemberg Cupro` or `100% Viscose` Lining**: Ask your tailor to show you their breathable **cupro/viscose lining book (`vải lót thoáng khí`)**. It wicks perspiration in tropical heat and prevents static cling.
- **Request `Half-Canvas` (`Dựng Mùng Nửa Ngực`) Over Cheap Glue Fusing**: Budget `$100` suits glue (`fuse`) the outer cloth to a stiff synthetic backing; after three dry-cleanings, the glue dissolves in spots and creates unsightly bubbles across the chest. At **Yaly**, **Bebe**, or **A Dong Silk**, request **half-canvas construction** (`+$25–$40 USD`), where a stitched horsehair/cotton canvas layer floats inside the chest and lapels so the jacket drapes naturally to your torso over time.

## The Mandatory 48-to-72-Hour, 3-Fitting Schedule (Why "24-Hour Suits" Fail)

Never commission a custom suit if you only have 1 night in Hoi An. While shops boast about *"24-hour express tailoring,"* a single fitting leaves zero time to correct shoulder pitch, collar gaps, or trouser drape. Structure your Hoi An stay around this **3-day / 3-fitting golden timeline**:

- **Appointment #1 — Day 1 Morning (`09:00–11:00 AM`, `60–90 minutes` — Design, Fabric & 25 Measurements)**: Walk into your chosen shop on your very first morning in Hoi An. Bring high-resolution photos on your phone showing the exact lapel width (`e.g., 8.0–8.5 cm notch or peak lapel`), button stance, vent style (`double side vents`), and trouser break you want. The master cutter records 25 to 35 body measurements and posture notes (`such as a slightly dropped right shoulder`). You pay a **`40%–50%` deposit** (`cash VND or credit card`).
- **Appointment #2 — Day 2 Morning (`10:00–11:00 AM`, `30 minutes` — The Crucial "Skeleton / Basted" First Fitting)**: Twenty-four hours later, you try on the semi-constructed garment (`often with white basting threads and one sleeve pinned`). **Do not hold your breath or stand like a soldier**—sit down in a chair, cross your arms, button the jacket, and walk around. The master cutter (not just the sales assistant!) chalks adjustments to the shoulder seam, chest suppression, waist taper, and trouser seat.
- **Appointment #3 — Day 2 Late Afternoon (`17:00 PM`) or Day 3 Morning (`09:30 AM` — Second Precision Fitting)**: The suit is now 95% finished with buttons and lining attached. Check five critical details in the three-way mirror:
  1. **Collar Hug**: The jacket collar must rest flush against your shirt collar at the back of your neck with zero gap when you move your arms.
  2. **Shoulder Divots**: The upper sleeve should fall smoothly from the shoulder bone without pinching inward.
  3. **Jacket Length**: With arms relaxed at your sides, the bottom hem of the jacket should rest right in the curl of your fingers (`covering the curve of your seat`).
  4. **Shirt Cuff Exposure**: Exactly **`1.0 to 1.5 cm` (`half an inch`)** of shirt cuff should show beyond the jacket sleeve.
  5. **Trouser Break**: Check that the trousers skim the top of your leather shoes with a clean slight break (and ask them to reinforce all buttons and belt loops!).
- **Final Pickup — Day 3 Afternoon (`15:00–18:00 PM`)**: Try the pressed garments on one final time after minor tweaks, settle the remaining 50% balance, and have them packed in a breathable garment bag (or shipped home via **DHL / FedEx Express** for **`$35–$65 USD` per suit** if you are backpacking onward through Vietnam).

## Step-by-Step HowTo: Commissioning a Custom Wardrobe in Hoi An

### 1. First Step — Save Reference Photos & Visit Your Tailor Before `10:30 AM` on Day 1
Before arriving in Hoi An, save 4 to 5 clear front-and-back photos of the exact suit cut, dress neckline, or coat silhouette you love, or bring your favorite well-fitting shirt/trousers from home in your suitcase so the cutter can clone the block measurements. Head straight to **Yaly Couture**, **Bebe Tailor**, **A Dong Silk**, or **Two Ladies Tailor** on your first morning so the workshop has a full **48 to 72 hours** for three unhurried fittings.

### 2. Second Step — Perform the Burn Test & Specify Bemberg Lining + Horn Buttons
Select fabrics from the **`70%–100% Merino wool`**, **`pure washed linen`**, or **`mulberry silk`** bolts, verify the swatch with a quick lighter burn test, and ask the consultant to write **"Breathable Bemberg/Viscose Lining (`Vải lót thoáng khí`)"** and **"Natural Horn / Corozo / Mother-of-Pearl Buttons (`Cúc sừng / Cúc vỏ trai`)"** directly onto your order ticket instead of shiny plastic buttons.

### 3. Final Step — Wear Proper Shoes & Underwear to Every Fitting and Inspect Stitching
Bring the dress shoes or sandals you actually plan to wear with the garment to Fitting #1 and Fitting #2 so the trouser hem and dress length are pinned to the millimeter. At final pickup, inspect the buttonholes, pocket bartacks, and crotch seam stitching before paying the final balance.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How much does a custom tailored suit cost in Hoi An in 2026?
In 2026, a well-constructed **2-piece custom suit** at a reputable mid-range Hoi An atelier (**Bebe Tailor**, **A Dong Silk**, **Kimmy**, or **Two Ladies Tailor**) costs **`$160 to $290 USD` (`4,000,000 to 7,250,000 VND`)** using high-grade wool-cashmere blends, tropical wool, or pure linen. At Hoi An's luxury benchmark **Yaly Couture**, suits crafted from imported Italian or English `Super 120s–150s` Merino wool range from **`$240 to $500+ USD` (`6,000,000 to 12,500,000 VND`)**. Avoid `$85–$110 USD` market stalls if you want real wool and durable construction.

### How many days do you need in Hoi An to get a suit or dress made?
Plan for **at least 3 full days (`48 to 72 hours`)** in Hoi An so you can complete **3 separate fittings**: initial measurements on Day 1 morning, the basted skeleton fitting on Day 2 morning, the precision adjustment fitting on Day 2 evening or Day 3 morning, and final pickup on Day 3 afternoon. Simple linen shirts or summer dresses can be completed in **`24 to 36 hours`** (`2 fittings`).

### Should I tip my tailor or bargain on prices in Hoi An?
At top-tier fixed-price houses like **Yaly Couture**, **Bebe Tailor**, and **A Dong Silk**, fabric bolt prices are standardized, though ordering multiple garments (`e.g., 2 suits + 3 shirts`) routinely earns a polite **`5% to 10%` multi-item bundle discount** or a complimentary custom dress shirt/tie. At smaller family shops (`Two Ladies`, `Ba Ri`, or Cloth Market stalls), polite negotiation (`10%–15%` off initial quotes) is normal. If your fitting consultant and cutter deliver an exceptional fit across three sessions, a **`100,000 to 250,000 VND` (`$4–$10 USD`)** tip is warmly appreciated.

### Can Hoi An tailors ship my finished clothes home and keep my measurements on file?
Yes. All major Hoi An ateliers (**Yaly**, **Bebe**, **A Dong Silk**, **Kimmy**) ship worldwide via **DHL or FedEx International Express** (`5 to 8 business days` delivery, costing roughly **`$35–$65 USD` for the first kg** and `$12–$15 USD` per additional kg) and store your digital paper pattern and measurement profile for 5+ years so you can reorder shirts or suits via WhatsApp or email from home.
