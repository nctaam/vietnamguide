# Where to Stay in Soc Trang (2026): Best Hotels & Stays

Soc Trang stands at the crossroad of Khmer and Vietnamese communities in the lower Mekong Delta. The town sits **230 km** (**4 hours 30 minutes to 5 hours**) southwest of Ho Chi Minh City along **National Highway 1A (QL1A)** and **60 km** (**1 hour 15 minutes**) south of Can Tho. Modern sleeper coaches operate every **30 minutes** from Western Bus Station (`Bến xe Miền Tây`) in Ho Chi Minh City for **180,000–220,000 VND ($7.20–$8.80 USD)**. Most visitors pause briefly to explore Bat Pagoda before heading onward to Can Tho or Bac Lieu. Others stay overnight to catch the **08:00 AM** or **08:30 AM** high-speed ferries from **Tran De Port (`Cảng Trần Đề`)** across to Con Dao Island. High-speed boat tickets cost **390,000–420,000 VND ($15.60–$16.80 USD)** for the **2 hours 15 minutes** crossing.

Location decisions reflect transit goals. Travelers who stay in downtown Soc Trang enjoy historic Theravada Buddhist temples, night food stalls along the Maspero River, and central taxi dispatchers. Those who select a lodge **35 km** southeast at Tran De port eliminate early morning highway transfers before morning ferry departures.

> **Tran De Morning Ferry Connection Warning:** Early morning passenger ferries to Con Dao depart Tran De Port promptly at **08:00 AM** and **08:30 AM**. Check-in desks close **30 minutes** before departure. If you stay in downtown Soc Trang, arrange a taxi pickup by **06:15 AM** at the latest. The **35 km** drive down **Provincial Road 934 (DT934)** requires **45 to 55 minutes**. Alternatively, book a transit guest house in Tran De town the night before sailing.

## 1. Central Ward 1 & Ward 3 Riverfront (`Trung Tâm Phường 1 & Bờ Kè Maspero`)

Ward 1 and Ward 3 form the urban center of Soc Trang along the **Maspero River (`Sông Maspero`)**. Streets follow a clean grid centered around **Hai Ba Trung Street**, **Tran Hung Dao Boulevard**, and **30 Thang 4 Street**.

Staying in central Ward 1 places you within **300 meters** of the **Khmer Cultural Museum (`Bảo tàng Khmer Sóc Trăng`)** and the **Som Rong Pagoda (`Chùa Bôtum Vong Sa Som Rong`)** with its **73-meter** reclining Buddha statue.

In the morning, local cooks boil cauldrons of `Bún nước lèo Sóc Trăng` (fermented fish soup with roasted pork, **35,000–50,000 VND**) along **Dong Khoi Street**.

### Top Hotels in Ward 1 & Ward 3
* **Satraco Royal Hotel (`Khách sạn Satraco Royal`, 198 Hai Ba Trung)**: A **3-star** hotel with elevators, clean rooms, and indoor parking for motorbikes. Double rooms rent for **520,000–1,100,000 VND ($21–$44 USD)** including breakfast.
* **Que Toi Hotel (`Khách sạn Quê Tôi`, 278 Phu Loi / Ward 3)**: A mid-range hotel featuring family suites, air-conditioning, and an on-site garden restaurant. Nightly rates run **450,000–850,000 VND ($18–$34 USD)**.
* **Thanh Tra Hotel (`Khách sạn Thanh Trà`, 18 Tran Hung Dao)**: A central hotel offering elevator access, clean tiled floors, and prompt laundry service for **400,000–750,000 VND ($16–$30 USD)** per night.
* **Minh Chau Guest House Network (`Đường Hùng Vương & Lê Duẩn`)**: Budget guest houses catering to travelers. Private air-conditioned rooms cost **280,000–420,000 VND ($11–$17 USD)**.

## 2. Ward 6 & Le Hong Phong (Bat Pagoda Quarter)

Travelers seeking quiet nights head south to **Ward 6**. This quarter follows **Le Hong Phong Street**, leading to the historic **Bat Pagoda (`Chùa Dơi` / Mahatup Pagoda)**, founded in 1569.

The neighborhood stays noticeably quieter than the commercial riverside. Ancient trees shelter thousands of fruit bats hanging from high branches in temple courtyards. Buddhist vegetarian restaurants (`Quán cơm chay`) and iced coffee stalls line the street.

### Top Stays in Ward 6
* **Thien Truc Hotel (`Khách sạn Thiên Trúc`, 593 Le Hong Phong)**: Located **800 meters** from the Bat Pagoda entrance. The hotel offers courtyard parking, firm mattresses, and hot showers for **350,000–650,000 VND ($14–$26 USD)**.
* **Ngo Gia Guest House (`Nhà nghỉ Ngô Gia`, 282 Le Hong Phong)**: A modern property with tiled double rooms, fast Wi-Fi, and courteous hosts. Rooms cost **300,000–480,000 VND ($12–$19 USD)**.
* **Chua Doi Eco-Stay & Garden Cottages (`Khuôn viên Chùa Dơi`)**: Family-owned bungalows surrounded by fruit trees. Nightly stays average **400,000–750,000 VND ($16–$30 USD)**.

Rent a scooter from your hotel reception for **120,000–160,000 VND ($4.80–$6.40 USD)** per day. You can easily ride between **Chua Chen Kieu (`Chùa Chén Kiểu` / Salon Pagoda)**, located **12 km** south in My Xuyen, and downtown food stalls.

## 3. Tran De Port & Coastal Ferry Terminal (`Thị Trấn Mỏ Ó & Cảng Trần Đề`)

Catching an early boat to Con Dao requires careful transit planning. **Tran De Port** sits **35 km** southeast of central Soc Trang, where the Hau River meets the East Sea.

High-speed catamarans operated by **Superdong** and **Phu Quoc Express** depart between **08:00 AM and 09:00 AM**. Heavy morning mist or bridge repairs along DT934 can delay taxis. Staying overnight in Tran De town eliminates all departure anxiety. You can wake up at **07:00 AM**, eat breakfast by the docks, and walk straight onto the boat pier.

### Top Stays in Tran De Town
* **Ngoc Thu Tran De Hotel (`Khách sạn Ngọc Thu Trần Đề`, Nam Song Hau Road)**: Located **1.2 km** from the ferry terminal. Air-conditioned double rooms cost **350,000–650,000 VND ($14–$26 USD)** per night, featuring elevator access and secure luggage storage while visiting Con Dao.
* **Ferry Port Mini-Hotels (`Khu vực Cầu Cảng Trần Đề`)**: No-frills guest houses sit within **300 meters** of ticket kiosks along **Kenh Dao Street**. Clean rooms cost **250,000–400,000 VND ($10–$16 USD)**.
* **Tran De Seafood Homestays (`Thị trấn Trần Đề`)**: Modest family properties near the commercial fishing harbor. Guests enjoy fresh seafood dinners and air-conditioned rooms for **300,000–500,000 VND ($12–$20 USD)**.

Most ferry port hotels provide vehicle parking for **30,000–50,000 VND ($1.20–$2.00 USD)** per day.

## 4. Transit Terminals & Regional Logistics

Connecting to Soc Trang and the coastal ferry terminal involves reliable highway and river transit routes:

* **Soc Trang Interprovincial Bus Station (`Bến xe Sóc Trăng`)**: Located at **38 Le Duan Street**, **2 km** southwest of downtown. **Phuong Trang (FUTA Bus Lines)** operates sleeper coaches from Saigon every **30 to 45 minutes** for **180,000–220,000 VND ($7.20–$8.80 USD)** with free local shuttle minibuses.
* **Can Tho to Soc Trang Connections**: Express minibuses depart Can Tho Central Bus Station every **20 minutes**. The **60 km** journey down QL1A takes **1 hour 15 minutes** and costs **60,000–80,000 VND ($2.40–$3.20 USD)**.
* **Soc Trang to Tran De Port Shuttle**: Dedicated shuttle minibuses run between Soc Trang city hotels and Tran De docks from **06:00 to 07:00 AM**. Shuttle fares run **40,000–50,000 VND ($1.60–$2.00 USD)**.
* **Con Dao High-Speed Ferries**: **Superdong** and **Phu Quoc Express** operate vessels from Tran De to Ben Dam Port on Con Dao. Crossing tickets run **390,000–420,000 VND ($15.60–$16.80 USD)** for economy seats. Travel time is **2 hours 15 minutes**.
* **Local Metered Taxis**: **Mai Linh Taxi (`0299 368 6868`)** and **Soc Trang Taxi (`0299 383 8383`)** provide metered transportation. Flag drops cost **11,000 VND**, then **15,000 VND** per km. A private taxi to Tran De runs **420,000–500,000 VND ($17–$20 USD)**.

## 5. Seasonal Pricing Dynamics & Festival Booking Rules

Soc Trang hotel prices remain stable through most of the year, but spike sharply during major regional festivals.

1. **Ooc Om Bok Festival & Ngo Boat Race (`Lễ hội Óoc Om Bók & Đua Ghe Ngo`)**: Takes place on the **14th and 15th days of the 10th lunar month** (usually November). Over **200,000 visitors** line both banks of the Maspero River for longboat races. Hotel rooms across Ward 1, Ward 3, and Ward 6 sell out months ahead. Rates surge by **80% to 150%**, with hotels demanding full deposits. Book at least **60 days** in advance.
2. **Chon Chnam Thmay (Khmer New Year, mid-April)**: Celebrates the traditional Khmer new year across local temples. Som Rong, Mahatup, and Chen Kieu pagodas host water blessings, driving high weekend demand.
3. **Monsoon vs Dry Season Dynamics**: The dry season spans **December through April**, delivering calm seas for the Tran De–Con Dao ferry crossing and dry roads for rural cycling. The monsoon runs from **May through November**, bringing downpours around **14:30 to 16:30**. Ferry sailings occasionally face cancellations during July squalls. Room rates drop by **20%** on rainy weekdays.

## 6. Practical Safety & Scam Prevention

* **Unauthorized Ferry Ticket Scalpers**: Street touts outside Tran De port gates claim boats are sold out, attempting to sell marked-up tickets for **550,000 VND**. Never buy from street scalpers. High-speed boat operators require your passport or citizen ID card (`CCCD`) printed directly on the boarding pass. Purchase tickets only at authorized ticket windows or verified online portals for the official **390,000–420,000 VND** price.
* **Temple Donation Solicitations**: When visiting Bat Pagoda or Som Rong Pagoda, disregard unauthorized touts selling small sticks of incense for **50,000 VND** at courtyard gates. Monasteries charge zero entrance fees. Place donations directly into locked charity boxes inside prayer halls.
* **Pia Cake Authenticity**: Soc Trang is famed for flaky mung bean and durian pastry (`Bánh Pía`). Counterfeit boxes packed with artificial durian essence crowd random highway roadside stalls. Buy fresh genuine pastries from established flagships like **Tan Hue Vien (`Tân Huê Viên`)** along QL1A, where sealed boxes range from **65,000 to 140,000 VND**.
* **Canal Night Driving Precautions**: Rural roads toward My Xuyen and Tran De feature narrow concrete bridges lacking guardrails. Avoid driving rented motorbikes along unlit village canals after **20:00**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it better to stay in Soc Trang city or near Tran De Port?
Stay in **Soc Trang city (Ward 1 or Ward 3)** to tour historic Khmer pagodas, visit the night food market along the Maspero River, and dine at local noodle shops. Base yourself in **Tran De Port** only if boarding the **08:00 AM** high-speed ferry to Con Dao without a rushed pre-dawn highway transfer.

### How much do hotels in Soc Trang cost per night?
Standard air-conditioned private rooms in clean local guest houses cost between **280,000 and 420,000 VND ($11–$17 USD)** per night. Reputable **3-star** hotels like Satraco Royal or Que Toi Hotel charge between **450,000 and 1,100,000 VND ($18–$44 USD)**, which includes air-conditioning, elevator access, and daily breakfast.

### How do I travel from Soc Trang city center to Tran De Port?
A private metered taxi via Mai Linh costs **420,000–500,000 VND ($17–$20 USD)** and takes **45 to 55 minutes** along DT934. Alternatively, book a seat on the ferry shuttle minibus for **40,000–50,000 VND ($1.60–$2.00 USD)** per person, departing city hotels between **06:00 AM and 06:45 AM**.

### When is the best time to visit Soc Trang for festivals?
The premier time is November during the **Ooc Om Bok Festival**, when colorful Khmer dragon boats compete along the Maspero River. For quiet temple visits and calm ocean crossings to Con Dao, plan your stay between **January and April** during the dry season.
