# Where to Stay in Cao Bang (2026): City vs Waterfall

Planning where to stay in **Cao Bang** hinges on a single geographic reality inside the **Non Nuoc Cao Bang UNESCO Global Geopark**: the provincial capital of **Cao Bang City** and the frontier valley of **Ban Gioc Waterfall (Thác Bản Giốc)** in **Trung Khanh District** sit **85 km apart**. Driving between them takes **2.0 to 2.5 hours** over the seven-tier limestone hairpins of **Ma Phuc Pass (Đèo Mã Phục)** along **QL3** and **DT206**. Travelers who book all their nights in Cao Bang City face a grueling **170 km round-trip day ride** that puts them at the waterfall after **10:30 AM**, right when tour buses arrive and midday glare flattens the cascades. A smarter route divides your nights between a staging hotel or motorbike hostel on the **Bang Giang River** in **Cao Bang City** (Night 1) and a traditional Tay stone stilt house in **Khuoi Ky Ancient Stone Village** just **2 km** from the falls (Night 2). That two-base split eliminates backtracking.

> **Upstream Hydroelectric Dam Release & Border Registration Rule:** Upstream dams on the **Quay Son River** hold back water overnight and release the main flow between **09:30 AM and 10:30 AM** daily. An overnight stay in **Khuoi Ky Village** or **Dam Thuy Commune** lets you enter **Ban Gioc Waterfall** (**45,000 VND** entrance ticket + **50,000 VND** bamboo raft) at **07:30–08:30 AM** when mist rises over empty travertine tiers, then watch the water volume double by **09:45 AM** before day-trippers arrive from Cao Bang City. Pack your original physical passport. Every hotel and homestay in **Trung Khanh** files your **border-belt temporary residence registration (Khai báo tạm trú vùng biên giới)** online at no extra charge. Unlike Ha Giang, **no separate $10 USD border permit is required** for foreign tourists staying at **Khuoi Ky** or **Ban Gioc**, provided you stay on public roads and avoid restricted military border markers.

## 1. Cao Bang City Core (Song Bang & Hop Giang Wards): The Transit & Loop Hub

Located **280 km** (**5.5 to 6.5 hours**) north of Hanoi along the **CT07** expressway and mountain highways **QL4A** and **QL3**, **Cao Bang City** straddles the confluence of the **Bang Giang River** and **Hien River**. Overnight VIP 24-cabin sleeper buses from Hanoi's My Dinh and Gia Lam stations (**350,000–450,000 VND** / **$14–$18 USD** one way, operated by **Thanh Ly**, **Bon Bon**, **Vinh Dung**, and **42**), along with daytime 9-seat limousine vans (**350,000–400,000 VND**), drop passengers directly in the city center. Because sleeper coaches pull in between **04:00 AM and 05:30 AM**, booking a city hotel or backpacker homestay with early-morning check-in is essential. Never ride mountain passes sleep-deprived.

### Riverfront Towers, Boutique Hotels & Motorbike Hostels
- **Muong Thanh Luxury Cao Bang (1,400,000–2,400,000 VND / $56–$95 USD):** Rising **22 stories** above **Kim Dong Street** in **Hop Giang Ward**, this is the only full-scale 5-star hotel in the province. Upper-floor river-view suites look straight across the **Bang Giang River**, with an indoor pool, a **06:00 AM** breakfast buffet, and secure basement motorcycle parking.
- **Mid-Range Boutique & Quiet Riverside Hotels (450,000–1,100,000 VND / $18–$44 USD):** **Max Boutique Hotel** (**750,000–1,100,000 VND**), **Jeanne Hotel** (**550,000–850,000 VND**), and **Sunny Hotel** (**450,000–700,000 VND**) provide elevators, high-pressure hot showers, and two-way inverter air conditioning. On Friday and Saturday nights, avoid rooms facing **Kim Dong Pedestrian Street (Phố đi bộ Kim Đồng)**: police erect road barriers from **19:30 to 23:00**, and outdoor loudspeaker music echoes off the facades. Book quiet riverside rooms along **Hien Giang Street** or **Vuon Cam Street** instead.
- **Loop Staging Homestays & Motorbike Rental Hubs (300,000–950,000 VND privates / 150,000–250,000 VND dorms):** **Primrose Homestay**, **Cao Bang Eco Homestay**, and **Hao's Homestay** welcome riders arriving at **04:30 AM**. They offer early-morning rest beds, free suitcase storage, loop map briefings, and well-maintained **110cc–150cc motorbikes** for **150,000–250,000 VND per day** (**$6–$10 USD**).

### City Bus Connections, ATMs & Local Specialties
Car-free travelers can rely on Cao Bang City's public bus network. Local **Bus #07** departs near the central market and **Muong Thanh Luxury** every **30 to 45 minutes** from **05:30 AM to 17:30**, covering the **85 km** route to **Nguom Ngao Cave** and **Ban Gioc Waterfall** in **2.5 hours** for **70,000 VND** (**$2.80 USD**). Heading northwest, **Bus #01** and **Bus #03** run **52 km** to the **Pac Bo Historical Site (Suối Lê-nin)** near the Chinese border (**45,000 VND** entrance ticket plus electric buggy). Withdraw cash from **Vietcombank**, **BIDV**, or **Agribank** ATMs in Hop Giang before leaving town. Fuel up on **Bánh cuốn trứng Cao Bằng** (steamed rice rolls in hot pork-bone broth with poached egg and sausage for **35,000–45,000 VND**) and **Phở vịt quay** (crispy roasted duck pho for **45,000–60,000 VND**).

## 2. Khuoi Ky Ancient Stone Village (Làng Đá Cổ Khuổi Ky): 400-Year-Old Tay Stone Houses

Situated along **DT206** in **Dam Thuy Commune (Trung Khanh District)**—just **2 km** west of **Ban Gioc Waterfall** and **1 km** from **Nguom Ngao Cave**—**Khuoi Ky Ancient Stone Village (Làng đá cổ Khuổi Ky)** is the premier overnight base in the eastern geopark. Founded during the Mac Dynasty over **400 years ago**, the village preserves **14 traditional two-story stone stilt houses (Nhà sàn đá)** built by the Tay ethnic community on limestone foundations along the **Khuoi Ky Stream**.

### Best Khuoi Ky Stone Homestays & Nightly Rates
- **Yen Nhi Stone Homestay, Khuoi Ky Eco Homestay, Lan's Homestay, Minh Phuong & Lang Da Homestay (400,000–1,100,000 VND / $16–$44 USD):** Upstairs in the timber loft beneath yin-yang roof tiles, individual padded floor mattresses with mosquito nets rent for **150,000–200,000 VND per person** (**$6–$8 USD**). Private stone-walled rooms and stream-view bungalows with en-suite hot showers run **400,000–1,100,000 VND**. Order a home-cooked Tay dinner by **16:00** (**150,000–200,000 VND/person**) featuring river fish, smoked sausage (**Lạp sườn**), wild **Rau dạ hiến** greens, and **Trung Khanh chestnuts (Hạt dẻ Trùng Khánh, 80,000–120,000 VND/kg)**.

### The 07:30 AM Waterfall & Cave Advantage
Sleeping in Khuoi Ky unlocks the quietest hours at the border. Ride **5 minutes** (**2 km**) to the **Ban Gioc Waterfall** gate by **07:30 AM** (**45,000 VND** entrance ticket + **50,000 VND** bamboo raft). You get the lower riverbank to yourself for two hours before the **09:30–10:30 AM Upstream Hydroelectric Dam Water Release (Xả nước thủy điện)** swells the cascades and before **10:30 AM** tour buses arrive from Cao Bang City. Afterward, climb to **Truc Lam Phat Tich Pagoda (0 VND entry)** for panoramic views over the **Quay Son River** border with China, then walk **1 km** from Khuoi Ky into **Nguom Ngao Cave (Động Ngườm Ngao)** (**45,000 VND** standard walkway or **195,000 VND** deep branch expedition).

## 3. Quay Son River & Ban Gioc Resort Zone (Dam Thuy Commune)

Travelers wanting hotel mattresses and riverfront lawns within **500m to 3 km** of the falls have two main options in **Dam Thuy**:

- **Saigon Ban Gioc Resort (1,300,000–2,400,000 VND / $52–$96 USD):** Set directly opposite the **Ban Gioc Waterfall** ticket gate (**500m** walk), this 4-star resort offers 60 hillside rooms facing the karst border peaks, buffet breakfast, and free border-belt passport registration (**Khai báo tạm trú vùng biên giới**).
- **Quay Son River & Yen Nhi Bungalows (800,000–1,400,000 VND / $32–$56 USD):** Located **1.5–3 km** upstream along the turquoise **Quay Son River**, properties like **Lan Rung** and **Yen Nhi Riverside** offer quiet timber cabins beside bamboo waterwheels.

## 4. Phia Thap Incense Village & Quang Uyen Craft Belt (35 km East)

Immediately east of **Ma Phuc Pass** in **Quang Uyen (35 km from Cao Bang City)** lies a cluster of **Nung An** ethnic craft villages ideal for cultural cyclists:

- **Phia Thap Incense Village (Làng hương Phia Thắp) & Mr. Linh's Homestay (350,000–750,000 VND / $14–$30 USD private room; 150,000 VND dorm):** Families in **Phia Thap** dry natural bamboo incense across stone courtyards. Staying at **Mr. Linh's Homestay** connects you on a **6 km** walking loop to **Phuc Sen Blacksmith Village (Làng rèn Phúc Sen)** and **Lung Ri Roof-Tile Village (Làng ngói âm dương Lũng Rì)**.

## 5. Mountain God Eye (Tra Linh) & Bao Lac (Western Ha Giang Link)

- **Mountain God Eye (Núi Mắt Thần / Thung lũng Nà Ma, Tra Linh — 35–40 km from Cao Bang City):** Famous for a **50-meter** karst hole through **Phja Piot Mountain** above seasonal pastures near **Thang Hen Lake (30,000 VND ticket)**. Avoid wild valley floor tents during summer rains (**June–August**) when underground karst streams flood the basin; sleep on higher ground at **Thang Hen Eco-Resort (600,000–1,000,000 VND)** or visit on a morning ride from Cao Bang City.
- **Bao Lac (Thị trấn Bảo Lạc — 130 km West via Khau Coc Cha Pass):** Riders linking **Cao Bang** with **Meo Vac (Ha Giang)** tackle the **15-tier Khau Coc Cha Pass (Đèo Khau Cốc Chà)** along **QL34**. Break the **7-hour** mountain transit in **Bao Lac** at **Sunny Hotel Bao Lac** (**450,000–850,000 VND** / **$18–$34 USD**).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I stay in Cao Bang City or near Ban Gioc Waterfall in Khuoi Ky?
Split a 2-night trip between both hubs. Book **Night 1 in Cao Bang City** to recover from the **04:30 AM** Hanoi sleeper bus, rent your **110cc–150cc motorbike (150,000–250,000 VND/day)**, and visit **Pac Bo Historical Site (52 km)**. Reserve **Night 2 in Khuoi Ky Stone Village (83 km east)** to enter **Ban Gioc Waterfall** at **07:30 AM** ahead of the **09:30 AM** dam water release and tour buses.

### Do foreigners need a $10 border permit to stay in Khuoi Ky or Ban Gioc in 2026?
No separate paid border permit is required for tourists staying in **Cao Bang City**, **Khuoi Ky Village**, or **Ban Gioc Waterfall**. However, you **must carry your original physical passport** so your hotel or stone homestay can submit your free **border-belt temporary residence registration (Khai báo tạm trú vùng biên giới)** upon check-in.

### Can I reach Khuoi Ky homestays and Ban Gioc Waterfall without a motorbike?
Yes. Board local **Bus #07** in **Cao Bang City** (runs every **30–45 minutes** from **05:30 to 17:30**, **70,000 VND** ticket, **2.5 hours**). Ask the conductor to drop you at the **Khuoi Ky Village** stone arch on **DT206** or at the **Ban Gioc Waterfall** gate **2 km** further east.
