# Where to Stay in Bac Lieu (2026): Best Hotels & Stays

Bac Lieu balances Southern Delta heritage with green energy infrastructure. Travelers reach the provincial capital along **National Highway 1A (QL1A)**, positioned **280 km** (**5 hours 30 minutes to 6 hours**) southwest of Ho Chi Minh City and **110 km** (**2 hours 15 minutes**) south of Can Tho. Modern sleeper coaches operated by **Phuong Trang (FUTA Bus Lines)** run departures every **45 minutes** from Western Bus Station (`Bến xe Miền Tây`) in Ho Chi Minh City for **220,000–260,000 VND ($8.80–$10.40 USD)**. Most regional travelers treat the town as a quick transit pause before driving to Ca Mau. That schedule misses the quiet charm of the city center at dusk, when the river air cools down and noodle carts open along **Tran Phu Street**.

Downtown hotel rooms cluster within walking distance of the historic **Bac Lieu Prince Mansion (`Nhà Công tử Bạc Liêu`)**. Coastal resorts sit **8 km to 14 km** further southeast in **Nha Mat Ward (`Phường Nhà Mát`)**, facing the mudflats and offshore wind turbines.

> **Coastal Wind Turbine Access Alert:** Do not rely on walking between rural seaside properties and city restaurants after sunset. The coastal access road along **Cao Van Lau Street** lacks street lighting across rural canal sections. Ride-hailing cars via Grab become scarce after **20:00** in Nha Mat. Travelers without a rented motorbike or a negotiated taxi driver must dine at their coastal resort or return to the urban core before dark.

## 1. Ward 3 & Historic Riverside (`Phường 3 & Bờ Kè Bạc Liêu`)

Ward 3 forms the historic heart of Bac Lieu. The neighborhood spans the northern bank of the Bac Lieu River along **Dien Bien Phu Street**, **Hai Ba Trung Street**, and **Tran Phu Street**. Staying here puts you on foot within **200 to 500 meters** of the city's celebrated architectural landmark: the **Bac Lieu Prince Mansion (`Dinh thự Công tử Bạc Liêu`)**, constructed in 1919 with imported French tiles and ironwork.

Morning street life moves at an unhurried pace. Street vendors set up steamers of duck curry vermicelli (`Bún cà ri vịt`, **40,000–55,000 VND**) and spicy beef soup (`Bún bò cay Bạc Liêu`, **45,000–60,000 VND**) along **Phan Ngoc Hien Street** at **06:30 AM**.

### Top Hotels in Ward 3
* **Bac Lieu Prince Hotel (`Khách sạn Công tử Bạc Liêu`, 13 Dien Bien Phu)**: Situated inside the heritage grounds, this hotel offers classic rooms with dark wood furnishings. Standard doubles run **650,000–1,150,000 VND ($26–$46 USD)** per night.
* **Sai Gon Bac Lieu Hotel (`04-06 Hoàng Văn Thụ`)**: A dependable **3-star** property with elevator service, air-conditioning, and private parking. Rates average **600,000–1,050,000 VND ($24–$42 USD)** including breakfast.
* **Chi Lang Guest House Network (`Đường Chi Lăng & Hai Bà Trưng`)**: Clean mini-hotels line the blocks behind the central square. Air-conditioned double rooms cost **300,000–450,000 VND ($12–$18 USD)** per night.

## 2. Ward 7 & Tran Huynh Administrative Square (`Phường 7 & Quảng Trường Hùng Vương`)

Travelers arriving with personal motorbikes, rental SUVs, or large luggage often prefer **Ward 7**. The district sits **2 km** west of the older riverfront market. Roads here widen into four-lane boulevards lined with tamarind trees and government complexes.

The anchor of this district is **Hung Vuong Square (`Quảng trường Hùng Vương`)**, which displays the giant **Dan Kim Monument** (a stylized traditional moon lute). The monument lights up every evening from **18:30 to 21:30**. Hotels in Ward 7 provide modern elevators, basement parking bays, and quiet rooms away from canal boat traffic.

### Top Hotels in Ward 7
* **Tran Vinh Hotel (`Khách sạn Trần Vinh`, 85-87 Hai Ba Trung)**: A **3-star** tower with spacious rooms, firm mattresses, and on-site sauna facilities. Rooms range from **550,000 to 1,200,000 VND ($22–$48 USD)**.
* **Dream Palace Hotel (`Khách sạn Dream Palace`, 278 Vo Thi Sau)**: Clean finishes, fast fiber-optic Wi-Fi, and family suites priced between **650,000 and 1,400,000 VND ($26–$56 USD)**.
* **New Palace Hotel (`Khu Đô thị Địa Ốc, Phường 1`)**: A contemporary resort-style hotel featuring an outdoor swimming pool. Double rooms run **750,000–1,350,000 VND ($30–$54 USD)**.

Motorbike rentals in Ward 7 cost **130,000–180,000 VND ($5.20–$7.20 USD)** per 24-hour cycle. Ask your front desk to arrange delivery of a scooter by **07:00 AM** to beat midday highway heat.

## 3. Nha Mat Coastal Belt & Wind Farm Road (`Khu Du Lịch Nhà Mát & Đê Biển`)

Travelers seeking sea breezes and spiritual landmarks base themselves in **Nha Mat Ward**, located **8.5 km to 12 km** southeast of central Bac Lieu. The drive down **Cao Van Lau Street** takes roughly **20 minutes** on a scooter, passing duck farms and ancient longan orchards (`Vườn nhãn cổ Bạc Liêu`).

Nha Mat accommodates domestic pilgrims visiting the **Quan Am Nam Hai Buddhist Sanctuary (`Khu Quán Âm Nam Hải`)**, which features a **33-meter** seaside statue of the Goddess of Mercy facing the sea. Accommodation here consists of pilgrimage rest houses, seaside mini-hotels, and family bungalows.

### Top Stays in Nha Mat
* **Nha Mat Resort Hotel (`Khu du lịch Nhà Mát`)**: Positioned adjacent to the coastal recreation park. Family bungalows and private rooms rent for **500,000–900,000 VND ($20–$36 USD)**. Room units deliver effective air-conditioning.
* **Ho Nam Eco-Resort (`Khu Du lịch Sinh thái Hồ Nam`, Tran Phu extended)**: Located halfway between downtown and Nha Mat (**4 km** from center). This property surrounds a **12-hectare** freshwater lake with wooden stilt bungalows costing **600,000–1,100,000 VND ($24–$44 USD)**.
* **Bac Lieu Wind Farm Homestays (`Vĩnh Trạch Đông`)**: Small agricultural homestays situated along the coastal mangrove boundary. Guests sleep in timber rooms overlooking shrimp canals for **380,000–650,000 VND ($15–$26 USD)**.

Early birds staying along this coast gain a distinct logistical benefit. You can walk out to the **Bac Lieu Wind Power Plant pier (`Cánh đồng điện gió Bạc Liêu`)** at **06:00 AM** sharp. Entry tickets cost **30,000 VND ($1.20 USD)** per adult. Arriving at dawn guarantees soft morning light on the **62 offshore turbine towers**, free of afternoon tourist crowds.

## 4. Transit Terminals & Arrival Logistics

Connecting to Bac Lieu is straightforward via land transit:

* **Bac Lieu Interprovincial Bus Station (`Bến xe Bạc Liêu`)**: Situated on **National Highway 1A**, approximately **3.5 km** northwest of the old market. FUTA Bus Lines operates free shuttle minibuses (`Xe trung chuyển`) delivering passengers straight to hotel doors within Ward 1, Ward 3, and Ward 7.
* **Direct Coach Options from Saigon**: Sleeper coaches operated by **Phuong Trang**, **Tuan Hiep**, and **Tri Nhan** depart Ho Chi Minh City regularly. Travel time takes **5 hours 30 minutes to 6 hours**. Limousine 22-cabin sleeper tickets cost **290,000–340,000 VND ($11.60–$13.60 USD)**.
* **Connecting to Ca Mau & Can Tho**: Regional buses to Ca Mau (**65 km**, **1 hour 30 minutes**) leave every **20 minutes** for **60,000–80,000 VND**. Vans north to Can Tho (**110 km**, **2 hours 15 minutes**) run continuously for **110,000–130,000 VND**.
* **Local Metered Taxis**: **Mai Linh Taxi (`0291 385 8585`)** and **Bac Lieu Taxi (`0291 392 9292`)** operate metered fleets. Base flag drops start at **12,000 VND**, followed by **15,500 VND** per km.

## 5. Seasonal Pricing & Festival Booking Rules

Room inventory in Bac Lieu remains readily available during normal weekdays. However, rates double and rooms sell out weeks in advance during major regional festivals.

1. **Quan Am Nam Hai Festival (`Lễ hội Quán Âm Nam Hải`)**: Held from the **22nd to the 24th day of the 3rd lunar month** (usually April). Over **100,000 pilgrims** converge on Nha Mat Ward. City center and coastal hotels impose **50% to 100% price surcharges**, and properties require full prepayment. Book at least **45 days** ahead if visiting during this lunar window.
2. **Prince Memorial Days (November / Lunar October)**: Celebrates the legacy of Don Ka Tai Tu folk music and the historical Prince heritage. Central rooms on Tran Phu and Dien Bien Phu fill with cultural delegations.
3. **Monsoon vs Dry Season Dynamics**: The dry season runs from **December through April**, offering dry paved roads and sunshine for motorbike outings. The rainy monsoon spans **May through November**, characterized by **45-minute** downpours around **14:00 to 16:00**. Hotel rates during monsoon months drop by **15% to 25%**.

## 6. Practical Safety & Scam Prevention

* **Unregistered Scooter Guides at Wind Farm Gates**: Unofficial motorbike operators loiter outside the wind farm entrance ticket booths offering shortcut rides to photo spots for **100,000 VND**. Decline firmly. The authorized concrete walking bridge built over the mudflats costs just **30,000 VND** and offers superior photography angles.
* **Seafood Weighing Discrepancies**: When ordering mud crab (`Cua biển Bạc Liêu`) or giant river prawns (`Tôm càng xanh`) at seafood shacks along Nha Mat beach, insist that the vendor re-weigh the live seafood without heavy water-soaked twine. Stalls use thick wet ropes weighing up to **300 grams** per kilogram of crab. Expect fair crab prices around **350,000–550,000 VND** per kg.
* **Tap Water Caution**: Tap water in coastal districts carries slight mineral salinity due to mangrove groundwater infiltration. Always use complimentary sealed bottled water for drinking and brushing teeth.
* **Canal Mosquito Protection**: Stays within rural homestays require insect repellent containing at least **15% DEET**, particularly between **17:30 and 19:30** as darkness falls over orchard ditches.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it better to stay in central Bac Lieu or along the coast in Nha Mat?
City center hotels in **Ward 3** provide the best base for most travelers. Downtown lodging puts you within easy walking distance of historical colonial mansions, clean cafes, and evening food rows along the river. Choose Nha Mat only if your itinerary focuses primarily on early morning Buddhist rituals at Quan Am Nam Hai or dawn photography at the wind farm turbine pier.

### How much should I budget for accommodation in Bac Lieu per night?
Standard air-conditioned private rooms in clean local guest houses cost between **300,000 and 450,000 VND ($12–$18 USD)** per night. Reputable **3-star** hotels like Sai Gon Bac Lieu or Tran Vinh Hotel charge **550,000 to 1,050,000 VND ($22–$42 USD)**, which normally includes air-conditioning, elevator access, and daily breakfast.

### Can I rent a scooter directly from my hotel in Bac Lieu?
Yes. Almost all front desks in Ward 3 and Ward 7 arrange scooter rentals through reliable local bike shops for **130,000–180,000 VND ($5.20–$7.20 USD)** per day. Inspect brakes, tire tread, and helmet straps before pulling away from the hotel entrance. Fuel up at Petrolimex stations along Tran Phu for **23,000–25,000 VND** per liter.

### How do I travel between Bac Lieu and Ca Mau?
Local air-conditioned shuttle buses depart from **Bac Lieu Bus Station** every **20 minutes** between **05:00 AM and 18:00**. The **65 km** trip along National Highway 1A takes **1 hour 30 minutes** and costs **60,000–80,000 VND ($2.40–$3.20 USD)** per person. Alternatively, a metered taxi journey between the two city centers costs approximately **850,000–950,000 VND ($34–$38 USD)**.
