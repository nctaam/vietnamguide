# Mekong Delta Floating Markets: Cai Rang & Cai Be

Spanning the southern bank of the Hau River (*Sông Hậu*, the southern distributary of the Mekong River), [Can Tho](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-can-tho/) (*Cần Thơ*) serves as the commercial, culinary, and transport capital of Vietnam's [Mekong Delta](/destinations/mekong-delta-travel-guide/). Situated 170 kilometers southwest of [Ho Chi Minh City](/destinations/ho-chi-minh-city-travel-guide/) (now a 2-hour and 45-minute drive via the CT01 Trung Luong–My Thuan–Can Tho Expressway), this river metropolis preserves the delta's flagship wholesale water exchange: **Cai Rang Floating Market** (*Chợ nổi Cái Răng*). Closer to Saigon at the 110-kilometer mark in Tien Giang province lies **Cai Be** (*Cái Bè*), where traditional river trade has shifted toward small-canal sampan excursions and orchard cycling on Tan Phong Islet.

Unlike stationary wholesale markets on dry land, Cai Rang functions as a floating agricultural exchange where skippers from across An Giang, Dong Thap, Vinh Long, and Soc Trang moor 15-ton to 30-ton wooden barges (*ghe bầu*) stacked with pineapples, watermelons, sweet potatoes, pumpkins, and Ri6 durians. Beyond the morning market, a 48-hour delta itinerary links artisanal rice-noodle workshops, community eco-tourism on **Con Son Islet** (*Cồn Sơn*), 19th-century Franco-Vietnamese merchant estates, and the evening promenade along **Ninh Kieu Wharf** (*Bến Ninh Kiều*).

This 2026 field guide provides verified boat charter tariffs at Ninh Kieu and An Binh piers, head-to-head comparisons of the five Mekong floating markets, dawn departure schedules, river-boat breakfast prices, and highway transit logistics.

### The Bamboo Barter Mechanism: The Cay Beo (Cây Bẹo)
Because diesel engine noise and river chop make shouting across 80 meters of water impractical, wholesale barges advertise their cargo visually using the **cay beo** (*cây bẹo*), an upright bamboo pole lashed to the bow 4 meters to 6 meters above the deck. River commerce follows four traditional rules known as *"4 treo"* (the four hanging customs):
1. **Treo gì bán nấy (Hang what you sell):** A farmer hoists three pineapples, a winter melon, or five sweet potatoes at the top of the bamboo pole so retail buyers scanning the horizon from 300 meters away know exactly which bulk crop that barge carries.
2. **Treo mà không bán (Hang what you do not sell):** Shirts, sarongs, and children's clothes hanging on lines across the stern cabin roof indicate that the wooden barge is the permanent floating home of a multi-generational river family; household laundry is never for sale.
3. **Không treo mà bán (Do not hang what you sell):** Nimble 6-meter wooden rowboats and longtail skiffs weaving between the big barges do not mount a bamboo pole. Instead, their steaming stockpots, rattling porcelain spoons, and hand-painted signs announce freshly cooked breakfast noodles (*hủ tiếu*), iced coffee (*cà phê sữa đá*), and coconut water.
4. **Treo cái này bán cái khác (Hang one item to sell the vessel itself):** When a boat owner ties a bundle of dry thatch grass or a woven coconut-leaf mat (*tấm lá dừa*) to the top of the bamboo pole, buyers understand that the wooden barge itself is for sale.

### How to Charter a Boat: Ninh Kieu Wharf vs An Binh Market Pier
Independent travelers in 2026 can choose between two departure piers depending on budget and boat preference:
* **Option 1: Ninh Kieu Wharf (*Bến Ninh Kiều*, Hai Ba Trung Street, Tan An Ward):** Walking straight from downtown hotels to the municipal pier at 05:00 AM is the most convenient option. Official ticket kiosks sell seats on shared 15-seat to 25-seat covered tourist boats for 80,000 VND to 120,000 VND ($3.20 to $4.80 USD) per person for a 2-hour market cruise. If you want an intimate wooden sampan (*thuyền gỗ nhỏ*) that can duck into narrow 4-meter-wide fruit canals where big steel boats cannot fit, charter a private boat for 1 to 4 passengers: a 2.5-hour market-only charter costs 400,000 VND to 500,000 VND ($16.00 to $20.00 USD) per boat, while the full 3.5-hour to 4-hour circuit (Cai Rang Market + Chin Cua Noodle Factory + Lung Cot Cau small canal + Ba Cong Fruit Orchard) costs **600,000 VND to 800,000 VND ($24.00 to $32.00 USD) per boat**.
* **Option 2: An Binh Market Pier (*Bến tàu Chợ An Bình*, 5.5 km southwest of Ninh Kieu):** To skip the 30-minute open-river commute from downtown and save money, take a 12-minute GrabCar at 05:15 AM from Ninh Kieu to **An Binh Market** for 65,000 VND to 85,000 VND ($2.60 to $3.40 USD). Because An Binh sits directly beside the floating market, hiring a private wooden sampan with a local female rower for 2.5 hours costs just **300,000 VND to 400,000 VND ($12.00 to $16.00 USD) for the entire boat**.

> [!NOTE]
> Arrive at the pier between **05:00 AM and 05:30 AM**. Boarding before sunrise puts you in the middle of Cai Rang at 05:45 AM when wholesale fruit transfers and breakfast sampans are at peak intensity. Travelers arriving after 08:00 AM see mostly fellow tourist boats, as farmers wrap up bulk trading before the 32°C morning heat sets in.

## Cai Be Floating Market & Tan Phong Islet: When to Pick Tien Giang Instead

If you only have a single day in Ho Chi Minh City and cannot stay overnight in Can Tho, **Cai Be** in Tien Giang province (110 km southwest of District 1, taking 1 hour 50 minutes via the CT01 Expressway) is the most rewarding day-trip alternative to crowded My Tho (70 km from Saigon).

While Cai Be's open-water wholesale barge count has shrunk to 15 to 30 vessels concentrated between 05:30 and 07:30 AM near the confluence of the Tien River, the surrounding water network around **Tan Phong Islet** (*Cù lao Tân Phong*) remains serene. Chartering a private wooden motorboat from **Cai Be Tourist Pier** (*Bến tàu du lịch Cái Bè*) costs 400,000 VND to 650,000 VND ($16.00 to $26.00 USD) for a 3-hour circuit. You will visit family cottages producing popped rice candy (*kẹo cốm*), coconut caramel, and rice paper, paddle for 30 minutes in a hand-rowed sampan (*xuồng ba lá*) beneath water-coconut palms (*dừa nước*, tip the rower 30,000 VND to 50,000 VND / $1.20 to $2.00 USD), and rent a bicycle for 40,000 VND ($1.60 USD) to ride 8 kilometers along shaded concrete footpaths through rambutan and jackfruit orchards on Tan Phong Islet.

### The Mechanics of a Floating Breakfast
As your sampan slows inside the market channel, a breakfast vendor uses a 2-meter bamboo gaff hook to latch her skiff against your gunwale. Balancing barefoot beside a charcoal-fired aluminum stockpot anchored in the bilge, she blanches rice noodles in 15 seconds, ladles boiling broth, and passes the ceramic bowl across the rails. Hold the bowl by its upper rim with your thumb and index finger so river wakes do not slosh the broth, pay in 10,000 VND to 50,000 VND banknotes, and hand the empty porcelain bowl and steel spoon back before the boats unhook.

### 1. Chin Cua Traditional Noodle Workshop (Lò Hủ Tiếu Chín Của)
Located 2 kilometers up the Rau Ram tributary off the Can Tho River (admission **0 VND**, open 06:00 to 16:00), Mr. Huynh Huu Hoai's family workshop produces 250 kilograms of traditional rice-and-tapioca noodles daily using natural coloring from magenta plant (*lá cẩm*), pandan leaf (*lá dứa*), and gac fruit (*gấc*). Visitors watch workers ladle liquid rice batter over a steamed cloth drum for 45 seconds, peel the translucent sheet onto woven bamboo drying racks for 3 hours of sun drying, and crank the sheets through a hand-turned cutter. Order a plate of **Pizza Hủ Tiếu** (crispy deep-fried rice noodles topped with braised pork, shredded coconut, roasted peanuts, and fried egg) for 35,000 VND to 50,000 VND ($1.40 to $2.00 USD).

### 2. Con Son Islet: Community-Based Eco-Tourism (Cồn Sơn)
Anchored in the Hau River in Binh Thuy district, 8 kilometers northwest of Ninh Kieu Wharf (a 15-minute taxi ride costing 75,000 VND to 95,000 VND / $3.00 to $3.80 USD), **Con Son Islet** is a 70-hectare alluvial sandbar run by a cooperative of 50 farming households:
* **Ferry Access:** Board the wooden passenger boat at **Co Bac Ferry Wharf** (*Bến đò Cô Bắc*, 158 Le Hong Phong Street). The 8-minute crossing runs every 15 minutes from 06:00 to 18:00 and costs **20,000 VND ($0.80 USD) round-trip**.
* **Flying Snakehead Fish (*Cá lóc bay*) & Fish Massage:** Walk 500 meters along shaded orchard dykes to Mr. Thanh Tam's or Mr. Tin's ponds (entry 30,000 VND / $1.20 USD), where thousands of snakehead fish leap 1.2 meters above the water surface to catch puffed rice pellets, and floating Hau River rafts host 4-kilogram red-tail catfish (*cá thác lác* and *cá chép*) that nibble your feet for 30,000 VND ($1.20 USD).
* **Southern Folk Cake Workshop (*Bánh dân gian Nam Bộ*):** Join local grandmothers in an open-air garden kitchen for 60 minutes to press and steam *bánh lá mít* (jackfruit-leaf rice cakes) and *bánh kẹp cuốn* (crispy coconut wafer rolls) over wood fires for 80,000 VND to 120,000 VND ($3.20 to $4.80 USD) per person.

### 3. Binh Thuy Ancient House (Nhà Cổ Bình Thủy)
Situated at **144 Bui Huu Nghia Street** (6 km northwest of downtown Can Tho; open 07:30 to 12:00 and 13:30 to 18:00, admission ticket **30,000 VND / $1.20 USD**), this five-bay merchant residence was constructed in **1870** (and expanded in 1904) by the Duong family. The exterior combines French colonial pastel arches, imported Louis XV floor tiles, and wrought-iron fences with a traditional southern Vietnamese ironwood (*gỗ lim*) interior inlaid with mother-of-pearl. French director Jean-Jacques Annaud filmed key interiors here for the 1992 movie adaptation of Marguerite Duras's novel *The Lover* (*L'Amant*).

* **05:00 to 05:15 AM**: Meet your private wooden sampan skipper at Ninh Kieu Wharf (pre-booked for 650,000 VND / $26.00 USD for the 4-hour boat loop) or at An Binh Pier (400,000 VND / $16.00 USD).
* **05:45 to 07:30 AM**: Cruise through **Cai Rang Floating Market** as the sun rises over the Can Tho Bridge. Pull alongside a breakfast skiff for a 45,000 VND ($1.80 USD) bowl of *Hủ tiếu* and a 20,000 VND ($0.80 USD) iced milk coffee, then climb aboard a wholesale pineapple barge to eat fresh spiral-cut pineapple for 20,000 VND ($0.80 USD).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Cai Rang Floating Market still worth visiting in 2026?
Yes, provided you arrive between `05:30 AM` and `07:00 AM` and hire a small private wooden sampan (`350,000 VND` to `650,000 VND` / `$14.00` to `$26.00 USD` per boat) rather than a large 30-seat tour boat. While highway expansion has reduced barge numbers compared to the 1990s, Cai Rang still hosts `150` to `250` working wholesale produce boats every morning, and combining the market with a `90-minute` detour through narrow backwaters and the Chin Cua noodle workshop creates a memorable `4-hour` river journey.

### Can you visit Cai Rang Floating Market on a day trip from Ho Chi Minh City?
It is strongly discouraged. Because Cai Rang peaks between `05:30 AM` and `07:00 AM`, a day trip from Ho Chi Minh City (`170 km` away, `2 hours 45 minutes` via the `CT01` Expressway) requires departing District 1 at `02:30 AM` by private car (`2,200,000 VND` / `$88.00 USD` round-trip). Standard group day tours leaving Saigon at `07:30 AM` only reach Can Tho at `10:30 AM`, when the floating market is empty. Always sleep 1 night in Can Tho (`450,000 VND` to `1,200,000 VND` / `$18.00` to `$48.00 USD` for a riverside hotel) to board at `05:00 AM` rested.

### What is the best month of the year to visit the Mekong Delta floating markets?
The Mekong Delta operates year-round with three distinct seasonal perks:
* **December through April (Dry Season):** Zero rain, `28°C` to `32°C` mornings, and pre-Tet flower barges in late January and February.
* **May through August (Peak Fruit Harvest):** Afternoon showers (`45 minutes` at `15:00`), but orchards overflow with ripe mangosteen, rambutan, longan, and Ri6 durian (`85,000 VND` / `$3.40 USD` per kg).
* **September through November (Floating Water Season / *Mùa nước nổi*):** High Mekong water levels flood lotus fields and cajuput forests (such as **Tra Su Cajuput Forest** in An Giang, `115 km` from Can Tho, boat ticket `200,000 VND` / `$8.00 USD`), bringing seasonal dishes like *Lẩu cá linh bông điên điển* (young mud carp hotpot with yellow sesbania flowers, `220,000 VND` / `$8.80 USD`).

### Are life jackets mandatory on Cai Rang floating market boats?
Yes. Under Can Tho Waterway Traffic Police regulations, every passenger boarding at Ninh Kieu Wharf or An Binh Pier must wear a fastened orange flotation vest while navigating the main Can Tho River channel (violation fines start at `1,000,000 VND` / `$40.00 USD` for the skipper). You may unbuckle the vest once your sampan turns into calm, shallow `1.5-meter` fruit canals.
