# Where to Stay in Ha Giang (2026): Loop Bases & Stays

Booking accommodation in Ha Giang requires planning a multi-stop mountain route rather than picking a single hotel. Unlike static resort towns, the **350 km Ha Giang Loop** along highways **QL4C** and **DT176** demands a sequence of overnight bases spaced **45 km to 110 km apart** so you never ride blind cliffside hairpins after sunset at 17:30. Most riders and Easy Rider pillion passengers split their trip into four distinct stages: a pre-loop staging night at **km 0 in Ha Giang City (or Tha Village)**, a plateau stop in **Dong Van (km 145) or Lo Lo Chai**, a canyon stay in **Meo Vac (km 170)**, and a final valley night in **Du Gia (km 245)**. Choosing the wrong village in winter can leave you shivering inside an unheated concrete box at 4°C, while picking a central courtyard hostel in Pa Vi can trap you next to booming karaoke speakers until 23:00.

> **04:00 AM Sleeper Bus & Border Permit Rule:** Overnight VIP cabin buses from Hanoi (Bang Phan, Quang Nghi, Cau Me — 6 hours, 350,000–450,000 VND) drop passengers in Ha Giang City between 03:30 AM and 04:30 AM. Book your Loop Day 1 motorbike rental or tour hostel in advance so you can claim a **free early-arrival dorm nap bed (04:00–07:30 AM)**, a hot shower, and free large-backpack storage. Every foreign national riding past Quan Ba also requires a **$10 USD (250,000 VND) Ha Giang Border Area Permit (Giấy phép vào khu vực biên giới)**, issued either at the **Ha Giang Immigration Office (296A Tran Phu)** or automatically by your first registered accommodation in Dong Van or Meo Vac.

## 1. Night 0: Ha Giang City (km 0 Staging) & Tha Village (Thôn Tha)

Every loop begins and ends in **Ha Giang City** along the Lo River (Sông Lô). Because sleeper buses from Hanoi's My Dinh Station or Old Quarter arrive in the pitch dark at 03:30–04:30 AM, your primary Day 0 priority is securing a base with a 24-hour reception, a rest bed until breakfast at 07:30 AM, and a secure luggage room where you can leave your 15–20 kg suitcase while carrying only a 10-liter drybag on the motorbike. Do not ride tired at dawn.

### Top City Rental Hostels, Stilt Villages & Riverside Hotels
- **Loop Staging Hostels with Early Rest Beds (150,000–250,000 VND dorms / 400,000–900,000 VND privates):** Operations such as **QT Motorbikes & Tours**, **Jasmine Hostel**, **Mama's Homestay**, **Hong Hao Hostel**, and **Bong Hostel** run dedicated transition hubs along Nguyen Trai and Tran Phu streets. If you rent a Honda XR150L or Blade 110cc (200,000–550,000 VND/day) or book an Easy Rider through them, they provide a complimentary dorm bunk for 3–4 hours of predawn sleep, pancake breakfast, route map briefings, and free locked baggage storage.
- **Tha Village (Thôn Tha) & Ha Thanh Village (450,000–1,100,000 VND / $18–$44 USD):** If you arrive on an afternoon limousine bus (16:00 arrival from Hanoi) and want a proper night's sleep before hitting QL4C, take a 70,000 VND taxi 5 km west of the city center to **Tha Village** or **Ha Thanh Village**. Here, ethnic Tay families host guests in palm-thatched wooden stilt houses (nhà sàn) surrounded by wet-rice paddies and karst streams (Cay's Homestay, YesHaGiang Lodge, Homestay Thon Tha).
- **Upscale City & Cliffside Resorts (2,200,000–12,000,000 VND / $88–$480 USD):** Travelers wanting high-thread-count mattresses and hot tubs after four dusty days on the road book **Four Points by Sheraton Ha Giang** or **Phoenix Hotel** (1,800,000–2,800,000 VND) on the Lo River bank. Fifteen kilometers outside town in Bac Me lies **P'apiu Resort** (10,000,000–15,000,000 VND), an ultra-private three-villa luxury compound with heated infinity pools and private butler service.

## 2. Night 1 (4D3N & 5D4N Riders): Quan Ba (Nam Dam) & Yen Minh (km 46–100)

Riders who leave Ha Giang City after 10:00 AM or stop frequently for photography at **Bac Sum Pass** and **Quan Ba Heaven's Gate (Cổng Trời Quản Bạ)** should not attempt to push all 145 km to Dong Van on Day 1. Attempting the final 45 km climb over Tham Ma Slope and Chin Khoanh Pass after 17:00 in thick mountain fog is a leading cause of two-wheel slides. Splitting Day 1 at Quan Ba or Yen Minh keeps your daily saddle time under four hours.

### Where to Sleep in Quan Ba & Yen Minh
- **Nam Dam Dao Clay Village (Thôn Nậm Đăm, km 48, 450,000–1,200,000 VND / $18–$48 USD):** Situated 3 km above Tam Son town near **Lung Khuy Cave (Hang Lùng Khúy)**, Nam Dam is an award-winning Red Dao (Dao Áo Dài) community where every house is built with 50 cm-thick yellow rammed-earth walls (nhà trình tường). Stay at **Toong Homestay**, **Nam Dam Homestay**, or **Dao Lodge** for private clay bungalows and evening herbal bath soaks (tắm lá thuốc người Dao, 120,000–150,000 VND).
- **Yen Minh Pine Valley (km 100, 500,000–1,100,000 VND / $20–$44 USD):** Halfway between Ha Giang City and Dong Van, Yen Minh sits in a warmer valley floor (600m elevation) framed by towering pine forests. **Bong Bang Homestay**, **Duong Thuong Valley View**, and **Ha Anh Homestay** offer quiet wooden cabins 2 hours closer to Dong Van for an easy Day 2 morning climb past the Vuong Family H'Mong King's Palace (Dinh Vua Mèo, 30,000 VND ticket).

## 3. Night 1 or 2: Dong Van Karst Plateau (km 145) & Lo Lo Chai (Lung Cu)

At **1,025 meters above sea level**, **Dong Van** is the commercial and historical heart of the UNESCO Global Geopark. Here you must choose between the stone street life of Dong Van town or the northernmost frontier village of **Lo Lo Chai (Thôn Lô Lô Chải)**, located 24 km further north at the base of the **Lung Cu Flagpole (Cột Cờ Lũng Cú, 40,000 VND ticket)**.

### Dong Van Old Quarter vs. Lo Lo Chai Village
- **Dong Van Old Quarter (Phố Cổ Đồng Văn, 500,000–1,400,000 VND / $20–$56 USD):** A two-street conservation zone of two-story 1920s stone-and-clay townhouses with yin-yang tiled roofs (ngói âm dương). Staying at **Ancient Town 29 Pho Co**, **Nha Co Homestay**, **Plum Homestay**, or **Lam Tung Hotel** puts you steps away from steamed rice rolls with egg soup (bánh cuốn trứng, 35,000–45,000 VND), drip coffee at **Café Phố Cổ**, black-chicken hotpot restaurants, and the **Sunday Morning Dong Van Market (05:30–10:00 AM)**.
- **Lo Lo Chai Village (Thôn Lô Lô Chải, 600,000–1,500,000 VND / $24–$60 USD):** Tucked directly beneath the dragon-crest mountain of Lung Cu right against the Chinese border fence, this Black Lo Lo (Lô Lô Đen) village preserves earthen-walled courtyard homes with dry-stacked stone fences. Book **Lo Lo Ancient House**, **Diem Hen Cua Cuk**, **Little Yen's Homestay**, or **Homie Homestay** at least 2–3 weeks ahead, and sip afternoon espresso at **Cực Bắc Café** (Northernmost Café) founded by a Japanese cultural conservationist.

## 4. Night 2: Meo Vac & Pa Vi H'Mong Cultural Village (km 170)

After crossing the **20 km Ma Pi Leng Pass (Đèo Mã Pì Lèng)** and taking a **120,000 VND boat cruise** (plus 30,000 VND electric shuttle) through **Tu San Canyon (Hẻm Tu Sản)** on the emerald **Nho Que River**, most riders descend into the valley of **Meo Vac** for Night 2. Because Dong Van and Meo Vac lie only 25 km apart, staying in Meo Vac allows you to linger on the Ma Pi Leng Skywalk until golden hour without backtracking.

### Navigating Pa Vi's Noise vs. Historic Clay Architecture
- **Pa Vi H'Mong Cultural Village (Làng Văn Hóa Pả Vi Hạ, 550,000–1,600,000 VND / $22–$64 USD):** Built in three large hexagonal clusters at the eastern foot of Ma Pi Leng (5 km from Meo Vac town), Pa Vi features traditional H'Mong timber frames and tiled roofs. **Critical Noise Warning:** The central hexagon hosts large 20–40 rider backpacker groups that blast outdoor speakers and toast corn wine (rượu ngô / "Happy Water") until 22:30–23:00. Independent travelers seeking sleep should book outer-ring properties like **Meo Vac Clay House** (1,100,000–1,600,000 VND) or **Mong Lodge**, which enforce strict quiet hours and offer private sauna rooms.
- **Auberge de Meo Vac (Chư Nhà Cổ, 1,400,000–2,800,000 VND / $56–$112 USD):** Located inside Meo Vac town near the Sunday cattle market grounds, this painstakingly restored 1930s H'Mong residence features 60 cm rammed-earth walls, hand-hewn sa-mu cedar columns, French-colonial brass fixtures, thick linen duvets, and quiet fireplace dining.

## 5. Night 3: Du Gia Valley (Xã Du Già, km 245 via DT176)

On the third afternoon of a 4D3N loop, riders turn south onto provincial road **DT176 through Mau Due (Mậu Duệ)** and drop off the arid limestone plateau into **Du Gia (Xã Du Già)**, a lush basin of terraced rice paddies, braided gravel rivers, and the **6 km trail to Du Gia Waterfall (Thác Bản Án)**. Stop here before returning to Ha Giang City.

### Stilt Homestays & Winter Heating Realities
- **Tay Stilt Homestays & Hillside Bungalows (500,000–1,300,000 VND / $20–$52 USD):** **Du Gia Panorama** perches on a ridge above the valley with panoramic sunrise balconies (800,000–1,300,000 VND), while valley-floor staples like **Du Gia Homestay**, **Chien's Lodge**, **Du Gia Field View**, and **Tuan's Homestay** (200,000 VND dorms / 500,000–750,000 VND privates) serve 100,000–150,000 VND family-style Tay dinners with crispy stream fish, braised pork belly, tofu in tomato sauce, and home-brewed rice wine.
- **Winter Heating Check (December–February):** Between December and February, night temperatures drop to **3°C–8°C in Dong Van and Meo Vac** and **8°C–12°C in Du Gia**. Because mountain homestays lack central insulation, always message hosts on WhatsApp or Zalo before booking to confirm the room provides **two-way inverter heating (điều hòa hai chiều), an electric mattress pad (đệm sưởi), or a ceramic space heater (máy sưởi)**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I book Ha Giang Loop homestays in advance or show up on the day?
From **September to November** (golden rice harvest and buckwheat flower festival season) and during Vietnamese public holidays, top-rated private rooms in **Lo Lo Chai**, **Nam Dam**, **Meo Vac Clay House**, and **Du Gia Panorama** sell out 2 to 4 weeks in advance. During shoulder months (May–August), you can book 24–48 hours ahead once you see the daily rain forecast.

### Where do I leave my big suitcase while riding the Ha Giang Loop?
Every major rental shop and staging hostel in **Ha Giang City (km 0)**—including **QT Motorbikes**, **Jasmine**, **Mama's**, and **Hong Hao**—maintains a free, locked ground-floor luggage room for loop guests. Pack 3–4 days of riding clothes into a 10L–20L waterproof backpack strapped to your rear rack and collect your main luggage when you return to Ha Giang City before boarding your evening bus to Hanoi, Sapa, or Ninh Binh.

### How does my homestay register my $10 USD (250,000 VND) Ha Giang Border Permit?
When you check into your first overnight stay inside the border belt (**Quan Ba, Yen Minh, Dong Van, Lo Lo Chai, or Meo Vac**), the receptionist photographs your passport and Vietnam e-Visa and submits your details to the commune police post (Công an xã), adding the statutory **250,000 VND ($10 USD) permit fee** directly to your room bill.
