# Phu Quoc Travel Guide: Beaches, Where to Stay & Costs (2026)

Resting in the warm waters of the Gulf of Thailand off the southwestern coast of Kien Giang Province, **Phu Quoc** is Vietnam's largest island, spanning 574 square kilometers (48 kilometers north to south and 25 kilometers east to west) across a spine of 603-meter forested ridges and 150 kilometers of sandy coastline. Situated 45 kilometers west of Ha Tien, 120 kilometers southwest of Rach Gia, and just 15 kilometers south of the Cambodian coast, the island pairs centuries-old anchovy fish sauce barrel houses (*nhà thùng*) and organic black pepper farms with five-star coastal resorts.

What separates Phu Quoc from mainland Vietnamese beach hubs is its statutory border regime. Under Vietnam Government Resolution 80/NQ-CP, foreign passport holders of all nationalities flying or sailing **directly** into Phu Quoc from an international port of origin receive a **30-day visa exemption** at 0 VND ($0.00 USD) fee, even if their nationality normally requires a $25.00 USD (625,000 VND) Vietnam E-Visa for mainland entry.

Geography and seasonal winds divide the island into four distinct operational zones. The central-west corridor around Duong Dong Town, Long Beach (*Bãi Trường*), and Ong Lang Beach anchors the independent dining scene and 15-minute airport transfers along Highway DT975. Twenty-five kilometers south at An Thoi Port, the 7,899.9-meter Hon Thom Cable Car and white-silica coves of Bai Sao and Bai Khem shelter swimmers when summer monsoon swells hit the west coast. Up north along Routes DT45 and DT973, 31,422 hectares of UNESCO-listed Phu Quoc National Park meet the red starfish shallows of Rach Vem.

> [!NOTE]
> **Strict 30-Day Phu Quoc Visa Exemption Rules**: The statutory 30-day visa waiver applies **only** if you fly directly into Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC) from abroad (such as Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur, Seoul, Taipei, or Singapore) or transit through Hanoi (HAN) / Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) on an international-to-domestic transfer where immigration clearance occurs at PQC. If you clear domestic immigration in Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City to spend even 1 day on the mainland, you must hold a standard 45-day bilateral exemption or a $25.00 USD (625,000 VND) 90-day Vietnam E-Visa. Your passport must have 6 months validity and a confirmed return ticket departing PQC within 30 days.

## The Definitive Beach Directory: Where to Swim & Stay by Season

Phu Quoc's 150-kilometer shoreline wraps around two opposing wind regimes. From November to April, prevailing northeast breezes leave the 20-kilometer western coast (Long Beach and Ong Lang) as smooth as glass. From May to October, southwest monsoon winds push 1.5-meter surf onto Long Beach while leaving southeastern **Bai Sao** and **Bai Khem** calm and clear.

### 1. Long Beach (*Bãi Trường*) & Duong Dong Corridor
Stretching 20 kilometers from Dinh Cau Rock in Duong Dong down past Phu Quoc Airport (PQC), Long Beach is the island's most practical base. The northern 5-kilometer strip along Tran Hung Dao Street (2 kilometers to 6 kilometers south of Duong Dong Night Market) packs independent cafes, dive shops, mini-marts, and beachfront bungalows priced from 550,000 VND ($22.00 USD) to 1,800,000 VND ($72.00 USD) per night. Sunsets between 17:30 and 18:15 drop directly into the Gulf of Thailand.

### 2. Ong Lang Beach (*Bãi Ông Lang*)
Located 8 kilometers (15 minutes by scooter or a 130,000 VND / $5.20 USD Grab ride) north of Duong Dong via Cua Can road (DT45), Ong Lang replaces high-rise concrete with shaded lanes winding 800 meters through cashew and mango groves to small sandy bays. Mid-range garden resorts here charge 850,000 VND ($34.00 USD) to 2,500,000 VND ($100.00 USD) per night, offering snorkel-friendly rocky headlands and relaxed beachfront wood-fired pizzerias and seafood grills.

### 3. Sao Beach (*Bãi Sao*) & Khem Beach (*Bãi Khem*)
Positioned 25 kilometers and 27 kilometers southeast of Duong Dong along Highway DT46 (35 minutes by motorbike or 320,000 VND / $12.80 USD by taxi), Bai Sao and Bai Khem boast the whitest quartz-silica sand on the island. Public entry to Bai Sao is 0 VND ($0.00 USD); beachfront restaurants rent padded sunbeds with umbrellas for 80,000 VND ($3.20 USD) to 100,000 VND ($4.00 USD), kayaks for 150,000 VND ($6.00 USD) per hour, and freshwater showers for 20,000 VND ($0.80 USD). Arrive before 09:30 to photograph the 7-kilometer curve before day-trip coaches arrive at 10:30.

### 4. Rach Vem Starfish Beach (*Làng Chài Rạch Vẹm*)
Twenty-five kilometers north of Duong Dong (45 minutes via Route DT973), Rach Vem is a working fishing hamlet of 180 households where red starfish (*Protoreaster nodosus*) gather in 30-centimeter-deep crystal shallows from November through April. Walk out along the 300-meter timber piers to floating seafood rafts (*nhà bè*) where live flower crab costs 450,000 VND ($18.00 USD) per kilogram. Never lift starfish out of the water for photos, as air exposure kills them within 30 seconds.

## Landmark Highlights: An Thoi Archipelago, Hon Thom Cable Car & History

### Sun World Hon Thom Cable Car & Sunset Town
Departing from the Mediterranean-styled **Sunset Town** (*Thị Trấn Hoàng Hôn*) at the southern tip of An Thoi (28 kilometers south of Duong Dong), the **Hon Thom Cable Car** spans **7,899.9 meters** across six towering concrete sea pillars (reaching 174 meters high) to Hon Thom Island. The 20-minute aerial ride glides 160 meters above emerald coral reefs and hundreds of anchored wooden squid-fishing boats.
- **2026 Return Ticket Tariffs**: **650,000 VND ($26.00 USD)** for adults over 1.4m; **500,000 VND ($20.00 USD)** for children 1.0m to 1.4m; free for children under 1.0m. Adding the Mango Restaurant lunch buffet brings the combo to **900,000 VND ($36.00 USD)** for adults and **650,000 VND ($26.00 USD)** for children. Tickets include full access to the 20-ride **Aquatopia Water Park** and the wooden roller coaster at Exotica Village.
- **Operating Schedule (Watch the Midday Closure!)**: The cable car runs in two daily blocks: **09:00 to 11:30** and **13:30 to 17:00**, shutting down for a 120-minute maintenance inspection between **11:30 and 13:30**.
- **Kiss Bridge (*Cầu Hôn*)**: Located next to the An Thoi departure station, this 810-meter twin-span architectural footbridge with a 30-centimeter gap at center stage charges **100,000 VND ($4.00 USD)** before 16:00 and **200,000 VND ($8.00 USD)** during the 16:00 to 19:00 sunset window.

### An Thoi 3-Island & 4-Island Speedboat Snorkeling Circuits
Twelve coral-fringed islands sit 5 kilometers to 12 kilometers south of An Thoi Port. Booking a small-group speedboat tour for **600,000 VND ($24.00 USD) to 850,000 VND ($34.00 USD)** per person (or a 1-way cable car + 3-island canoe combo for **1,150,000 VND / $46.00 USD**) covers hotel pickup at 08:15, mask and snorkel gear, a 6-dish seafood lunch, and 5 hours visiting:
- **Hon Gam Ghi (*Bãi Đá Ông Cụ*)**: Shallow 2-meter to 5-meter coral gardens packed with table coral and sergeant-major fish.
- **Hon Mong Tay (*Fingernail Island*)**: Postcard lagoon with turquoise water and white sandbars 8 kilometers offshore.
- **Hon May Rut Trong & Ngoai**: Sheltered coconut-palm beaches offering fresh coconuts (40,000 VND / $1.60 USD) and calm swimming.

### Phu Quoc Historical Prison, Ho Quoc Pagoda & Pepper Farms
Two kilometers north of An Thoi along Highway DT46 stands **Phu Quoc Prison Historical Site** (*Nhà Tù Phú Quốc*, open 08:00 to 11:30 and 13:30 to 17:00, Admission: **0 VND / $0.00 USD**, audio guide 50,000 VND / $2.00 USD), preserving the barbed-wire "Tiger Cages" (*Chuồng Cọp*) and underground escape tunnels where 40,000 wartime prisoners were held between 1967 and 1973. Five kilometers further north on the coastal cliff sits **Ho Quoc Pagoda** (*Chùa Hộ Quốc*, free admission, open 06:00 to 18:00), a grand 110-hectare Buddhist monastery facing the eastern sunrise. On your return toward Duong Dong along DT47, stop at **Khu Tuong Pepper Farm** (free entry; sun-dried organic red and black peppercorns cost 180,000 VND to 260,000 VND / $7.20 to $10.40 USD per kilogram).

### How to Eat Bún Quậy Like a Local
Originated by the **Kiến Xây** family at 28 Bach Dang Street in Duong Dong, **Bún Quậy** ("stirred noodles") is prepared fresh to order in 3 minutes. Cooks smear raw minced shrimp, squid, and barracuda paste onto the walls of a ceramic bowl, extrude fresh rice-flour threads directly into boiling cauldrons, and ladle scalding broth into your bowl to poach the seafood paste instantly. While your bowl cooks, walk to the spice station and briskly whisk 2 spoons of kumquat (*tắc*) juice, 1 spoon of sugar, 1 spoon of soup powder, and crushed red chilies until the mixture turns foamy orange. A full special bowl with squid, shrimp, and beef costs **75,000 VND ($3.00 USD)**, paired with a glass of sugarcane-kumquat juice for **15,000 VND ($0.60 USD)**.

### Traditional Fish Sauce Barrel Houses (*Nhà Thùng Nước Mắm*)
Phu Quoc's EU-protected PDO fish sauce (*nước mắm Phú Quốc*) ferments wild black anchovies (*cá cơm than*) with sea salt at a 3:1 ratio inside 14-ton tropical *bời lời* wooden barrels for 12 months to 15 months. You can walk into **Khải Hoàn Barrel House** (11 Hung Vuong Street, Duong Dong) or **Phụng Hưng** (opposite Phu Quoc Prison on DT46) between 07:30 and 17:00 for **0 VND ($0.00 USD)** to inspect 80 towering wooden vats and taste amber 40°N to 43°N first-press extract (220,000 VND to 320,000 VND / $8.80 to $12.80 USD per liter).

> [!TIP]
> **Airline Fish Sauce Ban & Shipping Workaround**: Vietnam Airlines, Vietjet Air, and Bamboo Airways strictly ban glass or plastic bottles of fish sauce in both cabin bags and checked suitcases at PQC Airport due to leakage risks. If you buy 43°N bottles at Khai Hoan or Phung Hung, pay 40,000 VND ($1.60 USD) at the factory desk to have your carton shipped by road ferry directly to your hotel in Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi within 3 days.

## Transportation: Free VinBus Electric Routes, Scooters & Mainland Ferries

### 1. Free 24/7 VinBus Electric Bus Network (Routes 17, 19 & 20)
Operated by green electric transit fleet **VinBus**, three primary routes connect Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC), Long Beach, Duong Dong Town, Ong Lang crossroad, and Grand World for **0 VND ($0.00 USD)**:
- **VinBus Route 17 (PQC Airport – Long Beach – Duong Dong – Grand World)**: Runs every 15 minutes to 30 minutes from 06:00 to 23:40 (with hourly night runs). Total 42-kilometer ride takes 75 minutes and stops at Holidayense, Novotel, Salinda, Highland Bungalow, Duong Dong Center, and Ong Lang intersection.
- **VinBus Route 19 (Intercontinental – PQC Airport – Duong Dong – Grand World)**: Runs from 06:00 to 24:00 every 20 to 30 minutes along the southern Long Beach resort strip.
- **VinBus Route 20 (Bai Vong Ferry Port – Duong Dong – Grand World)**: Synchronized with incoming Superdong and Phu Quoc Express ferry arrivals across the 48-kilometer route (80 minutes).

### 2. Scooter Rental, GrabCar & Private Day Charters
- **Automatic Scooter Rental**: 120,000 VND to 160,000 VND ($4.80 to $6.40 USD) per 24 hours for a Honda AirBlade or Vision 125cc. Filling the 4.5-liter tank with RON 95 petrol costs 95,000 VND ($3.80 USD) and covers 180 kilometers of riding along paved Routes DT45, DT46, and DT975.
- **GrabCar & Xanh SM Electric Taxis**: PQC Airport to central Duong Dong (10 km, 15 minutes) costs **140,000 VND to 180,000 VND ($5.60 to $7.20 USD)**; Duong Dong to Sunset Town / An Thoi (28 km, 35 minutes) costs **320,000 VND to 390,000 VND ($12.80 to $15.60 USD)**.
- **8-Hour Private Car Charter**: Hiring a 4-seat or 7-seat private car with driver for a full 8-hour southern or northern loop costs **750,000 VND to 950,000 VND ($30.00 to $38.00 USD)**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the 30-day visa exemption work for Phu Quoc in 2026?
All foreign passport holders receive a free **30-day visa exemption** (0 VND / $0.00 USD) when flying or arriving by sea **directly** into Phu Quoc from outside Vietnam, or transiting through HAN/SGN on an international connection where border clearance takes place at Phu Quoc Airport (PQC). You must stay within Phu Quoc Island for the entire trip and show an outbound flight ticket departing PQC within 30 days. Traveling onward to Ho Chi Minh City, Can Tho, or Hanoi requires applying for a $25.00 USD (625,000 VND) Vietnam E-Visa before leaving Phu Quoc.

### When is the best time of year to visit Phu Quoc beaches?
The prime dry season runs for 6 months from **November through April**, bringing 28°C to 31°C temperatures, low 72% humidity, and glass-calm turquoise water along the 20-kilometer western coast (Long Beach and Ong Lang). If visiting during the May to October rainy season when hotel rates drop by 30% to 45%, swim at southeastern **Bai Sao** and **Bai Khem**, which remain sheltered from western swells.

### How do I travel to Phu Quoc by ferry from Ha Tien or Rach Gia?
High-speed catamarans operated by **Superdong** and **Phu Quoc Express** sail daily into **Bai Vong Port** (14 kilometers east of Duong Dong). From **Ha Tien** (45 kilometers offshore), the ferry takes **75 minutes** and costs **215,000 VND to 250,000 VND ($8.60 to $10.00 USD)** one-way. From **Rach Gia** (120 kilometers offshore), the crossing takes **135 to 150 minutes** and costs **315,000 VND to 340,000 VND ($12.60 to $13.60 USD)** one-way for economy seats or 540,000 VND ($21.60 USD) for VIP cabins.

### How much do direct flights from Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi to Phu Quoc cost?
From **Ho Chi Minh City (SGN)**, over 18 daily nonstop flights take **55 minutes** across 300 kilometers, with one-way fares ranging from **750,000 VND to 1,450,000 VND ($30.00 to $58.00 USD)**. From **Hanoi (HAN)**, nonstop flights take **125 minutes** across 1,240 kilometers and cost **1,350,000 VND to 2,600,000 VND ($54.00 to $104.00 USD)** one-way.
