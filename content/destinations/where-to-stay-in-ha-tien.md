## Strategic District Geography in Ha Tien

Located in the far southwestern pocket of the Mekong Delta, Ha Tien sits 90 km west of Rach Gia (roughly 2 hours via National Highway `QL80`) and 90 km southwest of Chau Doc (about 2.5 hours across rural provincial roads). The town borders the Gulf of Thailand and functions simultaneously as an international border crossing into Cambodia, a departure pier for Phu Quoc Island speedboats, and a tranquil coastal riverfront retreat.

Travelers arriving here usually pursue one of three logistical goals: boarding an early-morning ferry to Phu Quoc, passing through the Xa Xia / Prek Chak border gate toward Kampot just 8 km north, or taking time to experience local coastal culture. Where you base yourself determines your transit efficiency.

> **ATM Cash Advisory**: Ha Tien provides three operational bank branches with street ATMs along Tran Hau and Mac Cuu near the market. ATM access lobbies lock at 22:00. Withdraw at least 500,000 VND in cash before reaching town, especially when arriving by late evening bus.

## Zone 1: Downtown Pier District (Tran Hau & Phuong Thanh)

### Accommodation Standards & Core Logistics

The commercial strip between the high-speed ferry pier (Ben Tau Cao Toc Ha Tien) and the night market along Tran Hau hosts the highest density of town lodging. This compact 400 meter corridor serves ferry passengers: Superdong and Ngoc Thanh express boats depart for Phu Quoc Island daily at 06:00, 08:30, and 13:00. Tickets cost 330,000–390,000 VND ($13.20–$15.60) for the 1 hour 15 minute maritime crossing.

River Hotel Ha Tien sits along the waterfront esplanade, providing clean double rooms from 450,000–650,000 VND ($18–$26) per night. Its front desk operates around the clock, accommodating 05:00 wake-up calls for the 3-minute walk to the morning terminal. Superior river-view rooms on the third and fourth floors look directly out across the To Chau River estuary. Hai Yen Hotel on Phuong Thanh offers simpler rooms from 300,000–400,000 VND ($12–$16) per night, equipped with silent air-conditioning units and tiled walk-in showers. Anh Van Guesthouse provides budget backpacker beds and basic private doubles for 250,000–320,000 VND ($10–$13) per night, catering to overland riders crossing from Cambodia.

Dining in this central area is inexpensive. Stalls in the night market serve whole grilled squid for 60,000–90,000 VND ($2.40–$3.60). Morning *hu tieu* noodle soup with crispy garlic costs 30,000–45,000 VND ($1.20–$1.80) at neighborhood street carts. Steamed sea snails tossed with lemongrass and chili cost 50,000–80,000 VND ($2.00–$3.20) per plate at sidewalk eateries opposite the ferry gates.

### Trade-Offs & Operational Friction

Motorbike traffic and early ferry loading noise begin along Tran Hau at 05:30 and taper off around 19:00. Front-facing rooms on the main street pick up regular engine vibrations from idling minibuses. Travelers seeking quiet sleep should request rear-facing rooms or upper-floor units upon arrival. During high-season weekends in December and January, speedboat tickets sell out early, making proximity to ticket booking booths essential.

## Zone 2: Mui Nai Beach (6 km Southwest)

### Beachfront Lodging Infrastructure

Bai Bien Mui Nai occupies a crescent bay 6 km southwest of the downtown district. Local motorbike taxis charge 30,000–50,000 VND ($1.20–$2.00) for the 10-minute trip along the coastal route. Lodging here features low-rise beachfront compounds alongside simple guesthouses. Coconut trees shade the shore, and the bay opens westward toward Gulf of Thailand sunsets.

La Beach House represents the upscale tier of Mui Nai lodging, offering comfortable bungalow rooms from 800,000–1,350,000 VND ($32–$54) per night. These timber chalets feature private verandas set within landscaped gardens 40 meters from the water line. Phu My Resort, located slightly farther along the beach road, provides rooms with balcony hammocks and garden views for 550,000–950,000 VND ($22–$38) per night. Family-run guesthouses behind the coconut fringe rent basic fan rooms for 220,000–350,000 VND ($8.80–$14.00) per night, favored by domestic travelers during national holidays.

### Seasonal Realities at Mui Nai

Between June and August, jellyfish appear more frequently in the shallow surf off Mui Nai. While local swimmers continue entering the sea, occasional stings occur. Keeping a bottle of vinegar (purchased for 12,000 VND at local pharmacies) is practical, and checking with guesthouse staff regarding current water conditions before swimming is recommended. Outside those summer months, the sea stays calm and pleasant, with water temperatures averaging 28–31°C.

Dining choices along Mui Nai remain modest. A handful of beachfront sheds serve grilled fish and prawns for 120,000–200,000 VND ($4.80–$8.00) per dish. Fresh crab caught by local inshore skiffs costs 220,000–350,000 VND ($8.80–$14.00) per kilogram cooked to order with tamarind sauce. Kitchens close by 21:00, requiring a trip into the downtown night market for late meals.

## Zone 3: Thach Dong & Nui Da Dung Temple Corridor

Heading northeast from town for 2 km along a quiet road toward limestone hills brings you into the Thach Dong pagoda area. The temple sits inside an open karst cave that affords sweeping vistas over green rice fields stretching across the border into Cambodia.

### Pagoda-View Homestays

Family homestays along the approach road toward Thach Dong and Nui Da Dung charge 200,000–350,000 VND ($8–$14) per night for clean rooms with fans, mosquito nets, and shared bathrooms. Breakfast consists of fresh baguette and Vietnamese coffee. Several rural hosts offer home-cooked family dinners featuring free-range chicken and braised swamp fish for 60,000–90,000 VND ($2.40–$3.60) per person.

This sector suits cyclists and independent riders looking for country calm. Renting a scooter downtown runs 130,000–160,000 VND ($5.20–$6.40) per day. Getting to the ferry pier for an early sailing takes roughly 10 minutes by road. Paved rural paths meander between lotus ponds and traditional Khmer hamlets where monks in saffron robes collect morning alms.

## Zone 4: Offshore Islands — Dao Hai Tac & Hon Phu Tu Coastal Excursions

### Pirate Island Archipelago

The Dao Hai Tac (Pirate Islands) archipelago lies roughly 28 km south of Ha Tien in the Gulf of Thailand. Wooden passenger boats can be chartered at the pier for 1,200,000–1,800,000 VND ($48–$72) round trip for groups up to 6 people. Daily public ferries also run at 07:00 and 14:00, charging 50,000 VND ($2.00) per person. The sea journey requires about 1 hour 30 minutes each way in fair weather.

Lodging on Pirate Island remains rustic. Local homestays provide simple floor mattresses, shared squat bathrooms, and generator electricity run between 18:00 and 22:00. Nightly rates range from 150,000–250,000 VND ($6–$10). Bringing a headlamp is essential. Visitors can snorkel shallow coral reefs or buy freshly harvested sea urchins from local diving skiffs for 15,000–25,000 VND ($0.60–$1.00) each.

### Sunset Cruise to Hon Phu Tu

Rising dramatically from coastal waters 25 km southeast of Ha Tien, the landmark sea rocks of Hon Phu Tu form a classic visual highlight. Visiting the area works best as an afternoon excursion by hired boat or coastal scooter ride along National Highway 80. Combined fishing and sunset cruises departing Ha Tien pier cost 350,000–500,000 VND ($14–$20) per passenger, leaving at 14:30 and returning around 18:30. Calm seas and clear skies prevail between November and March.

### Cross-Border Day Trip to Kampot

The Xa Xia / Prek Chak border gate lies 8 km north of central Ha Tien on QL80. Foreign passport holders holding an approved Cambodia e-visa ($36 online) can cross smoothly on foot or by vehicle. Scooter taxis to the border cost 40,000–60,000 VND ($1.60–$2.40). On the Cambodian side, private taxis to Kampot town charge approximately $12–$15 for the 35 km, 45 minute drive.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to stay overnight in Ha Tien to catch the Phu Quoc ferry?
Yes. Morning travelers heading to Phu Quoc on the 06:00 express boat must stay in Ha Tien overnight because buses from Rach Gia or Can Tho cannot arrive early enough. Booking a hotel within 400 meters of the terminal allows a relaxed 5-minute walk to the boarding gates.

### How much does a Ha Tien to Phu Quoc speedboat ticket cost in 2026?
Standard economy tickets on Superdong and Ngoc Thanh ferries cost 330,000–390,000 VND ($13.20–$15.60) for the 1 hour 15 minute ride. VIP seats with priority boarding cost 450,000 VND ($18.00).

### Is Mui Nai Beach safe for swimming?
Calm swimming conditions hold from September through May. During June to August jellyfish activity, swimmers should watch shallow water areas. Carrying a small bottle of vinegar and asking guesthouse staff about daily conditions is good practice.

### Are there money changers at the Cambodia border?
Money exchange stalls operate beside both sides of the Xa Xia / Prek Chak border post. Exchange rates favor converting US Dollars into Cambodian Riel. Carrying $20–$50 in small dollar bills covers initial transit costs easily.

### What is the cheapest room available in Ha Tien?
Basic fan-cooled rooms in family homestays around Thach Dong and side streets near Phuong Thanh begin at 150,000–200,000 VND ($6–$8) per night. These rooms provide a mattress, mosquito net, and shared washroom. Air-conditioned rooms cost roughly 100,000 VND more.
