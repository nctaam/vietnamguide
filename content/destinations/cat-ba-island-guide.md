# Cat Ba Island Travel Guide: Lan Ha Bay Cruises, National Park Hikes, and Island Logistics

Covering 260 square kilometers across an archipelago of 367 islands, **Cat Ba Island** is the ecological anchor of northern Vietnam's coastal karst landscape. While politically part of Hai Phong municipality, Cat Ba borders the southern rim of Ha Long Bay and sits directly inside **Lan Ha Bay** and the **Cat Ba UNESCO Biosphere Reserve**.

Unlike the commercial, high-rise cruise hubs on the Quang Ninh mainland, Cat Ba functions as an active outdoor staging base. It allows travelers to sleep on solid ground, hire local motorboats out of **Ben Beo Port**, hike through subtropical rainforest to mountain peaks, and kayak through tidal archways into secluded marine lagoons.

Planning a trip to Cat Ba in 2026 requires understanding island transport shifts, trail networks, seasonal marine conditions, and heritage preservation regulations. This comprehensive empirical guide covers national park trekking, Lan Ha Bay boat logistics, cliffside beaches, local dining, and island transport.

## Lan Ha Bay and Ben Beo Port Expedition Logistics

Most maritime exploration around Cat Ba departs from **Ben Beo Port** (*Ben Beo*), situated 1.5 kilometers southeast of Cat Ba town center. Electric passenger golf carts and taxis run continuously between the town strip and the port for **20,000 to 40,000 VND per ride**.

```
[Cat Ba Town Promenade]
         |
         v (1.5 km via electric cart / taxi: 20,000 – 40,000 VND)
[Ben Beo Harbor Terminal] (Port entry fee: 40,000 VND)
         |
         +---> [Cai Beo Ancient Floating Village] (7,000-year maritime lineage)
         |
         +---> [Ba Trai Dao Islets] (White sand tidal beaches & deep swimming)
         |
         +---> [Bright & Dark Cave] (Tidal tunnel into enclosed karst marine lake)
```

### 1. Cai Beo Floating Fishing Village
Cruises passing out of Ben Beo immediately navigate through **Cai Beo**, recognized by archaeologists as one of the oldest coastal fishing settlements in Southeast Asia, with marine artifacts dating back over 7,000 years. Approximately 300 floating wooden houses supported by plastic drums line the waterway, where families raise cobia, grouper, and red snapper in submerged mesh cages.

### 2. Kayaking Bright & Dark Cave (*Hang Sang - Hang Toi*)
Located on the border between Lan Ha Bay and Ha Long Bay, this marine karst formation features two tidal tunnels:
- **Bright Cave (*Hang Sang*):** Always above sea level, allowing small double kayaks or bamboo rowboats to pass through a 100-meter arch into an enclosed interior lake (*ang*) surrounded by sheer 60-meter limestone walls.
- **Dark Cave (*Hang Toi*):** A 200-meter cavern that floods entirely during spring high tides. Guided passage is only permitted when tidal levels drop below 1.2 meters.

### 3. Ba Trai Dao Beaches (Three Peaches)
This cluster of three rounded limestone islets features secluded sandy beaches exposed during low and medium tides. The water here is clearer and greener than along the inner coastline, making it the primary anchorage for swimming, stand-up paddleboarding, and snorkeling.

> [!NOTE]
> Booking an independent shared day boat through reputable local agencies (such as Cat Ba Ventures or Blue Swimmer) costs **650,000 to 850,000 VND per person**, which includes port harbor levies, kayak equipment, safety life vests, and a freshly prepared multi-course Vietnamese seafood lunch on board.

## Cat Ba National Park: Rainforest Hiking and Peaks

Established in 1986, **Cat Ba National Park** encompasses 17,360 hectares of terrestrial and marine ecosystems. It protects the critically endangered **Cat Ba Langur** (*Trachypithecus poliocephalus*), one of the rarest primates on Earth, with fewer than 85 individuals surviving in the wild on isolated karst cliffs.

```
[Park Headquarters Gate] (Elevation: 30m)
         |
         v (Flat gravel walking path / 800 meters)
[Kim Giao Forest Trailhead]
         |
         v (Steep limestone rocky ascent / 1,200 meters)
[Ngu Lam Intermediate Pavilion]
         |
         v (Narrow summit ridge scramble / 400 meters)
[Ngu Lam Peak Summit] (Elevation: 225m / 360° Karst Canopy Panorama)
```

### 1. The Ngu Lam Peak Trail Profile
The standard out-and-back hike to **Ngu Lam Peak** starts at the main park visitor center on highway DT356. The trail begins with an 800-meter walk through secondary forest before entering the **Kim Giao forest**, a stand of endemic evergreen trees (*Nageia fleuryi*) whose timber was historically used to carve chopstick sets for Vietnamese royalty to detect food poisons.

Past the Kim Giao forest, the trail transforms into a rugged, unpaved scramble up jagged limestone rocks with iron handrails anchored into steep sections. Hiking footwear with sturdy tread is essential; wet limestone becomes dangerously slick. Reaching the summit takes **60 to 75 minutes**, rewarded with a 360-degree panorama of forested karst ridges stretching inland toward Hai Phong.

### 2. Trung Trang Cave (*Dong Trung Trang*)
Located 1.2 kilometers south of the park entrance along the main highway, Trung Trang Cave cuts 300 meters horizontally through a limestone mountain. Walkway paths and interior electric lighting guide visitors through chambers containing active stalactites, flowstones, and natural bat colonies. The cave requires 45 minutes to traverse and exits onto a forest path that circles back to the highway.

> [!WARNING]
> **Hospital Cave (*Hang Quan Y*) Notice:** The historical military field hospital built inside a bomb-proof cave during the American War has been closed to commercial tourism indefinitely due to structural inspections. Travelers should verify open status before hiring transport specifically to this site.

## Cannon Fort: Heritage Artillery and Sunset Views

Perched on a strategic promontory 177 meters above Cat Ba Town, **Cannon Fort** (*Phao Dai Than Cong*) was established by French colonial forces in 1942 and later fortified by the Vietnamese military during conflicts of the 20th century.

The installation features two massive 130mm coastal defense artillery guns mounted on rotating turntables, interconnecting trench systems, underground ammunition bunkers, and an observation deck carved into the rock. From the summit, the vantage point looks directly out over the floating communities of Ben Beo, the jagged towers of Lan Ha Bay, and the open maritime approaches of the Gulf of Tonkin.

Travelers can reach the summit entrance via a steep 2.5-kilometer hike up a paved mountain road from town or hire a motorbike taxi for **30,000 to 40,000 VND**. Entry tickets cost **40,000 to 50,000 VND**. Late afternoon between 16:30 and 17:30 PM is the premier window to visit, as the setting sun illuminates the karst archipelago in deep amber tones.

## Coastal Beaches and the Cat Co Cliff Boardwalk

Cat Ba Town features three primary sandy coves situated along its southern headland: **Cat Co 1**, **Cat Co 2**, and **Cat Co 3**:

```
[Cat Co 1 Beach] (Broadest sand crescent, resort facilities)
         |
         v (450m concrete suspended boardwalk around seaside cliff)
[Cat Co 3 Beach] (Deeper water, calmer surf, sunset orientation)
```

1. **Cat Co 1:** The largest and busiest beach, flanked by large resort complexes. It offers wide sandy shores and beach loungers available for rent at **50,000 to 100,000 VND**.
2. **Cat Co 3:** Situated further south, offering deeper water and moderate wave conditions.
3. **The Cat Co Cliff Path:** A scenic concrete boardwalk suspended above the surf along the vertical rock face connecting Cat Co 1 and Cat Co 3. Walking this path takes 20 to 30 minutes, offering expansive views across the Lan Ha Bay horizon and small offshore limestone spires. Access is completely free to the public.

## Viet Hai Village: The Isolated Karst Basin

Surrounded by sheer vertical limestone walls inside the national park, **Viet Hai** is an agricultural hamlet of roughly 80 households that was accessible solely by boat or rugged mountain trail until recent years.

- **Access by Sea:** Take a 45-minute wooden longboat from Ben Beo Port to Viet Hai Pier (**100,000 to 150,000 VND per person** each way for shared boats). At the pier, rent a bicycle (**50,000 VND**) or board an electric cart (**30,000 to 50,000 VND**) for a 4.5-kilometer ride through a mountain rock tunnel and bamboo forest into the village.
- **Access by Jungle Trek:** Experienced hikers can complete the strenuous 12-kilometer trans-park trek from National Park Headquarters over Frog Lake (*Ao Ech*) to Viet Hai. This trek requires **4.5 to 6.0 hours**, mandatory trail registration with park rangers, and local guide hire (**400,000 to 600,000 VND** per group) to prevent disorientation on unmarked rainforest paths.
- **Village Activities:** Visitors cycle along flat paved paths, inspect traditional thatch and timber houses, and rest at natural stream channels where tiny freshwater doctor fish gently nibble dead skin cells from tired feet.

## Island Dining and Seafood Terroir

Dining on Cat Ba revolves around fresh marine catch landed directly at Ben Beo and town docks:

- **Geoduck Clams (*Tu Hai*):** The island's signature delicacy, prized for sweet, crunchy flesh. Typically steamed with lemongrass or grilled over charcoal with scallion oil and crushed roasted peanuts (**50,000 to 80,000 VND per piece**).
- **Mantis Shrimp (*Be Be*):** Sautéed with roasted salt and chili peppers or steamed with fresh beer (**350,000 to 500,000 VND per kilogram**).
- **Stir-Fried Sea Snails (*Oc Huong*):** Tossed with tamarind sauce or salted egg yolk (**120,000 to 180,000 VND per plate**).
- **Dining Corridor:** The central bayfront promenade along **One-Four Street** (*Duong Mot Thang Tu*) hosts dozens of casual seafood beer halls (*quan bia*). Always ask vendors to weigh live seafood from tanks in front of you and confirm price per kilogram before cooking.

## Island Transport and Arrival Logistics

Reaching Cat Ba Island in 2026 relies on modern road and ferry links from Hanoi:

```
[Hanoi Old Quarter]
         |
         v (CT04 Expressway via Daiichi / Cat Ba Express / 2.0 hours)
[Dong Bai Ferry Terminal] (Hai Phong Mainland)
         |
         +---> [Public Ro-Ro Ferry] (40 minutes across channel to Cai Vieng)
         |
         +---> [Sun World Ocean Cable Car] (15 minutes across sea to Phu Long)
         |
         v
[Cai Vieng / Phu Long Port] (North Cat Ba Island)
         |
         v (DT356 scenic coastal highway / 24 km / 40 minutes)
[Cat Ba Town Center]
```

Following the permanent closure of the legacy Got Ferry in 2024, vehicle and passenger traffic moves through the modernized **Dong Bai Ferry Terminal** (*Ben Pha Dong Bai*). 

The most seamless transit method remains booking an integrated **all-in-one tourist bus** (operated by Daiichi Travel, Cat Ba Express, or Good Morning Cat Ba) for **300,000 to 350,000 VND ($12 to $14 USD)**. The operator coordinates the highway coach from Hanoi, the high-speed passenger boat across the maritime channel, and the connecting island bus along scenic highway DT356 straight to your hotel door in Cat Ba town, completing the 150-kilometer trip in **3.5 to 4.0 hours**.

> [!TIP]
> Travelers wishing to explore the island independently can rent 110cc semi-automatic motorbikes in Cat Ba Town for **120,000 to 150,000 VND per day**. Highway DT356 between Cat Ba Town and the National Park features smooth asphalt, minimal commercial traffic, and gentle sweeping curves suitable for relaxed riding.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best season to visit Cat Ba Island?
The optimal travel window spans from **October to April**, characterized by mild temperatures (18°C to 25°C), low rainfall, and clear skies ideal for hiking and sailing. Summer months (May to August) bring warm seawater and sunny skies for swimming, but coincide with peak domestic holiday crowds and occasional tropical typhoons that can suspend ferry sailings.

### Can I do a day trip to Lan Ha Bay without booking an overnight cruise?
Yes. Cat Ba Island is the best base for affordable day trips. You can stay in a budget or boutique hotel in Cat Ba Town, walk or catch an electric cart to Ben Beo Port at 08:00 AM, board a full-day cruise exploring Lan Ha Bay and Bright Cave, and return to your island hotel by 16:30 PM.

### Is Cat Ba Island suitable for families with young children?
Yes. Children enjoy the tranquil rowboat rides through Bright Cave, level cycling in Viet Hai village, and playing at Cat Co 1 beach. However, families with toddlers should skip the rocky Ngu Lam Peak summit scramble, opting instead for the gentle 45-minute stroll through Trung Trang Cave.
