# Mang Den Travel Guide: Pine Plateau at 1,200m (2026)

Positioned inside Kon Plong district in northeastern Kon Tum province, the mountain plateau of **Mang Den** is one of Vietnam's wildest high-altitude forest reserves. Perched at an elevation of 1,200 meters (1,200 m) above sea level along the Truong Son mountain range, this temperate tableland sits 50 km (70 minutes by road at 45 km/h) northeast of Kon Tum City and 100 km (2.2 hours by shuttle) northeast of Pleiku Airport (PXU).

Unlike urbanized highland cities to the south, Mang Den maintains an 82% forest cover across 148,000 hectares of protected watershed. Sixty-year-old three-needle pine stands (*Pinus kesiya*) line National Highway QL24, giving way to primary subtropical cloud forest, 45 meters granite waterfalls, 14°C cold-water sturgeon raceways, and stilt-house villages inhabited by the indigenous Mo Nam (a branch of the Xo Dang) ethnic group.

The town's name originates from the Mo Nam phrase *T'măng Deeng*, meaning "flat, watered plateau between high peaks." For travelers exploring the Central Highlands in 2026, Mang Den delivers cool mountain air averaging 16°C to 22°C (with 80% to 85% mean humidity), uncrowded 5 km to 10 km forest hikes, and charcoal-roasted highland cuisine priced from 35,000 VND to 250,000 VND ($1.40 to $10.00 USD).

## The Prime Natural & Cultural Highlights of Mang Den

Mang Den's attractions revolve around mountain hydrology, Mo Nam village architecture, and high-ridge pine viewpoints within a 10 km radius of the town crossroads.

### 1. Pa Sỹ Waterfall (`Thác Pa Sỹ`) & Sculpture Garden
Located 8.0 km (15 minutes by motorbike at 35 km/h) northwest of town along a paved forest road, **Pa Sỹ Waterfall** is the largest of the plateau's three main cataracts. The name derives from the Mo Nam term *Pau Suh* ("three streams converging"), referencing the three subterranean headwaters that unite before plunging 45 meters off a vertical granite cliff into a 4 meters deep emerald pool.

Visitors pay a statutory entrance fee of 20,000 VND ($0.80 USD) per adult (children under 1.2 meters enter free at 0 VND / $0.00 USD; motorbike parking costs 5,000 VND / $0.20 USD) and walk 600 meters (10 minutes) down 186 stone steps under giant tree ferns. Near the top trailhead, a 2-hectare open-air garden displays over 100 hand-hewn hardwood statues carved by Xo Dang artisans, plus stilt huts serving hot arabica coffee (25,000 VND / $1.00 USD) and grilled wild boar skewers (30,000 VND / $1.20 USD).

### 2. Kon Tu Rằng Suspension Bridge & Terraced Valley
Just 1.5 km (4 minutes drive) past the Pa Sy gate, a steep concrete lane descends 200 meters in elevation to **Kon Tu Rằng Village**, an indigenous Mo Nam settlement of 75 households along the Dak Bla headwaters. A 120 meters long steel suspension footbridge (1.5 meters wide, free access at 0 VND / $0.00 USD) spans the river 15 meters above rushing rapids, leading directly to 30 hectares of tiered wet-rice terraces and wooden stilt granaries (*kho lúa*) built on 2 meters high log pilings to deter field rodents.

### 3. Đắk Ke Lake (`Hồ Đắk Ke` / `Toong Rơ Poong`)
Covering 3.0 hectares just 800 meters (10 minutes walk) from the central Vo Nguyen Giap boulevard, **Đắk Ke Lake** is the most accessible of the seven legendary crater lakes (*Bảy Hồ Ba Thác*). Ringed by a 1.5 km paved walking track (25 minutes stroll), the water reflects 25 meters tall pine trees and 400 pink Himalayan cherry blossom trees. Entry to the lake promenade is free (0 VND / $0.00 USD); renting a 2-person swan pedal boat costs 60,000 VND ($2.40 USD) per 30 minutes, horse-drawn carriage rides around the 1.5 km perimeter cost 100,000 VND ($4.00 USD), and lakeside cafes serve hot ginger tea for 25,000 VND ($1.00 USD).

### 4. Khánh Lâm Pagoda (`Chùa Khánh Lâm`)
Perched on a 1,200 meters ridge 5.5 km (12 minutes drive) northwest of town along the Pa Sy road, **Khánh Lâm Pagoda** spans 10 hectares of primary forest. Built between 2012 and 2017, its main sanctuary merges three-roof northern Buddhist timber joinery with the steep 15 meters high blade-shaped roof of a Central Highland *nhà rông* communal hall. Visitors climb 236 mossy stone steps (12 minutes ascent) beneath 30 meters pines to reach the courtyard and a 17 meters tall white statue of Avalokiteshvara (`Quan Thế Âm Bồ Tát`). Admission is 0 VND ($0.00 USD), and open-air pavilions stays open from 06:00 to 18:00 (12 hours daily).

### 5. Kon Pring Community Village & Our Lady of Mang Den
Heading 2.5 km (5 minutes by scooter) east of town along National Highway QL24 brings you to **Kon Pring Village**, home to 65 Mo Nam families living around a traditional 18 meters tall thatched *nhà rông* communal house. You can walk the 1.2 km village lanes for free (0 VND / $0.00 USD) or book a 90 minutes evening gong performance and rice-wine (*rượu cần*) gathering through the village cooperative for 1,200,000 VND ($48.00 USD) per group. Another 1.2 km west along QL24 sits the **Shrine of Our Lady of Mang Den** (`Đức Mẹ Măng Đen`), a 1.2 meters tall cement statue erected in 1971, rediscovered in the forest in 2002, and visited free of charge (0 VND / $0.00 USD) from 05:00 to 21:00 (16 hours daily).

## High-Tech Agroforestry, Cloud Hunting & Ngọc Lễ Trekking

Mang Den's cool 16°C to 22°C microclimate supports both alpine trekking and cold-water agriculture.

### The 37 Hộ Agricultural Belt & Café De Măng Đen
Driving 6.0 km to 9.0 km (15 minutes) south of town via Tran Nhan Tong road leads into the **37 Hộ** eco-farming zone. Here, 12°C to 17°C mountain streams feed concrete raceway tanks raising **Siberian Sturgeon** (*cá tầm*) and rainbow trout (*cá hồi*). Visitors can tour the aquaculture ponds for free (0 VND / $0.00 USD) and buy live 2.0 kg to 3.5 kg sturgeon at 280,000 VND to 320,000 VND ($11.20 to $12.80 USD) per kilogram, with an extra 50,000 VND ($2.00 USD) kitchen preparation fee for sashimi and sour bamboo hotpot.

Surrounding farms in 37 Ho open their hydroponic greenhouses from 07:30 to 17:00 (9.5 hours) at 0 VND ($0.00 USD) entry. You can hand-pick Japanese Skyberry strawberries at 250,000 VND to 350,000 VND ($10.00 to $14.00 USD) per kilogram and cherry tomatoes at 80,000 VND ($3.20 USD) per kilogram, then stop at **Ê Ban Farm** (entry 30,000 VND / $1.20 USD) or **Orfarm** (coffee 45,000 VND / $1.80 USD) overlooking a 400 meters deep valley.

### Cloud Hunting & Ngọc Lễ Peak (1,720 meters)
Between 05:15 and 06:45 (a 90 minutes dawn window), valley moisture condenses into a dense sea of white clouds at 1,000 meters elevation. The two best roadside viewpoints are **Đồi Đức Mẹ Viewpoint** (1.5 km east on QL24, 5 minutes ride, coffee stalls charging 25,000 VND / $1.00 USD) and **Vườn Trà Cổ** on Tran Nhan Tong hill (4.0 km south, 8 minutes ride). Hikers seeking a physical climb can hire a local guide (400,000 VND to 500,000 VND / $16.00 to $20.00 USD per group) to trek **Ngọc Lễ Peak** (1,720 meters elevation), a 5.5 km round-trip forest trail taking 3.0 hours up and 1.5 hours down (4.5 hours total).

## Step-by-Step Protocol: How to Reach Mang Den & Ride a 3-Day Loop

### Transportation Gateways (2026 Schedules & Tariffs)
1. **Via Pleiku Airport (PXU, 100 km southwest):** Fly into Pleiku from Hanoi (90 minutes flight, 1,400,000 VND to 2,200,000 VND / $56.00 to $88.00 USD) or Ho Chi Minh City (70 minutes flight, 1,100,000 VND to 1,800,000 VND / $44.00 to $72.00 USD). Shared 9-seat limousine vans (Tấn Tài, Tiến Đông, or Minh Quốc) pick up directly outside the PXU arrivals hall and reach Mang Den in 2.0 hours to 2.5 hours via QL14 and QL24 for 150,000 VND ($6.00 USD) per seat. A private 4-seat taxi from PXU to Mang Den costs 900,000 VND to 1,100,000 VND ($36.00 to $44.00 USD) one-way.
2. **From Kon Tum City (50 km southwest):** Green-and-yellow public Bus #06 (Kon Tum to Kon Plong) departs Kon Tum Bus Station every 40 minutes from 05:30 to 17:00, taking 75 minutes up Mang Den Pass along QL24 for 40,000 VND to 50,000 VND ($1.60 to $2.00 USD). A private 4-seat Grab or Mai Linh taxi from Kon Tum City takes 60 minutes and costs 450,000 VND to 550,000 VND ($18.00 to $22.00 USD).
3. **Overnight Sleeper Bus from Da Nang (260 km) or Ho Chi Minh City (620 km):** Phượng Thu, Minh Quốc, and Phong Phú VIP 22-cabin sleeper buses depart Da Nang Central Bus Station at 21:00 (arriving 04:30, 7.5 hours, 300,000 VND to 380,000 VND / $12.00 to $15.20 USD) and Mien Dong Station in HCMC at 17:30 (arriving 06:30, 13 hours, 450,000 VND to 600,000 VND / $18.00 to $24.00 USD).

### 3-Day / 2-Night Mang Den & Kon Tum Itinerary
- **Day 1 (25 km local riding, 4 hours active):** Arrive from Pleiku Airport by 11:00; rent a 110cc Honda Wave or Vision scooter from your homestay (130,000 VND to 150,000 VND / $5.20 to $6.00 USD per 24 hours; add 40,000 VND / $1.60 USD for 1.8 liters of fuel). Eat lunch at Cô Sinh (125,000 VND / $5.00 USD per person), ride 8 km to **Pa Sỹ Waterfall** (20,000 VND / $0.80 USD) and **Kon Tu Rằng Suspension Bridge** at 14:00, visit **Khánh Lâm Pagoda** at 16:00, and dine on **Lẩu Xuyên Tiêu** (160,000 VND / $6.40 USD per person) at 18:30.
- **Day 2 (22 km riding, 5 hours active):** Wake at 05:30 for dawn cloud hunting along QL24 and a claypot breakfast at **Bánh Canh A Kay** (45,000 VND / $1.80 USD). Spend 08:30 to 12:30 exploring the **37 Hộ** sturgeon farms, picking strawberries (250,000 VND / $10.00 USD per kg), and drinking arabica pour-over at **De Vivre Café** (50,000 VND / $2.00 USD). Walk the 1.5 km **Đắk Ke Lake** loop at 15:30 and explore **Kon Pring Village** before a sturgeon hotpot dinner (200,000 VND / $8.00 USD per person) and night market skewered meats (50,000 VND / $2.00 USD) at **Chợ Đêm Măng Đen** (open Friday to Sunday, 18:00 to 22:30).
- **Day 3 (100 km transit back to PXU, 4 hours):** Visit **Our Lady of Mang Den** at 08:00, board the 09:30 limousine down Mang Den Pass (50 km, 60 minutes) to **Kon Tum City**, spend 90 minutes visiting the 1913 **Kon Tum Wooden Cathedral** (0 VND / $0.00 USD) and **Kon Klor Suspension Bridge** (292 meters long), and continue 50 km (60 minutes) to Pleiku Airport for your afternoon flight.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many days are recommended to visit Mang Den?
A stay of 3 days and 2 nights (48 hours to 60 hours on the plateau) is optimal. Day 1 covers Pa Sy Waterfall (8 km), Kon Tu Rang Village, and Khanh Lam Pagoda; Day 2 covers dawn cloud hunting, the 37 Ho sturgeon and strawberry farms (6 km south), and Dak Ke Lake; and Day 3 allows a 90 minutes stop in Kon Tum City (50 km southwest) en route to Pleiku Airport (PXU).

### How does Mang Den differ from Da Lat?
While Da Lat sits at 1,500 meters with over 420,000 urban residents, greenhouses covering valley floors, and heavy weekend traffic, Mang Den sits at 1,200 meters with roughly 9,500 town residents and 82% intact pine and broadleaf forest cover. Room rates in Mang Den average 350,000 VND to 850,000 VND ($14.00 to $34.00 USD) per night inside quiet pine groves with zero traffic lights.

### Is Mang Den safe to drive by motorcycle?
Yes, provided you respect mountain gradients. National Highway QL24 climbing 12 km up Mang Den Pass from Kon Tum (or 25 km up Violak Pass from Quang Ngai) has smooth asphalt, steel guardrails, and convex mirrors, but includes 10% grades and afternoon fog that cuts visibility to 30 meters. Keep speeds between 30 km/h and 40 km/h, shift into 2nd gear on descents, and rent a well-maintained scooter (150,000 VND / $6.00 USD per day).

### Can I withdraw cash and find medical clinics in Mang Den?
Yes. Two 24-hour ATMs (Agribank and BIDV) stand on Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard (National Highway QL24) in the town center, dispensing up to 3,000,000 VND to 5,000,000 VND ($120.00 to $200.00 USD) per transaction on Visa and Mastercard cards. Always carry 500,000 VND ($20.00 USD) in small notes for village stalls. Basic urgent care is available 1.0 km away at the Kon Plong District Medical Center, while major hospital care is 50 km (60 minutes) southwest in Kon Tum City.

### When is the cherry blossom season in Mang Den?
Mang Den's 3,000 native pink cherry trees (*hoa mai anh đào*) bloom during a 30-day window between December 20 and January 25 when nighttime temperatures drop to 9°C to 13°C. Prime viewing corridors (all 0 VND / $0.00 USD entry) include the 1.5 km perimeter of Dak Ke Lake, Central Square (`Quảng Trường Măng Đen`), and the 3 km pine avenue along Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard.
