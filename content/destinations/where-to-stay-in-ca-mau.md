# Where to Stay in Ca Mau (2026): City Hotels to Dat Mui Eco-Lodges

Choosing where to stay in Ca Mau Province requires solving a 110 km geographic divide. Ca Mau City sits in the continental interior of the Mekong Delta, 305 km southwest of Ho Chi Minh City along National Route QL1A. In contrast, the southern continental terminus of Vietnam at Cape Ca Mau (Mui Ca Mau) lies inside Dat Mui Commune, accessible either via a 2-hour passenger speedboat from Ca Mau City or a 110 km drive along the Ho Chi Minh Highway. Travelers splitting their stay between downtown commercial hotels and remote mangrove eco-lodges gain access to both morning seafood wholesale markets and isolated coastal waterways.

> **Dat Mui Speedboat Terminal & Ferry Departure Rule:** High-speed passenger launches (Tàu cao tốc) run daily between Ca Mau Pier (Bến tàu khách Cà Mau on Ly Bon Street) and Dat Mui Pier. The one-way fare costs 140,000 VND per passenger for the 2-hour journey down the Ganh Hao and Cua Lon rivers. Boat operators depart promptly at 06:30, 08:30, and 12:30. If you take the highway route by bus, Futa Bus Lines (Phương Trang) runs hourly departures from Western Bus Station (Bến xe Miền Tây) in Ho Chi Minh City to Ca Mau Central Bus Station (Bến xe Cà Mau on Ly Thuong Kiet) for 230,000 VND per sleeper berth, taking 7.5 hours. From Ca Mau Central Bus Station, local green public buses (Tuyến 01) run southward to Nam Can for 45,000 VND, where local meter taxis charge 350,000 VND for the final 52 km leg along Ho Chi Minh Highway into Dat Mui village.

## 1. Tran Hung Dao & Ly Bon Commercial Waterfront (Ward 5 & Ward 2)

Urban accommodations in Ca Mau City concentrate across Ward 5 and Ward 2, split by the Ganh Hao River. Staying along Tran Hung Dao or Ly Bon places you within walking distance of the night market and local boat docks. Business hotels here cater to domestic traders, government workers, and tourists staging their transit to the cape.

### Hotel Selections on Tran Hung Dao
- **Anh Nguyet Hotel (Phuong 5, Tran Hung Dao):** Rates range between 650,000 VND and 1,200,000 VND per night. The property provides elevators, secure ground-floor motorbike parking, and reliable air conditioning. Rooms on higher floors avoid motorbike horn noise from the boulevard.
- **Quoc Te Hotel (Ly Thuong Kiet, Phuong 6):** Standard double rooms cost 450,000 VND to 700,000 VND per night. Located 400 meters from Ca Mau Central Bus Station, this location eliminates early-morning taxi transfers for onward buses back to Can Tho or Rach Gia.
- **Nha Nghi Mini Guesthouses (Phuong 2 & Ly Bon):** Family guesthouses charge 220,000 VND to 350,000 VND per night for private air-conditioned rooms. Water heaters operate on solar tanks, so evening showers maintain higher water temperatures than early morning arrivals.

Street dining along Tran Hung Dao concentrates around seafood halls. Live mud crabs (Cua Cà Mau) sell for 380,000 VND to 550,000 VND per kg depending on grade. Steamed mantis shrimp with sea salt and black pepper cost 280,000 VND per plate. Most downtown kitchens stop taking food orders at 21:00.

## 2. Hung Vuong Administrative Zone (Ward 1)

Ward 1 represents the modern administrative core of Ca Mau City. Broad tree-lined streets, landscaped boulevards, and newer concrete infrastructure characterize this sector. Government delegations and corporate engineers working on the Ca Mau Gas-Power-Fertilizer Complex base themselves here.

### Upscale Accommodations
- **Muong Thanh Luxury Ca Mau (Phuong 9, Ward 1 fringe):** Premium rooms range from 1,200,000 VND to 2,200,000 VND per night. Amenities include an outdoor swimming pool, international buffet breakfast, backup generator systems, and card-access elevators.
- **Phu Cuong Hotel (Ly Bon, Phuong 4):** Standard rooms cost between 950,000 VND and 1,600,000 VND per night. Patrons drinking at the rooftop bar look directly over the convergence of the Ganh Hao River and Ca Mau Canal. Concierge staff coordinate private car charters to Dat Mui for 1,400,000 VND round-trip.

Nighttime street activity in Ward 1 drops off significantly earlier than around the long-distance bus terminal. Motorbike taxis (xe ôm) cluster near Hung Vuong Park, charging 25,000 VND for short hops across the city center.

## 3. Dat Mui & Cape Ca Mau Mangrove Outposts

Staying overnight at Dat Mui Commune drops you directly into the brackish ecosystem of the Mui Ca Mau Biosphere Reserve. Instead of concrete high-rises, accommodation consists of wooden stilt houses constructed from mangrove timbers, suspended above tidal mudflats. Staying out here lets you watch the sun rise over the East Sea and set over the Gulf of Thailand from the same coastal point.

### Stilt Homestays & Community Lodges
- **Tu Ty Eco Homestay (Khu Du Lich Dat Mui):** Overnight lodging costs 250,000 VND per person in a communal timber hall with mattress and mosquito net, or 500,000 VND for a private waterfront bungalow. Showers use rainwater collected in ceramic cisterns combined with municipal trucked water.
- **Nguyen Hung Eco-Tourism Homestay (Ap Con Mui):** Private timber cabins rent for 450,000 VND to 750,000 VND per night. The host family leads evening mudflat foraging excursions to gather mudskippers (Cá thòi lòi) and blood cockles.
- **Mui Ca Mau Eco Lodge (Dat Mui):** Independent bungalows cost 600,000 VND to 950,000 VND per night, featuring solar-powered fans and screened river verandas.

```
* Daily high tides push brackish water directly beneath bedroom floorboards.
* Tap water carries noticeable salinity, requiring homestay hosts to distribute bottled rainwater for drinking.
* Electrical lines serving remote mangrove spurs suffer occasional evening brownouts lasting twenty minutes.
* Dense clouds of biting sandflies emerge from root systems at dusk around 17:30.
```

Homestay meals in Dat Mui highlight mangrove biodiversity. Grilled mudskippers with chili fish sauce cost 160,000 VND per portion. Fried egg-bearing mud crabs run 450,000 VND per kg. Boiled sea snails (Ốc len xào dừa) cooked in rich coconut cream cost 120,000 VND.

## 4. Forest Lodges on the U Minh Ha Peat Swamp Perimeter

U Minh Ha National Park lies 42 km northwest of Ca Mau City, covering 8,286 hectares of cajuput forest (Tràm) and peat soil swamp. Accommodations on the park perimeter sit alongside canal networks where local beekeepers harvest wild forest honey.

### Forest Farmstays & Eco Bases
- **Song Tram Forest Farmstay (Khanh An Commune):** Rustic timber bungalows rent for 350,000 VND to 600,000 VND per night. Guests can observe traditional honey collection (Gác kèo ong) during morning patrols.
- **Vo Doi Ranger Outpost Area Guesthouses:** Small family-run rooms along Provincial Road DT968 cost 250,000 VND to 400,000 VND per night. Facilities are basic, featuring hard mattresses, wall-mounted fans, and cold-water bucket showers.

Staying near U Minh Ha requires independent transport. Renting a 110cc semi-automatic scooter from downtown Ca Mau City costs 150,000 VND per day, plus 70,000 VND for a full tank of Ron 95 gasoline.

Reaching Ca Mau and transiting to Cape Ca Mau involves clear logistical links. Ca Mau Airport (CAH) on Ly Thuong Kiet handles turboprop ATR-72 flights from Ho Chi Minh City on select weekdays via Vietnam Airlines, with one-way airfares averaging 1,150,000 VND to 1,850,000 VND.

### Motorbike Rentals and Road Route Rules
- **Downtown Scooter Rental:** Independent repair shops near Ca Mau Central Bus Station rent Honda Waves for 150,000 VND per 24 hours. A cash deposit of 1,000,000 VND or passport retention is customary.
- **Nam Can Bridge Checkpoint:** Crossing the Nam Can Bridge across the Cua Lon River connects motorists to the final stretch of Ho Chi Minh Highway. Speed limits drop to 50 km/h across residential bends; provincial traffic police operate radar checkpoints regularly.
- **Boat Charters at Dat Mui:** Chartered wooden longtail motorboats (Vỏ lãi) carrying 4 passengers through the Core Zone of the Biosphere Reserve cost 600,000 VND for a 90-minute tour.

## Seasonal Rates & Weather Factors

Ca Mau experiences two distinct monsoon seasons that dictate room availability and outdoor comfort. The southern monsoon runs from May to November, delivering torrential afternoon squalls, while the northeast monsoon brings dry days between December and April.

- **Dry Season Pricing Dynamics (December through April):** Tourism peaks during Lunar New Year (Tet) and April liberation holidays. City hotels fill with domestic travelers. Dat Mui eco-lodges require booking at least 10 days in advance. Nightly rates rise 20% to 35% across all tiers.
- **Rainy Season Operations (May through November):** Afternoon downpours flood low-lying streets in Ward 5 along the canal margins. Homestays in Dat Mui offer 15% discounts, but boat travel across wide estuaries encounters strong choppy waves. Always pack heavy-duty dry bags for camera electronics when boarding speedboats during July and August squalls.

## Practical Security, Safety & Scam Prevention

Ca Mau remains one of the safest provinces in southern Vietnam regarding violent crime, but practical operational scams occur around transport and seafood dining.

- **Speedboat Overcharging at Ly Bon Pier:** Unofficial touts loitering outside the passenger terminal entrance frequently claim public speedboats are full, attempting to redirect travelers to private wooden launches charging 1,500,000 VND. Walk directly inside the ticketing window inside the station building to pay the legal 140,000 VND ticket.
- **Crab Weight Scale Manipulation:** Unscrupulous street stalls sometimes tie heavy soaked ropes around crab claws, adding up to 300 grams of paid weight per crustacean. Insist on inspecting crabs tied with slim nylon strings, or dine at established city venues like Quan Cua Thu Thiet on Nguyen Trai.
- **Brackish Water Corrosion:** Saltwater humidity near Dat Mui accelerates motorcycle chain rust and oxidizes smartphone charging ports. Wipe electronics down with dry microfiber towels nightly.
- **Mosquito & Dengue Protection:** Mangrove swamps host dense populations of black flies and Aedes mosquitoes. Apply DEET repellent containing 25% or higher concentration before 17:00.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I stay in Ca Mau City or sleep at Dat Mui?
Single-night visitors should base themselves in Ca Mau City for dependable air conditioning, diverse seafood dining, and fast access to early-morning express buses. Travelers with two or more nights can reserve their second night at a Dat Mui mangrove stilt homestay to watch the dawn over the East Sea and explore tidal waterways by boat.

### How do I travel between Ca Mau City center and Dat Mui?
Two viable transit corridors link the city to the cape. Travelers can board the public scheduled passenger launch from Ly Bon Pier for 140,000 VND, reaching Dat Mui in two hours via wide river estuaries. Alternatively, highway travelers can hire private meter taxis down National Route QL1A and the Ho Chi Minh Highway for approximately 1,100,000 VND to complete the 110 km journey in 2.5 hours.

### Can I withdraw cash at ATMs near Cape Ca Mau?
Banking services in Dat Mui are extremely limited. There is an Agribank ATM in Nam Can town 52 km north, but cash machines in Dat Mui village frequently run out of bills or reject international debit cards. Travelers should withdraw at least 1,500,000 VND in cash while in downtown Ca Mau City before heading south.

### Is tap water safe for brushing teeth in Dat Mui homestays?
No. Water supplies in coastal mangrove settlements are brackish and drawn from shallow underground aquifers or rain cisterns. Use bottled drinking water for drinking and brushing teeth to avoid bacterial stomach infections.
