# Da Nang Beaches Guide: My Khe, Non Nuoc or Hoi An Coast?

The continuous shoreline stretching from Da Nang's Son Tra Peninsula south to the Thu Bon River estuary at Cua Dai forms a **30-kilometer** ribbon of fine pale quartz sand. International travel brochures often group this entire coastal corridor under the single label of My Khe. On the ground, the coast divides into six distinct maritime sectors. High-rise urban beaches along Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard transition within **15 minutes** into gated five-star resort strands at Non Nuoc, granitic jungle coves around Son Tra, digital nomad surf breaks at An Thuong, and thatched-roof fishing villages bordering Hoi An Ancient Town.

Selecting where to book your hotel or spend your beach days depends on your travel month, your preference for roped swimming zones versus winter surf breaks, and how often you plan to commute the **28 km** (**40 minutes**, **320,000 VND / $12.80 USD** by GrabCar) between central Da Nang and Hoi An. This 2026 operational guide compares every coastal sector using verified municipal lounger tariffs, lifeguard patrol hours, surfboard and jet-ski rental rates, seafood market pricing, and seasonal wave data.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Seasonal Wave Inversion (Winter Swell vs. Summer Calm):** Central Vietnam's coastal waters split into two distinct marine seasons. From **April through August**, the East Sea remains flat and clear with water temperatures of **27°C to 30°C**, gentle **0.3-meter to 0.6-meter** ripples, and **8.0 hours** of daily sun for family swimming, snorkeling, and stand-up paddleboarding. From **late September through March**, the Northeast Monsoon (*gio mua Dong Bac*) pushes **1.2-meter to 2.5-meter** swells onto My Khe and Non Nuoc, turning Da Nang into Vietnam's surf capital while prompting municipal lifeguards to enforce red-flag swimming closures on rough days.

## 2. Da Nang Urban Beaches: Pham Van Dong, My Khe & Bac My An

### My Khe & Pham Van Dong Beaches: The Serviced Urban Core

Stretching **4.2 kilometers** along Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard from East Sea Park (*Cong Vien Bien Dong*) south to Nguyen Van Thoai Street, [My Khe Beach](/destinations/best-things-to-do-in-da-nang/) serves as Vietnam's cleanest and most heavily patrolled city shoreline. Only **3.5 km** (**8 minutes** by taxi for **65,000 VND / $2.60 USD**) from Dragon Bridge (*Cau Rong*) and **6.5 km** (**15 minutes**, **110,000 VND / $4.40 USD**) from Da Nang International Airport (DAD), this sector pairs wide **60-meter to 90-meter** low-tide sandbanks with immediate city convenience.

The Management Board of Son Tra Peninsula and Da Nang Tourist Beaches operates staffed lifeguard towers along three numbered swimming zones (*Bai tam so 1, 2, 3*) plus Pham Van Dong Park from **04:30 to 19:00** between April and September (and **05:00 to 18:00** in winter). Bright yellow and red floating buoy lines cordon off swimmer-only zones **50 meters to 80 meters** offshore, physically blocking jet-skis and parasailing boats from entering bathing areas. Municipal concrete changing rooms at Pham Van Dong Park and Sao Bien charge fixed statutory rates: **5,000 VND ($0.20 USD)** for a freshwater rinse, **10,000 VND ($0.40 USD)** for soap showers, and **5,000 VND to 10,000 VND ($0.20 to $0.40 USD)** for guarded motorbike parking.

Public wooden deckchairs beneath thatched palm umbrellas rent for a regulated flat rate of **40,000 VND to 50,000 VND ($1.60 to $2.00 USD)** for the entire day. Cold fresh coconuts cost **30,000 VND to 40,000 VND ($1.20 to $1.60 USD)**, and chilled local Larue or Huda beers sell for **25,000 VND to 35,000 VND ($1.00 to $1.40 USD)**. Licensed water-sport operators near East Sea Park and My Khe Beach Sector 2 offer 1300cc Yamaha jet-ski rentals at **600,000 VND ($24.00 USD)** for **15 minutes** or **1,000,000 VND ($40.00 USD)** for **30 minutes**, single parasailing flights at **550,000 VND ($22.00 USD)** (**800,000 VND / $32.00 USD** tandem for **10 minutes** at **70 meters** altitude), and 5-person banana boat tows for **750,000 VND ($30.00 USD)** per **15-minute** ride. High-rise 4-star and 5-star beachfront hotels lining Vo Nguyen Giap Street (such as Sala Danang Beach Hotel, TMS Hotel, and Hilton Garden Inn) price ocean-view rooms at **1,200,000 VND to 2,600,000 VND ($48.00 to $104.00 USD)** per night.

Dawn is the signature cultural hour on My Khe. By **05:15**, over 3,000 local residents cycle or jog down to the water to swim **300 meters** along the buoy line, play beach volleyball, and practice synchronized laughter yoga before the tropical UV index climbs past 8 at **08:30**.

### Bac My An & The An Thuong Surf Enclave

Walking south past the Nguyen Van Thoai intersection, My Khe connects directly into **Bac My An Beach**, fronting the **An Thuong Tourist Quarter** (*Khu pho tay An Thuong*, spanning An Thuong 1 through An Thuong 38 streets). Situated **4.5 km** from the city center, this walkable **1.5-square-kilometer** grid acts as Central Vietnam's hub for remote workers, surfers, and solo travelers. Surf culture anchors the neighborhood. Boutique studio apartments and 3-star hotels situated **150 meters to 400 meters** from the sand rent for **450,000 VND to 900,000 VND ($18.00 to $36.00 USD)** per night (or **7,500,000 VND to 12,000,000 VND / $300.00 to $480.00 USD** per month).

Along the beachfront promenade fronting My An Beach Club and Holiday Beach, surf outfitters including **Danang Surf School**, **Surf Shack**, and **Sunny Surf** operate daily from **06:00 to 18:00**. Soft-top beginner foam boards (8 ft to 9 ft) and epoxy shortboards rent for **150,000 VND to 250,000 VND ($6.00 to $10.00 USD)** for **2 hours** or **350,000 VND to 500,000 VND ($14.00 to $20.00 USD)** for a **24-hour** full-day rental. Private **90-minute** surf lessons with ISA-certified instructors cost **850,000 VND to 1,100,000 VND ($34.00 to $44.00 USD)** per student, while 2-to-4-person group lessons cost **650,000 VND ($26.00 USD)** per person. During calm summer mornings (**05:00 to 08:00**, May to August), the same kiosks rent inflatable stand-up paddleboards (SUP) for **150,000 VND to 200,000 VND ($6.00 to $8.00 USD)** per **2-hour** sunrise session.

## 3. Southern Sands, Son Tra Coves & Da Nang Bay

### Non Nuoc Beach: The Five-Star Resort Corridor

Beginning **8.5 km** south of Dragon Bridge along Truong Sa Boulevard directly east of the [Marble Mountains](/destinations/best-things-to-do-in-da-nang/) (*Ngu Hanh Son*), **Non Nuoc Beach** arcs for **5.0 kilometers** down to the Quang Nam provincial border. Unlike the high-density 30-story hotel towers of My Khe, Non Nuoc is zoned for low-rise luxury resorts set inside **15-hectare to 35-hectare** landscaped beachfront compounds, including **Hyatt Regency Danang Resort & Spa**, **Melia Danang Beach Resort**, **Marriott Resort Danang Non Nuoc**, and **Sheraton Grand Danang** (room rates **3,200,000 VND to 6,800,000 VND / $128.00 to $272.00 USD** per night).

Under Vietnamese maritime law, all intertidal sand remains open to the public for **0 VND**, even in front of five-star resorts. Independent visitors reach Non Nuoc via the municipal public beach access road beside the Hoa Hai ward stone-carving village (**9.2 km** from central Da Nang, **15 minutes** by GrabCar for **130,000 VND / $5.20 USD**), where scooter parking costs **10,000 VND ($0.40 USD)** and freshwater showers cost **10,000 VND ($0.40 USD)**. Because resort groundskeepers rake the sand every morning at **05:30** and day-tripper crowds remain under **10%** of My Khe's volume, Non Nuoc provides a quiet coastal setting. Travelers frequently pair a **2-hour** morning climb up **Thuy Son** at the Marble Mountains (**800 meters** inland, admission **40,000 VND / $1.60 USD**, glass elevator **15,000 VND / $0.60 USD** each way, Am Phu Cave **20,000 VND / $0.80 USD**) between **07:00 and 09:00** with a cooling swim at Non Nuoc before lunch.

### Son Tra Peninsula Coves: Bai But, Bai Rang & Bai Da Den

North of My Khe, Hoang Sa Road climbs the southern granite flanks of the **4,400-hectare Son Tra Nature Reserve** (*Ban dao Son Tra*, peaking at **696 meters** elevation). Between **10 km and 15 km** from the city center (**20 minutes** by motorbike or GrabCar for **160,000 VND to 220,000 VND / $6.40 to $8.80 USD**), the coastline breaks into rocky headlands and sheltered turquoise bays sheltered from southern winds.

- **Bai But (*Bãi Bụt*) & Linh Ung Pagoda:** Situated **10.5 km** northeast of Dragon Bridge directly below the **67-meter** white Lady Buddha statue (*Chua Linh Ung Bai But*, admission **0 VND**, open **06:00 to 21:00**). Anchored fishing basket boats (*thung chai*) dot the calm bay, and the InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort occupies the private Bai Bac cove **4.5 km** further east (day passes when available cost **1,500,000 VND to 2,500,000 VND / $60.00 to $100.00 USD** redeemable on dining).
- **Bai Rang (*Bãi Rạng*) & Bai Nam:** Located **12.5 km** from downtown along Hoang Sa Road. Steep wooden stairs descend **40 meters** through coastal boulders to a **600-meter** crescent of coarse golden sand and smooth granite rocks. Local families rent private shaded wooden stilt huts (*choi go*) over the rocks for **150,000 VND to 300,000 VND ($6.00 to $12.00 USD)** for **4 hours** (entry only costs **20,000 VND / $0.80 USD** plus **5,000 VND** motorbike parking). From May to August, group SUP and snorkeling tours launch from Man Thai fishing beach (**6 km** north of Dragon Bridge) at **05:15** to paddle **2.5 km** out to Hon Sup rock and the Son Tra nearshore coral reef (**2.0 meters to 4.5 meters** depth) for **250,000 VND to 350,000 VND ($10.00 to $14.00 USD)** per person including board, life vest, snorkel mask, and drone photos.

### Xuan Thieu & Nguyen Tat Thanh Beach (Da Nang Bay)

Often overlooked by short-stay tourists, the **12-kilometer** curved bay fronting **Nguyen Tat Thanh Boulevard** (**6 km to 14 km** northwest of Dragon Bridge toward the foot of Hai Van Pass) faces inside sheltered Da Nang Bay rather than the open East Sea. Because the Son Tra Peninsula blocks open-ocean swells, **Xuan Thieu Beach** (*Bai bien Xuan Thieu*, also called Red Beach) maintains gentler waves than My Khe during shoulder months. Recent investments such as Da Nang Mikazuki Japanese Resorts & Spa (featuring a year-round indoor heated water park, day ticket **350,000 VND to 450,000 VND / $14.00 to $18.00 USD**) make Xuan Thieu a solid rainy-day backup for families with children.

> [!WARNING]
> **Recognizing Rip Currents and Red Flag Warnings:** Open-ocean beaches at My Khe, Bac My An, and Non Nuoc develop strong seaward rip currents (*dong chay xa bo*) where sandbars break, especially during tide changes and from September to March. Rip currents look like darker, calmer channels of water cutting across white breaking surf. Never enter the water where lifeguards plant black-and-red warning boards or red flags (*co do cam tam*). If pulled seaward by a rip current, do not swim straight back against the flow; tread water, signal a lifeguard, and swim parallel to the shore for **30 meters to 50 meters** until you exit the narrow channel.

## 4. The Hoi An Coast: Ha My, An Bang & Cua Dai

### An Bang Beach: Village Character & Beach Clubs

Located in Quang Nam province just **4.5 kilometers** northeast of [Hoi An Ancient Town](/destinations/hoi-an-ancient-town-guide/) (a **15-minute** flat bicycle ride along Hai Ba Trung Street through the Cam Ha rice paddies, or a **10-minute** GrabCar ride costing **70,000 VND to 100,000 VND / $2.80 to $4.00 USD**), **An Bang Beach** is the social hub of the Hoi An shoreline. Strict height regulations prevent high-rise towers here; instead, narrow sandy lanes lined with bougainvillea lead to low-slung thatched beach bars, wooden bungalows (**800,000 VND to 1,800,000 VND / $32.00 to $72.00 USD** per night), and open-air seafood decks shaded by palms.

Established beachfront venues such as **The DeckHouse An Bang**, **Shore Club**, **Soul Kitchen**, and **Dolphin Kitchen** provide complimentary cushioned sun loungers, umbrellas, towels, and freshwater showers whenever you order food or drinks (smoothies and fresh juices cost **65,000 VND to 95,000 VND / $2.60 to $3.80 USD**, craft beers **75,000 VND to 110,000 VND / $3.00 to $4.40 USD**, and wood-fired pizzas or grilled seafood platters **180,000 VND to 380,000 VND / $7.20 to $15.20 USD**). Independent local vendors along the public sand rent twin loungers under a palm parasol for **40,000 VND to 50,000 VND ($1.60 to $2.00 USD)** per day. Official municipal bicycle parking at the main Hai Ba Trung entrance costs **5,000 VND ($0.20 USD)**, and scooter parking costs **10,000 VND ($0.40 USD)**.

### Quiet Coastlines: Ha My Stretch & Restored Cua Dai

- **Ha My Beach (*Bãi biển Hà My*):** Situated **3.5 km** north of An Bang (**7.5 km** from Hoi An Ancient Town and **20 km** south of Da Nang). Framed by casuarina pines and home to the ultra-luxury **Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai** (**16,000,000 VND to 28,000,000 VND / $640.00 to $1,120.00 USD** per villa night) alongside quiet family homestays (**600,000 VND to 1,100,000 VND / $24.00 to $44.00 USD**), Ha My receives a fraction of An Bang's foot traffic. Early visitors at **05:30** watch coastal fishermen haul hand-pulled shore nets onto the wide flat sand.
- **Cua Dai Beach (*Bãi biển Cửa Đại*) & Cham Islands Pier:** Located **4.0 km** southeast of An Bang along Au Co Street. After severe typhoon erosion narrowed Cua Dai between 2014 and 2020, Quang Nam province installed a **1.8-kilometer** offshore submerged geotube breakwater and pumped **600,000 cubic meters** of sand back onto the northern beachfront near Victoria Hoi An Beach Resort and Palm Garden Resort (**2,200,000 VND to 3,800,000 VND / $88.00 to $152.00 USD** nightly). Just **2.5 km** south of Cua Dai beach sits **Cua Dai Port**, where daily high-speed canoes depart at **08:30 and 09:00** for the **20-minute** (**18 km**) crossing to **Cu Lao Cham (Cham Islands)** UNESCO Biosphere Reserve (standalone round-trip speedboat ticket **350,000 VND to 400,000 VND / $14.00 to $16.00 USD** plus **70,000 VND / $2.80 USD** marine conservation fee and **20,000 VND / $0.80 USD** port fee).

## 5. Step-by-Step Coast Selection & Daily Logistics (HowTo)

1. **Choose Your Overnight Base by Evening Routine:** Book along **My Khe or An Thuong (Bac My An)** if you want **15-minute** airport transfers (**110,000 VND / $4.40 USD**), rooftop skybars, weekend Dragon Bridge fire shows (**21:00** on Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays), and morning surf breaks. Base yourself at **An Bang or Ha My Beach** if you want a low-rise village setting within a **10-minute** (**80,000 VND / $3.20 USD**) taxi or **15-minute** bicycle ride of Hoi An's lantern-lit heritage streets.
2. **Schedule Beach Time Around UV Peaks:** Between May and August, midday temperatures hit **34°C to 37°C** and sand surface temperatures exceed **48°C** between **11:00 and 15:00**. Swim during the calm dawn window (**05:30 to 08:00**) or the late afternoon sea-breeze window (**16:00 to 18:15**), using midday hours for air-conditioned museum visits, spa massages (**350,000 VND to 550,000 VND / $14.00 to $22.00 USD** for **60 minutes**), or custom tailoring in Hoi An.
3. **Use Split Stays for Trips Over 5 Nights:** Rather than spending **600,000 VND ($24.00 USD)** daily commuting **56 km** round-trip between Da Nang and Hoi An, split a **6-night** Central Vietnam holiday into **3 nights** at My Khe or Non Nuoc (covering Son Tra Peninsula, Marble Mountains, and Ba Na Hills **35 km** west) followed by **3 nights** in Hoi An or An Bang Beach. A private car transfer between Da Nang and Hoi An with a **90-minute** stop at Marble Mountains costs **450,000 VND to 550,000 VND ($18.00 to $22.00 USD)**.
4. **Verify Live Seafood Pricing Before Ordering:** Da Nang's Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard is famous for open-air live-tank seafood restaurants (*nha hang hai san tuoi song*). Always walk to the aerated glass tanks with your server, watch the staff weigh your crab, prawns, or grouper in a perforated plastic basket drained of water on an electronic scale, and confirm the per-kilogram price (**VND/kg**) printed on the digital LED board before cooking begins.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### Which beach is best for families traveling with young children?
**Non Nuoc Beach** (for resort-based families) and **An Bang Beach** (for boutique/villa families) are the top family picks from April through August. Non Nuoc resorts such as Hyatt Regency Danang and Centara Sandy Beach offer zero-entry children's pools, shaded lawns, and private beach lifeguards steps from your room (**3,200,000 VND+ / $128.00+ USD**). At An Bang, venues like The DeckHouse and Soul Kitchen sit directly on flat, gradually sloping sand where parents can watch kids build sandcastles from shaded dining tables (**60,000 VND to 250,000 VND / $2.40 to $10.00 USD** per dish).

### Can I surf in Da Nang, and when is the best surf season?
Yes, Da Nang is Vietnam's most reliable surf town. The main surf season runs for **6 months from October through March**, when northeast monsoon winds produce consistent **1.0-meter to 2.0-meter** (**3-foot to 6-foot**) beach-break waves at **My Khe and Bac My An (An Thuong)**. Water temperatures in December and January dip to **22°C to 24°C**, so most surfers wear a **2 mm** neoprene spring suit or rashguard top. Board rentals along An Thuong cost **150,000 VND to 250,000 VND ($6.00 to $10.00 USD)** for **2 hours**, and private **90-minute** coaching sessions cost **850,000 VND to 1,100,000 VND ($34.00 to $44.00 USD)**.

### How does My Khe compare to the beaches of Nha Trang or Phu Quoc?
My Khe has a much gentler underwater slope and finer white-gold sand than Nha Trang's central Tran Phu Beach, which drops off steeply into deeper water within **10 meters** of shore. Compared to [Phu Quoc Island](/destinations/best-islands-in-vietnam/), Da Nang offers a **30 km** continuous coastline with far better urban infrastructure and cultural day trips (Hoi An and Hue), whereas Phu Quoc's west-coast beaches (Long Beach and Ong Lang) are calmest from **November through March** when Da Nang has winter surf swells. Compare seasonal windows across the country in our [Best Beaches in Vietnam Guide](/destinations/best-beaches-in-vietnam/).

### Where are the best beachfront seafood restaurants along My Khe and An Bang, and what do they cost?
Along northern My Khe Beach (Vo Nguyen Giap Street, Son Tra District), reliable high-turnover restaurants with transparent digital scales include **Hai San Be Man** (Lot 8–9 Vo Nguyen Giap), **Hai San Nam Danh** (139/59/38 Tran Quang Khai, tucked **200 meters** behind the beach with **60,000 VND to 100,000 VND / $2.40 to $4.00 USD** dishes), and **My Hanh Seafood** (03–05 Vo Nguyen Giap, MICHELIN Selected). Typical 2026 live-tank rates at My Khe are **450,000 VND to 650,000 VND ($18.00 to $26.00 USD) per kg** for live tiger prawns (*tom su*), **350,000 VND to 450,000 VND ($14.00 to $18.00 USD) per kg** for sweet sea snails (*oc huong*), and **650,000 VND to 850,000 VND ($26.00 to $34.00 USD) per kg** for mud crab (*cua thit*). At An Bang Beach, family restaurants like **Nam Gia** and **7 Son** serve whole grilled red snapper or mackerel wrapped in banana leaf for **180,000 VND to 280,000 VND ($7.20 to $11.20 USD)**.
