# Da Nang Beaches Guide: My Khe, Non Nuoc or Hoi An Coast?

The shoreline stretching from Da Nang's Son Tra Peninsula south to the Thu Bon river mouth at Hoi An forms a continuous `30-kilometer` ribbon of pale quartz sand. While international brochures often label this entire coast under the single name of My Khe, the coastal strip actually subdivides into five distinct maritime environments. High-rise urban sands in central Da Nang transition into secluded resort coves, quiet expat enclaves, and low-slung fishing villages fringing Hoi An's ancient core.

Choosing where to base your beach time depends on your travel season, your need for roped swimming zones versus surf breaks, and how frequently you plan to explore the evening lantern streets of Hoi An. This guide analyzes Da Nang and northern Quang Nam's coastal sectors using verified 2026 lounger rates, safety patrol schedules, water-sports tariffs, and wave conditions.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Seasonal Wave Inversion (Winter Swell vs. Summer Calm):** Central Vietnam's coastal conditions split into two distinct periods. From **April to August**, the East Sea is flat and emerald, with water temperatures hovering between `27°C and 30°C`—ideal for swimming, stand-up paddleboarding, and family wading. From **late September through March**, the Northeast Monsoon (`gió mùa Đông Bắc`) drives consistent `1.0 to 2.5-meter` swells onto My Khe and Non Nuoc, transforming the urban coast into Vietnam's surf capital while triggering red-flag swimming closures on rough days.

## 2. Da Nang Urban Beaches: My Khe & Bac My An

### My Khe Beach: The Urban Powerhouse
Fronting Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard directly east of Da Nang's Han River bridges, [My Khe Beach](/destinations/best-things-to-do-in-da-nang/) is the most heavily serviced and thoroughly protected urban beach in Vietnam. Safety remains paramount here. The municipal management board operates staffed lifeguard stations along numbered swimming sectors (`Bãi tắm số 1, 2, 3`) from `05:00 to 19:00` daily during summer months. Roped zones keep swimmers safe from motorized watercraft, while clean municipal shower pavilions charge a fixed `5,000 VND` fee.

Public lounger concessions under thatched umbrellas rent for `40,000 to 50,000 VND` (`~$1.60–$2.00 USD`) per day, with coconut vendors selling cold fresh coconuts for `30,000 to 45,000 VND`. Active travelers rent jet-skis for `600,000 to 800,000 VND` per `15 minutes`. Tandem parasailing flights over the bay cost `500,000 to 800,000 VND` per person. Dawn swimmers arrive early. Around `05:30`, thousands of local residents gather to swim and practice tai chi before the tropical sun climbs, creating a lively community atmosphere.

### Bac My An & The An Thuong Enclave
Continuing south along the sand, My Khe flows seamlessly into Bac My An, fronting the An Thuong tourist quarter. Anchored by streets named after Southeast Asian capitals, An Thuong serves as Da Nang's premier enclave for international travelers, long-stay nomads, and surf enthusiasts. Beach access points around the Holiday Beach hotel area feature board rental kiosks (`Danang Surf School`, `Surf Shack`) where fiberglass and soft-top surfboards rent for `150,000 to 250,000 VND` per hour or `400,000 to 600,000 VND` for a full day.

Between October and February, sandbars along Bac My An generate clean left and right peelers that accommodate both beginners on whitewash and intermediate longboarders. The sidewalk promenade is lined with craft breweries, vegan cafes, and relaxed cocktail terraces where you can step directly off the sand into an air-conditioned workspace.

## 3. Southern Sands & Secluded Coves: Non Nuoc to Son Tra

### Non Nuoc Beach: The Resort Corridor
Fronting the coastal plain immediately east of the [Marble Mountains](/destinations/best-things-to-do-in-da-nang/) (`Ngũ Hành Sơn`), Non Nuoc Beach stretches for `5 kilometers` of soft, gently sloping sand. This section is dominated by five-star properties including the Hyatt Regency Danang Resort, Vinpearl Resort, and Melia Danang. While all Vietnamese beach sands below the high-tide mark remain public property by law, private access lanes can be long; public entry paths with scooter parking (`10,000 VND`) sit near the southern tip of Truong Sa Street.

Because coastal resorts groom their frontages daily, Non Nuoc offers a pristine environment with far fewer day-trippers than My Khe. Water conditions remain calm from May through July, making it an excellent base for families seeking resort amenities and tranquil morning walks. The proximity to Marble Mountains also allows travelers to climb Thuy Son peak (`40,000 VND` admission plus `15,000 VND` glass elevator) in the cool morning before walking down to the beach for a swim.

### Son Tra Peninsula Coves: Bai Rang & Bai But
Travelers who prefer granitic rock formations over endless sandbanks should head north toward the Son Tra Nature Reserve. Nestled along the southern flank of the peninsula beneath the `67-meter` Lady Buddha statue of Linh Ung Pagoda (`Chùa Linh Ứng Bãi Bụt`), Bai Rang (`Bãi Rạng`) and Bai But (`Bãi Bụt`) feature protected coves where green jungle drops straight into turquoise water.

Local family-run stilt restaurants rent shaded wooden platforms (`100,000 to 300,000 VND` for half a day) where you can order fresh steamed sea snails, grilled squid, and hot fish porridge. During calm summer mornings (**April to July**), nearshore coral beds in `2 to 4 meters` of water can be reached on rented stand-up paddleboards (`150,000 to 250,000 VND` per hour). Watch the shoreline carefully: wild red-shanked douc langurs occasionally forage in the canopy above the coastal road.

> [!WARNING]
> **Recognizing Rip Currents and Red Flag Warnings:** Like many oceanic coasts, Da Nang's beaches develop powerful rip currents (`dòng chảy xa bờ`), particularly during autumn transitions and outgoing tides. Rip currents appear as calm, darker channels of water between breaking waves that carry floating foam seaward. Never swim outside roped lifeguard zones when red warning flags (`cờ đỏ cấm tắm`) fly on the towers. If caught in a current, stay calm, float on your back, and swim parallel to the beach until you escape the pull.

## 4. The Hoi An Coast: An Bang, Ha My & Cua Dai

### An Bang Beach: Village Character & Beach Bars
Sitting `4 kilometers` north of [Hoi An Ancient Town](/destinations/hoi-an-travel-guide/) and reachable in `15 to 20 minutes` on an easy bicycle ride through the Cam Ha rice paddies, An Bang is the undisputed social heart of the Hoi An coast. Unlike Da Nang's modern boulevard towers, An Bang preserves a village scale of thatched-bamboo beach clubs, wooden beach loungers, and seafood stalls shaded by casuarina trees.

Most venues (such as Soul Kitchen, The Deck House, and Shore Club) provide free use of cushioned loungers and freshwater showers if you purchase a fresh fruit smoothie or beer (`50,000 to 90,000 VND`). Independent loungers rent for `40,000 to 50,000 VND`. The sea floor deepens more gradually here than at central My Khe, creating family-friendly swimming conditions from March through September.

### Quiet Coastlines: Ha My Stretch & Restored Cua Dai
North of An Bang lies Ha My Beach, a tranquil strip fronted by coconut groves and luxury villas including the Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai. This shoreline remains remarkably peaceful, visited primarily by morning fishermen hauling shore seines and couples seeking uncrowded sand.

South of An Bang, Cua Dai Beach was once the primary resort shore of Hoi An until severe coastal erosion washed away large sand volumes between 2015 and 2020. Today, massive submerged geotextile groynes and ongoing sand-nourishment projects have successfully stabilized the northern sectors near Victoria Hoi An Beach Resort, though southern sections remain fortified with rock revetments. For broad sandy lounging, An Bang and Ha My remain the superior choices.

## 5. Step-by-Step Coast Selection & Daily Logistics (HowTo)

1. **Pick Your Base Based on Your Evening Priorities:** Stay along **My Khe or Bac My An** if you want modern city amenities, international dining, rooftop bars, and surf lessons. Base near **An Bang Beach** if you want bicycle-friendly village life and easy evening tuk-tuk runs into Hoi An Ancient Town (`80,000–120,000 VND` via GrabCar).
2. **Time Your Swim for Local Comfort Windows:** Tropical midday sun is intense between `11:00 and 15:00`. Follow local residents by swimming during the dawn golden hour (`05:30–07:30`) or the breezy late afternoon (`16:00–18:00`) when municipal lifeguards are fully active and beach volleyball games assemble.
3. **Budget for Transport Between Hubs:** A private car or Grab ride between central Da Nang (`Dragon Bridge`) and An Bang Beach takes `35 minutes` and costs `280,000 to 350,000 VND` (`~$11–$14 USD`). If visiting both areas, split your stay: book `3 nights` in Da Nang for city sightseeing and Son Tra drives, followed by `3 nights` in Hoi An for heritage walking and village beaches.
4. **Secure Parking Receipts:** When arriving by rented scooter at My Khe or An Bang, pull into official marked parking bays. Attendants charge a regulated fee of `5,000 to 10,000 VND` and provide a chalk mark or plastic ticket; avoid unlicensed individuals waving flags on roadway shoulders.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### Which beach is best for families traveling with young children?
**An Bang Beach** (near Hoi An) and **Non Nuoc Beach** (in southern Da Nang) are the best choices for families. Both feature wide stretches of soft sand, gradual water drop-offs during summer, and sheltered dining pavilions right behind the tideline. Families staying at Non Nuoc resorts also benefit from dedicated kids' clubs and beachfront lagoon pools.

### Can I surf in Da Nang, and where should I rent a board?
Yes, Da Nang is Vietnam's main surf hub. The surf season runs from **October through March**, with winter monsoon swells creating consistent `3-to-6-foot` waves. Head to the beach breaks fronting **An Thuong (Bac My An)**, where outfitters such as Danang Surf School and Surf Shack provide board rentals (`150,000–250,000 VND/hour`) and beginner lessons (`800,000–1,100,000 VND` for a 90-minute private coaching session).

### How does My Khe compare to the beaches of Nha Trang or Phu Quoc?
My Khe offers wider, cleaner sands and better municipal safety infrastructure than Nha Trang's urban beach, without the steep drop-offs. Compared to [Phu Quoc Island](/destinations/best-islands-in-vietnam/), Da Nang's beach is larger and far more walkable, but Phu Quoc's west-coast coves remain flatter and clearer during the northern winter months (**November to April**). Read our complete [Best Beaches in Vietnam Guide](/destinations/best-beaches-in-vietnam/) for regional climate comparisons.

### Where are the best beachfront seafood restaurants located along the coast?
For authentic local seafood with live seawater tanks and transparent digital scales, visit **Quan Be Man** (`Quán Bé Mặn`, Lot 11 Vo Nguyen Giap) or **Quan My Hanh** along the northern end of My Khe Beach. Steamed sweet snails (`ốc hương`), tamarind-fried tiger prawns (`tôm sú rang me`), and grilled clams (`nghêu nướng mỡ hành`) cost between `120,000 and 350,000 VND` per portion. If staying near Hoi An, the rustic thatch-roof shacks flanking the southern entrance of An Bang Beach serve fresh mackerel grilled in banana leaf for `100,000 to 160,000 VND`.
