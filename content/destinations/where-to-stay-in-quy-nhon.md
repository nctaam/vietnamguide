# Where to Stay in Quy Nhon (2026): Beach Areas & Resorts

Framed by Ghenh Rang's granite headlands and the sand dunes of Phuong Mai Peninsula, Quy Nhon in Binh Dinh Province remains South-Central Vietnam's best-value coastal city. Live-tank seafood dinners and beachfront hotel rooms here cost 30%–45% less than comparable stays in Nha Trang or Da Nang. Dry beach season runs from March through August. Daytime highs sit at 28°C to 34°C while offshore winds flatten the turquoise coves of Ky Co and Hon Kho into glass.

Quy Nhon's coastline splits into an urban crescent, southern granite coves, and a dramatic peninsula across Thi Nai Lagoon. Book along the 5 km **Xuan Dieu and An Duong Vuong City Beach Crescent** for walkable seafood boulevards, fishcake noodle shops, and four-star or five-star ocean-view hotels at 750,000–2,400,000 VND ($30–$96 USD) per night. Head 12–15 km south along coastal road **QL1D** for the car-free fishing alleys of **Bai Xep** (500,000–2,800,000 VND) or the private bay luxury of **Bai Dai** (3,200,000–22,000,000 VND). Cross the 2.47 km **Thi Nai Bridge** to **Nhon Ly**, **Nhon Hai**, or **Cat Tien** for morning speedboats to Ky Co and Hon Kho Island.

> [!TIP]
> **Phu Cat Airport (UIH) & Dieu Tri Train Transfers:** **Phu Cat Airport (UIH)** lies 32 km northwest of downtown Quy Nhon. Outside Arrivals, the official **Airport Shuttle Bus** departs 20 minutes after every flight and drops passengers at **01 Nguyen Tat Thanh Street** in central Quy Nhon for 50,000 VND ($2.00 USD; 40 minutes), while a metered taxi or GrabCar costs 300,000–350,000 VND ($12–$14 USD). Arriving on **SE1/SE3** Reunification Express trains or **The Vietage** luxury rail carriage? Alight at **Dieu Tri Railway Station**, 12 km west of Xuan Dieu Beach (20 minutes, 140,000–170,000 VND by taxi).

## 1. Xuan Dieu & An Duong Vuong City Beach Crescent (Downtown Quy Nhon)

Sweeping 5 km in a golden-sand curve right across downtown Quy Nhon, **Xuan Dieu Boulevard** and its southern extension **An Duong Vuong Boulevard** anchor the city's walkable beachfront. Commercial sunbed touts are rare here. Locals swim at 05:30 and 17:00 before gathering under candlelit palms at open-air **Surf Bar** on the sand (fruit smoothies and iced coffee 45,000–65,000 VND / $1.80–$2.60 USD).

Modern four-star and five-star sea-view hotels line the crescent at 750,000–2,400,000 VND ($30–$96 USD) per night, all within a 10 minutes walk of the **01 Nguyen Tat Thanh** airport shuttle stop. Top properties include **Fleur De Lys Hotel Quy Nhon** (16 Nguyen Hue, 1,500,000–2,400,000 VND, with full bay balconies and rooftop lounge), **Anya Premier Hotel Quy Nhon** (44 An Duong Vuong, 1,400,000–2,200,000 VND, boasting a 24th-floor rooftop infinity pool facing the central park), **Seagull Hotel** (*Khách sạn Hải Âu*, 489 An Duong Vuong, 850,000–1,400,000 VND directly on the sand), and **L'Amor Boutique Hotel** (1H1 Nguyen Thi Dinh, 750,000–1,300,000 VND), while resort-seekers compare these urban towers with **AVANI Quy Nhon Resort** (3,200,000–5,800,000 VND) 15 minutes south.

Dinner requires zero taxis. Stroll **Xuan Dieu Seafood Boulevard** for steamed **Cua Huỳnh Đế** emperor crab (750,000–950,000 VND/kg) and lemongrass clams at **Ốc Thúy Kiều** (5D Tang Bat Ho, 30,000–50,000 VND per dish). Five blocks inland along **Nguyen Hue and Dien Hong Food Streets**, order crispy jumping-shrimp rice crepes wrapped in mustard greens at **Bánh xèo tôm nhảy Gia Vỹ 2** (14 Dien Hong, 35,000–55,000 VND) and mackerel fishcake noodle soup at **Bún chả cá Ngọc Liên** (379 Nguyen Hue, 40,000 VND / $1.60 USD). The 12th-century **Thap Doi Twin Cham Towers** (20,000 VND entry) stand 3 km northwest.

## 2. Bai Xep Fishing Village (Ghềnh Ráng — 12 km South on QL1D)

Twelve kilometers south of Quy Nhon along coastal highway **QL1D** lies **Bai Xep** (*Ghềnh Ráng*), a traditional fishing village sheltering a granite boulder cove. Cars cannot enter the narrow village lanes. Taxis drop you at the highway arch (a 15-minute, 150,000 VND / $6.00 USD ride from downtown), and you walk 200m down car-free brick alleys past bougainvillea trellises and painted bamboo coracle boats (*thuyền thúng*) to the sand.

Nightly rates span 500,000–2,800,000 VND ($20–$112 USD). Backpackers, solo travelers, and indie couples book **Life's a Beach** (500,000–1,100,000 VND / $20–$44 USD for bamboo dorms and sea-breeze bungalows), **Haven Vietnam** (1,100,000–1,900,000 VND / $44–$76 USD for beachfront guesthouse rooms and wood-fired pizza nights on the sand), or **Mira Bai Xep** (900,000–1,600,000 VND). Travelers wanting a full resort swimming pool and 1.5-hectare coconut grove book four-star **Casa Marina Resort** just 400 meters south of the village alley (1,800,000–2,800,000 VND / $72–$112 USD). Five minutes north on QL1D sits the **ExploraScience Planetarium** (*Trung tâm Khám phá Khoa học Quy Nhơn*, 120,000–150,000 VND entry for 12-meter dome astronomy shows).

## 3. Bai Dai Private Bay Resorts (QL1D Between Quy Nhon & Song Cau)

At Km 15 on **QL1D** between Quy Nhon and Song Cau (14 km / 20 minutes south of downtown, 180,000–220,000 VND GrabCar), two cliffside resorts share an undeveloped 600 meters golden-sand cove. Seclusion is total here.

On the southern beachfront floor, **Anantara Quy Nhon Villas** charges 11,000,000–22,000,000 VND ($440–$880 USD) per night for 26 granite-and-timber pool villas with private butler service and in-villa wine cellars—and serves as the official destination terminus of **The Vietage** luxury 12-seat rail carriage from Da Nang ($420 USD one-way into Dieu Tri Station). Perched on the headland directly above the same sandy cove, **AVANI Quy Nhon Resort** prices 63 contemporary ocean-balcony rooms at 3,200,000–5,800,000 VND ($128–$232 USD) per night, sharing cliffside **Tre Restaurant** and the hillside jungle spa pavilions.

## 4. Nhon Ly Peninsula & Eo Gio / Ky Co (22 km Northeast via Thi Nai Bridge)

Cross the 2.47 km **Thi Nai Bridge** over Thi Nai Lagoon and drive 22 km northeast (30 minutes, 250,000–320,000 VND GrabCar) to reach **Nhon Ly Peninsula**. Sleeping here puts you right beside three coastal icons before 09:30 tour buses arrive:
* **Eo Gio** (*Strait of Wind*): Dramatic red-railed stone cliff walkways curving above crashing surf (25,000 VND / $1.00 USD entry, open 06:00–18:30).
* **Ngoc Hoa Monastery Twin Buddha**: A 30m double-sided Quan Am statue overlooking the peninsula (0 VND free entry).
* **Ky Co Beach**: Neon-turquoise bay reached via a **650,000–850,000 VND ($26–$34 USD) Nhon Ly speedboat & Hon Seo coral snorkeling tour** (including a 7-dish seafood lunch) or the **170,000 VND** ($6.80 USD) over-the-mountain land shuttle road.

Compare two distinct stays in Nhon Ly. Sprawling across a 36-hole Nicklaus-designed golf course, **FLC Luxury Resort & FLC Grand Hotel Quy Nhon** offer massive lagoon pools and private beach access for 1,600,000–3,800,000 VND ($64–$152 USD) per night. Outside the resort gates, colourful blue-and-white **Nhon Ly Fishing Village homestays** (**Chài Village**, **Life's a Beach Ky Co**, **Eden Homestay**) rent sea-facing rooms steps from the speedboat jetty for 500,000–1,200,000 VND ($20–$48 USD).

## 5. Nhon Hai (Hon Kho Island) & Trung Luong – Cat Tien Coast (28 km North)

On the southern tip of Phuong Mai Peninsula (20 km east of downtown), quiet **Nhon Hai Fishing Village** faces **Hon Kho Island**, just 500m offshore. Seawall homestays rent clean air-conditioned rooms for 550,000–1,100,000 VND ($22–$44 USD), while fishermen run 5-minute wooden boat transfers and guided coral snorkeling trips over an ancient submerged Champa sea wall at Hon Kho for 100,000–350,000 VND ($4–$14 USD). Visit from **May to July** at low tide when golden *Sargassum* seaweed reefs (*mùa rong mơ*) carpet the shallow seabed.

Twenty-eight kilometers north along scenic coastal highway **DT639** through **Trung Luong and Cat Tien** (20 minutes east of Phu Cat Airport UIH beneath the 72m sitting Buddha at **Ong Nui Temple / Linh Phong Pagoda**, 0 VND), luxury travelers book **Maia Resort Quy Nhon** (4,200,000–6,800,000 VND, including daily Vela Spa treatments or gourmet half-board dining at Vị Restaurant) or cliffside pine bungalows at **Crown Retreat Quy Nhon** (3,500,000–5,200,000 VND; overall resort range 3,500,000–6,800,000 VND / $140–$272 USD).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it better to stay in downtown Quy Nhon (Xuan Dieu) or Bai Xep Fishing Village?
Your choice hinges on evening plans. Stay on **Xuan Dieu or An Duong Vuong** in downtown Quy Nhon (750,000–2,400,000 VND / $30–$96 USD) for walkable access to Nguyen Hue food streets, Xuan Dieu seafood halls, and the 50,000 VND UIH Airport bus stop at 01 Nguyen Tat Thanh. Pick **Bai Xep** (500,000–2,800,000 VND, 12 km south) for car-free fishing village lanes and beachfront bars when a 15-minute, 150,000 VND taxi into town fits your schedule.

### Should I stay at FLC / Nhon Ly or commute from downtown to visit Ky Co and Eo Gio?
Split stays work best. Commuting from downtown Quy Nhon across the 2.47 km Thi Nai Bridge to Nhon Ly takes 30 minutes (22 km, 250,000–320,000 VND GrabCar). On a 3-night to 4-night trip, spend 2 nights on **Xuan Dieu Beach** for evening street food and 1–2 nights in **Nhon Ly** (or **Maia Resort in Cat Tien**) to walk **Eo Gio** (25,000 VND) at 06:00 and catch the first 07:30 **Ky Co speedboat** (650,000–850,000 VND) before crowds arrive.

### What is the best time of year to book a beach resort in Quy Nhon?
March through August brings peak beach weather. Expect 28°C–34°C sunshine and calm turquoise seas, with **May to July** offering the golden *Sargassum* seaweed snorkeling window at **Hon Kho Island**. Skip boat-dependent peninsula stays from **October to December**, when northeast monsoon swells halt speedboats and downtown Xuan Dieu hotels offer far better storm-season protection.
