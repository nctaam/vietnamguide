# Where to Stay in Da Nang: Best Areas & Hotels (2026)

Da Nang splits neatly across the tidal Han River into two distinct coastal and urban worlds, all sitting within 6 km to 15 km of Da Nang International Airport (DAD). On the eastern shore, a 30 km ribbon of white sand runs from the forested granite cliffs of Son Tra Peninsula south past My Khe Beach and Non Nuoc Beach toward Hoi An. On the western shore, Hai Chau District anchors the civic downtown with breezy riverfront promenades, traditional wet markets, specialty coffee roasters, and direct access to Da Nang Railway Station.

Match your neighborhood to your daily routine. Travelers who want morning ocean swims and rooftop infinity pools at mid-range prices should book along My Khe Beachfront or 300m inland inside the walkable An Thuong Expat Quarter. Visitors staying 1 night to 2 nights for weekend Dragon Bridge shows, local street food, and train connections over the Hai Van Pass belong on the Han Riverfront. Families wanting self-contained five-star grounds should head 8 km south to Non Nuoc Beach, while ultra-luxury honeymooners retreat to the rainforest coves of Son Tra Peninsula.

## 1. My Khe Beachfront (Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard, Phuoc My Ward)

Stretching 4 km from Pham Van Dong Park south to Nguyen Van Thoai Street, My Khe Beachfront is Da Nang's signature postcard shoreline. A 25 meters wide palm-lined promenade separates the fine white sand from six-lane Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard. Municipal lifeguards patrol roped swimming zones daily from 05:00 to 19:00, when thousands of residents gather at dawn for ocean laps, beach volleyball, and coconut water breaks (25,000 VND / $1.00 USD). Dawn brings the calmest water. Sunbed and umbrella rentals on the sand cost a regulated 40,000 VND ($1.60 USD), while soft-top surfboard rentals run 150,000 VND ($6.00 USD) for 2 hours.

My Khe delivers Southeast Asia's strongest value for modern four-star high-rise hotels with panoramic ocean balconies and 20th-floor rooftop infinity pools. Properties lining Vo Nguyen Giap and parallel Loseby or Ha Bong streets price standard rooms at 900,000 VND to 2,600,000 VND ($36 USD to $105 USD) per night—roughly half the cost of equivalent beachfront towers in Phuket or Bali. Upscale five-star towers such as Sala Danang Beach Hotel, TMS Hotel Da Nang Beach, and Four Points by Sheraton charge 2,400,000 VND to 4,200,000 VND ($96 USD to $168 USD). Reaching your lobby from Da Nang International Airport (DAD) takes 15 minutes across the Dragon Bridge or Tran Thi Ly Bridge (6 km, 90,000 VND to 130,000 VND / $3.60 USD to $5.20 USD by GrabCar).

Crosswalk warning: Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard carries fast coastal traffic. Use the signalized pedestrian crossings at Nguyen Van Thoai or Pham Van Dong rather than sprinting across six lanes at dusk.

## 2. An Thuong Expat & Digital Nomad Quarter (Bac My An)

Immediately behind the southern half of My Khe Beach lies the An Thuong grid—a checkerboard of numbered lanes running from An Thuong 1 to An Thuong 38 between Nguyen Van Thoai and Ngo Thi Si streets. Paved stone sidewalks, buried power cables, and low-speed traffic make this 1.2 km square pocket the most walkable neighborhood in Central Vietnam. You stand just 300m (a 4 minutes walk) from the surf at My Khe Beach, yet pay thirty to forty percent less for accommodation than on the main boulevard.

An Thuong caters to solo travelers, digital nomads, and stays lasting 4 days to 30 days. Bright boutique hotels and modern studio apartments with kitchenettes, washing machines, and 150 Mbps fiber internet rent for 550,000 VND to 1,400,000 VND ($22 USD to $56 USD) per night. On An Thuong 1 through An Thuong 6, 24/7 air-conditioned coffee shops, co-working lounges (180,000 VND / $7.20 USD day pass), craft beer taprooms, and wood-fired pizzerias stay busy until midnight. Walk 250 meters west across Chau Thi Vinh Te or Ngo Thi Si streets and prices drop to local student levels: steaming bowls of turmeric noodles (*mi quang*), duck porridge, and crispy *banh xeo* pancakes cost 25,000 VND to 45,000 VND ($1.00 USD to $1.80 USD), while Bac My An Market's famous whipped avocado ice cream (*kem bo*) costs 20,000 VND ($0.80 USD).

Book between An Thuong 15 and An Thuong 34 if you want dead-quiet nights away from sports bars.

## 3. Han Riverfront (Bach Dang & Tran Hung Dao, Hai Chau / Son Tra)

If beaches rank second to street food, coffee culture, and regional rail or flight logistics, stay downtown along the Han Riverfront. The western bank follows **Bach Dang Street** in Hai Chau District, while the quieter eastern bank follows **Tran Hung Dao Street** in Son Tra District. Every Friday, Saturday, and Sunday night at exactly 21:00, police close the 666 meters **Dragon Bridge** (*Cau Rong*) to traffic for 20 minutes while the steel dragon head blasts fireballs and high-pressure water plumes over the river.

Staying along Bach Dang puts you within a 5 minutes to 12 minutes walk of **Han Market** (open 06:00 to 19:00 for dried mangoes, cashews, and tailored linen), the pink spires of Da Nang Cathedral, and the **Da Nang Museum of Cham Sculpture** at 2 2 Thang 9 Street (60,000 VND / $2.40 USD entry, housing 300 sandstone artifacts from the 7th to 15th centuries). Better still, DAD Airport sits just 10 minutes (3.5 km, 60,000 VND to 85,000 VND GrabCar) west, and Da Nang Railway Station stands 8 minutes (2.5 km) away for morning heritage trains over the Hai Van Pass to Hue (2.5 hours, 110,000 VND to 180,000 VND). River-view rooms at mid-range boutiques and international towers like Novotel Danang Premier Han River, Hilton Da Nang, and Wink Hotel Danang Riverside range from 850,000 VND to 2,800,000 VND ($34 USD to $112 USD) per night.

## 4. Non Nuoc Beach (Truong Sa Road, Ngu Hanh Son District)

Starting 5 km south of An Thuong, Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard transitions into **Truong Sa Road**, flanking the wide, uncrowded curve of Non Nuoc Beach. Here, high-rise city blocks give way to gated five-star beachfront resorts set on 10-hectare to 20-hectare tropical lawns with direct private access to the sand. Unlike My Khe, no public road separates the resort swimming pools from the ocean surf.

Vietnam's premier family and spa resorts line this 6 km corridor: **Hyatt Regency Danang Resort and Spa** (featuring five pools and Camp Hyatt), **Pullman Danang Beach Resort**, **Melia Danang Beach Resort**, **Danang Marriott Resort & Spa**, and **Sheraton Grand Danang Resort** (home to a 250 meters infinity pool). Nightly rates in 2026 run 3,800,000 VND to 8,500,000 VND ($150 USD to $340 USD) for garden and ocean-view rooms, or 11,000,000 VND to 22,000,000 VND ($440 USD to $880 USD) for multi-bedroom pool villas. Non Nuoc sits directly beside the limestone caves and pagodas of **Marble Mountains** (*Ngu Hanh Son*, 40,000 VND entry plus 15,000 VND elevator each way) and cuts your transfer time to **Hoi An Ancient Town** down to 20 minutes (18 km south, 220,000 VND to 280,000 VND by GrabCar). Nothing is walkable outside the resort gates. You will take a 10 minutes GrabCar north to An Thuong for independent dining.

## 5. Son Tra Peninsula (Bai Bac / Bai But Reserve)

Rising 693 meters above the northern hook of Da Nang Bay, Son Tra Peninsula (*Monkey Mountain*) protects 4,400 hectares of primary coastal rainforest. Winding coastal roads climb past the 67m white **Lady Buddha** statue at Linh Ung Pagoda (free entry, open 06:00 to 21:00, 12 km from My Khe Beach) into steep jungle canopies inhabited by critically endangered **Red-Shanked Douc Langurs**.

Tucked into the private northern amphitheater of Bai Bac Bay sits **InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort**, an architectural landmark designed by Bill Bensley cascading across four levels—Heaven, Sky, Earth, and Sea—linked by a private funicular railway. Standard 70-square-meter resort rooms and ocean penthouses command 14,000,000 VND to 25,000,000 VND ($560 USD to $1,000 USD) per night, with Michelin-starred dining at La Maison 1888 and a private 700 meters beach cove. Choose Son Tra only when the resort itself is your primary destination; riding into downtown Da Nang takes 25 minutes to 30 minutes (14 km, 220,000 VND each way by taxi).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it better to stay near My Khe Beach or the Han River in Da Nang?
Stay at **My Khe Beach** or **An Thuong** if you visit between February and August, want daily ocean swimming within 300m of your hotel, and enjoy walkable international cafes and rooftop pools at 550,000 VND to 2,600,000 VND ($22 USD to $105 USD). Choose the **Han Riverfront** (Bach Dang Street) for short 1-night to 2-night stays, rainy-season trips (October to December), weekend Dragon Bridge shows at 21:00, and 10 minutes transfers to DAD Airport.

### Should I stay in Da Nang or Hoi An as my Central Vietnam base?
Split your stay or pick by priority: **Da Nang** offers superior airport convenience (10 minutes to 15 minutes from DAD), modern high-rise ocean-view hotels at half the price of Hoi An resorts, and wider swimmable beaches. **Hoi An** (30 km / 40 minutes south, 350,000 VND GrabCar) delivers lantern-lit 17th-century heritage streets and boutique rice-paddy villas. On a 5-day trip, spend 3 nights in Da Nang and 2 nights in Hoi An.

### What is the best month to book a beachfront hotel in Da Nang?
Target **March through August** for calm, turquoise water at My Khe and Non Nuoc beaches, 30°C to 34°C sunshine, and minimal rainfall. Avoid booking ocean-facing balconies for swimming during peak northeast monsoon months (**October and November**), when heavy rains and rough surf close swimming flags and downtown Han River hotels offer better storm-season value.
