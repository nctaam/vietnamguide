# Where to Stay in An Giang: Chau Doc to Long Xuyen Guide

An Giang province occupies a strategically vital quadrant of the western Mekong Delta, bordering Cambodia along the Bassac River (*Sông Hậu*) corridor. Unlike the flat, monocultural fruit orchards characterizing Tien Giang or Ben Tre, An Giang combines vast alluvial floodplains, dense cajuput swamp forests, dramatic granite karst hills (*Thất Sơn* or the Seven Mountains), and an intricate demographic weave of Kinh, Khmer, Cham, and ethnic Chinese communities.

Choosing where to base yourself across An Giang requires selecting among four distinct operational zones separated by 30 to 65 kilometers of delta highway. Travelers crossing borders into Cambodia or seeking riverboat cruises base themselves along the historic riverfront of **Châu Đốc**. Spiritual travelers and cultural photographers head 6 kilometers southwest to the granite slopes of **Núi Sam** (Sam Mountain), home to the monumental Ba Chua Xu pilgrimage shrine. Business travelers and market researchers prioritize **Long Xuyên**, the provincial capital located 60 kilometers southeast along National Route 91 (QL91), positioning themselves close to the floating market. Meanwhile, adventurous motorcyclists and eco-tourists venture into the southwestern border districts of **Tri Tôn** and **Tịnh Biên**, where Khmer pagoda complexes rise above emerald palmyra sugar palm fields (*cây thốt nốt*) and stilt houses border the flooded cajuput wetlands of Tra Su.

## Zone 1: Châu Đốc Riverfront (Border Transit & Heritage)

Châu Đốc functions as the primary tourism gateway in northern An Giang. The town faces the broad waters of the Hau River, flanked by floating catfish fish-farm cages and traditional Cham Muslim stilt settlements across the water in Da Phuoc.

```

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

       |                                                                   |

       |-- Hang Chau Tourist Express Pier (Daily speedboats to Phnom Penh, 07:30)
       |
[Tran Hung Dao Commercial Strip]
       |-- Dong Bao Hotel, Trung Nguyen Guesthouse (300k - 550k VND)

       |-- Local Minibus Stands to Tinh Bien & Tri Ton
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
```

### Neighborhood Character & Infrastructure
Staying along Le Loi or Bach Dang places you within walking distance of the Chau Doc Central Market (*Chợ Châu Đốc*), famous for dozens of varieties of fermented fish (*mắm thái*, *mắm cá lóc*) and fresh palmyra fruit snacks. Pavements are wide and pedestrian-friendly along the riverfront, offering unobstructed views of passing rice barges and passenger sampans.

### Recommended Accommodations
*   **Victoria Chau Doc Hotel**: Located directly on the riverfront at 1 Le Loi. This four-star heritage property features classic colonial wooden floorboards, a swimming pool overlooking the river confluence, and a private jetty for international boat transfers to Cambodia. Superior and Deluxe river-view rooms cost between 2,200,000 and 4,500,000 VND per night.
*   **Dong Bao Hotel**: Positioned at 18 Phan Van Vang, 100 meters behind the waterfront. Reliable mid-range property featuring spacious air-conditioned rooms, elevator access, solar hot water systems, and English-speaking staff who arrange local boat charters. Double rooms cost 450,000 to 650,000 VND.
*   **Hai Chau Hotel**: Located along Tran Hung Dao. Budget concrete guesthouse offering spotless tiled rooms with strong air conditioning, mini-fridges, and indoor motorcycle parking. Nightly rates span 300,000 to 420,000 VND.

## Zone 2: Núi Sam (Pilgrimage Center & Hillside Retreats)

Rising abruptly 284 meters out of the pancake-flat delta rice fields, Núi Sam (Sam Mountain) anchors the spiritual geography of southwestern Vietnam. The mountain foot houses the revered Lady of the Realm Temple (*Miếu Bà Chúa Xứ Núi Sam*), alongside Tay An Pagoda (*Chùa Tây An*) and the tomb of military governor Thoai Ngoc Hau.

### Contrasting Lodging Tiers: Hillside Luxury vs. Pilgrim Inns
Accommodations split sharply by elevation:
*   **Hillside Retreats**: **Victoria Nui Sam Lodge** sits midway up the mountain ridge along Vinh Nguon. Constructed from indigenous granite slabs, its private bungalows overlook endless checkerboards of rice paddies extending westward into Cambodia. Standard bungalows cost 1,600,000 to 3,000,000 VND per night, offering quiet mountain air far above the street commotion below.
*   **Lowland Pilgrim Guesthouses**: The roads circling the base of the shrine (Chau Thi Te and Tan Lo Kieu Luong) host over 80 budget pilgrim inns. Rooms cater to large family groups with multiple double beds, tile floors, wall fans, and basic shower stalls. Standard rates span 180,000 to 350,000 VND on normal dates.

> [!WARNING]
> **Severe Price Surges During the Ba Chua Xu Festival:** The annual pilgrimage festival (*Lễ hội Vía Bà Chúa Xứ Núi Sam*) peaks between the 22nd and 27th days of the 4th lunar month (falling between May and June). During this week, more than two million pilgrims converge on Núi Sam. Lowland guesthouse rates jump 300 to 500 percent (reaching 800,000 to 1,500,000 VND for basic rooms), local traffic freezes entirely, and loudspeaker chanting echoes around the clock. Non-pilgrims must avoid staying at Núi Sam during this festival window.

## Zone 3: Long Xuyên (Provincial Commercial Capital)

Located 60 kilometers southeast of Chau Doc along National Route 91, Long Xuyên is the administrative and commercial capital of An Giang province. With a population exceeding 300,000, it feels distinctly more metropolitan than Chau Doc.

```
LONG XUYEN URBAN AXIS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[North: QL91 from Chau Doc / Can Tho]
       |
       v
[Long Xuyen Commercial Center]
       |-- Tran Hung Dao Highway Artery (Futa Bus Terminal, Commercial Banks)
       |-- Hoa Binh Hotel 1 & 2 (3-star business lodging, 600k - 1,200k VND)
       |-- An Long Hotel, Lara Hotel (Budget business stays, 350k - 500k VND)
       |
       v  5 km south along Hau River
[O Moi Ferry Pier & Floating Market Staging]
       * Sampan hire for Long Xuyen Floating Market (05:30 - 07:30 morning peak)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
```

### Why Stay in Long Xuyên?
Long Xuyên suits travelers who prefer authentic regional trade over international tourism infrastructure. The city hosts the **Long Xuyên Floating Market** (*Chợ nổi Long Xuyên*), located 5 kilometers south along the river. Unlike the commercialized Cai Rang floating market in Can Tho, Long Xuyên's market remains dedicated entirely to wholesale produce distribution among local delta farmers, with dozens of wooden boats laden with sweet potatoes, pineapples, pumpkins, and sugar cane.

### Recommended Hotels in Long Xuyên
*   **Hoa Binh 1 Hotel**: Located at 130 Tran Hung Dao. Premier full-service hotel offering modern executive rooms, an outdoor swimming pool, secure gated vehicle parking, and an on-site restaurant serving regional delta specialties. Nightly rates span 650,000 to 1,200,000 VND.
*   **Lara Hotel**: Situated at 46 Ton Duc Thang. Clean, contemporary boutique property offering minimalist rooms, rapid Wi-Fi, and work desks suitable for remote workers. Double rooms cost 400,000 to 550,000 VND per night.

## Zone 4: Tri Tôn & Tịnh Biên (The Khmer Palmyra Heartland)

Spreading across southwestern An Giang along the border with Takeo province in Cambodia, Tri Tôn and Tịnh Biên showcase an ancient rural landscape dominated by towering palmyra sugar palm trees, terraced emerald rice fields, and ornate Khmer Theravada Buddhist monasteries.

### Rural Homestay and Ecotourism Stays
Lodging here centers on immersion in rural ecology and Khmer cultural traditions:
*   **Tra Su Forest Edge Homestays**: Small timber stilt houses near the entrance to the Tra Su Cajuput Forest reserve provide quiet, nature-focused accommodations. Private bamboo cabins cost 250,000 to 450,000 VND per night, with owners preparing meals of grilled snakehead fish wrapped in lotus leaves and sour soup with sesbania flowers (*canh chua hoa điên điển*).
*   **Tri Tôn Town Guesthouses**: Establishments such as **Khách sạn Bảy Núi** and **Nhà nghỉ Phát Triển** along Nguyen Trai offer air-conditioned private rooms for 220,000 to 350,000 VND per night, providing a dependable launching pad for exploring Ta Pa Pagoda and Soai Chek Lake.

## Regional Transit, Speedboats & Terminal Logistics

An Giang connects efficiently to Ho Chi Minh City, Can Tho, and Phnom Penh via modern highway express buses and river speedboats.

```

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Origin / Destination       Mode & Carrier           Tariff (VND)        Transit Duration
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HCMC <-> Chau Doc          Futa Bus Lines (Sleeper) 175,000 - 210,000   5.5 - 6.0 hours
HCMC <-> Long Xuyen        Futa Bus Lines (Sleeper) 150,000 - 180,000   4.5 hours
Chau Doc <-> Phnom Penh    Hang Chau Speedboat      850,000 - 950,000   4.5 - 5.0 hours
Chau Doc <-> Can Tho       Local Minibus / Futa     90,000 - 120,000    2.5 - 3.0 hours
Chau Doc <-> Tri Ton       Local Bus No. 03         35,000 VND          1.2 hours
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
```

*   **Futa Bus Lines (Phương Trang)**: Operates modern sleeper buses every hour between Ho Chi Minh City's Western Bus Station (*Bến xe Miền Tây*) and Chau Doc Terminal on Ton Duc Thang. Free shuttle vans deliver passengers directly to hotels in central Chau Doc.
*   **Hang Chau Tourist Express**: High-speed passenger catamarans depart Chau Doc pier at 07:30 daily, clearing border formalities at Vinh Xuong international checkpoint before arriving at Sisowath Quay in Phnom Penh by 12:30. One-way tickets cost approximately 880,000 VND ($35 USD).

Maintaining awareness prevents unnecessary expenses and ensures smooth travel throughout the border province.

*   **Núi Sam Fortune-Telling and Incense Aggression**: Aggressive hawkers outside Miếu Bà Chúa Xứ frequently thrust incense bundles, lucky charms, or caged release birds into visitors' hands, subsequently demanding 200,000 to 500,000 VND. Politely decline all items with a firm wave of the hand. If you wish to purchase incense, buy standard packets inside official temple administrative counters for 20,000 VND.
*   **Floating Market Boat Overcharging in Long Xuyên**: Avoid independent touts lurking outside O Moi ferry pier who quote 400,000 to 600,000 VND for private boat rides. Proceed directly to the registered municipal boat dispatch window to hire a licensed sampan for 150,000 to 200,000 VND total for a two-hour morning market excursion.
*   **Motorcycle Travel along Border Roads**: When riding along DT955A parallel to the Cambodian border canal, keep your passport and motorcycle registration papers (*cà vẹt xe*) readily accessible. Border guard posts occasionally conduct routine identification checks on foreign riders.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many days should I stay in An Giang to cover the highlights?
A three-day, two-night itinerary proves optimal. Spend night one along the Chau Doc riverfront to explore the local markets and Cham villages. On day two, visit Núi Sam and Tra Su Cajuput Forest before traveling south to sleep in Long Xuyên or Tri Tôn. This schedule allows you to experience the Long Xuyên floating market at 06:00 on morning three before returning toward Can Tho or Ho Chi Minh City.

### Which area is best if I am taking the morning speedboat to Phnom Penh?
Stay along the Chau Doc riverfront on Le Loi or Bach Dang. The Victoria Chau Doc and Dong Bao Hotel lie within a five-minute walk or three-minute taxi ride of the Hang Chau Tourist Express pier. Boarding begins at 07:00, making an overnight stay in Núi Sam or Long Xuyên inconvenient for morning departures.

### Can I visit Tra Su Cajuput Forest while basing myself in central Chau Doc?
Yes. Tra Su Cajuput Forest lies approximately 28 kilometers south of Chau Doc via QL91 and DT948. The drive takes 45 to 50 minutes by rental motorcycle or private taxi (costing around 350,000 VND each way). Basing yourself in Chau Doc gives you convenient access to a wide selection of dining options after a day trip to the forest.

### What is the best season to stay in An Giang?
The high-water floating season (*mùa nước nổi*) from September to November offers the most striking landscape. During these months, alluvial floodwaters submerge the cajuput forest floor, creating lush green carpets of duckweed across Tra Su, while local fishing markets overflow with fresh river fish and water lilies.
