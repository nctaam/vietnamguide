# Can Tho to Ha Tien Transport: Bus Schedules, Limousines, Border Transit, and Ferry Logistics

Connecting the economic center of the Mekong Delta to Vietnam's far southwestern coastal frontier, the 190-kilometer transit corridor between Can Tho and Ha Tien leads travelers to maritime piers and international border crossings. Ha Tien sits on the Gulf of Thailand in Kien Giang Province, directly adjacent to the Kingdom of Cambodia. Most travelers take this overland route for two distinct logistical reasons. Some passengers journey toward Bến tàu Hà Tiên to catch short-haul high-speed catamarans to Phu Quoc Island. Others proceed 8 kilometers past town to cross the Ha Tien / Prek Chak international border gate into Kampot Province.

Transit across this southwestern territory requires careful geographic navigation. Unlike the expressway connection to Rach Gia, transit toward Ha Tien relies on regional trunk corridors like National Route 80 (QL80) and National Route N2. Road surface variations, localized town choke points, and ferry timing rules make forward planning essential. This operational guide provides verified departure timetables, exact fares in Vietnamese Dong, terminal transfer workflows, and cross-border protocols.

## Choosing Your Transport: Detailed Vehicle Comparison

Vehicle choice affects arrival punctuality, seating comfort over a four-hour run, and onward connections to ferries or international border posts.

### 1. Intercity Express Buses (FUTA Bus Lines)

FUTA Bus Lines (Phương Trang) maintains the most consistent commercial service along the Can Tho to Ha Tien corridor. Fleet operations use 34-berth sleeper coaches and modern 29-seat reclining seating buses. Departures leave Bến xe Trung tâm Cần Thơ every 60 to 90 minutes between 04:30 AM and 18:30 PM. Standard tickets cost between 170,000 VND and 200,000 VND per passenger.

Tickets include transport aboard company shuttle vans (*xe trung chuyển*). Upon arriving at Bến xe Hà Tiên, located in Hẻm 51 off National Route 80 in Tô Châu Ward, passengers can transfer directly into a company minibus bound for Bến tàu Hà Tiên or downtown hotels across the Tô Châu bridge. Inform the station dispatcher immediately after disembarking.

### 2. Executive 9-Seat Limousines

Shared limousine operators such as Vũ Linh Limousine and Trang Ngọc Phát deploy customized vans featuring wide leather captain chairs, USB device charging ports, and active air purification systems. Fares range from 210,000 VND to 250,000 VND per seat. 

These operators run direct routes, bypassing intermediate passenger depots along National Route 80. Transit durations average 3 hours and 30 minutes. Limousines collect travelers directly from hotels in Can Tho's central Ninh Kieu District and deliver them directly to the ferry pier or the border gate approach in Ha Tien, eliminating local terminal transfers.

### 3. Private Chattered Cars

Hiring a private sedan or 7-seat SUV provides maximum schedule security for travel parties carrying heavy dive luggage or connecting to afternoon flights from Phu Quoc. Fixed-rate quotes from Can Tho downtown to Ha Tien range from 1,700,000 VND for a 4-seat sedan to 2,200,000 VND for an expansive 7-seat vehicle like a Toyota Fortuner or Mitsubishi Xpander. Drivers complete the 190-kilometer trip in 3 hours to 3.5 hours, depending on regional traffic conditions.

### 4. Motorbike Riding

Navigating a motorbike along the 190-kilometer route takes between 4.5 and 5.5 hours. Motorcyclists exit Can Tho along National Route 91B, merge onto National Route 80 at Thot Not, and ride northwest through Chau Thanh, Hon Dat, and Kien Luong. The stretch between Tri Ton and Kien Luong passes limestone karst formations rising dramatically above green rice paddies. However, road conditions feature loose gravel, unpaved canal bridge ramps, and heavy dust from cement transport trucks. Riders need certified helmets, clear eye protection, and protective dust masks. Fuel costs total approximately 80,000 VND to 100,000 VND each way.

### Segment 1: Can Tho to Lo Te and Hon Dat

Vehicles leave Can Tho traveling northwest via the Lo Te - Rach Soi expressway corridor or National Route 91B. Commercial coaches and private cars follow the divided dual-carriageway of CT02 up to the Lo Te interchange before cutting across toward National Route 80. Pavement quality along this first 80-kilometer section is smooth asphalt concrete with an 80 km/h speed threshold.

### Segment 2: Hon Dat through Kien Luong to Ha Tien

The final 110 kilometers run along National Route 80 through the towns of Hon Dat and Kien Luong. This road segment handles mixed traffic, including agricultural tractors, container flatbeds serving coastal cement factories, and local scooters. Road resurfacing over recent seasons has smoothed many older potholes, yet drivers must remain alert around sharp canal curves and narrow concrete bridge approaches. Traffic moves at 50 to 60 km/h through these commercial settlements.

## Step-by-Step Terminal Departure and Ferry Synchronization

Coordinating your bus departure from Can Tho with ferry schedules in Ha Tien requires strict attention to travel buffers.

```
04:00 AM - Check out from hotel in Can Tho (Ninh Kieu District)
04:20 AM - Taxi transfer (6 km, 75,000 VND) to Bến xe Trung tâm Cần Thơ
04:45 AM - Board FUTA express bus to Ha Tien (185,000 VND ticket)
08:45 AM - Arrive at Bến xe Hà Tiên (Hẻm 51, Phường Tô Châu)
08:55 AM - Transfer to FUTA shuttle van or GrabBike to sea port (3 km)
09:10 AM - Check in at Bến tàu Hà Tiên ticket office (Tran Hau Street)
09:15 AM - Manifest check completed; board high-speed vessel
09:45 AM - Superdong or Phu Quoc Express ferry departs for Bai Vong Port
```

### Departing from Can Tho

Passengers depart from **Bến xe Trung tâm Cần Thơ**, located on National Route 1A in Cai Rang District. A taxi ride from downtown Ninh Kieu costs 70,000 VND to 85,000 VND and takes 12 to 15 minutes. 

Inside the central booking hall, purchase your ticket at the **Phương Trang (FUTA)** counter. Payment is accepted in Vietnamese Dong cash or through banking app QR codes (VietQR). If you booked via the FUTA mobile application, proceed straight to the departure platforms behind the building to board the coach.

### Arriving at Bến xe Hà Tiên and Pier Transfer

Buses arrive at **Bến xe Hà Tiên**, located on Hẻm 51, Quarter 1, Tô Châu Ward. This terminal sits on the southern bank of the Dong Ho estuary, roughly 3 kilometers south of the passenger ferry pier. 

Do not hire expensive private taxis outside the gate. Present your ticket stub at the FUTA transfer desk inside the depot. A company minibus will take you across the Tô Châu bridge directly to **Bến tàu Hà Tiên**, situated along Tran Hau and Kim Du streets in Binh San Ward. If your ticket does not include shuttle service, a local motorbike taxi (*xe ôm*) will make the run for 25,000 VND to 30,000 VND.

### Superdong Fast Ferries

Superdong operates fast passenger vessels between Ha Tien and Bai Vong Port. The voyage takes 1 hour and 15 minutes. Adult tickets cost 330,000 VND, while children pay 230,000 VND. Daily departure slots leave Ha Tien at 07:15, 08:00, 09:45, and 13:15. The vessel interior features cushioned airline-style seating, air-conditioning, overhead luggage racks, and clean washroom facilities.

### Fast Catamarans of Phu Quoc Express

Operating sleek twin-hull vessels, Phu Quoc Express delivers passengers to Bai Vong Port in 1 hour and 10 minutes. Standard passenger tickets cost 350,000 VND, while VIP upper-deck tickets cost 390,000 VND. Morning sailings depart at 07:45, 09:00, and 11:30. These dual-hull vessels deliver superior stability, offering a calm ride even during moderate coastal swell.

## Cross-Border Transit to Cambodia via Prek Chak

Geographically, Ha Tien serves as the premier land border crossing between southern Vietnam and coastal Cambodia.


### Reaching the Border Gate

The **Ha Tien International Border Gate** (also known locally as Cửa khẩu Xà Xía) is situated 8 kilometers northwest of downtown Ha Tien along National Route 80. A metered taxi to the border costs 100,000 VND to 120,000 VND. Alternatively, a motorbike taxi (*xe ôm*) charges 50,000 VND to 70,000 VND. The border station operates daily from 06:00 AM to 18:00 PM.

### Immigration Procedures and Visa Requirements

To exit Vietnam, travelers present their valid passport and active electronic visa (Vietnam e-Visa) at the departure counters. Processing takes approximately 10 to 15 minutes. 

After exiting Vietnam, walk 150 meters across no-man's-land to the **Prek Chak** checkpoint. Cambodia offers 30-day tourist visas on arrival at this border post for 30 to 35 USD cash. Make sure you carry crisp, undamaged US Dollar banknotes, as immigration officers reject torn or folded currency. Once stamped into Cambodia, private taxis and shared minivans wait outside the gate to transfer travelers to Kep (25 km, 15 to 20 USD) or Kampot (45 km, 25 to 30 USD).

## Practical Safety Tips and Scam Warnings

1. **Beware of Visa Touting at the Border**: Touts loitering near the Xà Xía border post frequently target foreign tourists, claiming the official Cambodian visa window is closed or charging inflated rates of 45 to 50 USD for fraudulent "fast-track processing." Decline these offers firmly. Walk directly into the official Cambodian government immigration hall to process your arrival stamp.
2. **Strict Manifest Deadlines**: Just like in Rach Gia, harbor authorities in Ha Tien close passenger manifests 45 minutes prior to vessel departure. Have your passport and printed ticket ready before entering the pier concourse.
3. **Rough Weather Alternatives**: When severe southwestern monsoons halt ferry departures from Rach Gia, services from Ha Tien frequently continue operating. Ha Tien's sea lane stays sheltered in the lee of Phu Quoc Island, making this route the most reliable bad-weather fallback.
4. **Currency Exchange Cautions**: Avoid exchanging large sums of Vietnamese Dong into Cambodian Riel or US Dollars at informal street counters near the ferry terminal. Exchange rates are unfavorable. Instead, withdraw US Dollars from ATMs in Kampot or use banking facilities in central Ha Tien before crossing the border.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which is better from Can Tho to Phu Quoc: via Rach Gia or via Ha Tien?
Selecting Rach Gia involves 2.5 to 3 hours of highway driving followed by a 2.5-hour ferry voyage. Conversely, routing via Ha Tien requires 4 to 4.5 hours on the bus, but cuts the sea crossing to just 1 hour and 15 minutes. If you are prone to motion sickness, transit via Ha Tien to minimize rough sea time. If you prefer shorter road journeys, travel via Rach Gia.

### Can I catch the 09:45 AM ferry to Phu Quoc by taking the first bus from Can Tho?
Catching this boat is feasible. The 04:30 AM FUTA express coach from Bến xe Trung tâm Cần Thơ reaches Bến xe Hà Tiên around 08:45 AM. Taking the free company shuttle van puts you at the passenger ferry pier by 09:05 AM sharp. This timing grants 40 minutes to collect boarding passes and clear manifest gates before the 09:45 AM horn sounds.

### Can I bring my motorbike onto the high-speed ferry from Ha Tien to Phu Quoc?
Motorbikes are permitted on designated vessels. Both Superdong and Thanh Thoi operate car and passenger roll-on/roll-off (RoRo) ferries from Bến phà Thạnh Thới in Ha Tien. Vehicle transport tickets cost between 80,000 VND and 120,000 VND per motorbike, depending on engine displacement. Fast catamarans like Phu Quoc Express carry only foot passengers and do not accept two-wheelers.

### Are there luggage storage services available near Bến tàu Hà Tiên?
Luggage lockers and storage points exist. Cafes and official ferry booking offices along Tran Hau and Kim Du streets offer temporary luggage storage for 20,000 VND to 30,000 VND per bag per day. This service allows travelers to explore the Ha Tien night market or visit Mac Cuu Tombs unencumbered before boarding an afternoon boat or crossing into Cambodia.
