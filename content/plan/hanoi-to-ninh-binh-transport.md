# Hanoi to Ninh Binh Transport: Train, Van or Car?

Ninh Binh sits 95 km south of central Hanoi along the Red River Delta corridor. That short distance makes it an easy transfer, yet picking the wrong vehicle still costs you 90 minutes of backtracking or unexpected taxi fares. Most visitors skip the urban grid of Ninh Binh City; they book homestays 7 km to 22 km west of town in Tam Coc, Trang An, Mua Cave, or Bai Dinh.

Four main transit modes cover the corridor: 9-to-11-seat VIP limousine vans on the CT01 expressway, Reunification Express trains on the North–South railway, private door-to-door cars, and public coaches from southern Hanoi bus terminals. Independent riders also tackle National Highway QL1A by motorbike. Below is the complete operational breakdown of routes, real-world travel times, baggage limits, and 2026 pricing in VND and USD.

## VIP Limousine Vans via the CT01 Expressway

Nine-seat and eleven-seat converted Ford Transit DCar and Hyundai Solati vans dominate the Hanoi–Ninh Binh route. They solve the last-mile transfer problem. Trains and municipal buses drop passengers inside Ninh Binh City, whereas limousine operators exit the CT01 Phap Van–Cau Gie–Cao Bo Expressway and drive straight into Tam Coc, Trang An, and Hoa Lu.

Four major operators run hourly departures from 05:00 to 20:00 daily:
* **Trang An Limousine**: Runs 9-seat DCar VIP vans with leather captain chairs, USB ports, and water. Tickets cost 210,000 VND ($8.40) for front/rear seats and 230,000 VND ($9.20) for the four middle swivel seats.
* **Binh Minh Limousine**: Offers hourly pickups around the Hanoi Opera House (01 Trang Tien Street) and Hoan Kiem Lake. Fares range from 200,000 VND to 240,000 VND ($8.00 to $9.60) with direct Tam Coc and Mua Cave drop-offs.
* **X.E Vietnam**: Maintains lounges at 71 Tran Nhat Duat Street, 10 Ngoc Ha Street, and Giai Phong Street. Tickets cost 170,000 VND to 220,000 VND ($6.80 to $8.80).
* **Duy Khang Limousine & Non Nuoc Ninh Binh**: High-frequency fleets running every 60 minutes from 05:30 to 20:00 at 170,000 VND to 210,000 VND ($6.80 to $8.40).

Highway driving on the 95 km CT01 tollway takes 80 to 95 minutes at 100 km/h to 120 km/h. Add 30 minutes for Hanoi Old Quarter feeder shuttles and 15 to 25 minutes for local drop-offs in Ninh Binh. Because narrow Hoan Kiem streets restrict 16-seat chassis vans, operators use 7-seat feeder cars to bring passengers to a southern ring-road hub near Phap Van.

> [!WARNING]
> **Luggage Constraints on 9–11 Seat Limousine Vans**: Converted DCar vans sacrifice rear trunk depth to give the back row of 3 seats full recline capability. Each passenger is strictly limited to one 20-inch or 24-inch suitcase plus a small backpack. If two travelers bring four 28-inch hard-shell trunks, the driver will either charge an extra seat fee of 170,000 VND ($6.80) or refuse boarding. Book the train or a private 7-seat SUV if you travel with oversized baggage.

## Reunification Express Trains (Hanoi Station to Ninh Binh Station)

Taking the state railway (`dsvn.vn`) trades door-to-door convenience for legroom, predictable schedules, and zero highway weaving. Trains depart from Hanoi Railway Station at 120 Le Duan Street (1.8 km west of Hoan Kiem Lake, a 10-minute, 45,000 VND / $1.80 Grab ride) and travel 115 km south to Ninh Binh Railway Station at 01 Hoang Hoa Tham Street, Thanh Binh Ward.

Six daily Reunification Express trains serve this sector:
* **SE7 (Morning Day-Tripper)**: Departs Hanoi at 06:00, arrives at 08:13 (2 hours 13 minutes). Ideal for reaching Tam Coc or Trang An piers before tour buses arrive at 10:00.
* **SE5 (Mid-Morning Express)**: Departs Hanoi at 08:50, arrives at 11:01 (2 hours 11 minutes). Fits an 08:00 hotel checkout and noon lodge arrival.
* **SE9 (Afternoon Regional)**: Departs Hanoi at 12:50, arrives at 15:11 (2 hours 21 minutes).
* **SE3, SE19, and SE1 (Evening Southbound)**: Depart Hanoi at 19:20 (SE3, arrives 21:35), 19:40 (SE19, arrives 21:54), and 20:55 (SE1, arrives 23:06).

Skip sleeper berths and book an air-conditioned soft seat (`Ngoi mem dieu hoa`, code `NML`). Soft-seat tickets cost 116,000 VND to 165,000 VND ($4.65 to $6.60) on weekdays and up to 210,000 VND ($8.40) on weekends. Carriages feature overhead racks that hold 28-inch suitcases, 220V wall sockets, and clean restrooms.

From Ninh Binh Station (01 Hoang Hoa Tham Street), take a metered Mai Linh Taxi or GrabCar to your lodge:
* **Station to Tam Coc Boat Pier (7 km / 14 minutes)**: 95,000 VND to 130,000 VND ($3.80 to $5.20).
* **Station to Trang An Wharf (9 km / 17 minutes)**: 120,000 VND to 160,000 VND ($4.80 to $6.40).
* **Station to Mua Cave / Hang Mua (8.5 km / 15 minutes)**: 110,000 VND to 145,000 VND ($4.40 to $5.80).
* **Station to Bai Dinh Pagoda (22 km / 32 minutes)**: 260,000 VND to 320,000 VND ($10.40 to $12.80).

## Private Cars, Direct Airport Transfers, and Local Buses

For groups of three or four, a private car costs barely more per person than limousine tickets. A 4-seat sedan (Toyota Vios or Hyundai Accent) from any Hanoi Old Quarter hotel straight to Tam Coc or Trang An costs 1,150,000 VND to 1,300,000 VND ($46.00 to $52.00) one-way, including the 125,000 VND ($5.00) CT01 expressway toll. A 7-seat MPV or SUV (Mitsubishi Xpander or Toyota Innova) costs 1,350,000 VND to 1,600,000 VND ($54.00 to $64.00) and holds four adults with four large suitcases. Without intermediary pickup loops, driving time drops to 1 hour 35 minutes.

From Noi Bai International Airport (HAN), book a direct transfer down Ring Road 3 (CT20) onto the CT01 expressway. The 125 km airport-to-Tam Coc drive takes 1 hour 50 minutes to 2 hours 10 minutes. Private sedans from HAN cost 1,450,000 VND to 1,750,000 VND ($58.00 to $70.00), while shared airport limousine vans run by Trang An and Duy Khang charge 300,000 VND to 400,000 VND ($12.00 to $16.00) per seat.

Public coaches from Giap Bat Bus Station (Km 6 Giai Phong Street, 6.5 km south of Hoan Kiem) and Nuoc Ngam Bus Station (01 Ngoc Hoi Street, 8.5 km south) cost 80,000 VND to 120,000 VND ($3.20 to $4.80). Once you add an 85,000 VND ($3.40) Grab ride to Giap Bat plus a 100,000 VND ($4.00) taxi from Ninh Binh Central Bus Station (207 Le Dai Hanh Street) to Tam Coc, you pay more and spend 45 minutes longer than taking a limousine van.

Motorbikes are banned from the CT01 expressway. Riders on 125cc to 150cc bikes must follow National Highway QL1A for 95 km through Phu Ly (heavy truck traffic, 2 hours 45 minutes) or take the quieter western rural route along DT429 past Huong Son (110 km, 3 hours 15 minutes).

## Step-by-Step Protocol: Choosing and Booking Your Transfer

1. **Pin your exact lodge coordinates**: Check whether your lodge is in Tam Coc, Trang An, Mua Cave, Hoa Lu, or Van Long Nature Reserve. Van Long sits 20 km north of Ninh Binh City; limousine vans or private cars exit at Gian Khau and reach Van Long in 90 minutes.
2. **Select middle seats on a limousine van**: Seats 3, 4, 5, and 6 are individual captain chairs with full recline. Seats 1 and 2 next to the driver are tight, and seats 7, 8, and 9 sit over the rear axle. Pay the extra 20,000 VND ($0.80) for a middle seat 24 hours ahead.
3. **Confirm your Old Quarter pickup window**: Limousine operators send a feeder shuttle 30 to 45 minutes before highway departure. Wait in your hotel lobby 40 minutes prior with WhatsApp or Zalo active.
4. **Book morning trains 3 to 5 days ahead on weekends**: Train SE7 (06:00) and SE5 (08:50) sell out soft-seat carriages on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday mornings. Buy official QR e-tickets via `dsvn.vn` to avoid third-party markups of 60,000 VND to 100,000 VND ($2.40 to $4.00).
5. **Pre-book Sunday return tickets before 15:00**: Sunday afternoons between 15:00 and 19:00 see heavy northbound traffic back to Hanoi. Reserve your return limousine van or train SE8 (16:28) / SE6 (17:51) at least one day prior.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I book a limousine van or the train from Hanoi to Ninh Binh?
Book the VIP limousine van (170,000 VND to 250,000 VND / $6.80 to $10.00) if you stay in Tam Coc, Trang An, or Mua Cave with standard bags and want direct hotel-to-homestay delivery. Take the Reunification Express train (116,000 VND to 210,000 VND / $4.65 to $8.40) if you experience road motion sickness, carry 28-inch suitcases, or want guaranteed arrival via morning train SE7 (06:00) or SE5 (08:50).

### Can I use Grab to book an on-demand car from Hanoi all the way to Ninh Binh?
While the Grab app quotes 1,100,000 VND to 1,400,000 VND ($44.00 to $56.00), many city drivers cancel a 95 km one-way ride or demand an off-app empty-return surcharge of 300,000 VND to 400,000 VND ($12.00 to $16.00) plus 250,000 VND ($10.00) for round-trip CT01 tolls. Pre-booking a fixed-rate private transfer at 1,150,000 VND ($46.00) avoids roadside fare disputes.

### Where do limousine vans drop passengers in Tam Coc and Trang An?
Operators (Trang An Limousine, Binh Minh, X.E Vietnam) drive directly to hotel gates along the main Tam Coc road, Thai Vi Temple road, Hang Mua road, and Trang An boulevard. If your bungalow sits down a narrow brick footpath over the Ngo Dong River, the driver drops you at the nearest main intersection or the Tam Coc Boat Pier lot 100 m to 400 m away.

### How late do transport options run from Ninh Binh back to Hanoi?
The last daytime northbound trains leave Ninh Binh Station in the late afternoon: SE8 departs at 16:28 (arriving Hanoi 19:12) and SE6 departs at 17:51 (arriving Hanoi 20:20). If you stay for sunset at Mua Cave (17:45 to 18:15), book a limousine van instead; vans run hourly evening pickups from Tam Coc and Trang An at 18:00, 19:00, and 20:00.
