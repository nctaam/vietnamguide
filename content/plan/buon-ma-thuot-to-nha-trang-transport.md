# Buon Ma Thuot to Nha Trang Transport (2026): Bus & Car

Connecting the coffee capital of Buon Ma Thuot in Dak Lak Province (**536m** elevation) with the South Central Coast beach resort of Nha Trang in Khanh Hoa Province covers **185 km** east along National Highway **QL26** and **QL1A**. The route crosses fertile agricultural basins in Krong Pak, Ea Kar, and M'Drak before dropping **800m** down the winding **12 km Phoenix Pass (Đèo Phượng Hoàng)** to the coastal plain at Ninh Hoa, where vehicles turn south onto **QL1A** for the final **33 km** coastal run into Nha Trang. In addition, newly completed early-stage sections of the **Khanh Hoa – Buon Ma Thuot Expressway (CT24)**—including approach corridors toward the **1.7 km** Phuong Hoang twin mountain tunnel—have begun relieving heavy freight truck congestion on the upper pass.

Because no direct flights or railway lines link Dak Lak and Khanh Hoa, 100% of travelers go by road. For 2026, a **9-seat or 11-seat VIP limousine van** is the best-value choice, taking **3.5 to 4 hours** and costing **200,000 to 260,000 VND** (**$8.00 to $10.40 USD**) with door-to-door hotel pickup in Buon Ma Thuot and direct drop-off along Nha Trang's **Tran Phu** beach strip. Standard 34-berth sleeper buses cost **150,000 to 190,000 VND** (**$6.00 to $7.60 USD**) station-to-station, while a private 4-seater or 7-seater car charter costs **1,800,000 to 2,300,000 VND** (**$72.00 to $92.00 USD**).

To coordinate door-to-door van transfers at both ends of your journey, review our neighborhood guides on [Where to Stay in Buon Ma Thuot](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-buon-ma-thuot/) and [Where to Stay in Nha Trang](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-nha-trang/).

> [!TIP]
> **Motion Sickness Seat Selection on Phoenix Pass:** The **12 km** descent down Phoenix Pass features **35+ tight hairpin curves** and a **7% to 10% gradient**. Always pay the extra **30,000 VND** (**$1.20 USD**) to reserve **Seats 3, 4, 5, or 6** (the middle two rows of single captain's chairs) directly over the van's wheelbase, and avoid the rear three-seat bench (**Seats 7, 8, and 9**) behind the rear axle.

## Option 1: 9-Seat & 11-Seat VIP Limousine Vans (Recommended)

Converted **Ford Transit (9-seat)** and **Hyundai Solati Skybus (11-seat)** VIP limousine vans are the fastest public transit option between Buon Ma Thuot and Nha Trang. Their compact wheelbase handles the switchbacks of **Phoenix Pass (Đèo Phượng Hoàng)** faster than full-length 12-meter coaches, shaving **30 to 45 minutes** off the mountain descent.

### Top Limousine Operators, Timetables, and Ticket Prices

Vans depart hourly from **05:30 to 18:00** in both directions. Most companies operate tiered pricing based on seat placement: front two seats beside the driver cost **200,000 VND** (**$8.00 USD**), middle four electric-massage captain's chairs cost **240,000 to 260,000 VND** (**$9.60 to $10.40 USD**), and the rear three-seat bench costs **210,000 to 220,000 VND** (**$8.40 to $8.80 USD**).

* **Khanh Phong VIP Limousine (Nhà Xe Khánh Phong):** Runs flagship 11-seat Hyundai Solati vans departing at **06:00, 08:30, 11:30, 14:00, 16:30, and 18:00**. Tickets cost **220,000 to 260,000 VND** (**$8.80 to $10.40 USD**). Includes hotel pickup in central Buon Ma Thuot and drop-off at hotels across Nha Trang's Loc Tho, Tan Lap, Phuoc Tien, and Vinh Nguyen wards.
* **Rung Thong Limousine (Nhà Xe Rừng Thông):** Operates hourly departures from **05:30 to 17:30**. Fares range from **200,000 to 240,000 VND** (**$8.00 to $9.60 USD**). Known for disciplined driving speeds on Phoenix Pass and reliable 15-seat shuttle vans that collect passengers from Buon Ma Thuot hotels **30 minutes** before departure.
* **Quang Hanh & Lien Hung Limousine (Quang Hạnh / Liên Hưng):** Both coastal-highland operators run 11-seat VIP limousines (**210,000 to 250,000 VND** / **$8.40 to $10.00 USD**) and 34-room VIP cabin buses along **QL26**, departing every **60 to 90 minutes** between **06:00 and 18:00**.
* **Mai Linh Willer (Mai Linh Express):** Joint Vietnamese-Japanese coach service operating safety-monitored vans and buses on the Buon Ma Thuot–Nha Trang route for **200,000 to 240,000 VND** (**$8.00 to $9.60 USD**), with strict driver rest protocols and GPS speed governors capped at **50 km/h** on mountain grades.

## Option 2: 34-Berth Sleeper and Cabin Buses

If you are traveling on a tight budget or carrying surfboards, oversized suitcases (**28 inches+**), or boxes of Dak Lak roasted coffee beans that will not fit inside a limousine van trunk, full-size **34-berth VIP sleeper buses** and **40-berth standard sleepers** run throughout the day.

* **Buon Ma Thuot Departure Terminals:** Buses depart either from **Buon Ma Thuot Northern Bus Station (Bến Xe Phía Bắc)** at **71 Nguyen Chi Thanh Street** (**4.5 km** from city center) or **Buon Ma Thuot Southern Bus Station (Bến Xe Phía Nam / Bến Xe Quyết Thắng)** on Vo Van Kiet Boulevard, Khanh Xuan Ward (**6 km** southwest of downtown). Many operators also allow boarding at private offices along **Nguyen Chi Thanh Street**.
* **Nha Trang Arrival Terminal:** Coaches terminate at **Nha Trang Southern Bus Station (Bến Xe Phía Nam Nha Trang)** on **23 Thang 10 Street (Vo Nguyen Giap Junction), Vinh Trung Commune**, located **7.5 km** west of Tran Phu Beach. Most major bus lines (Phuong Trang / FUTA, Lien Hung, Quang Hanh) provide a **free 16-seat shuttle van (*xe trung chuyển*)** into downtown Nha Trang; otherwise, a Grab car or taxi to the beach strip takes **18 to 22 minutes** and costs **100,000 to 130,000 VND** (**$4.00 to $5.20 USD**).
* **Schedules & Fares:** Tickets cost **150,000 to 190,000 VND** (**$6.00 to $7.60 USD**) for a standard 34-berth sleeper and **220,000 to 250,000 VND** (**$8.80 to $10.00 USD**) for a 22-room single VIP cabin coach. Major operators include **Phuong Trang (FUTA Bus Lines)**, **Lien Hung**, **Quang Hanh**, and **Hoa Nghia**, departing at **06:30, 08:00, 09:30, 12:30, 14:00, 16:00, 18:00, and 22:00**. Total transit time is **4 to 4.5 hours**, including a **20-minute** rest stop at M'Drak or Ninh Xuan.

## Option 3: Private Car Charter (Highland-to-Coast Sightseeing)

Booking a private car with an experienced mountain driver is the most comfortable option for families with children, elderly travelers, or groups of 3 to 6 passengers who want to avoid bus motion sickness on Phoenix Pass and stop at landmarks along the way.

* **4-Seater Sedan (Toyota Vios, Honda City, Mazda 3):** **1,800,000 to 2,000,000 VND** (**$72.00 to $80.00 USD**) one way.
* **7-Seater SUV/MPV (Toyota Fortuner, Innova, Kia Carnival):** **2,100,000 to 2,300,000 VND** (**$84.00 to $92.00 USD**) one way.
* **16-Seater Van (Ford Transit, Hyundai Solati):** **3,000,000 to 3,500,000 VND** (**$120.00 to $140.00 USD**) one way.

Fares include all fuel, driver wages, and the two BOT toll stations (**Ea Kar BOT** on QL26 in Dak Lak and **Ninh Xuan BOT** in Khanh Hoa, totaling **75,000 to 100,000 VND**). Direct point-to-point driving takes **3.5 hours**. You can customize your itinerary with sightseeing stops (**+300,000 to 500,000 VND** / **$12.00 to $20.00 USD** waiting fee):

1. **World Coffee Museum (Bảo Tàng Thế Giới Cà Phê):** Visit Buon Ma Thuot's curved longhouse museum (**150,000 VND** / **$6.00 USD** entry) before departing at **09:30**.
2. **Dray Nur Waterfall:** Detour **25 km** south of Buon Ma Thuot (**40,000 VND** / **$1.60 USD** ticket) in the morning, then head east to Nha Trang after lunch.
3. **Doc Let Beach (Bãi Biển Dốc Lết):** Once you reach Ninh Hoa at the junction of **QL26** and **QL1A**, detour **14 km** east to Doc Let's shallow turquoise water and white sand dunes for a seafood lunch (**250,000 to 400,000 VND** per person) before driving the final **45 km** south into Nha Trang.

## Option 4: Self-Drive Motorbike Across Highway 26 and Phoenix Pass

Riding the **185 km** from Buon Ma Thuot to Nha Trang by motorbike takes **4.5 to 5.5 hours** of active saddle time and transitions from cool basalt highlands through mist-shrouded mountain jungle down to coastal sunshine.

* **Leg 1 – Buon Ma Thuot to Krong Pak & Ea Kar (52 km, 75 minutes):** Head east on **QL26** through Phuoc An (Krong Pak District), Vietnam's premier **Musang King and Ri6 durian** growing hub. Between **August and October**, heavy fruit trucks crowd the two-lane highway; respect the **50 km/h** urban speed cameras in Phuoc An and Ea Kar towns.
* **Leg 2 – Ea Kar to M'Drak Town (38 km, 55 minutes):** The road climbs past eucalyptus forests, sugarcane fields, and boulder-strewn hills into M'Drak (**450m to 800m** elevation). Fill your fuel tank in M'Drak town before starting the mountain descent.
* **Leg 3 – Phoenix Pass / Đèo Phượng Hoàng (12 km, 25 to 30 minutes):** Situated between **Km 31 and Km 43** of **QL26**, Phoenix Pass drops sharply into Khanh Hoa Province. Use **2nd or 3rd gear** on semi-automatic or manual motorbikes (*engine braking*) rather than riding your brake pads continuously, which causes brake fade on automatic scooters.
* **Leg 4 – Ninh Hoa to Nha Trang via QL1A & Pham Van Dong Coastal Road (83 km, 110 minutes):** From the bottom of the pass, ride **50 km** east through Ninh Xuan and Ninh Hoa town to the **QL1A** coastal intersection. Turn south on **QL1A** past Nha Phu Lagoon, then take the coastal **Pham Van Dong Boulevard** around **Deo Luong Son** cliffside directly over the Tran Phu Bridge into Nha Trang beach.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is Phoenix Pass safe during the October to December rainy season?
While Buon Ma Thuot enters its dry season in November, **M'Drak and Phoenix Pass** catch heavy Northeast monsoon rain and thick pea-soup fog blown inland from the East Sea between **October and late December**. Visibility on the pass can drop below **15 meters** after **15:30**. Always book a morning departure (**07:00 to 10:00**) during late autumn so your vehicle crosses Phoenix Pass in full daylight.

### Can I take a limousine van directly from Buon Ma Thuot to Cam Ranh Airport (CXR)?
Limousine vans terminate in central Nha Trang, roughly **35 km** north of Cam Ranh International Airport (**CXR**). Ask your driver to drop you at the southern end of Tran Phu Street or Nguyen Tat Thanh Boulevard in Nha Trang, where you can catch a Dat Moi airport bus (**65,000 VND** / **$2.60 USD**, **45 minutes**) or a Grab car (**300,000 to 350,000 VND** / **$12.00 to $14.00 USD**) directly to **CXR**.
