# Can Tho to Phu Quoc Transport (2026): Direct Flights vs. Rach Gia Bus and Fast Ferry

Traveling from Can Tho, the commercial heart of the Mekong Delta, to Phu Quoc Island spans 200 km by direct regional aircraft or 230–235 km across highway and sea corridors via Kien Giang Province. Because most Mekong Delta itineraries wrap up with an early morning boat tour of Cai Rang Floating Market (05:30–07:30), choosing the right departure window determines whether you reach Phu Quoc beach resorts by early afternoon or get stranded overnight on the mainland coast.

Travelers have three practical configurations in 2026: boarding the daily Vietnam Airlines / VASCO ATR-72 turboprop flight from Can Tho International Airport (VCA) to Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC) in 50–55 minutes (1,150,000–1,950,000 VND / $46–$78 USD), riding a 2.5-hour expressway bus (110 km) to Rach Gia Passenger Port to catch a 2.5-hour Superdong or Phu Quoc Express catamaran (460,000–520,000 VND / $18.40–$20.80 USD combined), or taking a 4-hour bus (190 km) to Ha Tien for the shorter 75-minute ferry crossing (386,000–450,000 VND / $15.45–$18.00 USD combined).

> [!TIP]
> **Post-Floating Market Connection Rule:** If you tour Cai Rang Floating Market from 05:30 to 07:30, book the 09:00 Vu Linh Limousine (170,000 VND, hotel pickup in Ninh Kieu) or 09:00 Phuong Trang bus (130,000 VND) to Rach Gia. You will arrive at Rach Gia Port by 11:30, leaving a comfortable 75-minute buffer before boarding the 12:45 Phu Quoc Express or 13:10 Superdong catamaran to Phu Quoc.

## Option 1: Direct Flight from Can Tho (VCA) to Phu Quoc (PQC)

Vietnam Airlines (operated in partnership with its regional subsidiary VASCO) runs the sole non-stop air link between Can Tho International Airport (VCA) and Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC). Unlike the high-frequency Airbus jet trunk from Ho Chi Minh City, the VCA–PQC sector utilizes a 68-seat **ATR-72-500 twin-turboprop** aircraft.

### Schedule, Fares, and Cai Rang Timing

The flight operates 1 daily rotation (expanding to 2 daily rotations during June–August summer peaks and Tet), typically departing VCA between 11:45 and 13:10 and touching down at PQC 50 to 55 minutes later.

* **Ticket Cost:** Economy Classic fares range from 1,150,000 to 1,650,000 VND ($46–$66 USD) one-way when booked 14+ days out, climbing to 1,750,000–1,950,000 VND ($70–$78 USD) within 72 hours of departure.
* **Floating Market Alignment:** This midday slot pairs cleanly with a 05:30–08:00 boat trip to Cai Rang Floating Market. You can return to your hotel near Ninh Kieu Quay by 08:30, check out at 09:30, and reach VCA terminal by 10:00.

### Strict ATR-72 Baggage Restrictions

Because the ATR-72-500 has compact overhead bins and strict center-of-gravity weight limits, ground staff at Can Tho Airport weigh carry-on bags rigorously:

1. **Cabin Baggage:** Maximum 7 kg (15.4 lbs) with dimensions not exceeding 56 x 36 x 23 cm (unlike standard Vietnam Airlines Airbus flights that permit 10 kg in the cabin).
2. **Checked Baggage:** One piece up to 23 kg (50 lbs) is included in Economy Classic and Flex tickets. Oversized surfboards or hard-shell dive trunks over 158 cm linear dimensions may be offloaded if the hold reaches capacity.

### Airport Transfers in Can Tho and Phu Quoc

Can Tho International Airport (VCA) sits at 179B Le Hong Phong Street, Tra An Ward, Binh Thuy District, 10 km northwest of Ninh Kieu Quay. A metered Mai Linh, Mekong, or Xanh SM electric taxi takes 20 to 25 minutes and costs 140,000–180,000 VND ($5.60–$7.20 USD).

At Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC), located 10 km south of Duong Dong town, board the **free VinBus electric shuttle (#17 or #19)** outside the arrivals curb (0 VND, every 20–30 minutes toward Duong Dong and Grand World) or take a metered taxi into Duong Dong for 150,000–220,000 VND ($6.00–$8.80 USD).

## Option 2: Express Bus to Rach Gia Port + Fast Ferry (230 km Total)

Because the single daily VCA–PQC flight only holds 68 passengers, the highway-and-catamaran corridor via **Rach Gia Port** handles over 80% of traveler traffic between Can Tho and Phu Quoc. The Lo Te–Rach Soi Expressway (CT02) cuts overland driving times between Can Tho and Rach Gia down to 2.5 hours.

For full bus schedules and station gates along the overland leg, read our [Can Tho to Rach Gia Transport](/plan/can-tho-to-rach-gia-transport/) guide.

### Step 1: Early Morning Bus or Limousine from Can Tho to Rach Gia (110 km)

To catch the late-morning or midday speedboats to Phu Quoc, depart Can Tho between 06:00 and 09:00:

* **Phuong Trang (Futa Bus Lines):** Coaches depart Can Tho Central Bus Station (QL1A, Hung Thanh Ward, Cai Rang District, 6 km south of Ninh Kieu Quay) every 45 to 60 minutes from 05:30 to 18:30. Fare is 130,000 VND ($5.20 USD). After the 2.5-hour drive to Rach Soi Bus Station, Futa transfers ferry-bound passengers into a **free 16-seat shuttle van** directly to Rach Gia Passenger Port at Nguyen Cong Tru Street.
* **Vu Linh Limousine & Thien Thanh Vans:** Luxury 9-seat Ford Transit Dcar limousines depart hourly from 05:30 to 17:30, offering hotel pickup in central Ninh Kieu and direct drop-off at Rach Gia Port for 160,000–180,000 VND ($6.40–$7.20 USD).

### Step 2: Superdong or Phu Quoc Express Ferry to Bai Vong Port (120 km)

From Rach Gia Passenger Port (1 Nguyen Cong Tru Street), high-speed catamarans cross 120 km (65 nautical miles) to Bai Vong Port on Phu Quoc's east coast in 2.5 hours.

Target these morning and midday ferry sailings:

1. **10:15 / 10:30 Phu Quoc Express & 10:40 Superdong:** Board the 06:30 or 07:00 bus/limousine from Can Tho (arriving at Rach Gia Port by 09:30). Reaches Phu Quoc at 12:45–13:10.
2. **12:45 Phu Quoc Express & 13:10 Superdong:** Ideal after visiting Cai Rang Floating Market (05:30–07:30). Catch the 08:30 or 09:00 bus/limousine from Can Tho, reach Rach Gia Port by 11:30, and land at Bai Vong Port by 15:15–15:40.
3. **13:45 Superdong / Phu Quoc Express (Final Daily Sailing):** Never board a bus leaving Can Tho later than 10:00, or you risk missing the 13:45 gangway closure.

**2026 Ferry Ticket Fares (Rach Gia – Phu Quoc):**
* **Superdong Speed Ferry:** 330,000 VND ($13.20 USD) Adult Economy; 240,000 VND ($9.60 USD) Child (6–11 years).
* **Phu Quoc Express Catamaran:** 340,000 VND ($13.60 USD) Adult Economy; 270,000 VND ($10.80 USD) Child/Senior; 540,000 VND ($21.60 USD) VIP upper deck.

## Option 3: Bus to Ha Tien + 75-Minute Ferry (235 km Total)

If you suffer from sea motion sickness or are riding a motorbike onto Phu Quoc Island, route through **Ha Tien Port** (190 km west of Can Tho) instead of Rach Gia.

* **Overland Leg (Can Tho to Ha Tien):** Futa Bus Lines and Kumho Samco depart Can Tho Central Bus Station at 06:00, 08:00, and 10:00, taking 3.5 to 4 hours (170,000–200,000 VND / $6.80–$8.00 USD) to Ha Tien Bus Station (2 km from Ha Tien Pier, 40,000 VND taxi).
* **Sea Leg (Ha Tien to Bai Vong):** Because Ha Tien is only 45 km from northern Phu Quoc, Superdong and Phu Quoc Express speedboats complete the crossing in 75 minutes for 216,000–250,000 VND ($8.65–$10.00 USD). Motorbike travelers can board the **Thanh Thoi Car Ferry** (185,000 VND passenger + 80,000 VND motorbike, 2.5 hours). ## Arrival at Bai Vong Port & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

All Rach Gia and Ha Tien ferries dock at **Bai Vong Port** in Ham Ninh Commune, 15 km east of Duong Dong town. Superdong and Phu Quoc Express shuttle buses inside the port gate charge 50,000–70,000 VND ($2.00–$2.80 USD) per seat to Duong Dong (25 minutes), while metered Xanh SM or Mai Linh taxis cost 200,000–250,000 VND ($8.00–$10.00 USD) to Duong Dong or 320,000–380,000 VND to An Thoi (24 km).

### What happens if my morning bus from Can Tho is delayed and I miss the ferry?
If you hold a confirmed ticket on the 10:15 or 10:40 sailing and arrive late due to highway traffic, proceed immediately to the port ticket counter at 1 Nguyen Cong Tru. Both Superdong and Phu Quoc Express allow same-day standby rebooking onto the 12:45, 13:10, or 13:45 departure for a 30,000–50,000 VND ($1.20–$2.00 USD) administrative fee, subject to seat availability.

### Should I buy a combined bus-and-ferry package in Can Tho or book tickets separately?
While travel desks along Hai Ba Trung Street (Ninh Kieu Quay) sell bundled limousine-plus-Superdong vouchers for 520,000–560,000 VND ($20.80–$22.40 USD), booking separately through the official Phuong Trang Futa Bus app (130,000 VND, which already includes the free Rach Soi port shuttle van) and the Phu Quoc Express or Superdong portal (330,000–340,000 VND) saves 60,000–90,000 VND per passenger and lets you select lower-deck midship seats that minimize wave pitch.

### Can I bring a rented motorbike from Can Tho onto the Superdong fast ferry at Rach Gia?
Superdong catamarans on the Rach Gia–Phu Quoc route prioritize foot passengers and accept at most 4 to 6 manual scooters per sailing (200,000–250,000 VND freight fee, fuel tank drained). For guaranteed vehicle loading at lower cost, ride via Ha Tien and board the Thanh Thoi roll-on/roll-off car ferry (80,000 VND per motorbike).
