# Ha Tien to Phu Quoc Ferry (2026): Speedboat & Vehicle Ro-Ro Guide

Departing from Kien Giang's northwest border town gives travelers the shortest sea crossing between the Vietnamese mainland and Phu Quoc Island. Measured pier-to-pier, the maritime channel spans just `45 km` (`24 to 28 nautical miles`) across the sheltered northeastern pocket of the Gulf of Thailand. While the competing mainland hub at Rach Gia requires a `120 km` open-water voyage lasting `135 to 150 minutes`, high-speed catamarans and mono-hull ferries leaving Ha Tien reach Phu Quoc's eastern coastline in `60 to 75 minutes`.

That `75 km` reduction in open-sea exposure changes the entire physical feel of the crossing. Because Phu Quoc's northern landmass and the Cambodian coast of Kampot block long-period swells from the wider gulf, the Ha Tien corridor remains noticeably calmer during shoulder-season winds. Short crossings save stomachs. The route serves three distinct traveler profiles: passengers prone to motion sickness who want the briefest possible time on the water, overland travelers crossing into Vietnam from Kampot or Kep via the Xa Xia border gate, and motorists transporting their own motorbikes or four-wheeled vehicles aboard roll-on/roll-off (`Ro-Ro`) barges. For travelers comparing overland feeder buses from Saigon against direct flights, consult our [Ho Chi Minh City to Phu Quoc Transport](/plan/ho-chi-minh-city-to-phu-quoc-transport/) breakdown.

> [!TIP]
> **Pier Selection Rule:** Book Ha Tien Port instead of Rach Gia if you are crossing overland from Cambodia, bringing a 110cc to 150cc motorbike on the Thanh Thoi Ro-Ro barge (80,000 VND vehicle fee), or traveling during choppy shoulder weeks when cutting sea time from 150 minutes down to 65 minutes prevents motion sickness.

## High-Speed Passenger Operators: Superdong vs. Phu Quoc Express

Choosing between the two fast boat operators comes down to hull geometry, departure clock alignment, and cabin seating layout. Both companies dock at the exact same concrete piers on Kim Du Street in Ha Tien and at Bai Vong Port on Phu Quoc.

### Phu Quoc Express (`Tàu Cao Tốc Phú Quốc Express`)
Phu Quoc Express operates crimson twin-hull aluminum catamarans engineered to European maritime stability standards, carrying roughly 300 passengers per vessel. The twin-hull catamaran footprint spans a wider beam than older single-hull launches. This width dampens lateral rolling when quartering swells sweep through the channel between Pirate Islands (`Quần đảo Hải Tặc`) and Hon Mot.

- **Crossing Duration:** `60 to 70 minutes` dock-to-dock.
- **2026 Ticket Fares:** Economy class (`Hạng Phổ Thông`) costs `216,000 to 250,000 VND` (`$8.60 to $10.00 USD`) for adults depending on weekday promotional tiers, `148,000 to 170,000 VND` (`$5.90 to $6.80 USD`) for children aged `6 to 11`, and `182,000 to 210,000 VND` (`$7.30 to $8.40 USD`) for seniors aged `60+`. VIP class (`Hạng VIP`) in the forward upper deck costs `350,000 VND` (`$14.00 USD`) and features leather armchairs with `130-degree` recline, leg rests, and complimentary water.
- **Standard Timetable (Ha Tien to Phu Quoc):** Morning departures run at `06:00`, `07:25`, `09:40`, and `12:30` (with an additional `13:15` or `14:00` sailing added during Friday–Sunday peaks and Tet holidays). Return sailings from Bai Vong Port to Ha Tien depart at `07:50`, `09:10`, `11:30`, and `14:30`.

### Superdong Fast Ferry (`Tàu Cao Tốc Superdong`)
Superdong is the longest-running high-speed maritime operator in Kien Giang province, deploying mono-hull vessels (`Superdong VI`, `Superdong VII`, `Superdong VIII`, `Superdong IX`, and `Superdong X`) along the Ha Tien route. While mono-hull boats pitch slightly more in choppy `1.5-meter` head seas than twin-hull catamarans, Superdong maintains a deeper fleet reserve and more consistent daily departure slots during low-demand months.

- **Crossing Duration:** `75 minutes` (`1 hour 15 minutes`).
- **2026 Ticket Fares:** Single-class Economy seating only. Adult tickets cost `230,000 VND` (`$9.20 USD`). Seniors (`60+`) and people with disabilities pay `195,000 VND` (`$7.80 USD`), while children aged `6 to 11` pay `160,000 VND` (`$6.40 USD`). Toddlers under `6 years old` sitting on an adult's lap travel free.
- **Standard Timetable (Ha Tien to Phu Quoc):** Sailings depart Ha Tien at `06:10`, `07:30`, `08:00`, `09:30`, `12:00`, and `13:45`. Return boats from Phu Quoc to Ha Tien leave Bai Vong Port at `08:00`, `09:45`, `11:00`, `12:30`, `14:00`, and `15:30`.
- **Motorbike Cargo Policy:** Superdong can hoist `10 to 15` light manual motorbikes or scooters onto the aft open deck per sailing (`150,000 VND` fee plus `20,000 to 30,000 VND` stevedore tie-down charge), provided your fuel tank is drained to near empty. However, space sells out days in advance; riders with heavy touring bikes (`150cc+`) should book the Ro-Ro car ferries instead.

## Vehicle & Motorbike Ro-Ro Ferries: Thanh Thoi and Binh An

If you are riding a motorbike across southern Vietnam or driving a private family car, **Thanh Thoi Ferry** (`Phà Thạnh Thới` / Thriving Maritime) and **Binh An Ferry** (`Phà Bình An`) are purpose-built steel landing craft equipped with hydraulic bow ramps. No cranes touch your bike. You ride or drive directly onto the lower steel vehicle deck, lock the steering column while deckhands lash the frame with ratcheting straps, and walk upstairs to the air-conditioned passenger lounge or open-air cafeteria deck.

- **Crossing Duration:** 2 hours 30 minutes to 2 hours 45 minutes (`150 to 165 minutes`).
- **Passenger & Motorbike Pricing:** Foot passengers pay 185,000 VND ($7.40 USD). Bringing a standard manual underbone (`Honda Wave`, `Yamaha Sirius`) or automatic scooter (`Honda AirBlade`, `Vision`) adds 80,000 VND ($3.20 USD). Larger displacement motorcycles (`175cc` and above) cost 150,000 to 200,000 VND ($6.00 to $8.00 USD).
- **Automobile Pricing:** Small `4-seat` hatchbacks and sedans cost 700,000 VND ($28.00 USD) each way. Mid-size `7-seat` SUVs and MPVs (`Toyota Fortuner`, `Mitsubishi Xpander`) cost 1,000,000 VND ($40.00 USD), while `9 to 16-seat` passenger vans pay 1,400,000 VND ($56.00 USD).
- **Pier Note:** Most Thanh Thoi and Binh An sailings depart directly from the Ro-Ro slipway at Ha Tien Port (Kim Du Street) and dock at Bai Vong Port, though select heavy cargo runs operate between Thanh Thoi's dedicated terminal in To Chau and Da Chong Port (`Cảng Đá Chồng`, Bai Thom) at the northern tip of Phu Quoc. Verify the exact arrival pier printed on your boarding pass before departure.

## Ha Tien Passenger Port (`Cảng Tàu Hà Tiên`) Logistics & Cambodia Border Transit

The main terminal facility sits at **Kim Du Street, Ward 1, To Chau Ward, Ha Tien City**, along the southern bank of the Giang Thanh River mouth where it meets the East Lagoon (`Đầm Đông Hồ`).

### Connecting from Ha Tien Bus Station (`Bến Xe Hà Tiên`)
Overnight sleeper buses arriving from Ho Chi Minh City's Mien Tay Bus Station (`310 to 350 km`, `6.5 to 8 hours`, `230,000 to 330,000 VND` on **Futa Bus Lines / Phương Trang**, **Kumho Samco**, or **Tuan Nga**) and regional coaches from Can Tho (`195 km`, `4.5 hours`, detailed in our [Can Tho to Phu Quoc Transport](/plan/can-tho-to-phu-quoc-transport/) guide) terminate at Ha Tien Bus Station on National Highway 80 (`Quốc lộ 80`).
- The distance from Ha Tien Bus Station across the To Chau Bridge (`Cầu Tô Châu`) to Ha Tien Ferry Terminal is 2.2 km (5 minutes by car).
- Futa Bus Lines and Kumho Samco operate free `16-seat` transit vans (`xe trung chuyển`) that shuttle ticketed sleeper passengers directly to the Kim Du Street ferry gate between `05:00` and `06:30`. If arriving independently, a Mai Linh taxi costs 40,000 to 60,000 VND ($1.60 to $2.40 USD).

### Crossing Overland from Cambodia (Kampot & Kep via Xa Xia / Prek Chak)
Ha Tien is the primary maritime gateway for travelers combining southern Cambodia with Phu Quoc. The **Ha Tien International Border Gate** (`Cửa khẩu Quốc tế Hà Tiên` / Xa Xia, paired with **Prek Chak** on the Cambodian side) lies just 9 km (15 minutes by taxi) northwest of Ha Tien Passenger Port along Coastal Road `QL80`.
- **Border Operating Hours:** `06:00 to 18:00` daily.
- **Morning Cut-Off Strategy:** Minivans departing Kampot (45 km from the border) at `07:30` or `08:00` typically clear Cambodian exit stamping and Vietnamese e-Visa inspection between `09:30` and `10:30`. From the Vietnamese border barrier, take a metered taxi (120,000 to 150,000 VND / $4.80 to $6.00 USD, 15 minutes) straight to Kim Du Street to catch the `12:00` Superdong, `12:30` Phu Quoc Express, or `13:45` afternoon speedboat.
- **Visa Check:** Note that Vietnam's `30-day` Phu Quoc visa-free exemption applies **only** to foreigners arriving directly at Phu Quoc International Airport (`PQC`) or an international cruise seaport on Phu Quoc itself. Because entering via Ha Tien or Xa Xia means setting foot on the Vietnamese mainland first, you **must** hold a valid Vietnamese e-Visa ($25 USD single-entry) or qualify for a bilateral mainland visa waiver before crossing the border.

## Arrival at Bai Vong Port (`Cảng Bãi Vòng`) & Onward Island Transfers

All passenger speedboats from Ha Tien dock at **Bai Vong Port** (`Ấp Bãi Vòng, Xã Hàm Ninh`), located on Phu Quoc's sheltered east coast. The concrete pier extends 400 meters into the bay; expect a 5-minute walk from the vessel gangway to the main parking plaza (10,000 VND porter trolleys are available for heavy suitcases).

Once outside the port gate, three onward transport options connect you to the west coast resorts:
1. **Express Ferry Shuttle Buses (`Xe buýt trung chuyển`):** Both Superdong and Phu Quoc Express partner with `16-seat` and `29-seat` shuttle buses parked directly inside the terminal loop. Tickets cost 40,000 to 50,000 VND ($1.60 to $2.00 USD) per person and drop passengers along Nguyen Trung Truc and Tran Hung Dao streets in central Duong Dong (15 km, 25 to 30 minutes).
2. **Metered Taxis & Grab (`Xanh SM`, `Mai Linh`, `Vinasun`):** Electric Xanh SM cabs and petrol taxis queue outside the security barrier. A direct ride from Bai Vong Port to Duong Dong Town or Long Beach (`Bãi Trường`) costs 200,000 to 250,000 VND ($8.00 to $10.00 USD, 25 minutes). Southbound rides to An Thoi (26 km) or Sunset Town cost 320,000 to 380,000 VND ($12.80 to $15.20 USD, 35 minutes), while northern transfers to Ong Lang (24 km, 300,000 VND) or Ganh Dau / Vinpearl (38 km, 50 minutes) run 480,000 to 550,000 VND ($19.20 to $22.00 USD).
3. **Pier-Side Motorbike Rentals:** Independent rental shops opposite the Bai Vong entrance gate lease `110cc to 125cc` automatic scooters for 130,000 to 150,000 VND ($5.20 to $6.00 USD) per 24 hours, allowing you to ride straight out onto Provincial Road `DT47` and `DT973`.

## Step-by-Step Booking Rules, ID Verification & Monsoon Protocols

Vietnamese Port Authority (`Cảng vụ Hàng hải Kiên Giang`) regulations enforce strict passenger manifest checks prior to boarding. Bring physical ID. Every passenger's full legal name, year of birth, nationality, and passport number (or Vietnamese CCCD ID number) must be printed on the ticket and matched against original documents at the pier turnstile. Arrive at the Kim Du Street terminal at least 30 minutes before scheduled departure, or 45 minutes early when checking a motorbike onto a Ro-Ro ferry.

During the Southwest Monsoon (`May to October`), tropical depressions over the Gulf of Thailand occasionally generate **Beaufort Force 6 to 7** winds (`39 to 61 km/h`) and waves exceeding `2.0 to 2.5 meters`. Safety rules are absolute. When winds cross Force 6, the Maritime Administration suspends all high-speed departures on both the Ha Tien and Rach Gia routes, issuing a `100%` refund or free rebooking onto the next cleared sailing; compare both mainland ports in our master [Phu Quoc Ferry Guide](/plan/phu-quoc-ferry-guide/) or check backup hotels in [Where to Stay in Rach Gia](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-rach-gia/).
