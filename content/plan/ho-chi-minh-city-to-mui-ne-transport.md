# Ho Chi Minh City to Mui Ne: Express Bus, Train & Car

Covering the **210 km** coastal corridor from Ho Chi Minh City east to Phan Thiet and the kitesurfing beaches of Mui Ne once required **5 to 5.5 hours** of stop-and-go driving along truck-choked National Highway **QL1A**. Today, the **99 km** Dau Giay–Phan Thiet Expressway (**CT01**), linked directly to the **55 km** Ho Chi Minh City–Long Thanh–Dau Giay Expressway, has slashed highway transit times down to just **2.5 to 3 hours**. That infrastructure upgrade turned Binh Thuan's red sand dunes and palm-lined resorts along **Nguyen Dinh Chieu** and **Huynh Thuc Khang** streets into an easy half-day transfer from central Saigon.

Travelers heading east have four practical options: **9-seat and 11-seat VIP DCar limousine vans** (**320,000 to 420,000 VND** / **$12.80 to $16.80 USD**), **34-berth sleeper and cabin buses** (**190,000 to 280,000 VND** / **$7.60 to $11.20 USD**), the morning **SPT2 tourist train** to Phan Thiet Railway Station (**185,000 to 285,000 VND** / **$7.40 to $11.40 USD**), or a **door-to-door private car charter** (**1,500,000 to 1,950,000 VND** / **$60.00 to $78.00 USD**).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Why the CT01 Expressway Beats the Train for Mui Ne Resorts:** While the **SPT2** train is comfortable, it terminates at **Phan Thiet Railway Station**, which sits **22 km** (**30 to 35 minutes** by taxi or local bus) west of the main Ham Tien–Mui Ne resort strip. By contrast, expressway limousine vans and tourist buses exit **CT01** at the Ba Bau or Phan Thiet–Vinh Hao interchanges and drop passengers directly at beachfront resort lobbies along **Nguyen Dinh Chieu** and **Huynh Thuc Khang** streets in **2.5 to 3.5 hours** total.

## Option 1: VIP Limousine Vans & Sleeper Buses via the CT01 Expressway

Road coaches and executive minivans use the four-lane **CT01 Expressway** (**120 km/h** maximum speed limit), bypassing Bien Hoa and Long Khanh urban traffic entirely. Before booking your seat, check your hotel's exact address in [Where to Stay in Mui Ne](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-mui-ne/)—the resort zone stretches **18 km** from Phu Hai (Km 5) through Ham Tien (**Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street**, Km 9–16) to Mui Ne fishing village (**Huynh Thuc Khang Street**, Km 17–22).

### 9-Seat & 11-Seat VIP DCar Limousine Vans (320,000–420,000 VND / $12.80–$16.80 USD)

Converted from 16-seat Ford Transit and Hyundai Solati vans into 9-seat or 11-seat executive lounges, VIP DCar limousines feature wide Boeing-style leather captain's chairs with electric recline, built-in massage functions, USB-A and Type-C fast chargers, high-speed Wi-Fi, and complimentary bottled water. Because they carry fewer than a dozen passengers, loading takes **10 minutes** and vans cruise at the full **100 to 120 km/h** expressway pace.

* **Vie Limo:** Runs flagship 9-seat Boeing massage limousines departing from its District 1 lounge (**131 Nguyen Thai Binh**, near Ben Thanh Market) and Tan Son Nhat Airport every **60 minutes** from **07:00 to 18:00**. Tickets cost **360,000 to 420,000 VND** (**$14.40 to $16.80 USD**) and include coffee or iced tea in the departure lounge plus direct drop-off at any Phu Hai, Ham Tien, or Centara Mirage Mui Ne resort.
* **G5Car Limousine & MexBus:** Operate 9-seat and 11-seat VIP vans departing District 1 (**Pham Ngu Lao**), Hang Xanh, and Tan Son Nhat Airport between **06:30 and 19:00**. Fares range from **320,000 to 380,000 VND** (**$12.80 to $15.20 USD**) depending on seat position (front cab seats cost **320,000 VND**; middle massage captain's chairs cost **360,000 to 380,000 VND**).
* **Nam A Chau & 3S Limo:** Run hourly expressway vans from District 1 with hotel pickup in central Saigon and door-to-door drop-off along **Nguyen Dinh Chieu** and **Huynh Thuc Khang** streets in **2 hours 45 minutes** for **320,000 to 370,000 VND** (**$12.80 to $14.80 USD**).

### 34-Berth Sleeper & 24-Cabin Buses (190,000–280,000 VND / $7.60–$11.20 USD)

If you want to stretch out flat or are traveling on a backpacker budget with large surfboards or **28-inch** suitcases, book a highway sleeper coach. Even though the ride now takes only **3 to 3.5 hours**, operators still use full reclining-berth coaches on this corridor.

* **Hanh Cafe (Hạnh Café):** A longtime favorite for international travelers because buses depart directly from the backpacker hub at **273 Pham Ngu Lao** (District 1) at **07:00, 08:00, 09:30, 11:30, 14:00, 16:30, and 22:30** and drive straight down **Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street** to the Hanh Cafe Mui Ne terminal (**117 Nguyen Dinh Chieu**, Ham Tien), stopping at beachfront hotels along the way. Fares cost **190,000 to 220,000 VND** (**$7.60 to $8.80 USD**) for a 34-berth sleeper or **260,000 to 280,000 VND** (**$10.40 to $11.20 USD**) for a 24-cabin VIP coach.
* **Phuong Trang (Futa Bus Lines):** Operates 34-berth sleeper buses every **30 to 60 minutes** from **231 Le Hong Phong** (District 5), **272 De Tham** (District 1 feeder), and **Ben Xe Mien Dong Moi**. Tickets cost **190,000 to 210,000 VND** (**$7.60 to $8.40 USD**) and include free shuttle van drop-off (*xe trung chuyển*) from the Phan Thiet or Mui Ne bus terminal to your resort lobby.
* **Kumho Samco & Tam Hanh:** Run frequent 34-berth sleepers from Pham Ngu Lao (District 1) and Mien Dong Moi to Ham Tien for **190,000 to 210,000 VND** (**$7.60 to $8.40 USD**).

## Option 2: The SPT2 / SPT1 Tourist Train to Phan Thiet Station

Vietnam Railways operates one dedicated daily pair of daytime tourist trains (**SPT2** northbound/eastbound and **SPT1** return) linking **Saigon Railway Station** (**01 Nguyen Thong, Ward 9, District 3**) with **Phan Thiet Railway Station** (**Le Duan Street, Phong Nam Commune, Phan Thiet**).

### 2026 Train Schedules and Ticket Prices

* **Outbound Train SPT2 (HCMC to Phan Thiet):** Departs Saigon Railway Station daily at **06:40** and arrives at Phan Thiet Railway Station at **10:35** (**3 hours 55 minutes** total). On Friday through Sunday peak weekends, Vietnam Railways occasionally adds the **SPT4** morning train departing at **07:30**.
* **Return Train SPT1 (Phan Thiet to HCMC):** Departs Phan Thiet Railway Station daily at **13:10** and arrives back at Saigon Railway Station at **17:10** (**4 hours** total).
* **Seat & Berth Fares:**
  * **Air-Conditioned 64-Seat Soft Seat (*Ngồi mềm điều hòa*):** **185,000 to 235,000 VND** (**$7.40 to $9.40 USD**) on weekdays; **250,000 VND** (**$10.00 USD**) on weekends.
  * **4-Berth Soft Sleeper Cabin (*Nằm khoang 4 điều hòa*):** **265,000 to 285,000 VND** (**$10.60 to $11.40 USD**).
* **Why the Train Takes 4 Hours:** The railway follows the main North–South mainline for **175 km** to **Binh Thuan Station (Muong Man)** before branching east onto a **12 km** spur track into Phan Thiet city. At Binh Thuan junction, the locomotive must uncouple and switch ends (**15-minute** operational stop). Do **not** book regular North–South reunification trains (**SE2, SE4, SE6, SE8**) unless you are willing to disembark at **Binh Thuan (Muong Man) Station**, which lies **37 km** (**50 minutes** and **450,000 VND** by taxi) southwest of Mui Ne.

### Connecting from Phan Thiet Railway Station to Mui Ne Resorts (22 km)

When **SPT2** pulls into Phan Thiet Station at **10:35**, you are still **18 to 25 km** west of the hotels on **Nguyen Dinh Chieu** and **Huynh Thuc Khang** streets:

1. **Public Bus #9 (35,000 VND / $1.40 USD):** Red-and-yellow **Bus #9** (operated by Binh Thuan Bus) waits inside the Phan Thiet Railway Station forecourt specifically timed to meet the **10:35** **SPT2** arrival. Conductors ask for your hotel name on board and drop you directly at your resort gate along Nguyen Dinh Chieu and Huynh Thuc Khang streets in **35 to 45 minutes**. Fare is **35,000 VND** (**$1.40 USD**) per passenger.
2. **Metered Taxi or GrabCar (250,000–320,000 VND / $10.00–$12.80 USD):** Sun Taxi (white), Mai Linh Taxi (green), and Vinasun wait outside the station gates. A ride to central Ham Tien (**Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street**) costs **250,000 to 280,000 VND** (**$10.00 to $11.20 USD**), while resorts further east near Mui Ne Market or Suoi Nuoc cost **300,000 to 350,000 VND** (**$12.00 to $14.00 USD**) and take **25 to 30 minutes**.

## Option 3: Private Car Charter via the CT01 Expressway (2.5 Hours)

For families, groups of three to six travelers, or kitesurfers carrying board bags, booking a private air-conditioned car with an experienced driver is the fastest way from Ho Chi Minh City or Tan Son Nhat Airport (**SGN**) straight to your resort lobby. Using the **CT01 Expressway**, a private car completes the **210 km** drive in **2 hours 30 minutes**—half the time of the train once station transfers are included.

* **4-Seater Sedan (Toyota Vios / Honda City / Mazda 3):** **1,500,000 to 1,650,000 VND** (**$60.00 to $66.00 USD**) one way, inclusive of driver, fuel, and **130,000 VND** in expressway tolls. Fits 2 adults and 2 suitcases.
* **7-Seater SUV / MPV (Toyota Innova Cross / Fortuner / Mitsubishi Xpander):** **1,700,000 to 1,950,000 VND** (**$68.00 to $78.00 USD**) one way. Fits 3 to 5 passengers plus luggage.
* **9-Seat VIP DCar Limousine Private Charter:** **2,800,000 to 3,300,000 VND** (**$112.00 to $132.00 USD**) one way.
* **Optional Coastal Sightseeing Detour:** If you have an extra **90 minutes**, ask your driver to exit at Phan Thiet city to visit the **Po Sah Inu Cham Towers** (**15,000 VND** entry) or detour via **Ke Ga Lighthouse** (**30 km** south of Phan Thiet, boat crossing **60,000 to 100,000 VND**) for an extra **300,000 VND** (**$12.00 USD**).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I go straight from Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN) to Mui Ne after landing?
Yes. If your flight lands at **SGN** before **17:30**, you can book a **Vie Limo** or **G5Car** 9-seat limousine van (**380,000 to 420,000 VND** / **$15.20 to $16.80 USD**) from the terminal pickup columns, or pre-book a private 4-seater or 7-seater car (**1,600,000 to 1,950,000 VND** / **$64.00 to $78.00 USD**) that reaches your Mui Ne resort in **2 hours 40 minutes**.

### How does the Mui Ne beach drive compare with Vung Tau or continuing up to Da Lat?
Thanks to the **CT01 Expressway**, reaching Mui Ne (**2.5 to 3 hours**) takes barely **30 minutes** longer than the trip covered in our [Saigon to Vung Tau Transport](/plan/saigon-to-vung-tau-transport/) guide. After your beach stay, instead of returning directly to Saigon, you can climb **1,500m** into the cool pine highlands in **3.5 to 4 hours** via our [Da Lat to Mui Ne Transport](/plan/da-lat-to-mui-ne-transport/) route over Dai Ninh Pass (**QL28B**).
