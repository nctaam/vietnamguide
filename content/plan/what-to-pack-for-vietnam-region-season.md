# What to Pack for Vietnam by Region & Season (2026): Clothing, Plugs & Dress Codes

Packing for a 14-day north-to-south route across Vietnam requires balancing 1,650 km of latitude and 3,143 meters of vertical elevation. Between December and March, a single itinerary can expose travelers to 3–10°C mountain fog at 1,500 meters in Sa Pa or Ha Giang and 33°C tropical sunshine in Ho Chi Minh City or Phu Quoc 48 hours later. Overpacking a 28-inch suitcase adds unnecessary baggage fees when neighborhood laundry shops (`Giặt Ủi`) wash, tumble-dry, and fold garments for 30,000–50,000 VND ($1.20–$2.00 USD) per kg within 6–12 hours.

Domestic aviation rules also dictate a compact gear strategy. While Vietnam Airlines (`VN`) allows 10–12 kg of carry-on luggage plus a 23 kg checked bag on standard economy tickets, low-cost carrier Vietjet Air (`VJ`) enforces a strict 7 kg combined cabin limit across all 60–120 minute domestic flights.

```
[REGIONAL & SEASONAL PACKING MATRIX]

  |-- Sacred Temples & Heritage Citadels -------------------------> Trousers/Skirt Past Knees + Sleeved Shirt
  |-- Aviation & Grid (220V / 50Hz, CAAV <=100Wh Power Bank) -----> Type A/C/F Plug + 20,000mAh Cabin Battery
```

## 2. Domestic Aviation Baggage Limits & CAAV Power Bank Regulations

Flying domestic legs between Hanoi (`HAN`), Da Nang (`DAD`, `760 km`, `80 minutes`), and Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`, `1,160 km`, `130 minutes`) requires strict compliance with airline cabin weight limits and Civil Aviation Authority of Vietnam (`CAAV`) lithium battery directives.

### Vietnam Airlines (`VN`) vs Vietjet Air (`VJ`) Weight Enforcement
* **Vietnam Airlines (`VN` — Full-Service Carrier)**: Economy Classic and Economy Flex fares include **1 carry-on bag up to `10 kg` plus 1 personal item up to `2 kg` (`12 kg` total cabin allowance)** and **1 checked suitcase up to `23 kg`** with maximum linear dimensions of `158 cm`. Note that lowest-tier *Economy Lite* promotional tickets (`950,000–1,350,000 VND` / `$37.25–$53.00 USD` one-way) include the `10 kg` cabin allowance with 0 checked bags unless purchased as an add-on (`220,000 VND` / `$8.60 USD` for `23 kg`).
* **Vietjet Air (`VJ` — Low-Cost Carrier) & Bamboo Airways (`QH`)**: Standard Eco fares enforce a **strict `7.0 kg` combined cabin weight limit** across both your main carry-on (`56 cm x 36 cm x 23 cm`) and small laptop bag. Gate agents at Noi Bai (`HAN`), Da Nang (`DAD`), and Tan Son Nhat (`SGN`) weigh cabin backpacks on digital scales before boarding. Exceeding `7.0 kg` at the boarding gate triggers a mandatory gate-check penalty of **`450,000 to 600,000 VND` (`$17.65–$23.50 USD`)**, whereas pre-booking a **`15 kg` or `20 kg` checked baggage allowance online** 24 hours prior to departure costs only **`180,000 to 260,000 VND` (`$7.10–$10.20 USD`)**.

### CAAV Lithium Power Bank & Battery Rules (2026 Enforcement)
Under **Civil Aviation Authority of Vietnam (`CAAV`)** and ICAO safety mandates enforced at all 22 Vietnamese commercial airports:
1. **Zero Power Banks in Checked Luggage (`0 Units` Allowed in Hold)**: All lithium-ion power banks, spare drone batteries, and removable camera batteries (`NP-FZ100`, `LP-E6NH`) must travel in your **carry-on cabin bag**. Automated X-ray screening at check-in will hold your suitcase if a battery remains inside.
2. **Capacity Limits (`<= 100Wh` vs `100–160Wh`)**: Power banks rated up to **`100Wh` (equivalent to `~27,000mAh` at `3.7V`, including standard `10,000mAh` and `20,000mAh` units)** are permitted in cabin baggage (up to 4 units per passenger) with clearly printed manufacturer `Wh` or `mAh` capacity labels on the shell. Batteries rated between **`100Wh and 160Wh`** are restricted to a **maximum of `2 units` per passenger** and require check-in supervisor approval tags. Batteries exceeding `160Wh` are prohibited on passenger aircraft.
3. **In-Flight Usage Prohibition**: Vietnam Airlines and Vietjet Air cabin crew prohibit connecting or charging smartphones from a power bank during taxi, takeoff, cruise, and landing; keep your power bank stored in your visible seat pocket or under-seat daypack during the 80–130 minute flight.

### Overnight Sleeper Train (`SE1/SE3`) & Limousine Van Luggage Dimensions
Beyond domestic flights, intercity surface corridors impose specific spatial constraints on luggage:
* **Vietnam Railways (`SE1/SE3` `1,726 km` Hanoi–HCMC & `SP3/SP4` `296 km` Hanoi–Lao Cai)**: Standard tickets permit `20 kg` of baggage per passenger. Inside 4-berth soft sleeper compartments (`1,150,000–1,650,000 VND` / `$45.00–$65.00 USD`), the storage bay beneath the two lower berths measures `30 cm` high by `85 cm` wide, while the recessed overhead cubby above the corridor door fits two `40L–50L` soft backpacks. Hard-shell suitcases taller than `68 cm` (`26 inches`) must stand vertically in the narrow `55 cm` footwell between bunks.
* **9-Seater VIP Limousine Vans (`Hanoi–Ninh Binh` `95 km` at `250,000 VND` / `$9.80 USD` & `Hanoi–Ha Long` `155 km` at `350,000 VND` / `$13.75 USD`)**: Converted Ford Transit vans feature oversized reclining leather captains' chairs in the cabin, leaving a compact rear trunk bay that accommodates seven `22-to-24-inch` suitcases. Operators charge an extra seat fee (`200,000–250,000 VND` / `$7.85–$9.80 USD`) if a traveler brings two oversized `28-inch` trunks.
* **High-Speed Island Ferries (`Ha Tien–Phu Quoc` `45 km` / `75 minutes` at `310,000 VND` / `$12.20 USD` & `Soc Trang–Con Dao` `90 km` / `2.5 hours` at `390,000 VND` / `$15.30 USD`)**: Superdong and Phu Quoc Express catamarans include `15 kg` of free hand luggage stored on open forward racks. Keep a `15L` waterproof dry bag (`180,000 VND` / `$7.10 USD`) for cameras and passports when boarding across open concrete jetties.

## 3. Electrical Grid (`220V / 50Hz`) & Universal Socket Compatibility

Vietnam's national electrical grid operates at **`220 Volts` and `50 Hz` frequency**. Across Hanoi, Da Nang, Hoi An, and Ho Chi Minh City—as well as onboard Vietnam Railways `SE1/SE3` sleeper cabins (`1,726 km` corridor)—wall outlets use dual-compatible hybrid faceplates that accept two plug profiles directly:
* **Type A (North American / Japanese Two-Pin Flat)**: Two flat parallel ungrounded prongs.
* **Type C & Type F (European / Schuko Two-Pin Round)**: Two round pins spaced `19 mm` apart with `4.0–4.8 mm` pin diameter.

## 4. Temple Dress Code & Heritage Site Entry Standards

Active Buddhist pagodas, Confucian academies, royal mausoleums, and state monuments enforce a strict modesty dress code requiring **covered shoulders and covered knees**. Security officers stationed at ticket turnstiles deny entry to visitors wearing tank tops, spaghetti straps, crop tops, or shorts above the patella.

* **Hanoi Heritage Monuments**: Strictly enforced at the **Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum (`0 VND` entry, open 07:30–10:30 except Mondays and Fridays)**, **One Pillar Pagoda**, **Presidential Palace (`40,000 VND` / `$1.55 USD`)**, **Temple of Literature (`Văn Miếu`, `70,000 VND` / `$2.75 USD`)**, **Tran Quoc Pagoda (`West Lake`, `0 VND`)**, and **Ngoc Son Temple (`Hoan Kiem Lake`, `50,000 VND` / `$1.95 USD`)**.
* **Central Vietnam Royal & Spiritual Sites**: Enforced across all 10 gates of the **Hue Imperial Citadel (`Đại Nội`, `200,000 VND` / `$7.85 USD` adult entry)**, the royal tombs of **Minh Mang, Tu Duc, and Khai Dinh (`150,000 VND` / `$5.90 USD` each or `420,000 VND` / `$16.50 USD` 3-tomb combo)**, **Thien Mu Pagoda (`5 km` west of Hue center)**, and **Linh Ung Pagoda (`Son Tra Peninsula`, `12 km` from Da Nang center)**.
* **Ninh Binh & Southern Sanctuaries**: Enforced at **Bai Dinh Pagoda (`100,000 VND` / `$3.95 USD` electric tram + `100,000 VND` stupa lift)**, **Hoa Lu Ancient Capital (`20,000 VND` / `$0.80 USD`)**, **Jade Emperor Pagoda in Ho Chi Minh City**, and the **Cao Dai Holy See in Tay Ninh (`90 km` northwest of HCMC, `2.5 hours` by road)**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Why Wrap Sarongs Are Rejected at State Mausoleums**: While small rural pagodas permit tying a woven scarf around your waist, security checkpoints at the **Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum** and **Hue Imperial Citadel (`200,000 VND` / `$7.85 USD`)** reject sheer beach sarongs wrapped over athletic shorts. Pack **2 dedicated temple outfits** consisting of lightweight linen trousers, midi skirts extending 5 cm below the knee, or zip-off convertible trekking pants paired with a short-sleeve collared shirt or crew-neck tee.

### Activity-Specific Gear: Motorbike Loops, Cave Treks & Overnight Cruises
* **Ha Giang Loop (`350 km` over `3–4 days`, `1,400–1,500 meters` passes) & Cao Bang (`360 km`)**: Even when valley towns read `22°C`, riding pillion or self-driving at `40 km/h` down Ma Pi Leng Pass creates a `7–9°C` windchill drop. * **Phong Nha-Ke Bang Caving (`Quang Binh`, `500 km` south of Hanoi)**: For standard boardwalk visits to **Paradise Cave (`31 km` total length, `250,000 VND` / `$9.80 USD`)** and **Phong Nha Cave (`550,000 VND` / `$21.60 USD` boat + `150,000 VND` / `$5.90 USD` ticket)**, trail runners with grippy rubber outsoles prevent slips on damp limestone steps. For multi-day jungle river expeditions (`Hang En` `2 days` at `8,000,000 VND` / `$314.00 USD` or `Tu Lan` `1–4 days`), pack `2 pairs` of thick calf-length synthetic trekking socks (`no low-cut ankle socks`, which allow sand and leeches inside river boots), quick-dry nylon trekking pants, and `2` long-sleeve UPF-50 rashguards.
* **Ha Long Bay & Lan Ha Bay Cruises (`155 km` east of Hanoi, `2.5 hours` via CT04)**: Overnight vessels (`3,800,000–6,500,000 VND` / `$149.00–$255.00 USD`) include complimentary kayaks and life vests. Pack `2` swimsuits (as high humidity on balcony railings slows drying time to `12 hours`), a waterproof floating phone pouch (`50,000 VND` / `$1.95 USD` at Tuan Chau Marina) for kayaking around Dark & Bright Cave, and a smart-casual outfit for the evening 5-course dining room service.

1. **Step 1 — Pack a 5-Day Capsule Wardrobe in a 40L Backpack (`<= 6.8 kg`)**: Select 5 moisture-wicking or 100% linen tops, 2 pairs of quick-dry shorts, 2 pairs of full-length temple trousers, 5 pairs of underwear/socks, 1 swimsuit, and 1 ultralight rain shell (`180 grams`). If visiting the North between November and March, wear your 250-gram packable down jacket and trail runners onto the plane so your cabin bag weighs under Vietjet Air's `7.0 kg` threshold.
2. **Step 2 — Drop Laundry at a Local `Giặt Ủi` Shop Every 4th Morning**: Skip 4-star hotel per-piece laundry rates (`50,000–90,000 VND` / `$1.95–$3.55 USD` per shirt) and walk 50–150 meters outside your hotel in Hanoi Old Quarter, Tam Coc, Hoi An, or District 1 HCMC to a local laundry-by-weight shop (`Giặt Ủi Theo Cân`). Hand over 2–3 kg of clothes at 08:30, pay **`30,000–50,000 VND` (`$1.20–$2.00 USD`) per kg**, and collect your washed, dried, and folded bundle within **6–12 hours** (`60,000–150,000 VND` / `$2.35–$5.90 USD` total).
3. **Step 3 — Store Main Luggage Free During 3–4 Day Mountain Loops**: Before departing on the **350 km Ha Giang Loop (`3–4 days`)** or a **2-day Sa Pa valley trek (`25 km` total walking)**, leave your primary wheeled suitcase or 40L pack locked inside your Hanoi hotel storage room (`0 VND`) or Ha Giang hostel depot (`0 VND`). Carry only a **`20L` waterproof daypack (`2.5–3.5 kg`)** strapped to the rear rack of your `110cc–150cc` motorbike (`200,000–350,000 VND` / `$7.85–$13.75 USD` daily rental).
4. **Step 4 — Pre-Book 20 kg Checked Allowance After Hoi An Tailoring**: If you commission custom linen shirts (`650,000–900,000 VND` / `$25.50–$35.30 USD`), dresses (`900,000–1,500,000 VND` / `$35.30–$58.80 USD`), or wool suits (`3,200,000–5,500,000 VND` / `$125.00–$215.00 USD`) in Hoi An, open your airline app 24 hours before your Da Nang (`DAD`) departure flight and add a **20 kg checked bag for `180,000–260,000 VND` (`$7.10–$10.20 USD`)** to avoid the `600,000 VND` (`$23.50 USD`) airport gate penalty.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I bring e-cigarettes, vapes, or IQOS heated tobacco into Vietnam in 2026?
**No.** Under **National Assembly Resolution 173/2024/QH15** (enforced nationwide since January 1, 2025), the importation, possession, transport, and use of electronic cigarettes, vape pods, e-liquids, and heated tobacco devices (`IQOS`) are strictly prohibited across Vietnam. Customs X-ray scanners at Hanoi (`HAN`), Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`), Da Nang (`DAD`), and Phu Quoc (`PQC`) confiscate all vaping hardware upon arrival and issue administrative fines starting at `1,000,000–2,000,000 VND` (`$39.20–$78.50 USD`).

### Are drones (`DJI Mini 4 Pro`) allowed in carry-on luggage for Vietnam?
All drone lithium batteries (`<= 100Wh`) must be packed in your cabin bag, while the drone airframe can travel in cabin or checked luggage. Operating any drone in Vietnam legally requires an advance flight permit from the **Operations Department of the Ministry of National Defence (`Cục Tác chiến`, processed over `14 days` at `$250–$400 USD` via licensed tour operators)**. Airport customs in Da Nang (`DAD`) and Phu Quoc (`PQC`) frequently inspect unregistered drones, and flying near military zones, border posts (`Ha Giang`, `Ban Gioc` `360 km` from Hanoi), or Ba Dinh Square carries fines of `10,000,000–40,000,000 VND` (`$392–$1,568 USD`) plus equipment seizure.

### Is a wheeled spinner suitcase or a backpack better for trains and ferries?
A **20-to-24-inch wheeled suitcase (`55 cm–65 cm` height, `10–15 kg`)** or a **40L–50L travel backpack** fits easily inside all GrabCars, 9-seater limousine vans (`250,000–400,000 VND` / `$9.80–$15.70 USD`), Ha Long cruise tenders, and the `30 cm` under-bunk storage bay inside Vietnam Railways 4-berth sleeper compartments (`SE1/SE3`). Avoid oversized `28-to-30-inch` (`76 cm+`) trunks, which cannot slide beneath train berths and require manual lifting up narrow 1.2-meter gangways on Mekong Delta and Phu Quoc ferries (`310,000 VND` / `$12.20 USD`).

### What cash, ATMs, and eSIM connectivity items should I pack in my daypack?
Carry a multi-currency debit card (such as Charles Schwab, Wise, or Revolut) plus $200 USD in crisp, uncreased $100 bills printed after 2017 for emergency exchange at licensed gold shops or Vietcombank counters. TPBank and VPBank ATMs across Hanoi, Da Nang, and Ho Chi Minh City dispense up to 5,000,000 VND ($196.00 USD) per withdrawal with 0 VND local operator fees. For mobile data, install a Viettel 4G/5G eSIM or physical SIM card (150,000–250,000 VND / $5.90–$9.80 USD for 5GB per day over 15–30 days) at the airport arrival hall, as Viettel maintains the strongest cellular reception along remote mountain passes in Ha Giang, Sa Pa, and Phong Nha.
