# Best Travel Apps for Vietnam (2026): Rides, Maps & Pay

Ten minutes after clearing customs at Noi Bai International Airport (HAN) or Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN), your smartphone becomes your primary defense against rigged taxi meters, language barriers, and ticket touts. Vietnam runs on a hyper-digitized mobile ecosystem in 2026. Street-corner noodle vendors scan instant QR codes, electric VinFast taxis dispatch in 90 seconds via GPS, and island speedboats read mobile barcodes at the pier. Yet several domestic apps block foreign tourists who lack a Vietnamese national ID card or resident bank account.

This field-tested guide covers the 12 essential mobile apps that work reliably for international travelers in 2026, organized by operational task, plus the pre-flight setup rules you must finish before leaving home.

## 1. Ride-Hailing & Electric Taxis: No Haggle, No Meter Rigging

Waving down an unmarked street cab outside Hanoi Railway Station or Ben Thanh Market is the fastest way to lose 400,000 VND ($16.00 USD) on a 3 km ride. App-dispatched rides lock in the exact route and VND fare before you open the door.

### Grab (The Essential Super-App)

**Grab** operates across Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang, Hoi An, Hue, Nha Trang, Da Lat, Can Tho, Vung Tau, and Phu Quoc. Inside one English interface, you can book a **GrabBike** motorbike taxi for solo hops through congested traffic (5,000 VND to 8,000 VND per km), a **GrabCar 4-seater** or **7-seater** SUV with cold air-conditioning (12,000 VND to 18,000 VND per km), or order **GrabFood** meals straight to your hotel lobby for a 15,000 VND to 25,000 VND ($0.60 to $1.00 USD) delivery fee. For full-day sightseeing around Da Nang, Hue, or Da Lat, Grab also offers an in-app **Rent by the Hour** package starting around 650,000 VND ($26.00 USD) for 4 hours with a dedicated car and driver.

> [!WARNING]
> **Crucial Pre-Flight Rule & Fake Screenshot Scam:** Install Grab, register your account, and link your foreign Visa or Mastercard **at home BEFORE boarding your flight to Vietnam** so the mandatory SMS OTP arrives on your home phone number. If you wait until landing in Vietnam with a data-only eSIM, you cannot receive that home SMS. At `HAN`, `SGN`, and `DAD` airport pickup lanes, touts in green jackets wave fake static screenshots of the Grab app and demand 500,000 VND ($20.00 USD). Always verify the **5-digit license plate** on the bumper against your live app screen before loading bags.

### Xanh SM (`GSM Green & Smart Mobility`)

Backed by Vietnamese conglomerate Vingroup, **Xanh SM** runs an unmistakable fleet of 100% electric, cyan-blue **VinFast** sedans, SUVs, and electric scooters across more than 45 provinces. Travelers frequently prefer Xanh SM over Grab for daily rides. Every vehicle features a spotless cabin and zero petrol exhaust smell—a major relief for passengers prone to motion sickness in stop-and-go traffic. Uniformed salaried drivers open doors politely, and fixed app upfront fares (12,000 VND to 16,000 VND per km) often beat Grab during afternoon rain surge pricing. Foreign cards work smoothly.

### Be (`Yellow App`) & Mai Linh / Vinasun Apps

Keep **Be** (the yellow-and-black app) installed as a fast local backup when Grab surges at 17:30 or when booking rides in secondary towns. Traditional metered taxi leaders **Mai Linh** (green sedans nationwide) and **Vinasun** (white sedans across southern Vietnam) also maintain reliable smartphone apps with upfront fare quotes and driver tracking at provincial railway terminals.

## 2. Intercity Trains, Buses, Ferries & City Public Transit

Specialized local transport apps save hours of ticket-counter queuing when traveling between provinces or riding municipal transit.

### Vexere (`vexere.com` App)

**Vexere** aggregates over 2,000 Vietnamese intercity bus and limousine operators on a single screen. Instant digital vouchers arrive immediately. Use the app to filter specifically for 22-cabin VIP palace buses (*Xe phòng đôi / đơn*), inspect verified customer cabin photos, pick a smooth `Tầng 1` lower berth (`A2–A5` or `B2–B5` in the middle of the wheelbase), and pay 165,000 VND to 750,000 VND ($6.60 to $30.00 USD) with a foreign credit card.

### 12Go Asia & Baolau (Official Trains & Island Ferries)

Because the official **Vietnam Railways (`dsvn.vn`)** payment gateway routinely declines non-Vietnamese bank cards at the 3D-Secure check, **12Go Asia** and **Baolau** serve as the best English-interface aggregators accepting foreign Visa, Mastercard, and American Express cards ($2.00 to $4.00 USD service fee). Both platforms connect directly to the railway inventory to let you choose exact berths on **SE1, SE3, SE19, and SE21 Reunification Express** 4-berth soft sleeper trains (850,000 VND to 1,450,000 VND / $34.00 to $58.00 USD) and send official QR boarding passes to your phone. They also sell confirmed tickets for **Superdong** and **Phu Quoc Express** high-speed catamarans linking Rach Gia, Ha Tien, and Vung Tau to Phu Quoc and Con Dao (230,000 VND to 340,000 VND, 1.5 hours to 2.5 hours).

### BusMap (`BusMap.vn`) & VinBus

**BusMap** displays live GPS bus positions, stop names, and walking transfer paths across major Vietnamese cities:

- **Hanoi Public Transit:** Track the orange **#86 Airport Express Bus** (45,000 VND / $1.80 USD, 50 minutes from Noi Bai Airport Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 directly to Long Bien, Hoan Kiem Lake, and Hanoi Railway Station), budget **#17** (15,000 VND), and **#47A** from Long Bien to Bat Trang Ceramic Village (7,000 VND / $0.28 USD).
- **Ho Chi Minh City Transit:** Track airport buses **#109** and **#152** from Tan Son Nhat Airport to District 1 (5,000 VND to 15,000 VND, 30 minutes) and connect to **Metro Line 1 (`Bến Thành – Suối Tiên`)**, which runs 19.7 km across 14 underground and elevated stations for 7,000 VND to 20,000 VND ($0.28 to $0.80 USD) with contactless Visa or Mastercard tap-in at the gates.
- **Phu Quoc Free VinBus:** Use **VinBus** or BusMap to track the **free `0 VND` electric VinBus Routes `17 & 19`** linking Phu Quoc Airport (PQC), Duong Dong Night Market, and Grand World every 15 minutes to 30 minutes day and night.

## 3. Communication, Translation & Offline Trail Navigation

### Zalo (`zalo.me`) & WhatsApp

Forget email once your trip begins. Roughly 99% of Vietnamese boutique hotels, Ha Long Bay and Lan Ha Bay cruise boats, Hoi An custom tailors, and Ha Giang Easy Riders coordinate pickup times and send driver license plate photos via **WhatsApp** and **Zalo**. While urban hotels use WhatsApp daily, **Zalo** is Vietnam's primary local messaging app used by over 75 million residents. Register Zalo with your home phone number (`+1`, `+44`, `+61`) before arrival so rural homestays in Pu Luong and sleeper bus dispatchers can text you pin drops directly.

### Google Translate (Download Offline Vietnamese Pack — 45 MB)

Open Google Translate on hotel Wi-Fi and download the **45 MB Offline Vietnamese Pack**. Use the live camera lens (**Google Lens**) to decode handwritten street-food menus (*bún bò*, *cao lầu*, *cơm hến*, *ốc* at 25,000 VND to 50,000 VND) and inspect pharmacy medicine boxes (`Paracetamol 500 mg` vs `Ibuprofen 400 mg`) at Long Chau or Pharmacity stores. The split-screen voice conversation mode also helps explain food allergies clearly.

### Google Maps vs Organic Maps / Maps.me (Offline GPX Trails)

Google Maps works well for urban walking directions, checking café reviews, and copying exact alley (`Ngõ / Hẻm`) addresses into Grab. Never trust it on mountain footpaths. Before trekking in **Sa Pa (Muong Hoa Valley)**, **Pu Luong Nature Reserve**, **Cat Ba National Park**, or riding the **Ha Giang Loop (`DT176` and `QL4C` mountain passes)**, install **Organic Maps** or **Maps.me** and download the **110 MB Vietnam Offline Map** to view unpaved rice-terrace trails, water sources, and elevation contours up to 3,143 m with zero cellular signal.

## 4. Payments, e-Wallets (`VietQR`) & Weather Radar

Why can't short-term visitors open local e-wallets like **MoMo, ZaloPay, or VNPay**? State Bank of Vietnam regulations require biometric verification with a Vietnamese national ID card or resident visa and domestic bank account. Instead, rely on this three-part payment and weather setup:

1. **Apple Pay & Google Pay (`Tap-to-Pay`):** Contactless NFC readers work effortlessly at Circle K, WinMart, GS25, Highlands Coffee, Grab, Lotte Mart, Uniqlo, hotels, and chain pharmacies. Pair a zero-foreign-fee **Wise** or **Revolut** Visa or Mastercard and always insist on paying in **VND** to avoid 4% to 6% Dynamic Currency Conversion markups.
2. **Moreta Pay & Cash Buffer:** Tourist QR wallets like **Moreta Pay** (alongside cross-border ASEAN QR banking apps from Thailand, Singapore, Malaysia, and South Korea) allow international visitors to scan merchant **VietQR** standees at smaller cafés for a 1.5% to 2.5% fee. Always carry 500,000 VND to 1,000,000 VND ($20.00 to $40.00 USD) in physical 20,000 VND, 50,000 VND, and 100,000 VND polymer bills for street *bánh mì* carts (25,000 VND to 35,000 VND), wet markets, and 5,000 VND motorbike parking slips.
3. **Windy.com:** Skip default phone weather icons that show rain all summer. Use **Windy.com** to track real-time South China Sea (`Biển Đông`) typhoons, wind gusts, and Ha Long Bay or Phu Quoc marine wave height (`< 1.5 m` required for port authorities to clear overnight bay cruises and island catamarans).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use my foreign Visa or Mastercard on Grab and Xanh SM in Vietnam?
Yes. Both Grab and Xanh SM accept international Visa and Mastercard cards in 2026. Link your card inside Grab before leaving your home country so you can receive your bank's 3D-Secure and SMS OTP verification codes without roaming issues.

### Why is Xanh SM often better than Grab during heavy rain?
Xanh SM operates 100% electric VinFast taxis with regulated fare tiers (12,000 VND to 16,000 VND per km) that do not apply aggressive dynamic surge multipliers during 17:00 monsoon downpours, often saving you 25% to 35% compared to GrabCar.

### Do I need a local Vietnamese SIM voice number for these apps?
No. A data-only travel eSIM (such as Viettel) works seamlessly once Grab, WhatsApp, and Zalo are registered to your home phone number. All driver pickups and hotel messages happen via in-app text and photo chat.

### Can foreign tourists scan VietQR codes at street stalls?
You cannot link a foreign credit card directly to MoMo or VNPay without a Vietnamese bank account, but you can scan merchant VietQR codes using tourist fintech wallets like Moreta Pay or ASEAN cross-border banking apps, or simply pay cash in polymer VND notes.
