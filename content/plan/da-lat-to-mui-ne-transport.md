# Da Lat to Mui Ne Transport (2026): Bus, Van & Car

Dropping from the cool pine plateau of Da Lat (**1,500m** elevation in Lam Dong Province) down to the sun-baked red and white sand dunes of Mui Ne (**sea level** along Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street, Phan Thiet) is one of southern Vietnam's classic topography shifts. Although the straight-line distance is short, navigating the eastern escarpment of the Langbiang Plateau requires **155 to 165 km** of mountain pass driving across **4 to 4.5 hours**. No train or flight connects the two resorts directly; road vehicles carry 100% of travelers between the highlands and the Binh Thuan coast.

For most travelers in 2026, a **9-seat VIP limousine van** (**250,000 to 320,000 VND** / **$10.00 to $12.80 USD**) or a **daytime 29-seat tourist coaster** (**180,000 to 240,000 VND** / **$7.20 to $9.60 USD**) is the best way to make the descent. Both options take **4 to 4.5 hours**, depart in the morning (**07:30 to 08:00**) or early afternoon (**12:30 to 13:30**), and drop passengers directly along the **18 km** Ham Tien–Mui Ne beach strip. Families and small groups often upgrade to a **private 4-seater or 7-seater car** (**1,450,000 to 1,850,000 VND** / **$58.00 to $74.00 USD**) to stop at **Pongour Waterfall** and the **Bau Trang White Sand Dunes** en route.

Before locking in your seat, verify your hotel locations in our guides to [Where to Stay in Da Lat](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-da-lat/) and [Where to Stay in Mui Ne](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-mui-ne/) so your driver knows your exact resort kilometer marker on Nguyen Dinh Chieu or Huynh Thuc Khang Street.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **2026 Highway QL28B Widening Updates:** National Highway **QL28B** over **Dai Ninh Pass (Đèo Đại Ninh)** is the shortest **155 km** corridor between Da Lat and Mui Ne. Ongoing road widening and bridge realignments on the middle pass sections can cause **15 to 30-minute** single-lane flagger delays during weekday working hours (**08:00 to 17:00**). When heavy machinery slows **QL28B**, nimble 9-seat limousines and private cars often detour **10 km** further west via Di Linh and **Gia Bac Pass (Đèo Gia Bắc)** on **QL28** (**165 km** total) to keep transit times steady at **4 hours**.

## Comparing the Two Mountain Pass Routes: QL28B vs QL28

Every vehicle leaving Da Lat for Mui Ne starts by descending **Prenn Pass (Đèo Prenn)**—now widened to a smooth **14m** four-lane boulevard—and heading south on National Highway **QL20** past Lien Khuong Airport (**DLI**). From **QL20**, drivers choose between two mountain pass corridors:

### 1. The Dai Ninh Pass Corridor (QL20 + QL28B — 155 km)
* **Route Path:** Follow **QL20** for **48 km** to the **Ninh Gia T-junction** in Duc Trong District, then turn left onto **QL28B** for **68 km** across **Dai Ninh Pass (Đèo Đại Ninh)** down to Luong Son town on **QL1A**, before taking the coastal road past **Bau Trang (White Sand Dunes)** into eastern Mui Ne.
* **Road Conditions:** Drops **1,000m** over **25 km** of sweeping switchbacks overlooking the turquoise **Dai Ninh Hydropower Reservoir** and Song Luy valley. While the **QL28B** upgrade project is laying fresh two-lane asphalt across long stretches, roughly **8 to 12 km** of mid-pass hairpins still have loose gravel transitions where construction crews work.
* **Who Uses It:** All 29-seat tourist buses, most 9-seat VIP limousines, and sightseers combining the drive with **Pongour Waterfall** and the **White Sand Dunes**.

### 2. The Gia Bac Pass Corridor (QL20 + QL28 — 165 km)
* **Route Path:** Continue south on **QL20** for **80 km** to **Di Linh town**, then turn left onto **QL28** for **85 km** down winding **Gia Bac Pass (Đèo Gia Bắc)** through Ham Thuan Bac directly into Phan Thiet city, before turning east for **12 km** onto Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street in Ham Tien / Mui Ne.
* **Road Conditions:** Narrower two-lane tarmac with tighter, shaded forest hairpins over the **18 km** Gia Bac descent, but free of heavy road-widening construction zones.
* **Who Uses It:** Private 4-seater sedans, 7-seater SUVs, and limousine drivers bypassing **QL28B** construction slowdowns or dropping passengers at western Phu Hai / Victoria Phan Thiet resorts first.

## Option 1: 9-Seat VIP Limousine Vans (Top Recommendation)

Because descending **1,500m** of mountain hairpins causes strong lateral body roll inside high-deck 40-berth sleeper buses, **9-seat and 11-seat VIP limousine vans** (converted Ford Transit and Hyundai Solati vans) offer a much smoother, lower-center-of-gravity ride down to the coast.

### Top Limousine Operators, Schedules, and Fares

* **Lac Hong Limousine (Nhà Xe Lạc Hồng):** Runs modern 9-seat vans departing Da Lat daily at **07:30, 08:00, 12:30, 13:00, and 15:30**. Fares cost **260,000 to 300,000 VND** (**$10.40 to $12.00 USD**), including hotel pickup within **5 km** of Da Lat Market and direct drop-off at any resort along Nguyen Dinh Chieu and Huynh Thuc Khang streets in Mui Ne.
* **Dan Anh Limousine (Nhà Xe Đan Anh):** One of Da Lat's premier limousine fleets, operating morning (**07:30, 08:30**) and afternoon (**13:00, 14:00**) departures. Tickets cost **270,000 to 320,000 VND** (**$10.80 to $12.80 USD**) for plush leather captain's chairs with USB charging ports, drinking water, and strong air conditioning.
* **Nguyen Kim Limousine (Nhà Xe Nguyễn Kim):** Operates 9-seat VIP vans at **07:45 and 13:00** for **250,000 to 290,000 VND** (**$10.00 to $11.60 USD**). Known for careful drivers who maintain smooth braking on **Dai Ninh Pass**.
* **Thinh Phat Limousine (Nhà Xe Thịnh Phát):** Runs daily departures at **07:30 and 13:00** priced at **250,000 to 280,000 VND** (**$10.00 to $11.20 USD**) with door-to-door pickup in central Da Lat wards (Wards 1, 2, 3, 4, and 10).

### Seating Strategy for Motion Sickness

If you are prone to car sickness on mountain passes, pay the **20,000 to 30,000 VND** (**$0.80 to $1.20 USD**) supplement for **middle-row single captain's seats (Seats 3, 4, 5, or 6)** where you have a clear forward view through the windshield and sit directly between the front and rear wheels. Take motion sickness medication (*Thuốc chống say xe*, **10,000 VND** at any Da Lat pharmacy) **30 minutes** before descending Prenn Pass.

## Option 2: Tourist Open Buses & 29-Seat Coasters

Budget backpackers and travelers carrying oversized surfboards, kitesurfing bags, or **28-inch** suitcases typically book **29-seat Hyundai County/Thaco coasters** or **34-berth tourist coaches** operating fixed morning and early afternoon runs.

* **The Sinh Tourist (Sinhbalo):** Departs from its Da Lat office at **22 Bui Thi Xuan Street, Ward 2** daily at **07:30 and 13:00**, arriving at **144 Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street** in Ham Tien (Mui Ne) in **4 to 4.5 hours**. Tickets cost **190,000 to 230,000 VND** (**$7.60 to $9.20 USD**).
* **Hanh Cafe (Hạnh Café):** Runs daily **07:30 and 13:00** departures from Da Lat (**Nguyen Van Troi / Bui Thi Xuan area**) right to its Mui Ne main terminal at **117 Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street**. Fares run **180,000 to 220,000 VND** (**$7.20 to $8.80 USD**), including hotel pickup in Da Lat and drop-off along the main beach strip as buses enter from the east.
* **An Phu Travel & Tammys Travel:** Popular backpacker-oriented operators running 29-seat air-conditioned coasters departing at **07:30, 08:00, and 13:00** for **190,000 to 240,000 VND** (**$7.60 to $9.60 USD**). Buses make one **20-minute** rest stop at the top of Dai Ninh Pass for coffee and photos, plus a brief bathroom stop in Luong Son town.

## Option 3: Private Car Charter with Sightseeing Stops

Hiring a private sedan or SUV with a driver is the only way to combine the Da Lat-to-Mui Ne transfer with two of southern Vietnam's best natural landmarks located right along the **QL20 / QL28B** route:

* **4-Seater Sedan (Toyota Vios, Hyundai Accent, Mazda 3):** **1,450,000 to 1,600,000 VND** (**$58.00 to $64.00 USD**) point-to-point for 2 to 3 passengers.
* **7-Seater SUV/MPV (Toyota Innova, Fortuner, Mitsubishi Xpander):** **1,650,000 to 1,850,000 VND** (**$66.00 to $74.00 USD**) point-to-point for 4 passengers with heavy luggage or kitesurfing boards.
* **16-Seater Minibus (Ford Transit, Hyundai Solati):** **2,400,000 to 2,800,000 VND** (**$96.00 to $112.00 USD**) for groups up to 10 travelers.

### Recommended En-Route Detours (Add 300,000 to 400,000 VND / $12 to $16 USD)
1. **Pongour Waterfall (Thác Pongour):** Located **50 km** south of Da Lat, just **6 km** off **QL20** before the Ninh Gia junction. Seven terraced basalt rock steps span **100m** across and drop **40m**. Entrance ticket: **20,000 VND** (**$0.80 USD**); parking: **10,000 VND**. Allow **60 minutes**.
2. **Bau Trang White Sand Dunes (Đồi Cát Trắng / Bàu Trắng):** Located **130 km** into the drive along the scenic **DT716** coastal desert highway between Luong Son and Mui Ne. Entrance ticket: **15,000 VND** (**$0.60 USD**); optional 20-minute quad-bike (ATV) or Land Cruiser dune ride costs **600,000 to 800,000 VND** (**$24.00 to $32.00 USD**) per vehicle.

## Option 4: Self-Drive Motorbike Descent & Baggage Forwarding

Riding from Da Lat (**18°C** pine forests) down to Mui Ne (**32°C** coastal dunes) takes **4.5 to 5 hours**. If you are heading north instead, compare this coastal drop with our [Da Lat to Pleiku Transport](/plan/da-lat-to-pleiku-transport/) highland route.

* **One-Way Rental & Luggage Forwarding:** One-way motorbike rentals cost **200,000 to 300,000 VND/day** (**$8.00 to $12.00 USD**) plus a **350,000 to 450,000 VND** (**$14.00 to $18.00 USD**) bike return fee; agencies forward your main bag on a morning **Hanh Cafe** or **An Phu** bus to your Mui Ne hotel.
* **Braking & Speed Traps:** Test both disc brakes before departure. On the **25 km** descent down **Dai Ninh Pass** or **Gia Bac Pass**, use engine braking (2nd or 3rd gear) rather than riding the brake levers continuously. Watch for **50 km/h** urban speed cameras on **QL20** through Lien Nghia and Di Linh, and carry your physical **1968 Convention International Driving Permit (IDP)**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Are there overnight sleeper buses from Da Lat to Mui Ne?
No. Because the **155 km** drive takes only **4 hours**, no operators run overnight sleepers between Da Lat and Mui Ne. All buses and vans run during daylight hours between **07:00 and 15:30**.

### Should I take the morning (07:30) or afternoon (13:00) bus to Mui Ne?
Book the **07:30 morning departure**, especially during the rainy season (**May to October**). Morning buses descend **Dai Ninh Pass** between **08:45 and 10:00** under clear skies and reach Mui Ne around **11:45 to 12:00** for hotel check-in, avoiding the heavy highland rain and pass fog that roll in after **14:30**.
