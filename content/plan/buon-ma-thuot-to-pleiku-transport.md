# Buon Ma Thuot to Pleiku Transport (2026): Bus vs Van

Traveling north from Buon Ma Thuot to Pleiku covers **180 km** across the heart of Vietnam's Central Highlands along National Highway **QL14** (also designated as Asian Highway **AH17** and part of the Ho Chi Minh Highway corridor). The four-lane asphalt highway climbs gradually from Buon Ma Thuot in Dak Lak Province (**536m** elevation) across rolling red basalt plateaus planted with robusta coffee, black pepper, and rubber trees to Pleiku in Gia Lai Province (**750m** elevation). No commercial rail lines serve either highland capital, and flying between the two airports requires a detour via Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi. Road transit handles 100% of passenger movement along this backbone route.

For most independent travelers in 2026, a **9-seat VIP limousine van** is the fastest and most practical choice. Vans take **3.5 hours**, cost **180,000 to 220,000 VND** (**$7.20 to $8.80 USD**), and include door-to-door pickup in central Buon Ma Thuot with direct drop-off at hotels in Pleiku. Standard interprovincial sleeper and seater buses cost **130,000 to 160,000 VND** (**$5.20 to $6.40 USD**) for a **3.5 to 4-hour** ride between main bus terminals, while private 4-seater or 7-seater car charters run **1,700,000 to 2,200,000 VND** (**$68.00 to $88.00 USD**) one way.

Before booking your northbound departure, confirm your hotel addresses in our guides to [Where to Stay in Buon Ma Thuot](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-buon-ma-thuot/) and [Where to Stay in Pleiku](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-pleiku/) so your driver can log exact GPS pickup and drop-off coordinates.

> [!TIP]
> **Door-to-Door Fare Math:** While an interprovincial bus ticket at Buon Ma Thuot Northern Bus Station costs **40,000 to 60,000 VND** less than a 9-seat VIP limousine van, taking a metered taxi to the departure terminal (**60,000 to 85,000 VND**) and another taxi from Duc Long Gia Lai Bus Station into downtown Pleiku (**50,000 to 70,000 VND**) makes the bus more expensive overall for solo travelers and couples.

## Option 1: 9-Seat VIP Limousine Vans (Top Recommendation)

Shared **9-seat and 11-seat VIP limousine vans** (converted Ford Transit and Hyundai Solati minibuses) dominate point-to-point passenger transport between Buon Ma Thuot and Pleiku. Instead of hauling suitcases to outer highway terminals, limousine operators send a shuttle or the main van directly to your hotel lobby in Buon Ma Thuot, cruise up the four-lane stretches of **QL14**, and drop you at your hotel doorstep in central Pleiku.

### Major Limousine Operators, Schedules, and Fares

Departures run hourly from **05:30 to 18:30** daily. Fares are standardized across major highland fleets, though middle-row captain's chairs occasionally cost **20,000 to 30,000 VND** (**$0.80 to $1.20 USD**) more than front bench seats next to the driver or rear row seats.

* **Rung Thong Limousine (Nhà Xe Rừng Thông):** Runs modern 9-seat Ford Transit and Hyundai Solati vans departing hourly between **06:00 and 18:00**. Tickets cost **200,000 to 220,000 VND** (**$8.00 to $8.80 USD**). Free door-to-door pickup covers quarters within **5 km** of the Nga Sau Ban Me intersection in Buon Ma Thuot and drops off across central Pleiku wards (Hoi Thuong, Dien Hong, Phai Dong, and Hoa Lu).
* **Thu Duc Limousine (Nhà Xe Thu Đức):** Operates daily departures at **05:30, 07:30, 09:30, 11:30, 13:30, 15:30, and 17:30**. Fares range from **180,000 to 200,000 VND** (**$7.20 to $8.00 USD**). Vehicles feature leather reclining chairs, individual USB-A and USB-C charging ports, bottled water, and strong cabin air conditioning.
* **Minh Quoc Limousine (Nhà Xe Minh Quốc):** Based in the northern highlands, Minh Quoc runs reliable northbound vans through Buon Ma Thuot and Pleiku between **06:30 and 18:30**. Tickets cost **190,000 to 220,000 VND** (**$7.60 to $8.80 USD**). Ideal if you plan to continue later via [Pleiku to Kon Tum Transport](/plan/pleiku-to-kon-tum-transport/) (**50 km** further north).
* **Viet Tan Phat (Việt Tân Phát):** Operates both VIP limousine vans and 34-berth sleeper buses along the **QL14** corridor. Limousine tickets cost **190,000 to 210,000 VND** (**$7.60 to $8.40 USD**) with departures at **07:00, 10:00, 14:00, and 17:00**.

### Pickup Logistics and Luggage Limits

Drivers call or message via Zalo/WhatsApp **30 to 45 minutes** before arrival. Wait in your hotel lobby **20 minutes** prior to your slot. Each passenger is limited to **one medium suitcase (20 to 24 inches, up to 15 kg)** plus a small daypack. If you carry oversized hard-shell luggage (**28 inches** or larger), notify the dispatcher when booking or take an interprovincial sleeper bus with under-floor cargo bays.

## Option 2: Interprovincial Sleeper and Seater Buses

Full-size **29-seat coasters, 34-VIP cabin buses, and 40-berth sleeper buses** ply **QL14** all day and night.

* **Buon Ma Thuot Departure Point:** Northbound highway buses depart from **Buon Ma Thuot Northern Bus Station (Bến Xe Phía Bắc Buôn Ma Thuột)** at **71 Nguyen Chi Thanh Street, Tan An Ward**, **4.5 km** northeast of the Nga Sau city center. A Mai Linh, Vinasun, or Grab taxi from downtown takes **10 to 12 minutes** and costs **60,000 to 85,000 VND** (**$2.40 to $3.40 USD**).
* **Pleiku Arrival Point:** Buses pull into **Duc Long Gia Lai Bus Station (Bến Xe Đức Long Gia Lai)** at **43 Ly Nam De Street, Tra Ba Ward**, **3.5 km** southeast of Pleiku's Dai Doan Ket Square. A Grab car or taxi to central Pleiku hotels costs **50,000 to 70,000 VND** (**$2.00 to $2.80 USD**) and takes **8 to 10 minutes**.
* **Fares & Schedules:** Tickets cost **130,000 to 160,000 VND** (**$5.20 to $6.40 USD**) for standard coaches, and up to **200,000 VND** (**$8.00 USD**) for 34-berth VIP sleeper buses (**Viet Tan Phat**, **Bao Thinh**, **Co Hai**, **Khuong Vy**), departing every **45 to 60 minutes** between **05:00 and 19:00**. Total travel time is **3.5 to 4 hours**, including a **15-minute** rest stop near Ea H'leo or Chu Se.

## Option 3: Private Car Charter (4-Seater, 7-Seater, and 16-Seater)

Hiring a private car with a driver gives families, coffee buyers, and small groups full control over departure times and photo stops along the plateau.

* **4-Seater Sedan (Toyota Vios, Hyundai Accent, Honda City):** **1,700,000 to 1,900,000 VND** (**$68.00 to $76.00 USD**) one way for up to 3 passengers.
* **7-Seater SUV/MPV (Toyota Innova, Fortuner, Mitsubishi Xpander):** **1,900,000 to 2,200,000 VND** (**$76.00 to $88.00 USD**) one way for 4 passengers with luggage.
* **16-Seater Minibus (Ford Transit, Hyundai Solati):** **2,800,000 to 3,300,000 VND** (**$112.00 to $132.00 USD**) one way for groups up to 10 passengers.

Rates include highway fuel, driver wages, and the two BOT toll plazas along **QL14** (Ea H'leo BOT and Duc Long BOT, costing **35,000 to 50,000 VND** per pass). Non-stop driving takes **3 to 3.5 hours**. For an extra **300,000 to 400,000 VND** (**$12.00 to $16.00 USD**), drivers will stop at **Chu Se** pepper farms or **Bien Ho (T'Nung Lake)** volcanic crater (**7 km** north of Pleiku, **10,000 VND** ticket).

## Option 4: Self-Drive Motorbike Touring Along Highway 14

Riding a motorbike **180 km** north on **QL14** is a smooth single-day ride with gentle elevation gains from **536m** in Buon Ma Thuot to **750m** in Pleiku.

### Route Breakdown and Key Waypoints

1. **Buon Ma Thuot to Buon Ho (40 km, 50 minutes):** Ride north out of Tan An Ward past coffee plants and rubber estates on a 4-lane divided highway to Buon Ho town.
2. **Buon Ho to Ea H'leo / Cau 110 (45 km, 60 minutes):** Rolling red-earth hills flanked by tall wind turbine farms in Ea Nam and Ea H'leo. Cau 110 marks the Dak Lak–Gia Lai border.
3. **Cau 110 to Chu Prong & Chu Se (55 km, 75 minutes):** Pass black pepper plantations trained on wooden poles. Stop in **Chu Se town (Km 140)** for *Cơm Gà Chư Sê* (grilled chicken with sticky rice, **65,000 to 90,000 VND** / **$2.60 to $3.60 USD**) and dried Chu Se peppercorns (**180,000 to 240,000 VND/kg**).
4. **Chu Se to Pleiku City Center (40 km, 50 minutes):** Climb gently past Ham Rong Mountain (**1,028m** extinct volcano) into Southern Pleiku via Le Duan Boulevard.

### Highway Speed Traps and Licensing Rules

Provincial patrol officers (*CSGT*) in both Dak Lak and Gia Lai enforce speed limits strictly with mobile radar guns and fixed camera gantries along **QL14**. Watch for blue municipal town entry signs (*Khu đông dân cư*):

* **Urban / Town Zones (Buon Ho, Ea Drang/Ea H'leo, Phu Nhon, and Chu Se):** Maximum speed drops to **50 km/h** on undivided two-way roads and **60 km/h** on roads with a hard center median. Speeding by **6 to 10 km/h** through Buon Ho or Chu Se triggers immediate pull-overs and fines from **800,000 to 1,000,000 VND** (**$32.00 to $40.00 USD**).
* **Open Highway Zones & Rentals:** Outside town limits, motorbikes are capped at **60 km/h** (undivided) or **70 km/h** (divided median). Carry your physical **1968 Convention International Driving Permit (IDP)**. Local rental shops in Buon Ma Thuot charge **150,000 to 200,000 VND/day** (**$6.00 to $8.00 USD**) for a Honda Wave or **300,000 to 350,000 VND/day** (**$12.00 to $14.00 USD**) for a Honda XR150L, plus a **400,000 to 600,000 VND** (**$16.00 to $24.00 USD**) bus cargo return fee if dropping the bike in Pleiku.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I travel from Buon Ma Thuot to Pleiku and fly out of Pleiku Airport (PXU) the same day?
Yes. Pleiku Airport (**PXU**) lies **6 km** north of downtown Pleiku, and most limousine vans (**Rung Thong**, **Thu Duc**, **Minh Quoc**) will drop you directly at the **PXU** terminal gate for no extra fee or a small **30,000 VND** (**$1.20 USD**) surcharge. Book a van departing Buon Ma Thuot at least **5.5 to 6 hours** before your scheduled flight takeoff.

### When is the best time of year for the Highway 14 drive?
The dry season from **November to April** brings clear skies, cool mornings (**16°C to 19°C** in Pleiku), and white coffee blossoms in **February and March**. During the rainy season (**May to October**), afternoon downpours hit between **14:00 and 18:00**, washing slick red basalt clay onto **QL14** shoulders—book morning departures (**07:00 to 09:30**) for dry road conditions.
