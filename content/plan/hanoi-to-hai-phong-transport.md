## Corridor Overview: Meter-Gauge Railway vs Expressway CT04

Two high-frequency transport arteries connect Hanoi with Hai Phong, northern Vietnam's largest port city and the primary gateway to Cat Ba Island and Lan Ha Bay. The 102 km meter-gauge Hanoi–Hai Phong railway (built in 1902 and upgraded with **Hoa Phượng Đỏ** luxury carriages) runs directly from the edge of Hanoi's Old Quarter across the historic 1,682-meter Long Bien Bridge into downtown Hai Phong at 75 Luong Khanh Thien Street. Running parallel to the south, the 6-lane Hanoi–Hai Phong Expressway (CT04 / QL5B) stretches 105.5 km from Thanh Tri Bridge to Dinh Vu Port, allowing passenger vans and expressway coaches to cruise at 120 km/h and complete the highway run in 75 minutes to 90 minutes.

Choosing between the train and an expressway van depends on your destination. If you are heading to central Hai Phong for a half-day street food tour (*food tour Hải Phòng*), the train drops you right in the historic French Quarter within 400 meters of the city's best crab noodle soup stalls. If you carry heavy luggage, travel from western Hanoi (Cau Giay or Ba Dinh), or connect directly to Dong Bai Ferry Terminal for Cat Ba Island, a 9-seat DCar limousine or **Hai Au** expressway bus saves 45 minutes.

```
[Hanoi Old Quarter]
   |
   +---> [Option A: Rail Corridor (102 km, 2.5 - 2.75 hours)]
   |     Hanoi Station (120 Le Duan) / Long Bien Station (Old Quarter)
   |        ---> Gia Lam ---> Hai Duong ---> Thuong Ly ---> Hai Phong Station
   |             (75 Luong Khanh Thien — Core Food Tour District)
   |
   +---> [Option B: CT04 / 5B Expressway Corridor (105 km, 80 - 110 mins)]
         Gia Lam Bus Station / Nuoc Ngam / Old Quarter Hotel Pickup
            ---> Expressway CT04 @ 120 km/h ---> Exit QL10 / Pham Van Dong
            ---> Lach Tray / Niem Nghia / Thuong Ly Bus Stations
```

> [!WARNING]
> **Weekday vs. Weekend Station Rule (Hanoi vs. Long Bien):** Do not blindly take a taxi to Hanoi Main Station (120 Le Duan Street) for mid-day trains without checking the day of the week. On **weekdays (Monday–Friday)**, only the 06:00 AM **HP1** train originates at Hanoi Main Station; trains **LP2** (09:25 AM), **LP6** (03:26 PM), and **LP8** (06:20 PM) originate exclusively at **Long Bien Station** (Ga Long Biên, right beside Long Bien Bridge at the northern tip of the Old Quarter). On **Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays**, all four trains originate at Hanoi Main Station 10 minutes earlier and then stop at Long Bien Station to pick up Old Quarter passengers.

## Vietnam Railways: Schedule, Stations & "Hoa Phượng Đỏ" Carriages

Four daily train pairs run between Hanoi and Hai Phong under the national rail timetable: **HP1, LP2, LP6, and LP8** heading east to the coast, and **LP3, LP5, LP7, and HP2** returning west to the capital. Because the meter-gauge track crosses directly over the single-track 1902 Long Bien cantilever bridge at 15 km/h, passengers get an unobstructed view of the Red River banana farms and steel trusswork right from their window.

### Official 2026 Train Timetable (Hanoi / Long Bien to Hai Phong)

- **Train HP1:** Departs Hanoi Station at **06:00 AM** (stops at Long Bien Station **06:10 AM**) -> Arrives Hai Phong Station at **08:25 AM** (2 hours 25 minutes). Runs daily.
- **Train LP2:** Departs Hanoi Station at **09:15 AM** (Sat–Sun only) / Departs Long Bien Station at **09:25 AM** (Daily) -> Arrives Hai Phong Station at **12:00 PM** (2 hours 35 minutes).
- **Train LP6:** Departs Hanoi Station at **03:15 PM** (Sat–Sun only) / Departs Long Bien Station at **03:26 PM** (Daily) -> Arrives Hai Phong Station at **06:00 PM** (2 hours 34 minutes).
- **Train LP8:** Departs Hanoi Station at **06:10 PM** (Sat–Sun & Holidays) / Departs Long Bien Station at **06:20 PM** (Daily) -> Arrives Hai Phong Station at **09:05 PM** (2 hours 45 minutes).

### Official 2026 Return Timetable (Hai Phong to Long Bien / Hanoi)

- **Train LP3:** Departs Hai Phong at **09:05 AM** -> Arrives Long Bien at **11:32 AM** (terminates at Hanoi Station **11:45 AM** on Sat–Sun).
- **Train LP5:** Departs Hai Phong at **03:00 PM** -> Arrives Long Bien at **05:38 PM** -> Terminates at Hanoi Station at **05:50 PM** (Daily).
- **Train LP7:** Departs Hai Phong at **06:10 PM** -> Arrives Long Bien at **08:43 PM** -> Terminates at Hanoi Station at **08:55 PM** (Daily).
- **Train HP2:** Departs Hai Phong at **06:40 PM** -> Arrives Long Bien at **09:04 PM** -> Terminates at Hanoi Station at **09:15 PM** (Daily).

### Three Seat Classes & "Hoa Phượng Đỏ" Upgrades

Launched in May 2024 and expanded across the fleet for 2026, the **Hoa Phượng Đỏ** (Red Flamboyant) carriages transform this commuter route into a boutique rail trip:

1. **VIP 34-Seat Indochine Salon Carriage (Toa VIP 34 chỗ):** Fitted with hardwood floors, brass sconces, 16 leather club armchairs, and 5 four-person sofa booths, plus a beverage bar and free Wi-Fi. Tickets cost **250,000 VND** ($10.00) Monday–Thursday and **300,000 VND** ($12.00) Friday–Sunday, including one complimentary drink.
2. **First-Class 56-Seat Rotating Carriage (Toa hạng nhất 56 chỗ):** Features reclining seats that rotate 180 degrees so groups of four can face each other. Fares run **150,000 VND** ($6.00) on weekdays and **180,000 VND** ($7.20) on weekends.
3. **Economy 64-Seat Air-Conditioned Soft Seat (Ngồi mềm điều hòa - NML):** Clean 2x2 airline-style seating with overhead racks and wall power outlets. Fares cost **95,000 VND to 105,000 VND** ($3.80 to $4.20) Monday–Thursday and **115,000 VND to 125,000 VND** ($4.60 to $5.00) Friday–Sunday.

> [!TIP]
> **Long Bien Station Entrance Trick:** When boarding at **Long Bien Station** from the Old Quarter, head to the pedestrian gate on **Gam Cau Street / Tran Nhat Duat intersection** right beneath the bridge approach arch (10 minutes on foot or a 30,000 VND / $1.20 GrabBike from Hoan Kiem Lake). Arrive 20 minutes before departure. Note that if you want to ship a rented motorbike on the train's baggage car (*toa hành lý*), Long Bien Station does not load motorcycles—you must bring the bike to **Gia Lam Station** (4 km east across the river) 60 minutes prior to departure and pay the 180,000 VND to 250,000 VND ($7.20 to $10.00) freight fee.

## 5B Expressway Coaches & 9-Seat Limousines

When speed and flexible departure times matter more than rail nostalgia, the CT04 Expressway buses and DCar limousines dominate. Road operators dispatch vehicles every 30 to 60 minutes from 05:00 AM until 09:00 PM.

**Hai Au Bus (Xe khách Hải Âu)** leads the Hanoi–Hai Phong highway market with its red-and-yellow fleet, offering two distinct service tiers:

- **Standard 5B Expressway Coach (Xe chạy cao tốc 5B):** 29-seat and 45-seat buses departing **Gia Lam Bus Station** (3.5 km from Hoan Kiem) and **Nuoc Ngam Bus Station** every 30 minutes. Board the **Chạy Cao Tốc 5B (Full Expressway)** bus rather than the slow **Quốc Lộ 5 (Old Highway 5)** bus. The 5B ticket costs **130,000 VND to 150,000 VND** ($5.20 to $6.00) and reaches **Thuong Ly Bus Station** (52 Hanoi Street) or **Niem Nghia Bus Station** (275 Tran Nguyen Han) in 75 to 90 minutes, including a free shuttle transfer (*xe trung chuyển*) within a 5 km central radius.
- **VIP 9-Seat & 11-Seat DCar Limousine:** Costs **220,000 VND to 250,000 VND** ($8.80 to $10.00) per seat. Vans depart hourly from 72 Tran Nhat Duat (edge of Hanoi's Old Quarter) and drop passengers directly at any central hotel in Hong Bang, Ngo Quyen, Le Chan, or Hai An districts in 90 to 105 minutes.

**Anh Huy Đất Cảng** and **Hoang Long Red Bus** provide strong competition from western and southern Hanoi. Their 45-seat expressway coaches depart **Yen Nghia** and **Giap Bat** stations for **120,000 VND to 140,000 VND** ($4.80 to $5.60) terminating at **Vinh Niem Bus Station**, while their 9-seat VIP limousines leave Cau Giay (30 Pham Hung) and Dong Da (91 Khuong Trung) for **230,000 VND to 250,000 VND** ($9.20 to $10.00).

## Connecting Through Hai Phong to Cat Ba Island & Food Tours

Hai Phong serves two major tourist itineraries: day-trippers arriving for the culinary circuit, and island-bound travelers transiting to Cat Ba.

**For the Hai Phong Street Food Tour:** Take the **HP1** (06:00 AM) or **LP2** (09:25 AM) train directly to **Hai Phong Railway Station** (75 Luong Khanh Thien). Step out of the French colonial terminal and walk within 600 meters to *Bánh Đa Cua Bà Cụ* (179 Cau Dat, 45,000 VND / $1.80 per bowl), *Nem Cua Bể Thuận Yến* (88 Tran Nhat Duat, 70,000 VND / $2.80), and *Dừa Dầm Hiền Béo* (124 Lach Tray, 30,000 VND / $1.20). Alternatively, rent a motorbike outside the station gates for **120,000 VND to 150,000 VND** ($4.80 to $6.00) per day before catching the **LP5** (03:00 PM), **LP7** (06:10 PM), or **HP2** (06:40 PM) train back to Hanoi.

**For Cat Ba Island Connections:** Skip the train into downtown Hai Phong if your final destination is Cat Ba Island. Instead, book an integrated **Hanoi–Cat Ba combo ticket** with **Good Morning Cat Ba**, **Cat Ba Express**, or **Daiichi Travel** for **320,000 VND to 380,000 VND** ($12.80 to $15.20). These buses stay on Expressway CT04 past downtown Hai Phong, cross the 5.44 km Tan Vu–Lach Huyen Sea Bridge onto Cat Hai Island, and transfer you across the channel via the **Dong Bai – Cai Vieng Ferry** or the **Sun World Cat Hai Cable Car** (100,000 VND / $4.00, 10 minutes), reaching Cat Ba Town in 3.5 hours total.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How far in advance should I book the Hoa Phượng Đỏ VIP train?
For weekday trips (Monday through Thursday), booking 24 to 48 hours ahead on `dsvn.vn` or via 12Go is sufficient for Economy (95,000 VND / $3.80) and First-Class (150,000 VND / $6.00) seats. For Saturday morning outbound runs (**HP1** at 06:00 AM and **LP2** at 09:15 AM) and Sunday afternoon return runs (**LP5** at 03:00 PM and **LP7** at 06:10 PM), the 34-seat VIP Indochine carriage sells out 5 to 7 days in advance.

### Can I bring a bicycle or large suitcase onto the Hanoi–Hai Phong train?
Yes. Every ticket includes 20 kg of free carry-on baggage, and 24-inch or 26-inch suitcases fit on the overhead racks or end-of-carriage bays. Full-sized bicycles or 30-inch trunks must be checked into the **Baggage Car (Toa hành lý)** at Hanoi Station or Gia Lam Station 45 minutes before departure for 60,000 VND to 100,000 VND ($2.40 to $4.00).

### What is the fastest way from Noi Bai International Airport (HAN) to Hai Phong?
No direct rail link connects Noi Bai Airport to Hai Phong. The fastest option is a pre-booked private 4-seat sedan via Eastern Ring Road 3 and the CT04 Expressway (125 km, 1 hour 40 minutes, 1,250,000 VND to 1,400,000 VND / $50.00 to $56.00 including the 210,000 VND toll). Solo budget travelers can book shared 9-seat airport limousines operated by **Anh Huy Đất Cảng** or **Hai Au** from Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 for 350,000 VND to 400,000 VND ($14.00 to $16.00) per seat (2 hours).

### Which Hai Phong bus station is closest to the city center and Cat Bi Airport (HPH)?
**Thuong Ly Bus Station** (52 Hanoi Street) sits 2.5 km west of the Opera House and serves Hong Bang District hotels best. **Niem Nghia Bus Station** (275 Tran Nguyen Han) and **Vinh Niem Bus Station** (Bui Vien Boulevard) sit 3.5 km to 4.5 km south of the center and are just 5 km (12 minutes by taxi, 75,000 VND / $3.00) from **Cat Bi International Airport (HPH)**.
