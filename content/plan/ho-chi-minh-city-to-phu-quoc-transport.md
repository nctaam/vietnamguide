# Ho Chi Minh City to Phu Quoc: Flights, Sleeper Buses, and Fast Ferries (2026 Guide)

Crossing from Ho Chi Minh City to Phu Quoc Island in Kien Giang Province covers a 300 km direct air corridor or a 380–420 km combined overland-and-sea route across the Mekong Delta and the Gulf of Thailand. Because Phu Quoc sits 45 km off the southwestern coast of mainland Vietnam, every journey requires either a domestic flight or a coastal highway transfer paired with a high-speed passenger ferry.

Direct flights from Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN) to Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC) take 55–60 minutes in the air and cost 1,050,000–2,350,000 VND ($42–$94 USD) one-way. Budget travelers, motorbike riders, and passengers carrying oversized diving gear can instead take an overnight sleeper bus from Ho Chi Minh City's Western Bus Station (Ben Xe Mien Tay) to the coastal ports of Rach Gia (270 km) or Ha Tien (350 km), connecting to a morning catamaran or roll-on/roll-off car ferry for a total one-way outlay of 415,000–570,000 VND ($16.60–$22.80 USD).

> [!TIP]
> **Overnight Bus + Morning Boat Timing:** Book the 23:00 Phuong Trang (Futa) sleeper bus from Ben Xe Mien Tay to Rach Gia (190,000 VND). You arrive at 05:15, take the free Futa shuttle van straight to Rach Gia Passenger Port, and board the 07:00 Phu Quoc Express or Superdong catamaran—reaching Phu Quoc Island by 09:30 without paying for a mainland hotel night.

## Option 1: Direct Flights from Tan Son Nhat (SGN) to Phu Quoc (PQC)

Flying is the fastest, most reliable link between southern Vietnam's commercial hub and Phu Quoc Island. Three domestic carriers—Vietnam Airlines, VietJet Air, and Bamboo Airways—operate 12 to 18 daily departures across the 300 km route. Scheduled block time is 55 to 60 minutes, with actual airborne time averaging 42 to 45 minutes aboard Airbus A320 and A321 jets.

### Terminal Assignments and Ground Access at SGN

Domestic flights at Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN), located 8 km north of District 1, operate across Domestic Terminal T1 and Domestic Terminal T3. Check your e-ticket carrier code carefully. Vietnam Airlines and Bamboo Airways consolidate domestic trunk operations through Terminal T3, whereas VietJet Air utilizes Terminal T1.

From central District 1 (Ben Thanh Market or Pham Ngu Lao Street), a metered Vinasun or Mai Linh 4-seat taxi to SGN costs 140,000–190,000 VND ($5.60–$7.60 USD) and takes 25 to 45 minutes. Solo travelers can ride Airport Bus #109 from Saigon Bus Station (23/9 Park) to the domestic terminals for 15,000 VND ($0.60 USD) between 05:45 and 23:30. Arrive at least 90 minutes prior to takeoff; domestic check-in counters close strictly 40 minutes before departure.

### Airfares and Baggage Allowances

* **VietJet Air (Eco vs. Deluxe):** Base Eco tickets start at 1,050,000–1,450,000 VND ($42–$58 USD) one-way, including 7 kg of cabin baggage (56 x 36 x 23 cm) and zero checked bags. Adding a 20 kg checked bag online costs 210,000–260,000 VND ($8.40–$10.40 USD), compared to 500,000 VND ($20 USD) at the airport gate.
* **Bamboo Airways:** Economy Saver fares range from 1,250,000 to 1,750,000 VND ($50–$70 USD) with 7 kg carry-on; Economy Smart tiers add a 20 kg hold bag for 1,450,000–1,900,000 VND ($58–$76 USD).
* **Vietnam Airlines:** Full-service Economy Classic tickets cost 1,550,000–2,350,000 VND ($62–$94 USD) one-way and bundle 10 kg of cabin luggage plus one 23 kg checked bag.

### Arrival at Phu Quoc Airport (PQC) and Island Transfers

Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC) sits in Duong To Commune, 10 km south of Duong Dong town center. Upon exiting the arrivals hall, travelers have two clean ground options:

1. **Free VinBus Electric Shuttle (#17 and #19):** Green VinBus electric buses depart the PQC terminal curb every 20 to 30 minutes from 06:00 to 22:40. Route #17 runs north along Tran Hung Dao Street through Duong Dong, Ong Lang (18 km), and Cua Can straight to Grand World (60 minutes). Fare is 0 VND.
2. **Metered Taxis and Grab:** Xanh SM, Mai Linh, and Grab operate outside Gate A2. Expect to pay 150,000–220,000 VND ($6.00–$8.80 USD) for the 15-minute (10 km) ride into Duong Dong town, 250,000–320,000 VND ($10.00–$12.80 USD) to An Thoi (18 km south), or 420,000–520,000 VND ($16.80–$20.80 USD) to Ganh Dau (28 km north).

## Option 2: Sleeper Bus + Rach Gia Port Fast Ferry (390 km Total)

If flights are sold out during holiday peaks, the overnight sleeper bus to Rach Gia paired with a morning high-speed catamaran is the primary overland-and-sea alternative. This corridor covers 270 km by highway from Ho Chi Minh City to Rach Gia followed by a 120 km (65-nautical-mile) sea crossing to Phu Quoc's Bai Vong Port.

### Step 1: Overnight Bus from Ben Xe Mien Tay to Rach Gia

Interprovincial coaches depart from Western Bus Station (Ben Xe Mien Tay) at 395 Kinh Duong Vuong, An Lac Ward, Binh Tan District, roughly 11 km southwest of District 1 (160,000–210,000 VND by Grab car or 7,000 VND on public Bus #02 or #39).

Three major operators run 34-berth sleeper buses and 22-cabin coaches down the Trung Luong–My Thuan Expressway (CT01) and Lo Te–Rach Soi Expressway (CT02):

* **Phuong Trang (Futa Bus Lines):** Departs every 45 to 60 minutes (22:30–23:59). Standard 34-berth sleeper tickets cost 190,000–230,000 VND ($7.60–$9.20 USD). Target the 23:00 departure, which arrives at Rach Soi Bus Station at 05:00–05:30. Futa provides a **free 16-seat shuttle van** (xe trung chuyen) directly to Rach Gia Passenger Port by 06:00.
* **Kumho Samco & Tuan Nga:** Operate 34-berth sleepers (200,000–230,000 VND) and 22-berth private cabins (280,000–320,000 VND) with complimentary port shuttle vans.

### Step 2: Fast Ferry from Rach Gia Port to Bai Vong Port

Rach Gia Passenger Port (1 Nguyen Cong Tru, Vinh Thanh Van Ward) dispatches high-speed catamarans across the Gulf of Thailand to Bai Vong Port in 2.5 hours.

* **Phu Quoc Express:** Sails at 07:00, 08:45, 10:30, and 13:10. Economy tickets cost 340,000 VND ($13.60 USD) for adults, 270,000 VND ($10.80 USD) for seniors/children, and 540,000 VND ($21.60 USD) for VIP recliners.
* **Superdong Speed Ferry:** Sails at 07:00, 08:10, 10:40, and 13:10. Standard one-way fare is 330,000 VND ($13.20 USD) for adults and 240,000 VND ($9.60 USD) for children.

Consult our [Phu Quoc Ferry Guide](/plan/phu-quoc-ferry-guide/) for vessel details, or review our [Can Tho to Phu Quoc Transport](/plan/can-tho-to-phu-quoc-transport/) guide if stopping in the Mekong Delta.

## Option 3: Sleeper Bus + Ha Tien Port Ferry (395 km Total)

Routing via Ha Tien (350 km southwest of Ho Chi Minh City) extends the highway bus ride to 7 to 7.5 hours (230,000–280,000 VND / $9.20–$11.20 USD with Futa Bus Lines, Kumho Samco, or Tuan Nga departing Ben Xe Mien Tay between 22:30 and 23:30). However, because Ha Tien sits directly opposite northern Phu Quoc, the open-sea crossing shrinks to just 45 km.

1. **75-Minute High-Speed Ferry:** Superdong and Phu Quoc Express catamarans from Ha Tien Pier (Kim Du Street, 2 km from Ha Tien Bus Station, 40,000 VND taxi) reach Bai Vong Port in 75 minutes. Tickets cost 216,000–250,000 VND ($8.65–$10.00 USD) Economy and 350,000 VND VIP.
2. **Thanh Thoi Car & Motorbike Ferry:** Motorbike riders head to Thanh Thoi Ferry Terminal in Ha Tien. Vessels take 2.5 hours and charge 185,000 VND ($7.40 USD) per passenger plus 80,000 VND ($3.20 USD) for a scooter. Bring your original vehicle registration card (Cavet xe). See our [Ha Tien to Phu Quoc Ferry](/plan/ha-tien-to-phu-quoc-ferry/) guide for loading rules.

## Arrival at Bai Vong Port and Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

All ferries from Rach Gia and Ha Tien dock at **Bai Vong Port** in Ham Ninh Commune, 15 km east of Duong Dong town. Inside the port gate, Superdong and Phu Quoc Express shuttle minibuses transfer passengers to Duong Dong for 50,000–70,000 VND ($2.00–$2.80 USD, 25 minutes), while metered Xanh SM and Mai Linh taxis cost 200,000–250,000 VND ($8.00–$10.00 USD) to Duong Dong or 320,000–380,000 VND to An Thoi (24 km).

### Can foreigners use the Phu Quoc 30-day visa exemption when taking the bus and ferry from Ho Chi Minh City?
No. Phu Quoc's 30-day visa-free exemption applies strictly to international passengers arriving directly into Phu Quoc from abroad (or transiting airside at SGN without clearing mainland immigration). Entering mainland Ho Chi Minh City to board a domestic flight or sleeper bus requires a regular Vietnamese e-visa or bilateral visa exemption.

### Is there a direct ferry from Ho Chi Minh City or Vung Tau to Phu Quoc?
No. No commercial passenger ferry connects Ho Chi Minh City or Vung Tau directly to Phu Quoc Island because rounding the Ca Mau Peninsula spans more than 450 km of exposed ocean. Every sea itinerary from Ho Chi Minh City requires an overland bus or private car transfer across the Mekong Delta to Rach Gia Port (270 km) or Ha Tien Port (350 km) before boarding a Gulf of Thailand vessel.

### What happens if afternoon monsoon winds cancel the Rach Gia or Ha Tien ferries?
During the southwest monsoon season (June through October), Beaufort Force 6–7 gusts (40–55 km/h) occasionally trigger Kien Giang Maritime Port Authority suspension orders (cam tau). When sailings are halted, Superdong and Phu Quoc Express issue 100% ticket refunds or free rebooking onto the first cleared morning boat, so avoid scheduling tight same-day international flight connections out of Tan Son Nhat Airport.
