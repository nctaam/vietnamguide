# Hue to Hoi An: Train, Car, Bus & Hai Van Pass

Connecting the former imperial capital of **Hue (Huế)** with the lantern-lit merchant port of **Hoi An (Hội An)** spans **125 to 145 kilometers** depending on whether your vehicle bores straight through the 6.28-kilometer **Hai Van Road Tunnel (Hầm Hải Vân)** or climbs 500 vertical meters over the winding coastal switchbacks of the **Hai Van Pass (Đèo Hải Vân — the "Ocean Cloud Pass")**.

Because **Hoi An has no railway station and no airport**, every rail passenger traveling south from Hue must first ride the cliffside coastal tracks into **Da Nang Railway Station (Ga Đà Nẵng)** and complete the final 30-kilometer (45-minute) road leg to Hoi An by private car or Grab. Conversely, booking a **door-to-door sightseeing private car**, an **Easy Rider motorbike tour**, or a **direct VIP limousine van** takes you straight from your Hue hotel lobby to your Hoi An resort. Here is the complete 2026 comparison of all five transport modes, with exact VND fares, luggage rules, and Hai Van Pass stopover logistics.

## Option 1: Private Sightseeing Car Over the Hai Van Pass (1,350,000–1,650,000 VND) — The Smartest Choice for 2+ Travelers

For two or more people traveling together, chartering a private 4-seater sedan (1,350,000–1,650,000 VND / $54–$66 USD total per vehicle) or 7-seater SUV (1,550,000–1,900,000 VND / $62–$76 USD) through local operators (Hue Private Cars, VM Car, Hoi An Express) turns a routine transfer day into a 5-hour guided coastal road trip while keeping your suitcases locked in the trunk.

### The 4 Classic Sightseeing Stopovers Included on the Route:
1. **Lap An Lagoon (Đầm Lập An — 62 km south of Hue)**: A brackish 800-hectare oyster lagoon framed by the Bach Ma mountain range. Stop for 30 minutes at a stilted waterside café to drink **Hue salted coffee (cà phê muối, 25,000–35,000 VND)** and walk out onto the exposed white tidal sandbar at low tide.
2. **Lang Co Bay Viewpoint (Vịnh Lăng Cô — 68 km south of Hue)**: At the northern foot of the Hai Van Pass ascent, pull over at the curved highway overlook to photograph the 10-kilometer white-sand spit of Lang Co separating the turquoise lagoon from the East Sea.
3. **Hai Van Pass Summit (Đỉnh Đèo Hải Vân — 496m Elevation)**: Instead of taking the dark 6.28 km highway tunnel (which saves 25 minutes but skips all views), your driver climbs the 21-kilometer ribbon of mountain switchbacks to the **1826 Nguyen Dynasty brick gate (Hải Vân Quan, restored and free/nominal admission)** and French-American concrete bunkers straddling the Thua Thien Hue – Da Nang provincial border.
4. **Marble Mountains (Ngũ Hành Sơn — 18 km north of Hoi An)**: Descending through Da Nang along My Khe Beach, pause for 60 to 90 minutes to ride the glass elevator (15,000 VND) and explore the limestone cave pagodas of **Water Mountain (Thủy Sơn, 40,000 VND entry)** and **Am Phu Cave (20,000 VND)** before rolling into Hoi An Ancient Town.

> [!TIP]
> **Specify "Over the Pass, Not the Tunnel" When Booking**: If you simply book a generic point-to-point taxi or Grab Intercity from Hue to Hoi An (~1,200,000 VND), the driver will automatically take the **Hai Van Road Tunnel (Hầm Hải Vân)** to save fuel and time (2.5 hours total). Always confirm in writing that your quote includes **driving over the Hai Van Pass (đi đường đèo Hải Vân)** plus waiting time at Lap An Lagoon and Marble Mountains.

## Option 2: The Scenic Coastal Train (Ga Huế to Ga Đà Nẵng) + 45-Minute Transfer to Hoi An

The 103-kilometer rail section between **Hue Station (02 Bùi Thị Xuân)** and **Da Nang Station (791 Hải Phòng)** is the single most scenic railway stretch in Vietnam. For nearly 75 minutes between Lang Co Station and Kim Lien (Da Nang), the meter-gauge train creeps along a narrow stone ledge carved 100 to 200 meters directly above crashing ocean waves and hidden jungle coves—a coastal cliff face completely inaccessible to cars and motorbikes higher up on the road pass.

### Choosing Between the HD1/HD3 Heritage Train vs Standard SE Trains (2026 Timetables):
- **The "Connecting Central Heritage" Tourist Train (HD1 & HD3)**:
  - **Schedule**: **HD1** departs Hue at **07:45 AM** (arrives Da Nang at **10:35 AM**); **HD3** departs Hue at **14:25 PM** (arrives Da Nang at **17:40 PM**).
  - **2026 Fare**: **180,000 to 210,000 VND (~$7.20–$8.40 USD)**.
  - **Why Book It**: Includes a **10-minute photo pause at Lang Co Station**, a cultural community carriage featuring live **Ca Huế traditional chamber music** and central Vietnamese snacks (bánh ít, bánh lọc), and large panoramic windows.
- **Standard Reunification Express Trains (SE1, SE3, SE5, SE7)**:
  - **Schedule**: Morning and midday departures (08:30, 09:15, 10:40, 13:50), taking **2.5 to 3.0 hours**.
  - **2026 Fare**: **110,000 to 165,000 VND (~$4.40–$6.60 USD)** for an air-conditioned **Soft Seat (Ngồi mềm điều hòa)**.
- **Crucial Window Seat Rule**: When heading **south from Hue to Da Nang**, the South China Sea and Lang Co Bay sit on the **LEFT (East) side of the train**. When heading **north from Da Nang to Hue**, sit on the **RIGHT side**.
- **Completing the Final 30 km from Da Nang Station to Hoi An**: Walk out the front gate of Da Nang Railway Station (791 Hải Phòng) and book a **pre-arranged private 4-seater car (260,000–320,000 VND / $10.40–$12.80 USD)** or hail a **GrabCar / Xanh SM electric taxi (330,000–400,000 VND)** for the 45-minute coastal drive into Hoi An.

## Option 3: Easy Rider Motorbike Tour vs One-Way Scooter Rental (With Luggage Transfer)

Riding over the Hai Van Pass in the open mountain breeze is a highlight of central Vietnam, and one-way rental companies (Motorvina, Style Motorbikes, Le Family Riders) make luggage logistics effortless:
- **How One-Way Luggage Forwarding Works**: You drop your heavy 24-to-28-inch suitcases at the rental office in Hue at 08:00 AM. While you ride the coastal backroads, the company transports your suitcases by van and delivers them directly to your hotel reception in Hoi An by 16:00 PM (included free in the rental/tour price).
- **Option 3A — Ride Pillion Behind a Licensed Easy Rider (1,250,000–1,600,000 VND / $50–$64 USD per person)**: No motorcycle license required. You sit comfortably on the back of a touring motorcycle or maxi-scooter while an experienced local guide navigates the pass and stops at the **City of Ghosts (An Bằng Cemetery — ornate multi-story ancestral tombs)**, **Elephant Springs (Suối Voi natural rock pools, 20,000 VND entry)**, **Lap An Lagoon**, **Hai Van Pass**, and **Marble Mountains**.
- **Option 3B — Self-Drive One-Way Scooter Rental (380,000–550,000 VND / $15–$22 USD per bike)**: Available with Honda Air Blade (125cc), Honda Vision, or manual Honda XR150 (650,000 VND). Requires a valid home-country motorcycle license paired with a **1968 Vienna Convention International Driving Permit (IDP)**.

> [!WARNING]
> **Hai Van Pass Safety & Fog Advisory**: Although heavy cargo trucks and sleeper buses divert through the Hai Van Tunnel below, fuel tankers carrying hazardous materials (which are barred from the tunnel) still drive over the open Hai Van Pass! Keep strictly to the right-hand lane on blind uphill hairpins, test both front and rear disc brakes before descending the steep Da Nang side (10% gradient), and check the sky before departing—between **October and January**, afternoon sea clouds can shroud the 496m pass summit in dense fog.

## Option 4 & 5: Direct VIP Limousine Vans (220k–320k VND) & Tourist Buses (150k–220k VND)

If you are traveling solo on a budget or visiting during heavy October–November autumn rains when the mountain pass is socked in by clouds:
- **9-Seat VIP Limousine Vans (Havaz Limousine, Hạnh Café, Cửa Ông)**: Depart Hue hourly from 07:00 to 18:00 (220,000–320,000 VND per leather recliner seat), driving directly through the Hai Van Tunnel to drop you in Hoi An in **3.0 to 3.5 hours**.
- **Tourist Sleeper / Seated Buses (Hưng Thành, Camel Travel, Queen Cafe, The Sinh Tourist)**: Morning (08:00) and afternoon (13:30) departures cost **150,000–220,000 VND**, arriving at their Hoi An offices (Cửa Đại or Hai Bà Trưng) in 3.5 to 4.0 hours.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is there a direct train from Hue to Hoi An?
No. **Hoi An does not have a railway station.** To travel by train, book the scenic **HD1/HD3 Heritage Train** (180k–210k VND) or a standard **SE train** (110k–165k VND) from **Hue Station (Ga Huế)** to **Da Nang Station (Ga Đà Nẵng)** (2.5 to 3 hours), and then take a 45-minute private car (280k–320k VND) or GrabCar (340k–400k VND) for the remaining 30 km south to Hoi An.

### Which side of the train has the ocean view from Hue to Da Nang?
When traveling **southbound from Hue to Da Nang**, book seats on the **LEFT (East) side of the train carriage** so your window faces **Lang Co Bay** and the open cliffs of the East Sea below the Hai Van Pass.

### Do buses from Hue to Hoi An drive over the Hai Van Pass?
No. Commercial tourist buses and shared 9-seat limousine vans drive **through the 6.28 km Hai Van Road Tunnel (*Hầm Hải Vân*)** inside the mountain rather than climbing the scenic pass road. To cross over the top of the **Hai Van Pass**, you must take a **private sightseeing car**, ride an **Easy Rider / rented motorbike**, or take the **coastal train** along the lower sea cliffs.

### How much extra does it cost to add An Bang Cemetery (City of Ghosts) or My Son Sanctuary to a Hue-to-Hoi An private car?
Adding the ornate multi-story ancestral tombs of **An Bang Cemetery (*Nghĩa trang An Bằng*, the "City of Ghosts," located 35 km southeast of Hue near Phu Vang coastal lagoon)** adds roughly 45 minutes of detour driving and costs **200,000 to 250,000 VND ($8.00 to $10.00 USD)** above the standard four-stop Hai Van Pass private car rate. Adding **My Son Sanctuary** on the same day as the Hue-to-Hoi An transfer is feasible only if you depart Hue by **07:30 AM**, costs an additional **450,000 to 550,000 VND ($18 to $22 USD)** in vehicle mileage, and stretches total journey duration to **8.0 to 8.5 hours**.

### Can I travel from Hoi An to Hue in the reverse direction using the same options?
Yes. Every transport option operates identically northbound from **Hoi An to Hue**: private sightseeing cars pick you up at your Hoi An hotel at 08:00 or 08:30 AM and climb the Hai Van Pass from south to north (1,350,000–1,650,000 VND for a 4-seater), while the northbound **Heritage Train HD2 (07:50 AM from Da Nang Station) and HD4 (15:00 PM from Da Nang Station)** run along the exact same ocean cliffs (remember to book seats on the **RIGHT / East side** of the carriage when traveling northbound from Da Nang to Hue).


### What is the best time of day to cross the Hai Van Pass by car or motorbike?
Depart Hue between **08:00 and 09:00 AM** so your vehicle reaches Lap An Lagoon around 09:30 AM and crests the 496-meter Hai Van Pass summit between **10:30 and 11:30 AM**. Morning hours offer the clearest ocean visibility across Lang Co Bay and Da Nang Bay before afternoon convective clouds build against the coastal mountains after 14:30 PM.
