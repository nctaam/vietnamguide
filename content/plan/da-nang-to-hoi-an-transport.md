# Da Nang to Hoi An Transport: Grab, Private Car, Shuttle Bus, and Motorbike

Connecting the modern coastal hub of Da Nang with the UNESCO-listed Ancient Town of Hoi An is one of the most traveled transit corridors in central Vietnam. Spanning approximately 30 kilometers (19 miles) south along a flat, multi-lane coastal boulevard, the journey takes between 40 and 50 minutes under normal traffic conditions.

Whether arriving at Da Nang International Airport (DAD), departing from the central Da Nang Railway Station, or vacationing along My Khe Beach, international travelers have access to five distinct transit methods: in-app ride-hailing (Grab), metered taxis, private cars with optional sightseeing stopovers at the Marble Mountains, shared airport minivans, and coastal motorbike rentals.

This guide provides practical data for traveling from Da Nang to Hoi An: cost comparisons across vehicle classes, airport pickup navigation, luggage guidelines, and routing advice for self-drive scooters.

The coastal highway route (Võ Nguyên Giáp transitioning into Trường Sa and Lạc Long Quân) is the preferred corridor for all passenger vehicles. It bypasses commercial truck congestion on inland Route 607 and provides ocean vistas alongside luxury beachfront resorts.

## Detailed Evaluation of Primary Transport Options

### 1. GrabCar Ride-Hailing: The Default Choice for Most Travelers
Grab operates with high driver density across metropolitan Da Nang and at the airport.
- **How It Works**: Connect to the free terminal Wi-Fi at Da Nang International Airport, input your destination hotel in Hoi An, and select either a 4-seater economy sedan (Toyota Vios, Hyundai Accent) or a 7-seater vehicle (Mitsubishi Xpander, Toyota Innova).
- **Airport Pickup Points**: At Terminal 1 (Domestic), exit through the sliding glass doors, cross the initial pedestrian crossing, and meet your driver in the ride-hailing pickup lanes. At Terminal 2 (International), drivers pull up directly along the outer curbside bay.
- **Toll Fees**: The standard Da Nang Airport exit toll of 10,000–15,000 VND ($0.40–$0.60 USD) is typically added automatically to your in-app digital receipt.

### 2. Private Car Hire with a Marble Mountains Stopover
If you have half a day between landing in Da Nang and checking into your Hoi An accommodation, combining transit with a sightseeing excursion at the Marble Mountains (*Ngũ Hành Sơn*) is exceptionally cost-effective.
- **The Experience**: Located 10 km south of Da Nang directly along the coastal highway, the Marble Mountains consist of five limestone and marble outcrops named after the five elements (Water, Metal, Wood, Fire, Earth).
- **Logistics**: Pre-arrange a private car through your Hoi An hotel or a local travel agency. The driver collects you from Da Nang, stores your backpacks and suitcases securely inside the vehicle trunk, and waits at the foot of Thủy Sơn (Water Mountain) for 1.5 to 2.0 hours.
- **Sightseeing Tariffs**: Admission to Water Mountain costs 40,000 VND per person ($1.60 USD). An optional glass elevator costs 15,000 VND each way, and entry to the adjacent Âm Phủ Cave (*Hell Cave*) costs 20,000 VND.
- **Total Transfer Cost**: 400,000 to 550,000 VND ($16.00–$22.00 USD) total for the vehicle, representing exceptional value for pairs and small groups.

### 3. Shared Airport Minivans: The Solo Traveler Route
Several reputable operators (such as Hoi An Express and Barri Ann Travel) run shared 16-seat Ford Transit vans between Da Nang Airport and central Hoi An.
- **Fare Structure**: Tickets cost between 120,000 and 150,000 VND per passenger ($4.80–$6.00 USD).
- **Trade-Off**: Because the van collects passengers arriving from multiple flights and drops guests off at individual hotels in Hoi An, the transit duration can stretch to 70–75 minutes. Solo travelers save roughly 50% compared to a private GrabCar.

### 4. Coastal Motorbike Ride: The Independent Scenic Route
Renting a 110cc–125cc scooter (such as a Honda Vision or Air Blade) in Da Nang and riding south to Hoi An is straightforward:
- **Route Navigation**: Follow the broad oceanfront avenue of Võ Nguyên Giáp southward past Mỹ Khê Beach and Non Nước Beach. The road is flat, paved, and well-lit, featuring dedicated outer lanes for two-wheeled vehicles.
- **Luggage Constraints**: Riding a motorbike is suitable only if you travel with a single daypack or small backpack that fits between your knees or on your shoulders. Never hang heavy suitcases from scooter handlebars.
- **Parking in Hoi An**: Motorbikes are strictly prohibited from driving inside the pedestrianized pedestrian zone of Hoi An Ancient Town during designated walking hours (09:00–11:00 AM and 15:00–21:30 PM). Park your scooter at licensed municipal parking lots along the perimeter for 10,000 VND per entry.

## Return Journey Logistics: Hoi An to Da Nang International Airport

Returning to Da Nang Airport from your Hoi An accommodation requires careful scheduling based on flight departure requirements:

```
[Hoi An Hotel Lobby Pickup]
        |  (~40-45 mins via Lac Long Quan & Truong Sa Highway)
        v
[Da Nang River Bridge Crossing]
        |  (Nguyen Van Troi, Tran Thi Ly, or Dragon Bridge)
        v
[Da Nang International Airport (DAD)]
        |  (Terminal 1 for Domestic / Terminal 2 for International)
```

- **Domestic Flight Departure Buffer**: Domestic departures (Vietnam Airlines, Vietjet Air, Bamboo Airways to Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City) mandate checked baggage counters closing 40 minutes before flight departure. Schedule your vehicle pickup from Hoi An at least **2.5 hours prior to your scheduled takeoff time** (45 minutes transit + 105 minutes airport buffer).
- **International Flight Departure Buffer**: International departures (direct services to Singapore, Seoul, Taipei, Tokyo, Bangkok) enforce counter closures 50 minutes prior to departure. Reserve your transfer at least **3.5 hours prior to scheduled departure** to navigate immigration and customs security queues comfortably.
- **Urban Bottlenecks in Da Nang**: During weekday evening rush hours (17:00 PM – 18:30 PM), traffic crossing the Dragon Bridge (*Cầu Rồng*) or the Tran Thi Ly Bridge (*Cầu Trần Thị Lý*) slows down noticeably. Add an extra 15 to 20 minutes of travel time if your transfer coincides with this afternoon commuting window.

## Scenic En-Route Stopovers: Beyond the Marble Mountains

Travelers driving between Da Nang and Hoi An can incorporate multiple brief cultural stops along the 30-kilometer coastal corridor:

1. **Non Nuoc Stone Carving Craft Village**: Situated at the southern foot of the Marble Mountains along Huyen Tran Cong Chua street, this centuries-old craft settlement features local stone carvers crafting marble statues, garden ornaments, and jewelry. While commercial souvenir stores flank the main road, wandering back into family courtyards reveals master artisans working raw stone with pneumatic chisels.
2. **An Bang Beach Coastal Detour**: Instead of heading straight into Hoi An Ancient Town, take a 2-kilometer coastal detour eastward onto An Bang Beach (*Bãi biển An Bàng*). This palm-fringed shoreline offers laid-back beachfront cafes (such as The Deckhouse and Soul Kitchen), where you can enjoy fresh young coconut water (30,000–45,000 VND) or a seafood lunch while listening to the surf before checking into your town hotel.

## Luggage Capacity Guidelines for Families and Groups

When booking a transfer from Da Nang Airport to Hoi An, matching vehicle volume to passenger baggage prevents curb-side booking disputes:

- **Standard 4-Seater Sedan**: Accommodates maximum 2 passengers with 2 large checked suitcases (23 kg each) plus 2 small carry-on daypacks.
- **7-Seater MPV / SUV (Xpander, Innova)**: Accommodates 3 to 4 passengers with 3 to 4 large suitcases. The third-row passenger seats fold flat to expand luggage trunk volume.
- **16-Seater Van (Ford Transit / Hyundai Solati)**: Essential for travel parties of 5 or more individuals, golf groups carrying full golf bags, or travelers transporting oversize sports gear (surfboards, folding bicycles). Private 16-seat transfers cost 750,000–950,000 VND ($30.00–$38.00 USD).

> [!TIP]
> **Avoiding Airport Taxi Solicitations**: When walking out of the terminal arrival hall at Da Nang Airport, unauthorized touts frequently approach international tourists whispering "Grab, sir?" while waving personal smartphones. Never follow these drivers to unmarked vehicles. Authentic Grab drivers are summoned exclusively through your verified mobile application, where vehicle license plates and pre-fixed tariffs are clearly displayed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I hail a Grab from Hoi An back to Da Nang Airport?
Yes. Grab drivers operate extensively throughout Hoi An and the surrounding beach districts (An Bang and Cua Dai). You can hail a ride directly from your hotel lobby back to Da Nang International Airport at any hour. For early morning flights (departing before 07:00 AM), pre-booking a private transfer through your hotel reception 24 hours in advance guarantees prompt vehicle arrival.

### Is the public bus between Da Nang and Hoi An currently operating?
Public Bus Line 01 runs between Da Nang Central Bus Station and Hoi An Bus Station on Nguyen Tat Thanh street from 05:30 AM to 17:30 PM, departing every 20 to 30 minutes for 30,000–35,000 VND. However, it does not enter Da Nang Airport directly and stops roughly 1.5 km outside Hoi An's pedestrian center, making Grab or private cars far more practical for luggage-carrying travelers.

### What should I do if my flight arrives late at night?
GrabCar drivers and licensed airport metered taxis (Mai Linh, Vinasun) operate around the clock at Da Nang International Airport. Even for midnight arrivals, vehicles are readily available outside the arrivals hall with fixed tariffs to Hoi An.
