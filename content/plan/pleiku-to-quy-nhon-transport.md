# Pleiku to Quy Nhon Transport (2026): Bus vs Private Car

The overland corridor from the volcanic red-basalt plateau of Pleiku in Gia Lai Province (`750 to 800 meters` above sea level) eastward to the coast of Quy Nhon in Binh Dinh Province covers **165 km** along **National Highway QL19**. As the primary East-West economic artery linking the Central Highlands (*Tay Nguyen*) and the Cambodia/Laos border gates to Quy Nhon Seaport, this highway drops through two major mountain saddles—**Mang Yang Pass** (*Deo Mang Yang*) and the **8 km An Khe Pass** (*Deo An Khe*)—before flattening out across the Kon River plain of Tay Son District.

Thanks to the World Bank-funded widening and resurfacing of Highway QL19 completed in late 2024 and 2025, point-to-point transit times between Pleiku and Quy Nhon now average **3.5 to 4 hours** by road. Travelers can book a shared **9-seat or 16-seat limousine van** (`160,000 to 220,000 VND` / `$6.40 to $8.80 USD`) with hotel-to-hotel pickup, charter a **private 4-seater or 7-seater car** (`1,600,000 to 2,100,000 VND` / `$64.00 to $84.00 USD`) that turns the descent into a cultural sightseeing tour of Quang Trung Museum and the Banh It Cham Towers, or ride a motorbike across the passes with careful timing around container-truck convoys.

Below is the verified 2026 operator timetable, fare table, pass-by-pass route breakdown, and safety protocol for traveling from Pleiku to Quy Nhon.

## Option 1: Limousine Vans & Express Buses (Pleiku to Quy Nhon)

Because no railway line connects the Central Highlands to the South-Central Coast, road vans and buses handle 90% of passenger traffic between Gia Lai and Binh Dinh. Most intercity coaches depart from **Duc Long Gia Lai Bus Station** (*Ben Xe Duc Long Gia Lai*, `43 Ly Nam De Street`, Yen The Ward, about 4.5 km east of central Pleiku along the QL19 exit corridor) and terminate at **Quy Nhon Central Bus Station** (*Ben Xe Trung Tam Quy Nhon*, `71 Tay Son Street`).

However, the top VIP limousine operators send shuttle vans directly to hotels listed in our [Where to Stay in Pleiku](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-pleiku/) guide and drop passengers off at beachfront hotels in our [Where to Stay in Quy Nhon](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-quy-nhon/) directory.

### Top 2026 Bus & Limousine Operators on Highway QL19

1. **Tuan Anh Limousine Gia Lai & Anh Phat Limousine (9-Seat & 11-Seat VIP Vans):**
   * **Departure Schedule:** Every 60 minutes from **05:30 to 18:30** daily.
   * **2026 Ticket Fare:** **180,000 to 220,000 VND ($7.20 to $8.80 USD)** per seat (middle reclining massage seats cost 200,000 to 220,000 VND; front cab seats next to the driver cost 170,000 to 180,000 VND).
   * **Service Notes:** Includes free pickup within 5 km of Pleiku's Dai Doan Ket Square, complimentary bottled water, USB-A/USB-C charging ports at every seat, and a 15-minute restroom break at the foot of An Khe Pass in Tay Son.
2. **Mai Linh Willer & Quy Nhon – Pleiku Express (16-Seat Ford Transit & Hyundai Solati):**
   * **Departure Schedule:** **06:00, 07:30, 09:00, 11:00, 13:30, 15:00, and 17:00** from `43 Ly Nam De` (with central pickup points on Hung Vuong Street).
   * **2026 Ticket Fare:** **160,000 to 180,000 VND ($6.40 to $7.20 USD)**.
   * **Service Notes:** Operated with Japanese safety-monitored speed limiters (`70 km/h` highway cap, `40 km/h` on An Khe Pass hairpins). Ideal for travelers prone to motion sickness on winding descents.
3. **Nha Xe Hoang Thuy & Truong Thinh (Limousine & 34-Berth Sleeper Coaches):**
   * **Departure Schedule:** Morning runs at **07:00, 08:30, 10:00** and afternoon runs at **13:00, 15:30, 18:00**.
   * **2026 Ticket Fare:** **160,000 to 200,000 VND ($6.40 to $8.00 USD)**, including downtown shuttle drop-off near Quy Nhon Beach (`Nguyen Hue` and `An Duong Vuong` boulevards).

### Luggage Allowance & Rest Stop Logistics on Highway QL19

Standard 9-seat DCar and Skybus limousine vans built on Ford Transit or Hyundai Solati chassis have compact rear boots once the back three-seat bench is reclined. Each passenger is allocated one medium check-in suitcase (`20 to 24 inches`, up to `20 kg`) plus one soft daypack free of charge. If you are carrying oversized hard-shell trunks (`28 inches`), golf bags, or boxed bicycles from a Central Highlands cycling tour, inform the dispatcher when booking; operators either assign your bags to a 16-seat express coaster with a deeper rear cargo bay or charge **50,000 to 80,000 VND ($2.00 to $3.20 USD)** for an extra cargo slot.

All daytime vans make a single scheduled **15-minute rest and coffee stop** midway along the 165 km run—either at the **Song Ba rest station** just east of **An Khe Town** (`Km 85`) before starting the steep mountain descent, or at the **Phu Phong highway plaza** in **Tay Son District** (`Km 118`) immediately below the pass. Iced Gia Lai Robusta coffee (*ca phe sua da*) costs **20,000 to 25,000 VND ($0.80 to $1.00 USD)**, while steamed pork buns (*banh bao*) and local *banh it la gai* (sticky rice pyramids wrapped in ramie leaves, a Binh Dinh specialty) sell for **15,000 to 35,000 VND ($0.60 to $1.40 USD)** per box. Restrooms at both stops are clean and free.

## Option 2: Private Car Charter with Highland & Cham Sightseeing Stops

Hiring a private car with a local driver turns the 165 km transfer across QL19 into a full-day highland-to-sea excursion. A direct 3.5-hour transfer from Pleiku hotels (or Pleiku Airport `PXU`) to Quy Nhon costs **1,600,000 to 1,800,000 VND ($64.00 to $72.00 USD)** for a 4-seater sedan or **1,900,000 to 2,100,000 VND ($76.00 to $84.00 USD)** for a 7-seater SUV. Adding 3 hours of sightseeing stops along the corridor adds roughly **300,000 to 400,000 VND ($12.00 to $16.00 USD)** in waiting time and detour mileage. All rates include fuel, driver meals, and the two BOT highway toll plazas on QL19 (`35,000 to 50,000 VND` per pass).

### Recommended West-to-East Stop Sequence along QL19

* **Km 0 (Optional Morning Pre-Departure Loop) — Bien Ho T'Nung Crater Lake:** Located 7 km north of downtown Pleiku off Highway QL14, this 230-hectare flooded volcanic caldera sits framed by century-old pine avenues. Admission is free (`10,000 VND` / `$0.40 USD` car parking, `15,000 VND` electric buggy option).
* **Km 52 — Mang Yang Pass (*Deo Mang Yang*) & Kon Ka Kinh Southern Edge:** East of the coffee and black-pepper estates of Dak Doa District, QL19 drops through Mang Yang Pass (`500 meters` elevation), marking the climate boundary between the western plateau and the Ba River valley.
* **Km 88 to Km 96 — An Khe Pass (*Deo An Khe*):** Just east of historic An Khe Town, the highway plunges down an **8 km mountain pass** from 480 meters elevation to the coastal lowlands of Binh Dinh Province. The upgraded four-lane asphalt features wide concrete drainage channels and runaway truck ramps overlooking dense tropical forest canopy.
* **Km 120 — Quang Trung Museum (*Bao Tang Quang Trung*, Tay Son District):** Located 4 km north of QL19 in Phu Phong Town, this museum honors the three Tay Son brothers (led by Emperor Quang Trung / Nguyen Hue) who unified Vietnam and defeated the Qing army in 1789.
  * **2026 Ticket Tariff:** **30,000 VND ($1.20 USD)** per adult general entry. Booking a private 20-minute performance of **Tay Son Battle Drums (*Nhac Vo Tay Son*, utilizing a set of 12 war drums)** and traditional Binh Dinh martial arts costs **400,000 VND ($16.00 USD)** per group of up to 20 visitors (or join a shared domestic tour group on site for `20,000 to 30,000 VND` per person). Allow 60 minutes.
* **Km 148 — Banh It Cham Towers (*Thap Banh It*, Tuy Phuoc District):** Perched on a hilltop 18 km northwest of downtown Quy Nhon right beside the QL19–QL1A junction, this cluster of four 11th-century red-brick Champa sanctuary towers overlooks the Kon River delta.
  * **2026 Ticket Tariff:** **20,000 VND ($0.80 USD)** per adult + `5,000 VND` parking. Allow 40 minutes before completing the final 25-minute drive into Quy Nhon.

## Option 3: Riding a Motorbike Down QL19 (Safety & Truck Advisory)

Independent riders traveling from the Central Highlands down to the coast to connect with our [Quy Nhon to Phu Yen Coastal Drive](/plan/quy-nhon-to-phu-yen-coastal-drive/) route can complete the 165 km Pleiku–Quy Nhon ride in **4 to 4.5 hours** of saddle time. Petrolimex fuel stations operate every 15 to 20 km in Dak Doa, Mang Yang, An Khe, and Phu Phong; a full tank of RON 95 gasoline (`75,000 to 85,000 VND` / `$3.00 to $3.40 USD`) easily covers the entire crossing. While the asphalt across Dak Doa, Mang Yang, and An Khe passes is now smooth and pothole-free, two specific hazards require strict attention:

> [!WARNING]
> **Container Truck Convoys & Highland Fog on An Khe Pass:** Highway QL19 is the main freight corridor for 18-wheel tractor-trailers hauling raw cassava, timber, coffee, and granite blocks from Gia Lai and southern Laos down to Quy Nhon Port. On the 8 km descent of **An Khe Pass**, heavy trucks crawl in low gear at `25 to 30 km/h` while oncoming trucks swing wide across the centerline on tight right-hand hairpins. Never overtake a container truck on a blind curve, use engine braking (2nd or 3rd gear on a semi-automatic or manual bike) to prevent brake-fluid boiling, and cross An Khe Pass between **08:00 and 14:30**—after **15:30**, orographic cloud banks and sudden highland rain showers frequently drop visibility on the upper switchbacks below 25 meters.

## Step-by-Step Booking & Transit Checklist

1. **Reserve Front or Middle Limousine Seats for Pass Comfort:** If you are sensitive to winding mountain roads on **Mang Yang Pass** and **An Khe Pass**, call the operator hotline (`Tuan Anh` or `Anh Phat`) via Zalo or book on `vexere.com` 24 to 48 hours ahead to request **Seats 2, 3, 4, or 5** in the middle of the 9-seat cabin rather than the rear bench over the back axle.
2. **Coordinate Direct Pleiku Airport (`PXU`) Transfers:** Travelers landing at Pleiku Airport who need to reach Quy Nhon the same day because of full flights into Phu Cat Airport (`UIH`) can either take a 15-minute taxi (`90,000 to 110,000 VND` / `$3.60 to $4.40 USD`) to `43 Ly Nam De` for an hourly limousine van, or pre-book a private 4-seater car (`1,650,000 VND` / `$66.00 USD`) waiting directly outside the `PXU` arrival gate.
3. **Pack a Light Windbreaker for the Morning Departure:** Early morning departures from Pleiku (`06:00 to 07:30`) start in crisp highland air around **18°C to 21°C (64°F to 70°F)** before dropping into **30°C to 33°C (86°F to 91°F)** coastal sunshine in Quy Nhon by mid-morning.
4. **Plan Onward Southbound Coastal Connections:** Once you arrive at `71 Tay Son` in Quy Nhon, you can connect the same afternoon or the next morning to southbound buses or trains from Dieu Tri Station; see our [Quy Nhon to Nha Trang Transport](/plan/quy-nhon-to-nha-trang-transport/) guide for complete 2026 timetables.
