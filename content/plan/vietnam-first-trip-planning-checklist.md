# Vietnam First Trip Planning Checklist

**Quick Verdict**: Preparing for your first trip to Vietnam follows a four-stage countdown from **90 days before departure down to your first 60 minutes on the ground**. Start at **60 to 90 days out** by booking an **open-jaw multi-city flight** (*Arrive Hanoi `HAN` / Depart Ho Chi Minh City `SGN`* or reverse) to save **1 full day** and **$60 to $110 USD (`1,500,000 to 2,750,000 VND`)** across the country's **1,650 km** S-curve. At **30 to 45 days out**, submit your **$25 USD** single-entry or **$50 USD** multiple-entry **90-day e-Visa** exclusively on **`evisa.gov.vn`** and reserve capacity-capped bottlenecks like **Ha Long / Lan Ha Bay cruises**, **Oxalis Phong Nha cave treks**, and **Reunification Express 4-berth soft sleepers (`AnL`)**. Between **7 and 14 days out**, verify SMS OTPs on **Grab** while still on your home mobile network, pack **two fee-free debit cards** for **`0 VND`-fee VPBank and TPBank ATMs**, and check **1968 Vienna Convention IDP** rules. Finally, in the last **48 hours**, pack power banks (`<= 100Wh`) in carry-on bags only, leave vapes at home under **Resolution 173/2024/QH15**, and inspect your purple immigration entry stamp at the airport.

## Phase 1 (60 to 90 Days Out): Route Shape, Open-Jaw Flights & Weather Window

Geography dictates every smart Vietnamese itinerary. Stretching **1,650 km** from the Chinese frontier down to the Gulf of Thailand, Vietnam curves like a narrow coastal corridor where backtracking steals an entire vacation day.

Skip closed-loop round-trip tickets into a single gateway if you want to see both ends of the country. Booking an **open-jaw multi-city ticket** (`Arrive HAN / Depart SGN` or vice versa) saves **1 full day (`6 to 8 hours` of airport transit)** and **$60 to $110 USD (`1,500,000 to 2,750,000 VND`)** per person on a redundant domestic flight. Long-haul airlines price multi-city open-jaw tickets within **$15 to $40 USD** of standard round-trip fares when booked **60 to 90 days out**.

Match your route shape to Vietnam's three regional climate zones before paying for hotels:

* **The National Golden Window (`March to April`)**: Warm, dry sunshine spans all three regions simultaneously (`22°C to 31°C`, under `60 mm` monthly rain), supporting an unbroken top-to-bottom traverse.
* **Dry North & Dry South Window (`November to April`)**: Crisp skies rule Hanoi, Ninh Binh, and Ha Long Bay (`14°C to 24°C`), while Ho Chi Minh City, the Mekong Delta, and Phu Quoc enjoy dry tropical heat (`28°C to 34°C`).
* **Sunny Central Coast Window (`February to August`)**: While afternoon downpours hit the south from May onward, the central coastline across **Da Nang, Hoi An, Quy Nhon, and Nha Trang** delivers calm seas and `29°C to 34°C` beach weather.
* **The Central Typhoon Bypass (`October to November`)**: Peak autumn storms drop `400 to 800 mm` of rain on Hue and Hoi An. During October and November, avoid rigid central coast bookings or route **Hanoi -> Ninh Binh -> Ha Long -> Ho Chi Minh City -> Phu Quoc** via a **2-hour 15-minute** flight instead.

Enforce the **"Maximum 4 Bases in 10 Days / 5 Bases in 14 Days" Rule**. Every intercity transfer burns **3.5 to 6 hours**. Commit to a **minimum of 2 to 3 nights per base** so you explore local alleys instead of living inside transit vans.

## Phase 2 (30 to 45 Days Out): Official e-Visa (`evisa.gov.vn`), Passport Rules & High-Demand Bookings

Four to six weeks before departure is the hard deadline for border paperwork and capacity-capped transport. Inspect your physical passport first.

Your passport must hold **at least 6 months (`180 days`) of validity** beyond your arrival date and contain **2 blank visa pages**. Citizens of the **13 unilateral visa-exempt nations**—**UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland, Russia, Japan, South Korea, and Belarus**—receive a free **45-day visa-free entry stamp** (`$0 USD`) on arrival. Travelers flying directly into **Phu Quoc (`PQC`)** from abroad without entering mainland Vietnam also qualify for a **30-day island exemption**.

All other travelers—including **US, Canadian, Australian, and New Zealand** passport holders, or anyone staying up to **90 days**—must obtain an official e-Visa:

1. **Apply Only on the Official Portal**: Submit your application exclusively via the Ministry of Public Security portal at **`evisa.gov.vn`**. Third-party clone sites charge `$85 to $160 USD` for the same document.
2. **Official Fees & Turnaround**: The **single-entry e-Visa costs `$25 USD` (`625,000 VND`)** and the **multiple-entry e-Visa costs `$50 USD` (`1,250,000 VND`)**, valid up to **90 days**, with **3 to 5 business days (`72 to 120 hours`)** processing.
3. **Exact ICAO Line Order & Port Match**: Enter your full name (including middle names) in the exact sequence shown on your passport's bottom **ICAO machine-readable zone (MRZ)** line. Upload a clean portrait JPEG plus a full bio-page scan, select your exact entry checkpoint (`HAN`, `SGN`, `DAD`, `PQC`, or land border), and print **two physical copies** of the issued PDF.

Next, book the four high-demand bottlenecks that sell out **30 to 45 days ahead**:

* **Ha Long, Lan Ha, and Bai Tu Long Overnight Cruises**: Boutique steel vessels (`$150 to $320 USD` / `3,750,000 to 8,000,000 VND` per person for 2D1N) sell out balcony cabins weeks ahead from October to April.
* **Oxalis Phong Nha Cave Treks**: National park quotas cap **Hang En (`$330 USD` / `8,250,000 VND`)** and **Tu Lan (`$75 to $800 USD`)**, while **Son Doong (`$3,000 USD`)** books **6 to 12 months** out.
* **Reunification Express 4-Berth Soft Sleepers (`AnL`)**: Reserve lower berths (`$35 to $65 USD` / `875,000 to 1,625,000 VND`) on trains `SE1`, `SE3`, `SE19`, and `SP3` directly on **`dsvn.vn`** (or *Baolau* / *12Go Asia*).
* **Tet Lunar New Year Flights**: For late January to mid-February travel, book domestic flights **60 to 90 days ahead** before fares surge by `1,500,000 to 3,500,000 VND`.

## Phase 3 (7 to 14 Days Out): Banking, Essential Apps, Vaccines & Insurance Setup

Two weeks out, configure your phone apps, ATM cards, and motorbike insurance. Do this while still on your home mobile carrier.

Download and verify five essential apps before flying:

* **Grab**: Install Grab and **link your foreign Visa or Mastercard BEFORE leaving home** so the bank's **3D-Secure SMS OTP** arrives on your home phone number. Waiting until you are on a data-only eSIM in Vietnam blocks the OTP text.
* **Xanh SM**: VinFast's electric blue taxi fleet offers metered rides (`20,000 VND` base fare, `15,500 VND/km`) across major cities.
* **12Go Asia, Baolau & Vexere (`vexere.com`)**: Essential for digital train tickets, **VIP 22-cabin sleeper buses (`350,000 to 550,000 VND`)**, and **9-seat Dcar limousines (`220,000 to 400,000 VND`)**.
* **Google Translate**: Download the **45 MB Offline Vietnamese pack** for instant camera menu scanning.
* **Zalo & WhatsApp**: Every Vietnamese hotel, cruise line, and Easy Rider dispatches pickup license plates and pins via **WhatsApp** or **Zalo**.

Pack **two fee-free debit cards** (*Charles Schwab*, *Wise*, *Revolut*, *Starling*) stored in separate bags. Withdraw cash at **`0 VND`-fee VPBank and TPBank ATMs** (`5,000,000 VND` / `$200 USD` max per withdrawal) or **ACB and HSBC ATMs** (`8,000,000 VND` max), avoiding `50,000 to 66,000 VND` fees at Vietcombank or Agribank. Carry **$100 to $200 USD in crisp post-2013 `$100` bills** (zero tears, creases, or stamps) for emergency gold-shop exchange. Pack `30% DEET` repellent, rehydration salts, and travel insurance covering `$100,000 USD` medical evacuation (*Vinmec*, *FV Hospital*, *Family Medical Practice*, emergency hotline **`115`**).

> [!CAUTION]
> **The 1968 IDP Motorbike Insurance Rule**: Vietnam only recognizes the **1968 Vienna Convention International Driving Permit (IDP)** paired with a full home motorcycle license (`>50cc`). The **1949 Geneva IDP** issued in the **US (AAA), UK, Canada, and Australia is invalid**, voiding medical insurance if you crash. Book a licensed **Easy Rider driver (`$55 to $75 USD` / `1,375,000 to 1,875,000 VND` per day)** in Ha Giang or Da Lat to keep pillion medical coverage intact.

## Phase 4 (48 Hours Out to Day 1 Touchdown): Packing & Arrival Execution

Audit your bags 48 hours before departure against strict customs and airline rules:

* **Power Banks (`<= 100Wh` / `20,000mAh`)**: Carry inside **cabin luggage only**; checked bags containing lithium batteries are detained at `HAN`, `SGN`, and `DAD` X-ray screening.
* **Strict Ban on E-Cigarettes & Vapes (`Resolution 173/2024/QH15`)**: Vietnam strictly prohibits importing or carrying vapes, e-cigarettes, and heated tobacco (*IQOS*), alongside **unregistered drones** lacking Ministry of National Defence permits.
* **Domestic Cabin Weight (`7.0 kg` Limit)**: Pre-buy `20 kg` checked luggage online (`180,000 to 300,000 VND` / `$7 to $12 USD`) on VietJet Air and Vietnam Airlines Economy Lite rather than paying `600,000 VND` at the gate.

Upon landing at `HAN`, `SGN`, or `DAD`, execute four steps in your first 60 minutes:

1. **Check the Purple Entry Stamp**: Verify the **"Permitted to remain until"** date matches your **90-day e-Visa** or **45-day exemption** before leaving the immigration counter.
2. **Activate Data & Pull Cash**: Turn on your **Viettel / Vinaphone** eSIM (`150,000 to 250,000 VND`) and withdraw `2,000,000 to 3,000,000 VND` at an arrival **TPBank or VPBank ATM**.
3. **Break `500,000 VND` Blue Notes**: Buy water or coffee at the airport to break **`500,000 VND` (`$20 USD`)** blue notes into `100,000` and `50,000 VND` bills so you never confuse them with **`20,000 VND` (`$0.80 USD`)** blue notes in a dark cab.
4. **Board Verified Airport Transit**: Bypass terminal touts. At **Hanoi (`HAN`, `30 km`, `45 to 60 minutes`)**, take orange **Express Bus `#86` (`45,000 VND` / `$1.80 USD`)** to Hoan Kiem or book Grab (`300,000 to 380,000 VND`). At **Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`, `8 km`, `25 to 40 minutes`)**, walk to **Pillar 12–15** for a **`120,000 to 180,000 VND`** GrabCar or take **Bus `#109` (`15,000 to 20,000 VND`)**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How far in advance should I apply for a Vietnam e-Visa in 2026?
Apply **30 to 45 days before departure** on the official government portal **`evisa.gov.vn`**. Standard processing takes **3 to 5 business days (`72 to 120 hours`)** and costs **`$25 USD`** (`625,000 VND`) for single entry or **`$50 USD`** (`1,250,000 VND`) for multiple entry up to **90 days**. Do not wait until your final week. Applying a month early gives you a stress-free buffer if immigration requests a clearer JPEG passport scan or an exact ICAO middle-name match.

### Do I need cash in Vietnam or can I pay with credit cards?
Both are essential. Vietnam operates on a **50% card / 50% cash split**. Hotels, shopping malls, domestic airline websites, and the **Grab** app accept foreign Visa and Mastercard, whereas street-food stalls, wet markets, night markets, rural homestays, and temple ticket booths (`30,000 to 150,000 VND`) require physical **`VND` cash**. Keep **`1,500,000 to 2,500,000 VND` (`$60 to $100 USD`)** in small notes (`20,000 to 100,000 VND`) inside your daily wallet.

### Why must I set up the Grab app before flying to Vietnam?
Home SMS verification is mandatory. Linking a foreign Visa or Mastercard inside **Grab** triggers a **3D-Secure bank SMS OTP** sent directly to your home phone number. Completing card verification before boarding your flight ensures cashless rides work the moment you activate a Vietnamese data-only eSIM at `HAN`, `SGN`, or `DAD`.

### What is the easiest mistake to avoid with Vietnamese Dong banknotes?
Watch the blue polymer bills closely. The **`500,000 VND` note (`~$20.00 USD`)** and the **`20,000 VND` note (`~$0.80 USD`)** share a nearly identical cyan-blue color, while the **`100,000 VND` note (`~$4.00 USD`)** and **`10,000 VND` note (`~$0.40 USD`)** both look greenish-yellow. Store `500,000 VND` bills in a separate zippered compartment and count every zero under bright light before paying taxi drivers or street vendors.
