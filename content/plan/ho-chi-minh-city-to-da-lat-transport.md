# Ho Chi Minh City to Da Lat: Bus, Flight & Car

Trading the tropical heat of the southern delta for the pine-forested hills of Lam Dong Province requires climbing **1,500m** in elevation across **305 km** of highway and mountain switchbacks. No railway connects the two cities. Instead, travelers choose between frequent overnight sleeper coaches, luxury 22-cabin and 24-cabin highway buses, short domestic flights into Lien Khuong Airport (**DLI**), or door-to-door private cars.

Overland vehicles follow a single primary highway corridor. Coaches exit eastern Ho Chi Minh City along the Ho Chi Minh City–Long Thanh–Dau Giay Expressway (**CT01**) for **55 km** before turning north onto National Highway **QL20** at the Dau Giay junction in Dong Nai Province. From there, the two-lane road crosses the La Nga River bridge, climbs Chuoi Pass (**Đèo Chuối**) past Madagui at **350m** elevation, and tackles the demanding **10 km** ascent of Bao Loc Pass (**Đèo Bảo Lộc**), which winds through **108** sharp curves to reach the **900m** tea plateau of Bao Loc. After passing Di Linh and Duc Trong, traffic joins the **19 km** Lien Khuong–Prenn Expressway (**CT14**) and ascends the newly widened four-lane Prenn Pass (**Đèo Prenn**, reopened in 2024 with a **14m** roadbed) straight into central Da Lat.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Prenn Pass & Eastern Bus Station Updates:** The widened four-lane **Prenn Pass (Đèo Prenn)** is fully open to passenger buses and private cars, cutting **20 to 30 minutes** off the final approach into Da Lat compared to the older Mimosa Pass detour. In Ho Chi Minh City, interprovincial sleeper buses officially route through **Ben Xe Mien Dong Moi** (New Eastern Bus Station in Thu Duc), but major operators provide free shuttle vans from central **District 1 (Pham Ngu Lao)** and **District 5 (Le Hong Phong)** ticket offices so you do not have to commute to Thu Duc on your own.

## Option 1: Sleeper Buses and 22/24-Cabin VIP Coaches

Highway coaches carry the vast majority of passengers between Saigon and the Central Highlands. Departures run 24/7 every **30 to 60 minutes**, with a dense wave of overnight buses leaving between **22:00 and 23:59** so travelers arrive in Da Lat between **04:30 and 06:30** the next morning.

### Standard 34-Berth Sleeper Buses (290,000–320,000 VND / $11.60–$12.80 USD)

Phuong Trang (Futa Bus Lines) dominates this corridor with its orange fleet of 34-berth Thaco Mobihome sleeper buses. Unlike older 40-berth coaches, the 34-berth layout provides wider reclining bunks arranged in three rows across two tiers, individual privacy curtains, USB-A and USB-C charging ports, reading lamps, and fleece blankets.

* **Ticket Price:** **290,000 to 320,000 VND** (**$11.60 to $12.80 USD**) one way.
* **HCMC Boarding Points:** While municipal regulations route interprovincial buses through the New Eastern Bus Station (**Ben Xe Mien Dong Moi** in Thu Duc City, **22 km** northeast of District 1 and linked via Metro Line 1), you can check in at Futa's central offices at **272 De Tham / Pham Ngu Lao** (District 1) or **231–233 Le Hong Phong** (District 5). Complimentary feeder vans transfer passengers from these downtown lounges to the highway coach terminal, or you can board directly at Mien Tay and An Suong stations.
* **Da Lat Arrival & Free Hotel Shuttle:** All Futa buses terminate at the Da Lat Interprovincial Bus Station (**01 To Hien Thanh**, Ward 3), located **2.5 km** south of Xuan Huong Lake. Inside the arrival hall, passengers show their ticket stub to dispatch staff and board green 16-seat Ford Transit shuttle vans (*xe trung chuyển*) that deliver travelers directly to their hotel doors within a **7 km** radius of central Da Lat at **0 VND** extra cost.
* **Rest Stops:** Buses make one scheduled **20-minute** rest stop at the Futa highway plaza in Madagui (Km 102 on **QL20**) for clean restrooms, hot noodle soup (**45,000 to 65,000 VND**), and coffee (**25,000 to 35,000 VND**).

### 22-Cabin and 24-Cabin VIP Luxury Coaches (420,000–650,000 VND / $16.80–$26.00 USD)

Travelers taller than **1.75m** (**5 ft 9 in**) or couples seeking enclosed privacy should upgrade to a 22-cabin or 24-cabin luxury sleeper coach (*xe phòng đôi / phòng đơn*). These buses replace open aisles with two-tier enclosed sliding-curtain suites arranged in two rows (1-1 layout). Each cabin measures **1.85m** in length and features a flat-folding leather bed with electric lumbar massage, personal air-conditioning vents, an HD entertainment screen, headphones, and fast charging ports.

* **Long Van Limousine:** Operates 22-cabin single VIP coaches (**450,000 VND** / **$18.00 USD**) and 24-cabin double-bed coaches (**650,000 to 750,000 VND** / **$26.00 to $30.00 USD** per cabin for two passengers). Departs from **152 Chu Van An** (Binh Thanh) and **315 Le Hong Phong** (District 5).
* **An Anh Limousine:** Runs modern 24-cabin coaches with onboard flush toilets (a major advantage on **QL20**) and free Da Lat hotel drop-off vans. Single cabins cost **420,000 to 450,000 VND** (**$16.80 to $18.00 USD**), and double cabins cost **650,000 VND** (**$26.00 USD**). Departs from **522B An Duong Vuong** (District 5) and **95 Nguyen Duy Duong**.
* **Phong Phu Limousine & Kumho Samco:** Offer 24-cabin and 34-cabin sleepers (**420,000 to 480,000 VND** / **$16.80 to $19.20 USD**) with pickup points near Pham Ngu Lao (District 1), Tan Son Nhat Airport, and Hang Xanh.
* **Mountain Pass Motion Sickness Tip:** Book a **lower-tier cabin (Tầng A / Lower Deck) in rows 2 through 5**. Upper-deck cabins (Tầng B) sway noticeably more across the **108** switchbacks of Bao Loc Pass (**Đèo Bảo Lộc**).

## Option 2: Direct Flights from Tan Son Nhat (SGN) to Lien Khuong (DLI)

If you suffer from motion sickness on mountain roads or prefer daytime travel without spending **7 to 8 hours** on National Highway **QL20**, flying is the fastest way into the highlands.

* **Airlines & Daily Schedules:** Vietnam Airlines (**VN**) and VietJet Air (**VJ**) operate **3 to 5** direct flights daily between Ho Chi Minh City's Tan Son Nhat International Airport (**SGN**) and Lien Khuong Airport (**DLI**). Early departures typically leave between **06:50 and 08:30**, midday planes take off around **12:45 to 14:10**, and evening services depart at **17:30 to 19:15**.
* **Flight Time vs Total Door-to-Door Time:** Block flight time is just **50 minutes** (**35 minutes** airborne). Once you factor in a **25-minute** taxi to Tan Son Nhat, arriving **90 minutes** prior to departure for domestic security, and the **35-minute** ground transfer from Lien Khuong Airport into Da Lat, total door-to-door transit takes **3 to 3.5 hours**.
* **Airfare:** One-way economy fares range from **1,100,000 to 1,550,000 VND** (**$44.00 to $62.00 USD**) on VietJet Air (**7 kg** carry-on included; **20 kg** checked luggage adds **220,000 VND** / **$8.80 USD**) and **1,450,000 to 2,100,000 VND** (**$58.00 to $84.00 USD**) on Vietnam Airlines (which includes **10 kg** cabin baggage and **23 kg** checked baggage).

### Transferring from Lien Khuong Airport (DLI) to Da Lat City Center (30 km)

Lien Khuong Airport sits in Duc Trong District at **962m** elevation, **30 km** south of Da Lat Market along the four-lane Lien Khuong–Prenn Expressway (**CT14**) and widened Prenn Pass (**Đèo Prenn**). Three ground transfer options wait outside the arrivals curb:

1. **Airport Shuttle Bus (40,000–50,000 VND / $1.60–$2.00 USD):** Blue and white airport coaster buses park directly outside the domestic arrivals exit and depart **20 to 25 minutes** after each flight lands. Buy your ticket at the baggage claim counter or onboard. The bus takes **35 to 40 minutes** up the expressway and drops passengers at the Ngoc Phat Hotel (**10 Ho Tung Mau**, beside Xuan Huong Lake) or along Pasteur and Le Thi Hong Gam streets near [Where to Stay in Da Lat](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-da-lat/).
2. **Fixed-Rate Airport Taxi (250,000–300,000 VND / $10.00–$12.00 USD):** Lado Taxi (light blue EVs and sedans), Mai Linh Taxi (green), and Quoc Te Taxi run dedicated airport dispatch queues. Rather than running the meter (**420,000+ VND**), ask the dispatcher for the standard fixed airport-to-center package (*trọn gói sân bay - trung tâm Đà Lạt*), which costs **250,000 VND** (**$10.00 USD**) for a 4-seater car or **300,000 to 350,000 VND** (**$12.00 to $14.00 USD**) for a 7-seater SUV, inclusive of the **22,000 VND** expressway toll.
3. **GrabCar:** Available via the Grab app for **280,000 to 340,000 VND** (**$11.20 to $13.60 USD**), though passengers pay the airport gate fee (**15,000 VND**) and **CT14** expressway toll (**22,000 VND**) on top of the app fare.

## Option 3: Private Car Charter (2,600,000–3,300,000 VND)

For families of three to six travelers, groups carrying golf bags or photography gear, or visitors who want sightseeing stops along the highland route, chartering a private car with a driver takes **6 to 6.5 hours** door-to-door.

* **4-Seater Sedan (Toyota Vios / Honda City):** **2,600,000 to 2,800,000 VND** (**$104.00 to $112.00 USD**). Ideal for two passengers with two standard suitcases.
* **7-Seater SUV / MPV (Toyota Innova Cross / Fortuner / Mitsubishi Xpander):** **2,900,000 to 3,300,000 VND** (**$116.00 to $132.00 USD**). Suited to three to five passengers with full luggage.
* **9-Seat VIP DCar Limousine Van Charter:** **4,200,000 to 4,800,000 VND** (**$168.00 to $192.00 USD**).
* **Recommended En-Route Sightseeing Stops:** Leaving District 1 at **07:00** gets you past the Long Thanh Expressway toll plaza before peak container truck congestion. Ask your driver to stop at **Dambri Waterfall** (*Thác Đamb'ri*, **18 km** detour off **QL20** in Bao Loc, entry **250,000 VND** / **$10.00 USD** including the alpine coaster and elevator), **Tam Chau Tea Plantation** (*Đồi Chè Tâm Châu*), or **Pongour Waterfall** (*Thác Pongour* in Duc Trong, **7 km** off **QL20**, entry **40,000 VND** / **$1.60 USD**) before ascending Prenn Pass into Da Lat by **15:00**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Should I take the daytime or overnight sleeper bus from Ho Chi Minh City to Da Lat?
Overnight cabin buses departing between **22:30 and 23:30** save a hotel night and bypass daytime truck traffic on **QL20**, reaching Da Lat at **05:00 to 06:00** in **6.5 hours**. However, light sleepers or travelers who want to see the tea hills and mountain passes should book an **07:00 or 08:00** daytime VIP cabin bus or take a **50-minute** flight into **DLI**.

### How do I connect from Da Lat to the southern beaches without backtracking to Saigon?
From Da Lat, you can descend the scenic Dai Ninh Pass (**Đèo Đại Ninh**) or Gia Bac Pass (**Đèo Gia Bắc**) straight to the coast in **3.5 to 4 hours**; read our guide to [Da Lat to Mui Ne Transport](/plan/da-lat-to-mui-ne-transport/) for daily limousine and coaster schedules. Once on the coast, the **99 km** Dau Giay–Phan Thiet Expressway (**CT01**) returns you to Ho Chi Minh City in **2.5 to 3 hours**, or you can compare shorter weekend beach transfers in our [Saigon to Vung Tau Transport](/plan/saigon-to-vung-tau-transport/) breakdown.
