# Vietnam Travel Guide: First Trip Planning Order

Geography dictates everything here. Vietnam stretches **1,650 km** from the Chinese border down to the Gulf of Thailand, tracing an S-shaped coastal corridor that crosses three distinct climate zones and two major river deltas. Most first-time trips fail for one reason: overpacking the schedule. Travelers glance at a map, underestimate mountain highway speeds of 45 km/h, and cram eight cities into ten days.

Plan in reverse. Start with your total nights on the ground, match that window to regional weather, lock in your open-jaw flights, and only then book hotels or sleeper trains. Following this six-step sequence keeps transit fatigue low and protects your budget from costly backtracking.

> **The Golden Open-Jaw Rule for 2026**: Never book a round-trip international ticket into and out of the same city if your route spans more than one region. Fly into **Hanoi (`HAN`)** and depart from **Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`)**—or run the exact reverse south-to-north line. Open-jaw routing saves **$65 to $110 USD** (`1,650,000 to 2,800,000 VND`) per person in backtracking domestic airfare and recovers a full 6-hour travel day.

## Step 1: Match Trip Length to Regional Geography (The Anti-Overpacking Rule)

Distance in Vietnam is measured in hours, not kilometers. Count every intercity transfer—whether a 1 hour 20 minute domestic hop or a 3.5-hour limestone valley drive—as a half-day commitment once airport buffers and hotel check-ins are included.

### 7 to 9 Days: Commit to One Single Region

Short trips demand geographic discipline. Pick **one region only** and explore its core hubs without boarding multiple domestic flights:

- **Option A — Northern Vietnam (`Hanoi + Karst Bays + Valleys`)**: Base yourself in **Hanoi for 3 nights**, transfer 100 km south to **Ninh Binh for 2 nights** among the Trang An and Tam Coc river caves, and sail **Ha Long Bay or Lan Ha Bay for 2 nights** aboard an overnight vessel. Travelers with 9 full days can add **Sapa for 2 nights** via the 5.5-hour Noi Bai–Lao Cai highway.
- **Option B — Central Coast (`Da Nang + Hoi An + Hue`)**: Spend **4 nights in Hoi An or Da Nang** splitting time between the ancient lantern quarters and My Khe Beach, add day excursions to **Ba Na Hills** or the **Cham Islands**, and ride the scenic coastal rail 105 km north over the Hai Van Pass into **Hue for 2 nights**.
- **Option C — Southern Tropical Circuit (`Saigon + Mekong + Island`)**: Allocate **3 nights in Ho Chi Minh City** (including a morning speedboat to the Cu Chi Tunnels), travel 2.5 hours south into the **Mekong Delta (`Ben Tre` and `Can Tho`) for 2 nights**, and fly 55 minutes to **Phu Quoc or Con Dao for 3 nights**.

### 12 to 14 Days: The Classic North-to-South Spine

Two weeks is the sweet spot for a first visit. Connect **two to three regional hubs** using **one or two short domestic flights (`1 hour 20 minutes` each)** or an overnight train. The proven 14-night blueprint runs north to south: **Hanoi (3 nights)** to **Ha Long Bay or Lan Ha Bay (1 to 2 nights)**, overland to **Ninh Binh (2 nights)**, overnight aboard the **SE1 or SE3 Reunification Express train** from Ninh Binh Station (or a morning flight from `HAN`) to **Hoi An and Da Nang (3 to 4 nights)**, and a final 1 hour 25 minute flight south to **Ho Chi Minh City and the Mekong Delta (3 nights)**.

### 18 to 21+ Days: Full Spine Plus Highlands and Caves

Three weeks unlocks Vietnam's frontier mountains and subterranean river systems. Start with the **Ha Giang Loop (3 to 4 days by Easy Rider motorbike)** or **Sapa's Muong Hoa Valley (2 to 3 days)**, link Hanoi and Lan Ha Bay with **Ninh Binh (2 nights)**, stop at **Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park (2 nights)** for Paradise Cave and Hang En expeditions, continue through **Hue (2 nights)** and **Hoi An (3 nights)**, and finish with **Ho Chi Minh City (3 nights)** plus a southern beach retreat in **Phu Quoc, Con Dao, or Quy Nhon (3 nights)**.

## Step 2: Decode the 3 Distinct Climate Zones by Latitude

Ask "When is the best month to visit Vietnam?" and you will get three different answers. Because the country spans 15 degrees of latitude from 23°N to 8°N, one region's dry beach window often coincides with another region's monsoon rains.

- **Northern Vietnam (`Hanoi, Ha Long Bay, Ninh Binh, Sapa, Ha Giang`)**: Expect four distinct seasons. **November to February** brings a cool, dry winter where Hanoi sits at **12°C to 20°C** and mountain passes in Sapa or Dong Van drop to **2°C to 10°C** at night. **March to April** warms up to a comfortable **22°C to 28°C**. **May to September** is hot and humid (**32°C to 37°C**) with heavy summer showers, though late August and September reward trekkers with golden harvest rice terraces. **October to November** delivers crisp autumn skies and low humidity.
- **Central Coast (`Hue, Da Nang, Hoi An, Quy Nhon`)**: Protected by the Truong Son mountain range, Central Vietnam enjoys dry, sunny beach weather from **February through August** (**26°C to 35°C**) with calm turquoise seas. Its peak rain and typhoon window runs from **late September through December**, when heavy downpours can cause temporary flooding along Hoi An's Thu Bon River and Hue's Perfume River.
- **Southern Vietnam (`Ho Chi Minh City, Mekong Delta, Phu Quoc, Con Dao, Mui Ne`)**: Temperatures remain tropical year-round at **27°C to 34°C**. The dry season runs from **December to April** with abundant sunshine and flat seas off Phu Quoc. From **May to November**, the southwest monsoon brings predictable 45-minute afternoon downpours that clear quickly, leaving mornings bright for sightseeing.

**The Best All-Country Overlap Window**: **March and April** represent the single strongest nationwide window. During these two months, the North is mild and green, the Central Coast is sunny and dry, and the South enjoys clear skies before the May rains arrive. **October and November** rank second best for travelers prioritizing northern trekking and Hanoi street food while skipping central beach time.

## Step 3: Lock Entry Visas and Customs Rules (60 to 30 Days Out)

Check your passport bio-page first. Vietnam Immigration requires **at least 6 months of remaining passport validity** beyond your arrival date and **2 blank visa pages** for physical entry and exit stamps.

- **90-Day Official e-Visa (`$25 / $50 USD`)**: Citizens of all nationalities—including the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, India, and all European Union states—can apply online for a **90-day e-Visa**. It costs **$25 USD** for single entry or **$50 USD** for multiple entry. Apply **exclusively through the official government portal (`evisa.gov.vn` or `thithucdientu.gov.vn`)** at least 10 to 15 business days prior to departure; processing takes 3 to 5 working days, and commercial copycat sites charge `$80 to $160 USD` for the exact same document.
- **45-Day Visa-Free Exemption (`13 Countries`)**: Passport holders from **13 countries**—the **United Kingdom, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland, Russia, Japan, South Korea, and Belarus**—receive an automatic **45-day visa-free stamp** upon arrival at any airport or land border at zero cost. ASEAN citizens receive 14 to 30 days visa-free.
- **Phu Quoc 30-Day Island Exemption**: Any foreign traveler flying **directly into Phu Quoc International Airport (`PQC`)** from an international origin (such as Singapore, Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur, Seoul, or Taipei) and remaining solely on Phu Quoc Island qualifies for a **30-day visa-free stay**. Visiting Hanoi, Da Nang, or Ho Chi Minh City afterward requires a standard e-Visa.
- **Mandatory Customs Bans (`Resolution 173/2024/QH15`)**: Under National Assembly **Resolution 173/2024/QH15** (effective since January 1, 2025), Vietnam enforces a **total nationwide ban on e-cigarettes, vapes, heated tobacco devices (`IQOS`), and e-liquids**. Airport X-ray scanners confiscate all vaping hardware on arrival. Likewise, **camera drones (`flycams`) require an individual military flight permit** from the Ministry of National Defence; unpermitted drones are impounded at customs until your departure flight.

## Step 5: Master the Transport Hierarchy and Pre-Flight Setup

Pick the right vehicle for every distance bracket. Over distances above 500 km—such as Hanoi to Da Nang (`760 km`) or Da Nang to Ho Chi Minh City (`960 km`)—**1 hour 20 minute domestic flights** beat overland transit every time. **Vietnam Airlines** (`1,350,000 to 2,400,000 VND` one-way) includes a **23 kg checked bag** and a **10 kg carry-on** with strong on-time performance, whereas budget carrier **Vietjet Air** (`850,000 to 1,650,000 VND`) strictly weighs carry-on bags at **7 kg** at the boarding gate.

For medium-distance overnight links (`Hanoi/Ninh Binh to Dong Hoi or Hue`) and scenic coastal stretches (`Hue to Da Nang` over the **Hai Van Pass** on the **HD1/HD2 Heritage Train**, `150,000 to 180,000 VND`), book **4-berth soft-sleeper cabins (`Khoang 4 điều hòa`, `950,000 to 1,450,000 VND`)** directly on the official state railway portal **`dsvn.vn`**. Where rail tracks do not reach—such as Hanoi to **Sapa (`5.5 hours`)** or **Ha Giang (`6 hours`)**—book **22-cabin VIP single-pod sleeper buses** (`400,000 to 550,000 VND`) or **9-seat DCar Limousine vans** (`350,000 to 450,000 VND`) via **Vexere (`vexere.com`)**.

Before boarding your international flight, complete three digital tasks at home:

1. **Install `Grab` and `Xanh SM` Before Departure**: Register your home mobile number and link your Visa or Mastercard while you can still receive bank **SMS OTP verification codes**. Using app-hailed cars at Noi Bai (`HAN`) and Tan Son Nhat (`SGN`) eliminates meter tampering and clone taxi scams.
2. **Connectivity (`Viettel` eSIM or SIM)**: Install a travel eSIM before takeoff or buy an official **Viettel 4G/5G tourist SIM (`150,000 to 250,000 VND` for 5GB–8GB/day)** inside the arrival hall so you have instant data for ride-hailing and Google Translate.
3. **Zero-Fee ATMs & Clean Polymer Cash**: Carry **1,500,000 to 3,000,000 VND** in small notes for street stalls, and withdraw cash at **VPBank, TPBank, or ACB ATMs**, which charge **0 VND in local operator fees** (unlike international banks charging `55,000 to 66,000 VND` per withdrawal). Always inspect **20,000 VND** (deep blue) and **500,000 VND** (cyan-blue) notes for two full seconds before paying.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it better to travel Vietnam from North to South or South to North?
Both directions work equally well on an open-jaw ticket, so let your travel month decide. From **October to December**, start in **Northern Vietnam (`Hanoi`, `Ninh Binh`, `Ha Long Bay`)** during crisp autumn skies and finish in the South (`Ho Chi Minh City`, `Phu Quoc`) as the southern dry season peaks. From **January to April**, either direction enjoys dry weather across most of the country.

### Can I drink tap water or iced drinks in Vietnam?
Never drink unboiled tap water in Vietnam; use sealed bottled water (`5,000 to 10,000 VND` at Circle K or WinMart) or hotel filtered water stations. However, commercial **cylindrical tube ice (`đá bi`)** served with iced coffee (`cà phê sữa đá`) and street drinks in cities is factory-produced from purified water and safe for travelers.

### How far in advance should I book domestic flights, trains, and Ha Long Bay cruises?
Outside of major national holidays, book **Ha Long or Lan Ha Bay overnight cruises** and **4-berth soft-sleeper train cabins (`SE1/SE3`)** **3 to 4 weeks in advance** to secure lower-berth beds and mid-ship balcony cabins. During the **Lunar New Year (`Tết Nguyên Đán`, late January or February)** and **Reunification Day (`April 30 – May 1`)**, lock in all domestic flights and train tickets **60 to 90 days ahead**.
