Covering 530 km (329 miles) along Vietnam's South Central Coast, the corridor between Da Nang and Nha Trang in Khanh Hoa Province links two of the country's largest beachfront hubs. Three transit modes compete across this distance in 2026. You can fly nonstop in 1 hour 05 minutes, ride the Reunification Express railway for 9.5 to 10.5 hours past the granite headlands of Phu Yen, or book an overnight highway sleeper coach for 9.5 to 11 hours down the CT01 and QL1A trunk roads.

Your choice comes down to daytime coastal scenery versus clock efficiency. Flying cuts total door-to-door transit down to 3.5 to 4 hours, including the 35 km ground transfer north from Cam Ranh International Airport (CXR) into central Nha Trang. Trains take longer on paper, yet they connect two downtown stations directly and trace the cliffside ledge around Dai Lanh Cape (Mui Dien) where highway traffic travels inside a mountain tunnel.

If you plan to break the journey halfway down the coast for two or three nights of quieter bays and Cham towers, review our companion guides on [Da Nang to Quy Nhon Transport](/plan/da-nang-to-quy-nhon-transport/) and [Quy Nhon to Nha Trang Transport](/plan/quy-nhon-to-nha-trang-transport/).

> [!TIP]
> **Best Scenic Train Selection:** For full daylight views of Vung Ro Bay and the Dai Lanh cliffs between Phu Yen and Khanh Hoa, book southbound express **SE21** (departing Da Nang around 08:00, arriving Nha Trang at 18:30) or **SE3** (departing around 10:50, arriving at 20:20) and reserve a left-side window seat facing the direction of travel.

## Option 1: Reunification Express Train (Best Scenery & City-Center Arrival)

Rail remains the most rewarding overland link between Da Nang and Khanh Hoa. Unlike Cam Ranh Airport or the suburban highway bus terminals, both train stations sit inside the urban core.

You board at **Da Nang Railway Station** (791 Hai Phong Street, Thanh Khe District), located 3 km west of the Dragon Bridge (50,000–70,000 VND or $2.00–$2.80 USD by Grab). You step off at **Nha Trang Railway Station** (17 Thai Nguyen Street, Phuoc Tan Ward), opposite Stone Cathedral and 1.8 km (6 minutes by taxi for 40,000–60,000 VND / $1.60–$2.40 USD) from Tran Phu Beach.

### Daytime Scenic Trains vs Evening Expresses

Six daily southbound Reunification Express trains cover the 524 km track between Da Nang and Nha Trang. Picking the right departure hour determines whether you see the ocean cliffs or dark window glass.

* **SE21 (Morning Coastal Express):** Departs Da Nang around 07:55–08:05 and reaches Nha Trang between 18:15 and 18:45 (10 hours 20 minutes). Clearing Phu Yen Province between 15:00 and 17:00, it offers afternoon sunlight as the train hugs the cliff edge over Vung Ro Bay and Dai Lanh Cape (Mui Dien).
* **SE5 (Morning North-South Express):** Leaves Da Nang around 04:25 and pulls into Nha Trang at 14:10 (9 hours 45 minutes). The early boarding hour is tough, but the entire run through Quang Ngai, Binh Dinh, Phu Yen, and Van Phong Bay unfolds in daylight.
* **SE7 (Overnight Arrival Express):** Departs Da Nang around 17:25 and reaches Nha Trang at 03:35 (10 hours 10 minutes). Avoid this slot unless you book a sleeper berth and accept a pre-dawn arrival.
* **SE3 & SE1 (Mainline Premier Trains):** SE3 departs Da Nang around 10:45–11:00 and arrives in Nha Trang at 20:15–20:30 (9 hours 30 minutes). SE1 leaves Da Nang around 12:40 and reaches Nha Trang at 21:55 (9 hours 15 minutes). Both run the newest shock-absorbed carriages on the network, though the final 90 minutes into Khanh Hoa happen after sunset.
* **SE19 (Tourist Express):** Departs Da Nang around 08:30 and reaches Nha Trang at 18:55 (10 hours 25 minutes), featuring refurbished wood-paneled 4-berth cabins.

### 2026 Seat Classes and Ticket Fares

Vietnam Railways (DSVN) prices tickets by train code (SE1 and SE3 cost 8% to 12% more than SE21 or SE5) and deck level:

1. **Air-Conditioned Soft Seat (NML):** 390,000 to 520,000 VND ($15.60 to $20.80 USD). Includes a reclining seat, tray table, and 220V socket. Request a left-side window seat in Carriages 1 or 2 to face the East Sea over Ca Pass (Deo Ca).
2. **6-Berth Soft Sleeper (BNL):** 540,000 to 720,000 VND ($21.60 to $28.80 USD). Three tiers of bunks per cabin; lower berths (Tang 1) offer the best window view and headroom.
3. **4-Berth Soft Sleeper (ANL):** 680,000 to 1,050,000 VND ($27.20 to $42.00 USD). Even on daytime trains like SE21 or SE3, a lower berth in a 4-berth cabin allows you to stretch out horizontally, lock the cabin door, charge devices via USB and 220V outlets, and store two 20 kg suitcases beneath the bottom bunk.

Book directly on the official portal (dsvn.vn) with a Visa or Mastercard, or via Vexere and 12Go Asia (35,000–60,000 VND / $1.40–$2.40 USD fee). Pack a light jacket, banh mi, and bottled water from Da Nang; the canteen car sells hot rice plates (50,000 VND / $2.00 USD) and noodles (25,000 VND / $1.00 USD).

## Option 2: Direct Flights from DAD to CXR (Fastest Transit)

When vacation days are tight, flying saves half a day of overland travel. **Vietnam Airlines** (VN) and **VietJet Air** (VJ) operate 2 to 4 daily nonstop flights between **Da Nang International Airport (DAD Terminal T1)** and **Cam Ranh International Airport (CXR Terminal T1)**.

### Flight Schedules, Baggage Rules, and 2026 Pricing

Flight time is 1 hour 05 minutes. Da Nang Airport sits 4 km (12 minutes, 60,000–90,000 VND / $2.40–$3.60 USD by Grab) from the Han River bridges.

* **Vietnam Airlines:** Operates midday and afternoon departures (11:15 and 15:40) on Airbus A321 or ATR-72 aircraft. One-way Economy fares run 1,450,000 to 2,200,000 VND ($58.00 to $88.00 USD), including 10 kg carry-on and one 23 kg checked bag.
* **VietJet Air:** Runs morning and afternoon flights (08:50 and 13:20) on Airbus A320 and A321 jets. Base fares cost 1,150,000 to 1,650,000 VND ($46.00 to $66.00 USD) with 7 kg carry-on; adding a 20 kg checked bag online costs 210,000 VND ($8.40 USD) versus 450,000 VND ($18.00 USD) at the airport counter.

### Cam Ranh Airport (CXR) to Nha Trang Transfer (35 km)

Do not confuse Cam Ranh Airport with downtown Nha Trang. The passenger terminal sits on the southern Cam Ranh Peninsula, 35 km south of Tran Phu Beach along the coastal Nguyen Tat Thanh Boulevard and Cu Hin Pass. After landing and claiming your checked luggage (allow 25 minutes from touchdown), three reliable ground transfer options cover the drive into the city center in 40 to 50 minutes:

1. **Dat Moi Airport Bus #18:** Bright yellow-and-white shuttle buses park outside the Domestic Arrivals curb (Gate 4) and depart 30 minutes after every commercial flight lands. Tickets cost 65,000 VND ($2.60 USD) per passenger, dropping travelers along Nguyen Thien Thuat, Tran Quang Khai, and Tran Phu streets before terminating at Yersin Gate.
2. **Grab or Fixed-Rate Airport Taxi:** Pre-booking a 4-seat Grab car on your phone or buying a fixed-rate voucher at the Mai Linh, Vinasun, or Sun Taxi counter inside the Arrivals hall costs 320,000 to 400,000 VND ($12.80 to $16.00 USD) inclusive of the 15,000 VND airport toll gate.
3. **Bai Dai Resort Strip:** If your beachfront resort is along Cam Ranh Long Beach (Bai Dai—including Alma, Radisson Blu, The Anam, or Mövenpick), you are only 6 to 12 km (10 to 15 minutes, 120,000–180,000 VND / $4.80–$7.20 USD by metered cab) north of CXR Terminal T1.

## Option 3: Overnight Sleeper Buses & Frequently Asked Questions

To save a night's hotel cost and wake up in Khanh Hoa at dawn, highway coaches depart Da Nang every evening between 18:30 and 21:30. Via the North-South Expressway (CT01) and Highway QL1A, overnight road transit takes 9.5 to 11 hours.

### Best Bus Operators and Terminal Logistics

* **Futa Bus Lines (Phuong Trang):** Runs 34-berth sleeper buses (340,000 VND / $13.60 USD) and 22-room luxury Limousine coaches (460,000 VND / $18.40 USD) departing at 19:00, 20:00, and 21:00, including a free shuttle minivan (Xe Trung Chuyen) to Nha Trang city hotels.
* **Quang Hanh & Lien Hung:** South Central Coast specialists operating 22-cabin VIP single pods (480,000–550,000 VND / $19.20–$22.00 USD) with privacy curtains, massage mattresses, reading lamps, and USB-A/C ports.
* **Cuc Tu & Hanh Cafe:** Budget 34-berth coaches (320,000–380,000 VND / $12.80–$15.20 USD) making two 20-minute rest stops in Quang Ngai and Song Cau (Phu Yen).

Coaches depart from **Da Nang Central Bus Station** (185 Ton Duc Thang Street, Lien Chieu District, 7 km northwest of downtown; 90,000–120,000 VND / $3.60–$4.80 USD by Grab) and arrive at **Nha Trang Southern Bus Station** (Km6 23/10 Street, Vinh Trung, 7.5 km west of the beach; 100,000–130,000 VND / $4.00–$5.20 USD by taxi). Always reserve a **lower-deck middle berth (Tang Duoi, rows A2 to A5)** to minimize sway over Ca Pass.
