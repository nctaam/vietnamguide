## The 315 km Corridor: Direct Sleeper Bus vs Dong Hoi Rail & Cave Transfer

Connecting the coastal city of Da Nang with the limestone valleys of Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park requires a 315 km journey north through Thua Thien Hue, Quang Tri, and Quang Binh provinces. Because Phong Nha town (officially **Son Trach** commune) sits 45 km inland along the Son River with neither an airport nor a railway station, travelers leaving Da Nang must weigh a single-seat highway coach against a two-leg rail-and-road combination via **Dong Hoi**.

Three distinct routing options serve this corridor in 2026:

1. **Reunification Express Train to Dong Hoi + Road Transfer (270 km Rail + 45 km Road | 6.5–7.5 hours total)**: Ride a northbound Vietnam Railways train (`SE20`, `SE8`, `SE6`, `SE4`, or `SE2`) from **Da Nang Railway Station** (791 Hai Phong Street) across the coastal ledges of the Hai Van Pass to **Dong Hoi Railway Station** in 5.5 to 6 hours, then cover the final 45 km west into Son Trach via **Public Bus B4** (35,000 to 50,000 VND / $1.40 to $2.00 USD, 75 minutes) or private taxi (500,000 to 650,000 VND / $20.00 to $26.00 USD, 50 minutes).
2. **Direct Tourist Sleeper Bus (315 km Highway | 6–7 hours)**: Board an open-tour sleeper bus or VIP cabin coach operated by **Queen Cafe**, **Camel Travel**, **Hanh Cafe**, or **Namaly / Hung Long** directly from Da Nang to the main street of Son Trach (`DT20`) for 300,000 to 450,000 VND ($12.00 to $18.00 USD).
3. **Private Car Charter with Sightseeing Stops (315–350 km | 6–9 hours)**: Hire a private 4-seater sedan or 7-seater SUV from your Da Nang hotel to your Phong Nha lodge for 3,200,000 to 4,200,000 VND ($128.00 to $168.00 USD), turning the transit day into an excursion over the **Hai Van Pass**, **Lap An Lagoon**, **Hien Luong Bridge**, and the **Vinh Moc Tunnels**.

## Option 1: Train to Dong Hoi + Cave Transfer (Best for Coastal Views)

Taking the Reunification Express train 270 km from Da Nang to Dong Hoi is the most comfortable daytime way to travel north. While highway buses bypass the mountains through the 6.28 km concrete bore of the Hai Van Tunnel, the railway tracks climb directly along the jungle-covered cliffs of the **Hai Van Pass** (`Đèo Hải Vân`) at elevations up to 100 meters above the East Sea before skirting **Lang Co Bay** and Hue.

### Step 1: Da Nang Railway Station to Dong Hoi Station (270 km | 5.5–6 Hours)

All northbound trains depart from **Da Nang Railway Station** (`791 Hải Phòng, Thanh Khê District`), located 3 km west of the Han River Bridge and 6.5 km from My Khe Beach (a 15-minute GrabCar ride costing 75,000 to 110,000 VND / $3.00 to $4.40 USD).

* **Morning Departures (`SE20` and `SE8`)**: Train `SE20` typically departs Da Nang between 06:00 and 07:05, reaching Dong Hoi around 12:00 to 12:45 (5 hours 45 minutes to 6 hours). Train `SE8` departs Da Nang around 10:15 to 10:35 and pulls into Dong Hoi between 15:50 and 16:15 (5 hours 35 minutes). Both morning services cross the Hai Van Pass in full daylight. For seat-side tips on the mountain segment, consult our [Da Nang to Hue Train Guide](/itineraries/da-nang-to-hue-train-vs-car/).
* **Afternoon Departures (`SE6`, `SE4`, and `SE2`)**: Train `SE6` departs Da Nang around 18:45 (arriving Dong Hoi at 00:15), while flagship expresses `SE4` (departing 12:30 to 13:00, arriving 18:05 to 18:30) and `SE2` (departing 13:15 to 13:35, arriving 18:40 to 19:00) cover the 270 km rail corridor in 5 hours 25 minutes to 5 hours 40 minutes.
* **2026 VNR Rail Fares**:
  * **Air-Conditioned Soft Seat (`Ngồi mềm điều hòa` / NML)**: 230,000 to 350,000 VND ($9.20 to $14.00 USD). Pick a right-hand window seat (facing north) to watch the surf crash against the granite boulders of Hai Van Pass during the first 75 minutes out of Da Nang.
  * **6-Berth Air-Conditioned Sleeper (`Khoang 6 điều hòa` / BnLT)**: 380,000 to 460,000 VND ($15.20 to $18.40 USD).
  * **4-Berth Air-Conditioned Sleeper (`Khoang 4 điều hòa` / AnLT)**: 480,000 to 580,000 VND ($19.20 to $23.20 USD).

### Step 2: Connecting from Dong Hoi Station to Phong Nha (45 km)

When your train arrives at **Dong Hoi Railway Station** (`Thuận Đức, Đồng Hới`), you are still 45 km southeast of Son Trach town. Complete the final leg using one of two ground options:

* **Public Bus Route B4 (35,000 to 50,000 VND / $1.40 to $2.00 USD)**: Operated by the Quang Binh bus company, the green-and-yellow B4 bus runs every 60 to 90 minutes between 05:30 and 17:00. Because Bus B4 does not pull directly into the railway station forecourt, walk 1.8 km east or take a 5-minute taxi (40,000 to 50,000 VND / $1.60 to $2.00 USD) to the **Nam Ly / Tran Hung Dao Bus Stop** to flag it down. The 45 km ride to Son Trach takes 75 minutes. Note that if you take afternoon trains `SE4` or `SE2` (arriving after 18:00), Bus B4 has already finished running for the day.
* **Private Car or Metered Taxi (500,000 to 650,000 VND / $20.00 to $26.00 USD)**: **Mai Linh**, **Sun Taxi**, and **Xanh SM** electric taxis wait outside the Dong Hoi station exit gate for every incoming train. A pre-booked private 4-seater car from your Phong Nha homestay costs 500,000 to 550,000 VND ($20.00 to $22.00 USD) and reaches Son Trach or the Bong Lai Valley in 50 minutes.

## Option 2: Direct Tourist Sleeper Buses (6–7 Hours Door-to-Door)

If you want to go from Da Nang to Phong Nha without changing vehicles in Dong Hoi, book a direct tourist sleeper bus. Rather than climbing over the coastal pass, highway buses travel north through the **6.28 km Hai Van Road Tunnel**, bypass central Hue via the **La Son–Tuy Loan** and **Cam Lo–La Son Expressway (`CT01`)**, and turn west onto Provincial Road `DT20` straight into the heart of Son Trach.

### Top Operators, Pickup Points, and Schedules

* **Queen Cafe Bus & Camel Travel**: Both open-tour operators run daily morning (08:00 to 08:30) and afternoon/evening (13:30, 16:00, and 17:30) departures from Da Nang. Pickups occur at their downtown offices near the **Han River / Bach Dang Street**, the **An Thuong Tourist Quarter** (My An Ward), or central meeting points along Nguyen Tri Phuong Street. Standard 34-berth sleeper tickets cost 300,000 to 380,000 VND ($12.00 to $15.20 USD), while 22-cabin VIP coaches cost 400,000 to 450,000 VND ($16.00 to $18.00 USD).
* **Hanh Cafe & Namaly / Hung Long**: Departing from **Da Nang Central Bus Station** (`185 Tôn Đức Thắng, Liên Chiểu District`) or designated highway pickup points at 07:30, 12:30, and 19:30, these coaches reach Son Trach in 6 to 7 hours for 320,000 to 450,000 VND ($12.80 to $18.00 USD).

> [!TIP]
> **Avoid Overnight Arrivals at 02:00:** Several long-distance buses running from Hoi An and Da Nang to Ninh Binh or Hanoi depart Da Nang around 20:00 and drop Phong Nha passengers on the dark roadside of `DT20` between 01:30 and 02:30. Unless your hostel has a 24-hour front desk and confirmed late check-in, book a morning (08:00) or early afternoon (13:30) departure instead.

## Option 3: Private Car Transfer via Hai Van Pass & DMZ (3,200,000–4,200,000 VND)

For families or groups of three to six travelers, chartering a private vehicle from Da Nang to Phong Nha costs 2,800,000 to 3,400,000 VND ($112.00 to $136.00 USD) for a direct 6-hour drive via the `CT01` expressway, or **3,200,000 to 4,200,000 VND ($128.00 to $168.00 USD)** for a full-day 8.5 to 9-hour sightseeing itinerary.

### Step-by-Step 4-Stop Sightseeing Route (345 km | 8.5–9 Hours)

1. **07:30 – Da Nang Hotel Pickup & Hai Van Pass (28 km)**: Depart My Khe Beach or Hai Chau District and ascend the winding 21 km mountain ribbon of the Hai Van Pass, pausing for 25 minutes at the 496-meter summit bunker (`Hải Vân Quan`, free admission).
2. **09:15 – Lap An Lagoon (`Đầm Lập An`)**: Descend to the brackish oyster lagoon framed by the Bach Ma mountain range for a 30-minute Vietnamese salted coffee (`cà phê muối`, 35,000 VND / $1.40 USD) stop.
3. **12:00 – Hien Luong Bridge & 17th Parallel (Quang Tri DMZ)**: After bypassing Hue via the ring road, stop at the historic Cold War demarcation bridge over the Ben Hai River (50,000 VND / $2.00 USD admission; 40 minutes).
4. **13:15 – Vinh Moc Tunnels**: Walk through the 2 km coastal subterranean village carved 12 to 23 meters into red laterite clay (50,000 VND / $2.00 USD admission; 60 minutes), then drive the final 105 km north into Son Trach by 16:30.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is it better to take the train or the bus from Da Nang to Phong Nha?
Take the **morning train (`SE20` or `SE8`) to Dong Hoi** if you want coastal cliff views over the Hai Van Pass and a wider, upright seat or flat 4-berth sleeper cabin (230,000 to 580,000 VND / $9.20 to $23.20 USD, plus Bus B4 for 35,000 to 50,000 VND or a 500,000 VND taxi). Take a **direct tourist sleeper bus** (Queen Cafe, Camel Travel, Hanh Cafe, or Namaly / Hung Long for 300,000 to 450,000 VND / $12.00 to $18.00 USD) if you carry heavy luggage and want one-seat drop-off on Son Trach's main road (`DT20`) in 6 to 7 hours.

If you want to break the 315 km ride in the former imperial capital for a night or two before heading to the caves, combine our [Da Nang to Hue Transport](/itineraries/da-nang-to-hue-train-vs-car/) and [Hue to Phong Nha Transport](/plan/hue-to-phong-nha-transport/) guides.
