# Ho Chi Minh City to Ben Tre Transport (2026): Van & Bus

Situated **85 kilometers (53 miles) southwest of Ho Chi Minh City** across the cable-stayed **Rach Mieu Bridge (*Cầu Rạch Miễu*)**, **Ben Tre Province (*Bến Tre*)** is the closest rural Mekong Delta destination to Saigon that remains genuinely agrarian and unhurried. Unlike neighboring **My Tho** (which sits directly at the end of the highway and catches the bulk of commercial 40-seat day-trip tour buses), Ben Tre is an island province formed by three alluvial silt islets (**An Hoa, Bao, and Minh**) braided by four branches of the Tien River and shaded by more than 78,000 hectares of water-coconut and fruit groves.

Thanks to the four-lane **Ho Chi Minh City – Trung Luong Expressway (CT01)**, traveling from District 1 or District 5 in Saigon to Ben Tre takes just **1.5 to 2.0 hours** by road. Whether you are heading down for an independent day trip or checking into a riverside eco-lodge in **Chau Thanh, Giong Trom, or Mo Cay**, this 2026 guide compares shared 9-seat VIP limousine vans, sleeper coaches from Mien Tay Bus Station, door-to-door private cars, and backroad motorbike routes with verified VND fares.

## Option 1: 9-Seat VIP Limousine Vans (140,000–200,000 VND) — Fastest & Most Convenient from Central Saigon

Because the distance between Ho Chi Minh City and Ben Tre is under 90 kilometers, luxury 9-seat and 11-seat **Ford Transit / Hyundai Solati DCar Limousines** dominate the route. Instead of forcing you to take a 35-minute taxi out to suburban Mien Tay Bus Station in Binh Tan District, limousine operators maintain pickup lounges right along the **District 1 – District 5 border** (just 10 minutes by Grab from Ben Thanh Market or Pham Ngu Lao).

### Top Verified Limousine Operators & 2026 Fares:
1. **Thinh Phat Limousine (*Thịnh Phát*) & Ben Tre Limousine**:
   - **Saigon Departure Office**: 255 Lê Hồng Phong, Ward 4, District 5 (40,000 VND Grab ride from District 1), plus scheduled airport pickups at **Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN)** for an extra 50,000 VND.
   - **2026 Fare**: **150,000 to 200,000 VND ($6.00–$8.00 USD)** for middle executive massage seats; **140,000 VND** for front/rear seats.
   - **Frequency**: Every **30 to 45 minutes** from **05:00 AM to 20:30 PM**.
2. **Thao Chau (*Thảo Châu*) & Kim Hoang (*Kim Hoàng*)**:
   - **Saigon Departure Office**: Lê Hồng Phong / Nguyễn Chí Thanh corridor (District 5 / District 10).
   - **2026 Fare**: **130,000 to 170,000 VND ($5.20–$6.80 USD)**.
- **Crucial Ben Tre Homestay Drop-Off Benefit**: When your limousine crosses the Rach Mieu Bridge into Ben Tre Province, operators transfer passengers onto a complimentary connecting 7-seat or 16-seat shuttle van (*xe trung chuyển*) that drops you directly at hotels inside **Ben Tre City (Phú Khương, Phường 5, Mỹ Thạnh An)** and riverside homestays along the **Chau Thanh – Phu Tuc** canal belt!

## Option 2: Express Buses from Mien Tay Bus Station (95,000–130,000 VND)

If you prefer Vietnam's largest national bus carrier or are connecting onward toward Tra Vinh or Can Tho:
- **Where to Board**: **Mien Tay Coach Station (*Bến xe Miền Tây*, 395 Kinh Dương Vương, Binh Tan District, 10 km west of District 1)**. If you buy a **FUTA Bus Lines (*Phương Trang*)** ticket (105,000–120,000 VND) at their **District 1 ticket office (272 Đề Thám, Pham Ngu Lao)**, FUTA provides a **free shuttle van** from De Tham Street to Mien Tay Station!
- **Arrival in Ben Tre**: Buses terminate at **Ben Tre Provincial Bus Station (Bến xe Bến Tre, 303 Đoàn Hoàng Minh, QL60)**. From the terminal, FUTA's orange complimentary shuttle van drops passengers anywhere within a 5-kilometer radius of downtown Ben Tre, or you can hail a metered **Mai Linh / Open99 / Xanh SM electric taxi (60,000–120,000 VND)** to rural eco-lodges.

> [!TIP]
> **Matching Your Ben Tre Eco-Lodge District to the Right Drop-Off Point**: Ben Tre's riverside homestays cluster in three distinct rural districts, and knowing which one you booked saves you a 200,000 VND taxi backtrack:
> 1. **Chau Thanh District (Châu Thành — Phú Túc, Quới Sơn, Tam Phước)**: Located immediately south of the **Rach Mieu Bridge** (10 km *before* Ben Tre City). If staying here (*Forever Green Resort*, *Mekong Home*, *Jardin du Mekong*), tell your limousine driver to drop you **just after the Rach Mieu Bridge exit** rather than riding all the way into town!
> 2. **My Thanh An / Southern Ben Tre Riverfront (5 km south of town across the Ben Tre River)**: Home to *Ben Tre Riverside Resort*, *Oasis Hotel*, and *Coco Farmstay*; covered by free limousine city shuttles.
> 3. **Giong Trom (Giồng Trôm — *Mango Home Riverside*) & Mo Cay (Mỏ Cày)**: Located 12 to 18 km southeast/southwest of town across the Ham Luong Bridge; requires a 15-to-20-minute local taxi (160,000–240,000 VND) from the Ben Tre bus/limousine hub.

## Option 3: Private Car (950k–1,250k VND) & Option 4: Self-Drive Motorbike Routing

- **When a Private Car Makes Sense**: For a family or group of 3 to 4 travelers (250,000–320,000 VND per person split four ways), booking a private 4-seater sedan (**950,000–1,250,000 VND**) or 7-seater SUV (**1,150,000–1,450,000 VND**) picks you up at your District 1 hotel or **Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN)** and drives straight down narrow coconut-lined lanes to rural lodges in **Giong Trom, Cho Lach, or Ba Tri** without any van transfers. You can also book a **full-day round-trip private car from Saigon (1,500,000–1,800,000 VND for 8–10 hours)** that waits while you hire a private sampan at **Hung Phong or Chet Say Pier (250,000–400,000 VND per boat)**.
- **Critical Rule for Motorbike Riders (Motorbikes Are Banned on Expressway CT01)**: Two-wheeled vehicles are strictly prohibited from entering the **HCMC – Trung Luong Expressway (CT01)**. If riding a rented scooter from Saigon to Ben Tre, set Google Maps to **"Avoid Tolls / Motorbike Mode"** and take **National Highway QL50** south through Binh Chanh, Can Giuoc, and Go Cong into My Tho (82 km), then ride across the outer motorbike lane of the **Rach Mieu Bridge (QL60)** into Ben Tre (92 km total, 2.5 hours).

> [!WARNING]
> **Sunday Afternoon Rach Mieu Bridge Bottleneck (15:00–19:30 PM)**: Until the parallel **Rach Mieu 2 Bridge** completes full traffic diversion, the single-deck **Rach Mieu 1 Bridge (QL60)** between Ben Tre and My Tho experiences heavy weekend congestion on **Sunday afternoons between 15:00 and 19:30 PM** as delta weekenders return to Saigon. When returning from Ben Tre to Ho Chi Minh City on a Sunday—especially if catching an evening flight at SGN—depart Ben Tre **before 13:00 PM** or allow an extra 45 to 60 minutes of bridge crossing time.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How long does it take to get from Ho Chi Minh City to Ben Tre?
By **9-seat VIP limousine van** or **private car** via the Ho Chi Minh City – Trung Luong Expressway (CT01) and Rach Mieu Bridge, the **85-kilometer journey takes 1.5 to 2.0 hours** under normal traffic conditions.

### Can I visit Ben Tre as a day trip from Ho Chi Minh City without a tour group?
Yes. Take a **07:00 AM Thinh Phat or Thao Chau limousine van** (160,000 VND) from District 5, arriving in Ben Tre by **08:45 AM**. Take a 10-minute taxi to **Cai Cam Pier, Chet Say Pier, or Hung Phong Coconut Islet**, hire a private sampan and village bicycle for a 4-hour coconut canal and brick-kiln circuit (300,000–450,000 VND), eat an elephant-ear fish lunch (*cá tai tượng chiên xù*), and board the **16:00 PM limousine van** back to Saigon by **18:00 PM**.

### How do I travel onward from Ben Tre to Can Tho instead of returning to Saigon?
From **Ben Tre Bus Station (QL60)** or your homestay, book a direct **Ben Tre – Can Tho minivan or bus** (Thảo Châu, Phương Trang, or Thành Công, costing **110,000 to 150,000 VND** / $4.40–$6.00 USD) or a **private car (1,100,000–1,350,000 VND)**. The **105-kilometer cross-delta route** crosses the Ham Luong and Co Chien bridges through Vinh Long into Can Tho in **2.0 to 2.5 hours**, putting you at Ninh Kieu Wharf in time for the next morning's **Cai Rang Floating Market**.

### Is it better to take a boat from My Tho into Ben Tre or drive straight across the Rach Mieu Bridge into Ben Tre Province?
If your goal is to experience **authentic, unhurried coconut-canal life**, **drive straight across the Rach Mieu Bridge into Ben Tre City, Hung Phong Islet, or Chau Thanh District** rather than stopping at the My Tho 30/4 Tourist Pier in Tien Giang Province. The My Tho marina on the northern bank of the Tien River is the main turnaround point for hundreds of big Saigon tour buses running the standard four-island "Unicorn Islet (*Cồn Thới Sơn*)" circuit. By staying on your limousine van or private car for an extra **15 minutes across the Rach Mieu Bridge into Ben Tre Province proper**—and boarding a private sampan at **Chet Say Bridge Pier (*Bến tàu Cầu Chẹt Sậy*), Cai Cam Pier, or Phu Tuc**—you enter quiet interior rivers lined with brick kilns (*lò gạch*), coconut-husking yards, and family-run sedge-mat weavers with almost zero tour-bus crowds.

### Can I bring a full-sized 24-inch or 28-inch suitcase on a 9-seat VIP limousine van from Saigon to Ben Tre?
Yes, with one important booking note: a 9-seat Ford Transit DCar Limousine has a **compact rear luggage bay** behind the last row of reclining seats that comfortably holds **4 to 5 medium suitcases plus soft backpacks**. If you are traveling with **two or more oversized 28-inch hard-shell suitcases**, inform the operator (Thịnh Phát, Thảo Châu, or Kim Hoàng) on WhatsApp or Vexere when reserving your seats so the dispatcher places your bags wisely or assigns a 16-seat high-roof Solati van, OR book **FUTA Bus Lines (Phương Trang, 105,000–120,000 VND)**, which uses full-sized 40-berth coaches with massive underbelly cargo holds and free hotel transit shuttles in Ben Tre.
