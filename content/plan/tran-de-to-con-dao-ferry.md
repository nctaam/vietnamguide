## Why Tran De (Soc Trang) Is the Shortest Sea Crossing to Con Dao

Measured point-to-point across the southern Vietnamese shelf, **Tran De Passenger Port** (`Cảng Trần Đề`) in Soc Trang Province sits just `90 km` (`48 to 50 nautical miles`) northwest of **Ben Dam Port** (`Cảng Bến Đầm`) on Con Son Island. That distance is less than half the `185 km` open-ocean voyage on the [Vung Tau to Con Dao Ferry](/plan/vung-tau-to-con-dao-ferry/). Located at `Kinh Ba, Tran De Town` right where the silt-laden Hau River (Bassac River) empties into the South China Sea, Tran De lies `35 km` southeast of Soc Trang City and `95 km` southeast of Can Tho.

For budget travelers, Mekong Delta explorers, and anyone visiting Con Dao during the breezy autumn and winter transition months when longer routes face cancellations, Tran De is the workhorse maritime gateway. Two major ferry lines compete head-to-head on this `90 km` channel: the monohull speedboats of **Superdong (Con Dao I & II)** and the twin-hull catamarans of **Phu Quoc Express (Trung Nhi / Con Dao Express)**. Crossing times range from `1 hour 45 minutes` to `2 hours 30 minutes`, and adult one-way tickets start at `310,000 VND` (`$12.40 USD`)—making it by far the cheapest way to reach the archipelago in 2026.

## Operator Comparison: Superdong vs Phu Quoc Express

Although both companies sail to the exact same pier at **Ben Dam Port** on Con Son Island, they depart from two separate terminals situated `1.8 km` apart along the Hau River estuary in Tran De Town, and their boats handle ocean swells differently.

### 1. Superdong Speedboat (`Tàu cao tốc Superdong Côn Đảo I & II`)

**Superdong** pioneered fast passenger service between Soc Trang and Con Dao, operating two Malaysian-built monohull aluminum vessels (**Superdong Con Dao I** and **Con Dao II**), each carrying `306 passengers` at `24 to 26 knots`.

* **The Timetable Advantage:** Superdong is the only operator offering **two daily departures** in each direction during peak and shoulder months (`08:00` and `13:15` from Tran De; `08:00` and `13:00` from Ben Dam Port). That `13:15` afternoon sailing from Tran De is a lifesaver: it allows travelers leaving Can Tho at `09:30` or Ho Chi Minh City at `06:00` to catch a same-day ferry without taking an overnight sleeper bus.
* **Pricing:** Flat pricing of `310,000 to 340,000 VND` (`$12.40 to $13.60 USD`) for adults and `220,000 VND` (`$8.80 USD`) for children aged `6 to 11`, with zero weekend surge markups.
* **Ride Comfort:** Because a monohull vessel rides on a single deep V-hull, it rolls side-to-side more noticeably than a wide catamaran when crossing beam swells outside the river mouth. Sit on the lower deck near the back third of the cabin to minimize vertical bounce.

### 2. Phu Quoc Express Catamaran (`Tàu hai thân Phú Quốc Express`)

Operating from the main **Tran De Fishing & Passenger Port** (`Cảng Cá Trần Đề`) roughly `1.8 km` upriver from the Superdong terminal, **Phu Quoc Express** assigns its `598-seat` twin-hull catamarans (*Trung Nhi* or *Con Dao Express*) to the route.

* **Speed & Stability:** Powered by four Rolls-Royce MTU engines cruising at `30 knots`, the catamaran cuts the `90 km` crossing down to `1 hour 45 minutes` to `2 hours`. Its `12.2-meter` twin-hull beam resists rolling far better than a monohull when winds pick up to `15 or 20 knots`.
* **Pricing & Classes:** Standard **Eco Class** costs `390,000 VND` (`$15.60 USD`) Monday through Thursday and `450,000 VND` (`$18.00 USD`) Friday through Sunday and public holidays. The forward upper-deck **VIP Cabin** costs `590,000 VND` (`$23.60 USD`) and features wide leather reclining seats with footrests.
* **Schedule:** Departs Tran De daily at `08:30` (arriving at Ben Dam around `10:30`) and returns from Con Dao at `12:30` (arriving back at Tran De around `14:30`).

## How to Get to Tran De Port from Ho Chi Minh City and Can Tho

Tran De is a small coastal fishing town with no airport and no train station. Getting to the pier on time for an `08:00` or `08:30` morning sailing requires matching your overland bus or car leg carefully.

### Route A: Overnight Sleeper Bus from Ho Chi Minh City (`230 km`)

For solo travelers and couples starting in Saigon, an overnight sleeper bus is the most efficient strategy because you sleep through the `5.5-hour` highway drive and wake up directly at the ferry terminal gate.

1. **Direct Tran De Port Buses (My Duyen & Futa Bus Lines):** Board at **Mien Tay Bus Station** (`Bến xe Miền Tây`, `395 Kinh Duong Vuong, Binh Tan District`, about `25 minutes` by taxi from District 1 for `140,000 to 180,000 VND`). Book the `23:00` or `23:59` departure operated by **My Duyen** (`Mỹ Duyên`) or **Phuong Trang (Futa Bus Lines)** bound directly for **Tran De** (not just Soc Trang City).
2. **Fares & Cabin Types:** Standard `34-berth` sleeper buses cost `200,000 to 230,000 VND` (`$8.00 to $9.20 USD`), while `22-cabin` VIP privacy sleeper buses cost `260,000 to 310,000 VND` (`$10.40 to $12.40 USD`).
3. **Dawn Drop-Off:** The bus pulls into Tran De Town between `05:00` and `05:45`. Both operators provide a free local shuttle van or drop passengers directly at the **Superdong Pier** and **Phu Quoc Express Terminal** gates, giving you two full hours to wash up, eat a `35,000 VND` bowl of pork noodle soup (*hủ tiếu*), and drink iced coffee before boarding starts at `07:30`.

### Route B: Morning Transfer from Can Tho (`95 km`)

Combining a Mekong Delta floating-market trip in Can Tho with a Con Dao island stay is one of southern Vietnam's best two-part itineraries. From central Can Tho (`Ninh Kieu Wharf`), Tran De Port is `95 km` southeast via National Highway `QL91B`—known locally as the **South Hau River Highway** (*Đường Nam Sông Hậu*), which hugs the southern bank of the Bassac River all the way to the coast.

* **Direct Shared Shuttle Van (Can Tho → Tran De Port):** Local limousine vans (**Hung Cuong**, **Vu Linh**, or ferry-linked shuttles booked through your hotel) pick up passengers at Ninh Kieu hotels between `05:30` and `06:00`, arriving directly at Tran De Port at `07:45` (`2 hours` transit time). Tickets cost `120,000 to 150,000 VND` (`$4.80 to $6.00 USD`) per person.
* **Private Taxi or Charter Car:** A `4-seat` private sedan from Can Tho to Tran De Port costs `850,000 to 1,000,000 VND` (`$34.00 to $40.00 USD`), while a `7-seat` SUV costs `1,000,000 to 1,200,000 VND` (`$40.00 to $48.00 USD`). Depart Can Tho no later than `05:45` to make the `08:00` Superdong or `08:30` Phu Quoc Express departure.
* **Via Soc Trang City (`35 km`):** If you arrive at Soc Trang Bus Station first, catch the local public bus (`Route 04` Soc Trang – Tran De, `25,000 VND`, `50 minutes`) or take a Mai Linh taxi for `350,000 to 420,000 VND` (`$14.00 to $16.80 USD`, `35 minutes`) straight down Provincial Road `DT934` to the port.

## Sea Conditions, Monsoon Reliability, and Terminal Facilities

Why do island regulars favor Tran De over Vung Tau between October and March? Geography and time exposed to swells. While both routes cross the open continental shelf, cutting the distance from `185 km` to `90 km` reduces open-sea exposure to roughly `100 minutes` once the ferry clears the `15-minute` sheltered mouth of the Hau River.

* **Calm Season (March to September):** Flat seas (`0.5 to 1.2-meter` waves), light southerly breezes (**Beaufort Force 2–4**), and near-`99%` schedule reliability.
* **Northeast Monsoon Season (October to February):** Strong northeast winds (**Beaufort Force 5–6**) create choppy `2.0 to 3.0-meter` cross-swells. When Vung Tau ferries are locked down in port, Tran De catamarans and Superdong speedboats often continue sailing—unless winds hit **Beaufort Force 7** (`28+ knots`), at which point the Soc Trang Maritime Port Authority halts all passenger vessels. Even so, winter crossings are bumpy: take `25mg` of **Cinnarizine** (`Stugeron`) `45 minutes` prior to departure and pick a lower-deck rear seat. For a broader look at seasonal island trade-offs, see our [Con Dao vs Phu Quoc](/compare/con-dao-vs-phu-quoc/) comparison.

### Terminal Check-In, Motorbike Parking, and Cargo Rules

Both the **Superdong Terminal** (Ap Dau Giong, Tran De Town) and the **Phu Quoc Express Terminal** (Cang Ca Tran De) feature covered air-conditioned waiting halls, clean restrooms, ticket counters, and secure overnight parking lots (15,000 VND / $0.60 USD per night for motorbikes; 70,000 to 100,000 VND / $2.80 to $4.00 USD per night for cars). Small dockside cafes outside both gates serve hot rice plates (*cơm tấm*), pork noodle soup, and Vietnamese drip coffee starting at 05:00 every morning for 30,000 to 45,000 VND ($1.20 to $1.80 USD).

> **Crucial Terminal Warning:** Tran De has two separate ferry piers located 1.8 km apart along the Hau River bank. **Superdong** departs from its own dedicated white passenger terminal near the estuary mouth, whereas **Phu Quoc Express** boards inside the main **Tran De Fishing Port** (*Cảng Cá Trần Đề*) further upriver. Check your electronic QR ticket carefully before telling your taxi or shuttle driver which gate to enter.

Present your original physical passport or Vietnamese ID card (CCCD) at the boarding gate 30 to 45 minutes before sailing. Foreign nationals must carry their physical passport booklet; border police stationed at the Tran De gangway log every foreign passport number and visa stamp before permitting embarkation. Each passenger gets 15 kg of free carry-on luggage. Oversized cardboard boxes, coolers packed with mainland fruit or seafood, and scuba gear bags over 15 kg incur a cargo handling fee of 40,000 to 100,000 VND ($1.60 to $4.00 USD). Motorbike freight across to Con Dao costs 250,000 to 400,000 VND ($10.00 to $16.00 USD) each way depending on engine size (110cc vs 150cc), plus 30,000 VND for dock stevedores to lash it onto the stern deck and drain excess fuel for fire safety; call the ticket office 24 to 48 hours ahead because each vessel only holds 10 to 15 motorbikes.

### Advance Ticket Reservations and Weather Refund Rules

Peak domestic holidays—especially **Reunification Day (April 30 – May 1)**, summer school holidays from June to August, and spiritual pilgrimage weekends honoring national heroine **Vo Thi Sau**—see both Superdong and Phu Quoc Express sell out 7 to 14 days in advance. Book electronic QR boarding passes ahead of time using official operator portals or Vietnamese ticketing agencies rather than showing up without a reservation at 07:30. If the Soc Trang Port Authority orders a weather suspension due to Force 7 winds, both lines refund 100% of the ticket price to your original payment method or roll your seat onto the next cleared departure without change fees.

## Disembarking at Ben Dam Port and Reaching Con Son Town (`12 km`)

Upon docking at **Ben Dam Port** (`Cảng Bến Đầm`) on Con Son Island, follow the `250-meter` concrete pier out to the main transport plaza. Con Son Town lies `12 km` northeast along a paved coastal road that climbs past **Shark Cape** (`Mũi Cá Mập`) and **Bai Nhat Beach**. Read our detailed breakdown of [Where to Stay in Con Dao](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-con-dao/) before choosing your drop-off zone.

1. **Shared Electric Shuttle Buggy (*Xe điện*):** Waiting right outside the port gate, these open-sided `12-passenger` electric carts charge `40,000 to 50,000 VND` (`$1.60 to $2.00 USD`) per person directly to your hotel door in Con Son Town (`20 minutes`).
2. **Taxi to Town or Dam Trau:** A `4-seat` or `7-seat` taxi into Con Son Town costs `220,000 to 300,000 VND` (`$8.80 to $12.00 USD`), or `400,000 to 500,000 VND` (`$16.00 to $20.00 USD`) to resorts near Dam Trau Beach (`24 km`).
3. **Pier Scooter Delivery:** Pre-book an automatic `125cc` scooter (`150,000 to 200,000 VND` / `$6.00 to $8.00 USD` per day) via Zalo with your guesthouse so they leave the bike at Ben Dam Port—letting you ride the scenic coastal pass straight into town on your own schedule.
