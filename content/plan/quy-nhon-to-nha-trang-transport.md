# Quy Nhon to Nha Trang Transport (2026): Train vs Bus

Moving south along the South-Central Coast from Quy Nhon in Binh Dinh Province to Nha Trang in Khanh Hoa Province covers 215 km by highway or 219 km by rail. Both corridors trace some of the sharpest granite headlands and turquoise bays in central Vietnam, crossing Phu Yen Province before entering northern Khanh Hoa around Van Phong Bay.

Most independent travelers complete the transit in 4 to 4.5 hours. Your core decision comes down to two competing logistics models: taking a 20-minute taxi inland to **Dieu Tri Railway Station** for a southbound Reunification Express train that hugs the open ocean cliffs around Vung Ro Bay, or boarding a direct 9-seat VIP limousine van from downtown Quy Nhon (`71 Tay Son`) that cuts through the 4.1 km **Deo Ca Road Tunnel**. Families and small groups with extra time often skip public transit altogether and charter a private coastal car, turning the transfer into an 8-hour sightseeing run via Ganh Da Dia and Mui Dien Lighthouse.

Below is the verified 2026 schedule, ticket pricing breakdown, station transfer protocol, and seat-selection guide for traveling from Quy Nhon to Nha Trang.

## Option 1: Southbound Trains from Dieu Tri to Nha Trang

Mainline North-South trains do not enter downtown Quy Nhon. Instead, all Reunification Express services stop at **Dieu Tri Railway Station** (*Ga Dieu Tri*, located on Nguyen Van Troi Street in Tuy Phuoc District), roughly 11 km west of Quy Nhon's Xuan Dieu beachfront.

Getting from hotels listed in our [Where to Stay in Quy Nhon](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-quy-nhon/) guide to Dieu Tri Station takes 20 to 25 minutes along National Highway QL19 and costs 150,000 to 180,000 VND ($6.00 to $7.20 USD) in a metered Mai Linh, Lado, or Xanh SM electric taxi. On the arrival end, **Nha Trang Railway Station** sits at `17 Thai Nguyen Street` in Phuoc Tan Ward—just 1.5 km (5 minutes, 40,000 to 60,000 VND / $1.60 to $2.40 USD by Grab) from Tran Phu Beach.

### 2026 Southbound Train Timetable (Dieu Tri to Nha Trang)

Six core Vietnam Railways (*Duong Sat Viet Nam*) passenger trains cover the 219 km rail segment daily:

* **Train SE7 (Early Morning Express):** Departs Dieu Tri at **04:20**, stops briefly in Tuy Hoa at 06:16, and arrives at Nha Trang Station at **08:35** (4 hours 15 minutes). Ideal if you want a full morning in Nha Trang, though you must wake up at 03:30 in Quy Nhon.
* **Train SE5 (Morning Coastal Daylight Run):** Departs Dieu Tri at **09:18**, passes Tuy Hoa at 11:12, and reaches Nha Trang at **13:35** (4 hours 17 minutes). This is the top daytime choice for admiring the Deo Ca cliffside track in full sunlight without a pre-dawn alarm.
* **Train SE9 (Midday Standard Service):** Departs Dieu Tri at **11:05** and arrives in Nha Trang at **15:40** (4 hours 35 minutes). Makes additional county stops at Bong Son and Ninh Hoa.
* **Train SE21 (Afternoon Regional Express):** Departs Dieu Tri at **15:52**, stops in Tuy Hoa at 17:50, and pulls into Nha Trang at **20:15** (4 hours 23 minutes). Originating in Da Nang rather than Hanoi, SE21 tends to suffer fewer cumulative northern delays.
* **Train SE3 & Train SE1 (Late-Night / Overnight Runs):** **SE3** departs Dieu Tri at **16:42** (arriving Nha Trang at **20:50**), while **SE1** departs at **18:10** (arriving Nha Trang at **22:18**). Both travel the Phu Yen coastal cliffs after sunset.

### Fares, Classes & The Left-Side Window Rule

For a 4-hour daytime ride on **SE5** or **SE21**, book an **Air-Conditioned Soft Seat (`Ngoi mem dieu hoa`, code NML)**. In 2026, NML seats cost **140,000 to 210,000 VND ($5.60 to $8.40 USD)** depending on the train number and weekday demand. If you prefer to stretch out on the early **SE7** departure, a 6-berth cabin (`Nam khoang 6`, code BnLT) costs **260,000 to 310,000 VND ($10.40 to $12.40 USD)**, while a 4-berth soft sleeper (`Nam khoang 4`, code AnLT) runs **330,000 to 390,000 VND ($13.20 to $15.60 USD)**.

> [!TIP]
> **Sit on the Left (East) Side for Deo Ca & Vung Ro Views:** About 2 hours 15 minutes after leaving Dieu Tri (shortly after departing Tuy Hoa Station), the railway climbs onto the granite ledges of **Deo Ca Pass** above **Vung Ro Bay** and **Dai Lanh Beach**. Highway vehicles vanish into a concrete mountain tunnel here, whereas the train tracks cling directly to the open sea cliffs 50 meters above crashing waves for 25 minutes. When booking on `dsvn.vn`, select window seats on the **east (left-hand) side** of the carriage facing the direction of travel.

### Luxury Option: The Vietage by Anantara

Travelers celebrating a special occasion can book **The Vietage by Anantara**, a custom-built 12-seat luxury carriage hitched to the morning southbound train departing Dieu Tri around **08:15 to 09:18** and arriving in Nha Trang around **13:00 to 13:35**. One-way tickets cost approximately **10,500,000 VND ($420.00 USD)** per passenger and include private semi-enclosed booths, a three-course gourmet lunch featuring Quy Nhon seafood and French cheeses, free-flow wine and craft cocktails at the curved sit-up bar, and a 15-minute head-and-shoulder spa treatment.

## Option 2: VIP Limousine Vans & Sleeper Buses via QL1D and QL1A

If you want to skip the 11 km taxi backtrack to Dieu Tri Station, road operators depart directly from **Quy Nhon Central Bus Station** (*Ben Xe Trung Tam Quy Nhon*, `71 Tay Son Street`, Ghenh Rang Ward)—located just 2.5 km from Quy Nhon Beach—or pick up passengers from downtown hotels.

Road coaches exit southern Quy Nhon via the scenic 33 km **National Highway QL1D** cliff road past Bai Xep and Song Cau before joining upgraded four-lane **Highway QL1A** through Phu Yen. Instead of climbing the hairpin switchbacks of Deo Ca Pass (which used to add 45 minutes of slow mountain driving), all buses now pass through the **4.1 km Deo Ca Road Tunnel** (*Ham duong bo Deo Ca*), cutting total highway travel time to **4 to 4.5 hours**.

### Top 2026 Bus & Limousine Operators

1. **Sao Limousine & Cuc Tu Limousine (9-Seat & 11-Seat VIP Vans):**
   * **Schedule:** Hourly departures from **06:30 to 18:30**.
   * **2026 Ticket Fare:** **220,000 to 280,000 VND ($8.80 to $11.20 USD)** per seat (middle leather captain chairs cost 250,000 to 280,000 VND; front cab and rear bench seats cost 200,000 to 220,000 VND).
   * **Pickup & Drop-off:** Free hotel shuttle pickup within a 5 km radius of central Quy Nhon (Xuan Dieu, Nguyen Hue, An Duong Vuong) and drop-off along Tran Phu or Nguyen Thien Thuat in central Nha Trang.
2. **Phuong Trang / Futa Bus Lines (34-Berth Sleeper Coach):**
   * **Schedule:** Departures at **07:30, 09:30, 12:00, 14:30, 17:00, and 20:00** from `71 Tay Son`.
   * **2026 Ticket Fare:** **180,000 to 210,000 VND ($7.20 to $8.40 USD)**.
   * **Arrival Terminal:** **Nha Trang Southern Bus Station** (*Ben Xe Phia Nam Nha Trang*, located at `Km 6, 23 Thang 10 Street` in Vinh Trung Commune, roughly 7 km west of Nha Trang beach). Futa provides a **free orange shuttle minibus** from the southern terminal to downtown Nha Trang hotels; confirm drop-off with the ticket clerk before boarding in Quy Nhon so your room address is logged on the driver's manifest.
3. **Lien Hung & Hong Son Phu Yen (VIP 22-Cabin & 34-Berth Coaches):**
   * **Schedule:** Daytime and evening departures between **08:00 and 21:30** from `71 Tay Son`.
   * **2026 Ticket Fare:** **200,000 to 250,000 VND ($8.00 to $10.00 USD)**.

## Option 3: Private Car Transfer with Phu Yen & Vung Ro Stops

For groups of 3 to 5 passengers or travelers who want to see Phu Yen's volcanic coast without changing hotels in Tuy Hoa, booking a **one-way private car from Quy Nhon to Nha Trang** is the most flexible strategy.

* **Direct Point-to-Point Transfer (4 hours):** **2,000,000 to 2,300,000 VND ($80.00 to $92.00 USD)** for a 4-seater sedan or **2,300,000 to 2,600,000 VND ($92.00 to $104.00 USD)** for a 7-seater SUV/MPV (Toyota Fortuner, Mitsubishi Xpander, or Kia Carnival), inclusive of QL1A and Deo Ca Tunnel tolls (`65,000 to 115,000 VND`).
* **Full-Day Coastal Sightseeing Transfer (7.5 to 8.5 hours, 250 km):** **2,500,000 to 3,000,000 VND ($100.00 to $120.00 USD)**. Your driver follows the exact coastal route detailed in our [Quy Nhon to Phu Yen Coastal Drive](/plan/quy-nhon-to-phu-yen-coastal-drive/) guide, stopping at **Ganh Da Dia Basalt Columns** (`40,000 VND` / `$1.60 USD` entry), **O Loan Lagoon** for a blood-cockle and oyster lunch (`180,000 to 250,000 VND/kg`), **Nghinh Phong Tower** in Tuy Hoa (free entry), **Vung Ro Bay**, and **Mui Dien Lighthouse** (`20,000 VND` / `$0.80 USD` entry) before bypassing the Deo Ca tunnel to drive over the historic surface pass and down along **Dai Lanh Beach** into Nha Trang by 17:00.

## Step-by-Step Booking & Station Transfer Checklist

1. **Book Morning Daylight Slots 3 to 5 Days Ahead:** If you want left-side window seats on **Train SE5 (09:18)** or middle-row captain chairs on a morning **Sao Limousine** van, reserve online via `dsvn.vn` or `vexere.com` at least 72 hours in advance. Weekend runs fill quickly with domestic beach travelers from Binh Dinh and Khanh Hoa.
2. **Coordinate Bai Xep Pickups carefully:** If you are staying 13 km south of Quy Nhon in **Bai Xep Fishing Village**, do not backtrack 24 km north to Dieu Tri Railway Station unless you are taking *The Vietage*. Instead, arrange for a southbound limousine van (`220,000 to 280,000 VND`) to flag you down directly at the **Bai Xep welcome arch on Highway QL1D** 20 minutes after its `71 Tay Son` departure.
3. **Verify Nha Trang Arrival Points:** Train passengers arrive right downtown at `17 Thai Nguyen`, whereas standard highway coaches terminate 7 km inland at the Southern Bus Station on `23/10 Street` (`90,000 to 120,000 VND` / `$3.60 to $4.80 USD` Grab ride to Tran Phu Beach if you do not use Futa's complimentary transfer shuttle).
