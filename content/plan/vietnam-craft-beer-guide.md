# Vietnam Craft Beer Guide (2026): Breweries, Taps & Prices

Vietnam drinks more beer per capita than almost any nation in Southeast Asia, yet the glass in your hand splits into two distinct worlds. On neighborhood plastic stools, unpasteurized street **Bia hơi** costs **10,000 VND to 15,000 VND** ($0.40 to $0.60 USD) per mug, while ice-filled bottles of macro lagers like Saigon Red, Saigon Special, Hanoi Beer, Huda, and Larue sell for **20,000 VND to 35,000 VND** ($0.80 to $1.40 USD). Walk **300 m** from those sidewalk corners in [Ho Chi Minh City](/destinations/ho-chi-minh-city/), [Hanoi](/destinations/hanoi/), or [Da Nang](/destinations/da-nang/) and you enter Southeast Asia's premier craft microbrewery scene.

Since pioneering taprooms fired up their stainless-steel mash tuns between 2014 and 2026, local brewers have paired imported American Mosaic and Citra hops with indigenous Vietnamese botanicals: single-origin Marou cacao nibs, Phu Quoc black pepper, Dalat passionfruit, calamansi (**Tắc**), red dragon fruit, jasmine blossoms, and northern **Mắc mật** mountain leaves. Expect to pay **85,000 VND to 145,000 VND** ($3.40 to $5.80 USD) for a **330 ml to 400 ml** draft pour and **220,000 VND to 320,000 VND** ($8.80 to $12.80 USD) for a four-glass or six-glass tasting flight.

> **Field Note:** Vietnam enforces a strict **0.00% Blood Alcohol Concentration (BAC)** limit under **Decree 100/2019** and **Decree 168/2024/2026**. Traffic police (**CSGT**) set up evening breathalyzer checkpoints **500 m** outside major taproom districts between **20:00 and 23:30**. Riding a motorbike after even half a **4.5% ABV** sour ale triggers an immediate **6,000,000 VND to 8,000,000 VND** ($240 to $320 USD) fine and **7-day** vehicle impoundment. Leave your scooter at the hotel and book a **25,000 VND to 80,000 VND** ($1.00 to $3.20 USD) GrabBike or GrabCar.

## 2. Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon): The Birthplace of Vietnamese Craft Brewing

Southern brewhouses in [Ho Chi Minh City](/destinations/ho-chi-minh-city/) anchor Vietnam's craft beer output, with flagship production facilities in District 12 and Cu Chi supplying high-energy taprooms across District 1, Binh Thanh, and Thao Dien.

### District 1 Flagship Taprooms
- **Heart of Darkness Craft Brewery** (31D Ly Tu Trong, Ben Nghe, District 1 — **600 m** from the Saigon Opera House, open **11:00 to 00:00**): Named after Joseph Conrad's novella, this regional powerhouse exports to **8** Asian markets and pours **20+** rotating beers on tap. Order a **330 ml** glass of **Dream Alone Pale Ale** (**5.7% ABV**, **42 IBU**, **105,000 VND** / $4.20 USD) or test your palate against **Kurtz's Insane IPA** (**7.1% ABV**, **102 IBU**, **120,000 VND** / $4.80 USD) alongside **Conquistador Mexican Pilsner** infused with fresh calamansi (**4.7% ABV**, **95,000 VND** / $3.80 USD).
- **Pasteur Street Brewing Company (PSBC)** (144 Pasteur and 144/3 Pasteur, District 1 — **400 m** from Notre-Dame Cathedral; plus 120 Xuan Thuy in Thao Dien): The original 2015 pioneer of Vietnamese-American craft brewing. Climb the narrow **20-meter** staircase at the original 144 Pasteur alley taproom for a **6-glass** flight (**290,000 VND** / $11.60 USD). Must-orders include the flagship **Jasmine IPA** (**6.5% ABV**, **50 IBU**, **110,000 VND** / $4.40 USD), **Passion Fruit Wheat Ale** (**4.0% ABV**, **15 IBU**, **95,000 VND** / $3.80 USD), and the World Beer Cup gold-winning **Cyclo Imperial Chocolate Stout** (**11.0% ABV**, **65 IBU**, **135,000 VND** / $5.40 USD) brewed with hand-roasted Marou chocolate from Southern Vietnam.
- **East West Brewing Co.** (181-185 Ly Tu Trong, District 1 — **150 m** from Ben Thanh Market, open **11:00 to 23:30**): Housed inside a soaring industrial hall where **1,500-liter** copper and steel fermentation tanks sit directly behind the bar. Try the piney **Far East IPA** (**6.7% ABV**, **62 IBU**, **115,000 VND** / $4.60 USD), the crisp **Saigon Rosé** raspberry wheat (**3.5% ABV**, **95,000 VND** / $3.80 USD), or the **Coffee Vanilla Porter** (**6.0% ABV**, **110,000 VND** / $4.40 USD).

### Neighborhood Taprooms: Pham Viet Chanh, Da Kao & Thao Dien
- **Rooster Beers** (40E Pham Viet Chanh, Binh Thanh — **1.8 km** from District 1, plus 98 Nguyen Thi Minh Khai): Known for approachable, sessionable pours priced **15% to 20%** below tourist-center rooms. Pints of **Rooster Blonde** (**5.0% ABV**) and **Dark Principle Stout** run **75,000 VND to 95,000 VND** ($3.00 to $3.80 USD).
- **Belgo Belgian Craft Beer** (159A Nguyen Van Thu, Da Kao, District 1 — **1.2 km** north of the Reunification Palace): Brewed under a Belgian master brewer using abbey yeast strains. Order the **Belgo Amber** (**5.9% ABV**, **110,000 VND** / $4.40 USD) or **Cherry Blossom Witbier** (**4.7% ABV**, **105,000 VND** / $4.20 USD) paired with **160,000 VND** ($6.40 USD) Belgian fries.

## 3. Hanoi: Lakeside Microbreweries in Tay Ho & Truc Bach

In [Hanoi](/destinations/hanoi/), the craft beer scene clusters around the breezy shorelines of West Lake (**Hồ Tây**), Truc Bach Lake, and the historic alleys of Hoan Kiem, blending experimental northern herbs with panoramic water views.

- **Standing Bar** (170 Tran Vu, Truc Bach, Ba Dinh — **1.5 km** northwest of the Old Quarter, open **15:00 to 23:30**): Occupying a two-story corner overlooking Truc Bach Lake, Standing Bar operates **19 rotating taps** curated from independent northern and central brewers. Pours cost **85,000 VND to 130,000 VND** ($3.40 to $5.20 USD), with weekday happy hours from **16:00 to 19:00** knocking **25,000 VND** ($1.00 USD) off select pints.
- **Furbrew Craft Beer** (8B/52 To Ngoc Van, Quang An, Tay Ho — **5 km** north of Hoan Kiem Lake): Hanoi's boldest experimental microbrewery. Furbrew gained international fame for its **Phở Beer** (**5.2% ABV**, **115,000 VND** / $4.60 USD), infused with charred ginger, star anise, cinnamon, black cardamom, and coriander seed, alongside cloudy New England IPAs and sour goses brewed with northern **Mắc mật** leaves (**95,000 VND to 135,000 VND** / $3.80 to $5.40 USD).
- **7 Bridges Brewing Co. Hanoi Taproom** (74 Au Co, Tay Ho, plus Hoan Kiem taproom near Ly Thuong Kiet — **10 minutes** by taxi from the Opera House): Famous for its zero-waste brewing philosophy and bold hop profiles. Order the crimson-hued **Dragon IPA** (**6.7% ABV**, **55 IBU**, **110,000 VND** / $4.40 USD) brewed with red dragon fruit, or the resinous **Imperial IPA** (**9.0% ABV**, **90 IBU**, **135,000 VND** / $5.40 USD).
- **Turtle Lake Brewing Company** (105 Quang Khanh, Tay Ho — right on the West Lake water's edge) and **iBiero Vietnamese Craft Beer** (99 Le Duan, Hoan Kiem — **700 m** from Hanoi Railway Station): Ideal for sunset pints (**90,000 VND to 125,000 VND** / $3.60 to $5.00 USD) and seasonal pomelo ales.

## 4. Da Nang & Hoi An: Coastal Taprooms & Beachfront Rooftops

Central Vietnam launched Vietnam's zero-impact craft movement. In [Da Nang](/destinations/da-nang/), head to **7 Bridges Da Nang Beach Taproom** in the **My An / An Thuong** neighborhood (**250 m** from My Khe Beach, open **14:00 to 23:30**), where a four-story rooftop terrace looks out toward the Son Tra Peninsula while pouring fresh **Beach Blonde Ale** (**4.5% ABV**, **85,000 VND** / $3.40 USD) and **Sunset Tangerine Wheat** (**95,000 VND** / $3.80 USD). Down the street in An Thuong, **Brewman** and **East West / Heart of Darkness partner bars** serve chilled **330 ml** cans and draft pints for **80,000 VND to 120,000 VND** ($3.20 to $4.80 USD).

Thirty minutes south in [Hoi An](/destinations/hoi-an/) (**28 km** down the coast), **Pasteur Street Brewing Co. Hoi An Taproom** and **7 Bridges Hoi An** sit within **300 m** of the Japanese Covered Bridge, offering air-conditioned relief after a **2-hour** walking tour with **240,000 VND** ($9.60 USD) four-beer flights.

## 5. Practical Protocol: Taproom Etiquette, "++" Pricing & Zero-BAC Transport

Follow three operational rules to avoid bill surprises and police fines during your taproom crawl:

1. **Decode the `++` Menu Tax Rule**: Roughly **60%** of upscale craft taprooms in District 1 and Tay Ho print prices in thousands (e.g., `110++`) and append a **5% service charge** plus **8% to 10% government VAT** at the bottom of the menu. A **110,000 VND** ($4.40 USD) IPA and **180,000 VND** ($7.20 USD) burger billed as `++` will total **334,950 VND** ($13.40 USD) at checkout. Tipping on top of a **5% service charge** is entirely optional.
2. **Leverage Happy Hour Windows (15:00 to 19:00)**: Almost every brewery runs a daily **3-hour to 4-hour** afternoon promo offering **Buy-1-Get-1-Free** select cores, **75,000 VND** ($3.00 USD) flat pints, or free bar snacks within **15 minutes** of arrival.
3. **Strict Zero-Tolerance BAC Transport**: Never drive a rented scooter to a brewery. Even **200 ml** of beer registers above **0.00% BAC** on police digital breathalyzers. Hail a GrabCar (**45,000 VND to 95,000 VND** / $1.80 to $3.80 USD for a **3 km to 5 km** ride) directly from the taproom curb.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a pint of craft beer cost in Vietnam in 2026?
A standard **330 ml to 400 ml** pour at a Vietnamese craft taproom costs **85,000 VND to 145,000 VND** ($3.40 to $5.80 USD), while a **4-glass** or **6-glass** tasting flight runs **220,000 VND to 320,000 VND** ($8.80 to $12.80 USD). That is roughly **4 to 8 times** the price of a **25,000 VND** ($1.00 USD) bottle of Saigon Special, reflecting imported specialty malts and cold-chain storage.

### What is the legal drinking age and blood alcohol limit in Vietnam?
Vietnam sets the legal alcohol purchasing and drinking age at **18 years old**. For drivers of motorbikes, scooters, and automobiles, the legal blood alcohol concentration limit is **0.00% BAC** (**zero tolerance**), enforced year-round with fines starting at **6,000,000 VND** ($240 USD) for two-wheelers and up to **40,000,000 VND** ($1,600 USD) for cars.

### Can I buy Vietnamese craft beer in cans to bring home or drink at my hotel?
Yes. Pasteur Street, Heart of Darkness, East West, 7 Bridges, and Rooster sell **330 ml** cans directly from their taproom fridges and through Annam Gourmet supermarkets for **55,000 VND to 95,000 VND** ($2.20 to $3.80 USD) per can, or **320,000 VND to 520,000 VND** ($12.80 to $20.80 USD) for a mixed **6-pack**.
