# Da Nang Airport (DAD) to Hoi An & My Khe Beach Transfer Guide (2026): Private Car, Grab & Shuttle Bus

Hoi An does not have its own commercial airport or railway station. Every domestic and international traveler bound for the lantern-lit port town (`Phố Cổ Hội An`), **An Bang Beach (`Biển An Bàng`)**, or the coastal resorts of central Vietnam lands at **Da Nang International Airport (`DAD`)**, located right inside the heart of Da Nang city.

From the terminal gates of `DAD`, **My Khe Beach (`Biển Mỹ Khê`)** is a mere **`5.5 kilometers` (`12 to 15 minutes`)** east across the Dragon Bridge (`Cầu Rồng`), while **Hoi An Ancient Town** sits **`28 to 30 kilometers` (`40 to 50 minutes`)** south down the palm-fringed **`Võ Nguyên Giáp – Trường Sa – Lạc Long Quân` coastal highway**. Unlike Hanoi (`HAN`) or Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`), where Grab is almost always the cheapest private car option, the **Da Nang-to-Hoi An corridor has a unique economic quirk**: because hundreds of Hoi An-based private cars drop passengers at `DAD` every hour and need a paying fare for the return leg south, **pre-booking a private 4-seater car (`250,000–320,000 VND` / `$10.00–$12.80 USD`) is actually `15% to 25%` cheaper than hailing a live GrabCar (`340,000–410,000 VND` / `$13.60–$16.40 USD`)**, and includes an arrival gate name-board greeting. Here is the complete 2026 breakdown of every transfer option.

## Option 1: Pre-Booked Private Car (`250,000–320,000 VND`) — Why It Beats Grab to Hoi An

If your final destination is **Hoi An (`Old Town`, `Cam Chau`, `Cam Thanh`, `An Bang Beach`, or `Cua Dai`)**, booking a private car 12 to 24 hours ahead via Klook, a local Hoi An transport company (`Hoi An Private Car`, `VM Car`), or your hotel (`250,000 to 320,000 VND` / `$10.00–$12.80 USD` for a 4-seater sedan; `330,000 to 390,000 VND` / `$13.20–$15.60 USD` for a 7-seater SUV) outperforms hailing a live Grab at the airport for three concrete operational reasons:
1. **Empty Return-Leg Economics (`15%–25%` Cheaper)**: Hundreds of Hoi An drivers bring morning and afternoon flyers up to `DAD`. Rather than driving `30 km` back to Hoi An with an empty trunk, local dispatchers sell the return leg at `250,000–300,000 VND` all-inclusive—whereas the Grab algorithm charges full one-way inter-provincial pricing (`340,000–410,000 VND`) plus the `10,000 VND` airport exit toll.
2. **Curbside Name-Board Pickup (Saves the `120-Meter` Walk)**: At Da Nang Airport, app-dispatched Grab cars are prohibited from stopping directly in front of the T1 or T2 terminal exit doors; you must wheel your luggage `120 to 150 meters` across the road into the outdoor ride-hailing lot. A pre-booked private driver greets you **directly outside the T1 Domestic or T2 International arrival sliding doors** with a printed name card and loads your bags immediately.
3. **Zero Late-Night Surge Pricing**: If you land on a late evening flight from Seoul (`ICN`), Bangkok (`BKK`), Singapore (`SIN`), or Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`) after `22:00`, live Grab availability drops and surge multipliers push fares past `450,000 VND`. Your pre-booked fare remains locked in.

> [!TIP]
> **The Marble Mountains (`Ngũ Hành Sơn`) Stopover Hack**: The coastal highway from Da Nang Airport to Hoi An passes directly beside the dramatic limestone caves and pagodas of **Marble Mountains (`Ngũ Hành Sơn`)**—roughly `11 km` (`18 minutes`) south of the airport and `18 km` north of Hoi An. Instead of checking into your Hoi An hotel and paying `400,000 VND` round-trip to backtrack later in the week, ask your pre-booked airport driver to add a **90-minute sightseeing stop at Marble Mountains (`Non Nước`)** on the way from `DAD` to Hoi An. Drivers charge just **`100,000 to 150,000 VND` (`~$4.00–$6.00 USD`) extra waiting fee** and safely guard all your suitcases inside the locked trunk while you climb up via the glass elevator (`15,000 VND` elevator + `40,000 VND` entrance ticket).

## Option 2: GrabCar & Xanh SM Electric Taxis — Best for My Khe Beach & Da Nang City

If you are staying in **Da Nang City (`Han Riverfront` / `Hai Chau`)** or across the bridges at **My Khe Beach (`Phước Mỹ` / `An Thượng` tourist quarter)** or **Son Tra Peninsula**, **GrabCar** and **Xanh SM (`GSM` cyan-blue VinFast electric taxis)** are unbeatable:
- **Cost to Dragon Bridge / Han Market (`3.5 km`)**: **`55,000 to 80,000 VND` (`~$2.20–$3.20 USD`)** including the `10,000 VND` airport gate toll (`8–10 minutes`).
- **Cost to My Khe Beach / An Thuong (`5.5–6.5 km`)**: **`85,000 to 125,000 VND` (`~$3.40–$5.00 USD`)** (`12–15 minutes`).
- **Cost to Hoi An / An Bang Beach (`28–30 km`)**: **`340,000 to 410,000 VND` (`~$13.60–$16.40 USD`)** for a 4-seater or **`420,000 to 520,000 VND`** for a 7-seater (`40–50 minutes`).

### How to Walk Between `T1 Domestic` and `T2 International` & Find the Grab Lot
1. **Terminal Layout**: Da Nang Airport is one of the easiest airports in Asia to navigate. **Terminal 1 (`Domestic`)** and **Terminal 2 (`International`)** sit side-by-side under a continuous covered walkway—walking between T1 and T2 takes **less than `4 minutes` (`250 meters`)** on flat pavement.
2. **Where to Board Grab / Xanh SM**: Exit either T1 or T2 arrivals, ignore the touts standing by the pillars, cross the inner taxi lane via the zebra crossing, and follow the green overhead signs (`120 meters` straight ahead) into the **Designated Ride-Hailing Pickup Lot (`Bãi Đón Xe Công Nghệ`)**. Match your driver's **yellow commercial license plate (`biển số vàng`)** before boarding, and never accept a driver's offer to "cancel the booking in the app and pay cash."

## Option 3: Shared Airport Shuttle Buses (`130,000–150,000 VND`) — Best for Solo Travelers

If you are traveling **solo** to Hoi An during the daytime (`07:00–22:00`), a private car costs `$11–$13 USD`, whereas a **shared 16-seat Ford Transit or Hyundai Solati limousine shuttle** costs less than half that price and still drops you off directly at your hotel reception:
- **Top Operators**: **Barri Ann Travel** and **Hoi An Express Shuttle**.
- **2026 Price**: **`130,000 to 150,000 VND` (`~$5.20–$6.00 USD`) per passenger**, including one `24-inch` suitcase and one carry-on bag.
- **Frequency & Duration**: Departs every **`60 to 75 minutes`** between `07:00` and `22:00`. Total journey time is **`55 to 70 minutes`** depending on whether your hotel is first or third in the drop-off sequence.
- **Breakeven Math**: For **1 solo traveler**, the `140,000 VND` shuttle saves you `~150,000 VND` (`$6 USD`). For **2 or more travelers**, two shuttle tickets (`280,000–300,000 VND`) cost the **exact same price as a private 4-seater car (`280,000–320,000 VND`)**—so couples and friends should always book a private car and skip the `30–45 minute` shuttle departure wait.

## Crucial Hoi An Rule: Ancient Town Pedestrian Zone Curfews & Public Bus `LK02` Reality Check

Automobiles—including Grab, Xanh SM, metered taxis, and private hotel sedans—are **permanently banned from driving onto the narrow heritage streets inside Hoi An's UNESCO Ancient Town core** (`Trần Phú`, `Nguyễn Thái Học`, `Bạch Đằng`, `Công Nữ Ngọc Hoa`, and the Japanese Covered Bridge precinct). Furthermore, motorcycles and cyclos are barred during daily pedestrian hours (**`09:00–11:00`** and **`15:00–21:30`**).

If your boutique hotel, tailor shop, or homestay is located inside the pedestrian perimeter or across the footbridge on **An Hoi Islet (`Nguyễn Phúc Chu`)**, your airport driver will legally drop you at the nearest perimeter barrier—such as **`Hai Bà Trưng / Phan Châu Trinh`**, **`Lý Thường Kiệt`**, or **`Ngô Quyền / Cầu An Hội`**. From the barrier, your lobby is a flat `100 to 250-meter` walk; most Ancient Town hotels provide a complimentary electric buggy or luggage porter if you send your WhatsApp or Zalo ETA when leaving Da Nang Airport.

What about the old yellow Public Bus #1 mentioned on older backpacking blogs? Be aware that **old Bus #1 was suspended and reorganized into the inter-provincial `LK02` bus route** (`Da Nang VKU University <-> Hoi An Cua Dai`). Because `LK02` does **not** enter Da Nang Airport (`DAD`), riding it requires paying `70,000–100,000 VND` for a taxi out of the airport to a suburban stop, waiting `25 minutes` in the tropical sun, and paying a second taxi from Hoi An's outer bus station to your hotel—making it neither cheap nor practical when carrying luggage.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Luggage Capacity Warning (`4-Seater` Sedan vs `7-Seater` SUV)**: In central Vietnam, standard 4-seater cars (`Toyota Vios`, `Hyundai Accent`, `Kia Soluto`, `VinFast VF 5`) have compact rear trunks that can only accommodate **two `24-inch` medium suitcases** plus soft backpacks on the cabin floor. If you are a couple traveling with **two oversized `28-inch` hard-shell suitcases**, or a group of **3 to 4 passengers**, always select a **7-seater SUV (`330,000–390,000 VND` / `$13.20–$15.60 USD`)** when pre-booking so the third-row seats can be folded flat for your baggage.

## Zone-by-Zone Distance & Fare Guide: Where Are You Staying in Da Nang or Hoi An?

Before booking your transfer, check which coastal micro-zone your accommodation sits in along the `30 km` resort strip:
1. **My Khe Beach & An Thuong Quarter (`Đà Nẵng` — `5.5 to 6.5 km`, `12–15 minutes`)**: Located immediately east of the Han River across the Dragon Bridge (`Cầu Rồng`) or Tran Thi Ly Bridge. Best reached via **live GrabCar or Xanh SM (`85,000–125,000 VND` / `$3.40–$5.00 USD`)** from the airport ride-hailing lot.
2. **Non Nuoc Beach & Marble Mountains Resorts (`Đà Nẵng South` — `12 to 15 km`, `20–25 minutes`)**: Home to five-star beachfront properties (`Hyatt Regency`, `Melia`, `Sheraton Grand`, `Marriott Non Nuoc`). Costs **`160,000–220,000 VND` (`$6.40–$8.80 USD`)** via GrabCar or pre-booked sedan.
3. **An Bang Beach & Tra Que Vegetable Village (`Hội An North` — `24 to 26 km`, `35–42 minutes`)**: Entering Quang Nam Province along `Lạc Long Quân` coastal road, An Bang Beach is actually **`4 km` closer to Da Nang Airport than Hoi An Ancient Town**. Pre-booked 4-seater cars charge **`250,000–300,000 VND` (`$10.00–$12.00 USD`)**, dropping you at the main An Bang beach car park (`Hai Bà Trưng` beach access lane) where resort staff meet you with luggage carts.
4. **Hoi An Ancient Town, Cam Pho & An Hoi (`28 to 30 km`, `40–50 minutes`)**: The historic heritage center; **`250,000–320,000 VND` (`$10.00–$12.80 USD`)** by pre-booked private car or **`340,000–410,000 VND`** by live GrabCar.
5. **Cam Thanh Coconut Village & Cua Dai Beach (`Hội An East` — `31 to 34 km`, `45–55 minutes`)**: Located southeast of the Ancient Town toward the Thu Bon River estuary and Cham Islands ferry pier (`Cửa Đại`). Some private car operators add a small **`30,000–50,000 VND` (`$1.20–$2.00 USD`) distance supplement** (`total ~300,000–350,000 VND`) for villas deep inside the Cam Thanh nipa palm waterways.

## Returning from Hoi An to Da Nang Airport (`DAD`): Early-Morning Flight Strategy

When your stay in Hoi An ends and you need to catch a morning departure (`06:00–08:30 AM`) out of Da Nang International Airport (`DAD`), **never rely on waking up at `04:00 AM` and pressing "Book" on the Grab app**. Unlike Da Nang city center, very few Grab or Xanh SM drivers roam the quiet streets of Hoi An between `03:30 AM` and `05:30 AM`, and those who are online frequently ignore early-morning airport pings.

Instead, book your return airport sedan through your Hoi An hotel reception or your original private car WhatsApp dispatcher by **`18:00` the evening before** (`250,000–300,000 VND` for a 4-seater). Leave Hoi An at least **2.5 to 3 hours before domestic flights (`T1`)** and **3.5 hours before international flights (`T2`)** to allow `45 minutes` for the coastal drive and `45–60 minutes` for airline bag drop and security screening at `DAD`.

## Step-by-Step HowTo: Getting from Da Nang Airport (`DAD`) to Hoi An or My Khe Beach

### Step 1: Choose Between Private Car (`Hoi An`) vs Grab (`My Khe Beach`)
- **Heading to Hoi An / An Bang Beach (2+ people or late night)**: Pre-book a private 4-seater (`250k–320k VND`) or 7-seater (`330k–390k VND`) at least 12 hours before takeoff and provide your flight number (`VN`, `VJ`, `SQ`, `CX`, `AK`).
- **Heading to Hoi An Solo (`07:00–21:30`)**: Reserve a seat on the **Barri Ann / Hoi An Express Shuttle** (`130k–150k VND`) with your hotel address.
- **Heading to My Khe Beach / Da Nang City (`5.5 km`)**: Book a live **GrabCar** or **Xanh SM electric taxi** (`85k–125k VND`) upon exiting the terminal.

### Step 2: Clear Customs, Grab Cash & Meet Your Driver
Exit Terminal 1 (`Domestic`) or Terminal 2 (`International`). If you pre-booked a private car, look for your printed name sign immediately outside the arrival gate canopy. If using Grab or Xanh SM, walk `120 meters` across the zebra crossing into the official **Ride-Hailing Parking Lot (`Bãi Đón Xe Công Nghệ`)** and match the yellow license plate.

### Step 3: Enjoy the Coastal Drive (and Optional Marble Mountains Stop)
Ask your driver to take the scenic **`Võ Nguyên Giáp – Trường Sa` beachfront boulevard** past My Khe Beach and Non Nuoc Beach rather than the inland Highway `QL1A` truck route.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How much is a Grab vs a private car from Da Nang Airport (`DAD`) to Hoi An in 2026?
A pre-booked **private 4-seater car** from Da Nang Airport (`DAD`) to Hoi An costs **`250,000 to 320,000 VND` (`~$10.00–$12.80 USD`)** all-inclusive with arrival gate pickup, whereas a live **GrabCar 4-seater** or **Xanh SM taxi** booked in the app costs **`340,000 to 410,000 VND` (`~$13.60–$16.40 USD`)** plus a `120-meter` walk to the outdoor ride-hailing lot.

### How long does it take to get from Da Nang Airport to Hoi An and An Bang Beach?
The `28 to 30 km` drive from Da Nang International Airport (`DAD`) to **Hoi An Ancient Town** or **An Bang Beach** takes **`40 to 50 minutes`** via the coastal boulevard (`Võ Nguyên Giáp – Trường Sa`). Allow **3 hours before domestic flights** and **3.5 to 4 hours before international flights** when departing Hoi An for the airport.

### Can I stop at Marble Mountains (`Ngũ Hành Sơn`) on the way from Da Nang Airport to Hoi An?
Yes. Marble Mountains sits directly on the coastal road halfway between Da Nang Airport and Hoi An (`18 minutes` south of `DAD`). Most pre-booked private car operators let you add a **60 to 90-minute sightseeing stop at Marble Mountains** for an extra **`100,000 to 150,000 VND` (`~$4–$6 USD`)** while the driver watches your luggage in the locked car.

### How far is My Khe Beach from Da Nang Airport (`DAD`)?
**My Khe Beach (`Biển Mỹ Khê`)** and the **An Thuong (`An Thượng`)** expat/traveler neighborhood are only **`5.5 to 6.5 kilometers` (`12 to 15 minutes`)** east of Da Nang Airport across the Han River, costing **`85,000 to 125,000 VND` (`~$3.40–$5.00 USD`)** via GrabCar or Xanh SM.
