Moving north from the former imperial capital of Hue to Dong Hoi—the coastal capital of Quang Binh Province—covers 166 km (103 miles) on the North-South Railway or 165 km (102.5 miles) by road via National Highway QL1A and the Cam Lo–La Son Expressway (CT01). While many travelers treat Dong Hoi strictly as a rail and airport hub for [Hue to Phong Nha Transport](/plan/hue-to-phong-nha-transport/), the city itself anchors Nhat Le Beach, the Bao Ninh sand peninsula, and Dong Hoi Airport (VDH).

Three primary transport modes serve this three-hour northbound corridor in 2026. Reunification Express trains operated by Vietnam Railways (dsvn.vn) run directly from central Hue to Dong Hoi Railway Station in 2 hours 45 minutes to 3 hours 15 minutes for 125,000–175,000 VND ($5.00–$7.00 USD) in an air-conditioned soft seat. Shared 9-seat limousine vans and interprovincial coaches cover Highway QL1A in 3 to 3.5 hours for 180,000–250,000 VND ($7.20–$10.00 USD). Direct private cars cost 1,700,000–2,100,000 VND ($68.00–$84.00 USD) point-to-point in 2 hours 45 minutes, or 2,200,000–2,600,000 VND ($88.00–$104.00 USD) via the Demilitarized Zone (DMZ) at Hien Luong Bridge and the Vinh Moc Tunnels.

Trains win on price and legroom. Limousine vans win on door-to-door hotel convenience.

> [!TIP]
> **Daytime Rail Strategy:** Book Air-Conditioned Soft Seat (NML) on afternoon express trains SE4 (15:10) or SE2 (15:54) for 140,000–175,000 VND ($5.60–$7.00 USD). You avoid paying double for a sleeper berth on a 2 hour 50 minute daytime run while arriving at Dong Hoi Railway Station before 18:45 with daylight to reach Nhat Le Beach.

## Option 1: Reunification Express Trains (Best Overall Value)

The North-South Railway provides the most predictable transit between Hue and Dong Hoi. Unlike Highway QL1A—where container trucks and October–November autumn rainstorms slow road traffic—the 166 km rail line cuts straight across northern Thua Thien Hue, Quang Tri, and southern Quang Binh.

### Departure and Arrival Stations

Trains depart from **Hue Railway Station** (Ga Hue, 02 Bui Thi Xuan Street, Duc Ward), situated on the south bank of the Perfume River 1.8 km (1.1 miles) west of the Pham Ngu Lao tourist quarter and 2.2 km from the Imperial Citadel. A metered Mai Linh or Vinasun taxi or GrabCar from central Hue hotels takes 6 to 10 minutes and costs 40,000–65,000 VND ($1.60–$2.60 USD). Arrive at least 25 minutes before departure; platform gates open 15 to 20 minutes prior to boarding.

All northbound trains call at **Dong Hoi Railway Station** (Ga Dong Hoi, Sub-zone 4, Nam Ly Ward, Dong Hoi City). Because Dong Hoi is a major provincial railhead on the Hanoi–Saigon trunk line, every northbound SE express pauses here for 5 to 10 minutes to change crews. Stage your bags near the carriage door 10 minutes before arrival.

### 2026 Northbound Train Schedule (Hue to Dong Hoi)

Six Reunification Express services cover the northbound Hue-to-Dong Hoi sector daily. Because five originate 688 km south in Ho Chi Minh City (and SE20 originates 102 km south in Da Nang), minor cumulative delays of 10 to 25 minutes occasionally occur at Hue:

* **Train SE20:** Departs Hue at 00:55, arrives Dong Hoi at 04:08 (3 hours 13 minutes).
* **Train SE8:** Departs Hue at 02:17, arrives Dong Hoi at 05:22 (3 hours 05 minutes).
* **Train SE6:** Departs Hue at 04:49, arrives Dong Hoi at 08:13 (3 hours 24 minutes).
* **Train SE10:** Departs Hue at 12:15, arrives Dong Hoi at 15:18 (3 hours 03 minutes).
* **Train SE4:** Departs Hue at 15:10, arrives Dong Hoi at 18:06 (2 hours 56 minutes).
* **Train SE2:** Departs Hue at 15:54, arrives Dong Hoi at 18:41 (2 hours 47 minutes).

Skip pre-dawn SE20 and SE8 departures unless connecting to an early morning cave trek in Phong Nha. For travelers checking out of a Hue hotel after breakfast or lunch, **SE10 (12:15)**, **SE4 (15:10)**, and **SE2 (15:54)** offer the cleanest daytime schedules. Flagship consists SE4 and SE2 complete the trip in under 3 hours.

### Seat Classes and 2026 Fares

For a daytime journey under 3 hours, **Air-Conditioned Soft Seat (Ngoi Mem Dieu Hoa, code NML)** is the smartest booking choice. Tickets cost 125,000–175,000 VND ($5.00–$7.00 USD) depending on train tier. Soft-seat carriages feature 64 reclining seats in a 2+2 layout, overhead racks for 55 cm suitcases, and 220V wall sockets.

If you board SE6 (04:49) or prefer to lie flat, sleeper berths are available on every train:
* **6-Berth Soft Sleeper (Nam Khoang 6, code BnLT):** 220,000–275,000 VND ($8.80–$11.00 USD). Bottom berths (Tier 1) cost 25,000 VND ($1.00 USD) more than middle (Tier 2) or top (Tier 3) bunks.
* **4-Berth Soft Sleeper (Nam Khoang 4, code AnLT):** 285,000–350,000 VND ($11.40–$14.00 USD). Quieter four-bunk compartments with reading lamps, favored by travelers continuing on [Hanoi to Hue Transport](/plan/hanoi-to-hue-transport/).

Book directly on the official Vietnam Railways portal (dsvn.vn) or through Baolau, 12Go Asia, or Vexere (which add a 25,000–45,000 VND / $1.00–$1.80 USD service fee). Smartphone QR tickets scan directly at the gate.

## Option 2: Shared 9-Seat Limousine Vans & Highway 1A Buses

Travelers staying on the Nhat Le beachfront or Bao Ninh peninsula who want to skip station taxis can book a shared **9-seat Ford Transit or Hyundai Solati limousine van** or interprovincial coach.

Official coaches depart from **Hue Northern Bus Station** (Ben Xe Phia Bac Hue, 132 Ly Thai To Street, An Hoa Ward), 5.5 km (3.4 miles) northwest of the tourist center (75,000–105,000 VND / $3.00–$4.20 USD by GrabCar, 15 minutes). However, limousine operators including **Tan Nhat**, **Hung Thanh**, **Queen Cafe**, and **Camel Travel** provide complimentary hotel pickups across Hue's Phu Hoi and Vinh Ninh wards or board at offices on Hung Vuong and Chu Van An streets.

Vehicles run north via **National Highway QL1A** through Dong Ha (165 km) or the **Cam Lo–La Son Expressway (CT01)**, taking 3 to 3.5 hours including a 15-minute rest stop:
* **9-Seat VIP Limousine Van:** 200,000–250,000 VND ($8.00–$10.00 USD) per seat with leather recliners and USB ports.
* **Open-Tour Sleeper Coach:** 180,000–220,000 VND ($7.20–$8.80 USD) per berth. Because northbound sleeper coaches primarily serve Phong Nha or [Hanoi to Phong Nha Transport](/plan/hanoi-to-phong-nha-transport/), they often drop Dong Hoi passengers along the **Dong Hoi QL1A Bypass** (Ly Thanh Tong Street) or **Nam Ly Bus Station**, requiring a 60,000–90,000 VND ($2.40–$3.60 USD) taxi to the beach. Confirm central hotel drop-off when booking.

## Option 3: Private Car Transfer (Direct vs. DMZ Sightseeing Route)

Hiring a private vehicle delivers door-to-door comfort and the option to tour Vietnam's Demilitarized Zone (DMZ) en route.

### Direct Express Transfer (2 hours 45 minutes)

Via the Cam Lo–La Son Expressway (CT01) and Highway QL1A, a direct private car covers the 165 km in 2 hours 45 minutes to 3 hours (fuel and tolls included):
* **4-Seat Sedan (Toyota Vios, Honda City):** 1,700,000–1,850,000 VND ($68.00–$74.00 USD) for 1–2 passengers.
* **7-Seat SUV (Toyota Innova, Xpander, Fortuner):** 1,900,000–2,100,000 VND ($76.00–$84.00 USD) for 3–4 passengers.
* **16-Seat Minibus (Ford Transit):** 2,400,000–2,700,000 VND ($96.00–$108.00 USD) for larger groups.

### Northbound DMZ Sightseeing Transfer (5.5 to 6.5 hours)

Because the 17th Parallel sits at the Ben Hai River (95 km north of Hue and 70 km south of Dong Hoi), upgrading to a **DMZ sightseeing car** costs 2,200,000–2,600,000 VND ($88.00–$104.00 USD) and follows a clean northbound timeline:
1. **08:00 – Depart Hue Hotel:** Drive 55 km north (1 hour 10 minutes) into Quang Tri.
2. **09:10 – La Vang Basilica (Hai Lang District):** A 30-minute stop at the Catholic pilgrimage center (free entry).
3. **10:15 – Hien Luong Bridge & Ben Hai River (17th Parallel):** Spend 45 minutes walking the demarcation bridge (50,000 VND / $2.00 USD ticket).
4. **11:25 – Vinh Moc Tunnels (Vinh Linh District):** Detour 13 km east to inspect the three-tier coastal tunnel village carved 12 to 23 meters underground (50,000 VND / $2.00 USD ticket; 60 minutes).
5. **14:00 – Arrive Dong Hoi City:** Drive the final 65 km (1 hour 15 minutes) north to your Nhat Le Beach hotel.

## Arriving in Dong Hoi: Onward Connections & Frequently Asked Questions

From **Dong Hoi Railway Station (Sub-zone 4, Nam Ly Ward)**, onward transfers split into two directions:

* **To Dong Hoi Center & Nhat Le Beach (4.5 to 6 km):** Metered **Mai Linh (0232 385 8585)**, **Sun Taxi (0232 368 6868)**, and **Xanh SM electric taxis** wait outside the exit. A ride to central Dong Hoi (Tran Hung Dao Street) takes 8 minutes and costs 55,000–75,000 VND ($2.20–$3.00 USD). Crossing the bridge to **Nhat Le Beach (Truong Phap Street)** or **Bao Ninh Peninsula (Vo Nguyen Giap Street)** covers 5 to 7.5 km (12 to 15 minutes) for 70,000–100,000 VND ($2.80–$4.00 USD).
* **To Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park (45 km northwest):** A private 4-seat taxi from Dong Hoi Station along Highway DT20 to Son Trach takes 45 to 55 minutes and costs 500,000–600,000 VND ($20.00–$24.00 USD). Budget travelers can take a 35,000 VND ($1.40 USD) taxi 2.2 km east to **Nam Ly Bus Station (Tran Hung Dao Street)** to board **Quang Binh Public Bus B4**, departing every 45 to 60 minutes between 05:30 and 17:30 (75 minutes, 35,000–50,000 VND / $1.40–$2.00 USD cash onboard). For southbound return schedules, see [Dong Hoi to Hue Transport](/plan/dong-hoi-to-hue-transport/).
