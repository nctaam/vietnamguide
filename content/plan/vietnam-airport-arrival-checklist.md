# Vietnam Airport Arrival Checklist: Step-by-Step Terminal Protocol for 2026

Your first forty-five minutes inside a Vietnamese international terminal dictate how smoothly your trip begins. Stepping off a widebody jet into Hanoi Noi Bai (`HAN` Terminal 2), Ho Chi Minh City Tan Son Nhat (`SGN` Terminal 2), Da Nang (`DAD` Terminal 2), Cam Ranh (`CXR`), or Phu Quoc (`PQC`) places you in front of five consecutive checkpoints: passport control, customs X-ray screening, local currency withdrawal, cellular activation, and curb transport dispatch. Miss a single detail—such as a misdated purple entry stamp, a blue 500,000 VND banknote mistaken for a 20,000 VND bill, or a curb tout impersonating an app driver—and you lose an hour or $30.00 USD before reaching your hotel. Follow this chronological checklist from the jetbridge to your hotel lobby.

## Step 1: Immigration Desk, Visa Verification & Fast-Track Triage

Walk straight from the arrival gate down the sterile corridor to the Immigration Hall (`Công an Cửa khẩu`). Keep your boarding pass tucked inside your passport; border officers regularly inspect the flight number printed on the stub.

1. **Passport Validity Check**: Your passport must hold at least **6 months of remaining validity** (`>= 6 months`) beyond your arrival date and two blank pages.
2. **90-Day Electronic Visa (`e-Visa`)**: Travelers with an official 90-day e-Visa from `evisa.gov.vn` ($25.00 USD single-entry or $50.00 USD multiple-entry) skip the Landing Visa counter and join the **"All Passports" (`Hộ chiếu nước ngoài`)** queue. Present a paper printout showing your registration code, full name, and designated entry port (`Noi Bai`, `Tan Son Nhat`, `Da Nang`, `Cam Ranh`, or `Phu Quoc`).
3. **45-Day Unilateral Visa Exemption**: Citizens of **13 countries**—the **UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland, Russia, Japan, South Korea, and Belarus**—receive a **45-day visa-free entry stamp** at zero cost ($0.00 USD). ASEAN citizens receive 14 to 30 days.
4. **30-Day Phu Quoc (`PQC`) Direct-Flight Exemption**: Any foreign passport holder flying directly into **Phu Quoc (`PQC`)** from abroad—or transiting `HAN` or `SGN` airside without entering mainland Vietnam—receives a **30-day island-only visa exemption**. Visiting mainland Vietnam afterward requires holding a valid e-Visa before leaving the island.
5. **When Fast-Track ($25–$40 USD) Makes Sense**: At `SGN` T2 and `HAN` T2, queues surge during peak widebody arrival banks (**10:00 to 14:00** and **21:00 to 23:30**), when standard lines take 45 to 75 minutes. Pre-booking VIP Fast-Track ($25.00 to $40.00 USD) puts an agent at the escalator to escort you through the priority lane in 10 to 15 minutes. Outside peak hours, skip Fast-Track.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Inspect Your Purple Stamp Before Stepping Away:** Do not leave the immigration booth until you read the purple ink stamp inside your passport. Verify the **"Permitted to remain until" (`Được phép tạm trú đến ngày`)** date (`DD / MM / YYYY`). During rush hours, officers occasionally stamp a 45-day waiver date into a 90-day e-Visa holder's passport by mistake. Pointing it out at the glass booth takes 30 seconds to correct; discovering it weeks later triggers `500,000 VND to 2,000,000 VND` (`$20.00–$80.00 USD`) per-day overstay fines.

## Step 2: Baggage Claim & Customs Green/Red Channel Rules

Down the escalators past passport control, electronic monitors list your baggage carousel. Retrieve your checked bags and approach the final Customs (`Hải quan`) X-ray machines. Every passenger—even in the **Green Channel (`Luồng Xanh` — Nothing to Declare)**—must place all suitcases and cabin backpacks onto the X-ray conveyor belt before exiting the sliding glass doors.

* **Duty-Free Allowances**: Adults aged 18 and over may import up to **1.5 liters of spirits** (`>= 20% ABV`), OR **2.0 liters of wine** (`< 20% ABV`), OR **3.0 liters of beer**, plus up to **200 cigarettes** (or 20 cigars / 250 grams of loose tobacco). Exceeding these volumes requires declaring the surplus in the **Red Channel (`Luồng Đỏ`)**.
* **Cash Declaration Threshold**: Carrying more than **$5,000.00 USD** (or foreign currency equivalent) or more than **15,000,000 VND** in physical banknotes requires filing a Red Channel customs declaration form (`HQ/2015/KC`). Declaring carries 0 VND in tax and preserves your legal right to carry unspent currency above $5,000.00 USD back out of Vietnam.
* **Strict Ban on E-Cigarettes & Vapes (`Resolution 173/2024/QH15`)**: Under National Assembly **Resolution 173/2024/QH15** (enforced across all ports in 2025 and 2026), Vietnam enforces a complete ban on the import, possession, and use of **e-cigarettes, vapes, e-liquids, and heated tobacco devices (`IQOS`)**. Customs officers confiscate vape hardware spotted on exit X-rays and levy administrative fines starting from 1,000,000 VND to 2,000,000 VND ($40.00–$80.00 USD).
* **Strict Ban on Unregistered Drones (`UAV`)**: Camera drones (such as DJI Mini, Air, or Mavic units) require a prior flight permit from the **Ministry of National Defence**. Customs X-ray scanners at dual-use military/civilian airports (`DAD`, `HAN`, `SGN`) routinely impound unregistered drones at baggage exit and hold them in bonded airport storage until your outbound international flight.

## Step 3: First VND Cash Withdrawal & Polymer Note Strategy

Once through the sliding doors into the public landside arrivals hall, skip airside currency booths with 4% spreads and walk to the official bank ATMs along the outer concourse wall (Pillars 2 and 15 at `HAN` T2; far left and right wings at `SGN` T2).

1. **ATM Fees & Limits (`VPBank`, `TPBank`, `BIDV`, `Vietcombank`, `Agribank`)**: **VPBank** and **TPBank** charge **0 VND in local ATM fees** for foreign Visa and Mastercard debit cards and dispense **5,000,000 VND to 10,000,000 VND** ($200.00–$400.00 USD) per single withdrawal. State-owned banks (**Vietcombank, BIDV, Agribank**) cap withdrawals at **3,000,000 VND to 5,000,000 VND** ($120.00–$200.00 USD) per transaction and charge a **50,000 VND to 66,000 VND** ($2.00–$2.65 USD) local fee, but never run out of banknotes. Always choose **"Continue Without Conversion"** to reject 6% dynamic currency conversion markups.
2. **Breaking 500,000 VND Blue Notes**: Airport ATMs dispense crisp blue **500,000 VND** ($20.00 USD) polymer bills. Bus conductors on `#86` or `#109` and cab drivers paying 10,000 to 15,000 VND toll gates rarely carry large change. Buy a 25,000 VND bottle of water or iced coffee at an airport convenience store (`Circle K`, `Highlands Coffee`, or `BigBowl`) to break one 500,000 VND bill into **20,000 VND, 50,000 VND, and 100,000 VND notes**.
3. **The 500,000 VND vs 20,000 VND Color Confusion**: Both the **500,000 VND note** ($20.00 USD) and the **20,000 VND note** ($0.80 USD) are printed on cyan-blue polymer. Count the zeros under your phone flashlight before paying a driver at night.

## Step 4: SIM / eSIM Activation & The `*098#` Verification Check

Never step into ground transport without active mobile data—you need live GPS and the Grab app at the curb to verify your assigned car.

* **Pre-Installed Travel eSIMs vs Airport Kiosks**: If using a pre-installed eSIM (`Airalo`, `Nomad`, `Holafly`, or an official Viettel QR code), connect to free airport Wi-Fi, enable **Data Roaming**, and confirm 4G/5G bars appear. Register your Grab account with your home phone number and credit card *before* leaving home so you receive the SMS verification code effortlessly.
* **Airport Viettel & Vinaphone Kiosks**: Official **Viettel** (strongest nationwide mountain and island network) and **Vinaphone** counters in the arrival hall sell 15-to-30-day tourist data packages (5GB to 8GB per day) for **200,000 VND to 350,000 VND** ($8.00–$14.00 USD) after scanning your passport page.
* **Verify Before Walking Away**: Turn off airport Wi-Fi, load a browser page, and dial **`*098#`** (or open the **MyViettel** app) right at the counter to confirm your active data balance and expiration date before stepping outside.

## Step 5: Verified Ground Transport & Scam Defense

The 50 meters between the terminal exit doors and the pickup lanes are where most arrival scams happen. Follow these exact terminal coordinates:

```
TERMINAL EXIT TO VERIFIED TRANSPORT PROTOCOL
========================================================================
[Clear Customs X-Ray] ---> [Withdraw VND + Break 500k Note]
                                  |
                     [Verify 4G Data + Open Grab App]
                                  |
     +----------------------------+----------------------------+
     |                            |                            |
[HAN Terminal 2]          [SGN Terminal 2 Int'l]        [DAD Terminal 2]
Walk to Lane 2,           Turn LEFT outside doors ->    Cross arrival lane
Pillars 10-14             Walk to Domestic Car Park     to Ride-Hailing Lot
(or Bus #86 Pillar 2)     Lane D1/D2 or Int'l Pillar 12 (or pre-booked car)
```

* **Hanoi Noi Bai (`HAN` Terminal 2)**:
  - **Grab / Xanh SM**: Cross the first roadway to **Lane 2, Column 10–14**. A 4-seater GrabCar to the Hoan Kiem Old Quarter (28 km, 40 to 55 minutes) costs **300,000 VND to 380,000 VND** ($12.00–$15.20 USD), including the 10,000 to 15,000 VND airport exit toll.
  - **Express Bus `#86`**: Turn right outside Arrivals to **Pillar 2**. Orange **Express Bus `#86`** runs 07:00 to 22:00 every 25 to 30 minutes (45,000 VND cash onboard, interior luggage racks included) straight to Hoan Kiem Lake and Hanoi Railway Station.
  - **Domestic Transfer (`T2` to `T1`)**: Turn left to **Pillar 18** for the free green shuttle bus (0 VND, every 10 to 15 minutes).
* **Ho Chi Minh City Tan Son Nhat (`SGN` Terminal 2 International & T1/T3 Domestic)**:
  - **Grab / Xanh SM**: Check your app pin for **International Pillar 12** or walk left (3 minutes) along the covered walkway to the **Domestic Car Park (Lane D1/D2)**. Fare to District 1 (8 km, 25 to 45 minutes) is **120,000 VND to 180,000 VND** ($4.80–$7.20 USD) including the 10,000 VND gate toll. Free airport shuttle buses connect Terminal 2 and Terminal 1 with the new **Terminal 3 Domestic** concourse (15 minutes).
  - **Public Buses (`#109` & `#152`)**: Board yellow **Bus `#109`** (15,000 VND, 05:30 to 22:00) or green **Bus `#152`** (5,000 VND plus 5,000 VND per large suitcase, 05:30 to 19:00) straight to Ben Thanh Market and Pham Ngu Lao in District 1.
  - **Official Metered Taxis**: Across Vietnam's airports, board only **Vinasun** (`SGN`), **Mai Linh** (green cabs nationwide), **G7** (`HAN`), or **Xanh SM** (cyan VinFast electric taxis).
* **Da Nang (`DAD` Terminal 2)**: Cross the arrival lane to the Grab pickup lot (90,000 to 140,000 VND for the 5.5 km, 15-minute ride to My Khe Beach, or pre-book a 250,000 to 320,000 VND private car for the 29 km transfer to Hoi An).

> [!WARNING]
> **Defeating the "Fake Grab Driver Screenshot Scam":** At `SGN`, `HAN`, and `DAD` exit doors, curb touts wearing green jackets flash a static screenshot of the Grab logo, ask *"You book Grab?"*, glance at your screen fare, and claim *"My car is right here, same price"*—or grab your phone to tap **"Cancel Ride"** before leading you to an unlicensed car that demands `600,000 VND` at your hotel.
> **The Ironclad Rule:** Never hand your unlocked phone to anyone. Only board a car whose **physical metal license plate matches the exact alphanumeric plate displayed in your live Grab app**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to print my Vietnam e-Visa or can I show it on my phone?
Always carry **one crisp paper printout** of your 90-day e-Visa (`evisa.gov.vn`). Airline check-in counters and border officers at `HAN`, `SGN`, and `DAD` process paper sheets faster than phone screens.

### Should I tip immigration officers or customs staff at Vietnam airports?
**No.** Official immigration clearance at `HAN`, `SGN`, `DAD`, `CXR`, and `PQC` carries zero desk fee once your $25.00/$50.00 USD e-Visa or 45-day waiver is valid.

### What should I do if I arrive after midnight when buses and kiosks are closed?
For **00:00 to 05:00** arrivals, install a **travel eSIM** before flying, withdraw cash from 24-hour **VPBank or Vietcombank ATMs**, and hail a **24/7 GrabCar** (320,000 to 400,000 VND at `HAN`; 140,000 to 200,000 VND at `SGN`) or pre-book a hotel car ($15.00–$22.00 USD).

### Can I connect from an international arrival to a domestic flight in 90 minutes?
**No—allow at least 3 hours (180 minutes)** at `HAN` or `SGN` to clear immigration (20 to 60 minutes), claim bags, pass customs X-ray, ride the 15-minute shuttle to the Domestic Terminal (`T1` at `HAN`, `T1/T3` at `SGN`), and check in before domestic bag drop closes **40 minutes before departure**.
