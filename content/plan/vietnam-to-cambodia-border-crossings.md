# Vietnam to Cambodia Border Crossings: Routes & Visas (2026)

Vietnam and Cambodia share a `1,158 km` land and river frontier stretching from the Gulf of Thailand in Kien Giang Province north to the tri-border highlands of Kon Tum and Ratanakiri. Seven primary international checkpoints handle foreign tourist traffic across this boundary in 2026. Choosing the right gate depends on your origin city in southern or central Vietnam, your Cambodian destination, and whether you hold a pre-approved e-Visa or plan to purchase a Cambodian Visa on Arrival at the window.

Overland buses between Ho Chi Minh City and Phnom Penh funnel through Moc Bai – Bavet (`230 km`, `6 to 6.5 hours`, `$24 to $32 USD`). Travelers already exploring the Mekong Delta save hours of backtracking by boarding a morning speedboat from Chau Doc via Song Tien – Kaam Samnor (`135 km`, `5 hours`, `$35 to $65 USD`) or crossing the coastal Ha Tien – Prek Chak gate (`9 km` from Ha Tien town) straight into Kep and Kampot.

> **2026 Cross-Border Visa Alert:** Vietnam does not issue Visa on Arrival at any land or river border gate—you must hold a printed Vietnam 90-day e-Visa (`evisa.gov.vn`, `$25 USD` single / `$50 USD` multiple) or qualify for a visa exemption before approaching Vietnamese immigration. Entering Cambodia, both the Cambodia e-Visa (`evisa.gov.kh`, `$36 USD`) and Visa on Arrival (`$30 USD` cash) are accepted at six major gates, with the sole exception of Oyadav (Ratanakiri), which accepts Cambodian Visa on Arrival only.

## Detailed Breakdown of Major Cross-Border Corridors

### 1. Moc Bai (Tay Ninh) – Bavet (Svay Rieng): The Highway Express Corridor
Moc Bai – Bavet is the busiest overland crossing between Vietnam and Cambodia. Situated `70 km` northwest of District 1, Ho Chi Minh City, at the end of National Highway `QL22`, the checkpoint connects directly to Cambodia's National Road `NR1` running `160 km` west into Phnom Penh across the Neak Loeung (Tsubasa) Mekong Bridge.

Direct international coaches depart Ho Chi Minh City's Pham Ngu Lao backpacker district and Mien Dong / Mien Tay terminals between `06:30` and `15:30` daily. Total door-to-door journey time is `6 to 6.5 hours` (`230 km`).
- **Top Operators & 2026 Fares**: **Giant Ibis Transport** (`$28 to $32 USD` / `700,000 to 800,000 VND`, Wi-Fi, water, pastry, and vetted border assistance with zero hidden markup on e-Visas), **Mekong Express Limousine Bus** (`$26 to $30 USD` / `650,000 to 750,000 VND`), **Kumho Samco** (`$24 to $26 USD` / `600,000 to 650,000 VND`), and **Virak Buntham (VET Air Bus)** (`$24 to $28 USD`).
- **How Clearance Works**: Passengers unload all luggage for X-ray screening at the Moc Bai departure hall, get stamped out of Vietnam (`20 to 30 minutes`), re-board the coach for the `800-meter` drive past duty-free malls and Bavet casinos, and disembark at the Bavet immigration shed for Cambodian entry stamping and fingerprint scans (`25 to 40 minutes`).

### 2. Song Tien / Vinh Xuong (An Giang) – Kaam Samnor (Kandal): The Mekong River Ferry
For travelers linking the Mekong Delta with Phnom Penh, the international river corridor out of Chau Doc beats highway dust. High-speed boats depart Chau Doc (`01 Le Loi` or `10 Nguyen Cong Tru`) every morning at `07:00–07:30`, reaching Phnom Penh's Sisowath Quay (`Street 104`) around `12:30–13:30` (`5 to 5.5 hours` total).
- **2026 Operators & Fares**: **Hang Chau Tourist Express** (`$35 to $40 USD` / `880,000 to 1,000,000 VND`), **Mandarin / Blue Cruiser** (`$38 to $45 USD`), and **Victoria Speedboat** (`$48 to $65 USD` / `1,200,000 to 1,625,000 VND`).
- **Logistics**: Boats clear Vietnamese exit formalities on the bank of the Tien River at Song Tien (`Cửa khẩu Vĩnh Xương`) and motor `1.5 km` upstream to the floating pontoon at Kaam Samnor for Cambodian Visa on Arrival or e-Visa verification. Read our complete [Vietnam to Cambodia Boat Guide](/plan/vietnam-to-cambodia-boat-guide/) for pier locations, boat classes, and river checkpoint steps. To reach Chau Doc the afternoon before departure, review our [Ho Chi Minh City to Chau Doc Transport](/plan/ho-chi-minh-city-to-chau-doc-transport/) guide.

### 3. Ha Tien / Xa Xia (Kien Giang) – Prek Chak (Kampot): The Gulf of Thailand Coastal Gate
Located `9 km` northwest of Ha Tien town center and `11 km` from the Ha Tien Ferry Terminal (where Superdong and Phu Quoc Express catamarans arrive from Phu Quoc Island in `75 to 90 minutes` for `230,000 to 250,000 VND` / `$9.20 to $10.00 USD`), the Ha Tien (Xa Xia) – Prek Chak checkpoint is the gateway to Cambodia's southern coast.
- **Onward Distances & Fares from Prek Chak**: Once stamped into Cambodia at Prek Chak, shared minivans and private taxis wait outside the barrier. **Kep** (Crab Market and Kep Beach) is only `25 km` west (`30 to 40 minutes`, `$15 to $20 USD` for a private 4-seat taxi or `$7 to $9 USD` by minivan). **Kampot** lies `45 km` northwest (`55 to 70 minutes`, `$25 to $30 USD` private taxi or `$10 to $12 USD` minivan). **Sihanoukville** (for Koh Rong ferries) is `145 km` west along National Road `NR33` and `NR3` (`3 to 3.5 hours`, `$55 to $65 USD` private car or `$16 to $20 USD` shuttle).
- **Connecting from the Delta**: If you are traveling overland from Can Tho to Ha Tien before crossing into Kampot, see our [Can Tho to Ha Tien Transport](/plan/can-tho-to-ha-tien-transport/) guide for bus schedules (`200 km`, `4.5 to 5 hours`, `170,000 to 210,000 VND`).

### 4. Tinh Bien (An Giang) – Phnom Den (Takeo): The Chau Doc Overland Alternative
If you miss the `07:30` morning boats in Chau Doc or travel during low-water cancellations, **Tinh Bien – Phnom Den** sits just `25 km` southwest of Chau Doc along Highway `QL91` (`35 minutes` by Mai Linh taxi, `320,000 to 380,000 VND` / `$13 to $15 USD`). From the Cambodian side at Phnom Den, National Road `NR2` runs smoothly north through Takeo (`55 km`, `1 hour`) to Phnom Penh (`125 km`, `2.5 hours`, `$40 to $50 USD` private taxi or `$12 USD` shared Camry). Both Vietnamese and Cambodian e-Visas are fully honored here.

### 5. Xa Mat, Hoa Lu & Le Thanh: Central Highlands & Northeastern Cambodia Gates
Three northern checkpoints serve overland motorcyclists, cyclists, and independent travelers heading toward Kampong Cham, Kratie (Irrawaddy dolphin river pools), Mondulkiri, and Ratanakiri:
- **Xa Mat (Tay Ninh) – Trapeang Phlong (Tbong Khmum)**: Located `150 km` northwest of Ho Chi Minh City on Highway `QL22B`. Useful for reaching Kampong Cham (`110 km` west on Cambodia `NR7`).
- **Hoa Lu (Binh Phuoc) – Trapeang Sre (Kratie)**: Located `145 km` north of Ho Chi Minh City at the end of Highway `QL13` (`3.5 hours` from Mien Dong Bus Station via Lộc Ninh). From Trapeang Sre, it is `20 km` to Snoul junction and `105 km` north on `NR7` to Kratie (`2 hours`, `$30 to $40 USD` taxi) or `135 km` east on `NR76` to Sen Monorom in Mondulkiri Province.
- **Le Thanh (Gia Lai) – Oyadav (Ratanakiri)**: Located in Vietnam's Central Highlands, `75 km` west of Pleiku along Highway `QL19` (`90 minutes` by local bus or taxi, `800,000 VND` / `$32 USD`). Across the gate at Oyadav, Cambodia's `NR78` leads `70 km` west (`75 minutes`) to Banlung, the provincial capital of Ratanakiri (`$20 to $25 USD` taxi). **Crucial 2026 Visa Alert**: While Le Thanh accepts the Vietnam 90-day e-Visa (`evisa.gov.vn`), Oyadav **does not accept the Cambodian e-Visa** (`evisa.gov.kh`). Travelers entering Cambodia at Oyadav must purchase a Cambodian **Visa on Arrival** (`$30 USD` cash + `1 passport photo`) at the border booth.

## Step-by-Step Overland Border Clearance Protocol

Crossing between Vietnam and Cambodia by international highway coach or pedestrian walk-through follows a strict four-stage sequence across a `400 to 900 meter` demilitarized buffer zone. Knowing the exact choreography keeps you from losing your bus or overpaying third-party runners.

1. **Pre-Border Manifest & Luggage Tagging (`30 minutes` prior)**: On full-service coaches (Giant Ibis, Mekong Express, Kumho Samco), the onboard attendant collects passports and checks your Cambodian e-Visa printout or distributes paper Visa on Arrival forms while the bus is still `25 km` inside Vietnamese territory. Keep your passport, visa printouts, boarding pass stub, and US dollar cash in a small waist pack; take all suitcases and daypacks off the bus when arriving at the Vietnamese departure building for mandatory X-ray customs scanning.
2. **Vietnamese Exit Immigration (`15 to 30 minutes`)**: Walk into the Vietnamese Border Guard (`Bộ đội Biên phòng`) hall and queue behind the yellow line. Hand the officer your passport (and loose-leaf visa if applicable). There is zero exit fee on the Vietnamese side. Confirm the red exit date stamp is inked clearly before walking out the glass doors toward the buffer zone.
3. **Buffer Zone Transit (`5 to 10 minutes`)**: At Moc Bai – Bavet, buses wait outside the Vietnamese exit doors to shuttle passengers `800 meters` past duty-free plazas to the Cambodian arch. At smaller pedestrian gates like Ha Tien – Prek Chak (`300 meters`) and Tinh Bien – Phnom Den (`450 meters`), you wheel your suitcase on foot across the painted boundary line. Ignore unofficial moneychangers waving thick wads of riel and dong in the no-man's-land corridor; their exchange spreads run `12% to 18%` worse than town gold shops.
4. **Cambodian Entry Stamp & Biometrics (`20 to 45 minutes`)**: If you carry a printed Cambodia e-Visa (`evisa.gov.kh`), bypass the crowded Visa on Arrival windows on the left and walk straight to the e-Visa / Immigration arrival booths. Hand over your passport and one paper copy of your e-Visa certificate, place your four fingers and thumbs on the optical scanner, look into the webcam, and ensure the officer staples your departure stub inside your passport and stamps a `30-day` stay limit.

## 2026 Visa Rules & Digital e-Arrival Cards

### Entering Cambodia from Vietnam
1. **Cambodia e-Visa (`$36 USD`)**: Apply at least `4 business days` in advance exclusively via the official government portal (`evisa.gov.kh`). Cost is `$30 USD` visa fee + `$6 USD` processing charge. Print **two black-and-white or color paper copies** (one stapled at entry, one collected on exit). Accepted at Moc Bai/Bavet, Song Tien/Kaam Samnor, Ha Tien/Prek Chak, Tinh Bien/Phnom Den, Xa Mat/Trapeang Phlong, and Hoa Lu/Trapeang Sre.
2. **Cambodia Visa on Arrival (`$30 USD` Tourist / `$35 USD` Ordinary)**: Available on the spot at all seven international gates above. Bring **one `4x6 cm` passport photo** (or pay `$2 USD` for a camera scan) and crisp US dollar bills.
3. **Cambodia e-Arrival Card (`arrival.gov.kh`)**: Free mandatory registration rolled out for air arrivals and expanding across land checkpoints in 2026. Submit your form within `7 days` of travel and screenshot the QR code; land and river booths still keep paper arrival cards as a backup during network outages.

### Entering Vietnam from Cambodia
Vietnam **never** issues Tourist Visa on Arrival at land or river borders. Unless your nationality qualifies for a unilateral `45-day` or ASEAN `30-day` visa exemption, you must hold a **pre-approved Vietnam 90-day e-Visa** (`$25 USD` single-entry / `$50 USD` multiple-entry via `evisa.gov.vn`) before approaching the border. All seven Vietnamese gates listed in our matrix above (Moc Bai, Song Tien, Ha Tien, Tinh Bien, Xa Mat, Hoa Lu, and Le Thanh) are officially designated e-Visa entry and exit checkpoints. Always carry a **physical paper printout** of your Vietnam e-Visa PDF; Vietnamese Border Guard officers at remote gates will not stamp a smartphone screen.

## Border Scam Defense & USD Banknote Standards

- **Zero-Tear US Dollar Rule**: Cambodian immigration officers and banks reject pre-2013 bills or any note with a tear, ink mark, staple hole, or worn crease. Inspect every `$5`, `$10`, and `$20` bill before leaving Ho Chi Minh City, Chau Doc, or Ha Tien.
- **Avoiding the "Quarantine / Temperature Check" Scam**: At Ha Tien – Prek Chak, Tinh Bien – Phnom Den, and Hoa Lu – Trapeang Sre, uniformed or semi-official clerks at a side desk before the main Cambodian immigration window occasionally wave pedestrians over to take a forehead thermometer reading and demand `$1 to $2 USD` (`25,000 to 50,000 VND`) for a yellow "health slip." In 2026, Cambodia has no mandatory temperature or yellow-card fee for travelers arriving from Vietnam. Politely decline and walk straight to the official glass Visa on Arrival / Immigration counter.
- **Visa on Arrival "Tea Money"**: Statutory Cambodian tourist VoA fees are `$30 USD` printed directly on the visa sticker. At quiet land gates (Prek Chak, Phnom Den, Oyadav) and river pontoons (Kaam Samnor), officers or bus couriers routinely request `$32 to $35 USD` (`$2 to $5 USD` processing surcharge). Carrying an approved `$36 USD` e-Visa from `evisa.gov.kh` and walking past the VoA window directly to the e-Visa stamping lane eliminates cash negotiation at the counter.
- **Cross-Border SIM Cards & Onward Taxis**: Avoid buying overpriced tourist SIM cards inside the Bavet or Prek Chak immigration halls (`$10 to $15 USD` for small data packages). Wait until your bus stops for lunch or reaches Phnom Penh or Kampot town, where an official Smart Axiata or Metfone store sells a `30-day` tourist eSIM or physical SIM with `30 to 50 GB` of 4G/5G data for `$5 to $6 USD` (`20,000 to 24,000 KHR`).
