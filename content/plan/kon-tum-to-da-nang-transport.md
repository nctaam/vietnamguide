# Kon Tum to Da Nang Transport (2026): Bus vs Car

Descending from the northern Central Highlands of Kon Tum Province to the central coast at Da Nang is one of Vietnam's classic mountain-to-sea road journeys. The direct corridor spans 290 km to 300 km along the historic Ho Chi Minh Highway (QL14 / Asian Highway AH17 and QL14B), carving north through Dak To, Ngoc Hoi, and Dak Glei before dropping down the winding 27 km **Lo Xo Pass** (*Đèo Lò Xo*) into western Quang Nam and Da Nang. Because Kon Tum has no railway station and its nearest airport sits 42 km south via [Pleiku to Kon Tum Transport](/plan/pleiku-to-kon-tum-transport/) at Pleiku (PXU), road transit handles nearly 100% of passenger traffic between the northern plateau and Da Nang.

Travelers in 2026 choose between two primary public vehicle configurations—daytime or evening 9-seat/11-seat VIP limousine vans (5.5 to 6.5 hours, 280,000 to 350,000 VND) and 34-berth overnight sleeper coaches (6.5 to 7.5 hours, 230,000 to 280,000 VND)—or charter a private 4-seat/7-seat car (2,900,000 to 3,600,000 VND). More importantly, choosing the right highway alignment depends heavily on the calendar: while the direct QL14 Lo Xo Pass route is faster during the dry months from December to August, the 315 km eastern detour via QL24 (Mang Den and Violak Pass) and the CT01 Da Nang–Quang Ngai Expressway is far safer during heavy October and November monsoon rains.

## Route Geography: Ho Chi Minh Highway (QL14) vs Mang Den (QL24)

Before booking a ticket or hiring a driver, understand the two distinct road geometries connecting Kon Tum City (525m elevation) with sea-level Da Nang.

### Route A: The Direct Northern Corridor via Lo Xo Pass (QL14 / QL14B, 290 km)

All scheduled commercial coaches and limousine vans between Kon Tum City and Da Nang use this corridor unless severe weather forces a detour. Leaving Kon Tum north along QL14, the highway passes **Dak Ha** (20 km), **Dak To** (42 km), and **Ngoc Hoi** (62 km, the junction town 18 km east of the Bo Y Laos/Cambodia *Ngã Ba Đông Dương* border tri-point). From Ngoc Hoi, the road climbs along the Po Ko River gorge through **Dak Glei** (95 km) to the summit of **Lo Xo Pass** (*Đèo Lò Xo*, 1,050m elevation).

For 27 km, Lo Xo Pass winds through the dense primary rainforest of **Song Thanh Nature Reserve**, straddling the Kon Tum–Quang Nam provincial border. The road surface consists of concrete panels (*bê tông xi măng*) rather than asphalt to withstand heavy timber and agricultural trucks braking on 8% to 10% downgrades. After clearing the bottom of the pass at **Kham Duc** (Phuoc Son District, 165 km from Kon Tum), the route follows the Cai and Vu Gia river valleys past **Thanh My** (215 km), turns east onto QL14B at **Dai Loc**, and enters southwestern Da Nang via Hoa Khuong (290 km total).

### Route B: The Eastern Monsoon Bypass via Mang Den & Violak Pass (QL24 + CT01, 315 km)

During peak Central Vietnam typhoon and monsoon season (October 1 to November 30), prolonged torrential rainfall saturates the steep clay embankments along Lo Xo Pass and Kham Duc, occasionally triggering rockfalls or 3-to-6-hour highway clearance delays. When storm warnings are active, experienced private-car drivers and Mang Den shuttle operators take National Highway QL24 east instead.

This 315 km route climbs northeast from Kon Tum (55 km) through the cool pine plateau of **Mang Den** (1,200m), descends the wide, recently resurfaced **Violak Pass** (*Đèo Vi Ô Lắk*) through Ba To into **Quang Ngai** (175 km), and merges directly onto the four-lane **CT01 Da Nang–Quang Ngai Expressway** for a fast 140 km (1 hour 40 minutes) motorway cruise straight into southern Da Nang. Total driving time is 6 hours flat.

## Option 1: 9-Seat & 11-Seat VIP Limousine Vans

For daytime travelers who want to watch the dramatic transition from highland coffee estates to Song Thanh's jungle gorges—and avoid descending Lo Xo Pass in the middle of the night—shared 9-seat or 11-seat Ford Transit and Hyundai Solati VIP limousines are the smartest choice.

### Leading Operators and 2026 Schedules

- **Minh Quoc Limousine (0260 3855 855):** Kon Tum's flagship locally owned transport company, headquartered at 647 Nguyen Hue Street, Kon Tum City. The dispatcher runs daytime VIP limousine vans departing Kon Tum between 07:00 and 08:00 (arriving in Da Nang by 13:00 to 14:00) as well as evening departures at 19:30 and 20:00. Fares are 300,000 to 350,000 VND ($12.00 to $14.00 USD) for leather massage-chair middle rows (Seats 3 to 6) and 280,000 VND ($11.20 USD) for front or rear bench seats.
- **Viet Tan Phat / Viet Tan (0260 3911 911):** Operates both morning 07:30 limousine shuttles (280,000 to 320,000 VND / $11.20 to $12.80 USD) and evening sleeper coaches.
- **Mang Den–Da Nang Direct Limousines (Quoc Dat, Phuong Thu):** If you are staying up in Mang Den rather than downtown Kon Tum, dedicated 9-seat limousines depart Mang Den town at 08:00, 13:00, and 20:00 via the QL24 Violak Pass + CT01 Expressway route (320,000 to 350,000 VND / $12.80 to $14.00 USD, 5 to 5.5 hours).

### Pickup and Drop-Off Logistics

All limousine operators include complimentary door-to-door pickup within a 5 km radius of central Kon Tum—covering the riverside lodges and Duy Tan Ward hotels featured in [Where to Stay in Kon Tum](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-kon-tum/)—between 30 and 45 minutes prior to departure. Alternatively, you can board directly at **Kon Tum Bus Station** (281 Phan Dinh Phung Street) or Minh Quoc's private terminal on Nguyen Hue Street. In Da Nang, vans drop passengers at **Da Nang Central Bus Station** (*Bến xe Trung tâm Đà Nẵng*, 185 Ton Duc Thang Street, Lien Chieu District) or provide free feeder drop-off to hotels near Da Nang International Airport (DAD) and the Han River bridges.

## Option 2: Overnight Sleeper Coaches (34-Berth & 40-Berth)

Budget travelers and backpackers looking to save a night's accommodation bill overwhelmingly book overnight sleeper coaches departing Kon Tum between 18:30 and 20:30.

### Schedule Reality Check: The Early Morning Arrival

Because the 290 km drive takes 6.5 to 7.5 hours by heavy coach (including a mandatory 25-minute rest and tire-cooling stop at Kham Duc or Dak Glei), a bus leaving Kon Tum at 19:30 pulls into Da Nang Central Bus Station between 02:30 and 03:30 AM. Reputable operators (**Minh Quoc**, **Viet Tan**, **Mai Linh Express**, and **Phuc Thuan Thao**) allow passengers to remain asleep inside the parked air-conditioned bus at the Da Nang terminal until 05:30 AM, when city buses and cheaper Grab cars start operating.

- **34-Berth VIP Cabin Coach:** 280,000 to 330,000 VND ($11.20 to $13.20 USD). Features privacy curtains, USB-A/USB-C charging ports, and wider 75 cm mattresses.
- **40-Berth Standard Sleeper:** 230,000 to 280,000 VND ($9.20 to $11.20 USD). Three-aisle layout (A, B, C tiers). Always book a **lower-deck middle-section berth (A4 to A9)** when traversing Lo Xo Pass; upper-deck rear berths experience noticeable lateral sway around the pass's 45-degree hairpin turns.

## Option 3: Private Car Charter (QL14 or QL24 Sightseeing)

For groups of three to six travelers, or anyone connecting directly to an afternoon international flight out of Da Nang (DAD), chartering a private car provides complete control over departure timing, route selection (QL14 vs QL24), and photo stops.

- **4-Seat Sedan (Toyota Vios / Hyundai Accent):** 2,900,000 to 3,200,000 VND ($116.00 to $128.00 USD) all-inclusive of driver, fuel, and CT01 expressway tolls (190,000 VND if taking the Quang Ngai route).
- **7-Seat SUV / MPV (Toyota Innova / Fortuner):** 3,300,000 to 3,600,000 VND ($132.00 to $144.00 USD).
- **Recommended Stopovers along QL14:** Depart your Kon Tum hotel at 07:00. Stop after 42 km at the **Dak To – Tan Canh Victory Monument** (displaying two preserved M41 tanks from the 1972 highland campaign), pause for highland drip coffee in **Dak Glei** (95 km), photograph the mist-shrouded valleys at the **Lo Xo Pass Memorial** (130 km), and break for a riverside lunch of grilled stream fish (*cá niên nướng*) in **Kham Duc** (165 km) before rolling into Da Nang or Hoi An by 13:30. Travelers beginning their trip further south in Gia Lai—see [Where to Stay in Pleiku](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-pleiku/)—can book the combined 340 km Pleiku–Kon Tum–Da Nang charter for 3,500,000 to 4,000,000 VND ($140.00 to $160.00 USD).

## Step-by-Step Booking & Mountain Pass Protocol

1. **Select Daytime Limousine Over Night Bus if Motion-Sick:** The 27 km concrete descent of Lo Xo Pass involves dozens of tight curves through Song Thanh Nature Reserve. Booking a morning 07:30 VIP limousine (Seats 3, 4, or 5) reduces motion sickness compared to riding an upper-tier sleeper bunk in pitch darkness.
2. **Confirm Your Da Nang Drop-Off Zone Before Boarding:** Da Nang Central Bus Station (185 Ton Duc Thang Street) lies 8 km northwest of My Khe Beach and 32 km northwest of Hoi An Ancient Town. Ask Minh Quoc or Viet Tan Phat dispatchers to put you on their free downtown shuttle van to avoid a 130,000 VND ($5.20 USD) taxi fare from the bus terminal.
3. **Carry a Light Hoodie for the Overnight Cabin:** Sleeper coaches set cabin air-conditioning to 20°C (68°F) while crossing the 1,050m summit of Lo Xo Pass at midnight, and the provided thin fleece blanket rarely covers tall passengers.
