Separated by 295 km (183 miles) of coastline, salt lagoons, and Cham temple hills, the journey south from Hoi An to Quy Nhon in Binh Dinh Province presents a classic central Vietnam logistics puzzle. Hoi An has no railway station of its own. Nor does the UNESCO-protected Ancient Town have an express highway bus terminal inside its historic center. Every southbound trip therefore requires either a short road transfer to catch a train, a highway pickup along National Route QL1A, or a door-to-door private vehicle.

Most independent travelers complete the trip in 5 to 6.5 hours door-to-door. How you connect those segments depends on your budget and appetite for transfers. You can ride a shared 9-seat limousine van or sleeper bus down the Da Nang–Quang Ngai Expressway (CT01) for 250,000 to 380,000 VND ($10.00 to $15.20 USD), catch a southbound Reunification Express train from nearby Tra Kieu or Da Nang down to Dieu Tri Railway Station, or charter a private car (3,100,000–3,800,000 VND / $124.00–$152.00 USD) to visit the My Son Sanctuary, My Lai Memorial, and Banh It Cham Towers along the way.

If you are traveling north in the opposite direction later in your itinerary, check our reverse schedule breakdown in [Quy Nhon to Hoi An Transport](/plan/quy-nhon-to-hoi-an-transport/), or continue down the coast using our [Quy Nhon to Nha Trang Transport](/plan/quy-nhon-to-nha-trang-transport/) guide.

> [!TIP]
> **Smartest Rail Shortcut from Hoi An:** Skip the 30 km backtrack north into Da Nang by taking a 30-minute morning taxi (250,000–300,000 VND / $10.00–$12.00 USD) southwest to **Tra Kieu Railway Station** in Duy Xuyen District to board southbound train **SE21** at 08:38, arriving at Dieu Tri Station (Quy Nhon) at 13:15.

## Option 1: Taking the Train (Tra Kieu vs Da Nang to Dieu Tri)

Because neither Hoi An nor Quy Nhon beach sits directly on the main North-South Reunification Express track (the short branch spur into downtown Quy Nhon Station handles limited local traffic), rail travel involves a three-step chain: a taxi out of Hoi An, a 4.5 to 5.5-hour coastal train ride, and a final 20-minute cab into Quy Nhon.

### Step 1: Choosing Between Tra Kieu and Da Nang Stations

You have two boarding hubs near Hoi An, and choosing the right one saves both time and taxi fare:

1. **Tra Kieu Railway Station (Best for Morning Trains):** Located in Duy Son Commune, Duy Xuyen District (22 km / 30 minutes southwest of Hoi An Ancient Town; private taxi 250,000–300,000 VND or $10.00–$12.00 USD). Because Tra Kieu lies south of Hoi An, you head directly toward Quy Nhon without backtracking 30 km north into Da Nang. However, flagship non-stop trains (SE1 and SE3) race straight through Tra Kieu without stopping. Only regional expresses—specifically **SE21** (departing Tra Kieu around 08:38), **SE9** (departing around 10:42), and **SE7** (departing around 18:05)—call at Tra Kieu platform.
2. **Da Nang Railway Station (Most Departures):** Located at 791 Hai Phong Street (30 km / 45 minutes north of Hoi An; private taxi 350,000–400,000 VND / $14.00–$16.00 USD, or Hoi An Express shuttle 140,000 VND / $5.60 USD). Every North-South train stops at Da Nang for 15 to 20 minutes to change locomotives. Here you can board **SE5** (04:25), **SE21** (08:00), **SE3** (10:50), **SE1** (12:40), or **SE7** (17:25).

### Step 2: Rail Fares and The Vietage Luxury Carriage

From Da Nang or Tra Kieu down to **Dieu Tri Railway Station**, standard DSVN tickets on dsvn.vn cost:

* **Air-Conditioned Soft Seat (NML):** 210,000 to 340,000 VND ($8.40 to $13.60 USD). Comfortable for a 5-hour daytime hop through the rice valleys of Quang Nam, the coastal lagoons of Sa Huynh (Quang Ngai), and the coconut groves of Tam Quan (Binh Dinh).
* **4-Berth Soft Sleeper (ANL):** 380,000 to 560,000 VND ($15.20 to $22.40 USD). Ideal if you are traveling with heavy 20 kg suitcases or want a quiet horizontal bunk during midday hours.
* **The Vietage by Anantara (08:00 Departure):** Coupled to the rear of the morning train out of Da Nang (arriving Dieu Tri at 14:00), this custom-built 12-seat heritage carriage costs 10,500,000 VND ($420.00 USD) per passenger one-way. The fare includes a three-course gourmet lunch, free-flow French sparkling wine, craft cocktails, barista coffee, and a 15-minute head-and-shoulder massage in a dedicated treatment room.

### Step 3: Dieu Tri Station to Quy Nhon Beach (11 km)

All mainline trains drop you at **Dieu Tri Railway Station** in Tuy Phuoc District, located 11 km west of Quy Nhon's municipal promenade. Outside the station exit gate, metered Mai Linh taxis, Sun Taxis, and Grab cars wait for every arriving train. The 20-minute ride down National Highway 19 into the beachfront hotels along An Duong Vuong and Xuan Dieu streets costs 150,000 to 180,000 VND ($6.00 to $7.20 USD), or 280,000 to 320,000 VND ($11.20 to $12.80 USD) if you are staying 16 km south at the Bai Xep fishing village and Avani or Anantara resorts.

## Option 2: Direct Limousine Vans & Sleeper Buses (5–5.5 Hours)

Since the completion of the 139 km **Da Nang–Quang Ngai Expressway (CT01)**, road transit between Hoi An and Quy Nhon has become faster and simpler than the train. High-roof 9-seat VIP limousine vans and 22-cabin or 34-berth highway coaches cover the distance in 5 to 5.5 hours.

### Best Operators and Pickup Logistics

Because heavy highway coaches are barred from entering Hoi An's narrow historic streets, operators use two pickup methods:

* **Son Tung Bus (Xe Son Tung) & Mien Trung Limousine:** The top-rated regional operators running hourly daytime and evening departures (07:30, 09:30, 13:00, 16:00, and 21:30). Fares cost 280,000 to 380,000 VND ($11.20 to $15.20 USD) for a 22-cabin VIP pod or 9-seat limousine leather recliner. Both companies operate a free feeder minivan (Xe Trung Chuyen) from northern Da Nang or coordinate pickups at the **Vinh Dien Highway QL1A intersection** (Nga Ba Vinh Dien, Dien Ban Town), which sits 10 km (15 minutes, 120,000–150,000 VND / $4.80–$6.00 USD by Grab) west of Hoi An Ancient Town.
* **Queen Cafe & Hanh Cafe (Tourist Open Buses):** Depart directly from their Hoi An offices along Hai Ba Trung or Ly Thuong Kiet streets around 18:30–19:30 (or midday connects via Da Nang) for 250,000 to 320,000 VND ($10.00 to $12.80 USD). While convenient for walking from your Hoi An homestay, these overnight open-tour buses continue south toward Nha Trang and drop Quy Nhon passengers along the outer QL1A bypass near Dieu Tri around 00:30 unless you verify inner-city drop-off before paying.
* **Futa Bus Lines (Phuong Trang):** Departs from Da Nang Central Bus Station or picks up pre-booked passengers at the Vinh Dien QL1A office (260,000–350,000 VND / $10.40–$14.00 USD) and pulls directly into **Quy Nhon Central Bus Station** (71 Tay Son Street, Ghenh Rang Ward), just 2 km (40,000 VND / $1.60 USD Grab ride) from Quy Nhon Beach.

## Option 3: Private Car & Frequently Asked Questions

If you are traveling as a couple, family, or group of three to four friends, booking a private sedan or SUV transforms a routine transit day into a curated historical road trip through central Vietnam's heartland.

### 2026 Private Vehicle Rates

Local Hoi An charter fleets quote flat all-inclusive vehicle prices (covering driver, fuel, and CT01 / QL1A highway tolls):

* **4-Seat Sedan (Toyota Vios / Honda City):** 3,100,000 to 3,400,000 VND ($124.00 to $136.00 USD) direct (5 hours), or 3,500,000 to 3,800,000 VND ($140.00 to $152.00 USD) with 2 to 3 sightseeing detours (7.5 to 8.5 hours total).
* **7-Seat SUV (Toyota Innova / Fortuner / Mitsubishi Xpander):** 3,500,000 to 4,100,000 VND ($140.00 to $164.00 USD), recommended whenever you carry more than two 20 kg hard-shell suitcases.

### Recommended En-Route Stops Along Highway 1A

1. **My Son Sanctuary (Km 45 Southwest of Hoi An):** Leave Hoi An at 07:30 to explore the red-brick Hindu temple ruins of the Champa Kingdom (150,000 VND / $6.00 USD entry including electric buggy) before tour buses arrive, then join the CT01 expressway at Chu Lai.
2. **My Lai Memorial (Son My, Quang Ngai Province — Km 135):** Located 12 km east of Quang Ngai city off Highway 24B. This quiet, deeply moving museum and preserved village site (20,000 VND / $0.80 USD admission; allow 60 minutes) documents the March 1968 civilian massacre during the American War.
3. **Sa Huynh Salt Fields & Lagoon (Km 195):** Right along QL1A at the southern border of Quang Ngai, glistening salt pans sit beside coastal fishing lagoons where you can stop for a 45-minute fresh seafood lunch (120,000–180,000 VND / $4.80–$7.20 USD per person).
4. **Banh It Cham Towers (Thap Bac — Km 280):** Perched on a breezy hilltop in Tuy Phuoc District just 15 km north of Quy Nhon (20,000 VND / $0.80 USD ticket), these four 11th-century sandstone-and-brick Cham towers overlook the Kone River delta and make a fitting final stop before checking into your beachfront hotel.
