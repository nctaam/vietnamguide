## Quick Verdict: Should You Visit Vietnam in February?

February delivers the most balanced nationwide weather window of the calendar year across Vietnam. Dry sunshine warms the southern beaches at 28–34°C, while the central heritage coast from Hue to Hoi An sheds its winter monsoon clouds and settles into pleasant 20–26°C days. Across the northern mountains, warming afternoon temperatures of 15–22°C coax white plum and pink peach orchards into bloom across limestone valleys at 1,000 meters to 1,600 meters elevation.

However, February logistics revolve around Tết Nguyên Đán (Lunar New Year). In 2026, Mùng 1 Tết falls on Tuesday, February 17, with official public holidays running for 9 days from February 14 to February 22. In 2027, Mùng 1 Tết arrives on Saturday, February 6, with state holidays spanning 7 days from February 4 to February 10. Travelers who lock in domestic flights and rail berths 45 days ahead—and hold a static resort or heritage base during the core 72 hours of Tet—enjoy low rainfall, calm 20-meter underwater diving visibility, and festive temple rituals without transit disruption.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tết Holiday Transit Rule:** Never schedule multi-city overland or domestic flight transfers between 28 Tết and Mùng 3 Tết. Anchor in a single base such as [Hoi An](/destinations/hoi-an-ancient-town-guide/), [Da Nang](/destinations/da-nang-travel-guide/), or [Phu Quoc](/destinations/phu-quoc-travel-guide/) for 4 to 5 nights before interprovincial transport resumes normal frequencies on Mùng 4.

## February Regional Microclimates and Terrain Analysis

### Northern Vietnam: Spring Warming and Highland Orchards

Winter loosens its grip across the northern delta by mid-February. Daytime readings in [Hanoi](/destinations/hanoi-travel-guide/) climb from 16°C to 22°C during late-month afternoons. Morning drizzle (*mưa phùn*) coats Phố Hàng Mã between 06:00 and 09:30, while afternoon clouds lift above 800 meters.

Highland valleys transform into blossom corridors. Along National Route `QL6`, Moc Chau sits at 1,050 meters elevation, where white plum blossoms cover Nà Ka valley under 14–18°C skies. Northwest along Expressway `CT05`, [Sa Pa](/destinations/sapa-travel-guide/) records 12–17°C days and 7°C nights as pink peach trees bloom around Tả Van village at 1,250 meters. On the [Ha Giang Loop](/destinations/ha-giang-loop-planning-guide/), daytime readings along `QL4C` over Mã Pí Lèng Pass (1,500 meters elevation) exceed 15°C across the 350 km circuit over 4 days. South via Expressway `CT01` (95 km, 90 minutes), [Ninh Binh](/destinations/ninh-binh-travel-guide/) averages 18–22°C across the Tràng An grottos.

### Central Vietnam: Monsoon Clearing and Coastal Sunshine

Central provinces experience their sharpest climate upgrade in February. Monthly rain in [Da Nang](/destinations/da-nang-travel-guide/) and [Hoi An](/destinations/hoi-an-ancient-town-guide/) drops from January's 110mm to 35mm. Afternoon highs reach 25–27°C along Đường Võ Nguyên Giáp at Mỹ Khê Beach and An Bàng Beach. Tailoring shops along Phố Trần Phú and cafes on Đường Bạch Đằng enjoy dry skies for 15 km bicycle loops through Cẩm Thanh groves.

North of the 496-meter Hải Vân Pass, [Hue](/destinations/hue-imperial-city-guide/) warms to 21–25°C. Light mist lingers over the Minh Mạng royal tomb (12 km from Ga Huế, 25 minutes by car), yet heavy monsoon downpours vanish. South along Highway `QL1A` and Cam Ranh Airport (`CXR`, 35 km south of Nha Trang, 40 minutes transfer), bays enjoy 24–29°C sunshine and 20mm of rain. Up Highway `QL27C` (135 km, 3.5 hours), Da Lat pairs 23°C afternoons with 11°C nights as cherry trees bloom around Xuân Hương Lake.

### Southern Vietnam: Peak Dry Season and Marine Clarity

Southern Vietnam operates at peak dry-season stability throughout February. [Ho Chi Minh City](/destinations/ho-chi-minh-city-travel-guide/) records highs of 31–34°C with 4mm of rain. Morning walks along Đường Đồng Khởi, Phố đi bộ Nguyễn Huệ, and Chợ Lớn (6 km west, 20 minutes by taxi) stay comfortable between 07:00 and 10:30.

Offshore islands in the Gulf of Thailand hit peak marine clarity. [Phu Quoc](/destinations/phu-quoc-travel-guide/) maintains 29–32°C air temperatures, 27°C water, and 0.5-meter swells along Bãi Sao. Snorkeling boats departing Bến An Thới (26 km south of Dương Đông, 35 minutes drive) encounter 15 meters to 20 meters of underwater visibility. On the mainland, Mũi Né (185 km east via `CT01`, 2.5 hours) delivers 26–31°C sunshine, while Côn Đảo (230 km offshore, 45 minutes flight from `SGN`) sees trade winds calm by late February.

## Step-by-Step Logistics: Navigating the Three Phases of Tet

Planning February travel requires mapping transit dates against the lunar calendar: **February 17, 2026** (break February 14–22) and **February 6, 2027** (break February 4–10). Follow our [Tet in Vietnam Travel Guide](/plan/tet-in-vietnam-travel-guide/) and this step-by-step protocol:

1. **Step 1: Audit Phase 1 (Tuần Trước Tết, 7 Days Before Mùng 1).** Before Lunar New Year's Eve, millions of workers travel from Ho Chi Minh City northward along `QL1A`, `CT01`, and the railway. Northbound flights from `SGN` to `HAN`, `DAD`, and `HUI` climb from 1,400,000 VND ($56.00 USD) to 3,800,000 VND ($152.00 USD), while southbound flights from `HAN` to `SGN` offer fares near 950,000 VND ($38.00 USD). Book flights and [Vietnam Train Travel](/plan/vietnam-train-travel/) berths 45 days ahead on `dsvn.vn`.
2. **Step 2: Hold a Static Base in Phase 2 (Mùng 1 to Mùng 3 Tết, Core 72 Hours).** For the first 3 lunar days, Hanoi and Saigon empty into quiet boulevards. About 70% of street stalls close, and state museums pause entry from 12:00 on 29 Tết until 08:00 on Mùng 4. Base in a resort in Hoi An, Da Nang, or Phu Quoc, or board a 2-night Ha Long Bay cruise where crews remain staffed.
3. **Step 3: Pivot Around Crowds in Phase 3 (Mùng 4 to Mùng 8 Tết, Du Xuân Surge).** At 06:00 on Mùng 4, domestic families launch spring trips (*du xuân*). Cable cars at Fansipan, Ba Na Hills (28 km west of Da Nang, 45 minutes drive), and Chùa Hương (60 km south of Hanoi, 2 hours drive) face 90-minute queues, and hotels in Da Lat and Sa Pa add a 40% holiday surcharge. Schedule rural cycling in Ninh Binh or boat charters in Bến Tre (85 km south of Saigon, 90 minutes via `CT01`).
4. **Step 4: Run the Post-Tet Circuit (From Mùng 9 Onward).** Once offices reopen, domestic airfares drop 40% within 48 hours. Link a 14-day North-to-South circuit: 2 nights in Hanoi, 1 night on Ha Long Bay via `CT04` (155 km), 2 nights in Ninh Binh via `CT01` (190 km, 3.5 hours), an 80-minute flight to `DAD` for 3 nights in Hoi An, a 3-hour train over Hải Vân Pass to Hue (103 km), and an 85-minute flight to `SGN` for 4 nights in Saigon and Phu Quoc. See our [14 Days in Vietnam](/itineraries/14-days-in-vietnam/) guide.

## Cost Breakdown: Normal February vs Tet Surge Pricing

During the 10-day Tết window, operating restaurants, private drivers, and tour boats apply a mandatory staff holiday surcharge (*phụ thu Tết*) of 15% to 30%. Outside the holiday week, February pricing reverts to standard dry-season baselines:

- **Domestic Trunk Flights (`SGN`–`HAN` or `HAN`–`DAD`):** Standard February one-way fares with 20 kg baggage cost 1,250,000 VND to 1,950,000 VND ($50.00 to $78.00 USD). Peak Tết week fares jump to 3,400,000 VND to 4,200,000 VND ($136.00 to $168.00 USD).
- **Reunification Express Sleeper Trains (`SE1`/`SE2`/`SE3`/`SE4`):** A 4-berth soft sleeper from Ga Hà Nội (120 Đường Lê Duẩn) to Ga Đà Nẵng (791 km, 16.5 hours) costs 1,150,000 VND to 1,350,000 VND ($46.00 to $54.00 USD) in normal weeks, rising to 1,850,000 VND ($74.00 USD) during peak holiday dates.
- **Airport Transfers and Intercity Limousines:** Hanoi Noi Bai (`HAN`) to Hoan Kiem District (30 km, 45 minutes) costs 45,000 VND ($1.80 USD) on Express Bus 86 or 350,000 VND ($14.00 USD) by GrabCar. VIP 9-seat limousine vans from Hanoi to Ninh Binh (95 km, 90 minutes) or Ha Long Bay (155 km, 150 minutes) charge 250,000 VND to 350,000 VND ($10.00 to $14.00 USD) per seat.
- **Boutique Hotels and Beach Resorts:** Mid-range 4-star hotels in Hanoi and Hoi An average 1,200,000 VND to 2,200,000 VND ($48.00 to $88.00 USD) per night. Beachfront resorts on Phu Quoc charge 2,500,000 VND to 4,800,000 VND ($100.00 to $192.00 USD) per night, plus compulsory New Year gala dinners at 1,500,000 VND ($60.00 USD) per adult on Lunar New Year's Eve.
- **Street Food and Sightseeing Tickets:** A bowl of *phở bò* normally priced at 50,000 VND ($2.00 USD) rises to 75,000 VND ($3.00 USD) at stalls open on Mùng 1–3. Fixed monument tickets include Hue Imperial Citadel at 200,000 VND ($8.00 USD), Tràng An boat caves at 250,000 VND ($10.00 USD) for a 3-hour row, and Hoi An Ancient Town at 120,000 VND ($4.80 USD).

## On-the-Ground Pro-Tips and Cultural Etiquette

- **Withdraw VND Cash 48 Hours Before 28 Tết:** Retail bank counters shut for 7 to 9 days, and high-traffic ATMs along Phố Đinh Tiên Hoàng (Hanoi) and Đường Lê Lợi (Saigon) frequently run out of 500,000 VND notes. Withdraw 4,000,000 VND to 6,000,000 VND ($160.00 to $240.00 USD) beforehand. Review our [Money, Cash, Cards & ATMs](/plan/money-cash-cards-atms/) guide for zero-fee bank networks.
- **Prepare Crisp Red Envelopes (*Lì Xì*):** Keep crisp 20,000 VND ($0.80 USD), 50,000 VND ($2.00 USD), and 100,000 VND ($4.00 USD) polymer bills inside red envelopes (*phong bao lì xì*, 15,000 VND / $0.60 USD per pack at Phố Hàng Mã) to tip hotel housekeepers, cruise stewards, and drivers on Mùng 1 through Mùng 3.
- **Observe First-Visitor Etiquette (*Xông Đất*) and Dress Codes:** Never enter a private home uninvited on Mùng 1 morning, as the first visitor sets the household's luck for 12 months. When visiting Ngọc Sơn Temple (30,000 VND / $1.20 USD entry) or Chùa Vĩnh Nghiêm, wear bright red, yellow, or pastel shirts covering shoulders and knees, avoiding all-black or all-white mourning attire.
- **Use Buddhist Pagodas and Convenience Chains for Holiday Meals:** During Mùng 1 and Mùng 2, 24-hour Circle K and FamilyMart stores stock steamed buns (25,000 VND / $1.00 USD) and water (10,000 VND / $0.40 USD), while vegetarian kitchens (*cơm chay*) near Buddhist temples serve hot lunches for 50,000 VND ($2.00 USD); see our [Vegetarian in Vietnam](/plan/vietnam-vegetarian-travel-guide/) resource.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is February a good month to visit Vietnam?
Yes, February offers the highest probability of dry, sunny weather across all three regions simultaneously, with 29–34°C heat in the south, 23–27°C beach weather in Central Vietnam, and 18–22°C spring blossom weather in the north.

### What are the exact dates for Tet in 2026 and 2027?
In 2026, Mùng 1 Tết falls on Tuesday, February 17, 2026 (official holiday February 14–22). In 2027, Mùng 1 Tết falls on Saturday, February 6, 2027 (official holiday February 4–10).

### Do trains and domestic flights run on Tet New Year's Day?
Yes, Vietnam Airlines, Vietjet Air, and Reunification Express trains (`SE1`–`SE8`) operate throughout Mùng 1 to Mùng 3, though schedules must be booked 4 to 6 weeks in advance.
