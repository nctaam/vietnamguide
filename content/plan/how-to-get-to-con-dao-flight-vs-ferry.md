# How to Get to Con Dao: Flight vs High-Speed Ferry (2026)

Situated **185 kilometers off the southern coast of Ba Ria–Vung Tau** and **90 kilometers (50 nautical miles) southeast of the Mekong Delta's Hau River mouth** in the open South China Sea, the 16-island **Con Dao Archipelago (*Côn Đảo*)** is the most geographically isolated island destination in southern Vietnam. Unlike Phu Quoc—which sits inside the sheltered Gulf of Thailand and handles wide-body international jets on a 3,000-meter runway—**Con Dao Co Ong Airport (*Sân bay Côn Đảo*, code: VCS)** has a short **1,830-meter single-strip coastal runway** wedged between two granite headlands that can only accommodate regional turboprop aircraft and short-field regional jets during daylight hours.

Consequently, every traveler bound for Con Dao's sea turtle nesting beaches, pristine coral reefs, and historic colonial prison relics must choose between two fundamentally different transit corridors: **flying direct in 45 to 55 minutes from Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) or Can Tho (VCA)** vs **boarding a high-speed passenger catamaran across the open ocean from Tran De Port (Soc Trang) or Vung Tau (Cau Da Port)**. This 2026 transport guide provides verified flight and ferry fares in Vietnamese Dong (VND), seasonal Northeast Monsoon sea-state warnings, and Co Ong Airport transfer rules.

## Option 1: Flying to Con Dao Airport (VCS) from Saigon (SGN) or Can Tho (VCA)

For 85 percent of international visitors, flying is worth every Dong of the price premium over the sea ferry. You avoid six to seven hours of combined highway-and-ocean transit and arrive fresh on Con Son Island before lunch.

### 1. Airlines, Aircraft & Baggage Rules in 2026
- **Operators**: **Vietnam Airlines** (operated by its regional subsidiary **VASCO — Vietnam Air Services Company** using 68-seat **ATR 72-500/600 twin-turboprop aircraft**) runs 8 to 14 daily frequencies from **Ho Chi Minh City (SGN)** and 1 to 2 daily flights from **Can Tho (VCA)**.
- **Why Con Dao Flights Sell Out Early**: Because each ATR-72 aircraft holds only 66 to 68 passengers—less than a third of a standard Airbus A321—and Con Dao Airport lacks nighttime runway lighting (restricting all landings and takeoffs to the **06:00 AM to 17:45 PM daylight window**), daily seat capacity is tightly capped. Book your SGN–VCS tickets **3 to 6 weeks in advance**, especially for Friday-to-Sunday weekends and the March–August dry diving season.
- **Strict ATR-72 Cabin Carry-On Weight & Size Limits**: Overhead bins inside the ATR-72 turboprop are much smaller than on Airbus or Boeing jets. Carry-on baggage is strictly enforced at **7 kg maximum** (dimensions not exceeding 56 x 36 x 23 cm); standard roller bags that fit on mainline jets will be gate-checked into the rear hold at boarding. Always purchase a fare tier (**Economy Classic** or **Economy Flex**, 1,850,000–2,450,000 VND) that includes a **23 kg checked suitcase**.

### 2. Connecting from Hanoi (HAN) or Da Nang (DAD) to Con Dao
When direct long-haul flights from Hanoi to Con Dao are not operating seasonally, travelers from northern or central Vietnam connect through **Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN)** or **Can Tho Airport (VCA)**. Allow a minimum **2.5-to-3-hour domestic connection buffer at SGN** if booking separate tickets so you can claim your checked suitcase and check in at the VASCO counter at least 50 minutes before departure.

> [!TIP]
> **Which Window Seat to Pick Approaching Con Dao (VCS)**: Select a **LEFT-side window seat (Seats A)** when flying southbound from Ho Chi Minh City or Can Tho onto Runway 11 at Co Ong Airport. During the final three minutes of descent, the aircraft banks low over emerald coral reefs and skims just 30 meters above the golden crescent sand of **Dam Trau Beach (*Bãi Đầm Trầu*)** before touching down between two sheer mountains.

## Option 2: High-Speed Ferries from Tran De (Soc Trang) & Vung Tau — Routes, Fares & Sea Swell

If flights are sold out or you are traveling through the Mekong Delta on a backpacker budget, twin-hulled high-speed catamarans connect the southern mainland to **Ben Dam Port (*Cảng Bến Đầm*)** on the southwestern tip of Con Son Island.

### Route 2A: Tran De Port (Soc Trang, Mekong Delta) to Con Dao — The Shortest & Cheapest Sea Crossing
Located **105 kilometers (56 nautical miles)** northwest of Con Dao at the mouth of the Hau River (60 km southeast of Can Tho City), **Tran De Fishing & Passenger Port (*Cảng Trần Đề*)** offers the shortest open-water crossing to the archipelago:
- **Operators**: **Phu Quoc Express (*Tàu Cao Tốc Côn Đảo Express*)** (598-seat twin-hull catamaran) and **Superdong Speed Ferry** (*Superdong Côn Đảo I & II*, 306 seats).
- **2026 Schedule**: Departures leave Tran De Port daily between **08:00 AM and 09:00 AM**, arriving at Ben Dam Port (Con Dao) in **2 hours 15 minutes to 2 hours 30 minutes**. Return boats depart Ben Dam Port between **12:30 PM and 13:30 PM**.
- **2026 Fares**: **390,000 to 450,000 VND ($15.60–$18.00 USD)** for Economy air-conditioned seats (310,000–350,000 VND for children 6–11 and seniors 60+); **590,000 VND ($23.60 USD)** for VIP reclining seats on the upper forward deck.
- **How to Reach Tran De Port**: From **Can Tho**, take a 90-minute taxi (650,000–800,000 VND) or morning shuttle bus (120,000 VND) directly to Tran De Pier. From **Ho Chi Minh City (Mien Tay Bus Station)**, take an overnight **FUTA Phương Trang sleeper bus** (190,000–220,000 VND, departing 23:30 PM) to Soc Trang / Tran De in time for the 08:00 AM sailing.

### Route 2B: Vung Tau (Cau Da Port) to Con Dao — The Closest Port to Ho Chi Minh City
Located at **09 Ha Long Street, Vung Tau** (110 km / 2.0 hours southeast of Ho Chi Minh City via DCar limousine van costing 180,000–220,000 VND), **Cau Da Port (*Cảng Cầu Đá*)** handles the longer **185-kilometer (3.5-to-4.0-hour)** sea crossing:
- **Operators & Schedule**: Operated by twin-hull catamarans (*Con Dao Express 36*) and the 1,017-passenger monohull vessel *Thang Long* (note: Vung Tau sailings operate primarily during the **March-to-September calm sea season** and reduce frequency or suspend operations during the October–February winter monsoon). Morning departures leave Vung Tau at **07:30 or 08:00 AM**, returning from Con Dao at **13:00 or 13:30 PM**.
- **2026 Fares**: **790,000 to 950,000 VND ($31.60–$38.00 USD)** for Economy seats; **1,200,000 VND ($48.00 USD)** for VIP business cabins.

> [!WARNING]
> **Critical Northeast Monsoon (*Gió Chướng*) Sea-Sickness & Cancellation Warning (October to March)**: Unlike the calm waters around Phu Quoc or Ha Long Bay, the 105-to-185 km sea channel to Con Dao crosses the exposed **open South China Sea**. From **March through early September**, seas are generally flat (Beaufort Force 2–4) and the ferry ride is smooth. However, between **late October and early March**, the winter Northeast Monsoon drives **2.5-to-4.0-meter ocean swells (Force 6–7)**. Even on 600-seat catamarans, up to half the cabin can experience acute motion sickness, and the Maritime Port Authority regularly **cancels all ferries for 2 to 5 consecutive days** when winds hit Force 7. Never book a sea ferry back from Con Dao within 48 hours of an international flight home during winter—fly instead, or book a lower-deck center-row seat and take dimenhydrinate (*Nautamine*) 45 minutes before boarding.

## Arrival Logistics on Con Son Island: Co Ong Airport (VCS) & Ben Dam Port Transfers

Both the airport and the ferry pier sit 13 to 14 kilometers outside **Con Son Town (*Thị trấn Côn Sơn*)**, at opposite ends of the island's single coastal ring road:

1. **From Co Ong Airport (VCS — 14 km Northeast of Con Son Town, 20 Minutes)**:
   - **Shared Resort / Hotel Minivans (50,000–80,000 VND / $2.00–$3.20 USD per seat)**: Nearly every hotel and homestay in Con Son Town arranges a shared 16-seat shuttle van that greets every arriving ATR-72 flight outside the tiny terminal gate. Give your hotel your flight number (VN18xx / 0V80xx) via WhatsApp 24 hours before arrival.
   - **Metered & Electric Taxis (Côn Đảo Taxi, Mai Linh, Lado Electric)**: A private 4-seater taxi from Co Ong Airport into Con Son Town costs **220,000 to 280,000 VND ($8.80–$11.20 USD)** along the scenic coastal cliff road past Nose Cape (*Mũi Tàu Bể*).
2. **From Ben Dam Ferry Port (Cảng Bến Đầm — 13 km Southwest of Con Son Town, 20 Minutes)**:
   - **Shared Port Minibuses (50,000–60,000 VND per passenger)**: Wait directly on the Ben Dam jetty as soon as the ferry gangway drops, dropping passengers at the exact door of any hotel in Con Son Town.
   - **Scooter Rental at the Pier (150,000–200,000 VND/day)**: Local rental shops deliver 110cc–125cc scooters directly to Ben Dam Port or Co Ong Airport and transport your suitcase separately to your hotel free of charge.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Does Con Dao have the 30-day visa-free exemption like Phu Quoc?
**No.** Only **Phu Quoc Island** grants a special 30-day unilateral visa exemption for international arrivals. **Con Dao is subject to standard mainland Vietnamese immigration rules**, meaning you must hold a valid **Vietnamese e-Visa ($25 USD single-entry / $50 USD multiple-entry)** or qualify under your nationality's standard 45-day bilateral visa-free entry before boarding a domestic flight or ferry to Con Dao.

### Is it better to take the ferry to Con Dao from Tran De (Soc Trang) or Vung Tau?
If you are already in **Ho Chi Minh City**, the **Vung Tau ferry** requires only a 2-hour road transfer (110 km), whereas reaching **Tran De Port** from Saigon requires a 4-hour road journey (220 km). However, once on the water, the **Tran De to Con Dao ferry (2.25 hours, 390k–450k VND)** is **90 minutes shorter and half the price** of the Vung Tau ferry (3.75 hours, 790k–950k VND), making Tran De the clear winner if you are combining Con Dao with a **Can Tho / Mekong Delta** trip.

### What documents do I need to buy a Con Dao ferry or flight ticket?
Both Vietnam Airlines / VASCO and the high-speed ferry operators (*Phu Quoc Express* and *Superdong*) are required by maritime and aviation security regulations to record your **full passport name, nationality, passport number, and year of birth** on the passenger manifest. Always carry your physical original passport (not just a phone photo) when checking in at Co Ong Airport, Vung Tau Port, or Tran De Port.
