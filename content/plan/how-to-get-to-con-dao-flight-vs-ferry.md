# How to Get to Con Dao: Flight vs High-Speed Ferry (2026)

Situated **185 kilometers off the southern coast of Ba Ria–Vung Tau** and **90 kilometers (50 nautical miles) southeast of the Mekong Delta's Hau River mouth** in the open South China Sea, the 16-island **Con Dao Archipelago (*Côn Đảo*)** is the most geographically isolated island destination in southern Vietnam. Unlike Phu Quoc—which sits inside the sheltered Gulf of Thailand and handles wide-body international jets on a 3,000-meter runway—**Con Dao Co Ong Airport (*Sân bay Côn Đảo*, code: VCS)** has a short **1,830-meter single-strip coastal runway** wedged between two granite headlands that can only accommodate regional turboprop aircraft and short-field regional jets during daylight hours.

Consequently, every traveler bound for Con Dao's sea turtle nesting beaches, pristine coral reefs, and historic colonial prison relics must choose between two fundamentally different transit corridors: **flying direct in 45 to 55 minutes from Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) or Can Tho (VCA)** vs **boarding a high-speed passenger catamaran across the open ocean from Tran De Port (Soc Trang) or Vung Tau (Cau Da Port)**. This 2026 transport guide provides verified flight and ferry fares in Vietnamese Dong (VND), seasonal Northeast Monsoon sea-state warnings, and Co Ong Airport transfer rules.

## Option 1: Flying to Con Dao Airport (VCS) from Saigon (SGN) or Can Tho (VCA)

For 85 percent of international visitors, flying justifies every Dong of the fare premium over the sea crossing. You skip six to seven hours of combined highway-and-ocean transit and land on Con Son Island before lunch.

### 1. Airlines, Aircraft & Baggage Rules in 2026
- **Operators & Frequencies**: **Vietnam Airlines** (operated by its regional subsidiary **VASCO — Vietnam Air Services Company** flying 68-seat **ATR 72-500/600 twin-turboprops**) runs 8 to 14 daily departures across the **230 km** leg from **Ho Chi Minh City (SGN)** and 1 to 2 daily services across the **175 km** hop from **Can Tho (VCA)**.
- **Why Con Dao Flights Sell Out Early**: Each ATR-72 cabin holds only 66 to 68 passengers—under a third of an Airbus A321. Because Co Ong Airport lacks nighttime runway lighting, all operations stay locked inside the **06:00 AM to 17:45 PM daylight window**. Reserve SGN–VCS tickets **3 to 6 weeks ahead**, particularly for Friday–Sunday breaks and the calm March–August diving season.
- **2026 Fare Tiers & Strict ATR-72 Baggage Limits**: Overhead racks inside the ATR-72 are far narrower than mainline jets, so cabin luggage is capped at **7 kg** (maximum 56 x 36 x 23 cm) and rigid roller suitcases are gate-checked into the rear bay. **Economy Super Lite** starts at **1,650,000 VND ($66.00 USD)** with hand luggage only, whereas **Economy Classic** (**1,850,000 VND to 2,250,000 VND** / $74.00–$90.00 USD) and **Economy Flex** (**2,450,000 VND to 2,850,000 VND** / $98.00–$114.00 USD) include **23 kg checked baggage** and fee-free date changes. Pre-purchasing an extra 23 kg bag online costs **300,000 VND ($12.00 USD)** versus **600,000 VND ($24.00 USD)** at the check-in desk.

### 2. Connecting from Hanoi (HAN) or Da Nang (DAD) to Con Dao
When nonstop regional flights from Hanoi are unavailable, travelers from northern or central Vietnam connect through **Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN)** or **Can Tho Airport (VCA)**. Keep a **2.5 to 3.0 hours** domestic layover buffer at SGN on separate bookings. Moving between SGN Domestic Terminal T1 and the newer Terminal T3 spans **4 km** (15 minutes by shuttle bus at 0 VND or 65,000 VND / $2.60 USD by GrabCar), and VASCO counters close strictly **40 minutes** before takeoff.

> [!TIP]
> **Which Window Seat to Pick Approaching Con Dao (VCS)**: Select a **LEFT-side window seat (Seats A)** when flying southbound from Ho Chi Minh City or Can Tho onto Runway 11 at Co Ong Airport. During the final three minutes of descent, the aircraft banks low over emerald coral reefs and skims just 30 meters above the golden crescent sand of **Dam Trau Beach (*Bãi Đầm Trầu*)** before touching down between two sheer mountains.

## Option 2: High-Speed Ferries from Tran De (Soc Trang) & Vung Tau — Routes, Fares & Sea Swell

If flights are sold out or you are traveling through the Mekong Delta on a backpacker budget, twin-hulled high-speed catamarans connect the southern mainland to **Ben Dam Port (*Cảng Bến Đầm*)** on the southwestern tip of Con Son Island.

### Route 2A: Tran De Port (Soc Trang, Mekong Delta) to Con Dao — The Shortest & Cheapest Sea Crossing
Located **105 kilometers (56 nautical miles)** northwest of Con Dao at the mouth of the Hau River (60 km southeast of Can Tho City), **Tran De Fishing & Passenger Port (*Cảng Trần Đề*)** offers the shortest open-water crossing to the archipelago:
- **Operators**: **Phu Quoc Express (*Tàu Cao Tốc Côn Đảo Express*)** (598-seat twin-hull catamaran) and **Superdong Speed Ferry** (*Superdong Côn Đảo I & II*, 306 seats).
- **2026 Schedule**: Departures leave Tran De Port daily between **08:00 AM and 09:00 AM**, arriving at Ben Dam Port (Con Dao) in **2 hours 15 minutes to 2 hours 30 minutes**. Return boats depart Ben Dam Port between **12:30 PM and 13:30 PM**.
- **2026 Fares**: **390,000 to 450,000 VND ($15.60–$18.00 USD)** for Economy air-conditioned seats (310,000–350,000 VND for children 6–11 and seniors 60+); **590,000 VND ($23.60 USD)** for VIP reclining seats on the upper forward deck.
- **How to Reach Tran De Port**: From **Can Tho**, take a 90-minute taxi (650,000–800,000 VND) or morning shuttle bus (120,000 VND) directly to Tran De Pier. From **Ho Chi Minh City (Mien Tay Bus Station)**, take an overnight **FUTA Phương Trang sleeper bus** (190,000–220,000 VND, departing 23:30 PM) to Soc Trang / Tran De in time for the 08:00 AM sailing.

### Route 2B: Vung Tau (Cau Da Port) to Con Dao — The Closest Port to Ho Chi Minh City
Located at **09 Ha Long Street, Vung Tau** (110 km / 2.0 hours southeast of Ho Chi Minh City via DCar limousine van costing 180,000–220,000 VND), **Cau Da Port (*Cảng Cầu Đá*)** handles the longer **185-kilometer (3.5-to-4.0-hour)** sea crossing:
- **Operators & Schedule**: Operated by twin-hull catamarans (*Con Dao Express 36*) and the 1,017-passenger monohull vessel *Thang Long* (note: Vung Tau sailings operate primarily during the **March-to-September calm sea season** and reduce frequency or suspend operations during the October–February winter monsoon). Morning departures leave Vung Tau at **07:30 or 08:00 AM**, returning from Con Dao at **13:00 or 13:30 PM**.
- **2026 Fares**: **790,000 to 950,000 VND ($31.60–$38.00 USD)** for Economy seats; **1,200,000 VND ($48.00 USD)** for VIP business cabins.

> [!WARNING]
> **Critical Northeast Monsoon (*Gió Chướng*) Sea-Sickness & Cancellation Warning (October to March)**: Unlike the calm waters around Phu Quoc or Ha Long Bay, the 105-to-185 km sea channel to Con Dao crosses the exposed **open South China Sea**. From **March through early September**, seas are generally flat (Beaufort Force 2–4) and the ferry ride is smooth. However, between **late October and early March**, the winter Northeast Monsoon drives **2.5-to-4.0-meter ocean swells (Force 6–7)**. Even on 600-seat catamarans, up to half the cabin can experience acute motion sickness, and the Maritime Port Authority regularly **cancels all ferries for 2 to 5 consecutive days** when winds hit Force 7. Never book a sea ferry back from Con Dao within 48 hours of an international flight home during winter—fly instead, or book a lower-deck center-row seat and take dimenhydrinate (*Nautamine*) 45 minutes before boarding.

## Arrival Logistics on Con Son Island: Co Ong Airport (VCS) & Ben Dam Port Transfers

Both the airport and the ferry pier sit 13 to 14 kilometers outside **Con Son Town (*Thị trấn Côn Sơn*)**, positioned at opposite ends of the island's coastal ring road. Plan your ground transfer before landing or docking so you do not get stranded in midday heat:

1. **From Co Ong Airport (VCS — 14 km Northeast of Con Son Town, 20 minutes)**:
   - **Shared Resort / Hotel Minivans (50,000 VND to 80,000 VND / $2.00–$3.20 USD per seat)**: Nearly every property listed in our [Where to Stay in Con Dao](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-con-dao/) guide coordinates a 16-seat shuttle meeting each arriving ATR-72 flight outside the arrival door. Send your flight code (`VN18xx` or `0V80xx`) via WhatsApp 24 hours ahead.
   - **Metered & Electric Taxis (Côn Đảo Taxi, Mai Linh, Lado Electric)**: A private 4-seater taxi into Con Son Town costs **220,000 VND to 280,000 VND ($8.80–$11.20 USD)** along the cliffside coastal road past Nose Cape (*Mũi Tàu Bể*). Short hops to **Dam Trau Beach** (**1.5 km**, 4 minutes) cost **45,000 VND to 60,000 VND ($1.80–$2.40 USD)**, while transfers to **Six Senses Con Dao** (**8 km**, 12 minutes) run **150,000 VND to 180,000 VND ($6.00–$7.20 USD)**.
2. **From Ben Dam Ferry Port (Cảng Bến Đầm — 13 km Southwest of Con Son Town, 20 minutes)**:
   - **Shared Port Minibuses (50,000 VND to 60,000 VND / $2.00–$2.40 USD per passenger)**: Minibuses line up on the Ben Dam jetty as soon as the gangway lowers and drop riders directly at hotel gates along Ton Duc Thang or Nguyen Hue streets.
   - **Private Taxi or Scooter Delivery**: A private 4-seat cab from Ben Dam Port to town runs **200,000 VND to 260,000 VND ($8.00–$10.40 USD)**. Local rental shops also deliver 110cc–125cc scooters (**150,000 VND to 200,000 VND / $6.00–$8.00 USD per day**, plus 25,000 VND/liter fuel) straight to Ben Dam Pier or Co Ong Airport while transporting large suitcases separately to your hotel at 0 VND extra.

## Weather-Cancellation Buffers & 2026 Contingency Planning

Con Dao rewards advance buffer design more than any other Vietnamese island. Open-water swells and short-runway crosswinds disrupt tight schedules fast, especially during peak Northeast Monsoon surges from late October through February.

- **Build a 48-Hour International Flight Buffer**: Never book a morning Tran De or Vung Tau ferry on the same day as an international departure from Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN). Maritime Port Authorities issue Force 6–7 suspension notices at 17:00 PM the evening before—or as late as 06:30 AM on sailing morning—leaving stranded passengers scrambling for last-minute VASCO seats that jump to **2,450,000 VND to 2,850,000 VND ($98.00–$114.00 USD)** or sell out entirely for 72 hours.
- **Know Ferry Refund & Reschedule Rules**: When the Soc Trang or Ba Ria–Vung Tau Port Authority cancels a crossing due to gale conditions, both **Superdong** and **Phu Quoc Express** issue a **100% cash/card refund** or free transfer to the next available vessel within 30 days. Detailed pier boarding rules appear in our [Tran De to Con Dao Ferry](/plan/tran-de-to-con-dao-ferry/) and [Vung Tau to Con Dao Ferry](/plan/vung-tau-to-con-dao-ferry/) route breakdowns.
- **Emergency Mainland Overnight Costs**: If your morning ferry from Tran De cancels, budget **400,000 VND to 650,000 VND ($16.00–$26.00 USD)** for a clean night in Soc Trang City (**35 km** / 45 minutes inland, taxi 420,000 VND / $16.80 USD) or **600,000 VND to 1,100,000 VND ($24.00–$44.00 USD)** near Bai Truoc in Vung Tau (**2 km** from Cau Da Pier). If your Con Son return cancels unexpectedly, local town guesthouses charge **550,000 VND to 900,000 VND ($22.00–$36.00 USD)** per extra night.
- **Winter Alternative Without Open-Sea Risk**: Traveling strictly between November and February on a tight 7-day timeline? Compare our [Con Dao vs Phu Quoc](/compare/con-dao-vs-phu-quoc/) guide—Phu Quoc sits inside the sheltered Gulf of Thailand with jet runways and flat winter seas, whereas Con Dao shines brightest from March through August as detailed in our [Con Dao Travel Guide](/destinations/con-dao-travel-guide/).

## Frequently Asked Questions (2026)

### Does Con Dao have the 30-day visa-free exemption like Phu Quoc?
**No.** Only **Phu Quoc Island** grants a special 30-day unilateral visa exemption for direct international arrivals. **Con Dao follows standard mainland Vietnamese immigration law**, so you must hold either a valid **90-day Vietnamese e-Visa ($25.00 USD single-entry / $50.00 USD multiple-entry)** or passport eligibility under **Resolution 44/NQ-CP (2025–2028)** or **Resolution 229/NQ-CP** (45-day visa-free entry for 25 designated European and Asian countries) before boarding any flight or ferry to Con Dao.

### Is it better to take the ferry to Con Dao from Tran De (Soc Trang) or Vung Tau?
Starting from **Ho Chi Minh City**, reaching **Vung Tau (Cau Da Port)** takes just **2.0 hours** by highway limousine (**110 km**, 180,000 VND to 220,000 VND / $7.20–$8.80 USD), whereas reaching **Tran De Port** takes **4.0 to 4.5 hours** (**220 km**, 190,000 VND to 220,000 VND sleeper bus). Once aboard, however, the **Tran De crossing (2.25 hours, 105 km, 390,000 VND to 450,000 VND / $15.60–$18.00 USD)** is **90 minutes shorter and half the cost** of the Vung Tau sailing (**3.75 hours, 185 km, 790,000 VND to 950,000 VND / $31.60–$38.00 USD**), making Tran De the clear winner when pairing Con Dao with Can Tho.

### What documents do I need to buy a Con Dao ferry or flight ticket?
Both Vietnam Airlines / VASCO and high-speed ferry operators (*Phu Quoc Express* and *Superdong*) must verify your **full passport name, nationality, passport number, and date of birth** against official aviation and maritime manifests. Bring your physical original passport—phone photos are rejected at Co Ong Airport, Vung Tau Port, and Tran De Port security gates.

### What happens if my Con Dao ferry is canceled due to rough seas?
When winds reach Force 6–7 across the South China Sea and the Port Authority halts sailings, operators notify ticket holders via SMS or Zalo and provide either a **100% ticket refund** or a free seat rebooking on the next confirmed sailing. Because winter suspensions can stretch **2 to 5 days**, keep a refundable flight backup on VASCO (SGN–VCS, 55 minutes) if you hold a fixed onward international flight.
