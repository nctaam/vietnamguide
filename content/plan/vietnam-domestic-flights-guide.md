# Vietnam Domestic Flights Guide (2026): Airlines & Tips

Vietnam stretches 1,650 km from the Chinese border to the Gulf of Thailand. Covering that distance by Reunification Express sleeper train takes 32 to 35 hours. Domestic aviation compresses the journey into a 2-hour hop across 22 operational commercial airports, making short flights indispensable when combining Hanoi, Central Vietnam, and Ho Chi Minh City on a two-week schedule.

Picking the wrong fare tier or miscalculating terminal transfers can still derail a tight itinerary. Budget carriers weigh cabin backpacks at the boarding gate and collect 500,000 to 900,000 VND ($20 to $36 USD) for overweight bags. Afternoon aircraft rotations frequently accumulate 30 to 90 minutes of cascading delays after 14:00, while Ho Chi Minh City's Domestic Terminal 3 (T3) at Tan Son Nhat requires a surface shuttle or taxi transfer from International Terminal 2 (T2).

### Vietnam Airlines (VN) vs Vietjet Air (VJ): Operational Realities

**Vietnam Airlines (VN)** remains the benchmark for reliable domestic transit. Standard Economy Lite and Economy Classic tickets include a 10 kg carry-on allowance (`1 cabin bag + 1 personal item`) plus one 23 kg checked bag and priority rebooking during weather disruptions. Watch out for the **Economy Super Lite** fare bucket (`0 kg` checked baggage included). On the flagship Hanoi to Ho Chi Minh City (`HAN–SGN`) trunk route, Vietnam Airlines flies widebody Airbus A350 and Boeing 787 jets, while its regional arm **VASCO (`0V`)** operates ATR-72 turboprops to Con Dao (`VCS`).

**Vietjet Air (VJ)** offers low base promo fares from 750,000 to 1,950,000 VND ($30 to $78 USD), but enforces its **7 kg combined carry-on weight limit** strictly. Staff weigh both your roller bag and personal item at check-in and at the boarding gate. Overweight cabin bags incur a **500,000 to 900,000 VND ($20 to $36 USD)** gate fee. Instead, pre-purchase a 20 kg checked bag online at least 3 hours before departure for **180,000 to 260,000 VND ($7 to $10.50 USD)**. Because tight aircraft turnarounds trigger 30 to 90 minute afternoon delays after 14:00, always book **06:00 to 10:00 morning departures**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The 40-Minute Domestic Check-In Cutoff Rule**: Across all 22 Vietnamese airports, domestic check-in and bag-drop counters close strictly **40 minutes before scheduled departure**, and boarding gates close **15 minutes before departure**.

## 2. Terminal Transfer Buffers: International T2 to Domestic T1 & T3

Self-transferring from an international arrival to a separate domestic flight requires clearing immigration, collecting luggage, passing customs, and switching terminals. Always build a minimum **3.0 to 3.5 hour connection buffer**.

* **Tan Son Nhat International Airport, Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`)**: With the 2025/2026 opening of **Domestic Terminal 3 (`T3`, 20 million passenger capacity)**, domestic trunk flights operated by **Vietnam Airlines (`VN`)** and **Vietjet Air (`VJ`)** now use **Terminal 3 (`T3`)**, while remaining domestic operations use **Domestic Terminal 1 (`T1`)**. Clearing **International Terminal 2 (`T2`)** immigration, baggage claim, and customs takes 60 to 90 minutes. Passengers departing from **T1** walk 5 minutes (`300 meters`) along the covered walkway. Travelers departing from **Terminal 3 (`T3`)** must take a 10 to 15 minute shuttle bus or taxi transfer (35,000 to 60,000 VND) across the **Phan Thuc Duyen / Cong Hoa** corridor. Check whether your e-ticket lists `T1` or `T3` before leaving `T2`.
* **Noi Bai International Airport, Hanoi (`HAN`)**: International flights land at **Terminal 2 (`T2`)** while domestic flights depart from **Terminal 1 (`T1`, Halls A/B/E)** across a 1.2 km roadway. Exit `T2` on the 1st Floor, walk to **Pillar 18**, and board the **free white shuttle bus** (running every 15 minutes from 06:00 to 23:30; 5-minute ride) to Domestic `T1`.
* **Da Nang International Airport (`DAD`)**: A covered outdoor walkway links **International Terminal 2 (`T2`)** and **Domestic Terminal 1 (`T1`)** across 250 meters (a 3-minute walk). Allow 2.5 hours for separate tickets.

## 3. Key Domestic Flight Corridors vs Rail & Highway Alternatives

Seven domestic flight corridors handle over 90% of visitor itineraries:

1. **Hanoi (`HAN`) to Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`)**: Over 50 daily flights cover 1,160 km in 2 hours 10 minutes (`1,100,000–2,800,000 VND`, `$44–$112 USD`).
2. **Hanoi (`HAN`) or Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`) to Da Nang (`DAD`)**: 1 hour 20 minutes (`850,000–2,100,000 VND`). Da Nang Airport sits 4 km from the city center (`60,000 VND` GrabCar) and 30 km north of Hoi An (45 minutes, `320,000–380,000 VND`).
3. **Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`) or Hanoi (`HAN`) to Phu Quoc (`PQC`)**: 60 minutes from `SGN` (`900,000–1,900,000 VND`) or 2 hours 10 minutes from `HAN` (`1,400,000–3,100,000 VND`).
4. **Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`) to Con Dao (`VCS`)**: Served by ATR-72 / VASCO (`0V`) flights in 55 minutes (`1,650,000–2,400,000 VND`). Book 4 to 6 weeks ahead, or fly via **Can Tho (`VCA`)** / take the **Tran De ferry** (`2.5 hours`, `310,000–390,000 VND`).
5. **Highland & Coastal Feeders (`DLI`, `CXR`, `VDH`, `HUI`)**: **Da Lat (`DLI`)** Lien Khuong Airport sits 30 km south of town (40 minutes, `250,000–300,000 VND` taxi). **Cam Ranh (`CXR`)** lies 35 km south of Nha Trang (45 minutes, `300,000–350,000 VND`). **Dong Hoi (`VDH`)** sits 45 km from Phong Nha caves (`500,000 VND` car), and **Hue (`HUI`)** sits 15 km south of the Citadel.

### Why Sa Pa, Ha Giang, Ninh Binh & Ha Long Have No Commercial Airports

Four northern highlights have **no commercial airports** inside their destinations:
* **Sa Pa**: 315 km from Hanoi via the **CT05 Expressway** (5.5 hours by VIP bus for `350,000–450,000 VND`).
* **Ha Giang**: 300 km north of Hanoi by highway sleeper or limousine bus (6 hours, `300,000–350,000 VND`).
* **Ninh Binh**: 95 km south of Hanoi (1.5 hours by limousine van for `150,000–250,000 VND` or 2 hours by train).
* **Ha Long Bay**: 155 km east of Hanoi via the **CT04 Expressway** (2 hours by limousine). **Van Don Airport (`VDO`)**, 55 km northeast, only handles select flights from Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`).

## 4. Passport Name Order, Power Banks & E-Cigarette Security Rules

Airport security officers and airline check-in agents across Vietnam enforce strict document verification and dangerous-goods screening. Avoid three frequent airport traps that delay boarding:

* **The GIVEN NAME + MIDDLE NAME Portal Trap**: On Vietnam Airlines, Vietjet Air, and Bamboo Airways booking portals, enter your passport surname in the Last Name (Họ) field and **both your first and middle names** in the Given Name (Tên đệm và Tên) field in the exact character order printed on your passport bio page. If your passport lists Surname SMITH and Given Names JOHN WILLIAM, type SMITH as Last Name and JOHN WILLIAM as Given Name without hyphens or punctuation. Omitting a middle name or swapping the sequence triggers a mandatory **100,000 to 350,000 VND ($4 to $14 USD)** name-correction stamp at the airport ticket desk before staff can print your boarding pass.
* **Lithium Power Banks (`<= 100Wh`)**: Spare lithium-ion power banks, drone batteries, and camera cells are **strictly banned in checked luggage** and must travel in your cabin bag. Every checked suitcase passes through high-resolution X-ray screening immediately behind the check-in counter; if screeners spot a power bank inside your hold bag, gate agents will page you over the loudspeaker to walk back to the security room and open your lock. Each power bank must display a legible factory capacity label rated at or below **100Wh (approximately 20,000mAh at 5V)**, while units between 100Wh and 160Wh require airline approval at check-in.
* **Vape & E-Cigarette Ban (`Resolution 173/2024/QH15`)**: Under National Assembly **Resolution 173/2024/QH15**, the importation, carriage, possession, and use of electronic cigarettes, vapes, heated tobacco devices, and refill pods are prohibited nationwide in Vietnam. Leave all vaping equipment at home to prevent confiscation or administrative fines at customs and domestic security checkpoints.

## 5. Step-by-Step Domestic Flight Booking & Airport Survival Protocol

1. **Book Direct on Official Airline Portals**: Purchase seats directly on vietnamairlines.com or vietjetair.com rather than third-party aggregators. Direct reservations allow you to bundle a 20 to 23 kg checked bag at official rates (180,000 to 260,000 VND) and receive immediate SMS or email alerts whenever gate assignments shift between Tan Son Nhat Terminal 1 and Terminal 3.
2. **Complete Web Check-In 24 Hours Prior**: Online check-in opens 24 hours before scheduled departure and closes 60 minutes prior. Even when holding a mobile QR boarding pass, travelers flying budget airlines or carrying a roller bag should verify whether gate staff require a weighed paper cabin tag at the check-in desk.
3. **Arrive 2 Hours Before Domestic Takeoff**: Reach the domestic terminal **2 hours before departure** on regular weekdays, or **2.5 to 3 hours ahead** during the Tet Lunar New Year and April 30 national holiday period. Domestic security screening at Hanoi, Da Nang, and Ho Chi Minh City requires removing shoes, belts, jackets, watches, and laptops into separate plastic trays.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I fly Vietnam Airlines or Vietjet Air for domestic routes in 2026?
Choose **Vietnam Airlines (VN)** when checking a 23 kg suitcase, flying after 14:00, or connecting to an international long-haul flight on the same day, as its 84% to 88% on-time performance and inclusive baggage easily justify the 300,000 to 600,000 VND ($12 to $24 USD) fare difference. Choose **Vietjet Air (VJ)** for early-morning departures between 06:00 and 10:00 when travelling light or when you pre-book a 20 kg checked bag online (180,000 to 260,000 VND) at least 3 hours before takeoff.

### Which domestic terminal do Vietnam Airlines and Vietjet use in Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`)?
Following the commissioning of **Domestic Terminal 3 (T3)** at Tan Son Nhat Airport, trunk domestic flights operated by **Vietnam Airlines (VN)** and **Vietjet Air (VJ)** depart from **Terminal 3 (T3)**, while remaining regional routes use **Domestic Terminal 1 (T1)**. Always inspect the terminal designation (T1 vs T3) printed on your e-ticket receipt 24 hours before flying.

### How much time do I need to transfer from an international arrival to a domestic flight in Vietnam?
Schedule a minimum **3.0 to 3.5 hour connection buffer** at Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) or Hanoi (HAN) when self-transferring on separate tickets. Clearing Terminal 2 passport control, waiting for checked bags, walking through customs (60 to 90 minutes total), and riding the inter-terminal shuttle bus to Terminal 1 or Terminal 3 (5 to 15 minutes) leaves no margin for a 90-minute connection before the strict **40-minute check-in cutoff**.

### Can I board a Vietnamese domestic flight using a digital boarding pass on my phone?
Yes, passengers departing from major airports including **Hanoi (HAN), Ho Chi Minh City (SGN), Da Nang (DAD), Cam Ranh (CXR), Phu Quoc (PQC), and Da Lat (DLI)** without checked bags can clear security and board using a mobile QR pass. Budget carriers still weigh 7 kg cabin bags at the departure gate.
