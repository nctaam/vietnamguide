# Quy Nhon to Hoi An Transport (2026): Train, Bus & Car

The northbound journey from Quy Nhon in Binh Dinh Province to Hoi An Ancient Town in Quang Nam covers 295 km along Vietnam's South Central Coast. Neither city has a commercial airport inside its municipal boundary, and neither sits directly on the main North-South Reunification Express railway line. Every overland option requires either a station transfer, a highway drop-off, or a private door-to-door charter.

Most independent travelers complete the trip in 4.5 to 6.5 hours door to door. Daytime trains from Dieu Tri Railway Station to Tra Kieu Station cut out the northern detour through Da Nang, while high-roof limousine sleeper buses run up National Highway QL1A and the Da Nang–Quang Ngai Expressway (CT01). Below is the 2026 operational breakdown of train schedules, bus terminals, transfer fees, and private car rates for moving north from Quy Nhon to Hoi An.

## Option 1: Train from Dieu Tri Station to Tra Kieu or Da Nang

Rail travel is the safest overland method between Binh Dinh and Quang Nam. Because Quy Nhon's city-center branch station rarely handles main-line intercity traffic, all northbound Reunification Express trains board at **Dieu Tri Railway Station** (*Ga Dieu Tri*), located 11 km west of downtown Quy Nhon in Tuy Phuoc District.

### Getting from Quy Nhon to Dieu Tri Station

Budget 20 minutes for the 11 km transfer from central Quy Nhon (Xuan Dieu Boulevard or Nguyen Hue Street) to Dieu Tri Station. A metered 4-seater Mai Linh, Sun Taxi, or Xanh SM electric taxi costs 150,000 to 180,000 VND ($6.00 to $7.20 USD). If you are checking out of a southern cliff resort in Bai Xep or Ghenh Rang—covered in detail in our [Where to Stay in Quy Nhon](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-quy-nhon/) guide—allow 30 minutes (18 km) and 240,000 to 280,000 VND ($9.60 to $11.20 USD) for the taxi to the station. Arrive 25 minutes before departure.

### Route A: Alighting at Tra Kieu Station (Shortest Route to Hoi An)

**Tra Kieu Railway Station** (*Ga Tra Kieu*) in Duy Xuyen District, Quang Nam, is the closest railhead to Hoi An. Stopping here saves 90 minutes of backtracking compared to riding all the way into Da Nang.

* **Active Northbound Trains:** Three northbound services stop at Tra Kieu: **SE8**, **SE10**, and **SE22**.
* **Rail Transit Time:** 4.5 hours (262 km track distance).
* **2026 Train Fares (Dieu Tri to Tra Kieu):**
  * **Air-Conditioned Soft Seat (*Ngoi mem dieu hoa*):** 195,000 to 310,000 VND ($7.80 to $12.40 USD).
  * **6-Berth Hard Sleeper (*Nam khoang 6*):** 290,000 to 410,000 VND ($11.60 to $16.40 USD).
  * **4-Berth Soft Sleeper (*Nam khoang 4*):** 350,000 to 490,000 VND ($14.00 to $19.60 USD).
* **Schedules:** Train SE22 departs Dieu Tri around 05:35 and reaches Tra Kieu at 10:10. Train SE8 departs Dieu Tri around 16:45 and arrives at Tra Kieu near 21:15. Train SE10 departs Dieu Tri at 02:40 and reaches Tra Kieu at 07:25.
* **Tra Kieu Station to Hoi An Transfer:** Tra Kieu sits 22 km southwest of Hoi An Ancient Town (30 minutes by road via Provincial Road DT608). Pre-booking a hotel pickup or taking a station taxi costs 250,000 to 300,000 VND ($10.00 to $12.00 USD). Because Tra Kieu is a rural town station, Grab car availability drops sharply after 20:00; if you ride the afternoon SE8 train arriving at 21:15, arrange a private car through your Hoi An hotel before boarding in Dieu Tri.

### Route B: Alighting at Da Nang Railway Station (More Schedules & The Vietage)

Express trains **SE2**, **SE4**, and **SE6** bypass Tra Kieu completely and run nonstop to **Da Nang Railway Station** (202 Hai Phong Street). Trains SE8, SE10, and SE22 also continue to Da Nang.

* **Rail Transit Time:** 5 to 5.5 hours (300 km track distance).
* **2026 Train Fares (Dieu Tri to Da Nang):** Soft seats cost 220,000 to 340,000 VND ($8.80 to $13.60 USD), while 4-berth soft sleepers cost 380,000 to 560,000 VND ($15.20 to $22.40 USD).
* **The Vietage by Anantara:** Attached to the afternoon northbound train departing Dieu Tri around 14:15 and arriving in Da Nang around 19:30, *The Vietage* is a 12-seat luxury pullman carriage. The 10,500,000 VND ($420.00 USD) one-way ticket includes a 3-course gourmet meal, free-flow wine and craft beer, and a 15-minute head and shoulder treatment.
* **Da Nang Station to Hoi An Transfer:** Da Nang Station sits 30 km north of Hoi An (45 minutes by road). Metered taxis and Grab cars wait outside the main gate 24 hours a day for 320,000 to 380,000 VND ($12.80 to $15.20 USD), while shared shuttle vans charge 130,000 to 150,000 VND ($5.20 to $6.00 USD) per seat.

> [!TIP]
> **Daytime Rail Strategy:** Book Soft Seat Coach 1 or 2 on morning Train SE22 from Dieu Tri (05:35) to Tra Kieu (10:10) for 210,000 VND ($8.40 USD). You will pass the coastal salt fields of Sa Huynh in morning light and check into your Hoi An hotel before 11:00.

## Option 2: Limousine Sleeper Buses (Son Tung, Phuong Trang & Queen Cafe)

Intercity coaches depart from **Quy Nhon Central Bus Station** (*Ben Xe Trung Tam Quy Nhon*, 71 Tay Son Street, Ghenh Rang Ward) and travel north along National Highway QL1A before joining the CT01 Da Nang–Quang Ngai Expressway. Total highway transit time is 5 to 5.5 hours.

1. **Son Tung Bus (*Nha Xe Son Tung*):** The highest-rated local operator on the Binh Dinh–Da Nang corridor. Son Tung runs 22-cabin VIP single-berth limousine sleepers and 34-berth standard sleepers departing every 60 to 90 minutes between 06:30 and 23:30. Tickets cost 260,000 to 380,000 VND ($10.40 to $15.20 USD) and include free shuttle pickup from central Quy Nhon hotels.
2. **Phuong Trang (Futa Bus Lines):** Operates standard 34-berth orange sleeper coaches from 71 Tay Son (4 to 6 daily departures). Fares run 250,000 to 290,000 VND ($10.00 to $11.60 USD).
3. **Queen Cafe & Hanh Cafe Open Tour Buses:** Northbound open-tour sleeper buses pass the QL1A Go Gang / Phu Tai intersection on the outskirts of Quy Nhon around 00:30 to 02:00 or 12:30 and drop passengers directly at company offices inside Hoi An (430 Cua Dai or Ly Thuong Kiet Street). Tickets cost 300,000 to 360,000 VND ($12.00 to $14.40 USD).

**The Vinh Dien Highway Drop-Off Rule:** Main-line coaches operated by Son Tung and Phuong Trang bound for Da Nang Central Bus Station do not detour inside Hoi An Ancient Town. Instruct the driver when boarding at 71 Tay Son to let you off at **Vinh Dien Town (Dien Ban, Quang Nam)** along the QL1A bypass. From the Vinh Dien intersection, Hoi An's Old Quarter is 9 km east (15 minutes, 120,000 to 150,000 VND or $4.80 to $6.00 USD by Grab).

## Option 3: Private Car Charter with Coastal Sightseeing Stops

A private car with an experienced driver is the only true door-to-door link from Quy Nhon beachfront hotels to Hoi An resorts. A direct highway run up QL1A and the CT01 Expressway takes 4.5 to 5 hours (295 km). If you add coastal sightseeing stops across Binh Dinh, Quang Ngai, and Quang Nam, plan on 7.5 hours total.

* **4-Seater Sedan (Toyota Vios / Honda City):** 3,100,000 to 3,300,000 VND ($124.00 to $132.00 USD).
* **7-Seater SUV/MPV (Toyota Innova Cross / Mitsubishi Xpander):** 3,400,000 to 3,800,000 VND ($136.00 to $152.00 USD).
* **16-Seater Minibus (Ford Transit / Hyundai Solati):** 4,400,000 to 4,900,000 VND ($176.00 to $196.00 USD).

1. **Banh It Cham Towers (20 km north of Quy Nhon):** Four 11th-century red-brick Champa towers overlooking the Kon River delta in Tuy Phuoc. Entry ticket: 20,000 VND ($0.80 USD); allow 40 minutes.
2. **Sa Huynh Salt Fields & Beach (110 km north of Quy Nhon):** Located at the southern border of Quang Ngai right beside QL1A. Morning stops between 08:30 and 10:30 show farmers raking sea salt grids alongside Sa Huynh Lagoon. Free access; allow 30 minutes.
3. **My Lai Peace Park & Memorial (185 km north of Quy Nhon):** Located in Son My Village, 13 km east of Quang Ngai city off QL24B. Entry ticket: 20,000 VND ($0.80 USD); allow 60 minutes.
4. **Tam Thanh Mural Village (245 km north of Quy Nhon):** A coastal fishing village near Tam Ky where over 100 houses and bamboo basket boats are painted with murals. Free entry (10,000 VND parking); from here, drive 50 km (60 minutes) north over the Cua Dai Bridge into Hoi An.

## Step-by-Step Booking & Transit Protocol

1. **Select Your Arrival Gateway:** Pick Tra Kieu Station (22 km from Hoi An) for trains SE22 or SE8, or Da Nang Station (30 km from Hoi An) for trains SE2, SE4, and SE6.
2. **Reserve Tickets 5 to 7 Days Ahead:** Soft-seat allocations for Tra Kieu sell out on weekends. Book via `dsvn.vn`, 12Go Asia, or Baolau (25,000 to 45,000 VND fee).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is there a direct flight from Quy Nhon to Hoi An or Da Nang?
No. Zero direct flights operate between Quy Nhon's Phu Cat Airport (UIH, 32 km north) and Da Nang Airport (DAD, 30 km north of Hoi An). Connecting via Ho Chi Minh City takes 5.5 to 7.5 hours and costs 2,400,000 to 4,200,000 VND ($96.00 to $168.00 USD).

### Should I alight at Tra Kieu or Da Nang Station for Hoi An?
Alight at Tra Kieu on morning train SE22 (arriving 10:10), cutting rail time to 4.5 hours and your taxi ride to 22 km (30 minutes, 250,000 to 300,000 VND). Stay onboard to Da Nang if you arrive after dark or need express trains SE2, SE4, or SE6, where 24-hour taxis wait outside the gate.
