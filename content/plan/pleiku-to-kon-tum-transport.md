# Pleiku to Kon Tum Transport (2026): Bus, Taxi & Ride

Connecting the provincial capitals of Gia Lai and Kon Tum provinces is the easiest overland transit in the Central Highlands. Only 48 km to 50 km of smooth, four-lane asphalt separates central Pleiku from the northern banks of the Dak Bla River in Kon Tum City. The entire route follows upgraded National Highway QL14—designated regionally as Asian Highway AH17—across rolling red-basalt plateaus at elevations between 520m and 780m. Because Kon Tum Province lacks a commercial civilian airport, Pleiku Airport (PXU) functions as the primary aviation gateway for both Kon Tum City and the pine-forested hill station of Mang Den at 1,200m elevation.

Geography works in the flyer's favor here. Pleiku Airport (PXU) sits at Thong Nhat Ward on the northern fringe of Pleiku, 6 km north of the city center along Pham Van Dong Boulevard. When you walk out of the PXU arrivals hall, you are already positioned on the northern approach to Highway QL14, leaving just 42 km of direct driving to reach Kon Tum's Wooden Church and riverside quarter. Transit takes 45 minutes by taxi or 50 to 60 minutes by local public bus.

## Option 1: The Pleiku–Kon Tum Interprovincial Public Bus

Public buses rarely excel in rural Vietnam, yet the Pleiku–Kon Tum corridor is a notable exception. Because thousands of civil servants, university students, and agricultural traders commute daily between the two twin highland cities, the interprovincial bus line operates with commuter-rail frequency.

### Schedule, Route, and Boarding Points

Buses depart every 20 to 30 minutes continuously from 05:15 until 18:30 daily. The southern origin is **Duc Long Gia Lai Bus Station** (*Bến xe Đức Long Gia Lai*, 43 Ly Nam De Street, Tra Ba Ward, roughly 4.5 km southeast of downtown Pleiku). Rather than backtracking south to Duc Long Station, travelers staying in central Pleiku—as detailed in our [Where to Stay in Pleiku](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-pleiku/) guide—can board along the northbound trunk corridor:

1. **Hung Vuong & Nguyen Tat Thanh Intersection:** Wait at the marked bus shelter 200m north of the central Pleiku roundabout between 05:30 and 18:30.
2. **Pham Van Dong Boulevard (Airport Turnoff):** Flyers carrying a single daypack can walk 650m straight out of the Pleiku Airport (PXU) terminal down Road 17/3 to the Pham Van Dong intersection. Flag any northbound yellow-and-white or green-and-white Kon Tum coaster bus passing the junction.
3. **Biển Hồ Junction (Chu Pah Approach):** Travelers finishing a morning walk around Bien Ho T'Nung volcanic crater lake (7 km north of Pleiku) can catch the bus directly at the highway entrance gate without returning to town.

### Ticketing and Arrival in Kon Tum

Tickets are sold on board by the conductor in cash. For the full 48 km run from Pleiku to Kon Tum City, the 2026 flat fare is 35,000 VND ($1.40 USD), or 30,000 VND ($1.20 USD) if boarding north of the Biển Hồ junction. Conductors charge an extra 10,000 to 15,000 VND ($0.40 to $0.60 USD) if you bring a bulky 60-liter rucksack or hard-shell suitcase that occupies a floor seat.

From Pleiku, the bus climbs gently through the tea estates and rubber plantations of **Chu Pah District**, crosses the provincial boundary into **Dak Ha District** (20 km south of Kon Tum), and enters Kon Tum City across the **Dak Bla Bridge** (*Cầu Đăk Bla*). You can ask the conductor to drop you off right after the bridge at the Duy Tan or Tran Hung Dao intersections—convenient for riverside hotels covered in [Where to Stay in Kon Tum](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-kon-tum/)—or ride to the northern terminus at **Kon Tum Bus Station** (*Bến xe Kon Tum*, 281 Phan Dinh Phung Street, Duy Tan Ward).

## Option 2: Direct Taxis & Xanh SM EVs from Pleiku Airport (PXU)

For passengers stepping off morning or midday flights from Hanoi (HAN) or Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) on Vietnam Airlines or VietJet Air, taking a taxi directly from the PXU terminal curb saves 30 to 40 minutes of waiting and luggage hauling.

### 2026 Airport Taxi Operators and Fixed Package Rates

Three primary licensed fleet operators stage directly outside the Pleiku Airport arrival gates, alongside app-based ride-hailing:

- **Xanh SM Electric Taxi (VinFast EV Fleet):** Operating quiet VinFast VF e34 and VF 5 electric crossovers. You can book directly via the Xanh SM mobile app upon landing (PXU has strong 5G coverage) or walk to the cyan-colored dispatch umbrella outside Arrivals. App fares to central Kon Tum (42 km) run 460,000 to 510,000 VND ($18.40 to $20.40 USD).
- **Mai Linh Taxi Gia Lai (0269 3838 383):** Green 4-seat sedans and 7-seat Toyota Innovas. While the meter rate (14,500 VND/km for the first 30 km, dropping to 12,000 VND/km thereafter) calculates to roughly 580,000 VND ($23.20 USD), drivers readily offer an official fixed airport-to-Kon-Tum package rate of 480,000 VND ($19.20 USD) for a 4-seater or 550,000 VND ($22.00 USD) for a 7-seater.
- **Sun Taxi Gia Lai (0269 379 7979):** White-and-orange budget sedans with slightly lower per-kilometer tariffs. Negotiated package transfers from PXU to any hotel in Kon Tum City average 450,000 VND ($18.00 USD).

### Continuing Directly from PXU Airport to Mang Den

If your itinerary skips Kon Tum City on arrival and heads straight up to the cool pine plateaus of **Mang Den** (1,200m elevation, 95 km northeast of PXU via QL14 and QL24 over Violak Pass approaches), all three airport taxi dispatchers offer fixed package charters. Expect to pay 950,000 to 1,050,000 VND ($38.00 to $42.00 USD) for a 4-seat sedan or 1,150,000 to 1,200,000 VND ($46.00 to $48.00 USD) for a 7-seat SUV. The total drive time from PXU to Mang Den is 1 hour and 50 minutes.

## Option 3: Shared 9-Seat Limousine Vans

Travelers flying solo who want door-to-door hotel delivery without paying a 480,000 VND private taxi fare can book a seat on a shared 9-seat Ford Transit DCar limousine or 16-seat shuttle. Operators including **Thinh Phat – Tuan Anh**, **Lam Sang Limousine**, and **Minh Quoc** run continuous feeder shuttles linking downtown Pleiku, Pleiku Airport (PXU), Kon Tum City, and Mang Den.

- **Pleiku / PXU to Kon Tum City Fare:** 80,000 to 120,000 VND ($3.20 to $4.80 USD) per seat.
- **Pleiku / PXU to Mang Den Fare:** 180,000 to 250,000 VND ($7.20 to $10.00 USD) per seat.
- **Booking Logistics:** Reserve your seat via WhatsApp or Zalo at least 3 hours before your flight lands, providing your flight number (such as VN1613 or VJ392). The driver waits in the PXU short-term parking lot (10,000 VND airport gate entry fee included in the ticket) and drops you directly at your hotel reception in Kon Tum 45 to 55 minutes after departure.

## Option 4: Self-Drive Motorbike Loop via Chu Dang Ya & Yaly

If you are basing yourself in Pleiku or executing a one-way loop through the northern highlands after arriving on the [Pleiku to Quy Nhon Transport](/plan/pleiku-to-quy-nhon-transport/) coastal climb, riding a motorbike between Pleiku and Kon Tum turns a 50-minute highway transfer into an outstanding half-day excursion.

### Rental Costs and Road Conditions

Semi-automatic 110cc Honda Wave Alpha or 125cc Honda AirBlade scooters rent in central Pleiku for 150,000 to 180,000 VND ($6.00 to $7.20 USD) per 24 hours, requiring a 1,000,000 to 2,000,000 VND cash deposit or passport retention. Fuel for the 85 km scenic detour route costs less than 50,000 VND ($2.00 USD, roughly 2.2 liters of RON 95 petrol).

National Highway QL14 has wide, clearly painted motorbike lanes (2.5m shoulder width) and a 60 km/h to 70 km/h two-wheel speed limit outside urban zones. However, traffic police (*CSGT*) maintain regular speed-camera checkpoints at the Chu Pah district center (Km 18 north of Pleiku) and at the southern entrance to Kon Tum (Km 43). Keep your speed strictly below 50 km/h whenever you pass the white urban boundary signs (*khu đông dân cư*).

### Step-by-Step Scenic Detour Sequence (85 km, 3.5 Hours Total)

Instead of riding straight up QL14 in under an hour, depart Pleiku at 07:30 and chain four volcanic and hydroelectric landmarks along the Chu Pah corridor:

1. **Biển Hồ T'Nưng (7 km north of Pleiku):** Turn right 1.5 km off QL14 to walk the pine-shaded causeway projecting into a flooded three-million-year-old volcanic crater lake (10,000 VND parking fee).
2. **Hàng Thông Trăm Tuổi (11 km north of Pleiku):** Ride 4 km along inter-commune asphalt past Nghia Hung to the hundred-year-old pine avenue flanking lush green Robusta tea plantations (*Đồi Chè Biển Hồ*) and the Korean-architecture **Buu Minh Pagoda**.
3. **Chư Đăng Ya Volcano (20 km northeast of Pleiku):** Continue 9 km into Chu Dang Ya village. In November, wild yellow sunflowers (*dã quỳ*) carpet the funnel-shaped volcanic rim. Walking to the crater lip takes 25 minutes (20,000 VND local conservation fee during festival weeks).
4. **Yaly Hydropower Dam & Waterfall (22 km west of QL14 via Provincial Road DT673):** Turn left at Phu Hoa town in Chu Pah and ride 15 km west to the massive 720 MW Yaly dam on the Se San River (40,000 VND entrance ticket to view the spillway and underground turbine cavern). From Yaly, loop northeast via DT675 (32 km of quiet rural tarmac through Sa Thay) directly into western Kon Tum City.

## Step-by-Step Airport Arrival & Transfer Checklist

1. **Confirm Your Final Base Before Landing:** Decide whether you are sleeping in riverside Kon Tum City (42 km north of PXU) or continuing up Highway QL24 to Mang Den (95 km northeast). Sharing a 7-seat taxi with another couple cuts the per-couple cost to 250,000 VND ($10.00 USD).
2. **Carry Small VND Notes:** Bus conductors on Pham Van Dong Street rarely have change for 500,000 VND polymer bills; keep 20,000 VND and 50,000 VND notes handy.
3. **Request Dak Bla Bridge Drop-Off:** Ask to hop off right after crossing the Dak Bla River for central Kon Tum guesthouses instead of riding 3 km further north to the bus station.
