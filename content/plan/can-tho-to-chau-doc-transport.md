# Can Tho to Chau Doc Transport: Bus, Limousine, and Cambodia Speedboat Guide

Traveling from Can Tho northwest toward the Cambodian border brings passengers to Chau Doc, a vital riverine trading post situated along the Bassac River (Song Hau) in An Giang Province. Spanning 120 km along National Route 91 (Quoc Lo 91 or QL91), this overland corridor links the central delta with the border crossing at Vinh Xuong - Kaam Samnor and the pilgrimage sites around Sam Mountain (Nui Sam). The route traverses fertile agricultural districts, moving through O Mon, Thot Not, and the provincial capital of Long Xuyen before reaching Chau Doc.

Overland travelers can choose from scheduled intercity coaches, 9-seat executive limousine vans, private car transfers, and self-directed motorcycle rides. FUTA Bus Lines (Phuong Trang) operates reliable hourly departures between the two cities, while private shared minivans offer rapid transit without long terminal detours. In addition, Chau Doc serves as the primary embarkation point for international riverine speedboats bound for Phnom Penh. This guide provides exact 2026 fares, terminal protocols, road conditions, border scam warnings, and onward transit logistics.

## Detailed Modality Comparison & Verified Schedules

### 1. FUTA Bus Lines (Phuong Trang) Express Buses

Phuong Trang runs regular passenger coaches connecting Can Tho directly to Chau Doc. The fleet consists of modern 29-seat reclining chair buses alongside occasional 34-berth sleeper vehicles configured with powerful air conditioning, luggage storage bays, and passenger seatbelts.

- **Fare**: 130,000 to 150,000 VND ($5.20–$6.00 USD) per one-way ticket.
- **Departure Terminal**: Ben xe Trung tam Can Tho, situated along QL1A, Hung Thanh Ward, Cai Rang District (Hotline: 1900 6067). FUTA provides complimentary shuttle van transfers (*xe trung chuyen*) from their Ninh Kieu ticket office at 91 Nguyen Thai Hoc to the main bus station.
- **Arrival Terminal**: Ben xe Chau Doc (Chau Doc Bus Terminal), located along National Route 91 in Vinh My Ward, Chau Doc City.
- **Departure Frequency**: Buses depart hourly from 06:00 until 19:00 daily.
- **Transit Duration**: 3 hours to 3 hours 30 minutes, influenced by crossing traffic around Long Xuyen.

FUTA fares include bottled water and wet wipes. Checked baggage covers two bags up to 20 kg per passenger without extra fees.

### 2. 9-Seat VIP Limousines (Shared Minivans)

Executive limousine services utilize converted Ford Transit vans fitted with nine plush leather captain chairs, USB device charging ports, and privacy curtains. These shuttles bypass the large intercity bus terminals, picking up guests from downtown Can Tho hotels and dropping off in central Chau Doc.

- **Fare**: 160,000 to 200,000 VND ($6.40–$8.00 USD). Front cockpit seats beside the driver cost 160,000 VND; central massaging seats run 200,000 VND.
- **Key Operators & Hotlines**:
  - **Thao Nga Limousine**: Hotline 0296 386 8868. Vans depart every 60 minutes from 05:30 to 18:30.
  - **Thinh Phat Limousine**: Hotline 1900 6681. Services run every two hours from 06:00 to 17:00.
  - **Hue Nghia Limousine**: Hotline 1900 636 497. Departures every 90 minutes throughout the day.
- **Transit Duration**: 2.5 hours to 3 hours.
- **Luggage Constraints**: Standard luggage racks in minivans hold soft duffels or 20-inch carry-on rollers easily. If traveling with hard-shell suitcases larger than 26 inches, reserve an extra baggage space during telephone booking.

### 3. Private Car Transfers & Intercity Taxis

Hiring an air-conditioned private vehicle provides the fastest, most flexible transit between Can Tho and Chau Doc:

- **Rates**: Standard 4-seat sedans (Toyota Vios, Honda City) run 1,100,000 to 1,300,000 VND ($44.00–$52.00 USD) all-inclusive. Larger 7-seat SUVs or MPVs (Toyota Fortuner, Mitsubishi Xpander) charge 1,400,000 to 1,500,000 VND ($56.00–$60.00 USD).
- **Authorized Providers**: Mai Linh Taxi Can Tho (0292 365 6565), Vinasun Taxi Can Tho (0292 3827 272), or GrabCar Intercity booked through the Grab smartphone app.
- **Travel Time**: 2 hours 15 minutes to 2.5 hours.
- **Custom Sightseeing Stops**: A private charter lets you stop along the way to explore the Bang Lang Stork Sanctuary in Thot Not district (admission: 20,000 VND) or the floating fish farms on the Hau River for a modest driver waiting fee of 80,000 VND per hour.

## Step-by-Step Terminal Arrival and Boarding Protocol

Follow this structured protocol to board your intercity bus smoothly:

1. **Arrive at the Terminal**: Take a metered taxi or Grab ride from your central Can Tho accommodation to Ben xe Trung tam Can Tho (6 km, 70,000 to 90,000 VND). Arrive at least 30 minutes before your scheduled departure time.
2. **Collect Paper Boarding Pass**: Walk directly into the primary terminal hall to the designated FUTA ticket counter. Show your smartphone booking reservation QR code. The counter clerk will print your paper ticket indicating your bus registration number, seat assignment, and departure bay.
3. **Stow Cargo Baggage**: Proceed outside to the bus departure bays. Hand suitcases and oversized rucksacks to the baggage handler. Make sure an adhesive bag tag is affixed to your luggage and keep the plastic claim stub safely in your pocket. Carry cash, electronics, and travel documents in your small daypack.
4. **Take Your Seat**: Step aboard the coach and present your ticket to the conductor. Locate your assigned seat number printed along the overhead bin. Fasten your safety belt and adjust your air-conditioning vent overhead.
5. **Arrival in Chau Doc**: The bus terminates at Ben xe Chau Doc on National Route 91. Retrieve your baggage from the lower cargo compartment using your claim ticket. Hail an official Mai Linh taxi (0296 3838 3838) waiting outside the terminal gates to reach your hotel in downtown Chau Doc (2 km west, fare roughly 35,000 to 45,000 VND).

## Highway Conditions, Road Works, and Toll Plaza Checkpoints

The 120 km corridor follows National Route 91, running northwest along the southern bank of the Hau River:

- **Highway Surface and Geometry**: QL91 features two lanes in each direction with a concrete barrier dividing traffic over most stretches. The pavement is generally smooth, though localized canal bridge approaches can produce abrupt bumps. Bypasses around Thot Not and Long Xuyen divert traffic away from busy market corridors.
- **Toll Checkpoints**: The journey passes through two Electronic Toll Collection (ETC) gantries: BOT Cai Rang and BOT Thot Not (KM 56+150). Private car operators should keep their automated VETC or ePass balance above 80,000 VND to ensure frictionless passage through express lanes.
- **Roadside Plazas**: Commercial buses pause for 15 minutes at registered highway rest areas near Long Xuyen. These stations feature clean washroom facilities, takeaway coffee (*ca phe sua da*) for 20,000 VND, fresh seasonal fruit, and hot rice dishes (*com tam*) for 45,000 VND.

## Safety Guidelines, Scam Warnings, and Cambodia Speedboat Connections

### Avoiding Unlicensed Taxi Scams at Chau Doc Terminal
Upon stepping off the bus at Ben xe Chau Doc, independent motorcycle taxi operators (*xe om*) often surround arriving foreign travelers. Some drivers claim that downtown hotels have closed or quote inflated prices of 150,000 VND for a short 2 km hop. Reject these solicitations calmly. Walk straight to the marked taxi bay to take a metered Mai Linh or Sao Do cab, which charges under 50,000 VND.

### Onward Border Transit to Cambodia (Phnom Penh Speedboat)
Chau Doc represents the primary gateway for international river travel into Cambodia:

- **Hang Chau Tourist Speedboat**: Departs daily at 07:30 from Chau Doc Passenger Pier (Ben tau Chau Doc) at 26 Le Loi Street. The journey travels up the Mekong River, arriving at Phnom Penh International Port (Sisowath Quay) around 12:30. The transit time is 5 hours, covering 130 km of waterway. One-way tickets cost $35 USD (approximately 880,000 to 920,000 VND).
- **Victoria Speedboat**: Operated by Victoria Chau Doc Hotel, this premium cruise launch departs at 07:00 from the private hotel pier at 1 Phu Hiep, Ward Chau Phu B. Standard tickets on this service run approximately $48 USD (1,200,000 VND), featuring complimentary breakfast pastries, tea, and guided immigration assistance.
- **Cambodia Visa Protocol**: The boat crew collects passenger passports prior to departure to prepare arrival cards. International travelers can obtain a 30-day Cambodia Tourist Visa on Arrival at the Vinh Xuong - Kaam Samnor river border checkpoint for $30 USD in crisp cash, or present a pre-approved Cambodian e-Visa printout. Avoid rogue border agents asking for health check surcharges of $5 USD.

### Sam Mountain Excursions (Nui Sam)
Located 6 km southwest of central Chau Doc, Sam Mountain hosts revered religious monuments including Ba Chua Xu Temple and Tay An Pagoda. Local metered taxis charge 90,000 to 110,000 VND each way. If taking a scenic cable car to the summit (Cap treo Nui Sam), round-trip tickets cost 150,000 VND ($6.00 USD) per adult.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the earliest bus departure from Can Tho to Chau Doc?
FUTA Bus Lines operates its first express coach from Ben xe Trung tam Can Tho at 06:00 daily. Limousine operators like Thao Nga start service slightly earlier, with initial passenger pickups commencing at 05:30.

### Can I catch the 07:30 Phnom Penh speedboat on the same morning I leave Can Tho?
No. Because overland travel from Can Tho to Chau Doc requires 2.5 to 3 hours, departing Can Tho at dawn does not leave enough margin for immigration check-in at 07:00. Plan to travel to Chau Doc the day before and stay overnight at a local riverside hotel.

### Where do express buses drop passengers in Chau Doc?
Coaches arrive at Ben xe Chau Doc on National Route 91 in Vinh My Ward, approximately 2 km from the riverfront center. Shared limousines offer the added convenience of dropping passengers directly at hotels inside the urban district.

### How much does a metered taxi cost from Chau Doc center to Sam Mountain?
A metered taxi ride from central Chau Doc to the foot of Sam Mountain (a distance of 6 km) costs between 90,000 and 110,000 VND ($3.60–$4.40 USD) one-way via Mai Linh Taxi.
