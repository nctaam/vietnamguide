Connecting the imperial citadel of Hue with the karst valleys of Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park requires a 210 km door-to-door journey north through Thua Thien Hue, Quang Tri, and Quang Binh provinces. Unlike major intercity hubs, Phong Nha town (officially **Son Trach** commune) has neither an airport nor a railway station; the nearest railhead and airport sit 45 km east on the coast in **Dong Hoi**. Consequently, travelers moving from Hue to Phong Nha choose between three distinct routing strategies: boarding a direct tourist bus (4 to 4.5 hours), turning the transit day into a guided **Demilitarized Zone (DMZ)** historical transfer (7 to 8 hours), or taking a Reunification Express train 166 km to Dong Hoi (2 hours 45 minutes to 3 hours 15 minutes) followed by a 45 km road transfer to the caves.

Each route serves a different travel priority. Direct sleeper buses and minivans drop passengers right outside hostels and riverfront lodges along Provincial Road DT20 in Son Trach for 250,000 to 350,000 VND ($10.00 to $14.00 USD). Meanwhile, a private car or DMZ sightseeing bus transforms an ordinary highway ride into a full-day tour of the 17th Parallel and the Vinh Moc Tunnels. Here is the complete 2026 guide to schedules, verified VND and USD fares, and transfer logistics from Hue to Phong Nha.

## Option 1: Direct Tourist Buses and Limousine Shuttles (4–4.5 Hours)

If your goal is to reach Son Trach quickly and cheaply without changing vehicles in Dong Hoi, book a direct tourist sleeper bus or 16-seater shuttle van. These coaches follow National Route QL1A and the Cam Lo–La Son Expressway north before branching west onto the Ho Chi Minh Highway (QL15) and Provincial Road DT20 straight into the national park buffer zone.

### Operators, Departure Times, and Fares

* **Morning Departures (07:00 to 07:30)**: Operated by **Queen Cafe**, **Camel Travel**, and **Tammys Shuttle**. Buses collect passengers from central Hue hotels or office storefronts along Chu Van An, Pham Ngu Lao, and Doi Can streets between 06:30 and 07:00, arriving in Son Trach between 11:30 and 12:00 (4 to 4.5 hours). Fares run 250,000 to 300,000 VND ($10.00 to $12.00 USD). Taking the 07:00 bus gets you to Phong Nha in time for an afternoon kayak trip or a boat ride into Phong Nha Cave (200,000 VND ticket plus 550,000 VND shared dragon boat).
* **Afternoon Departures (13:30 and 16:30)**: Operated by **Hung Long**, **Queen Cafe**, and **Camel Travel**. The 13:30 departure reaches Phong Nha around 18:00, while the 16:30 bus arrives around 20:45 to 21:00. Fares range from 250,000 to 350,000 VND ($10.00 to $14.00 USD) for standard sleeper bunks or 350,000 to 400,000 VND ($14.00 to $16.00 USD) for VIP 9-seat limousine vans.

### What to Expect on Board

Even though the Hue-to-Phong Nha leg takes only 4 to 4.5 hours during daylight, many open-tour buses operating this route are long-distance 34-berth or 22-cabin sleeper coaches continuing north toward Ninh Binh and Hanoi. Remove your shoes at the cabin steps. Store them in the plastic bag provided by the driver's assistant. Buses make one 15-minute rest stop near Dong Ha in Quang Tri Province (70 km north of Hue) before dropping passengers along the 3 km main strip of Son Trach town. If your farmstay lies across the Son River or in the Bong Lai Valley (8 to 12 km east of town), budget 100,000 to 180,000 VND ($4.00 to $7.20 USD) for a local taxi from the bus stop.

## Option 2: DMZ Sightseeing Transfer (Private Car or Group Tour Bus)

Between Hue and Phong Nha lies **Quang Tri Province**, the site of the former Demilitarized Zone (DMZ) along the 17th Parallel that divided North and South Vietnam from 1954 to 1975. Because these historic sites sit directly along the 210 km northern corridor, combining your transit day with a DMZ tour saves backtracking from Hue.

### Private Car with DMZ Sightseeing (2,200,000–2,900,000 VND)

Hiring a private vehicle with an English-speaking driver is the most flexible option for couples, families, or groups of three to six travelers:

* **4-Seater Sedan (Toyota Vios / Hyundai Accent)**: 2,200,000 to 2,400,000 VND ($88.00 to $96.00 USD total per vehicle, suited for 1 to 2 passengers with 2 suitcases).
* **7-Seater SUV / MPV (Toyota Innova / Fortuner / Mitsubishi Xpander)**: 2,500,000 to 2,900,000 VND ($100.00 to $116.00 USD total per vehicle, suitable for 3 to 4 passengers with full luggage).
* **16-Seater Van (Ford Transit / Hyundai Solati)**: 3,200,000 to 3,600,000 VND ($128.00 to $144.00 USD total per vehicle).
* **Point-to-Point Direct Car (No DMZ Stops)**: 1,800,000 to 2,200,000 VND ($72.00 to $88.00 USD), completing the 210 km drive in 3.5 to 4 hours.

### DMZ Group Sightseeing Bus (550,000–750,000 VND)

Solo travelers can book a seat on a dedicated one-way **Hue to Phong Nha DMZ Bus** (550,000 to 750,000 VND or $22.00 to $30.00 USD per person). Your big backpack stays locked in the luggage hold while an English-speaking guide escorts the group through each stop over 7 to 8 hours.

> [!TIP]
> **Ticket Cash at DMZ Sites:** Both private car charters and DMZ group bus fares exclude historical monument admission tickets. Keep at least 150,000 VND ($6.00 USD) per person in crisp 50,000 VND banknotes for the ticket booths at Hien Luong Bridge (50,000 VND) and the Vinh Moc Tunnels (50,000 VND), which do not accept international credit cards.

## Step-by-Step 7.5-Hour DMZ Transfer Protocol & Entry Fees

1. **07:30 to 08:00 – Hue Hotel Pickup**: Load your luggage into the trunk in central Hue and drive 60 km north along Highway QL1A into Quang Tri Province.
2. **09:00 – La Vang Holy Land**: Pause for 30 minutes at Vietnam's most important Catholic pilgrimage basilica in Hai Lang District (free admission).
3. **10:15 – Hien Luong Bridge & Ben Hai River (17th Parallel)**: Located 90 km north of Hue. Walk across the painted steel truss bridge that marked the Cold War border and inspect the loudspeaker propaganda towers and exhibition house (50,000 VND / $2.00 USD entry fee; 45 minutes).
4. **11:30 – Vinh Moc Tunnels**: Located 15 km northeast on the Cua Tung coast. Unlike the cramped fighting tunnels of Cu Chi, Vinh Moc is a 2 km subterranean village carved 12 to 23 meters deep into red laterite clay across three levels where 300 villagers lived for six years (50,000 VND / $2.00 USD entry fee; 60 to 75 minutes).
5. **13:00 to 16:30 – Coastal Lunch & Drive to Son Trach**: Stop for a roadside lunch near Cua Tung or Dong Ha (80,000 to 150,000 VND / $3.20 to $6.00 USD), then drive 105 km north past Dong Hoi and west along Route DT20 directly to your Phong Nha lodge. Private cars can also add a western detour via Route 9 to **Khe Sanh Combat Base** (50,000 VND entry) for a 450,000 to 600,000 VND ($18.00 to $24.00 USD) surcharge.

## Option 3: Train to Dong Hoi (SE2, SE4, SE8, SE20) + Cave Transfer

Travelers who dislike highway buses can ride the Reunification Express train 166 km north from **Hue Railway Station** (02 Bui Thi Xuan) to **Dong Hoi Railway Station**, then complete the final 45 km (50 to 75 minutes) west by road. For complete rail schedules on the coastal leg, see our [Hue to Dong Hoi Transport](/plan/hue-to-dong-hoi-transport/) guide.

### Step 1: Hue to Dong Hoi by Train (2h 45m – 3h 15m)

Multiple daily northbound trains connect Hue and Dong Hoi, including early morning **SE20** and **SE8**, mid-day **SE10** (12:15 to 15:18, 3 hours 03 minutes), and afternoon flagship expresses **SE4** (15:10 to 18:06, 2 hours 56 minutes) and **SE2** (15:54 to 18:41, 2 hours 47 minutes). Train fares run 130,000 to 175,000 VND ($5.20 to $7.00 USD) for an air-conditioned soft seat (NML) or 210,000 to 280,000 VND ($8.40 to $11.20 USD) for a sleeper berth.

### Step 2: Dong Hoi to Phong Nha (45 km)

Once you exit Dong Hoi Railway Station, you have two ways to cover the 45 km inland road to Son Trach:

* **Public Bus Route B4 (35,000 to 50,000 VND / $1.40 to $2.00 USD)**: The green-and-yellow B4 municipal bus runs between Dong Hoi and Phong Nha every 60 to 90 minutes from 05:30 to 17:00. Take a 5-minute taxi (40,000 to 50,000 VND / $1.60 to $2.00 USD) or walk 1.8 km east to the **Tran Hung Dao / Nam Ly Bus Stop** to board. The ride to Son Trach takes 75 minutes.
* **Private Taxi or GrabCar (500,000 to 650,000 VND / $20.00 to $26.00 USD)**: Metered Mai Linh, Sun Taxi, and Xanh SM electric cabs wait right outside Dong Hoi Station and drive straight to Phong Nha hotels in 50 minutes.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the best way to get from Hue to Phong Nha?
For solo budget travelers, the **07:00 direct tourist bus** (250,000 to 300,000 VND / $10.00 to $12.00 USD) is the easiest door-to-door transfer, arriving before noon. For couples or small groups, booking a **DMZ Private Car** (2,200,000 to 2,900,000 VND / $88.00 to $116.00 USD) or **DMZ Group Bus** (550,000 to 750,000 VND) turns a 4-hour transfer into a full historical excursion through the Vinh Moc Tunnels and 17th Parallel.

### Where should I go after Phong Nha?
