# Vietnam in April: Weather Transitions, Coastal Heat, and Holidays

April serves as the premier cross-country transition month across Vietnam's 1,650 km north-to-south corridor. In Northern Vietnam, winter drizzle clears across Hanoi and Ha Long Bay (`155 km` east on the CT04 expressway), bringing 23–28°C days, 65–90 mm of monthly rainfall, and high visibility across limestone karsts. Along the Central Coast, Hue, Da Nang, and Hoi An enter their prime beach window with 26–31°C temperatures, 35–50 mm of rainfall, and calm seas for the 18 km, 20-minute speedboat crossing to the Cham Islands (`650,000–850,000 VND` / `$25.50–$33.30 USD`).

Meanwhile, Southern Vietnam experiences pre-monsoon heat of 28–36°C in Ho Chi Minh City and the Mekong Delta before late-month showers appear. Travelers visiting in late April must also plan around the April 30 Reunification Day and May 1 International Labor Day holiday surge by booking trains and flights 14–21 days ahead.

## Regional Climate Breakdown & Microclimate Logistics

### 1. Northern Vietnam: Hanoi, Ha Long Bay & High Mountain Valleys

April clears away the damp northeast monsoon mist of February and March across the Red River Delta. In Hanoi, daytime temperatures sit at 23–28°C with 65–90 mm of monthly precipitation distributed across 6–8 brief rain days. Walking circuits around Hoan Kiem Lake, the **Temple of Literature (`70,000 VND` / `$2.75 USD`)**, and the **Imperial Citadel of Thang Long (`100,000 VND` / `$3.95 USD`)** stay comfortable from 07:30 to 17:00.

East of the capital along the **155 km CT04 Hanoi–Ha Long Expressway (`2.5 hours` by 9-seater limousine van, `350,000 VND` / `$13.75 USD`)**, April delivers clear atmospheric visibility across Ha Long Bay, Lan Ha Bay, and Bai Tu Long Bay. Sea surface temperatures rise to 24–25°C, allowing 90-minute kayaking excursions around Dark & Bright Cave (`Hang Sáng Tối`) and swimming at Titop Island (`400 steps` to the 110-meter viewing deck). Day cruises (`6 hours`) depart Tuan Chau International Marina at 08:30 for `1,150,000–1,450,000 VND` (`$45.10–$56.85 USD`), while 2-day, 1-night vessels cost `3,800,000–5,500,000 VND` (`$149.00–$215.70 USD`) per guest.

In the Northwest Highlands at **1,500 meters elevation**, **Sa Pa (`315 km` from Hanoi, `5.5 hours` via CT05)** and **Mu Cang Chai (`255–300 km` from Hanoi via QL32, `6 hours`)** record 18–24°C days and 12–15°C nights. VIP 22-cabin sleeper buses from Hanoi (`My Dinh` or `Hoan Kiem`) charge **`320,000–450,000 VND` (`$12.50–$17.65 USD`)** one-way. During late April, H'mong and Dao farmers repair earthen dikes and channel first mountain streams into terraced paddies in preparation for the May watering season (*mùa nước đổ*), while rhododendron blossoms (*hoa đỗ quyên*) bloom at 2,200–3,000 meters on the slopes of Fansipan (`3,143 meters`, cable car `800,000 VND` / `$31.40 USD` round-trip plus `150,000 VND` / `$5.90 USD` funicular).

### 2. Central Coast: Hue, Da Nang, Hoi An & Cham Islands

Protected by the Truong Son Range, Central Vietnam experiences its driest conditions of the year in April. Across **Hue, Da Nang, and Hoi An**, daytime highs range from **26°C to 31°C** with only **35–50 mm of total monthly rain** and 8.0 hours of daily sunshine.

In Hue (`100 km` north of Da Nang, `2 hours` by limousine van for `180,000 VND` / `$7.05 USD` or `2.5 hours` on the scenic Hai Van Pass heritage train `HD1/HD2` for `150,000 VND` / `$5.90 USD`), dry courtyards make morning tours of the **Imperial Citadel (`200,000 VND` / `$7.85 USD`)** and the royal tombs of **Minh Mang and Khai Dinh (`150,000 VND` / `$5.90 USD` each)** effortless. South of the Hai Van Pass (`500 meters` crest elevation), Da Nang's My Khe Beach and Hoi An's An Bang Beach (`30 km` south of Da Nang Airport `DAD`, `45 minutes` by private car at `350,000 VND` / `$13.75 USD`) record calm 26°C ocean water.

With offshore swells staying under 0.5 meters, high-speed canoes depart Cua Dai Pier (`6 km` east of Hoi An Ancient Town) daily at 08:30 for the **18 km, 20-minute crossing to the Cham Islands (`Cù Lao Chàm`)**. All-inclusive day packages—covering the speedboat transfer, UNESCO biosphere fee (`70,000 VND` / `$2.75 USD`), 2 snorkeling stops at Hon Dai and Bai Xep with 15–20 meters visibility, and a seafood lunch at Bai Chong—cost **`650,000 to 850,000 VND` (`$25.50–$33.30 USD`)** per person.

### 3. Southern Vietnam: Ho Chi Minh City, Mekong Delta & Island Crossings

April marks the thermal peak of the pre-monsoon dry season across Southern Vietnam. In **Ho Chi Minh City** and the **Mekong Delta (`Can Tho`, `Ben Tre`, `Chau Doc`)**, daytime temperatures reach **28–36°C** between 11:30 and 15:30, with afternoon relative humidity building from 65% in early April to 78% during the final 10 days of the month.

To beat midday heat in Southern Vietnam, depart District 1 at 06:30 for the **70 km, 90-minute drive to Cu Chi Tunnels (`Ben Dinh` or `Ben Duoc` sector, `125,000 VND` / `$4.90 USD` entry)** or board a **75 km, 2-hour GreenlinesDP hydrofoil (`320,000 VND` / `$12.50 USD` one-way)** to Vung Tau. In Can Tho (`170 km` southwest of HCMC, `3 hours` via the My Thuan–Can Tho CT01 expressway), charter a private sampan from Ninh Kieu Wharf at **05:30 (`250,000–350,000 VND` / `$9.80–$13.75 USD` for `2.5 hours`)** to visit the **Cai Rang Floating Market (`6 km` downstream)** while morning air sits at a pleasant 26°C.

Offshore in Kien Giang and Ba Ria-Vung Tau provinces, **Phu Quoc Island** and **Con Dao Archipelago** enjoy calm seas through April. High-speed catamarans from **Ha Tien to Phu Quoc (`45 km`, `75 minutes`, `310,000 VND` / `$12.20 USD`)** and from **Soc Trang (`Tran De`) to Con Dao (`90 km`, `2.5 hours`, `390,000–450,000 VND` / `$15.30–$17.65 USD`)** operate on full schedules, while green sea turtles (*Chelonia mydas*) begin early nesting surveys on Bay Canh Island (`Con Dao National Park` overnight ranger permit `600,000 VND` / `$23.50 USD` plus boat charter).

## Reunification Day & Labor Day Holiday Surge (April 30 – May 1)

Vietnam celebrates two back-to-back national public holidays on **April 30 (Reunification Day / *Ngày Thống nhất*)** and **May 1 (International Labor Day / *Ngày Quốc tế Lao động*)**. When combined with adjacent weekend days, this creates a **4-to-5-day nationwide holiday corridor (April 29 to May 3)** second only to Tet Lunar New Year in domestic passenger volume.

> [!WARNING]
> **Mandatory 14–21 Day Booking Lead Time for Late April**: Between April 28 and May 3, over 8 million domestic travelers depart Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City for Da Nang, Hoi An, Nha Trang, Phu Quoc, Ha Long Bay, and Sa Pa. Book all `SE1/SE3` sleeper trains, `HAN–DAD` flights, and coastal hotels **14 to 21 days in advance** to avoid sold-out corridors and 40–100% last-minute price spikes.

* **Domestic Flights (`HAN–DAD` `760 km` & `SGN–DAD` `610 km`)**: An 80-minute economy ticket between Hanoi (`HAN`) and Da Nang (`DAD`) or Ho Chi Minh City (`SGN`) and Da Nang normally costs **`1,200,000 VND` (`$47.00 USD`)** in mid-April, surging to **`2,400,000–3,100,000 VND` (`$94.00–$121.50 USD`)** one-way on April 29 and April 30. Arrive at Noi Bai (`HAN`) or Tan Son Nhat (`SGN`) domestic terminals **120 minutes prior to departure** during holiday days.
* **Vietnam Railways (`SE1/SE3` `1,726 km` North–South Corridor)**: 4-berth air-conditioned soft sleepers on the **688 km Hanoi–Hue overnight leg (`13.5 hours`)** rise from standard fares of `880,000–1,150,000 VND` (`$34.50–$45.10 USD`) to `1,450,000–1,750,000 VND` (`$56.85–$68.60 USD`) and sell out 21 days before April 30.
* **Coastal Resort & Theme Park Crowds**: 4-star beachfront hotels along My Khe (`Da Nang`) and Tran Phu (`Nha Trang`) increase nightly rates from `1,400,000 VND` (`$55.00 USD`) to `2,500,000–3,200,000 VND` (`$98.00–$125.50 USD`) between April 29 and May 2. Avoid visiting **Sun World Ba Na Hills (`25 km` west of Da Nang, `950,000 VND` / `$37.25 USD` cable car)** or **Fansipan Legend (`Sa Pa`, `800,000 VND` / `$31.40 USD`)** on April 30 and May 1, when cable car queues reach 60–90 minutes.

### Itinerary 1: The 8-Day North & Central Golden Shoulder Circuit
1. **Days 1–2: Hanoi Heritage & Street Food (`23–28°C`)**: Land at Noi Bai (`HAN`, `30 km` north of Old Quarter, `45 minutes` via Express Bus 86 at `45,000 VND` / `$1.75 USD` or GrabCar at `320,000 VND` / `$12.50 USD`). Tour the Temple of Literature (`70,000 VND` / `$2.75 USD`), Hoa Lo Prison (`50,000 VND` / `$1.95 USD`), and a 3-hour evening street food walk (`650,000 VND` / `$25.50 USD`).
2. **Days 3–4: Ha Long & Lan Ha Bay Overnight Cruise (`155 km`)**: Ride a limousine van 155 km (`2.5 hours`, `350,000 VND` / `$13.75 USD`) to Tuan Chau or Got Pier. Board a 2D1N cruise (`3,900,000 VND` / `$153.00 USD`) for kayaking in 25°C water and cave visits before returning to Hanoi Airport (`HAN`).
3. **Days 5–6: Hue Imperial Citadel & Hai Van Pass (`760 km` Flight + `100 km` Rail)**: Fly 760 km (`80 minutes`, `1,250,000 VND` / `$49.00 USD`) from Hanoi to Hue (`HUI`) or Da Nang (`DAD`). Spend 1.5 days exploring the Hue Citadel (`200,000 VND` / `$7.85 USD`) and Tu Duc Tomb (`150,000 VND` / `$5.90 USD`), then ride the 100 km heritage train (`2.5 hours`, `150,000 VND` / `$5.90 USD`) over the Hai Van Pass to Da Nang.
4. **Days 7–8: Hoi An Ancient Town & Cham Islands Snorkeling (`30 km` + `18 km` Boat)**: Transfer 30 km south (`45 minutes`, `350,000 VND` / `$13.75 USD`) to Hoi An. Tour the Ancient Town (`120,000 VND` / `$4.70 USD` heritage ticket) and take a 20-minute speedboat (`18 km`, `750,000 VND` / `$29.40 USD`) to the Cham Islands.

### Itinerary 2: The 6-Day Holiday-Proof Rural Escape (April 28 – May 3)
If your travel dates overlap the April 30 – May 1 holiday, bypass crowded beach cities and spend 6 days across **Mai Chau (`140 km` southwest of Hanoi, `3.5 hours`, `250,000 VND` / `$9.80 USD` van)**, **Pu Luong Nature Reserve (`45 km` further, `90 minutes`, valley eco-lodges `1,200,000–2,200,000 VND` / `$47.00–$86.25 USD` per night)**, and **Ninh Binh (`120 km` east, `2.5 hours`, `250,000 VND` / `$9.80 USD` boat)**.

1. **Days 1–2: Mai Chau Valley Cycling (`140 km` from Hanoi)**: Depart Hanoi at 07:30 via the CT08 highway (`3.5 hours`, `250,000 VND` / `$9.80 USD` limousine van) over Thung Khe Pass (`1,000 meters` elevation, `22°C`) down into Mai Chau Valley (`26°C`). Rent a bicycle (`50,000 VND/day` / `$1.95 USD`) to ride `15 km` of flat concrete paths linking Lac Village and Pom Coong Village while White Thai weavers work beneath wooden stilt houses (`homestay private bungalow 650,000–1,100,000 VND` / `$25.50–$43.15 USD` per night).
2. **Days 3–4: Pu Luong Terraces & Cham Stream Rafting (`45 km` transfer)**: Transfer 45 km south (`90 minutes`, `350,000 VND` / `$13.75 USD` private car) into **Pu Luong Nature Reserve (`Thanh Hoa Province`)**. Trek the **11 km Kho Muong Bat Cave to Ban Don trail (`4 hours`, local guide `450,000 VND` / `$17.65 USD`)**, inspect giant bamboo waterwheels (`guồng nước`) along Cham Stream (`100,000 VND` / `$3.95 USD` bamboo raft ride for `60 minutes`), and swim at Hieu Waterfall (`25,000 VND` / `$1.00 USD` village conservation fee).
3. **Days 5–6: Ninh Binh Early-Morning Karst Circuits (`120 km` east)**: Ride a morning shuttle van 120 km east (`2.5 hours`, `280,000 VND` / `$11.00 USD`) to Trang An and Tam Coc. Beat holiday bus crowds by boarding the **first 06:30 sampan departure at Trang An Wharf (`250,000 VND` / `$9.80 USD` for `3 hours` across 9 water caves on Route 1)** and climbing the **486 stone steps at Hang Mua (`100,000 VND` / `$3.95 USD`)** at 16:30 before returning 95 km north to Hanoi (`1.5 hours` via CT01, `250,000 VND` / `$9.80 USD`).

## Cultural Festivals & Agricultural Highlights in April

### 1. Hung Kings Commemoration Day (`Giỗ Tổ Hùng Vương`)
Observed nationwide on the **10th day of the 3rd Lunar Month (April 26, 2026 and April 16, 2027)**, this statutory public holiday honors the 18 Hung King dynasties at the **Hung Temple Historical Complex on Nghia Linh Mountain (`Viet Tri City`, `Phu Tho Province`, `85 km` northwest of Hanoi, `90 minutes` via the CT05 Noi Bai–Lao Cai Expressway)**. Pilgrims ascend 500 stone steps through pine forests to the Lower, Middle, and Upper Temples (`0 VND` shrine admission; electric tram shuttle `50,000 VND` / `$1.95 USD`), while ceremonial bronze drum ensembles and *Xoan* UNESCO folk singers perform in the festival courtyard.

### 2. Phong Nha & Central Highlands Coffee-to-Cacao Transition
In **Quang Binh Province (`500 km` south of Hanoi)**, April marks the peak dry-cave and river-swimming month at **Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park**. Travelers combine **Paradise Cave (`31 km` length, `250,000 VND` / `$9.80 USD`)** and **Dark Cave (`450,000 VND` / `$17.65 USD` zipline and mud bath)** with clear 25°C spring swims at **Mooc Spring (`Suối Nước Moọc`, `180,000 VND` / `$7.05 USD` full kayak and bamboo bridge ticket)**.

Further south at **1,500 meters in Da Lat (`300 km` northeast of HCMC, `6.5 hours` by sleeper bus at `320,000 VND` / `$12.50 USD` or `50 minutes` flight to `DLI`)**, April mornings average 18–24°C for riding the **7 km Da Lat–Trai Mat heritage cog railway (`150,000 VND` / `$5.90 USD` round-trip)** to Linh Phuoc Mosaic Pagoda.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is April the best month of the year to visit all of Vietnam?
**Yes.** April is the strongest all-country month because Northern Vietnam (`23–28°C`, `65–90 mm` rain) has finished its winter fog, Central Vietnam (`26–31°C`, `35–50 mm` rain) is in peak dry beach condition, and Southern Vietnam (`28–36°C`, `50–85 mm` rain) has not yet entered the heavy May–October afternoon monsoon.

### Can I see flooded mirror rice terraces in Sa Pa and Mu Cang Chai in April?
**Late April (`April 20–30`) marks the start of terrace preparation.** Farmers in **Sa Pa (`315 km` from Hanoi, `1,500 meters` elevation)** and **Mu Cang Chai (`300 km` from Hanoi)** begin repairing mud walls and diverting first spring water into higher-elevation paddies in late April (`18–24°C`), leading into the full watering peak (*mùa nước đổ*) throughout May.

### When does the Hung Kings Temple Festival take place in April?
The **Hung Kings Commemoration Day (`Giỗ Tổ Hùng Vương`, 10th day of the 3rd Lunar Month)** is a statutory national holiday falling on **April 26, 2026** (and **April 16, 2027**). Celebrations center on the Hung Temple Complex atop Nghia Linh Mountain in **Phu Tho Province (`85 km` northwest of Hanoi, `90 minutes` via the CT05 expressway, `220,000 VND` / `$8.60 USD` limousine van)**.

### Are the Cham Islands and Phu Quoc ferries calm in April?
**Yes.** Both the **18 km, 20-minute Cua Dai to Cham Islands speedboat (`650,000–850,000 VND` / `$25.50–$33.30 USD` day tour)** in Central Vietnam and the **45 km, 75-minute Ha Tien to Phu Quoc Superdong ferry (`310,000 VND` / `$12.20 USD`)** in Southern Vietnam operate under calm `< 0.5 meters` wave heights throughout April.
