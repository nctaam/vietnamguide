# Nha Trang to Da Lat Transport (2026): Bus vs Van

Connecting the sunny South-Central beachfront of **Nha Trang (sea level)** with the pine-forested Langbiang Plateau of **Da Lat (1,500 meters / 4,920 feet elevation)** spans **135 kilometers (84 miles)** along **National Route 27C (QL27C)**—known locally as the **"Sea and Flower Highway" (*Con đường nối Biển và Hoa*)**. Because there is **no railway line and no direct commercial flight** between Nha Trang and Da Lat, 100 percent of travelers ascend by road across the dramatic **29-kilometer Khanh Le Pass (*Đèo Khánh Lê*, also called Omega Pass or Hòn Giao Pass)**, which climbs from tropical sugarcane valleys at 50 meters elevation to a mist-shrouded mountain crest at **1,700 meters** before rolling through vegetable greenhouses into Da Lat.

Taking **3.5 to 4.0 hours** from city center to city center, this mountain transfer is one of the best-value overland links in Vietnam—provided you choose the right vehicle seat to prevent motion sickness on the 30+ hairpin curves and depart before late-afternoon cloud cover envelopes the summit. Here is the complete 2026 guide to 9-seat VIP limousine vans, sleeper coaches, private cars, and motorbike rentals between Nha Trang and Da Lat.

## Route Profile: How the 135 km Khanh Le Pass (QL27C) Climb Works

Every bus, limousine van, and private car between Nha Trang and Da Lat follows the same three-stage topographical profile along **National Highway 27C**:
1. **Stage 1 — Coastal Plains (Km 0 to Km 38, Nha Trang to Khánh Vĩnh, 45 Minutes)**: Flat four-lane and two-lane highway heading west past Dien Khanh ancient citadel and green pomelo orchards along the Cai River basin (28°C–33°C). Buses make a 15-minute restroom and snack stop at the foot of the mountain in **Khanh Vinh (Cau Ba)**.
2. **Stage 2 — The 29 km Khanh Le / Omega Pass Climb (Km 38 to Km 67, Elevation 50m -> 1,700m, 60 Minutes)**: The highway climbs 1,650 vertical meters up the eastern escarpment of the Bidoup–Nui Ba National Park massif. Paved two-lane asphalt winds past granite waterfalls, vertical jungle walls, and deep valley drops before crossing the Khanh Hoa – Lam Dong provincial boundary at the **1,700-meter Hon Giao summit** (where air temperatures drop abruptly to 13°C–17°C inside the cloud forest).
3. **Stage 3 — The Langbiang Pine Plateau (Km 67 to Km 135, Đạ Chais & Thái Phiên to Da Lat, 75 Minutes)**: Once over the crest, the road levels out across rolling pine hills, K'Ho minority villages, persimmon orchards, and the terraced flower greenhouses of **Thai Phien (Làng Hoa Thái Phiên)** before descending into **Xuan Huong Lake** in central Da Lat.

## Option 1: 9-Seat VIP Limousine Vans (190,000–260,000 VND) — Fastest & Most Comfortable Seat

For solo travelers and couples, a **9-to-11-seat Ford Transit or Hyundai Solati VIP Limousine** is noticeably smoother around the tight curves of Khanh Le Pass than a high-center-of-gravity 40-seat coach:
- **Top Verified Operators (2026)**:
  1. **Khanh Phong VIP Limousine (*Khánh Phong*)**: Nha Trang lounge at 02 Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai (right in the central tourist quarter); drops near Xuan Huong Lake (Nguyễn Văn Cừ) in Da Lat. Fare: **220,000 to 260,000 VND ($8.80–$10.40 USD)**.
  2. **Cuc Tung Limousine (*Cúc Tùng*) & Minh Tri Limousine (*Minh Trí*)**: Hourly departures from **07:00 AM to 17:00 PM**, including free hotel pickup along central Tran Phu Street and free shuttle drop-off at central Da Lat hotels. Fare: **190,000 to 240,000 VND ($7.60–$9.60 USD)**.
  3. **Cat Thien Hai Travel (*Cát Thiên Hải*)**: Popular mid-size 16-to-29-seat tourist bus with generous luggage space (170,000–200,000 VND).
- **Which Seat to Book to Avoid Car Sickness**: Always reserve **Middle Captain Seats 3, 4, or 5** (the first two individual leather recliners directly behind the driver). They sit directly between the front and rear axles and experience less than half the lateral sway of the back bench (**Seats 7, 8, and 9**).

## Option 2: FUTA Bus Lines (Phương Trang, 165,000–190,000 VND) — Best for Heavy Luggage & Door-to-Door Shuttles

If you are traveling with oversized **28-inch suitcases** (which can be tight inside the rear trunk of a 9-seat limousine van), Vietnam's orange national carrier **FUTA Bus Lines (*Phương Trang*)** is the gold standard:
- **2026 Fare & Schedule**: **165,000 to 190,000 VND ($6.60–$7.60 USD)**, departing every **60 minutes between 06:30 AM and 18:00 PM**.
- **How FUTA's Free Hotel Shuttle (*Xe Trung Chuyển*) Works at Both Ends**:
  - **In Nha Trang**: Although FUTA coaches now depart from the new **Nha Trang Southern Bus Station (*Bến xe Phía Nam Nha Trang*, Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard, 8 km west of the beach)**, FUTA sends a free orange 16-seat shuttle van to pick you up at your central Nha Trang hotel 45 to 60 minutes before departure (or you can check in at their downtown office at 176 Trần Quý Cáp).
  - **In Da Lat**: Coaches pull into the scenic hillside **Da Lat Intercity Bus Station (*Bến xe Liên Tỉnh Đà Lạt*, 01 Tô Hiến Thành, 2.5 km south of the market)**. Walk 20 meters from your coach to the orange **FUTA Transit Van lane (Xe Trung Chuyển)**, show your bus ticket stub, and a free minivan will drive you directly to the front door of your Da Lat hotel or homestay!

> [!TIP]
> **Book a Morning (07:30–10:00 AM) Departure — Never the Late Afternoon (16:00+) Bus**: On the 1,700-meter Khanh Le Pass, warm humid air rising from the South China Sea condenses into **thick mountain fog (*sương mù*) and afternoon rain squalls** nearly every day after **15:00 PM**, cutting visibility to under 15 meters and obscuring the canyon views. Departing Nha Trang between **07:30 and 10:00 AM** guarantees bright morning sun on the waterfall cliffs and puts you in Da Lat between **11:30 AM and 14:00 PM** right at hotel check-in time.

## Option 3: Private Car (1,400,000–1,700,000 VND) & Option 4: Motorbike Riding Over Khanh Le Pass

- **Why Families & Motion-Sensitive Travelers Book a Private Car**: On the 29 kilometers of Khanh Le Pass switchbacks, commercial van drivers maintain fast schedules. Chartering a private 4-seater sedan (**1,400,000–1,700,000 VND** / $56–$68 USD) or 7-seater SUV (**1,650,000–2,000,000 VND** / $66–$80 USD) lets you ask your driver to take the curves gently, stop for 15 minutes of fresh mountain air at the **Khanh Le Waterfall Overlook** and **Hon Giao Summit**, and visit **Linh Phuoc Glass-Bottle Pagoda (Chùa Linh Phước in Trai Mat, 8 km east of Da Lat directly on the entry route)** before checking into your hotel!
- **Direct Transfer from Cam Ranh Airport (CXR) to Da Lat**: If you land at **Cam Ranh Airport (CXR)** and want to head straight up to Da Lat without staying in Nha Trang, a pre-booked private car (1,500,000–1,800,000 VND, 155 km, 3.5 hours via the Dien Khanh bypass) saves you an hour compared to taking an airport bus into downtown Nha Trang first.
- **Self-Drive Scooter & Easy Rider Precautions on QL27C**: One-way motorbike rentals (Style Motorbikes, VietChallenge, 350,000–500,000 VND including luggage transfer) and **Easy Rider pillion tours (1,250,000–1,500,000 VND)** cross QL27C daily. Be aware of two strict rules:
  1. **35 km Without a Petrol Station**: Fill your fuel tank completely in **Khanh Vinh (Km 38)** before starting the pass—there are **zero gas stations** along the entire 50-kilometer wilderness stretch across Bidoup–Nui Ba National Park until you reach **Da Chais (Đạ Chais)** in Lam Dong!
  2. **20°C Temperature Plunge**: You will leave Nha Trang sweating in 32°C coastal humidity and hit 13°C–15°C windchill and wet mist at the 1,700m summit 75 minutes later. Keep a **windproof rain jacket and long trousers** at the top of your daypack before climbing the pass.

> [!WARNING]
> **October–November Monsoon Landslide Advisory on Khanh Le Pass**: During peak South-Central typhoon and monsoon weeks (**mid-October through late November**), prolonged torrential rain can trigger rockfalls along the steep granite cuts of Khanh Le Pass (QL27C). If highway authorities temporarily close QL27C for clearing, buses divert south via **Phan Rang and Ngoan Muc Pass (QL27, adding 1.5 hours)**. Check with your hotel reception the morning of travel if visiting during a November tropical depression.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How long does the bus or limousine van take from Nha Trang to Da Lat?
The **135-kilometer drive** across National Route 27C (*Khánh Lê Pass*) takes **3.5 to 4.0 hours** by 9-seat VIP limousine van or FUTA coach (including one 15-minute rest stop at the base of the mountain pass), and **3.0 to 3.5 hours** by private car.

### Is the road from Nha Trang to Da Lat winding, and how can I avoid motion sickness?
Yes—the middle **29-kilometer section across Khanh Le Pass** climbs from 50m to 1,700m elevation through roughly 35 hairpin turns. If you are prone to car sickness: **1)** book a **private car** or **Seats 3, 4, or 5** in the middle row of a 9-seat limousine van (avoiding the rear row and upper-deck sleeper bunks), **2)** take a motion-sickness tablet (*Nautamine* / dimenhydrinate, available at any Vietnamese pharmacy for 10,000 VND) **40 minutes before departure**, and **3)** avoid looking down at your phone screen between Km 38 and Km 67.

### Can I travel from Da Lat to Nha Trang in the reverse direction?
Yes. Every operator (Khanh Phong, Cúc Tùng, FUTA Phương Trang, and private cars) runs the exact same schedule and pricing **downhill from Da Lat to Nha Trang** (07:00 AM to 17:30 PM). When riding a motorbike **downhill from Da Lat to Nha Trang**, use low gears (2nd or 3rd gear on a semi-automatic) and pulse both brakes intermittently on the 29 km descent so your brake pads do not overheat.
