# Nha Trang to Da Lat Transport (2026): Bus vs Van

No trains or commercial flights link Nha Trang with Da Lat. Every traveler makes the **135 km (84-mile)** climb by road along **National Route 27C (QL27C)**, linking coastal Khanh Hoa at sea level with the pine-covered Langbiang Plateau at **1,500 meters**. Locals call QL27C the **"Sea and Flower Highway"** (*Con đường nối Biển và Hoa*).

Its centerpiece is the **29 km Khanh Le Pass (*Đèo Khánh Lê*, also known as Omega Pass or Hon Giao Pass)**, which rises abruptly from sugarcane flats at **50 meters** to a cloud-forest crest at **1,700 meters** before descending past vegetable greenhouses into Da Lat.

Door-to-door transit takes **3.5 to 4.0 hours**. Choosing the right seat matters on more than 35 tight switchbacks, and leaving before mid-afternoon mist closes over the ridge keeps the climb safe and clear. Below is the complete 2026 breakdown covering 9-seat VIP limousine vans (Khanh Phong, Nguyen Kim, Minh Tri, Cuc Tung), Phuong Trang (FUTA) coaches, private cars, and one-way motorbike rentals.

## Route Profile: 2026 QL27C Khanh Le Pass Elevation & Kilometer Splits

Every coach, limousine van, and private car heading inland from [Nha Trang](/destinations/nha-trang-travel-guide/) to [Da Lat](/destinations/da-lat-travel-guide/) climbs the same eastern escarpment of the Bidoup–Nui Ba massif along National Highway 27C.

| Route Milestone (QL27C) | Distance Marker | Elevation | Segment Drive Time | Terrain & Microclimate Conditions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nha Trang Center (Tran Phu)** | 0 km | 5 meters | Start | Coastal humidity (28°C–33°C); urban exit via Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard |
| **Dien Khanh Citadel Junction** | 14 km | 18 meters | 20 minutes | Flat four-lane corridor across the Cai River valley floor |
| **Khanh Vinh (Cau Ba Base Stop)** | 38 km | 50 meters | 25 minutes | Final petrol stations and mandatory 15 minutes bus restroom stop |
| **Khanh Le Waterfall Overlook** | 54 km | 920 meters | 30 minutes | Steep 8–10% grades, granite retaining walls, and sweeping canyon drops |
| **Hon Giao Summit (Provincial Border)** | 67 km | 1,700 meters | 30 minutes | Cold cloud forest (13°C–17°C); afternoon mist cuts visibility below 15 meters |
| **Da Chais Valley & K'Ho Villages** | 108 km | 1,460 meters | 45 minutes | Rolling pine ridge; first petrol pump after 70 km of wilderness |
| **Trai Mat & Linh Phuoc Pagoda** | 127 km | 1,520 meters | 20 minutes | Terraced Thai Phien flower greenhouses and scenic descent into town |
| **Central Da Lat (Xuan Huong Lake)** | 135 km | 1,500 meters | 15 minutes | Cool highland air (18°C–23°C); hotel drop-offs across Ward 1 and Ward 2 |

Watch the transition across Stage 2 carefully. Between **Km 38 (Khanh Vinh)** and **Km 67 (Hon Giao Pass)**, vehicles ascend **1,650 vertical meters** in **60 minutes**. Passengers feel rapid ear-pressure changes and an abrupt 15°C to 20°C temperature drop as the van climbs from tropical pomelo groves into montane pine forest.

## Option 1: 9-Seat VIP Limousine Vans (190,000 VND–280,000 VND) — Schedules & Operators

Solo travelers and couples almost always prefer a **9-seat or 11-seat Ford Transit / Hyundai Solati VIP Limousine** over a high-deck coach. Lower center of gravity means noticeably gentler cornering through the 35 switchbacks of Khanh Le Pass.

- **Top Verified Operators & 2026 Schedules**:
  1. **Nguyen Kim Limousine (*Nguyễn Kim*)**: Runs plush 9-seat VIP Solati cabins departing Nha Trang at **08:00, 10:00, 12:30, 14:30, and 16:30** from its central city office, dropping near central Da Lat hotels in **3.5 hours**. Fare: **240,000 VND to 280,000 VND ($9.60–$11.20 USD)** per seat.
  2. **Khanh Phong VIP Limousine (*Khánh Phong*)**: Operates from 02 Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai in the central tourist district with departures every 60 to 90 minutes between **07:30 AM and 17:00 PM**, terminating near Xuan Huong Lake (Nguyễn Văn Cừ). Fare: **220,000 VND to 260,000 VND ($8.80–$10.40 USD)**.
  3. **Minh Tri Limousine (*Minh Trí*) & Cuc Tung (*Cúc Tùng*)**: Hourly vans from **07:00 AM to 17:00 PM** with free hotel collection along central Tran Phu Street and complimentary central Da Lat hotel drop-offs within 4 km of Da Lat Market. Fare: **190,000 VND to 240,000 VND ($7.60–$9.60 USD)**.
  4. **Cat Thien Hai Travel (*Cát Thiên Hải*)**: Mid-size 16-seat to 29-seat tourist buses departing 5 times daily with wider cargo holds for oversized backpacks (**170,000 VND to 200,000 VND** / $6.80–$8.00 USD).
- **Best Seats to Prevent Motion Sickness**: Reserve **Middle Captain Seats 3, 4, or 5** directly behind the driver. Centered between the front and rear axles, those seats cut lateral sway in half compared with rear bench **Seats 7, 8, and 9**.
- **Luggage Limit Warning**: Standard 9-seat limousine vans allow **one 20 kg medium suitcase plus one daypack** per passenger. Carrying two 28-inch hard-shell bags or surfboards? Choose FUTA Bus Lines or a 7-seat SUV charter instead.

## Option 2: FUTA Bus Lines (Phương Trang, 165,000–190,000 VND) — Best for Heavy Luggage & Door-to-Door Shuttles

If you are traveling with oversized **28-inch suitcases** (which can be tight inside the rear trunk of a 9-seat limousine van), Vietnam's orange national carrier **FUTA Bus Lines (*Phương Trang*)** is the gold standard:
- **2026 Fare & Schedule**: **165,000 to 190,000 VND ($6.60–$7.60 USD)**, departing every **60 minutes between 06:30 AM and 18:00 PM**.
- **How FUTA's Free Hotel Shuttle (*Xe Trung Chuyển*) Works at Both Ends**:
  - **In Nha Trang**: Although FUTA coaches now depart from the new **Nha Trang Southern Bus Station (*Bến xe Phía Nam Nha Trang*, Vo Nguyen Giap Boulevard, 8 km west of the beach)**, FUTA sends a free orange 16-seat shuttle van to pick you up at your central Nha Trang hotel 45 to 60 minutes before departure (or you can check in at their downtown office at 176 Trần Quý Cáp).
  - **In Da Lat**: Coaches pull into the scenic hillside **Da Lat Intercity Bus Station (*Bến xe Liên Tỉnh Đà Lạt*, 01 Tô Hiến Thành, 2.5 km south of the market)**. Walk 20 meters from your coach to the orange **FUTA Transit Van lane (Xe Trung Chuyển)**, show your bus ticket stub, and a free minivan will drive you directly to the front door of your Da Lat hotel or homestay!

> [!TIP]
> **Book a Morning (07:30–10:00 AM) Departure — Never the Late Afternoon (16:00+) Bus**: On the 1,700-meter Khanh Le Pass, warm humid air rising from the South China Sea condenses into **thick mountain fog (*sương mù*) and afternoon rain squalls** nearly every day after **15:00 PM**, cutting visibility to under 15 meters and obscuring the canyon views. Departing Nha Trang between **07:30 and 10:00 AM** guarantees bright morning sun on the waterfall cliffs and puts you in Da Lat between **11:30 AM and 14:00 PM** right at hotel check-in time.

## Option 3: Private Car Tariffs (1,400,000 VND–2,800,000 VND) & Option 4: Motorbike Over Khanh Le Pass

Commercial limousine drivers run tight timetables on the 29 km Khanh Le switchbacks. Booking a private chauffeured car puts you in control of speed, rest stops, and mountain photography along the crest.

- **2026 Private Car Rates (Nha Trang Center → Central Da Lat, 135 km, 3.0–3.5 hours)**:
  - **4-Seat Sedan (Toyota Vios / Hyundai Accent)**: **1,400,000 VND to 1,700,000 VND ($56.00–$68.00 USD)**, comfortably fitting 2 to 3 adults plus two 24-inch suitcases.
  - **7-Seat SUV (Toyota Innova / Mitsubishi Xpander / Fortuner)**: **1,650,000 VND to 2,000,000 VND ($66.00–$80.00 USD)**, recommended for 3 to 5 passengers or travelers with three large suitcases.
  - **16-Seat Passenger Van (Ford Transit / Hyundai Solati)**: **2,400,000 VND to 2,800,000 VND ($96.00–$112.00 USD)** for extended families or cycling groups.
  - **Sightseeing Stop Add-Ons**: Brief 15-minute photo breaks at **Khanh Le Waterfall Overlook (Km 54)** and **Hon Giao Summit (Km 67)** cost 0 VND extra. Adding a 60-minute detour to **Linh Phuoc Glass-Bottle Pagoda (Trai Mat, Km 127, 8 km east of Da Lat, 0 VND admission)** or **Cau Dat Tea Hill (25 km southeast of Da Lat)** typically adds **150,000 VND to 250,000 VND ($6.00–$10.00 USD)**.
- **Direct Transfer from Cam Ranh Airport (CXR) to Da Lat**: Landing at **Cam Ranh Airport (CXR)** and heading straight to [Where to Stay in Da Lat](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-da-lat/)? A pre-booked private sedan (**1,500,000 VND to 1,800,000 VND / $60.00–$72.00 USD**) or 7-seat SUV (**1,800,000 VND to 2,150,000 VND / $72.00–$86.00 USD**) bypasses downtown Nha Trang via Dien Khanh (**155 km**, 3.5 hours), saving 60 minutes and **350,000 VND ($14.00 USD)** in airport transfer taxi fees.
- **Self-Drive Scooter & Easy Rider Precautions on QL27C**: One-way motorbike rentals (Style Motorbikes, VietChallenge, **350,000 VND to 500,000 VND / $14.00–$20.00 USD** including suitcase van forwarding) and **Easy Rider pillion tours (1,250,000 VND to 1,500,000 VND / $50.00–$60.00 USD)** cross QL27C daily. Respect two hard rules:
  1. **70 km Without a Petrol Station**: Fill your fuel tank completely in **Khanh Vinh (Km 38)** before ascending the pass. Zero roadside petrol pumps exist across the Bidoup–Nui Ba National Park wilderness until **Da Chais (Km 108)** in Lam Dong.
  2. **20°C Temperature Plunge**: You depart Nha Trang in 32°C coastal humidity and enter 13°C–15°C windchill and wet mist at the **1,700 meters** summit 75 minutes later. Pack a windproof rain shell, long pants, and closed-toe shoes on top of your daypack.

> [!WARNING]
> **October–November Monsoon Landslide Advisory on Khanh Le Pass**: During peak South-Central typhoon and monsoon weeks (**mid-October through late November**), prolonged torrential rain can trigger rockfalls along the steep granite cuts of Khanh Le Pass (QL27C). If highway authorities temporarily close QL27C for clearing, buses divert south via **Phan Rang and Ngoan Muc Pass (QL27, adding 1.5 hours)**. Check with your hotel reception the morning of travel if visiting during a November tropical depression.

## Frequently Asked Questions (2026)

### How long does the bus or limousine van take from Nha Trang to Da Lat?
Covering **135 km** via National Route 27C (*Khánh Lê Pass*) takes **3.5 to 4.0 hours** by 9-seat VIP limousine van (Nguyen Kim, Khanh Phong, Minh Tri) or Phuong Trang (FUTA) coach, including a 15-minute restroom halt at Khanh Vinh. Private cars make the same climb in **3.0 to 3.5 hours**.

### Is the road from Nha Trang to Da Lat winding, and how can I avoid motion sickness?
Yes. The central **29 km** climb across Khanh Le Pass rises from **50 meters** to **1,700 meters** through roughly 35 hairpin turns. Motion-sensitive travelers should book a private car or **Seats 3, 4, or 5** in a 9-seat limousine van, take a dimenhydrinate tablet (*Nautamine*, **10,000 VND** / $0.40 USD at any Vietnamese pharmacy) **40 minutes** before departure, and keep eyes on the horizon between Km 38 and Km 67.

### Can I travel from Da Lat to Nha Trang in the reverse direction?
Yes. Every operator (Nguyen Kim, Khanh Phong, Minh Tri, Cúc Tùng, FUTA Phương Trang, and private car fleets) runs identical schedules and tariffs downhill on the [Da Lat to Nha Trang Transport](/plan/da-lat-to-nha-trang-transport/) corridor from 07:00 AM to 17:30 PM. Motorbike riders descending from Da Lat should stay in 2nd or 3rd gear and alternate front and rear brakes down the 29 km grade to prevent brake fade.

### Does FUTA Bus Lines pick up directly from Nha Trang hotels or only the Southern Bus Station?
FUTA coaches depart physically from **Nha Trang Southern Bus Station** (**8 km** west of Tran Phu Beach), yet every ticket includes a **0 VND** complimentary 16-seat pickup minivan from central Nha Trang hotels **45 to 60 minutes** prior to departure. At the Da Lat end, a free orange FUTA transit van also drives you from Da Lat Intercity Station (**2.5 km** south of town) straight to your hotel lobby.
