## The 500 km Corridor: Direct Road to Son Trach vs Rail & Air via Dong Hoi

Covering the 500 km corridor from Hanoi south to Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park in Quang Binh Province requires choosing between a single-seat overnight highway coach and a two-stage transit via Dong Hoi city. Phong Nha's gateway town of Son Trach sits 45 km inland along Provincial Road DT20 (`Đường 20 Quyết Thắng`) near the Son River. It has neither a commercial airport nor a passenger railway station.

Direct overnight VIP 22-cabin and standard sleeper buses bypass coastal Dong Hoi by following the CT01 North–South Expressway and Ho Chi Minh Highway or turning west onto DT20, dropping passengers straight along Son Trach's main hostel and hotel strip in 8.5 to 9.5 hours. Conversely, travelers taking the Reunification Express overnight train (`SE1`, `SE3`, `SE19`) from Hanoi Railway Station (120 Le Duan) or flying from Noi Bai International Airport (`HAN`) land at Dong Hoi Railway Station or Dong Hoi Airport (`VDH`) on the coast, requiring a second 45 km road transfer (50 to 75 minutes) west into the karst valley.

## Option 1: Direct Overnight VIP 22-Cabin & Standard Sleeper Buses

Direct overnight buses dominate the Hanoi to Phong Nha route because they drop you within walking distance of Son Trach hostels and cave tour headquarters (Oxalis Adventure and Jungle Boss) without changing vehicles in Dong Hoi. Most travelers book one of four established operators:
* **Hung Long Bus (now operating under the Cố Hương / Coop Travel fleet):** The primary local Quang Binh commuter fleet departing from Nuoc Ngam Bus Station (`Bến xe Nước Ngầm`, 1 Ngoc Hoi) and Tran Khat Chan offices every evening between 19:00 and 21:30. Hung Long runs both standard 38-berth coaches (**350,000 VND to 400,000 VND / $14.00 to $16.00 USD**) and 22-cabin VIP coaches (**500,000 VND to 550,000 VND / $20.00 to $22.00 USD**) with an onboard toilet.
* **HK Buslines & Queen Cafe Open Bus:** Departing from Hanoi Old Quarter offices (such as 208 Tran Quang Khai or 70B Nguyen Huu Huan) between **18:30 and 19:30**. Standard open sleeper berths cost **350,000 VND to 420,000 VND ($14.00 to $16.80 USD)**, while enclosed 22-cabin VIP pods cost **480,000 VND to 550,000 VND ($19.20 to $22.00 USD)**.
* **Camel Travel:** Departs around 18:30 to 19:00 from Tran Khanh Du / Old Quarter pickup points, charging **380,000 VND to 520,000 VND ($15.20 to $20.80 USD)** depending on standard vs VIP cabin class.

Following the opening of new CT01 expressway sections through Ninh Binh, Thanh Hoa, Nghe An, and Ha Tinh, overnight buses cover the 490 km run faster than in previous years, pulling onto DT20 main street in Son Trach between **04:00 AM and 04:30 AM**. Before boarding, review our [Vietnam Sleeper Bus Guide](/plan/vietnam-sleeper-bus-guide/) for pod dimensions and shoe-bag protocols.

> [!WARNING]
> **The 04:00 AM Son Trach Drop-Off Reality:** When your sleeper bus drops you on DT20 in Phong Nha at 04:00 AM, the town is pitch dark and standard hotel check-in does not open until 13:00 or 14:00. Always message your Phong Nha homestay or hostel 24 hours in advance: properties such as Central Backpackers Hostel, Easy Tiger / Commander Lodge partners, Nguyen Shack, and Victory Road Villas provide unlocked early-morning rest hammocks, lobby sofas, or half-rate early check-in rooms (`150,000–250,000 VND` / `$6.00–$10.00 USD`) so you can sleep until 08:00 AM before cave briefings.

## Option 2: Reunification Express Overnight Trains to Dong Hoi (SE1, SE3, SE19)

If you cannot sleep on swaying highway coaches or need a full 190 cm flat bed, book an overnight Reunification Express train from **Hanoi Railway Station (Main Gate, 120 Le Duan Street)** down the 522 km rail line to **Dong Hoi Railway Station** (`Ga Đồng Hới`). Three prime evening departures wake you up at a civil morning hour:
* **Train `SE3` (Flagship Express):** Departs Hanoi at **19:20**, arriving in Dong Hoi at **05:20 AM** (10 hours).
* **Train `SE19` (Regional Tourist Express):** Departs Hanoi at **19:40**, arriving in Dong Hoi at **06:10 AM** (10 hours 30 minutes).
* **Train `SE1` (Express):** Departs Hanoi at **20:55**, arriving in Dong Hoi at **06:50 AM** (9 hours 55 minutes).

Inside each train composition, you can choose between state-run Vietnam Railways carriages and private luxury tourist carriages attached to the same locomotive:
1. **Vietnam Railways 4-Berth Soft Sleeper (`Khoang 4 điều hòa - AnL`):** Costs **650,000 VND to 1,050,000 VND ($26.00 to $42.00 USD)** per berth one-way (lower berths cost 50,000–80,000 VND more than upper berths). Avoid 6-berth hard sleepers (`BnL`, 480,000–620,000 VND) unless on a strict budget, as the middle and top bunks have only 55 cm of vertical headroom.
2. **Private Luxury 4-Berth Tourist Carriages (Lotus Train, Livitrans Express, Violette Train, Laman Express):** Coupled to `SE1` and `SE19`, priced at **1,450,000 VND to 1,750,000 VND ($58.00 to $70.00 USD)** per berth. These upgraded carriages offer thicker mattresses, noise insulation, complimentary snacks, craft beer or water, and dedicated cabin attendants maintaining clean toilets.

### The 45 km Transfer from Dong Hoi Station to Phong Nha
Once you step off at Dong Hoi Station between 05:20 AM and 06:50 AM, two road transfer options cover the 45 km west to Son Trach:
* **Private 4-Seat or 7-Seat Car / Grab Taxi:** Takes **50 minutes** door-to-door directly from the station forecourt to your Phong Nha hotel for **500,000 VND to 650,000 VND ($20.00 to $26.00 USD)** per vehicle. Pre-booking through your Phong Nha homestay guarantees a driver holding a name sign on the platform at 05:20 AM.
* **Public Bus Route B4 (Dong Hoi – Phong Nha):** Costs **35,000 VND to 50,000 VND ($1.40 to $2.00 USD)** per person and takes **75 minutes**. Note that Bus B4 does not enter Dong Hoi Train Station; you must walk or take a 40,000 VND Grab bike 2.5 km east to Nam Ly Bus Station (`Bến xe Nam Lý`) or catch it along Ly Thuong Kiet Street between 05:30 and 17:00.

> [!TIP]
> **Same-Morning Cave Tour Cutoff:** Most full-day Phong Nha national park treks (including Paradise Cave + Dark Cave, Hang En, and Tu Lan expeditions) conduct gear checks in Son Trach between **07:30 AM and 08:30 AM**. If you arrive on Train `SE1` at 06:50 AM, do not wait for Public Bus B4—take a pre-booked 500,000 VND private car straight from Dong Hoi Station so you reach Phong Nha by 07:40 AM.

## Option 3: Direct Flights from Hanoi (HAN) to Dong Hoi Airport (VDH)

Flying is the fastest daytime option when you want to avoid overnight transit altogether. **Vietnam Airlines** (and seasonal **Bamboo Airways** services) operates 1 to 2 daily direct flights from **Noi Bai International Airport (`HAN` Domestic Terminal 1)** to **Dong Hoi Airport (`VDH`)**. Flight time is **1 hour 15 minutes**, with one-way fares ranging from **1,200,000 VND to 2,400,000 VND ($48.00 to $96.00 USD)** including 23 kg checked baggage on Vietnam Airlines Classic tickets.

Dong Hoi Airport sits in Loc Ninh commune, 6 km north of Dong Hoi city center and **43 km east of Phong Nha town**. Because public buses do not enter the airport terminal apron, arrange a private 4-seat car (**500,000 VND to 600,000 VND / $20.00 to $24.00 USD**, 45 to 50 minutes) or book a metered Mai Linh / Sun Taxi outside arrivals to reach Son Trach. After exploring Phong Nha's caves, most southbound travelers continue 210 km down the coast via our [Hue to Phong Nha Transport](/plan/hue-to-phong-nha-transport/) and [Hanoi to Hue Transport](/plan/hanoi-to-hue-transport/) corridors.

## Step-by-Step Booking & Transit Protocol

1. **Choose Between Direct Drop-Off vs Flat-Bed Sleep:** Book a **VIP 22-Cabin Bus** (480,000–550,000 VND, departing 18:30–19:30) for a single-seat journey straight to DT20 in Son Trach. Book **Train `SE3` (19:20) or `SE19` (19:40) 4-Berth Soft Sleeper** (650,000–1,050,000 VND) if you prefer horizontal rail beds and a 05:20–06:10 AM arrival.
2. **Pre-Arrange Your 04:00 AM Rest Bed or 05:20 AM Station Car:** Message your Phong Nha accommodation the day before departure to confirm early lobby access (for bus arrivals) or a 500,000 VND station pickup car (for Dong Hoi train arrivals).
3. **Break Up the 500 km Route at Ninh Binh (Optional):** If 9.5 hours feels too long, ride a 2-hour limousine van to Tam Coc first, then board the evening `SE19` or `SE3` train from Ninh Binh Station at 21:30–22:00 down to Dong Hoi.
4. **Keep Small Cash for Rest Stops & Transfers:** Carry 200,000 VND in 10,000–50,000 VND bills for highway rest stops in Ha Nam / Nghe An or the 35,000–50,000 VND Public Bus B4 ticket.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the train from Hanoi go directly to Phong Nha town?
No. All Reunification Express trains (`SE1`, `SE3`, `SE19`) stop at **Dong Hoi Railway Station** near the coast, which is **45 km (50 minutes by car)** east of Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park (Son Trach town). You must take a 500,000–650,000 VND ($20.00–$26.00 USD) private taxi/Grab or the 35,000–50,000 VND ($1.40–$2.00 USD) Public Bus B4 from Dong Hoi into Phong Nha.

### Which sleeper bus goes directly from Hanoi Old Quarter to Phong Nha?
**HK Buslines**, **Queen Cafe**, and **Camel Travel** depart from Hanoi Old Quarter offices (around Tran Quang Khai and Nguyen Huu Huan) between 18:30 and 19:30, while **Hung Long** departs from Nuoc Ngam Station and Tran Khat Chan. All four drop passengers directly on DT20 main street in Son Trach between 04:00 AM and 04:30 AM for **350,000 VND to 550,000 VND ($14.00 to $22.00 USD)**.

### Can I start a Phong Nha cave tour on the same morning I arrive from Hanoi?
Yes. Overnight sleeper buses arrive in Son Trach by 04:30 AM, giving you 3.5 hours to rest and eat breakfast before Oxalis and Jungle Boss pickups begin at 08:00 AM. If taking the overnight train, book **`SE3` (arriving Dong Hoi at 05:20 AM)** or **`SE19` (arriving 06:10 AM)** and pre-book a 50-minute private car to reach Phong Nha before 07:30 AM.

### How much is a taxi or Grab from Dong Hoi Airport (VDH) or Train Station to Phong Nha?
A private pre-booked 4-seat car or Grab taxi from either Dong Hoi Train Station (45 km) or Dong Hoi Airport (43 km) to Phong Nha town costs **500,000 VND to 650,000 VND ($20.00 to $26.00 USD)** per vehicle one-way and takes 45 to 50 minutes.
