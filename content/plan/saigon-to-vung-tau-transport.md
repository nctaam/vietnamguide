# Saigon to Vung Tau Transport (2026): Ferry vs Limousine

The coastal peninsula of Vung Tau in Ba Ria–Vung Tau Province sits 105 km southeast of Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon) by highway and 85 km across the water via the Saigon River and Ganh Rai Bay. Because Vung Tau is the closest saltwater beach resort to Southern Vietnam's largest metropolis, weekend commuter demand is intense. You have four distinct transit options: the **Greenlines DP** high-speed river-and-bay catamaran ferry (320,000–350,000 VND), 9-seat VIP DCar limousine vans (160,000–230,000 VND), the Can Gio sea ferry route for motorbikes (70,000 VND per passenger plus 50,000 VND per bike), and private door-to-door cars (1,100,000–1,450,000 VND).

Choosing between the boat and the highway comes down to two variables: where your hotel is located in Vung Tau and what day of the week you travel. The ferry drops you directly at Front Beach (*Bãi Trước*) with zero highway congestion. Limousine vans run every 15 to 30 minutes from District 1 and Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN), delivering passengers straight to Back Beach (*Bãi Sau*) hotels along Thuy Van Street.

> **Tactical Booking Rule:** If your hotel is along Front Beach (*Bãi Trước*), Tran Phu Street, or Quang Trung Park, book the Greenlines DP ferry from Bach Dang Pier. If you booked a resort on Back Beach (*Bãi Sau* / Thuy Van Street) or are landing directly at Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN), take a 9-seat VIP DCar limousine van for direct lobby drop-off.

## Option 1: Greenlines DP High-Speed Ferry (Bach Dang Pier to Front Beach)

Skip the highway bottlenecks. The high-speed catamaran ferry operated by **Greenlines DP** (*Cánh Ngầm Greenlines DP*) is the most relaxing way to cover the 85 km waterway corridor between central Saigon and Vung Tau. Modern twin-hull vessels—specifically fleet numbers **K7**, **C3**, and **C5**, holding between 50 and 132 passengers—replace the older Soviet-era hydrofoils that retired years ago.

### Departure & Arrival Terminals
In Ho Chi Minh City, all Greenlines DP ferries depart from **Bach Dang Pier** (*Bến Bạch Đằng*), located at 10B Ton Duc Thang Street, Ben Nghe Ward, District 1. The terminal sits right across from the foot of Ham Nghi Street and 50 meters north of the historic Thu Ngu Flagpole, complete with an open-air riverside cafe where you wait before boarding.

After casting off, the vessel cruises past the container cranes of Cat Lai Port, enters the wide mangrove channels of the Soai Rap River estuary bordering the Can Gio Biosphere Reserve, and crosses the open waters of **Ganh Rai Bay** (*Vịnh Gành Rái*) for the final 25 minutes. In Vung Tau, the boat docks at **Ho May Park Pier** (*Bến Cáp Treo Hồ Mây*), located at 1A Tran Phu Street at the northern end of **Front Beach** (*Bãi Trước*). If you plan to continue offshore to Con Son Island on the [Vung Tau to Con Dao Ferry](/plan/vung-tau-to-con-dao-ferry/), note that the Con Dao Express departs from Cau Da Pier at 9 Ha Long Street, which is a short 3.5 km (50,000 VND taxi) ride south along the same coastal road.

### 2026 Fares & Daily Timetable
Ferry ticket prices depend on age and day of the week:
* **Monday to Friday (Weekdays):** 320,000 VND ($12.80 USD) for adults (12–62 years old); 270,000 VND ($10.80 USD) for seniors (63+ years old) and children (6–11 years old).
* **Saturday, Sunday & Public Holidays:** 350,000 VND ($14.00 USD) for adults; 300,000 VND ($12.00 USD) for seniors and children.
* **Infants (0–5 years old):** Free when sharing a seat with an adult.

Greenlines DP runs 4 to 5 daily sailings in each direction:
* **Saigon (Bach Dang) to Vung Tau:** 08:00, 10:00, 12:00, and 14:00 (plus an extra 16:00 departure on high-demand weekends).
* **Vung Tau (Ho May Pier) to Saigon:** 10:00, 12:00, 14:00, and 16:00 (with an extra 16:30 or 17:00 weekend boat during summer peak months).

Check in at Bach Dang Pier at least 20 minutes before departure. While the cabin is air-conditioned and equipped with bottled water and Wi-Fi, passengers are allowed to step onto the rear outer deck once the ferry clears the busy Saigon port zone. Book weekend tickets 3 to 5 days ahead, as the 08:00 and 10:00 Saturday morning boats sell out completely.

## Option 2: 9-Seat VIP DCar Limousine Vans (District 1 & Airport to Back Beach)

Limousine vans dominate overland transit to Vung Tau. These modified 16-seat Ford Transit and Hyundai Solati chassis are reconfigured into 9-seat VIP cabins with wide leather recliners, USB-A and USB-C charging ports at every seat, cold towels, and high-speed expressway transit.

### The Highway Corridor (105 km)
Leaving District 1, vans cross the Thu Thiem Tunnel or Saigon Bridge into Thu Duc City and join the **Ho Chi Minh City – Long Thanh – Dau Giay Expressway** (CT01) for 24 km before turning south onto **National Highway QL51** at the Long Thanh interchange. The QL51 corridor passes industrial zones in Nhon Trach and Phu My, the Ba Ria roundabout, and the Phuoc Tinh bridge before entering Vung Tau Peninsula. Total driving time is 2 hours during off-peak midday windows and 2.5 hours during Friday afternoon or Sunday evening rush hours.

### Top 5 Limousine Operators, Hubs & Fares
Departures run every 15 to 30 minutes from 04:30 to 21:30 daily. You never wait long.
1. **Hoa Mai Limousine:** The largest fleet on the route, operating out of 44 Nguyen Thai Binh Street and 83 Nguyen Thai Binh Street in District 1 (400 meters from Ben Thanh Market). Fares are 190,000–200,000 VND ($7.60–$8.00 USD) for standard 9-seat DCar vans and 160,000 VND ($6.40 USD) for 16-seat commuter vans.
2. **Toan Thang Limousine:** Departing from 11 Nguyen Thai Binh Street (District 1) and directly from **Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN)** domestic and international arrival lanes. Fares run 190,000–210,000 VND ($7.60–$8.40 USD).
3. **Vie Limo:** The highest-spec cabin option, featuring Boeing-style electric massage seats and a cafe lounge waiting room at 131 Nguyen Thai Binh Street, District 1. Fares range from 210,000 to 230,000 VND ($8.40–$9.20 USD), with direct drop-offs at major resorts in both Vung Tau and Long Hai / Ho Tram (250,000 VND).
4. **Huy Hoang Limousine:** Operates from 175 Nguyen Thai Binh Street (District 1) and Tan Son Nhat Airport (200,000 VND / $8.00 USD).
5. **Anh Quoc Limousine:** Specializes in the **Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN) to Vung Tau** corridor (200,000–220,000 VND / $8.00–$8.80 USD), picking up passengers at Column B6–B9 (Domestic Terminal) and Column 11–12 (International Terminal) every 30 minutes.

### Seat Selection and Back Beach Drop-Off
Inside a 9-seat DCar limousine, fares are tiered by row:
* **Front Cab Seats (Seats 1 & 2):** Tightest legroom and no recline (`160,000–180,000 VND`). Avoid if you are over `175 cm` tall.
* **Middle VIP Captain Chairs (Seats 3–6):** Individual leather armchairs with electric calf rests and massage functions (`200,000–230,000 VND`).
* **Rear Bench (Seats 7–9):** Three connected seats over the rear axle (`190,000–200,000 VND`).

Unlike the Greenlines DP ferry (which terminates at Front Beach), all five limousine operators include **free door-to-door drop-off and hotel pick-up** across Vung Tau city center and along **Back Beach** (*Bãi Sau* / Thuy Van Street). On the return leg to Saigon, shuttle minivans collect you at your hotel lobby `30 minutes` before highway departure.

## Option 3: Step-by-Step Motorbike Route via Can Gio – Vung Tau Sea Ferry

Riding a motorbike down National Highway `QL51` alongside 40-ton container trucks heading to Cai Mep – Thi Vai Port is dusty and stressful. Instead, two-wheel riders take the coastal island-hopping route through **Can Gio Mangrove Forest** and board the **Can Gio – Vung Tau Sea Ferry** (*Phà biển Cần Giờ – Vũng Tàu*).

### Step-by-Step Two-Wheel Itinerary (`95 km` total)
1. **Saigon to Binh Khanh Ferry (`18 km`, `35 minutes`):** Ride south through District 7 down Huynh Tan Phat Street to **Binh Khanh Ferry Terminal** (*Phà Bình Khánh*) in Nha Be (`6,000 VND` / `$0.24 USD` for motorbike + rider, `10-minute` crossing).
2. **Binh Khanh to Tac Suat Pier (`40 km`, `50 minutes`):** Follow tree-lined *Đường Rừng Sác* through the Can Gio Mangrove Biosphere Reserve to Can Thanh town and **Tac Suat Pier** (*Bến phà Tắc Suất*). Watch for strict `60 km/h` speed checks near Duyen Hai bridge.
3. **Can Gio to Vung Tau Sea Ferry (`15 km` across Ganh Rai Bay, `30 minutes`):** Board the double-deck roll-on/roll-off steel sea ferry carrying cars, motorbikes, and foot passengers across the bay to **Vung Tau Sea Ferry Terminal** at `127 Tran Phu Street` (*Bến Đá*, `3 km` north of Front Beach).

### 2026 Sea Ferry Fares & Schedule
* **Passenger Ticket:** `70,000 VND` (`$2.80 USD`) per person each way.
* **Motorbike / Scooter Fee:** `50,000 VND` (`$2.00 USD`) per bike (`120,000 VND` / `$4.80 USD` total for rider + scooter).
* **4-Seat Car Fee:** `350,000 VND` (`$14.00 USD`) plus `70,000 VND` per passenger.
* **Schedule:** Hourly sailings from `06:00` to `18:00` daily.

## Option 4: Private Cars & Airport Transfers

For families of `3 to 6` travelers or groups carrying surfboards and golf bags, booking a private car is cost-competitive with multiple ferry tickets:
* **4-Seat Sedan (Vios / City):** `1,100,000–1,200,000 VND` (`$44.00–$48.00 USD`) one way, including all `CT01` and `QL51` highway tolls (`65,000 VND`).
* **7-Seat SUV (Fortuner / Xpander):** `1,250,000–1,450,000 VND` (`$50.00–$58.00 USD`) one way.
* **9-Seat Private Limousine Charter:** `2,200,000–2,500,000 VND` (`$88.00–$100.00 USD`) one way.

If Vung Tau is the first stop on a southern itinerary that later heads west into the Mekong Delta, return to Ho Chi Minh City and connect via Western Bus Station (*Bến Xe Miền Tây*) using our [Ho Chi Minh City to Chau Doc Transport](/plan/ho-chi-minh-city-to-chau-doc-transport/) or [Can Tho to Chau Doc Transport](/plan/can-tho-to-chau-doc-transport/) guides.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I bring a suitcase or motorbike on the Greenlines DP ferry?
One standard `20–25 kg` suitcase per passenger is free on Greenlines DP vessels; oversized boxes or folding bicycles incur a `50,000–100,000 VND` (`$2.00–$4.00 USD`) fee. Gas motorbikes are only carried on the Can Gio – Vung Tau sea ferry.

### How do I travel from Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN) to Vung Tau after 21:30?
Airport limousine vans (Toan Thang, Anh Quoc, Huy Hoang) stop running between `21:30` and `22:00`. For flights landing after `22:00`, pre-book a private 4-seat sedan (`1,150,000 VND` / `$46.00 USD`) for the `1 hour 50 minute` night drive down `CT01` and `QL51`.
