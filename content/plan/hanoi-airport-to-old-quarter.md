# Hanoi Airport (HAN) to Old Quarter Transfer Guide (2026): Express Bus 86, Grab, Taxi & Late-Night Cars

Noi Bai International Airport (`HAN`) sits **28 to 30 kilometers north** of Hoan Kiem Lake and Hanoi's Old Quarter across the Red River (`Sông Hồng`). Depending on whether your driver takes the six-lane **Vo Nguyen Giap Expressway (`Đường Võ Nguyên Giáp`)** over the cable-stayed **Nhat Tan Bridge (`Cầu Nhật Tân`)** or cuts across **Chuong Duong Bridge (`Cầu Chương Dương`)**, the road transfer takes **35 to 50 minutes** during off-peak hours and **60 to 75 minutes** during weekday afternoon rush hour (`16:30–19:00`).

Because Hanoi does not yet have a direct metro line connecting Noi Bai Airport to the city center, every arriving passenger must choose between five road-based options: the tourist-engineered **Express Bus 86 (`45,000 VND` / `$1.80 USD`)**, public commuter buses (`15,000 VND` / `$0.60 USD`), app-dispatched **GrabCar or Xanh SM electric taxis (`300,000–370,000 VND` / `$12.00–$14.80 USD`)**, official metered airport taxis (`350,000–420,000 VND`), or **pre-booked hotel/Klook private transfers (`350,000–450,000 VND` / `$14.00–$18.00 USD`)**. This guide breaks down exact terminal pillar numbers, curb toll rules, weekend pedestrian barricades, and late-night arrival strategies for 2026.

## Option 1: Express Bus 86 (`Tuyến Buýt Chất Lượng Cao Số 86`) — The Best Budget Choice

Operated by Transerco specifically for air travelers, **Express Bus 86** is painted bright orange and is fundamentally different from Hanoi's standard commuter buses. Each vehicle features a low-floor step-free entry, bilingual English/Vietnamese electronic stop announcements, air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, and oversized multi-tier interior luggage racks designed to hold `24-inch` and `28-inch` hard-shell suitcases without extra baggage fees.

### Where to Board Express Bus 86 at Noi Bai (`T1` & `T2`)
- **Terminal 1 (`Domestic` — Vietnam Airlines, VietJet Air, Bamboo Airways)**: Bus 86 originates at **T1**. Walk out of Ground Floor (`Level 1`) Arrivals Hall `A`, `B`, or `E`, cross the inner taxi lane to the **second curb island**, and look for the bright orange **Bus 86 signpost**. Boarding at T1 guarantees you a seated spot before the bus drives `1.2 km` over to T2.
- **Terminal 2 (`International`)**: Exit Customs on Level 1 through Door `A1` or `A2`, turn **right**, walk past the currency exchange kiosks, and cross the first pedestrian zebra crossing to the outer island at **Pillar `1` and Pillar `2`**. Look for the orange uniform of the Transerco dispatcher.

### How to Pay and Exact Route Stops in the Old Quarter
- **Fare & Payment**: **`45,000 VND` (`~$1.80 USD`)** per person one-way. You do **not** buy tickets at a booth or online in advance; simply board the bus, store your bags, and pay cash directly to the conductor who walks down the aisle after departure, issuing a paper receipt. Keep **`50,000 VND` or `100,000 VND` polymer notes** handy (`500,000 VND` bills are difficult for conductors to change early in the morning).
- **Schedule**: Runs daily from **`06:10` to `22:15`** from Noi Bai Airport (`05:05` to `21:40` from Hanoi Railway Station back to the airport), departing every **`25 to 30 minutes`**.
- **Which Stop to Get Off for Your Hotel**:
  1. **Au Co – Nghi Tam (`Âu Cơ – Nghi Tàm`)**: Stop here for **Tay Ho (`West Lake`)** hotels, Sheraton Hanoi, and InterContinental Westlake.
  2. **Long Bien Bus Transshipment Point (`Điểm Trung Chuyển Long Biên` / `Yên Phụ`)**: Best stop for the **northern Old Quarter** (`Hang Dau`, `Hang Than`, `Hang Giay`, `Dong Xuan Market`, and ceramic mosaic wall).
  3. **162 Tran Nhat Duat (`162 Trần Nhật Duật` / `Cầu Chương Dương`)**: **The #1 primary stop for the heart of the Old Quarter**. From here, it is a flat `350 to 600-meter` walk (`5–8 minutes`) into `Ma May`, `Ta Hien`, `Luon Ngoc Quyen`, `Hang Buom`, and `Hang Bac`.
  4. **Hanoi Opera House (`Nhà Hát Lớn Hà Nội` — `1 Tràng Tiền`)**: Best stop for the **French Quarter**, Sofitel Legend Metropole, Capella Hanoi, and the southeastern shore of Hoan Kiem Lake (`Tràng Tiền`, `Lý Thái Tổ`).
  5. **Melia Hanoi Hotel (`44B Lý Thường Kiệt`)**: Best stop for southern Hoan Kiem business hotels, Hoa Lo Prison area, and `Quang Trung` / `Tran Hung Dao` boulevards.
  6. **Hanoi Railway Station (`Ga Hà Nội` — `120 Lê Duẩn`)**: Final terminus—ideal if you are transferring directly onto an overnight sleeper train (`SE1`, `SE3`, `SE19`, `SP3`) to Ninh Binh, Phong Nha, Hue, Da Nang, or Lao Cai.

> [!TIP]
> **Taking Bus 86 Back to the Airport (`Old Quarter -> HAN`)**: When returning to Noi Bai Airport from the Old Quarter, you **cannot** board at `162 Tran Nhat Duat` (which is southbound only). Instead, walk to the **republican-yellow Vietcombank Tower stop at `198 Trần Quang Khải`** (directly across from `Hàng Thùng` / `Lò Sũ` on the eastern edge of the Old Quarter) or board at the **Long Bien Transshipment Hub (`Yên Phụ`)**. Allow at least **3 hours before domestic flights** and **4 hours before international departures**.

## Option 2: GrabCar & Xanh SM Electric Taxi — Exact Pickup Pillars & Anti-Scam Rules

If you are traveling as a pair or group of three, booking a ride-hailing car via **Grab** or **Xanh SM (`GSM` cyan-blue VinFast electric taxis)** costs **`300,000 to 370,000 VND` (`~$12.00–$14.80 USD`)** for a 4-seater sedan or **`360,000 to 450,000 VND` (`~$14.40–$18.00 USD`)** for a 7-seater SUV—only slightly more per person than Bus 86, with direct transport to your hotel doorstep.

### Exact Ride-Hailing Pickup Zones (`T1` & `T2`)
1. **Terminal 2 (`International`)**: Order your car only **after** you have cleared immigration, collected your checked bags, and walked out into the public arrivals hall. Connect to the free `NoiBai Airport Free Wi-Fi` or activate your Viettel eSIM, set your pickup pin to **T2 International Arrivals (`Pillar 7` to `Pillar 9`, outer lane)**, and cross the first roadway to the middle concrete island.
2. **Terminal 1 (`Domestic`)**: Exit Hall `A` or Hall `E` on Level 1, cross to the outer curb lane, and walk toward **Pillar `14` to Pillar `16`** (or follow the green Xanh SM dispatch flags at the designated tech-car zone).

> [!WARNING]
> **Defeating the Noi Bai "Fake Grab Screenshot" Solicitation Scam**: Do **not** engage with men standing inside the T2 arrival glass doors wearing fake green lanyards or waving a phone showing a Grab logo. They are unlicensed private touts (`xe dù`). If you get into their car, they will demand **`600,000 to 900,000 VND`** mid-highway under the pretext of "highway bridge tolls and night fees." Only board a vehicle whose **physical yellow commercial license plate (`biển số vàng`)** matches the exact 5-digit number displayed inside your live Grab or Xanh SM app, and verify that the `10,000–15,000 VND` airport exit gate toll is either included or settled transparently at the barrier.

## Option 3: Pre-Booked Private Transfers & Official Metered Taxis

### When a Pre-Booked Car (`350,000–450,000 VND`) Beats Grab
Booking a private car in advance through your Old Quarter boutique hotel or via Klook/GetYourGuide (`$14.00–$18.00 USD` total for a 4-seater sedan; `$18.00–$22.00 USD` for a 7-seater SUV) is the **smartest tactical choice in three specific scenarios**:
1. **Flights Landing After `22:30`**: Express Bus 86 runs its last departure at `22:15`, and app-based Grab drivers at Noi Bai frequently cancel cashless bookings or apply `1.4x` late-night surge pricing after midnight. A pre-booked driver tracks your flight tail number (`VN`, `VJ`, `SQ`, `QR`, `EK`) and waits outside **T2 Exit `A1`/`A2`** holding a printed signboard with your name regardless of delays.
2. **Families & Heavy Luggage**: Note that compact 4-seater cars in Vietnam (`Hyundai Grand i10`, `Kia Morning`, `Toyota Vios`) have small trunks that can only fit **two `24-inch` suitcases**. If you have **three passengers or three large bags**, always book a **7-seater SUV (`Toyota Innova`, `Mitsubishi Xpander`, `VinFast VF 8`)** so the third row can be folded flat for luggage.
3. **Direct Old Quarter Hotel Coordination**: When your hotel dispatches its own driver, the driver already knows whether your specific alley (`ngõ`) is wide enough for a car or requires a porter with a luggage trolley at the corner.

### Official Metered Airport Taxis (`G7`, `Mai Linh`, `Noi Bai Taxi`)
If you prefer to walk out and hop straight into a waiting cab without an app, use the official airport taxi queue at **T2 Left Curb (`Pillar 10–14`)** or **T1 Curb (`Pillar 3–6`)**. Stick strictly to **Mai Linh Taxi** (distinctive kelly-green cars, hotline `1055`), **G7 Taxi** (silver/grey cars with red-and-blue roof sign, hotline `(024) 32 32 32 32`), or **Noi Bai Airport Taxi**. Confirm with the uniformed curb dispatcher whether you are paying the **fixed airport-to-Hoan-Kiem board tariff (`350,000–400,000 VND` all-in)** or the meter plus the `15,000 VND` airport exit toll.

## Crucial Weekend Rule: The Hoan Kiem Pedestrian Zone Barricades (`Fri 19:00 – Sun 24:00`)

Every **Friday evening from `19:00` until Sunday night at `24:00`**, Hanoi municipal police erect metal pedestrian barricades around the entirety of **Hoan Kiem Lake (`Đinh Tiên Hoàng`, `Lê Thái Tổ`, `Hàng Khay`)** as well as the central Old Quarter night-market axis (**`Hàng Đào` – `Hàng Ngang` – `Hàng Đường` – `Đồng Xuân`**) and the backpacker nightlife hub (**`Tạ Hiện` – `Lương Ngọc Quyến` – `Đào Duy Từ` – `Mã Mây`**). During these 53 hours every weekend, the center of the Old Quarter transforms into a car-free walking festival (`Phố Đi Bộ Hồ Gươm & Phố Cổ`).

Neither Grab, Xanh SM, metered taxis, nor private hotel sedans are legally permitted to drive past the police checkpoints during weekend walking hours. Do not argue with your driver or suspect a scam if they stop at a perimeter intersection such as **`Hàng Tre / Hàng Thùng`**, **`Trần Nhật Duật`**, **`Quảng Trường Đông Kinh Nghĩa Thục` (`Cầu Gỗ`)**, or **`Hàng Bông / Hàng Gai`**. Your hotel reception will be a flat **`100 to 300-meter` walk** inside the barrier.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **How to Coordinate Weekend Luggage Assistance**: If your flight lands on Friday evening, Saturday, or Sunday and you are carrying heavy `24–28 inch` suitcases, send a quick WhatsApp or Zalo message to your Old Quarter hotel receptionist as soon as your car crosses the cable-stayed **Nhat Tan Bridge (`Cầu Nhật Tân`)** (`15 minutes` before arrival). Give them your driver's license plate number so a hotel bellhop can walk out to the nearest police barricade with a hand trolley and wheel your luggage directly into the lobby.

## Option 4: Ultra-Budget Public City Buses (`Bus 17`, `Bus 90`, `Bus 07` & `VinBus E10`)

Travelers carrying only a compact `35L–40L` backpack who want to pay local commuter rates (`15,000 VND` / `~$0.60 USD`) have four public bus alternatives, though none match the comfort or central routing of Express Bus 86:
- **Public Bus `17` (`Noi Bai T1 <-> Long Bien Transshipment Hub`)**: Costs **`15,000 VND`** (`05:00–22:00`, every `15–20 minutes`). Unlike Bus 86, Bus 17 only picks up at the **Terminal 1 Domestic bus bay** (it does not loop through T2 International), takes a slow detour through Dong Anh and Gia Lam districts (`75 to 90 minutes` total), and lacks luggage racks.
- **Public Bus `90` (`Noi Bai T1/T2 <-> Kim Ma / Hao Nam Metro Station`)**: Costs **`15,000 VND`** (`05:30–22:10`). Drops passengers along **`Au Co` (`West Lake`), `Lieu Giai` (`Lotte Center Hanoi`), and `Kim Ma` (`Cat Linh Metro Line 2A`)**—useful if you are staying in Ba Dinh or Tay Ho rather than Hoan Kiem.
- **Public Bus `07` (`Noi Bai T1/T2 <-> Cau Giay`)**: Costs **`15,000 VND`** (`05:00–21:30`) and serves western Hanoi (`Cau Giay` university and business district).
- **VinBus `E10` Electric Bus (`Noi Bai T1/T2 <-> Vinhomes Ocean Park`)**: Costs **`9,000–15,000 VND`** via modern green VinFast electric city buses with USB charging ports, passing **`Long Bien Bridge / Aeon Mall Long Bien`** before continuing east across the Red River.

## Step-by-Step HowTo: Getting from Noi Bai (`HAN`) to the Old Quarter Smoothly

### Step 1: Clear Immigration, Collect Bags & Get `200,000 VND` in Small Notes
After collecting your luggage on Level 1 of Terminal 2 (`International`) or Terminal 1 (`Domestic`), withdraw **`2,900,000 VND`** from an official **VPBank, Vietcombank, or BIDV ATM** inside the arrivals hall (turning down Dynamic Currency Conversion) and break a large note by buying a `20,000 VND` bottle of water so you have `20,000–50,000 VND` bills ready for Bus 86 or airport tolls.

### Step 2: Select Your Transfer Based on Arrival Hour and Group Size
- **Solo or Couple arriving `06:10–21:45` with standard bags**: Walk to **T2 Pillar `1–2`** (or **T1 Lobby A/B/E**) and board the bright orange **Express Bus 86** (`45,000 VND` cash onboard).
- **2–4 Travelers arriving with a working data SIM/eSIM**: Open the **Grab** or **Xanh SM** app *after* exiting the terminal doors, book a 4-seater (`300k–370k VND`) or 7-seater (`360k–450k VND`), and walk to **T2 Pillar `7–9`** (or **T1 Pillar `14–16`**).
- **Arriving after `22:15` or with Kids/Elderly**: Pre-book a private car (`350k–450k VND`) 24 hours ahead and meet your driver holding your name board outside **T2 Exit `A1`/`A2`**.

### Step 3: Verify License Plates or Alight at the Correct Old Quarter Stop
- If riding **Bus 86**, watch the electronic display and disembark at **`162 Tran Nhat Duat`** for the heart of the Old Quarter (`Ma May / Ta Hien / Hang Bac`) or **`Hanoi Opera House`** for the French Quarter.
- If riding **Grab/Xanh SM**, verify the yellow commercial license plate (`biển số vàng`) before loading your trunk, and prepare for a perimeter drop-off if arriving on Friday, Saturday, or Sunday evening after `19:00`.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How much is a Grab from Hanoi Airport (`HAN`) to the Old Quarter in 2026?
Expect to pay **`300,000 to 370,000 VND` (`~$12.00–$14.80 USD`)** for a standard **GrabCar 4-seater** or **Xanh SM electric sedan** from Noi Bai International Airport (`HAN`) to Hoan Kiem District. Larger **7-seater SUVs** run **`360,000 to 450,000 VND` (`~$14.40–$18.00 USD`)**, which includes the mandatory `10,000–15,000 VND` airport exit gate toll.

### Is Express Bus 86 still running from Hanoi Airport to the Old Quarter?
Yes, every single day. The bright orange **Express Bus 86** operates between **`06:10` and `22:15`** from Noi Bai (`T1` and `T2 Pillar 1–2`) directly to **`162 Tran Nhat Duat` (`Old Quarter`)**, **`Hanoi Opera House`**, and **`Hanoi Railway Station (`120 Le Duan`)`**, departing every `25–30 minutes` for **`45,000 VND` (`~$1.80 USD`)** cash paid onboard.

### How can I transfer between Terminal 1 (Domestic) and Terminal 2 (International) at Hanoi Airport?
Take the **free green-and-yellow NIA shuttle bus**. It links **Terminal 1 (Level 1, Lobby A)** and **Terminal 2 (Level 1, West Wing Pillar 18)** every **`15 to 20 minutes`** from `06:00` to `01:00` in just `5 minutes`. Never pay a curb tout `100,000–200,000 VND` to drive the `1.2 km` road between terminals.

### What is the best way to get from Hanoi Airport to the Old Quarter late at night (after `22:30`)?
Pre-book a private car (`350,000–450,000 VND` / `$14–$18 USD`). Because Express Bus 86 stops running at **`22:15`** and late-night Grab drivers frequently cancel cashless trips after midnight, arranging a driver via your Old Quarter hotel or Klook guarantees someone waiting at **T2 Exit `A1`/`A2`** with a printed name sign regardless of flight delays.
