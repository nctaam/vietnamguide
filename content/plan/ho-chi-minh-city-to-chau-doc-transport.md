# Ho Chi Minh City to Chau Doc Transport (2026): Bus & Van

Sitting 245 to 250 km southwest of Ho Chi Minh City along the banks of the Bassac (Hau) River right against the Cambodian frontier, Chau Doc in An Giang Province is the western anchor of the Mekong Delta. Because there is no commercial airport or railway line in An Giang, 100% of overland transit between Saigon and Chau Doc moves by highway. Travelers choose between three main options: 34-berth VIP sleeper coaches operated by **Phuong Trang (Futa Bus Lines)**, **Hue Nghia**, and **Hung Cuong** (200,000–250,000 VND / $8.00–$10.00 USD), 9-seat VIP limousine vans (260,000–320,000 VND / $10.40–$12.80 USD), or private door-to-door cars (2,400,000–2,900,000 VND / $96.00–$116.00 USD).

Recent infrastructure upgrades have cut an hour off the journey. With the completion of the full **Ho Chi Minh City – Trung Luong – My Thuan Expressway (CT01)**, the **My Thuan 2 Bridge**, and the **Long Xuyen Bypass**, buses now cover the 250 km corridor in 5.5 to 6 hours instead of the grueling 7 to 8 hours required five years ago.

> **Tactical Booking Rule:** Never pay for a private taxi all the way to Western Bus Station (*Bến Xe Miền Tây*) in Binh Tan District or out from New Chau Doc Bus Station on Highway QL91. Book Phuong Trang (Futa Bus Lines), check in at their central District 5 lounge (231 Le Hong Phong Street) for the free transfer van, and use their free orange shuttle minivans on arrival in Chau Doc for direct hotel drop-off.

## The Upgraded Highway Corridor (245–250 km)

Understanding the highway route helps explain why daytime and overnight buses move much faster in 2026 than older guidebooks claim. Coaches pull out of **Western Bus Station** (*Bến Xe Miền Tây*) in Binh Tan District and join the **CT01 Expressway** at the Binh Thuan interchange. For the next 105 km across Long An and Tien Giang provinces, buses cruise at 80–90 km/h on divided four-lane highway, crossing the Tien River over the cable-stayed **My Thuan 2 Bridge**.

Instead of detouring south through Can Tho, Chau Doc-bound buses angle northwest across Dong Thap Province via QL80 and the **Cao Lanh Bridge** or **Vam Cong Bridge** over the Hau River into An Giang Province. After skirting Long Xuyen City on the 15.3 km **Long Xuyen Bypass** (opened in mid-2024), coaches follow **National Highway QL91** through Chau Phu directly into Chau Doc. Every sleeper bus makes a single 20-minute rest stop halfway along the route at the **Futa StopStation** in Cai Be (Tien Giang) or Lap Vo (Dong Thap), where clean restrooms and hot noodle soup (45,000–65,000 VND) are available.

## Option 1: Phuong Trang (Futa Bus Lines) 34-Berth Sleeper Coaches

The bright orange coaches of **Phuong Trang (Futa Bus Lines)** are the gold standard for reliability, safety, and transparent pricing on the Saigon–Chau Doc run. Futa operates 34-berth curtains-drawn VIP sleeper coaches (*Giường nằm 34 chỗ*) with privacy curtains, individual USB charging ports, reading lamps, and fleece blankets.

### How the District 5 Pickup & Western Bus Station Transfer Works
While all long-distance Mekong buses officially depart from **Western Bus Station** (*Bến Xe Miền Tây*, located at 395 Kinh Duong Vuong Street, An Lac Ward, Binh Tan District, 11 km west of District 1), you do not need to take a costly taxi all the way to Binh Tan.
1. **Board in District 5:** Head to the main Futa Bus Lines ticket office and waiting lounge at 231–233 Le Hong Phong Street, Ward 4, District 5 (only 3.5 km or a 45,000 VND Grab ride from Pham Ngu Lao / Ben Thanh Market in District 1).
2. **Free Shuttle Transfer (*Xe Trung Chuyển*):** Present your ticket at the Le Hong Phong counter 45 to 60 minutes before your scheduled coach departure. Futa's free 16-seat shuttle vans run continuously between Le Hong Phong Street and Western Bus Station (25 minutes).
3. **24/7 Departure Frequency:** Buses leave Western Bus Station for Chau Doc every 30 to 60 minutes around the clock, starting at 00:01 and running until 23:59 (36 to 42 daily departures).

### Fares & Free Chau Doc Hotel Drop-Off
A one-way Futa sleeper ticket costs 210,000 to 230,000 VND ($8.40–$9.20 USD). In Chau Doc, coaches terminate at the **New Chau Doc Bus Station** (*Bến Xe Châu Đốc Mới*) on National Highway QL91 in Vinh My Ward, 4.5 km south of the town market. Do not pay local motorbike taxis waiting outside the gate. Walk directly to the orange Futa shuttle minivans parked inside the station yard; the driver will check your hotel address and drop you free of charge anywhere in central Chau Doc, along the Hau River waterfront, or out at **Nui Sam (Sam Mountain)** (6 km west). Check our [Where to Stay in Chau Doc](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-chau-doc/) guide to pick between riverfront piers and Sam Mountain rice-paddy lodges.

## Option 2: Hue Nghia & Hung Cuong Sleeper Coaches (Chinatown Departures)

Two strong private competitors run high-frequency sleeper buses directly between District 5 / District 10 (Cholon Chinatown) and central Chau Doc:

* **Hue Nghia Bus Lines:** Operates both standard 40-berth sleeper buses (200,000 VND / $8.00 USD) and 24-cabin luxury single/double room buses (*Xe Limousine Phòng Đôi*, 250,000–320,000 VND / $10.00–$12.80 USD). Departures leave from 4 Ton Duc Thang Street (District 1) or their main hub at 11 Le Dai Hanh Street (District 11) and Western Bus Station every 60 minutes from 04:30 to 23:30. In Chau Doc, Hue Nghia pulls into its own private terminal on Ton Duc Thang Street right in town, plus offers free shuttle drop-off.
* **Hung Cuong Bus Lines:** Popular with local traders and backpackers alike, departing from 96 Pho Co Dieu Street (District 5) and Western Bus Station between 04:00 and 23:00 (14 trips daily). Fares run 200,000 to 240,000 VND ($8.00–$9.60 USD), arriving at the Hung Cuong terminal at 96 Dong Da Street in the heart of Chau Doc (600 meters from Chau Doc Market and the riverfront).

## Option 3: 9-Seat VIP Limousine Vans & Private Cars

If you dislike climbing into sleeper bus bunks or want daytime highway views from an upright leather captain's chair, book a 9-seat VIP DCar limousine van (260,000–320,000 VND / $10.40–$12.80 USD). Operators such as **Huynh Gia**, **Thai Duong Limousine**, and **Lien Hung** run daytime departures (06:00, 09:00, 12:00, 15:00, 18:00) with pickups near Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN) and District 5, shaving 30 minutes off the coach schedule (5 to 5.5 hours total).

For small groups, a private 4-seat sedan (2,400,000 VND / $96.00 USD) or 7-seat SUV (2,700,000–2,900,000 VND / $108.00–$116.00 USD) allows you to turn the transit day into a sightseeing loop—stopping at **Dong Thap's Gao Giong Ecotourism Park** or the **Tra Su Cajuput Forest** (27 km / 40 minutes south of Chau Doc, 100,000 VND entrance + 120,000 VND sampan & motorboat ticket) before checking into your hotel.

## Step-by-Step Protocol: Connecting to the 07:00 Phnom Penh Speedboat

Many international travelers head to Chau Doc specifically to catch the morning high-speed boat up the Mekong River into **Phnom Penh, Cambodia** via the **Vinh Xuong – Kaam Samnor International River Border Gate**. Because the boats depart early in the morning, timing your bus from Ho Chi Minh City is critical.

### The Overnight Connection Blueprint
* **Step 1 (23:00–23:59 Night Before):** Board a late-night **Phuong Trang (Futa)** or **Hung Cuong** sleeper coach leaving Ho Chi Minh City between 23:00 and 23:59. Select a **lower-deck middle berth (A4–A12)** for the smoothest ride with minimal body roll.
* **Step 2 (05:00–05:30 Arrival in Chau Doc):** Your bus arrives in Chau Doc just before dawn. Take the free operator shuttle van directly to **Victoria Chau Doc Hotel Pier** (1 Le Loi Street) or the **Hang Chau Tourist Pier** at Chau Giang Ferry Terminal (300 meters upstream).
* **Step 3 (07:00–07:30 Speedboat Departure):**
  * **Hang Chau Tourist Express Boat:** Departs daily at 07:00 ($35–$40 USD / 880,000–1,000,000 VND one way).
  * **Victoria Speedboat:** Departs daily at 07:30 from the Victoria Hotel dock ($45–$48 USD / 1,125,000–1,200,000 VND one way, including bottled water, cold towels, and border assistant).
* **Step 4 (12:00–12:30 Arrival in Phnom Penh):** Both boats take 5 hours total (including 45–60 minutes at the Vinh Xuong / Kaam Samnor riverside immigration posts, where a Cambodian Visa on Arrival costs $30 USD in crisp cash plus $5 USD boat-crew processing fee; Cambodia e-Visas are also accepted at Kaam Samnor).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Which sleeper bus berth is best for tall travelers or motion sickness?
Always book a lower-deck berth in the middle rows (seats A4 through A10) on Phuong Trang's 34-berth VIP coaches. Upper-deck berths (row B) experience noticeably more lateral sway when crossing the My Thuan 2 and Vam Cong bridge approaches, and rear berths sit directly over the engine block.

### How do I visit Tra Su Cajuput Forest from Chau Doc after arriving by bus?
Tra Su Cajuput Forest is located 27 km southwest of central Chau Doc in Tinh Bien Town (a 40-minute drive). You can rent a 125cc scooter in Chau Doc for 150,000 VND ($6.00 USD) per day, or hire a half-day private 4-seat taxi round-trip for 550,000 to 650,000 VND ($22.00–$26.00 USD) including a 2-hour waiting window while you take the paddle sampan through the flooded melaleuca canopy.
