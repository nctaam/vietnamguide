Heading south from Dong Hoi—capital of Quang Binh Province and the rail and airport gateway to Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park—into the former imperial capital of Hue covers 166 km (103 miles) on the North-South Railway or 165 km (102.5 miles) by road along National Highway QL1A and the Cam Lo–La Son Expressway (CT01). Travelers completing cave treks in Phong Nha or beach stays on the Nhat Le and Bao Ninh coastline rely on this southbound corridor to link Quang Binh with Central Vietnam's heritage belt.

Three primary transport modes operate between Dong Hoi and Hue in 2026. Southbound Vietnam Railways Reunification Express trains (SE19, SE3, SE1, SE7, SE5) run directly from Dong Hoi Railway Station to Hue Railway Station in 2 hours 50 minutes to 3 hours 15 minutes for 110,000–160,000 VND ($4.40–$6.40 USD) in an air-conditioned soft seat. Direct 9-seat limousine vans and sleeper coaches cover Highway QL1A in 3 to 3.5 hours for 180,000–250,000 VND ($7.20–$10.00 USD). Southbound private cars cost 1,700,000–2,100,000 VND ($68.00–$84.00 USD) non-stop in 2 hours 45 minutes, or 2,200,000–2,700,000 VND ($88.00–$108.00 USD) for a 6-hour sightseeing transfer through the Vinh Moc Tunnels, Hien Luong Bridge (17th Parallel), and La Vang Basilica.

Trains offer unbeatable value. Private DMZ cars turn transit into a full historical tour.

> [!TIP]
> **Best Daytime Connection from Phong Nha:** If you are checking out of a Phong Nha homestay after breakfast, skip the pre-dawn trains and target southbound express SE7 (departing Dong Hoi at 14:05, arriving Hue at 17:15). You can ride the 10:30 or 11:15 Quang Binh Public Bus B4 down to Dong Hoi for 35,000–50,000 VND ($1.40–$2.00 USD) and board an air-conditioned soft seat (NML) for 110,000–150,000 VND ($4.40–$6.00 USD).

## Connecting from Phong Nha to Dong Hoi Station in the Morning

If you are sleeping in Phong Nha town (Son Trach, 45 km northwest of Dong Hoi) rather than along Nhat Le Beach, you have two ways to reach Dong Hoi for a southbound train—unless you book a direct bus covered in [Hue to Phong Nha Transport](/plan/hue-to-phong-nha-transport/):

* **Pre-Booked Private Taxi (45 to 55 minutes):** A 4-seat car from Phong Nha down Highway DT20 to Dong Hoi Railway Station costs 500,000–600,000 VND ($20.00–$24.00 USD). To catch early morning trains SE19 (06:05) or SE1 (06:11), depart Phong Nha no later than 04:50.
* **Quang Binh Public Bus B4 (75 minutes):** The white-and-green **B4 public bus** departs Phong Nha (near the Xuan Son ferry pier on DT20) every 45 to 60 minutes starting at 05:30 for 35,000–50,000 VND ($1.40–$2.00 USD). Because Bus B4 routes down Tran Hung Dao Street to **Nam Ly Bus Station** rather than the railway forecourt, disembark at the Tran Hung Dao / Hoang Dieu intersection and take a 35,000 VND ($1.40 USD) taxi the final 1.8 km (5 minutes) to Dong Hoi Railway Station. Only use Bus B4 when connecting to afternoon trains SE7 or SE5.

## Option 1: Southbound Reunification Express Trains (Best Value)

Vietnam Railways (dsvn.vn) operates five primary southbound SE express trains daily across the 166 km rail corridor between Dong Hoi and Hue. Because these trains originate 522 km north at Hanoi Railway Station the previous evening (see [Hanoi to Hue Transport](/plan/hanoi-to-hue-transport/) and [Hanoi to Phong Nha Transport](/plan/hanoi-to-phong-nha-transport/)), overnight progress is steady, with typical arrival variances under 15 minutes at Dong Hoi.

### Station Locations and Boarding Logistics

Passengers board at **Dong Hoi Railway Station** (Ga Dong Hoi, Sub-zone 4, Nam Ly Ward), located 5 km (3.1 miles) inland from Nhat Le Beach and 6.5 km from Bao Ninh. A metered Mai Linh (0232 385 8585), Sun Taxi (0232 368 6868), or Xanh SM electric taxi from Nhat Le waterfront hotels takes 10 to 15 minutes and costs 70,000–100,000 VND ($2.80–$4.00 USD). Arrive 25 minutes before departure.

All southbound services arrive at **Hue Railway Station** (Ga Hue, 02 Bui Thi Xuan Street, Duc Ward) on the south bank of the Perfume River. Metered taxis and GrabCars reach hotels in Hue's Phu Hoi and Vinh Ninh wards (1.8 to 2.5 km east) in 6 to 10 minutes for 40,000–65,000 VND ($1.60–$2.60 USD).

### 2026 Southbound Train Schedule (Dong Hoi to Hue)

Southbound departures cluster into an early morning wave and an afternoon wave:

* **Train SE3:** Departs Dong Hoi at 05:20 (seasonal 04:57 slot), arrives Hue at 08:12 (2 hours 52 minutes).
* **Train SE19:** Departs Dong Hoi at 06:05, arrives Hue at 09:15 (3 hours 10 minutes).
* **Train SE1:** Departs Dong Hoi at 06:11 (or 07:33 seasonal slot), arrives Hue at 09:01 (2 hours 50 minutes).
* **Train SE7:** Departs Dong Hoi at 14:05 (afternoon window 14:05–16:27), arrives Hue at 17:15 (3 hours 10 minutes).
* **Train SE5:** Departs Dong Hoi at 17:30 (or 18:11), arrives Hue at 20:45 (3 hours 15 minutes).

For travelers sleeping in Dong Hoi City, **SE1 (06:11)** and **SE19 (06:05)** deliver you to Hue by 09:00–09:15, leaving a full day to tour the Imperial Citadel. If you prefer a relaxed breakfast on Nhat Le Beach or are transferring down from Phong Nha after morning checkout, **SE7 (14:05)** is the best daytime option.

### Seat Classes and 2026 Fares

Southbound fares from Dong Hoi to Hue price 10,000–15,000 VND lower than northbound peak slots:
* **Air-Conditioned Soft Seat (Ngoi Mem Dieu Hoa, code NML):** 110,000–160,000 VND ($4.40–$6.40 USD). Reclining cushioned seats (2+2 layout), fold-down tray tables, 220V outlets, and overhead luggage racks.
* **6-Berth Soft Sleeper (Nam Khoang 6, code BnLT):** 210,000–265,000 VND ($8.40–$10.60 USD). Three tiers of padded bunks per cabin; useful if boarding SE3 at 05:20.
* **4-Berth Soft Sleeper (Nam Khoang 4, code AnLT):** 275,000–340,000 VND ($11.00–$13.60 USD). Four-berth lockable cabin on flagship SE1 and SE3 trains.

Purchase e-tickets 2 to 5 days ahead on dsvn.vn or via Baolau / 12Go Asia (25,000–45,000 VND / $1.00–$1.80 USD fee). While short-sector sleepers on SE1 and SE3 sometimes open only 24 to 48 hours before departure, soft seats (NML) on SE19, SE7, and SE5 can be booked weeks ahead.

## Option 2: Direct Limousine Vans & Highway 1A Coaches

Travelers who prefer hotel pickup in Dong Hoi without commuting to Nam Ly Ward can ride a **9-seat VIP limousine van** or sleeper coach south down National Highway QL1A.

* **9-Seat Limousine Vans (180,000–250,000 VND / $7.20–$10.00 USD):** Operators including **Tan Nhat**, **Hung Thanh**, and **Queen Cafe** run morning (07:30, 09:00), midday (13:00), and afternoon (15:30, 17:00) vans from Dong Hoi to Hue in 3 to 3.5 hours via QL1A and the Cam Lo–La Son Expressway (CT01). Vans pick up at Nhat Le Beach hotels (Truong Phap Street) and drop at central Hue offices near Hung Vuong Street or **Hue Northern Bus Station (132 Ly Thai To)**.
* **Southbound Open-Tour Sleeper Buses (180,000–220,000 VND / $7.20–$8.80 USD):** Coaches leaving Phong Nha (10:30 or 20:30) pass through Dong Hoi 60 minutes later (11:30 or 21:30), reaching Hue in 3.5 hours. Because highway sleeper buses often pick up along the **Dong Hoi QL1A Bypass**, confirm your boarding pin via WhatsApp or Zalo 2 hours prior.

## Option 3: Southbound Private Car & Frequently Asked Questions

Booking a private car from Dong Hoi to Hue turns a 165 km highway transfer into a curated Vietnam War history itinerary across Quang Tri Province.

### Direct Express Transfer (2 hours 45 minutes)

Cruising south down Highway QL1A and the Cam Lo–La Son Expressway (CT01), a direct private car takes 2 hours 45 minutes to 3 hours door-to-door:
* **4-Seat Sedan (Toyota Vios, Mazda 3):** 1,700,000–1,850,000 VND ($68.00–$74.00 USD).
* **7-Seat SUV/MPV (Toyota Innova, Fortuner, Xpander):** 1,900,000–2,100,000 VND ($76.00–$84.00 USD).
* **16-Seat Minibus (Ford Transit):** 2,400,000–2,700,000 VND ($96.00–$108.00 USD).

### Southbound DMZ Sightseeing Itinerary (5.5 to 6.5 hours)

Upgrading to a **Southbound DMZ Private Car** costs 2,200,000–2,700,000 VND ($88.00–$108.00 USD) inclusive of fuel, tolls, and waiting time (starting from Phong Nha adds 350,000–450,000 VND / $14.00–$18.00 USD):
1. **08:30 – Hotel Pickup in Dong Hoi:** Drive 65 km south (1 hour 15 minutes) along QL1A into coastal Vinh Linh.
2. **09:45 – Vinh Moc Tunnels (Vinh Linh District):** Walk the three-level underground tunnel village (1.7 km of galleries reaching 23 meters deep) that sheltered 300 villagers from 1966 to 1972. Entry fee: 50,000 VND ($2.00 USD); allow 75 minutes.
3. **11:20 – Hien Luong Bridge & Ben Hai River (17th Parallel):** Drive 13 km southwest to cross the 183-meter yellow-and-blue demarcation bridge and inspect the Exhibition House. Entry fee: 50,000 VND ($2.00 USD); allow 45 minutes.
4. **12:30 – La Vang Basilica (Hai Lang District):** Stop 30 minutes at **Our Lady of La Vang Basilica** (free admission) 18 km south of Dong Ha.
5. **14:30 – Drop-Off at Hue Riverside Hotel:** Drive the final 55 km (1 hour 10 minutes) south into central Hue for 14:30 check-in. For reverse schedules, consult [Hue to Dong Hoi Transport](/plan/hue-to-dong-hoi-transport/).
