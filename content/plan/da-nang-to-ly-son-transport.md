## The 3-Leg Multimodal Journey from Da Nang to Ly Son Island

Traveling from Da Nang south to the volcanic craters and garlic fields of **Ly Son Island** (`Đảo Lý Sơn`, Quang Ngai Province) requires a three-stage overland and maritime transfer covering roughly 178 km (`150 km` by road or rail plus `15 nautical miles` / `28 km` across the open East Sea). Unlike the 20-minute tourist speedboats to the Cham Islands near Hoi An, Ly Son has no direct ferry from Da Nang. Every traveler must route through **Sa Ky Passenger Port** (`Cảng Sa Kỳ`) in Tinh Ky Commune, located 20 to 23 km northeast of Quang Ngai City.

To complete the journey in 3.5 to 4.5 hours door-to-door, connect these three legs:

1. **Leg 1: Da Nang to Quang Ngai (128–130 km | 2–2.5 hours)** via the Reunification Express train from **Da Nang Railway Station** (`791 Hải Phòng`) to **Quang Ngai Railway Station** (95,000 to 160,000 VND / $3.80 to $6.40 USD for a soft seat), a shared highway limousine van along the **Da Nang–Quang Ngai Expressway (`CT01`)** (150,000 to 220,000 VND / $6.00 to $8.80 USD), or a direct private car from Da Nang straight to Sa Ky Port (150 km in 2.5 hours for 1,400,000 to 1,750,000 VND / $56.00 to $70.00 USD).
2. **Leg 2: Quang Ngai Station / City to Sa Ky Port (20–23 km | 30–45 minutes)** via **Quang Ngai Public Bus Route 03** (`Mai Linh Bus`, 20,000 to 30,000 VND / $0.80 to $1.20 USD, 45 minutes) or a metered **Mai Linh / Sun Taxi** (250,000 to 320,000 VND / $10.00 to $12.80 USD, 30 minutes).
3. **Leg 3: Sa Ky Port to Ben Dinh Port, Ly Son (`Đảo Lớn` | 15 Nautical Miles / 28 km | 35–40 minutes)** aboard an aluminum high-speed catamaran operated by **Super Bien Dong**, **Chin Nghia Express**, or **An Vinh Express** (178,000 to 210,000 VND / $7.10 to $8.40 USD one-way).

## Leg 1: Da Nang to Quang Ngai (128–150 km | 2–2.5 Hours)

Because the last afternoon speedboat from Sa Ky Port to Ly Son normally departs at **15:00** (or 15:30 during peak summer weekends), you must depart Da Nang in the morning to catch a same-day ferry.

### Option A: Reunification Express Train (128 km | 2.5 Hours)

Vietnam Railways (`VNR`) operates six daily southbound express trains (`SE1`, `SE3`, `SE5`, `SE7`, `SE9`, and `SE21`) from **Da Nang Railway Station** (`791 Hải Phòng`) to **Quang Ngai Railway Station** (`204 Nguyễn Chí Thanh, Quảng Ngai`).

* **Best Morning Trains for Same-Day Ferries**:
  * **Train `SE21`**: Departs Da Nang around **06:00 to 07:05**, arriving in Quang Ngai around **08:45 to 09:35** (2 hours 30 minutes to 2 hours 45 minutes). This connects comfortably with the **11:30** or **13:30** Sa Ky speedboat.
  * **Train `SE1` / `SE3`**: Depart Da Nang between **08:00 and 09:30**, reaching Quang Ngai between **10:30 and 12:05** in time for the **13:30** or **15:00** crossing.
  * **Train `SE7` / `SE5`**: Depart Da Nang between **11:15 and 14:40**; if you take the afternoon `SE5`, plan to sleep overnight in Quang Ngai City and catch the **07:30** ferry the next morning.
* **2026 Train Fares**: An air-conditioned soft seat (`Ngồi mềm điều hòa` / NML) costs **95,000 to 160,000 VND ($3.80 to $6.40 USD)** depending on the train code and weekday vs weekend tariff. Sleeper berths cost 180,000 to 310,000 VND ($7.20 to $12.40 USD) but are unnecessary for a 2.5-hour daytime ride.

### Option B: Highway `CT01` Limousine Van or Direct Private Car (130–150 km)

The 4-lane **Da Nang–Quang Ngai Expressway (`CT01`)** cuts straight across Quang Nam into Quang Ngai at 100 to 120 km/h:

* **Shared 9-Seat or 16-Seat Limousine Van**: Operators such as **Chin Nghia**, **Son Tung**, and **Quang Ngai Limousine** pick up passengers in central Da Nang every 60 minutes from 05:30 to 18:00. Fares to Quang Ngai City (`130 km`, 2 to 2.25 hours) run **150,000 to 220,000 VND ($6.00 to $8.80 USD)**, while direct morning vans continuing straight to Sa Ky Port (`150 km`, 2.5 hours) charge **250,000 to 300,000 VND ($10.00 to $12.00 USD)**.
* **Direct Private Car (Da Nang to Sa Ky Port)**: Hiring a private 4-seater sedan costs **1,400,000 to 1,550,000 VND ($56.00 to $62.00 USD)**, while a 7-seater SUV costs **1,600,000 to 1,750,000 VND ($64.00 to $70.00 USD)**. Leaving My Khe Beach at 06:30 puts you at the Sa Ky terminal gate by 09:00 for the 09:30 or 10:00 ferry.

## Leg 2: Quang Ngai Station / City to Sa Ky Port (20–23 km | 30–45 Minutes)

If you arrive by train at Quang Ngai Railway Station (located on the western edge of the city), you must cover 23 km east down National Route `QL24B` to reach **Sa Ky Port** (`Xã Tịnh Kỳ`):

* **Public Bus Route 03 (Quang Ngai – Sa Ky)**: The yellow-and-green municipal Bus 03 departs from Quang Ngai Bus Station and passes along Quang Trung and Le Thanh Ton streets every 30 to 45 minutes between 05:00 and 17:30. Take a short 2.5 km motorbike taxi or Grab (`25,000 to 35,000 VND` / `$1.00 to $1.40 USD`) from the railway station to the nearest Bus 03 stop, or catch the dedicated station feeder bus when timed with morning express trains. The Bus 03 fare is **20,000 to 30,000 VND ($0.80 to $1.20 USD)** and the ride to Sa Ky Port takes **45 minutes**.
* **Metered Taxi (Mai Linh, Sun Taxi, or Vinasun)**: Taxis queue directly outside Quang Ngai Railway Station. The 23 km drive to Sa Ky Port takes **30 minutes** and costs **250,000 to 320,000 VND ($10.00 to $12.80 USD)**.

## Leg 3: Sa Ky Port to Ly Son Speedboat Ferry (15 Nautical Miles | 35–40 Minutes)

All passenger ferries depart from **Sa Ky Port** and dock at **Ben Dinh Port** (`Cảng Bến Đình`), the modern passenger terminal on the southern shore of Ly Son's Big Island (`Đảo Lớn`).

* **Speedboat Operators & 2026 Fares**: Four main high-speed aluminum vessels operate the 15-nautical-mile (`28 km`) channel crossing in **35 to 40 minutes**: **Super Bien Dong** (139 to 152 seats, 178,000 to 195,000 VND / $7.10 to $7.80 USD), **Chin Nghia Express** (190,000 to 210,000 VND / $7.60 to $8.40 USD), **An Vinh Express** (185,000 to 200,000 VND / $7.40 to $8.00 USD), and **Super 2 Biển Đông**. VIP front-cabin seats add 20,000 VND ($0.80 USD).
* **Daily Departure Windows**: Year-round departures from Sa Ky Port run at **07:30, 08:30, 09:30, 11:30, 13:30, and 15:00** (with extra 30-minute frequency added between May and August). Return boats from Ben Dinh Port (Ly Son) to Sa Ky depart between **07:00 and 15:00**.
* **Luggage Allowances & Seasickness Advice**: Each adult ticket includes **15 kg** of free cabin baggage; bulky suitcases or dive bags over 15 kg incur a small freight fee of **20,000 to 50,000 VND ($0.80 to $2.00 USD)** collected at the gangway. Because the 28 km channel crosses open ocean currents without barrier islands, passengers prone to motion sickness should book a **rear lower-deck seat** near the center aisle where hull pitch is lowest, and take dimenhydrinate (`Thuốc chống say sóng`, 5,000 VND at the Sa Ky terminal pharmacy) 30 minutes before boarding.

> [!WARNING]
> **Original Passport Check & Monsoon Cancellations (Oct–Feb):** Because Ly Son is a strategic island district in the East Sea, every passenger must present an **original passport** (foreign nationals) or Vietnamese citizen ID (`CCCD`) to purchase tickets at `cangsa-ky.com` and pass the **Sa Ky Port Border Guard (`Biên Phòng`)** checkpoint 30 minutes before departure. Furthermore, during the **Northeast Monsoon (`Gió mùa Đông Bắc`) from October to February**, the Maritime Port Authority strictly suspends all Sa Ky–Ly Son ferries whenever offshore winds reach **Beaufort Force 6 (`Cấp 6`, 39–49 km/h)** or waves exceed 2.5 meters, sometimes stranding visitors on the island for 2 to 4 days.

## Getting Around After Docking at Ben Dinh Port & An Binh (Be Island)

Once your speedboat docks at **Ben Dinh Port** (`Thôn Đông, An Vĩnh`) on Big Island (`Đảo Lớn`), you can walk out of the terminal plaza to arrange local island transport across the 10 km² volcanic landmass:

* **Motorbike Rental (`Thuê xe máy`)**: Local homestays and dockside agencies rent 110cc to 125cc semi-automatic (`Honda Wave`) and automatic (`Honda Vision` / `AirBlade`) scooters for **120,000 to 150,000 VND ($4.80 to $6.00 USD) per 24 hours**, including a full tank of petrol to ride the 12 km coastal ring road past **To Vo Arch (`Cổng Tò Vò`)**, **Hang Pagoda (`Chùa Hang`)**, and **Thoi Loi Peak (`Đỉnh Thới Lới`)**.
* **Electric Buggy (`Xe điện`) & Island Taxis**: For families or groups of 4 to 8 passengers, open-air electric buggies and 7-seater taxis waiting outside Ben Dinh Port charge **50,000 to 100,000 VND ($2.00 to $4.00 USD)** for a short transfer to central An Vinh hotels, or **450,000 to 600,000 VND ($18.00 to $24.00 USD)** for a half-day island circuit.
* **Connecting to Little Island (`Đảo Bé` / An Binh Commune)**: To visit the basalt rock beaches and snorkeling reefs of **Be Island** (`3 nautical miles` / `5.5 km` northwest of Big Island), walk or ride 1.5 km west from Ben Dinh Port to the **Old Ly Son Pier (`Cảng Cũ Lý Sơn`)** before 07:30. Composite speed canoes depart between **07:30 and 11:20** (returning between 10:30 and 14:30), taking **12 to 15 minutes** each way for **120,000 to 140,000 VND ($4.80 to $5.60 USD) round-trip**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Step-by-Step: How do I book Sa Ky to Ly Son ferry tickets online?
1. Visit the official Sa Ky Port portal (`cangsa-ky.com`) 3 to 7 days before departure (or 14 days ahead for April–August weekends).
2. Select your departure time (07:30 to 15:00), vessel (**Super Bien Dong**, **Chin Nghia Express**, or **An Vinh Express**), and enter each passenger's full name, date of birth, and passport number exactly as printed.
3. Arrive at Sa Ky Port at least **40 minutes early** to verify your original passport at the terminal window and pay the 178,000 to 210,000 VND ($7.10 to $8.40 USD) ticket fare.

### Should I visit Ly Son Island or the Cham Islands from Da Nang?
If you only have one day or are traveling between October and February when rough seas disrupt offshore crossings, take a 20-minute speedboat from Cua Dai Pier to the Cham Islands; compare both archipelagos in our [Ly Son vs Cham Islands](/compare/ly-son-vs-cham-islands/) guide.
