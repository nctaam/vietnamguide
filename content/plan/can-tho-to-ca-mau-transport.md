# Can Tho to Ca Mau Transport: Bus, Limousine, and Dat Mui Transit Guide

Traversing from Can Tho to Ca Mau takes travelers across the deep south of Vietnam's Mekong Delta. Covering 150 km along National Route 1A (Quoc Lo 1A or QL1A), this overland route links the delta's primary economic river hub with the southernmost provincial capital of the nation. The journey crosses flat agricultural lowlands, passing through Hau Giang and Bac Lieu provinces before arriving in Ca Mau city, the primary staging point for expeditions to the mangrove forests of Dat Mui (Cape Ca Mau).

Overland travelers can choose between scheduled express intercity coaches, 9-seat VIP limousine vans, private car charters, or self-directed motorbikes. FUTA Bus Lines (Phuong Trang) runs express sleeper and seating coaches hourly, while boutique operators like Vu Linh and Giap Diep provide direct hotel pickups and door-to-door transit. This guide outlines exact 2026 fares, terminal protocols, highway conditions, scam warnings, and onward connections to the southernmost tip of Vietnam.

## Detailed Modality Comparison & Verified Schedules

### 1. FUTA Bus Lines (Phuong Trang) Express Coaches

Phuong Trang remains the most frequent carrier operating between Can Tho and Ca Mau. The company deploys modern 34-berth sleeper buses alongside high-roof seating coaches equipped with air conditioning, onboard Wi-Fi, and undercarriage luggage storage compartments.

- **Fare**: 145,000 VND ($5.80 USD) for standard reclining seat coaches; 165,000 VND ($6.60 USD) for 34-berth sleeper coaches.
- **Departure Terminal**: Ben xe Trung tam Can Tho (Can Tho Central Bus Station), located along QL1A, Hung Thanh Ward, Cai Rang District (Hotline: 1900 6067).
- **Arrival Terminal**: Ben xe Ca Mau (Ca Mau Central Bus Station), situated on QL1A, Ly Thuong Kiet Street, Ward 6, Ca Mau City.
- **Operating Schedule**: Daily coaches pull out every 60 minutes from 04:30 through 20:00.
- **Transit Duration**: 3.5 to 4.0 hours depending on congestion along the Soc Trang bypass and Bac Lieu town perimeter.

FUTA coaches run at monitored cruising speeds under an enforced single-passenger-per-berth rule, making them reliable for travelers carrying expedition backpacks.

### 2. 9-Seat VIP Limousines (Vu Linh & Giap Diep)

Limousine services run converted Ford Transit and Hyundai Solati vans configured with nine captain chairs. These vans bypass central bus stations, picking up at central Can Tho hotels and dropping off at Ca Mau city accommodations.

- **Fare**: 180,000 to 220,000 VND ($7.20–$8.80 USD) per seat. Front passenger seats beside the driver cost 180,000 VND; central reclining captain seats with massage features cost 220,000 VND.
- **Key Operators & Hotlines**:
  - **Vu Linh Limousine**: Hotline 1900 7009 / 0292 3831 831. Vans set out every hour between 05:00 and 18:30.
  - **Giap Diep Limousine**: Hotline 0290 3888 888 / 0939 600 011. Fleets roll every 90 minutes.
  - **Hao Limousine**: Hotline 0290 3575 757. Shared vans leave every two hours between 06:00 and 17:00.
- **Transit Duration**: 3.0 hours door-to-door.
- **Luggage Constraints**: Overhead racks hold small daypacks only. Suitcases larger than 24 inches must be declared during telephone booking, as rear cargo compartments fill rapidly when all nine seats are occupied.

### 3. Private Car Transfers & Taxi Charters

For family units, elderly travelers, or those carrying bulky photography equipment, private vehicle charters offer complete route flexibility:

- **Rates**: Standard 4-seat sedans (Toyota Vios, Hyundai Accent) charge 1,400,000 to 1,500,000 VND ($56.00–$60.00 USD) one-way, including highway tolls and fuel. Larger 7-seat SUVs (Toyota Innova, Mitsubishi Xpander) charge 1,700,000 to 1,800,000 VND ($68.00–$72.00 USD).
- **Reliable Fleets**: Mai Linh Taxi Can Tho (0292 365 6565), Vinasun Taxi Can Tho (0292 3827 272), or GrabCar Intercity via the mobile application.
- **Travel Time**: 2 hours 45 minutes to 3 hours.
- **Flexibility**: Drivers allow passengers to stop at roadside landmarks, such as the Bac Lieu Wind Farm (Nha mat Bac Lieu) or the ornate Khmer pagoda of Chua Chen Kieu (Wat Sro Loun) in Soc Trang province, for an extra waiting fee of 100,000 VND per hour.

## Step-by-Step Terminal Arrival and Boarding Protocol

Navigating intercity transport terminals in the Mekong Delta requires following an organized boarding routine:

1. **Terminal Transfer in Can Tho**: Board a local metered taxi or book a GrabBike from Ninh Kieu pier to Ben xe Trung tam Can Tho in Cai Rang district. The 6 km trip costs roughly 70,000 to 90,000 VND via taxi, taking 15 minutes.
2. **Ticket Office Counter**: Enter the central booking hall. Walk directly past freelance touts to the official ticket counter marked **Phuong Trang** or your reserved carrier. Present your electronic confirmation code on your phone. The clerk prints a physical paper boarding voucher stating your assigned seat or berth number, vehicle license plate, and boarding bay gate.
3. **Luggage Check-In**: Head directly to the loading bays. Give large bags to the luggage attendant. Verify that the baggage handler fastens a numbered tag to each piece, and retain your matching plastic claim token securely. Always keep passports, money, cameras, medication, and mobile batteries inside your daypack.
4. **Boarding Clearance**: Remove footwear when boarding sleeper coaches and place shoes inside the provided bag. Settle into your assigned berth (lower berths carry odd numbers, upper berths carry even numbers).
5. **Arrival in Ca Mau**: Disembark at Ben xe Ca Mau and retrieve checked bags using your claim token. Take a metered Mai Linh taxi (0290 378 7878) from the station rank to downtown hotels (4 km north) for 55,000 to 70,000 VND.

## Highway Conditions, Road Works, and Toll Plaza Checkpoints

The 150 km drive along QL1A crosses three administrative boundaries: Can Tho City, Hau Giang Province, Bac Lieu Province, and Ca Mau Province.

- **Surface Quality & Geometry**: QL1A is an asphalt-paved dual carriageway with concrete medians separating opposing traffic. Speed limits sit at 80 km/h for private cars outside urban zones and 60 km/h through town centers like Nga Bay, Soc Trang, and Gia Rai. Watch for uneven asphalt settlement caused by soft alluvial soil foundations across river canal bridges.
- **Toll Plazas**: The journey passes through two automated Electronic Toll Collection (ETC) stations: BOT Soc Trang (KM 2123+250) and BOT Bac Lieu. Private vehicle drivers must ensure their VETC or ePass tag maintains an account balance of at least 100,000 VND to avoid manual lane delays.
- **Rest Stops**: Long-distance coaches stop 20 minutes at highway service plazas like Tram Dung Chan Minh Khai. Facilities offer restrooms, hot noodle soups for 40,000 to 55,000 VND, and sugarcane juice for 15,000 VND.

## Safety Guidelines, Scam Warnings, and Onward Transit to Dat Mui

### Terminal Tout Avoidance
Unlicensed transportation brokers congregate outside the entrance gates of Ben xe Trung tam Can Tho. They often approach arriving travelers claiming that express coaches are completely booked or delayed. Never hand luggage to these individuals; walk straight to the branded company counter inside.

### Ticket Overcharging at Roadside Stops
Independent minivans operating as "xe du" (unregistered pirate vans) cruise QL1A looking for passengers standing roadside. They frequently quote a low initial fare of 100,000 VND, only to demand an additional 100,000 VND fee for luggage once your bags are secured on board. Always travel with verified operators like FUTA, Vu Linh, or Giap Diep.

### Onward Connections to Dat Mui (Cape Ca Mau)
Ca Mau city serves as the departure base for Dat Mui, the southern continental tip of Vietnam marked by the National GPS Coordinate Monument 0001:

- **Speedboat Route (Ca Mau to Dat Mui)**: High-speed passenger launches (tau cao toc) depart from Ben tau Ly Bon (Ly Bon Boat Pier, located at the intersection of Ly Bon and Phan Ngoc Hien streets). The express river journey navigates the Ganh Hao and Cua Lon rivers, covering 110 km of waterway in 2.0 hours. One-way tickets cost 140,000 VND ($5.60 USD). Launches depart every two hours between 06:30 and 13:30.
- **Road Route via Ho Chi Minh Highway**: Completed asphalt extensions of the Ho Chi Minh Highway connect Ca Mau city directly to Dat Mui by road (110 km). Public bus line number 12 departs Ben xe Ca Mau every 45 minutes (fare: 70,000 VND, travel time: 2.5 hours). Alternatively, private taxis charge a flat round-trip fare of 1,200,000 to 1,400,000 VND ($48.00–$56.00 USD), including three hours of driver waiting time while you explore the national park and mangrove walkways.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the fastest transport option between Can Tho and Ca Mau?
The 9-seat VIP limousine operated by Vu Linh or Giap Diep is the fastest mode. It completes the 150 km trip in 3 hours door-to-door, saving an hour compared to large sleeper buses that require separate terminal taxi connections.

### Can I book FUTA bus tickets online without a Vietnamese bank card?
Yes. International travelers can book FUTA bus tickets using foreign Visa or Mastercard credit cards directly through the official FUTA mobile app or online via the Vexere booking portal. You receive an electronic SMS and email confirmation containing an alphanumeric reservation code.

### Where can I store large luggage while visiting Dat Mui from Ca Mau?
Ben xe Ca Mau offers luggage storage lockers inside the central station management office for 30,000 VND per bag per day. Alternatively, if you book a night at an urban hotel in Ca Mau city, front desks provide complimentary luggage holding while you take a day excursion to Cape Ca Mau.

### Are sleeper bunks comfortable for tall passengers over six feet?
Standard 34-berth sleeper coaches on this route feature bunks measuring approximately 180 cm (5 feet 11 inches) in length. Taller travelers over six feet (183 cm) may find their feet touching the end compartment wall. If extra legroom is critical, book front-row captain seats in a 9-seat VIP limousine instead.
