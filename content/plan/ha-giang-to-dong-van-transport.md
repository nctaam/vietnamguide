# Ha Giang to Dong Van Transport (2026): Bus vs Motorbike

Crossing from Ha Giang City to the Dong Van Karst Plateau requires climbing 135 km to 148 km of steep limestone switchbacks along National Highway QL4C. No railway or airport serves this border frontier. The road gains over 1,385 m of vertical elevation before dropping into the stone valley of Dong Van at 1,025 m. Every traveler must choose between five road modes: local 29-seat County minibuses, 9-seat VIP limousine vans, self-drive manual or semi-automatic motorbikes, Easy Rider pillion chauffeurs, or 7-seat SUV charters.

Direct local minibuses cover the 140 km mountain corridor in 4.5 to 5 hours for 120,000 VND to 150,000 VND ($4.80 to $6.00 USD). Motorbike riders and private car passengers need 6.5 to 8 hours for the same stretch. Mandatory rest breaks, fuel stops, and photo halts at mountain passes extend the day. This operational guide breaks down 2026 fares, departure schedules from Cau Me Bus Station, police license checkpoints, and pass-by-pass road conditions along Highway QL4C.

## Route Overview & QL4C Mountain Corridor Architecture

National Highway QL4C, known locally as the Happiness Road (Đường Hạnh Phúc), starts at the Km 0 Milestone beside 26/3 Square in central Ha Giang City (115 m elevation). The two-lane asphalt highway heads north through Quan Ba, Yen Minh, and Dong Van districts.

During the first 18 km out of Ha Giang City, the road follows gentle valley contours past Thon Tha. At Km 19, the gradient tilts upward at 10% for the 12 km climb over Bac Sum Pass (Dốc Bắc Sum, 850 m). A short valley floor follows. The highway then ascends to Quan Ba Heaven Gate (Cổng Trời Quản Bạ) at Km 46, reaching 1,500 m above sea level before descending into Tam Son Town at Km 49.

Beyond Tam Son, QL4C drops sharply down Can Ty Pass (Dốc Cán Tỷ, Km 62 to Km 75) to cross the Mien River iron bridge at 420 m elevation. High-altitude pine forests line the next climb into Yen Minh Town at Km 98, which serves as the primary midday refueling and lunch hub. If rockslides block the main QL4C canyon north of Yen Minh, local vans divert onto Provincial Road DT176 via Mau Due and Meo Vac, adding 32 km and 90 minutes to the journey.

When QL4C remains open, vehicles depart Yen Minh and tackle the 9-hairpin ascent of Tham Ma Slope (Dốc Thẩm Mã) at Km 110. Next comes the 9-tier ramp of Chin Khoanh (Dốc Chín Khoanh, 1,380 m) at Km 116. The final 25 km cross the high karst plateau past Pho Cao, Sung La Valley (Km 121), and the Sa Phin junction (Km 125, 1,420 m, site of the Vuong Family Palace) before dropping into Dong Van Old Quarter at Km 140.

## Local Passenger Minibuses & Limousine Shuttles From Cau Me Station

For budget travelers without a valid motorcycle permit, local intra-provincial buses provide a reliable mechanical link to Dong Van. Large 40-berth sleeper coaches cannot clear the tight 15-meter hairpins on Bac Sum and Tham Ma passes. Smaller mountain vans take over here. Public transit north of Ha Giang City runs on short-wheelbase 29-seat Hyundai County buses, 16-seat Ford Transit vans, or 9-seat Hyundai Solati limousines.

Public routes originate at Ha Giang Provincial Bus Station (Bến xe khách Hà Giang), located in Cau Me village along National Highway QL2 about 5 km south of Km 0. Overnight sleeper buses from Hanoi drop passengers at this terminal between 03:30 and 04:30. Free dorm beds await inside. You can rest for 2 hours before boarding the morning highland minibus.

Three primary transport companies run daily departures on the Ha Giang–Dong Van corridor:

1. **Quang Nghi Transport (Nhà xe Quang Nghị — Hotline `0915 448 933`):** Operates 16-seat Ford Transit vans (150,000 VND / $6.00 USD) and 9-seat VIP Limousines (200,000 VND to 250,000 VND / $8.00 to $10.00 USD) at 07:00, 08:30, 13:00, and 14:30, with free hostel pickups in Ha Giang City.
2. **Cau Me Bus Company (Nhà xe Cầu Mè):** Runs 29-seat County coasters departing Cau Me Terminal at 05:00, 06:30, and 11:30 daily for 120,000 VND ($4.80 USD) paid in cash on board.
3. **A Sinh & Ngoc Cuong (Nhà xe A Sinh / Ngọc Cường):** Depart at 07:30 and 12:30 from Cau Me Station for 130,000 VND to 150,000 VND ($5.20 to $6.00 USD), pausing at Km 0 to collect pre-booked riders.

Local County buses double as highland freight carriers. Conductors load produce sacks and hardware parts into the rear bay, pausing for 3 minutes at markets in Tam Son and Yen Minh. Expect sharp lateral swaying across more than 120 hairpin turns. Take motion sickness medication 45 minutes before departure and reserve Seat 1 or Seat 2 behind the driver.

## Motorbike Self-Drive vs Easy Rider Chauffeur Logistics

Public buses do not pause at viewpoints along QL4C. Riding a motorbike lets you stop freely at Quan Ba Heaven Gate, Tham Ma Slope, and the Vuong Palace. Your choice between self-driving and hiring an Easy Rider depends on legal licensing and mountain gear control.

```
[Arrival in Ha Giang City]
       │
       ├─► Hold valid home motorcycle license + 1968 IDP (A/A1 stamped)?
       │      ├── YES ──► Experienced with manual gears on 10% wet gradients?
       │      │              ├── YES ──► Self-Drive 110cc Blade (200,000 VND/day) or 150cc XR150L (450,000 VND/day)
       │      │              └── NO  ──► Hire Easy Rider Chauffeur (1,200,000 - 1,500,000 VND/day)
       │      └── NO (US/AU/CA/1949 IDP or car-only license)
       │             ├── Want open-air stops at passes? ──► Easy Rider Chauffeur (100% legal pillion)
       │             └── Prefer enclosed cabin? ──► Quang Nghi Limousine (220,000 VND) or 7-Seat SUV (2,000,000 VND)
```

Established shops in Ha Giang City—including QT Motorbikes, Jasmine Hostel, Ha Giang Epic, and Hong Hao—rent three motorbike classes:

* **110cc Semi-Automatic (Honda Wave RSX, Honda Blade):** 180,000 VND to 220,000 VND ($7.20 to $8.80 USD) per day. Features a 4-speed rotary foot shifter without a hand clutch and strong engine braking in Gear 1 and Gear 2.
* **150cc Manual Underbones (Honda Winner X, Yamaha Exciter 150):** 280,000 VND to 350,000 VND ($11.20 to $14.00 USD) per day with a 6-speed manual clutch and dual disc brakes.
* **150cc Dual-Sport Bikes (Honda XR150L, Yamaha WR155R):** 450,000 VND to 550,000 VND ($18.00 to $22.00 USD) per day with long-travel suspension for broken asphalt.

The 140 km climb consumes 3.5 to 4.5 liters of RON 95 petrol, costing 85,000 VND to 110,000 VND ($3.40 to $4.40 USD). Fill your tank at the Km 0 Petrolimex station before leaving Ha Giang City. Official Petrolimex stations along the route operate at Tam Son Town (Km 49), Yen Minh Town (Km 98), and Dong Van Town (Km 139).

> [!WARNING]
> **CSGT Police Checkpoints & 1968 IDP Enforcement:** Traffic Police (CSGT) staff daily checkpoints at the northern exit of Ha Giang City (Km 4 QL4C), the base of Bac Sum Pass (Km 18), and Tam Son Town (Km 48). Under Decree 100/2019/ND-CP and Decree 123/2021/ND-CP, foreign riders must carry both a home motorcycle license and a physical **1968 Vienna Convention International Driving Permit (IDP)** with Category A or A1 stamped. Permits under the 1949 Geneva Convention (issued in the US, Australia, Canada, and the UK) are invalid in Vietnam. Riding without a 1968 IDP brings a fine of 1,500,000 VND to 4,000,000 VND ($60.00 to $160.00 USD), 7-day bike impoundment, and voided medical insurance.

Travelers without a 1968 IDP can hire an **Easy Rider** chauffeur for 1,100,000 VND to 1,500,000 VND ($44.00 to $60.00 USD) per day. That price includes a 150cc bike, fuel, rain gear, helmet, and the driver's homestay in Dong Van.

## Private SUV Charter & Step-by-Step Transfer Protocol

During the cold mist season from November to February, temperatures at Sa Phin drop to 6°C–10°C. Groups of 3 to 5 passengers often charter a private 7-seat SUV (Toyota Fortuner, Ford Everest, or Mitsubishi Xpander Cross) for 1,800,000 VND to 2,400,000 VND ($72.00 to $96.00 USD) one-way, following this five-step schedule:

1. **Step 1 — Reorganize Luggage in Ha Giang City (04:00 – 07:00):** Store 20-kg suitcases free at your hostel depot, pack a 10-kg daypack, and eat phở (40,000 VND / $1.60 USD) before departure.
2. **Step 2 — Climb Bac Sum Pass to Quan Ba (07:30 – 09:30):** Depart by 07:30 to beat quarry trucks and stop for 25 minutes at Quan Ba Heaven Gate (Km 46, 1,500 m) overlooking Twin Fairy Mountains.
3. **Step 3 — Cross Can Ty Pass to Yen Minh (09:30 – 12:30):** Descend Can Ty Pass in low gear to the Mien River Gorge, climb the pine ridge, and stop in Yen Minh Town (Km 98) for a 45-minute lunch (80,000 VND / $3.20 USD).
4. **Step 4 — Ascend Tham Ma Slope to Sa Phin (12:30 – 15:30):** Climb the 9 switchbacks of Tham Ma Slope (Km 110), visit Lung Cam Village in Sung La (Km 121, ticket 20,000 VND / $0.80 USD), and tour the Vuong Family Palace at Sa Phin (Km 125, ticket 30,000 VND / $1.20 USD).
5. **Step 5 — Reach Dong Van Before Dusk (15:30 – 16:45):** Finish the last 15 km into Dong Van Old Quarter before 17:00 to avoid dense valley fog.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What time does the last public bus leave Ha Giang for Dong Van?
The final public minibus and limousine departures leave Cau Me Bus Station and Ha Giang City between 13:30 and 14:30 (Quang Nghi and Cau Me). No public buses run after 15:00 because crossing Can Ty and Tham Ma passes in darkness is unsafe. Late afternoon arrivals from Hanoi must sleep in Ha Giang City and board the 06:30 or 07:30 bus next morning.

### Can I load a motorbike or bicycle onto the local Ha Giang to Dong Van bus?
Neither 16-seat Ford Transit vans nor 9-seat Limousines can carry bikes. However, the 29-seat Hyundai County buses departing Cau Me Terminal at 05:00, 06:30, and 11:30 fit one disassembled bicycle in the rear hold for a 100,000 VND to 150,000 VND ($4.00 to $6.00 USD) surcharge. Full 110cc motorbikes require a cargo truck transfer costing 400,000 VND to 600,000 VND ($16.00 to $24.00 USD).

### Why do rental shops refuse to rent 125cc automatic scooters for the Dong Van route?
Belt-driven scooters (Honda AirBlade, Vision, Lead) lack manual gears and provide near-zero engine braking on 10% descents like Can Ty Pass (13 km) and Bac Sum Pass (12 km). Riding the hand brakes overheats brake fluid and glazes pads within 15 minutes. Always rent a 4-speed semi-automatic (Honda Blade/Wave) or manual bike and descend in Gear 1 or Gear 2.

### Are there working ATMs and petrol stations between Ha Giang City and Dong Van?
Yes, at 45 km to 50 km intervals. Agribank operates ATMs in Tam Son Town (Km 49), Yen Minh Town (Km 98), and Dong Van Town (Km 140) with per-transaction limits of 3,000,000 VND to 5,000,000 VND ($120.00 to $200.00 USD). State Petrolimex stations sit in those same four towns.
