# Quy Nhon to Phu Yen Coastal Drive: Route & Stops (2026)

Driving south from Quy Nhon in Binh Dinh Province to Tuy Hoa, the coastal capital of Phu Yen Province, covers 100 km on a direct highway run or 135 km when tracing the full oceanfront loop. While heavy freight trucks turn inland onto National Highway QL1A through the Cu Mong Pass tunnel, leisure drivers and motorcyclists follow **National Highway QL1D**—a 33 km cliffside corniche linking southern Quy Nhon directly to Song Cau and Xuan Dai Bay before dropping into Phu Yen's volcanic headlands and tidal lagoons.

You can complete the direct drive from central Quy Nhon to Tuy Hoa in 2 hours without stops. Most travelers dedicate 7 to 9 hours to the corridor, stringing together eight coastal landmarks between Ghenh Rang and Nghinh Phong Tower—either as a full-day round-trip excursion from Quy Nhon or a one-way sightseeing transfer into Tuy Hoa. Below is the verified 2026 route log, kilometer-by-kilometer stop order, entrance ticket tariffs, seafood prices at O Loan Lagoon, and vehicle rental comparison.

## Segment 1: Highway QL1D Cliff Road (Km 0 to Km 33)

The coastal drive begins at the southern end of Xuan Dieu Boulevard in Quy Nhon. Instead of taking the northern Phuong Mai Peninsula detour to Ky Co Beach and Eo Gio (25 km northeast of downtown, best visited on a separate morning in Quy Nhon), point your vehicle south onto **Highway QL1D** (Quy Nhon–Song Cau Road).

For 33 km, four-lane paved asphalt hugs granite cliffs 40 to 80 meters above the South China Sea:

* **Km 3 — Ghenh Rang Tien Sa & Queen's Beach (*Bai Tam Hoang Hau*):** Smooth egg-shaped granite boulders line the cove below Han Mac Tu hill. Free parking costs 5,000 VND ($0.20 USD) per motorbike.
* **Km 13 — Bai Xep Fishing Village (Quy Nhon):** A granite-framed bay home to boutique cliffside resorts and beach bars detailed in our [Where to Stay in Quy Nhon](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-quy-nhon/) guide.
* **Km 17 to Km 24 — Bai Bau, Bai Rang & Bai Bang:** Three sheltered coves tucked below the QL1D highway guardrails. Entry costs 15,000 to 20,000 VND ($0.60 to $0.80 USD) if you take the access ramps down to the sand.
* **Km 33 — Song Cau & Xuan Dai Bay:** QL1D crosses the provincial boundary into Phu Yen Province at Song Cau Town, wrapping around the calm turquoise waters and lobster-farming rafts of **Xuan Dai Bay** (*Vinh Xuan Dai*) before merging briefly with QL1A at Km 48.

## Segment 2: North-to-South Stop Order in Phu Yen (Km 62 to Km 105)

At **Km 62** (Chi Thanh Town, Tuy An District), turn left (east) off QL1A toward the coast. Following this exact north-to-south sequence eliminates backtracking along the narrow rural lanes of Tuy An.

### Stop 1: Ong Cop Wooden Bridge (Km 64)

Spanning the Binh Ba River delta near An Ninh Tay Commune, **Ong Cop Bridge** (*Cau Go Ong Cop*, also called Tuy An Bridge) is the longest wooden bridge in Vietnam at **800 meters long** and 1.5 to 1.8 meters wide. Built from casuarina planks and bamboo pilings, the bridge is restricted to pedestrians, bicycles, and motorbikes.

* **Toll Fee:** 5,000 to 10,000 VND ($0.20 to $0.40 USD) per motorbike collected at the northern wooden booth.
* **Car Logistics:** Private cars cannot drive across the planks. Drivers stop at the northern riverbank for 15 minutes of photos, then drive 3 km around the concrete highway bridge to meet you near Mang Lang Church.

### Stop 2: Mang Lang Church (Km 67)

Located 2.5 km east of Ong Cop Bridge in An Thach Commune, **Mang Lang Church** (*Nha Tho Mang Lang*) was constructed in **1892** by French priest Joseph de La Cassagne. Its twin gray-green Gothic bell towers frame a shaded courtyard. Inside an artificial underground grotto beneath the front hill, a glass display case preserves an original edition of *Phep Giang Tam Ngay*—the first book printed in romanized Vietnamese script (*Quoc Ngu*), authored by Jesuit missionary Alexandre de Rhodes in **1651** in Rome.

* **Opening Hours:** 07:00 to 11:30 and 14:00 to 17:00 daily.
* **Admission:** Free entry (10,000 VND / $0.40 USD caretaker tip for unlocking the underground book vault; 5,000 VND motorbike parking). Allow 35 minutes.

### Stop 3: Ganh Da Dia Basalt Columns (Km 76)

Drive 9 km northeast along the coastal causeway to **Ganh Da Dia** (The Cliff of Stone Plates) in An Ninh Dong Commune. Formed by ancient volcanic lava flows fracturing upon contact with cold seawater, roughly 35,000 dark hexagonal and pentagonal basalt columns interlock like stacked dinner plates across a 200-meter-wide promontory.

* **2026 Entrance Ticket:** 40,000 VND ($1.60 USD) per adult; 10,000 VND ($0.40 USD) for the optional round-trip electric buggy from the parking gate down to the cliff edge (600 meters). Parking costs 5,000 VND for motorbikes or 25,000 VND for cars. Allow 45 to 60 minutes.

### Stop 4: O Loan Lagoon Seafood Lunch (Km 84)

Loop 8 km south from Ganh Da Dia to the **An Hai Bridge** overlooking **O Loan Lagoon** (*Dam O Loan*), a 1,570-hectare brackish-water lagoon famous for mineral-rich shellfish. Floating wooden rafts (*nha be*) cluster below the bridge in An Ninh Dong.

* **2026 Menu Prices (Thuy Kieu, Tuan, or Lam Hung Rafts):**
  * **O Loan Blood Cockles (*So huyet O Loan*):** 180,000 to 250,000 VND/kg ($7.20 to $10.00 USD) roasted with garlic, tamarind, or grilled rare.
  * **Grilled Lagoon Oysters (*Hau nuong mo hanh*):** 120,000 VND ($4.80 USD) per platter of 8 to 10 oysters with scallion oil and crushed peanuts.
  * **Steamed Jumping Snails (*Oc nhay*) or Blue Swimming Crab (*Ghe hap*):** 280,000 to 420,000 VND/kg ($11.20 to $16.80 USD).
  * **Seafood Hotpot & Jellyfish Salad:** 150,000 to 250,000 VND ($6.00 to $10.00 USD). Allow 60 to 75 minutes for lunch.

### Stop 5: Hon Yen Coral Reef & Stop 6: Bai Xep – Ganh Ong Cliff (Km 92 to Km 96)

* **Hon Yen Islet (Km 92, Nhon Hoi Village):** A cone-shaped volcanic rock 400 meters offshore. On the 1st and 15th days of the lunar month after 14:30 low tide, exposed purple and yellow soft-coral flats emerge above the waterline. Free access (5,000 VND parking).
* **Bai Xep Phu Yen & Ganh Ong Promontory (Km 95, An Chan Commune):** Not to be confused with Quy Nhon's Bai Xep, this grass-covered cliff above black volcanic rocks and wild pandanus bushes gained fame in the 2015 film *I See Yellow Flowers on the Green Grass*.
* **2026 Entrance Ticket:** 20,000 VND ($0.80 USD) per adult; allow 40 minutes to walk the cliff path.

### Stop 7: Nghinh Phong Tower in Tuy Hoa & Stop 8: Mui Dien Extension (Km 105 to Km 135)

Roll 10 km south along pristine Doc Let / Tuy Hoa beachfront boulevard to **Nghinh Phong Tower** (*Thap Nghinh Phong*) at the intersection of Nguyen Huu Tho and Doc Lap streets in Tuy Hoa City. Inspired by the basalt columns of Ganh Da Dia and the legend of *Lac Long Quan and Au Co*, the twin 35-meter stone fins channel sea wind through a central 2-meter gap to produce natural acoustic whistling notes. Admission is **free 24 hours a day**.

If you are continuing south toward Khanh Hoa or have 2.5 extra hours, drive 30 km south of Tuy Hoa along the Phuoc Tan–Bai Nga coastal road to **Vung Ro Bay** (historic Ho Chi Minh Sea Trail landing cove) and **Mui Dien Lighthouse** (*Hai Dang Dai Lanh*), where a 20,000 VND ($0.80 USD) ticket and a 20-minute stair climb reward you with views from mainland Vietnam's easternmost coastal cape.

> [!TIP]
> **Midday Glare & Tide Timing:** Leave Quy Nhon by 07:30 so you reach Ganh Da Dia before 10:00 when the black basalt columns absorb intense midday heat. Check the Tuy Hoa lunar tide chart if visiting Hon Yen—walking onto the exposed coral flats is only possible during low tide windows on the 1st, 2nd, 15th, and 16th days of the lunar month.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How long does the coastal drive from Quy Nhon to Tuy Hoa take?
Driving nonstop from Quy Nhon to Tuy Hoa via Highway QL1D and QL1A takes 2 hours (100 km). With sightseeing stops at Ong Cop Bridge, Mang Lang Church, Ganh Da Dia, O Loan Lagoon for lunch, Ganh Ong (Bai Xep), and Nghinh Phong Tower, the full 135 km coastal route takes 7 to 8.5 hours.

### Can a 4-seater or 7-seater car cross Ong Cop Wooden Bridge?
No. Ong Cop Wooden Bridge is only 1.5 to 1.8 meters wide and supports pedestrians, bicycles, and motorbikes (5,000 to 10,000 VND toll). Private cars stop at the northern bank for photos and use the parallel concrete highway bridge to reach Mang Lang Church in 5 minutes.
