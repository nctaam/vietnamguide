# Da Lat to Pleiku Transport (2026): Sleeper Bus Guide

Traveling north from Da Lat to Pleiku covers **380 km** across three Central Highlands provinces. No railway line climbs onto the plateau, and flying between Lien Khuong Airport (**DLI**) and Pleiku Airport (**PXU**) requires a layover in Ho Chi Minh City or Hanoi. Road transport handles nearly all passenger traffic along this south-to-north highland corridor.

The route splits into two geographic halves. First, buses and vans descend from Da Lat (**1,500m** elevation, Lam Dong Province) northwest along winding National Highway **QL27**, dropping over **Phu Son Pass (Đèo Phú Sơn)** and past **Lak Lake (Hồ Lắk)** into Buon Ma Thuot (**536m**, Dak Lak Province) after **200 km** (**4.5 to 5 hours**). From Buon Ma Thuot, vehicles merge onto smooth four-lane **QL14** (**AH17**) and cruise **180 km** north across basalt coffee and pepper plateaus into Pleiku (**750m**, Gia Lai Province) in another **3.5 to 4 hours**.

For direct point-to-point transit in 2026, an **overnight 34-berth sleeper bus or 22-cabin VIP coach** departing Da Lat between **17:30 and 19:30** is the top choice. Direct sleepers take **8 to 9 hours** and cost **320,000 to 380,000 VND** (**$12.80 to $15.20 USD**) for a standard berth, or **420,000 to 480,000 VND** (**$16.80 to $19.20 USD**) for a private single cabin. Travelers who dislike overnight mountain curves can break the journey in Buon Ma Thuot using two daytime **9-seat limousine vans** (**200,000 to 250,000 VND** Da Lat to Buon Ma Thuot plus **180,000 to 220,000 VND** Buon Ma Thuot to Pleiku), while private cars cost **3,800,000 to 4,600,000 VND** (**$152.00 to $184.00 USD**).

Check our guides to [Where to Stay in Da Lat](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-da-lat/) and [Where to Stay in Pleiku](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-pleiku/) before booking to align your hotel with shuttle zones.

> [!TIP]
> **Early Arrival Strategy:** Overnight buses pull into Duc Long Gia Lai Bus Station between **02:30 and 04:30**. Operators (**Viet Tan Phat**, **Minh Quoc**, **Rung Thong**) let passengers sleep onboard until **05:30** before running free dawn minibus transfers (`xe trung chuyển`) to central Pleiku hotels.

## Option 1: Direct Overnight Sleeper Buses & VIP Cabin Coaches

Direct interprovincial coaches link Da Lat and Pleiku every evening, handling the twisty **QL27** mountain descent before midnight and cruising up smooth **QL14** while passengers sleep.

### Major Bus Operators, Timetables, and Fares

Four established Central Highlands transport companies dominate the direct Da Lat–Pleiku route in 2026:

* **Viet Tan Phat (Nhà Xe Việt Tân Phát):** The largest operator on the Lam Dong–Gia Lai corridor. Runs both **34-berth sleeper buses** (**330,000 to 360,000 VND** / **$13.20 to $14.40 USD**) and **22-cabin VIP limousine coaches** (**440,000 to 480,000 VND** / **$17.60 to $19.20 USD**). Evening departures leave Da Lat at **17:30, 18:15, and 19:00**, plus a daytime sleeper departure at **07:15**.
* **Minh Quoc (Nhà Xe Minh Quốc):** Based in Kon Tum and Pleiku, Minh Quoc operates well-maintained **34-berth VIP sleepers** (**340,000 to 380,000 VND** / **$13.60 to $15.20 USD**) and **24-cabin luxury coaches** (**420,000 to 460,000 VND** / **$16.80 to $18.40 USD**). Buses depart Da Lat daily at **17:30 and 18:30**, reaching Pleiku around **02:30 to 03:30** before continuing **50 km** north to Kon Tum.
* **Rung Thong (Nhà Xe Rừng Thông):** Runs evening **34-berth sleeper buses** and **22-cabin coaches** departing Da Lat at **18:00 and 19:00**. Fares run **350,000 to 380,000 VND** (**$14.00 to $15.20 USD**) for standard berths and **430,000 to 470,000 VND** (**$17.20 to $18.80 USD**) for private curtained cabins with massage mattresses, USB-A/USB-C ports, and reading lamps.
* **Thinh Phat (Nhà Xe Thịnh Phát):** Operates reliable **34-berth sleeper coaches** departing at **17:45 and 19:30** for **320,000 to 350,000 VND** (**$12.80 to $14.00 USD**). Includes free minibus hotel pickup within a **5 km** radius of Da Lat Market.

### Terminal Logistics and Berth Selection

* **Da Lat Departure Terminal:** Coaches board at **Da Lat Interprovincial Bus Station (Bến Xe Liên Tỉnh Đà Lạt)** at **01 To Hien Thanh Street, Ward 3**, **2.5 km** south of Xuan Huong Lake. Free shuttle pickup (`xe trung chuyển`) collects hotel guests within **5 km** of the station **45 to 60 minutes** prior to departure; otherwise, a Grab car or Lado taxi costs **45,000 to 65,000 VND** (**$1.80 to $2.60 USD**).
* **Pleiku Arrival Terminal:** Buses arrive at **Duc Long Gia Lai Bus Station (Bến Xe Đức Long Gia Lai)** at **43 Ly Nam De Street, Tra Ba Ward**, **3.5 km** southeast of Dai Doan Ket Square. Operators provide free drop-off shuttles into central Pleiku, or you can take a Mai Linh, Sun Taxi, or Grab car for **50,000 to 70,000 VND** (**$2.00 to $2.80 USD**, **8 to 10 minutes**).
* **Where to Book Your Berth:** Because **QL27** over Phu Son Pass features tight switchbacks, book a **lower-deck middle berth (rows A2 to A6 or B2 to B6)** between the axles. Avoid the rear five-berth bench and upper-deck tail rows where suspension bounce is strongest.

## Option 2: Daytime Split Journey via Buon Ma Thuot (Limousine Vans)

To watch the highland valleys in daylight and avoid sleeping on mountain roads, split the **380 km** trip at Buon Ma Thuot using two **9-seat VIP limousine vans**:

1. **Leg 1 — Da Lat to Buon Ma Thuot (200 km, 4.5 to 5 hours):** Morning 9-seat vans (**Thinh Phat**, **Rung Thong**, **Quoc Thang**, **Loan Phuong**) depart Da Lat between **06:30 and 08:00** with hotel pickup for **200,000 to 250,000 VND** (**$8.00 to $10.00 USD**), descending Phu Son Pass and passing Lak Lake around **10:00** to reach Buon Ma Thuot by **11:30 to 12:30**.
2. **Midday Coffee & Lunch Stopover (1.5 to 2 hours):** Drop off near Nga Sau or the **World Coffee Museum** on Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street (**150,000 VND** / **$6.00 USD** entry) for lunch and Dak Lak espresso (**35,000 to 55,000 VND**).
3. **Leg 2 — Buon Ma Thuot to Pleiku (180 km, 3.5 hours):** Board an afternoon 9-seat limousine (**Rung Thong**, **Thu Duc**, **Minh Quoc**) departing **13:30 to 15:00** up four-lane **QL14**. Fares run **180,000 to 220,000 VND** (**$7.20 to $8.80 USD**) with Pleiku hotel drop-off by **17:00 to 18:30**. See our guide to [Buon Ma Thuot to Pleiku Transport](/plan/buon-ma-thuot-to-pleiku-transport/) for hourly schedules.

Combined daytime van fares cost **380,000 to 470,000 VND** (**$15.20 to $18.80 USD**)—matching a single VIP sleeper cabin with zero overnight fatigue.

## Option 3: Private Car Charter (4-Seater, 7-Seater, and 16-Seater)

Chartering a private vehicle turns the **380 km** transfer into a daylight sightseeing tour departing Da Lat at **07:00** and reaching Pleiku by **16:30**.

* **4-Seater Sedan (Toyota Vios, Honda City, Mazda 3):** **3,800,000 to 4,100,000 VND** (**$152.00 to $164.00 USD**) one way for up to 3 passengers.
* **7-Seater SUV/MPV (Toyota Fortuner, Innova Cross, Mitsubishi Xpander):** **4,200,000 to 4,600,000 VND** (**$168.00 to $184.00 USD**) one way; best for **QL27** clearance and 4 passengers with bags.
* **16-Seater Minibus (Ford Transit, Hyundai Solati):** **5,500,000 to 6,400,000 VND** (**$220.00 to $256.00 USD**) one way for up to 10 travelers.

Quotes include fuel, driver wages, and **QL14** BOT tolls (**120,000 to 160,000 VND** total). For a **300,000 to 400,000 VND** (**$12.00 to $16.00 USD**) waiting fee, add stops at **Elephant Falls & Linh An Pagoda** in Nam Ban (**30 km** from Da Lat, free entry), **Lak Lake & Jun Village** at Km 148 (**150 km** from Da Lat), and **Dray Nur Waterfall** (**40,000 VND** / **$1.60 USD** ticket).

## Option 4: Two-Day Motorbike Touring Along QL27 and QL14

Riding **380 km** in one day takes **8.5 to 10 hours** and is punishing on **QL27**. Experienced riders split the trip into two **180 to 200 km** days with an overnight in Buon Ma Thuot (see [Where to Stay in Buon Ma Thuot](/destinations/where-to-stay-in-buon-ma-thuot/)).

* **Day 1 — Da Lat to Buon Ma Thuot via QL27 (200 km, 5 hours):** Exit Da Lat via Ta Nung Pass (**DT725**) to Nam Ban and join **QL27** at Dinh Van. Descend **Phu Son Pass** at **35 to 45 km/h**, stop for *Bún Cá Hồ Lắk* (**40,000 to 50,000 VND**) beside **Lak Lake**, and ride into Buon Ma Thuot.
* **Day 2 — Buon Ma Thuot to Pleiku via QL14 (180 km, 4 hours):** Follow four-lane **QL14** north through Buon Ho, Ea H'leo, and **Chu Se** into Pleiku. Obey **50 to 60 km/h** urban limits at blue municipal signs (*Khu đông dân cư*), where police (*CSGT*) issue **800,000 VND** (**$32.00 USD**) speeding fines.

One-way motorbike rentals cost **250,000 to 350,000 VND/day** (**$10.00 to $14.00 USD**) for a Honda XR150L or Winner X, plus a **500,000 to 700,000 VND** (**$20.00 to $28.00 USD**) bus cargo return fee. Carry your physical **1968 Convention International Driving Permit (IDP)**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Are there restrooms on board Da Lat to Pleiku sleeper buses?
Most 34-berth sleepers and 22-cabin coaches on **QL27** lack onboard toilets due to steep mountain switchbacks. Drivers make two **15 to 20-minute** rest stops: around **21:30 to 22:00** near Buon Ma Thuot, and around **00:30** at Ea H'leo on **QL14**.

### What should I wear on the overnight bus from Da Lat?
Wear a fleece hoodie and socks. Da Lat evenings start at **14°C to 16°C**, and drivers run ceiling AC vents at **18°C to 20°C** all night. Each berth includes a thin blanket and pillow.
