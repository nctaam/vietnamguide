# Southern Vietnam Itinerary (2026): 5 to 7-Day Route Guide

Life in Vietnam's tropical south operates at an entirely different tempo than the Confucian north or the contemplative imperial center. Anchored by the commercial powerhouse of Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon) and fanning southwest into the vast aquatic labyrinth of the Mekong Delta, this tropical macro-region is defined by rapid economic modernization, 20th-century geopolitical conflict, and abundant agricultural life.

A dedicated southern itinerary provides the perfect blend of high-energy urban exploration, authentic riverine culture, and offshore tropical relaxation. In five to seven days, travelers can investigate underground wartime battlefield systems, cruise past wooden trading boats at dawn on the Mekong River, and unwind along white sand beaches in the Gulf of Thailand without navigating winter rain or mountain passes.

```
       SOUTHERN VIETNAM HIGHLIGHTS: 5 TO 7-DAY ROUTE
==================================================================
Day 1: Arrive Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) -> French Core & Street Dining
Day 2: Cu Chi Tunnels (Ben Duoc) -> War Remnants Museum & Cholon
Day 3: CT01 Expressway to Can Tho -> Hau River Orchards & Sunset
Day 4: Dawn Cai Rang Floating Market -> (5-Day Return to SGN)
Day 5: Extension: Flight VCA to PQC -> Phu Quoc Island Beach Resort
Day 6: Extension: An Thoi Snorkeling & Sao Beach White Sands
Day 7: Extension: Sunset Town & Seafood Night Market -> Depart PQC
==================================================================
```

## Route Architecture: 5-Day Urban-Delta vs. 7-Day Island Extension

Depending on your available travel window, the southern circuit divides cleanly into two high-yield options:

```
           SOUTHERN ROUTE CONFIGURATIONS & NIGHT ALLOCATION
+-------------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| Feature           | 5-Day City & Delta Core     | 7-Day City, Delta & Island  |
+-------------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| Night Allocation  | Saigon (2N), Can Tho (2N)   | Saigon (2N), Can Tho (1N), PQC (3N)|
| Key Themes        | War history, floating market| History, delta life, beach  |
| Primary Flights   | In/Out SGN (Round-trip)     | In SGN, Out PQC (Open-jaw)  |
| Intercity Transit | CT01 Highway limousine      | Limousine + Direct Flight   |
| Best Season       | Year-round (best Dec-Apr)   | November to April (Dry seas)|
+-------------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------------+
```

> [!TIP]
> If pursuing the 7-day extended itinerary, book an "open-jaw" ticket: fly into Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) and depart internationally from Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC) or via a quick domestic connection back through Saigon. This eliminates an arduous 7-hour land-and-ferry backtrack from the delta to the city.

## The Signature 5-Day Urban & Delta Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival in Saigon, French Colonial Relics, and Street Dining

Touch down at Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN). Clear customs, withdraw Vietnamese Dong from ATMs, and hail an official taxi (Vinasun or Mai Linh) or call a Grab car into District 1 (140,000–180,000 VND).

- **13:00 – Check-in in District 1 or 3**: Settle into a boutique property along Dong Khoi, Pasteur, or Nguyen Thi Minh Khai. The immediate visual impression is electric: tree-lined colonial avenues crisscrossed by thousands of scooters beneath glass skyscrapers.
- **14:30 – French Colonial Architectural Loop**: Walk down Dong Khoi Street past the neo-classical Saigon Opera House (1897) to the red-brick Notre-Dame Cathedral (under restoration through 2027). Step inside the magnificent **1891 Saigon Central Post Office**, designed with vaulted iron arches engineered by Gustave Eiffel's workshop and hand-painted historic cartography (free entry).
- **17:00 – Saigon River Sunset**: Stroll to Bach Dang Wharf and board the public Saigon Waterbus to Binh An and back (15,000 VND each way) to view the sunset over the illuminated Landmark 81 tower, Southeast Asia’s second-tallest skyscraper.
- **19:30 – Charcoal-Grilled Broken Rice Feast**: Dine at Com Tam Ba Ghiền (84 Dang Van Ngu) for *com tam suon bi cha* (broken rice with smoky marinated pork chop, steamed egg meatloaf, and crispy shredded pork skin, 65,000–85,000 VND).

### Day 2: Ben Duoc Underground Tunnels, War History, and Cholon

Dedicate your second day to modern history and Vietnam’s largest Chinatown:

- **07:30 – The Historic Ben Duoc Tunnels**: Avoid commercial tour bus crowds by booking a private vehicle or specialized small-group tour to **Ben Duoc Tunnels** in Cu Chi district (70 km northwest, 125,000 VND entrance). Unlike the widened, crowded Ben Dinh site, Ben Duoc preserves authentic multi-tier tunnel networks where National Liberation Front soldiers lived and operated underground during the American War. Crawl through claustrophobic original subterranean passages, inspect camouflaged trapdoors, and taste boiled tapioca dipped in salted peanut sugar.
- **13:00 – Return to Saigon & Southern Pancake Lunch**: Head to Banh Xeo 46A on Dinh Cong Trang Street for crispy golden *banh xeo* stuffed with prawns, pork slices, and bean sprouts, wrapped in fresh mustard leaves and herbs (80,000–120,000 VND).
- **14:30 – War Remnants Museum**: Tour this sobering institution on Vo Van Tan Street (60,000 VND entrance). The graphic photojournalism galleries—especially the "Requiem" collection commemorating war photographers killed in action—provide essential context on modern Vietnam’s post-war resilience.
- **16:30 – Cholon (Chinatown) Heritage**: Take a 15-minute Grab car to District 5. Visit the 1760 **Ba Thien Hau Pagoda**, dedicated to Mazu, goddess of the sea. Admire the porcelain roof figurines and aromatic giant incense coils suspended overhead (free entry). Walk through the wholesale corridors of Binh Tay Market.
- **20:00 – Craft Beer Exploration**: Sample craft beers fermented with tropical fruits (such as Passion Fruit Wheat or Dragonfruit IPA) at Pasteur Street Brewing (144 Pasteur) or Heart of Darkness on Ly Tu Trong.

### Day 3: Expressway into the Mekong Delta and Riverside Can Tho

Check out at 07:30 AM. Board a luxury limousine coach or private car heading southwest along the CT01 Expressway across the Tien and Hau branches of the Mekong River to Can Tho (170 km, 3.5 hours, 180,000–220,000 VND).

- **11:30 – Check-in in Can Tho**: Check into a boutique riverside resort along the Hau River or near Ninh Kieu Wharf.
- **13:30 – Rural Islet Cycling Excursion**: Take a five-minute passenger ferry across the river to **Son Islet** (*Con Son*). Rent a bicycle and pedal along narrow paved paths shaded by pomelo, jackfruit, longan, and rambutan trees. Visit a floating fish farm to see thousands of catfish leaping for feed, and sample traditional southern coconut cakes (*banh khot*) prepared in rural home kitchens.
- **17:00 – Ninh Kieu Wharf Promenade**: Walk along Can Tho’s pedestrian waterfront promenade, photographing the illuminated Can Tho suspension bridge in the evening dusk.
- **19:00 – Delta Countryside Dinner**: Feast on grilled snakehead fish rolled in rice paper with bitter herbs (*ca loc nuong trui*, 180,000 VND) and sweet river fish soup with yellow water flowers (*canh chua ca linh bong dien dien*, 120,000 VND).

### Day 4: Dawn at Cai Rang Floating Market and Delta Traditions

Wake early at 05:30 AM to experience the delta's most famous tradition before heat and commercial tour boats arrive.

- **06:00 – Private Wooden Sampan to Cai Rang**: Board a private wooden boat at Ninh Kieu Wharf (400,000–500,000 VND for a 3-hour private tour). Cruise 6 kilometers upriver as dawn light breaks over the water. Arrive at **Cai Rang Floating Market** by 06:40 AM as wholesale produce trading peaks.
- **07:00 – Breakfast on the River**: Watch wholesale boats display their produce on tall bamboo poles (*cay beo*)—cabbages, watermelons, pineapples, and sweet potatoes. Pull alongside a floating noodle boat to eat hot *hu tieu* noodle soup (45,000 VND) and sip sweet iced milk coffee (*ca phe sua da*, 20,000 VND) while bobbing on the river current.
- **08:30 – Traditional Rice Noodle Workshop**: Stop at a small family-run craft workshop to observe how rice flour batter is steamed into paper-thin crepes, sun-dried on woven bamboo racks, and hand-shredded into noodle strands.
- **10:30 – Disembarkation & Midday Departure**: Return to the wharf and check out.
- **Departure Logic**:
  - *5-Day Travelers*: Board a highway limousine back to Ho Chi Minh City (arriving by 15:30) for your international departure flight from Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN).
  - *7-Day Travelers*: Transfer 15 minutes to Can Tho Airport (VCA) for your afternoon flight to Phu Quoc Island.

## The 7-Day Extended Itinerary: Island Escape in Phu Quoc

Extend your southern journey by adding three nights of marine tranquility on Vietnam’s largest island, located in the warm waters of the Gulf of Thailand:

### Day 5: Flight to Phu Quoc and Sunset Beaches

- **13:00 – Flight from Can Tho to Phu Quoc**: Board a 50-minute direct flight from Can Tho (VCA) to Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC) (750,000–1,200,000 VND).
- **14:30 – Check-in at Beachfront Resort**: Settle into a resort along Bai Truong (Long Beach) on the west coast or Bai Khem in the southeast.
- **16:30 – Golden Hour Swim**: Swim in the calm, crystalline waters of the Gulf of Thailand. Phu Quoc’s west-facing beaches offer some of the most dramatic coastal sunsets in Asia.
- **19:00 – Dinh Cau Night Market**: Head into Duong Dong town to browse seafood stalls. Select live garrupa, blue crabs, mantis shrimp, and sea urchins grilled over charcoal with scallion oil and crushed peanuts (250,000–400,000 VND per person).

### Day 6: An Thoi Archipelago Snorkeling and Bai Sao White Sands

- **08:30 – Speedboat Island Hopping**: Take a speedboat tour from An Thoi port to the southern archipelago (Hon Gam Ghi, Hon May Rut, and Hon Mong Tay, 450,000–750,000 VND per person). Snorkel among shallow coral reefs and swim in transparent aquamarine coves.
- **13:00 – Seafood Lunch on a Deserted Islet**: Enjoy grilled mackerel and fresh fruit on a secluded white sand beach.
- **15:30 – Bai Sao Beach**: Visit Bai Sao on the southeastern coast, celebrated for its powdery white silica sand, gentle waves, and curving coconut palms.
- **19:00 – Authentic Stirred Noodle Dinner**: Dine on Phu Quoc’s signature dish, *bun quay* (stirred seafood noodle soup, 45,000–65,000 VND) at Bun Quay Kien Xay, where fresh squid and shrimp paste are pounded and cooked directly in steaming broth.

### Day 7: Hon Thom Cable Car, Pepper Farms, and Departure

- **09:00 – Hon Thom Over-Sea Cable Car**: Ride the world’s longest three-wire sea cable car (7,899 meters) from Sunset Town to Hon Thom Nature Park (650,000–700,000 VND). Gliding 160 meters above the sea, the 15-minute journey provides jaw-dropping aerial views of fishing fleets and coral reefs.
- **13:00 – Traditional Artisanal Visits**: Stop by a traditional fish sauce barrel house (*nha thung*) to learn how anchovies and sea salt are fermented in giant wooden vats for 12 months to create Vietnam's finest 40-degree fish sauce. Visit an organic black pepper plantation in Khu Tuong to sample sun-dried black and white peppercorns.
- **16:00 – Airport Transfer & Departure**: Transfer 15 minutes to Phu Quoc Airport (PQC) for your international departure or connecting flight.

> [!WARNING]
> While Phu Quoc provides a 30-day visa exemption for international visitors who fly directly into the island, traveling overland through Saigon and the Mekong Delta first requires you to hold a standard Vietnam visa or qualify for the general 45-day unilateral visa exemption. Ensure your paperwork matches your arrival gateway.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I visit the Cai Rang floating market on a day trip from Saigon?
No. Cai Rang Floating Market is located in Can Tho, 170 kilometers southwest of Ho Chi Minh City. Trading peaks between 05:30 and 07:30 AM. A day trip from Saigon cannot reach Can Tho before 10:30 AM, by which time wholesale trading has finished and only empty boats remain. Seeing Cai Rang authentically requires staying overnight in Can Tho.

### How does Cu Chi Tunnels Ben Duoc differ from Ben Dinh?
Ben Dinh is located closer to Saigon (50 km) and accommodates large commercial tour buses. Its tunnels have been heavily widened and illuminated with electric lights for mass tourism. Ben Duoc is located 20 kilometers further northwest (70 km), attracts very few tourist coaches, and preserves authentic multi-tier earthen tunnel networks and the historic Ben Duoc Memorial Temple. We strongly recommend visiting Ben Duoc.

### Is the rainy season a bad time to visit Southern Vietnam?
The southern rainy season runs from May to November. However, rain typically falls in intense, predictable 45-minute afternoon downpours rather than continuous grey drizzles. Mornings and early afternoons are almost always clear and sunny. Visiting during the wet season brings lush green delta orchards and significantly lower resort rates in Phu Quoc.
