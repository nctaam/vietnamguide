# 14 Days in Vietnam: The Definitive Two-Week Route Guide

A two-week holiday represents the ultimate canvas for exploring Vietnam. Fourteen days provides the necessary operational margin to experience four distinct UNESCO World Heritage sites, both historic capitals, northern mountain karst formations, central coastal trading ports, and southern riverine networks without frantic daily checkouts.

The primary risk of a 14-day holiday is overambition. Travel forums frequently suggest shoehorning seven or eight stopovers into two weeks—adding Sapa, Phong Nha, Dalat, and Phu Quoc onto an already dense schedule. This turns a vacation into an exhausting baggage-handling drill. 

The masterclass route outlined below focuses on seven carefully selected anchor bases. It combines high-speed expressways, scenic coastal rail journeys, and strategic domestic air hops to maximize your time on the ground while keeping transit fatigue to a minimum.

```
          14-DAY GRAND TRAVERSE: HANOI TO MEKONG DELTA
========================================================================
Days 1-2:   Hanoi Old Quarter -> Dynastic Shrines, Guild Alleys & Coffee
Day 3:      Ninh Binh Valley -> Trang An River Grottoes & Hang Mua Peak
Days 4-5:   Marine Karst -> Lan Ha Bay Luxury 2D1N Overnight Cruise
Days 6-7:   Imperial Hue -> Forbidden Purple City & Royal Mausoleums
Days 8-10:  Hoi An & Coast -> Lantern Alleys, Cham Temples & Beach Calm
Days 11-12: Ho Chi Minh City -> War Remnants, Cholon & French Boulevards
Days 13-14: Mekong Deep-Dive -> Can Tho Overnight & Cai Rang River Market
========================================================================
```

## Detailed 14-Day Day-by-Day Field Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival in Hanoi and Street-Level Orientation

Land at Noi Bai International Airport (HAN). Pass through immigration and customs, then take an authorized airport car (300,000–350,000 VND) across the Nhat Tan Bridge into Hanoi's Hoan Kiem district.

Settle into your hotel. In the late afternoon, take an orientation walk around Hoan Kiem Lake. Visit Ngoc Son Temple (30,000 VND) on its jade island. 

By 18:00, explore the maze of the Old Quarter's 36 Guild Streets. Dine on *pho bo tai lan* (seared flank beef noodle soup, 60,000 VND) at Pho Gia Truyen on Bat Dan Street. Conclude your evening sipping a hot egg coffee (*ca phe trung*, 35,000 VND) at Cafe Giang on Nguyen Huu Huan.

### Day 2: Hanoi’s Dynastic Foundations and Revolutionary History

Devote your first full day to the intellectual and political heart of Vietnam:

- **08:30 – Temple of Literature (*Van Mieu*)**: Explore the five stone courtyards of Vietnam's first imperial university, built in 1070 under Emperor Ly Thanh Tong. Walk among the 82 doctoral turtle stelae and admire the timber architecture of the Khue Van Cac pavilion (70,000 VND entrance).
- **11:00 – Hoa Lo Prison Memorial**: Examine the French colonial prison built in 1896, documenting Vietnamese anticolonial struggles and the subsequent confinement of American aviators (50,000 VND entrance; audio guide strongly recommended at 50,000 VND).
- **13:00 – Charcoal-Grilled Pork Lunch**: Feast on *bun cha* at Bun Cha Ta (21 Nguyen Huu Huan) for 70,000 VND.
- **15:00 – Vietnam Museum of Ethnology**: Travel 20 minutes to Cau Giay district (60,000 VND Grab car) to explore outdoor traditional dwellings—including an authentic tall communal house (*nha rong*) and Ede longhouse—representing Vietnam's ethnic diversity (40,000 VND entrance).
- **19:00 – Water Puppet Artistry**: Watch traditional agrarian water puppetry accompanied by live folk instruments at the Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre (100,000–200,000 VND).

### Day 3: Karst Grottoes and Dragon Summit in Ninh Binh

Check out of Hanoi at 07:30 AM in a 9-seat limousine van (150,000–220,000 VND per person). Travel 95 kilometers south down the CT01 Expressway into Ninh Binh's karst country.

- **09:30 – Trang An Scenic Landscape Complex**: Board a traditional 4-person wooden boat rowed by local boatwomen. Choose **Route 2** (250,000 VND per person), traversing four submerged caves and the historic King Le palace site.
- **13:00 – Regional Lunch**: Dine on roasted mountain goat meat with lemongrass and crispy scorched rice cakes (*com chay*, 150,000 VND).
- **15:30 – Hang Mua Dragon Summit**: Climb 486 carved limestone stairs to the top ridge of Ngoa Long Mountain (100,000 VND entrance). Enjoy sunset panoramas over the winding Ngo Dong River and jagged limestone cliffs.
- **Evening Overnight**: Stay overnight in a tranquil riverside bungalow in Tam Coc or Trang An (600,000–1,200,000 VND/night), cycling through quiet evening rural lanes.

### Day 4: Boarding a Marine Karst Cruise in Lan Ha Bay

Take an early 07:30 AM direct transfer van from Ninh Binh eastward to Got Pier in Hai Phong (2.5 hours, 250,000–300,000 VND).

- **12:00 – Embarkation**: Step aboard your luxury overnight cruise ship (3,500,000–7,500,000 VND per cabin for 2 days/1 night all-inclusive). We recommend Lan Ha Bay cruises for their pristine water quality and low boat density compared to Ha Long’s congested core.
- **14:30 – Sea Kayaking & Hidden Lagoons**: Paddle through the Dark and Bright Cave tidal arches into a quiet lagoon surrounded by sheer vertical jungle walls.
- **17:30 – Sun Deck Twilight**: Watch the sunset behind limestone spires while relaxing on the top deck.
- **19:30 – Multi-course Seafood Dinner**: Savor fresh grilled prawns, steamed squid, and sea bass, followed by evening squid fishing.

### Day 5: Morning Sea Mist, Disembarkation, and Transit to Hue

Wake at 06:15 AM for sunrise Tai Chi over the bay.

- **07:30 – Morning Cavern Excursion**: Explore Cat Ba Island’s limestone formations or visit a local fish farming cooperative.
- **10:00 – Brunch and Harbor Return**: Enjoy an early lunch while sailing back to the terminal. Disembark by 11:30 AM.
- **12:00 – Highway Return to Hanoi**: Take an express limousine van back along the CT04 Expressway to Hanoi (2 hours).
- **Evening Transit to Hue (Choice of Two Options)**:
  - *Option A (Romantic Heritage Rail)*: Board the overnight sleeper train **SE19** departing Hanoi Station at 19:50 and arriving in Hue at 09:30 the next morning. Book a 4-berth air-conditioned soft sleeper compartment (900,000–1,100,000 VND per berth).
  - *Option B (Time-Saver Flight)*: Transfer directly from the cruise to Noi Bai Airport (HAN) for an evening 70-minute flight to Hue (HUI), checking into a riverside hotel along the Perfume River by 21:00.

### Day 6: The Nguyen Dynasty Imperial Citadel in Hue

Spend your first day in Vietnam’s former imperial capital (1802–1945):

- **08:30 – The Imperial City (*Dai Noi*)**: Enter through the monumental Ngo Mon Gate into the walled citadel (200,000 VND entrance). Walk through the Thai Hoa Palace, the rebuilt Kien Trung Palace (restored in 2024), and the ruined courtyards of the Forbidden Purple City (*Tu Cam Thanh*).
- **12:30 – Royal Hue Cuisine**: Dine at Quan Hanh on Pho Duc Chinh. Sample delicate steamed rice cakes: *banh beo* in small porcelain dishes, crispy *banh khoai* pancakes, and shrimp-filled *banh bot loc* (40,000–70,000 VND).
- **14:30 – Thien Mu Pagoda & Perfume River**: Visit the iconic 7-tier Phuoc Duyen tower, built in 1601 on a scenic bluff overlooking the Perfume River (*Song Huong*). View the Austin motorcar that transported monk Thich Quang Duc to his 1963 self-immolation in Saigon (free entry).
- **19:00 – Royal Dragon Boat Dinner**: Cruise the Perfume River in the evening with traditional folk singing (*ca Hue*).

### Day 7: Royal Tombs of Khai Dinh and Tu Duc

Dedicate your morning to the elaborate royal mausoleums south of the city:

- **08:30 – Tomb of Emperor Khai Dinh**: Marvel at this 1931 hillside tomb combining French gothic and traditional Vietnamese architecture, famous for its intricate mosaics crafted from broken porcelain and glass bowls (150,000 VND entrance).
- **11:00 – Tomb of Emperor Tu Duc**: Tour this expansive garden estate featuring an ornamental lake, wooden pavilions on stilts, and pine groves designed by the poet-emperor as a personal retreat (150,000 VND entrance).
- **13:00 – Spicy Beef Noodle Soup**: Taste authentic *bun bo Hue* at Bun Bo Ba Tuyet (47 Nguyen Cong Tru) for thick rice vermicelli in a rich lemongrass and fermented shrimp broth (45,000 VND).
- **15:00 – Dong Ba Market**: Browse dried lotus seeds, traditional conical hats (*non la*), and royal teas at Hue’s central market.

### Day 8: Scenic Hai Van Pass to Hoi An Ancient Town

Travel south from Hue to Hoi An along one of Asia's most spectacular coastal routes:

- **09:00 – Coastal Rail Journey**: Board the daily **"Central Heritage Connection"** tourist train (HD1/HD3) from Hue to Da Nang (110,000–180,000 VND). The train snakes along the cliffs of the Hai Van Pass, providing dramatic vistas of turquoise waters and Lang Co Bay.
- **12:30 – Arrival in Da Nang & Lunch**: Grab lunch in Da Nang, sampling *mi Quang* noodles with turmeric-stained broth and fresh herbs (40,000 VND).
- **14:00 – Marble Mountains (*Ngu Hanh Son*)**: Stop at Thuy Son mountain to explore natural cave shrines and Buddhist grottoes carved into marble hills (40,000 VND entrance).
- **16:00 – Check-in in Hoi An**: Settle into a boutique heritage resort in Cam Chau or along the Thu Bon River. Walk through lantern-lit lanes in the evening, enjoying the car-free pedestrian atmosphere.

### Day 9: Hoi An Heritage Monuments and Bespoke Tailoring

Explore Hoi An's exceptionally preserved 16th-to-18th-century mercantile port:

- **09:00 – Historic Monument Tour**: Purchase the 120,000 VND ticket covering five heritage sites. Visit the Japanese Covered Bridge, the Tan Ky family residence, and the colorful Fujian Assembly Hall.
- **11:30 – Custom Tailoring Commission**: Visit skilled tailors such as Bebe Tailor or Yaly Couture to be fitted for tailored linen shirts, suits, or dresses (completed within 24 to 36 hours).
- **13:00 – Legendary Banh Mi**: Savor a crusty baguette sandwich at Madam Khanh "The Banh Mi Queen" (35,000–45,000 VND).
- **15:30 – Biking to An Bang Beach**: Rent a bicycle from your hotel and pedal 4 kilometers through green rice paddies to An Bang Beach for afternoon relaxation.
- **19:30 – Lantern Boat Ride on the Hoai River**: Board a licensed small wooden boat (150,000 VND per boat for 20 minutes) as hundreds of colorful lanterns illuminate the river.

### Day 10: Ancient Cham Ruins of My Son Sanctuary

Venture inland to Vietnam's most significant ancient religious site:

- **07:30 – My Son Early Tour**: Drive 45 kilometers west to the forested valley of My Son (150,000 VND entrance). Built by the kings of Champa between the 4th and 14th centuries, these red-brick towers honor the Hindu god Shiva. Visiting early avoids midday heat and tourist buses.
- **12:30 – Countryside Lunch**: Savor smoked carp pancakes or grilled river fish in Nam Phuoc.
- **14:30 – Cam Thanh Nipa Palm Forest**: Ride in a traditional round bamboo basket boat (*thuyen thung*) through tidal waterways fringed by water coconuts (150,000 VND for two passengers).
- **17:00 – Final Tailoring Fittings**: Pick up and inspect your finished tailored garments in Hoi An.

### Day 11: Flight to Ho Chi Minh City and the French Heritage Core

Transfer to Da Nang Airport (45 minutes, 300,000 VND) for your flight to Ho Chi Minh City.

- **10:00 – Flight DAD to SGN**: Board an 85-minute direct flight to Tan Son Nhat Airport (850,000–1,500,000 VND).
- **12:30 – Arrival in District 1**: Settle into your hotel. The transition from peaceful Hoi An to Vietnam's southern commercial powerhouse is striking: glass skyscrapers, bustling expressways, and vibrant urban energy.
- **14:00 – Colonial Architecture Walk**: Stroll along Dong Khoi Street. View the Saigon Opera House, Notre-Dame Cathedral exterior (under structural restoration until 2027), and the 1891 Central Post Office with its grand arched interior (free entry).
- **16:00 – War Remnants Museum**: Examine comprehensive photographic galleries, military aircraft, and historic exhibits on Vo Van Tan Street (60,000 VND entrance).
- **19:30 – Broken Rice Dinner**: Dine at Com Tam Ba Ghiền (84 Dang Van Ngu) for charcoal-grilled pork chops with broken rice (65,000–85,000 VND).

### Day 12: Cu Chi Underground Tunnels and Cholon Heritage

Explore wartime underground networks and Vietnam’s largest Chinatown:

- **07:30 – Ben Duoc Tunnels (Cu Chi)**: Take a small-group morning tour to the Ben Duoc tunnel complex (70 km northwest, 125,000 VND entrance). Unlike the heavily modified Ben Dinh tunnels, Ben Duoc preserves authentic underground networks across three subterranean levels.
- **13:00 – Return to Saigon & Lunch**: Enjoy *banh xeo* (crispy southern yellow pancakes filled with shrimp, pork, and bean sprouts, 80,000 VND) at Banh Xeo 46A on Dinh Cong Trang.
- **15:00 – Cholon Heritage Walk (District 5)**: Visit Ba Thien Hau Pagoda (1760), dedicated to the sea goddess Mazu. Admire intricate porcelain friezes and aromatic coils of hanging incense. Browse the massive covered corridors of Binh Tay Market.
- **18:30 – Saigon River Sunset**: Board the public Saigon Waterbus from Bach Dang Wharf to Binh An (15,000 VND) for skyline views at twilight.

### Day 13: Journey South to Can Tho and the Deep Mekong Delta

Depart Ho Chi Minh City early at 07:30 AM in a luxury limousine van heading southwest along the CT01 Expressway to Can Tho, the regional capital of the Mekong Delta (170 km, 3.5 hours, 180,000–220,000 VND).

- **11:30 – Arrival in Can Tho**: Check into a boutique riverside resort along the Hau River or near Ninh Kieu Wharf.
- **13:30 – Cycling Rural Islets**: Cross by local ferry to An Binh or Son Islet. Cycle along narrow concrete paths beneath pomelo, rambutan, and durian trees. Visit a local fish raft floating on the river.
- **17:00 – Ninh Kieu Wharf Sunset**: Stroll along Can Tho’s pedestrian promenade, admiring the bronze statue of Ho Chi Minh and the illuminated suspension bridge over the Hau River.
- **19:00 – Delta Evening Feast**: Dine at a local riverside restaurant on grilled snakehead fish wrapped in rice paper (*ca loc nuong trui*) and hot pot with yellow river flowers (*canh chua ca linh bong dien dien*, 180,000–250,000 VND).

### Day 14: Dawn at Cai Rang Floating Market and Departure

Wake early at 05:30 AM for the crowning experience of the lower Mekong:

- **06:00 – Private Wooden Boat to Cai Rang**: Board a private wooden sampan from Ninh Kieu Wharf (400,000–500,000 VND for a private 3-hour cruise). Arrive at Cai Rang Floating Market by 06:45 AM as trading peaks.
- **07:00 – Breakfast on the Water**: Pull alongside a floating vendor boat for hot *hu tieu* noodle soup (45,000 VND) and iced robusta coffee, watching wholesale traders identify goods tied to tall bamboo poles (*cay beo*).
- **08:30 – Rice Noodle Workshop**: Stop at a small family-run rice noodle workshop to see how rice paper sheets are dried on bamboo frames and shredded into thin noodles.
- **10:00 – Return to Wharf & Checkout**: Disembark and check out of your hotel.
- **Midday Options**:
  - *Option A*: Take a direct flight from Can Tho International Airport (VCA) to your international departure hub.
  - *Option B*: Transfer 3.5 hours back to Tan Son Nhat Airport (SGN) by private highway car for your evening international flight home.

## Seasonal Route Customization

Depending on which month you travel, small adjustments ensure the best weather conditions:

- **November to April (Dry Season Nationwide)**: The optimal window to run the exact itinerary above. The south is warm and dry, the center experiences decreasing rain, and the north enjoys pleasant autumn and spring temperatures.
- **May to August (Summer Heat & Central Beaches)**: Temperatures climb across Hanoi and Saigon (33°C–36°C). Shift one night from Hanoi to Hoi An or Da Nang to enjoy calm seas and sunny central beaches.
- **September to October (Central Typhoon Season)**: Hue and Hoi An can experience seasonal flooding. If heavy monsoons occur, substitute Hue and Hoi An with the Northern Mountain highlands (Sapa or Ha Giang Loop) or the southern shores of Phu Quoc Island.

> [!WARNING]
> During the annual Lunar New Year (*Tet*, occurring between late January and mid-February), intercity buses and trains sell out weeks in advance. Many independent restaurants and heritage monuments close for 3 to 5 days. Confirm all bookings well ahead if traveling during Tet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I add Sapa into a 14-day Vietnam itinerary?
You can, but it requires cutting either Ninh Binh or the Mekong Delta. Sapa lies 315 kilometers northwest of Hanoi and demands at least three full days (including overnight travel). For a balanced first-time experience, we recommend prioritizing the central heritage corridor over Sapa to avoid excessive travel fatigue.

### What luggage is most practical for this two-week route?
Travel with a medium rolling suitcase or a 40-to-50-liter travel backpack. Because you will board small sampans in Ninh Binh and the Mekong Delta, light and manageable luggage makes intercity transfers vastly smoother. Laundry service is fast and inexpensive everywhere in Vietnam (20,000–30,000 VND per kilogram, washed and folded within 24 hours).

### Is the tap water safe to drink during the trip?
Do not drink tap water in Vietnam. Reputable hotels provide complimentary sealed glass or recyclable bottles daily. Sealed bottled water is available at every street corner for 10,000 to 15,000 VND. Ice cubes in established cafes and restaurants are produced industrially from purified water and are generally safe.
